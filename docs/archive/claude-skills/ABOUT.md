# Archived Claude skills

This directory preserves the final forms of two inactive Skills from the former `claude-skills` repository. They are historical documentation only and are not part of the Kntnt Skills Collection.

## Status

The archived files are not operational instructions, dependencies, references, or sources of current behaviour. Do not install them, load them as Skills, keep them synchronized with their descendants, or use them to interpret the current Collection.

The two original `SKILL.md` files have neutral archive names so that tooling and readers do not mistake them for active Skills. Their contents and the contents of their companion files are otherwise preserved byte for byte.

Do not link this archive from `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `agents.d/`, the root `README.md`, or an active Skill. A reader looking specifically for the old `claude-skills/proofread` or `claude-skills/thomas-writer` material can discover it by searching the repository tree.
