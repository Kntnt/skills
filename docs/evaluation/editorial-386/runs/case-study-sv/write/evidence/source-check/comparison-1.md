## Part 3 — Translated quotations

Translation is permitted by the material, preserving stance and qualification. Each quotation checked for meaning, stance, certainty, distinctive wording, self-correction, anything additive, and for idiomatic Swedish speech rather than English syntax.

**Q1.** Draft: "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Meaning, person, number, tense and "redan"/"already" preserved. Stance: ownership of the categories asserted with the same firmness, and Svale's part kept subordinate ("hjälpte oss"). No hedge removed or added. The semicolon and two-clause shape kept; V2 order holds; no implicit referent made explicit. **No finding.**

**Q2.** Draft: "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar." Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
The comparison and the conditional are preserved, and fronting the object ("Den tiden skulle jag avsätta") keeps V2. One obstruction, below. **Translation finding T2.**

**Q3.** Draft: "Jag skulle välja att göra om testet. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
This is the quotation the material marks as her actual, qualified assessment, and the qualification survives intact: the concessive "men" is kept, the present-tense "hjälper oss" is not strengthened, "en extra vecka" is not generalised, and "skulle" is not turned into a recommendation. "one view of the reports" → "en samlad bild av anmälningarna" is idiomatic and neither narrows nor widens it. Nothing additive. **No finding.**

**T2 — "innan nästa hus börjar" is English syntax, not Swedish speech.**
Concrete obstruction: in Swedish, *börja* with an inanimate subject and no complement leaves the clause open — a Swedish reader waits for what the house is beginning to do, and the sentence ends instead. The English elliptical "starts" carries this intransitively; the Swedish does not. Smallest repair: **"innan nästa hus drar i gång"** — idiomatic, and it keeps the same ellipsis the speaker used. A fuller rendering such as "innan nästa hus börjar använda loggen" is *not* proposed: the supplied context does not settle what starts, so making the referent explicit would add what the speaker did not supply.
