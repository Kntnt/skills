## 2. Translation check on the quotations

The three quotations are translated under the material's permission. Each is checked for meaning, stance, certainty and distinctive voice in idiomatic Swedish speech, not for word-for-word correspondence.

1. "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log." → "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." Meaning, the pluperfect "had already done", the possessive emphasis of "were ours" and Svale's subordinate helping role are all carried. Idiomatic. No finding.

2. "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts." → "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus drar i gång." *lade tid på*, *komma överens om*, *avsätta* and *drar i gang* are ordinary spoken Swedish; the comparative and the conditional survive, and the fronting of *Den tiden* is a Swedish way of keeping the sentence's focus where the speaker put it. No finding.

3. "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation." → "Jag skulle välja att göra om testet. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." The endorsement, its conditional form and the attached reservation survive intact. "one view of the reports" → "en samlad bild av anmälningarna" was examined: *samlad bild* is a consolidated overview rather than literally a single view, a marginally more managerial register than the plain source phrase. But "one view" is not distinctive or unusual wording that the speaker reached for, the Swedish states the same thing the speaker claims helps them, and no concrete obstruction to the source's reading follows from it. A literal *en gemensam vy* would be a preferred synonym, not a repair. No finding.

No implicit referent was made explicit beyond what the material settles, and no fragmentary answer was welded into a fluent sentence: each quotation is one of the three complete usable quotations, used whole.

## 3. Findings

### Finding S1 — source support: the standfirst gives the count without its exclusions or its source

**Draft (standfirst):** "31 anmälningar registrerades, och arbetsledaren skulle göra om testet …"
**Material:** "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that **31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial.**"

**What is missing:** the two exclusions, and the fact that the figure is the internal note's. The base contract holds the boundary "in the title, summary, headings and body alike" and asks that scope and qualifications be preserved "wherever a claim appears".

**The case that stands:** suppose emergency reports were also entered in the shared log during the eight weeks — say a further dozen — but the note's count of 31 leaves them out, as the material says it does. The material's sentence then holds exactly, while the standfirst's unqualified "31 anmälningar registrerades" is false as a statement of what was registered. Nothing supplied excludes this: the material says the count "excludes emergencies", not that emergencies were kept out of the log. The body carries the exclusions two paragraphs later, but the standfirst's claim is read on its own.

**Smallest supported repair:** add the exclusion to the count in the standfirst, e.g. "31 anmälningar registrerades, akuta ärenden och tidigare beställda arbeten oräknade, och arbetsledaren skulle göra om testet …" — no other change, and no need to move the note's attribution forward.

### Finding S2 — source support: the lead asserts one store per channel

**Draft (lead):** "Telefonanmälningar på ett ställe, e-post på ett annat."
**Material:** "Previously telephone reports and emails **were stored separately**."

**What is changed:** the material says only that the two kinds of report were kept apart from one another. The draft adds that each kind sat in a single place — one store for telephone reports, one for email.

**The case that stands:** suppose telephone reports were noted in a paper log at each of the buildings, and emails sat in the individual inboxes of the staff who received them. The reports and the emails are then stored separately, so the material's sentence holds; but "telefonanmälningar på ett ställe, e-post på ett annat" fails, because neither channel had one place. Nothing in the material excludes this arrangement, and the following sentence — "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information" — is at least as compatible with scattered stores as with two tidy ones.

**Smallest supported repair:** drop the count without losing the rhetorical shape of the lead: "Telefonanmälningar för sig, e-post för sig." The rest of the sentence ("Så förvarades felanmälningarna hos Elm Quay Housing …") stands unchanged.

### Passages reported as editorial questions rather than defects

None. The three borderline passages examined — "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte" (§1.5), the heading "Beslutet om fler hus återstår" with "Laget vill först se …" (§1.11), and the attribution of the checklist to Svale Systems (§1.13) — are each defensible as written on the material supplied, and are recorded above rather than raised as defects. No passage sits on a genuinely unsettled line between a standpoint and a factual assertion: the brief's own positions (that Svale Systems is the publisher, that the case is supplier-published and not independent journalism, that the checklist is the optional next step) are carried as the publisher's, and the draft asserts no figure, event or technical effect beyond the material.
