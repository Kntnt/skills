Notes on the two echoes:

No heading is a label, none uses a colon for a verb, none is a question, and none overclaims: every heading's proposition is carried by the text it heads at the same strength (the H1's "saknar en ruta för förståelse" is carried by "det finns ingen ruta att skriva in det i" plus "ytterligare en ruta" / "prova en enkel fråga bredvid tiden").

## 3. F1 — **pass**

No invented scene, meeting, childhood memory, line of dialogue, bodily sensation, statistic or general trend appears — the four things the boundaries forbid by name. The column opens on the document observation the material supplies, and the avowals of ignorance are carried intact ("Det är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra", "Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre"). Six passages that move away from the notes, each checked in both directions:

1. **Headline, the thing named.** Notes state the absence of a box "för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta"; the headline states the absence of a box "för förståelse". The case that would break the draft while the notes hold — the template carries a purpose or understanding box but no decision box — is excluded by the notes' own "Ändå vill jag prova en enkel fråga bredvid tiden" and "ytterligare en ruta", both of which presuppose the room is not there. Supported.
2. **"Tiden, däremot, har mallen två rutor för."** A count-size judgement, and the supplied comparison is the notes' own enumeration "rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning", which names exactly two time fields. It does read that enumeration as the inventory, which the notes' contrastive "men ingen ruta för …" also does. Supported; no evaluative characterisation is attached to the number.
3. **Scope of the caveat.** In the notes, "Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra" closes a paragraph holding both the appreciation of differing understandings and the irritation about time. In the draft it sits with the irritation only, and the appreciation paragraph that follows carries no disclaimer. This is a narrowing of a caveat's scope and I record it as such — but the sentences left outside it ("Jag uppskattar möten där …", "Den upptäckten **kan** vara viktigare …") are a first-person preference and a modal possibility, neither of which asserts a measurement the caveat would have to defuse. No claim is upgraded. Noted, not a defect.
4. **"Också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans går det att säga vad vi vill undersöka. Det är ingen hög tröskel."** The notes carry the concessive ("också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans") attached to a deontic ("borde få formulera"), not a feasibility claim. The draft asserts feasibility flatly. It stands in the columnist's own voice as the argument for her own proposal, and the notes call that proposal "en enkel fråga", which is what "ingen hög tröskel" restates. Within the author's argument on supported material. Noted; see §7.
5. **"Pröva den gärna i din egen mall, och behåll tvivlet på rutan medan du gör det."** No second person anywhere in the material, whose closing sentence is first person ("Jag vill behålla både hoppet om den frågan och tvivlet på ännu en ruta"). The sentence asserts no fact about the world or about Nora Vik; it is advocacy, the doubt it hands the reader is the notes' own doubt, and the hope half is carried by the preceding "Ändå vill jag prova". Its one presupposition — that the reader has a template — is softened by "gärna" and sits inside the brief's audience, "yrkesverksamma som planerar möten". Not an unsupported fact.
6. **Wording shifts that change nothing.** "av dokumentet" → "av ett dokument" (less specific, true wherever the source is true, and the preceding sentence settles which document); "en mall" → "mallen" (a narrowing the general norm entails); "pressas" → "pressas fram"; "just" dropped from the standfirst but kept in the body. None adds a claim, strengthens a modality or moves who stands behind a statement.

Unknown is kept apart from absent throughout: the text says the template has no box for the decision (stated in the notes) and never says the template has no instructions or no other fields.

## 4. G1 — **pass**

The genre does its job for "yrkesverksamma som planerar möten". The reader gets a recognisable angle held from first line to last — a meeting template that counts the time but never asks what the time is for — and a concrete thing to consider against their own practice: "Ändå vill jag prova en enkel fråga bredvid tiden: vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?" The author's eye is visible, as the brief asks ("Hennes blick ska märkas"): "Tiden, däremot, har mallen två rutor för" is the dry understatement the assignment wanted, and "Det finns en komik i att försöka lösa ett problem med formulär genom att utöka formuläret" turns the joke on her own proposal. It is not a campaign and carries no purchase call, and it honours the brief's stance requirement — "hon medger att icke-beslutande samtal har värde" — in a section of its own ("Samtal kan skapa förtroende. De kan också få oss att upptäcka en fråga vi inte visste att vi behövde ställa.").

## 5. G2 — **fail**

Most of the criterion is met, and met well. Every part is present in the anatomy's order — headline, standfirst, byline, lead, three sections, ending. The byline "Av Nora Vik" names the author the brief names ("Författaren är den fiktiva bibliotekarien Nora Vik"), and the delivery account states it: "The byline carries the author the brief names." The standfirst stands alone: a reader who sees only it learns who is writing, what she found, what she argues and that she does not know whether it would work. The lead begins the work and comes before the first H2 (`parts.lead` precedes `parts.sections[0]` in the script's output, and it is 1 paragraph). The ending shows the lead's expectation met — the lead raises a missing box, the ending proposes the question that would fill it and refuses to promise it works. The column-specific demands hold: personal reflection rather than a compulsory anecdote, and a call to action that grows out of the reflection and stands beside honest uncertainty in the same sentence pair ("Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre … Pröva den gärna i din egen mall, och behåll tvivlet på rutan medan du gör det").

The failure is one required part not doing its own job. The anatomy requires subheadings to be "understood on their own without … repeating … the first sentence under them" — a plain statement, so a requirement. The third subheading repeats its first sentence almost word for word:

> ## Jag vet inte om rutan skulle hjälpa
>
> Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre.

Reader effect: this is the column's turn, the one section where the argument bends back on itself, and the heading buys the reader no ground — having read it, the first half of the sentence beneath is already spent. A reader scanning the three headings and then dropping into the last section reads the same proposition twice in the same words. Following the requirement was plainly possible: the paragraph holds two further ideas the heading could have named — the comedy of answering a form problem with more form, and the question itself — and the text is not better for the repetition. The other two subheadings show the writer can do it (`Jag värderar det mallen inte frågar efter` heads a section whose first sentence is about the time boxes; `Ett samtal utan beslut kan också ha ett syfte` shares only vocabulary with the sentence beneath it, not its proposition).

This is a contract rejection on a stated requirement, not a preference between valid editorial solutions: the requirement is satisfiable in many ways and the text satisfies none of them here.

## 6. P1 — **pass**

The reasoning is followable end to end, and it runs on real transitions rather than connective filler: "Tiden, däremot, har mallen två rutor för" turns the lead's inventory into the argument; "Men den som fyller i mallen borde få formulera …" marks the concession-to-claim pivot after the paragraph granting that conversations need no decision; "Ändå vill jag prova …" carries the doubt into the proposal. No concept is used before it is introduced — the template and its fields are laid out in the lead before anything is argued about them, and "rutan" in the third heading is anchored by "ytterligare en ruta" in the sentence beneath it.

Conclusions are proportionate to visible support, which is the hard part here, since the material is one person's reflection: the text claims no effect on meetings, says "kan" where the notes say "kan", and ends on "Jag vet inte". The one step the reader must take unaided is the link between the first heading ("Jag värderar det mallen inte frågar efter") and the paragraph that opens the section, which is about the time boxes — that is, about what the template *does* ask for. The contrast is recoverable from "däremot" in the first sentence, so the reader is not lost.

## 8. L1 — **pass**

The prose reads as written in Swedish, not translated into it. The syntax is Swedish-native throughout, including the fronted objects and inversions an English-shaped draft would not produce: "Tiden, däremot, har mallen två rutor för" (object first, verb second, stranded preposition at the end) and "Också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans går det att säga vad vi vill undersöka" (adverbial clause first, V2 inversion in the main clause). The subjunctive in "som om utrymmet vore detsamma som det som skulle rymmas där" is correctly formed, and the "utrymme / rymmas" pairing is a Swedish play on a shared root that no translation would arrive at. Idiom is native: "retar mig på", "ingen hög tröskel", "Pröva den gärna", "att ha åstadkommit", "kolleger". No English calque, no anglicised word order, no imported collocation. The register is the brief's — reflective, self-ironic, plain.

## 9. L2 — **pass**

The resolved locale is Swedish and it governs throughout. Spelling is Swedish with correct definite and plural morphology ("mötesmallen", "rutorna"/"rutor", "kolleger", "dagordning"). Punctuation follows Swedish rules: lower case after the colon in "en enkel fråga bredvid tiden: vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?"; no serial comma in "starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning"; commas set clause boundaries rather than English-style appositive pairs. The text contains no numerals, no dates and no currency, so nothing was invented in those forms — "två rutor" is spelled out, as Swedish prefers for small numbers.

One mechanical note, short of a failure: the standfirst's parenthetical dash is an em dash, `—` (U+2014), in "varandras tid — utan att hon vet om ännu en ruta gör möten bättre". Swedish typography's tankstreck is normally the en dash, `–` (U+2013), with spaces. It is a glyph choice inside established variation, it is applied consistently (the text has exactly one), and it changes no meaning, spelling or number form.
