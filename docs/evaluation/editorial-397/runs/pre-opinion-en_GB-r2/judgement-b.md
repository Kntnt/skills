## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The decisive change is the headline, from "Keep the telephone until Lervik knows why it is used" to "Keep Lervik's telephone booking through a six-month trial". The reply's reason is "to bring the headline within eight words". The input headline had no visible defect beyond "telephone" standing for "telephone booking", and fixing that would not have required changing the headline's chronology or deleting its clause about the unknown reasons. The criterion says clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy numerical guidelines. This rewrite was made to meet a word count, and it changes what the article's thesis line claims.

The standfirst and the conclusion-stating subheading are further taste changes to a text that did not need them. They add no new claims.

**Legitimate changes:** "both routes, telephone and web"; "The board can adopt"; and the ambiguity repair in the "cost of the work" sentence. That repair narrows the meaning, but the narrowing follows the text's own time figures.

**Losses the criterion rejects:** the headline's "until Lervik knows why it is used"; and, marginally, the general "cost" in L13.
