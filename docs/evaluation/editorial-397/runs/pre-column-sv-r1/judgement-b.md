In total there are 8 differences. Four of them are changes to what a claim says: D2 (meaning, and certainty slightly), D3 (certainty and scope), D5 (certainty) and D7 (scope and meaning). All four are in added text. No original sentence was rewritten.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The input is a clean, well-built column. The only concrete visible defect is the spaced em dash (D6), and the Skill fixed it correctly. Everything else (a new title, a standfirst, three subheadings and a call to action) rewrites a clean text to fit the genre's anatomy. That breaks *Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines.*

The added text also changes what claims say:

- `## Samtalen får vara öppna, men bokad tid är inget resultat` turns a bounded personal irritation into a flat general assertion.
- `… men den är värd att pröva` hardens *Jag vill prova den i alla fall*.
- *Pröva gärna själv nästa gång du fyller i en mötesmall …* replaces the deliberately ambivalent ending with advice to all readers.

The reply's account then denies any shift in scope or certainty. Voice, arguments and every original sentence are preserved, and no limiting sentence was lost. Even so, the unrequested structural rewrite, and the claim shifts it carries, decide the verdict.
