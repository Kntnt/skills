# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`. Compared line by line with `work/input.md`, there are two differences. The frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks and all other body sentences are identical.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall har ingen ruta för mötets syfte`. Class: a change of taste that is also a **change to what a claim says: meaning**. The original headline is allusive. Its referent is left open, so it can be read as the missing decision box in paragraph 1 or as the purpose question the author proposes in the last paragraph. The new headline states a literal fact and names the missing box as one for the meeting's *syfte*. The only missing box the body actually names is one for "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta" (the decision to be made). The body supports "purpose" only by inference, from "Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om" and "Den säger inte varför". The rewrite also turns a figurative title into a flat declarative claim at headline level. That raises how literally the headline asserts, but it bounds nothing and weakens no limit (see section 3). It is not a repair of a visible defect: the old headline has no error, and the first paragraph resolves it.
2. **Dash in paragraph 3.** Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid`. After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid`. Class: a **mechanical correction** (locale form). A spaced em dash becomes a spaced en dash, which is Swedish typographic practice. The spaces were already there, so only the glyph changed.

No difference follows from another.
