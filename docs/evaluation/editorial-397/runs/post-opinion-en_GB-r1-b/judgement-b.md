## 4. R1

**Fail, narrowly.** Every argument, claim, quotation and limiting sentence is preserved, and nothing is hardened. The failure is the clause "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines."

The Skill's own verdict is that the text is clean: "The piece itself holds up." Every change it made is still taste or a numerical guideline, not a concrete visible defect:

- **The deciding passage is the subheading.** It replaces a working, voiced heading, "Six months, both routes, and something to measure", with "Learn what each channel costs staff and why people still phone". The stated defect was that the heading repeats the sentence under it. The replacement does not remove that defect. It moves the echo to the paragraph's second sentence and loses the heading's voice.
- **The paragraph splits** are made purely for length, "with no wording changed".
- **The byline move** turns a conventional opinion-piece sign-off into a byline.

These edits are small, and none damages a claim. That is why the fail is narrow.
