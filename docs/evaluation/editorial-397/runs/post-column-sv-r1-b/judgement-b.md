# Judgement b

The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`. I compared it line by line with `work/input.md`. The frontmatter, the byline, paragraphs 1–5 and 7, and all Markdown formatting are identical. There are three differences, spread over two lines.

## 1. Differences

1. Headline, subject. Before: `# Mallen har …`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall har …`. Class: a change of taste, which the Skill presents as the repair of a visible defect. A definite headline noun that the first sentence resolves ("Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall") is an ordinary convention in a Swedish column. The "defect" is that the headline cannot stand alone, which is the anatomy guideline's requirement and not a flaw you can see in the text. The claim does not change. The new referent is the one the body already names, so there is no change of scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning, and nothing is hardened.
2. Headline, object. Before: `… en ruta för allt utom poängen`. After: `… rutor för allt utom poängen`. Class: a change of taste. It does not follow from difference 1: `Bibliotekets mötesmall har en ruta för allt utom poängen` would be grammatical and would have done the job. The idiom's meaning survives ("has a box for everything"). What is lost is the voice: the singular "en ruta" is the motif the column comes back to in "ytterligare en ruta" and "tvivlet på ännu en ruta". No claim element changes.
3. Paragraph 7. Before: `utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien`. After: `utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien`. Class: a mechanical correction, the locale form of the spaced dash (Swedish tankstreck is an en dash).

Changes to what a claim says: none.

## 2. The account

1. Headline subject: reported, and accurately as to what changed. "Rubriken förstods inte fristående. 'Mallen' förutsatte att läsaren redan visste vilken mall det gällde. Nu står 'bibliotekets mötesmall' i rubriken, hämtat ur inledningen. Rubriken är 54 tecken och 8 ord och påstår inget mer än förut." The counts check out (54 characters, 8 words). The words "påstår inget mer än förut" are true.
2. Headline object, "en ruta" to "rutor": not reported. The reply describes the headline change only as "skrivits om så att den säger vilken mall det gäller" and ends "I övrigt är texten oförändrad". That is inaccurate by omission, because the number change is a separate edit with its own effect on voice.
3. Dash: reported accurately. "Korrekturläsningen på slutet bytte också ett engelskt tankstreck (—) mot ett svenskt (–)."

## 4. R1

**Fail, narrowly.** Everything that matters for claims is preserved: every limiting sentence, every argument and every quotation, word for word. The reply is honest in listing the structural gaps it left for a person (lede, subheadings, call to the reader) and did not rewrite anything to fill them.

What decides the verdict is the headline. The text was clean apart from the dash. The Skill rewrote the headline to meet a stand-alone-headline guideline, not to repair a defect visible in the text. In doing so it also changed "en ruta" to "rutor". The finding did not require that change, and the reply does not report it ("I övrigt är texten oförändrad"). The change breaks the working motif that the column closes on: "Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta …" and "tvivlet på ännu en ruta". That is a change of taste to working voice with no finding behind it, and R1 does not allow it on a clean text.
