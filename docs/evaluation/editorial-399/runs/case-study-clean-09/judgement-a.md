# Judgement a: case-study-clean-09

## 1. Differences

A line-by-line diff of `work/input.md` against the fenced text in `response.md` finds exactly one difference. The frontmatter (there is none), the headline, the standfirst, the `Text: Iris Falk` byline, every body paragraph, both quotations, the numbers, the supplier publication disclosure and the checklist link are all unchanged, byte for byte.

- Line 15, second subheading.
  - Before: `## Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning` (39 characters)
  - After: `## Mediantiden till tilldelning sjönk från tre arbetsdagar till två` (64 characters)
  - Class: a change to what a claim says, in **causality** and **certainty**. The old subheading carried the section's own qualification: the two periods are not comparable. The new one states the result with no qualification, and it sits in a supplier-published customer case. A reader who only skims will take it as "the log made assignment faster", which is the causal reading the body refuses in so many words ("anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran"). The change also takes a subheading that met the 36–39-character limit and makes it 64 characters. It is not the repair of a visible defect: the old subheading is a clear, accurate label. The case for it (it has no verb and does not state the outcome) is a matter of taste.

## 2. The account

- The subheading change **is reported**, as finding 1 under "Åtgärdat". The reply describes the before and after text accurately. Its reason ("saknade verb och sa inte vad avsnittet visar ... 'två perioder' gick inte att förstå utan att läsa avsnittet") presents a taste preference as a defect.
- The claim-level consequence **is reported, and reported accurately**. Finding 2, "Olöst: uppstod genom korrigeringen", says the new subheading "ger resultatet utan dess förbehåll" and invites a causal reading "som texten uttryckligen avstår från". The "Påståenden som ändrats eller tagits bort" list records that the qualification was removed from the heading and is kept in full in the body. It also records the result being moved, unqualified, into the heading. The account is honest and complete. It documents a loss that the Skill itself caused.
- The opening line says "Den avslutande korrekturläsningen hittade inga mekaniska fel". This is consistent with the diff: there is no mechanical change.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The control is a clean text, and R1 says that "clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste". The run rewrote a correct subheading because it "saknade verb", which is a preference, not a visible defect. The rewrite changed what the heading claims. It went from "Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning", a heading that carried the caveat, to "Mediantiden till tilldelning sjönk från tre arbetsdagar till två", which states the result without the caveat in a supplier-published text. The reply's own finding 2 says so: "Det är ett orsakssamband som texten uttryckligen avstår från. Rubriken är alltså skarpare än avsnittet." Reporting the loss honestly does not undo an unwarranted change to a claim that was preserved before the run. The change also takes the subheading out of the 36–39-character range that it met.
