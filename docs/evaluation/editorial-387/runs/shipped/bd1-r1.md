## 1. Claim accounting

Each row: draft passage, then the source passage in the source's own words, then what differs.

**C1. Title.** "Rönnvik should not make Digitalt grundstöd permanent on this evidence"
Source: "Tes: utbildningsnämnden bör inte permanenta kursen Digitalt grundstöd på det underlag som nu ligger"
Difference: the title names "Rönnvik" where the source names the committee. This is ordinary headline shorthand for the municipality's decision-maker. The thesis is the brief's standpoint. Supported.

**C2. Byline.** "By Milad Aziz, chair of Lyssna Först"
Source: "Avsändare är Milad Aziz, ordförande för föreningen Lyssna Först."
Supported.

**C3.** "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 to decide whether Digitalt grundstöd becomes a permanent course with six rounds a year."
Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år." / "Lyssna Först föreslår att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning"
Difference: the source says the staff report was prepared for the April 14 meeting. It does not say the committee decides at that meeting. **Finding F1** (minor).

**C4.** "The course should keep running while that decision waits."
Source: "Han motsätter sig inte kursen och vill att den ska finnas kvar medan beslutet väntar."
Difference: none. It is Aziz's own standpoint, and the "while that decision waits" limit is kept. Supported.

**C5.** "What should not happen is a permanent commitment made on paperwork that does not answer the question the committee is being asked."
Source: this is the thesis as above, resting on "Någon jämförelse med arbetssökande som inte gick kursen finns inte i handlingarna, och ingen kostnad per deltagare är redovisad."
The author's own argument, resting on supported material. Supported.

**C6.** "The municipality's evaluation of February 5, 2026, covers three rounds of the four-week course for job seekers at Rönnvik's adult education service during the fall of 2025."
Source: "Kommunens utvärdering Digitalt grundstöd, 5 februari 2026, gäller tre kursomgångar vid vuxenutbildningen i Rönnvik under hösten 2025. Kursen är fyra veckor lång och riktar sig till arbetssökande."
Difference: "for job seekers" renders "riktar sig till arbetssökande" (aimed at job seekers). In context this reads as the course's target group. Supported.

**C7.** "It rests on attendance lists and the course leaders' written summaries."
Source: "Utvärderingen bygger på närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar."
Supported. The pronoun "It" refers to the evaluation. It is singular and carries no gender.

**C8.** "Sixty-two people signed up and forty-eight completed the course."
Source: "Sextiotvå personer anmälde sig och fyrtioåtta fullföljde."
The figures are attributed to the evaluation by the paragraph they sit in. The source's own wording says "personer", and the source immediately qualifies that; the draft keeps the qualification in the very next sentence (C9). Defensible as written, so this is not a finding. For contrast, see C15.

**C9.** "Those are registrations and completions, not individual people: some participants took the course twice."
Source: "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger."
Supported.

**C10.** "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
Source: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
Difference: "förkunskaper" means prior knowledge, meaning what participants knew before the course. "Knowledge" without a qualifier also covers knowledge at or after the end of the course. **Finding F2.**

**C11.** "Completion is worth knowing. It tells the committee that people who started the course could finish it. It does not tell the committee what those people could do afterward that they could not do before."
Source: "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs säger att kursen gick att gå klart. Det säger inte att den gav dem något de inte redan hade." / "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
This is the author's own reasoning, following Aziz's supplied standpoint. The sentences are about what completion shows, not about the whole contents of the evaluation. With no measure of prior knowledge, the before-and-after claim holds. Supported. The pronouns "those people" and "they" are plural and carry no gender.

**C12. Heading.** "The staff recommendation rests on one number"
Source: "Motivet i utlåtandet är den höga andelen fullföljda kurser."
Test: suppose the staff report gave another motive, such as demand or cost. That case is excluded by the definite singular "Motivet i utlåtandet är …". The completion share is one figure. Supported.

**C13.** "The recommendation prepared for the April 14 meeting gives the high completion rate as its reason for making the course permanent and expanding it to six rounds a year."
Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år. Motivet i utlåtandet är den höga andelen fullföljda kurser."
Difference: "high" comes from the source's own words, "den höga andelen". The draft is no more ambiguous than the source about whose judgement "high" is. "Completion rate" renders "andelen fullföljda kurser" and adds no person-based unit. Supported.

**C14.** "The documents contain no comparison with job seekers who did not take the course, and no cost per participant."
Source: "Någon jämförelse med arbetssökande som inte gick kursen finns inte i handlingarna, och ingen kostnad per deltagare är redovisad."
Supported.

**C15.** "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
Source: "föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år" / "gäller tre kursomgångar … under hösten 2025"
Difference: "doubling" assumes the course now runs three rounds a year. The source gives three rounds in one term, and an expansion to six a year. It does not give the current annual count. **Finding F3.** "Thin basis" is the author's own judgement and is supported as argument.

**C16.** "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished. It does not say it gave them something they did not already have."
Source: "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs säger att kursen gick att gå klart. Det säger inte att den gav dem något de inte redan hade." / "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger."
Difference: the brief says Aziz's statement may be quoted or referenced, and the reasoning is his supported standpoint. But "forty-eight people" is a figure, and a figure stays a factual claim whoever supplied it. The evaluation counts 48 completions, not 48 persons. This passage is separated from the qualification in C9, and it restates the count as people. **Finding F4.** The pronoun "them" is plural and carries no gender. Translation: this is not in quotation marks, so it is a reference, not a quotation. The rendering is faithful and idiomatic. No translation finding.

**C17. Heading.** "Ask for the missing figures first"
Source: "ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen"
Difference: one of the two items requested, how participants found the course, is not a figure. Coming after C14, "the missing figures" could also be read as the comparison and the cost per participant, which Lyssna Först does not request. This is **editorial question E1**, not a defect. The body right below says exactly what is asked.

**C18.** "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt."
Difference: the source records that the association does not claim to have a measurement of its own. The draft asserts that it has none, and drops "egen" (its own). **Finding F5.**

**C19.** "We are asking the committee to postpone the decision to the autumn budget preparation and, in the meantime, to ask the administration for the cost per round and for an account of how participants found their way to the course."
Source: "Lyssna Först föreslår att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen."
Supported. "We" is first-person plural for the association, which matches "föreningen".

**C20.** "We are not asking for the course to close. Keep it running."
Source: "Han motsätter sig inte kursen och vill att den ska finnas kvar medan beslutet väntar."
Difference: the source gives this as Aziz's personal standpoint, and the draft says "we". The association's proposal (C19) does not ask for closure, so "not asking for the course to close" is supported for the association as well. "Keep it running" drops "medan beslutet väntar" (while the decision waits). In context, though, it sits between C4, which has that limit, and "But … postpone", so an ordinary reader takes it as the interim position. Defensible as written.

**C21.** "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
Source: "Lyssna Först föreslår att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen."
Difference: the source postpones to a point in time, the autumn budget preparation. The draft postpones until a condition is met, and decides then. **Finding F6.**

## 2. Findings (source support)

**F1 (minor). C3: the April 14 decision is not in the material.**
Missing support: the material says only "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår …". It does not say the committee decides that day.
Case: the report is presented on April 14 and the decision is scheduled for a later meeting. The source holds and the draft fails. No supplied statement excludes this case.
Smallest repair: "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 with a staff recommendation to make Digitalt grundstöd a permanent course with six rounds a year."

**F2. C10: "knowledge" is broader than "förkunskaper".**
Changed support: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper".
Case: suppose the course leaders' written summaries, one of the two stated bases, assess what participants could do at the end of the course. The source still holds, because prior knowledge is not measured, but the draft's "does not measure participants' knowledge" fails. No supplied statement excludes this.
Smallest repair: "The evaluation does not measure what participants already knew before the course."

**F3. C15: "doubling" is not supported.**
Missing support: the material gives "tre kursomgångar … under hösten 2025" and a proposal to be "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år". It gives no current annual count.
Case: the course also ran rounds in the spring, for example two in spring and three in fall. Going to six a year is then an expansion but not a doubling. The source holds and the draft fails. No supplied statement excludes this.
Smallest repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and expanding it."

**F4. C16: "forty-eight people" contradicts the evaluation's counting unit.**
Changed support: "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger."
Case: the 48 completions were made by fewer than 48 people, because some completed twice. The evaluation's count holds and the draft's "forty-eight people finishing" fails. Aziz's own wording in the brief does not turn the figure into a supported count of persons.
Smallest repair: "Forty-eight completions say the course could be finished. They do not say it gave participants something they did not already have."

**F5. C18: a denial of possession where the source records only the absence of a claim.**
Changed support: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt."
Case: the association holds some informal material of its own on effect but makes no claim to it. The source holds and the draft's "has no measurement" fails. The draft also drops "egen", so "no measurement" would also cover measurements made by others.
Smallest repair: "Lyssna Först does not claim to have any measurement of its own of the course's effect."

**F6. C21: postponing to a time becomes postponing until a condition is met.**
Changed support: "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa …"
Case one: the figures arrive in June, and under the draft the committee decides in June, before the budget preparation. Case two: the budget preparation arrives without the figures, and under the draft the decision keeps waiting. Either way the proposal and the draft come apart.
Smallest repair: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision to the autumn budget preparation, ask for the cost per round and how participants reached the course in the meantime, and decide then."

The body settles what is asked. A neutral option is "Ask for the missing information first".

## 4. Translation findings

None. The draft contains no quotation marks, so there are no translated quotations to check. Aziz's statement is rendered as a reference in C16. That rendering is faithful and idiomatic, apart from the source-support issue with the figure in F4.

## 5. Pronoun accounting

- "It" (evaluation, completion) and "it" (the course, the committee): singular, no gender.
- "those people", "they", "them" (participants or completers): plural, no gender. "Them" in C16 inherits the person-count problem in F4.
- "we" (C18 to C20): first-person plural, Lyssna Först speaking through its chair. This matches "föreningen" for C18 and C19. For C20, the source attributes the standpoint to Aziz personally ("Han"), and the association's proposal is consistent with it. Defensible.
- No pronoun assigns natural gender to Aziz or anyone else.
