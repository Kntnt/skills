## 2. Source-support findings

**F1: meeting purpose (paragraph 1)**
Draft: "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 to decide whether Digitalt grundstöd becomes a permanent course…"
Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas…"
What differs: the source says only that a staff statement prepared for the April 14 meeting proposes permanence. It does not say that the committee decides at that meeting.
Case: the committee takes up the recommendation on April 14 as a first reading and decides at a later meeting. The source statement holds and the draft's fails. No supplied statement excludes this. "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning" places the decision before autumn, not on April 14.
Severity: low.
Smallest repair: "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 with a staff recommendation before it to make Digitalt grundstöd a permanent course with six rounds a year." ("that decision" in the next sentence still reads correctly.)

**F2: what the evaluation does not measure (paragraph 2)**
Draft: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
Source: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
What differs: the thing measured. The source excludes prior knowledge. The draft excludes all knowledge.
Case: the course leaders' written summaries, which the evaluation rests on, record what participants could do at the end of the course. The source statement stays true and the draft's is false. No supplied statement excludes this.
Smallest repair: "The evaluation does not measure what participants already knew." (or "…participants' prior knowledge.") This also sets up the argument in 3c more precisely.

**F3: "doubling" (paragraph 5)**
Draft: "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
Source: "…gäller tre kursomgångar … under hösten 2025" and "…byggs ut till sex omgångar per år."
What differs: the source gives three rounds in one term and a target of six a year. It does not give the current number of rounds per year, so the factor of expansion is unknown.
Case: the course also ran a round in spring 2025 or spring 2026, so the current rate is four or more a year and six is not a doubling. Or the three fall rounds were a one-off pilot, and six a year is a larger step than doubling. Neither case is excluded.
Smallest repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and expanding it." (The valuation "thin basis" is the author's and stays.)

**F4: "Forty-eight people finishing" (paragraph 5)**
Draft: "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished."
Source: Aziz, "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs…", but the evaluation: "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger."
What differs: the thing counted. The figure counts completions, and fewer distinct people may have finished. The brief allows Aziz's statement to be quoted or paraphrased. That establishes whose point it is, not that 48 persons finished. The boundaries also require that "Siffror ska tillskrivas utvärderingen där de bär argumentet."
Case: 45 individuals completed, three of them twice. Then 48 completions holds and "forty-eight people finishing" fails. The evaluation's own qualification does not exclude this, it allows it.
Smallest repair: "Forty-eight completions say the course could be finished."

**F5: Lyssna Först's own measurement (paragraph 6)**
Draft: "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt."
What differs: certainty and scope. The source records only that the association makes no claim to have a measurement, and only to a measurement of its *own*. The draft's first clause turns an unmade claim into a stated absence and drops "own". The second clause is already supported.
Case: the association holds a measurement it did not make itself, or informal data of its own that it does not put forward. The source statement holds and "has no measurement" fails. Nothing excludes this. "Inga effektstudier … är belagda" concerns the supplied material, not what the association holds.
Smallest repair: "Lyssna Först does not claim any measurement of its own of the course's effect." (or keep the sentence's rhythm: "Lyssna Först claims no measurement of its own of the course's effect.")

**F6: when to decide (closing sentence)**
Draft: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
Source: "…skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen."
What differs: time. The association proposes a fixed point, the autumn budget preparation, with the information requested in the meantime. The draft makes the moment of decision depend on when the information arrives.
Cases: the administration reports in June, and the draft has the committee decide in June while the source has it wait for the autumn budget preparation. Or the information is not complete by autumn, and the draft has the committee keep waiting while the source has it decide at the budget preparation. Nothing excludes either case.
Smallest repair: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision to the autumn budget preparation, ask in the meantime for the cost per round and how participants reached the course, and decide then."

The surrounding sentences, "not asking for the course to close" and "postpone … and decide then", bound it to the interim, so an ordinary reader takes it that way.
