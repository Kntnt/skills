# Claim comparison report

Draft language: en_US. Source language: Swedish. Material: the brief, "Underlag" and "Boundaries / judging" in the fixture.

## Claim accounting

Each row pairs a draft passage with the source passage it rests on, quoted in Swedish, and states what differs. A verdict of "supported" means I could name no case compatible with the material in which one statement holds and the other fails, or that the only case is excluded by the quoted material.

### Title and byline

1. Draft: "Rönnvik should not make Digitalt grundstöd permanent on this evidence"
   Source (brief): "Tes: utbildningsnämnden bör inte permanenta kursen Digitalt grundstöd på det underlag som nu ligger, utan först låta förvaltningen komplettera det."
   Difference: the actor is "Rönnvik" (the municipality, by metonymy) rather than the committee, and "evidence" stands for "underlag". This is the author's thesis, which the brief assigns to the author. A headline reader takes "Rönnvik" as the municipality acting through its committee. Supported as the author's standpoint.

2. Draft: "By Milad Aziz, chair of Lyssna Först"
   Source: "Avsändare är Milad Aziz, ordförande för föreningen Lyssna Först."
   Supported.

### Opening

3. Draft: "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 to decide whether Digitalt grundstöd becomes a permanent course with six rounds a year."
   Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år." Also: "Lyssna Först föreslår att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning".
   Difference: the source says a staff report prepared for the April 14 meeting proposes permanence. The draft says the meeting is held to decide the question. Proposal wording and a pending decision are supported, but the material never says that the decision is on the April 14 agenda. See finding F1.

4. Draft: "The course should keep running while that decision waits."
   Source: "Han motsätter sig inte kursen och vill att den ska finnas kvar medan beslutet väntar."
   Supported as Aziz's standpoint.

5. Draft: "What should not happen is a permanent commitment made on paperwork that does not answer the question the committee is being asked."
   Source: the brief's thesis, together with "Någon jämförelse med arbetssökande som inte gick kursen finns inte i handlingarna, och ingen kostnad per deltagare är redovisad."
   This is the author's argument resting on supported material. It attributes no motive to the administration. Supported.

### What the evaluation counted

6. Draft: "The municipality's evaluation of February 5, 2026, covers three rounds of the four-week course for job seekers at Rönnvik's adult education service during the fall of 2025."
   Source: "Kommunens utvärdering Digitalt grundstöd, 5 februari 2026, gäller tre kursomgångar vid vuxenutbildningen i Rönnvik under hösten 2025. Kursen är fyra veckor lång och riktar sig till arbetssökande."
   Difference: "for job seekers" renders "riktar sig till" (is aimed at). Read in context, "a course for job seekers" names the target group and makes no claim that every participant was a job seeker. Supported.

7. Draft: "It rests on attendance lists and the course leaders' written summaries."
   Source: "Utvärderingen bygger på närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar."
   Supported.

8. Draft: "Sixty-two people signed up and forty-eight completed the course. Those are registrations and completions, not individual people: some participants took the course twice."
   Source: "Sextiotvå personer anmälde sig och fyrtioåtta fullföljde. Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger."
   Difference: none. The draft follows the source's own sequence and keeps the qualification in the very next sentence. Supported.

9. Draft: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
   Source: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
   Difference: the source's "förkunskaper" means prior knowledge. The draft's "knowledge" is broader and includes knowledge at the end of the course. See finding F2.

10. Draft: "Completion is worth knowing. It tells the committee that people who started the course could finish it. It does not tell the committee what those people could do afterward that they could not do before."
    Source: "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs säger att kursen gick att gå klart. Det säger inte att den gav dem något de inte redan hade." and "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
    This is the author's argument, which restates Aziz's permitted standpoint. "people who started" is not a count. The material counts registrations ("anmälde sig"), not starts. See editorial question Q1.

### The staff recommendation rests on one number

11. Draft heading: "The staff recommendation rests on one number"
    Source: "Motivet i utlåtandet är den höga andelen fullföljda kurser."
    Difference: "rests on" against "Motivet ... är". One case is a recommendation that also relies on other grounds. The source excludes it: it names the report's motive in the definite singular ("Motivet ... är den höga andelen"). The heading is a sharp but supported characterization, which the brief permits ("kritisera beslutsunderlaget skarpt"). Supported.

12. Draft: "The recommendation prepared for the April 14 meeting gives the high completion rate as its reason for making the course permanent and expanding it to six rounds a year."
    Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år. Motivet i utlåtandet är den höga andelen fullföljda kurser."
    Difference: none. The draft keeps "high" as part of the recommendation's stated reason, as the source does. Supported.

13. Draft: "The documents contain no comparison with job seekers who did not take the course, and no cost per participant."
    Source: "Någon jämförelse med arbetssökande som inte gick kursen finns inte i handlingarna, och ingen kostnad per deltagare är redovisad."
    Supported.

14. Draft: "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
    Source: "gäller tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025" and "föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år".
    Difference: "thin basis" is the author's valuation and is supported as his standpoint. "Doubling" is a factual claim, namely that the current rate is three rounds a year. The material states only that the evaluation covered three rounds in fall 2025. See finding F3.

15. Draft: "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished. It does not say it gave them something they did not already have."
    Source: "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs säger att kursen gick att gå klart. Det säger inte att den gav dem något de inte redan hade." (Aziz, "Detta får citeras eller refereras.") Also "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger."
    Difference: this faithfully renders Aziz's permitted wording in his own voice, and the reasoning is supported. The figure "forty-eight people" is still a factual claim about a population, and the evaluation says it counts completions, not persons. See finding F4.

### Ask for the missing figures first

16. Draft heading: "Ask for the missing figures first"
    Source: "under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen"
    This is advocacy. "Figures" fits the cost per round only, and how participants found the course is not a figure. The material states that cost per participant is unreported, not cost per round. Read as an orienting heading over the association's request, it is defensible. Not a finding.

17. Draft: "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
    Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt." Boundaries: "Inga effektstudier ... är belagda."
    Difference: the source says the association makes no claim to have a measurement of its own. The draft asserts that it has none, and it drops "egen" (of its own). See finding F5.

18. Draft: "We are asking the committee to postpone the decision to the autumn budget preparation and, in the meantime, to ask the administration for the cost per round and for an account of how participants found their way to the course."
    Source: "Lyssna Först föreslår att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen."
    Supported.

19. Draft: "We are not asking for the course to close. Keep it running."
    Source: "Han motsätter sig inte kursen och vill att den ska finnas kvar medan beslutet väntar."
    Difference: "Keep it running" has no time limit, while the source says "medan beslutet väntar". In context the sentence answers "close" and follows the opening's "while that decision waits". "Han motsätter sig inte kursen" also supports a general stance for continuing. Supported.

20. Draft: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
    Source: "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen"
    Difference: the time of the postponement changes. The source postpones the decision to a fixed point, the autumn budget preparation. The draft postpones it until the information arrives. See finding F6.

## Pronouns: person, number and natural gender

- "we" / "We" (sections 3 and 4): first person plural. The referent is the association Lyssna Först, spoken for by its chair. The source gives the association as the party proposing and disclaiming ("Lyssna Först föreslår", "Föreningen gör inget anspråk"). The plural and the association voice are supported, and the pronoun conveys no gender.
- "It" (the evaluation, "It rests on"), "its" (the recommendation, "its reason"), "it" (the course, "could finish it", "Keep it running", "it gave them"), "It" (completion, "It tells the committee"): third person singular and non-personal, with no natural gender. The source's "den" (kursen, utvärderingen) carries grammatical common gender only, and no natural-gender assertion was lost or added.
- "them", "they", "those people" (participants): third person plural, with no gender conveyed. Source "dem", "de": third person plural. Supported.
- "it" in "until it has the cost per round" (the committee): third person singular and non-personal. Supported.
- Milad Aziz: the source uses "Han" (masculine). The draft uses no pronoun for him, so no natural-gender information is conveyed and none is contradicted.

## Findings: source support

F1. Opening sentence: "meets on April 14 to decide whether"
- Missing support: the source says only that the staff report "inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas". It does not say that the decision is taken, or on the agenda, at that meeting.
- Case: the report is presented on April 14 for discussion, and the decision is scheduled for a later spring meeting. The source holds and the draft fails. No supplied statement excludes this case.
- Smallest repair: "Rönnvik's education committee has before it, for its April 14 meeting, a staff recommendation to make Digitalt grundstöd a permanent course with six rounds a year."
- Severity: low. This is a circumstantial claim about the meeting's purpose.

F2. "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
- Changed support: "förkunskaper" is prior knowledge. The meaning changed when the term crossed from Swedish to English.
- Case: the course leaders' written summaries, which the evaluation rests on ("bygger på ... kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar"), record what participants could do at the end of the course. Then the source's statement holds (prior knowledge not measured) and the draft's fails (knowledge is measured in part). The reverse also matters: the draft's broader claim overstates the gap the author criticizes. No supplied statement excludes the case.
- Smallest repair: "The evaluation does not measure participants' prior knowledge."

F3. "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
- Missing support: the material says the evaluation covered "tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025" and that the proposal expands the course "till sex omgångar per år". It never states the current annual number of rounds, so "doubling" has no source.
- Case: the course also ran rounds in spring 2025 or spring 2026, so its current rate is four or five a year and six is not a doubling. This is compatible with the material, which says only what the evaluation covered.
- Smallest repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and expanding it to six rounds a year." A shorter alternative is "That is a thin basis for expanding a course."

F4. "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished."
- Changed scope: the figure is presented as forty-eight people. The evaluation states it counts "fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger". Aziz's authorized wording uses "fyrtioåtta personer", but a figure about an actual population stays a factual claim whoever supplied it. The draft's own section 1 already qualifies the figure, and this sentence drops the qualification where the figure carries the argument. The boundary asks that "Siffror ska tillskrivas utvärderingen där de bär argumentet".
- Case: three participants completed twice, so forty-five distinct people finished. The evaluation's count of forty-eight completions holds and "forty-eight people" fails.
- Smallest repair: "Forty-eight completions in the evaluation say the course could be finished. They do not say it gave participants something they did not already have."

F5. "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
- Changed certainty and scope: the source says "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt", which means the association makes no claim to a measurement of its own. The draft asserts an absence (what is unclaimed is not thereby known not to exist), and by dropping "egen" it extends that absence to any measurement at all.
- Case: the association has seen or holds some informal or third-party indication of effect but does not claim a measurement of its own. The source holds and "has no measurement" fails. "Inga effektstudier ... är belagda" says that none is attested, which does not exclude the case.
- Smallest repair: "Lyssna Först makes no claim to a measurement of its own of the course's effect."

F6. Closing sentence: "postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
- Changed time and condition: the association's proposal is "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa ...". That is a postponement to a fixed point, with the information requested in the meantime. The draft makes the postponement last until the information exists.
- Case 1: the administration delivers the figures in June. Under the draft the committee decides in June, while under the proposal it decides at the autumn budget preparation.
- Case 2: the figures are not ready by autumn. Under the draft the decision keeps waiting, while under the proposal it is taken at the autumn budget preparation.
- The paragraph above states the proposal correctly, so the ending contradicts it.
- Smallest repair: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision to the autumn budget preparation, ask in the meantime for the cost per round and how participants reached the course, and decide then."

## Findings: translation

None. The draft marks no quotation as a quotation. Aziz's permitted standpoint is referred to in his own voice in section 2, and in section 1 as argument. That rendering ("says the course could be finished. It does not say it gave them something they did not already have") keeps the meaning and the terse parallel voice of "säger att kursen gick att gå klart. Det säger inte att den gav dem något de inte redan hade". Its idiom has no concrete obstruction. The meaning change on "förkunskaper" is outside quoted speech and is reported above as source-support finding F2.

## Editorial questions (not defects)

Q1. "It tells the committee that people who started the course could finish it."
- Read existentially ("it was possible to finish"), this matches Aziz's "kursen gick att gå klart" and is defensible. Read universally ("those who started were able to finish"), it conflicts with 62 registrations against 48 completions. The material also records registrations ("anmälde sig"), not starts.
- If the author wants to remove the ambiguity: "It tells the committee that the course could be finished."

## Outside this comparison

The brief requires that "Slutet ska namnge nämndens möjliga beslut". Whether the ending names the committee's possible decisions clearly enough is a brief-compliance matter, not a claim-support matter, so it is not judged here. So are the mixed "fall" and "autumn" and the other locale mechanics.
