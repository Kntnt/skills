# Judgement

## My reading of the focus passage

Focus passage: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."

Material: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper." The Swedish word "förkunskaper" means *prior* knowledge, that is, what participants knew before the course. The draft drops "prior". An ordinary reader takes "does not measure participants' knowledge" as covering knowledge of any kind, including what participants knew at the end of the course. The material says nothing about end-of-course knowledge. It does say the evaluation rests on "kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar" (the course leaders' written summaries), and those summaries could record what participants could do at the end. In that case the material's statement stays true and the draft's is false. This changes what was measured, so the passage needs a repair, for example: "…does not measure participants' prior knowledge." All six reports file this finding, and all six are right to.
