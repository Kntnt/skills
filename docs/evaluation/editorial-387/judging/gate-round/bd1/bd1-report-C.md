## 3. Source-support findings

**F1 (minor): purpose of the April 14 meeting (paragraph 1)**
- Draft: "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 to decide whether Digitalt grundstöd becomes a permanent course with six rounds a year."
- Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år."
- What differs: the source says a staff report prepared for the meeting proposes the change. It does not say the committee will decide on April 14.
- Case: the proposal is presented on April 14 and the decision is scheduled for a later meeting. No supplied statement excludes this. "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning" only places the pending decision before the autumn.
- Smallest repair: "Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 with a staff recommendation to make Digitalt grundstöd a permanent course with six rounds a year."

**F2: what the evaluation does not measure (paragraph 2)**
- Draft: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
- Source: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
- What differs: "förkunskaper" means prior knowledge. The draft widens the absence to all knowledge.
- Case: the course leaders' written summaries, on which the evaluation rests ("bygger på ... kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar"), record what participants could do at the end of the course. The source statement then holds and the draft's fails. Nothing supplied excludes it.
- Smallest repair: "The evaluation does not measure participants' prior knowledge."

**F3: "doubling" (paragraph 5)**
- Draft: "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
- Source: "gäller tre kursomgångar vid vuxenutbildningen i Rönnvik under hösten 2025"; "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år"
- What differs: "doubling" implies the course runs three rounds a year now. The source gives three rounds in the fall term of 2025 and says the proposal expands the course to six a year. It gives no current annual rate.
- Case: the course also ran two rounds in spring 2025, so six a year is not a doubling. The source says only that the course is expanded ("byggs ut"), and no supplied statement excludes this.
- Smallest repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and expanding it to six rounds a year." A shorter option is "for expanding a course".

**F4: "forty-eight people" after the registrations caveat (paragraph 5)**
- Draft: "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished."
- Source: "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger." Aziz's own words ("Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs ...") may be quoted or paraphrased, but his assertion does not make the figure a count of people.
- What differs: the thing counted. The source's 48 are completed courses, not individual people. The draft states this in paragraph 2 and then drops it here, where the figure carries the argument.
- Case: 44 individuals completed, four of them twice. That gives 48 completions but 44 people. This is compatible with "några deltagare gick kursen två gånger".
- Smallest repair: "Forty-eight completions say the course could be finished." Alternatively, set Aziz's sentence as a quotation attributed to him, so that the wording stays his and the paragraph 2 caveat governs the figure.

**F5: Lyssna Först's measurement (paragraph 6)**
- Draft: "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt."
- What differs: (a) scope: "egen" (own) is dropped, so the draft denies holding any measurement, including third-party ones. (b) certainty: "makes no claim to have" becomes "has no", which turns an unclaimed state into a stated absence.
- Cases: (a) the association knows of a study of similar courses by another party, so it holds a measurement that is not its own. (b) the association has an informal member survey that it does not claim as a measurement. In each case the source statement holds and the draft's fails. The boundary "Inga effektstudier ... är belagda" says no effect study is documented in the material. It does not exclude either case.
- Smallest repair: "Lyssna Först does not claim any measurement of its own of the course's effect."

**F6 (minor): time limit on keeping the course (paragraph 7)**
- Draft: "We are not asking for the course to close. Keep it running."
- Source: "vill att den ska finnas kvar medan beslutet väntar"
- What differs: the time limit "medan beslutet väntar" is dropped where the claim is restated. Paragraph 1 carries it, but this unbounded imperative can be read as support for keeping the course indefinitely.
- Case: Aziz wants the course kept while the decision waits and takes no position on it after that. The source says only "motsätter sig inte kursen", which is not a call to keep it permanently.
- Smallest repair: "Keep it running while the decision waits."

## 5. Editorial question (not a defect)

**E1: when the committee should decide (paragraph 7)**
- Draft: "the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
- Source: the proposal is "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning". The thesis is "utan först låta förvaltningen komplettera det".
- The brief's thesis supports a decision that depends on the missing evidence, so this is the commissioning party's standpoint. The concrete proposal, however, names the autumn budget preparation, and the ending drops that date. If the figures arrive in June, the draft's wording implies a June decision, while the proposal places it in the autumn. The author should choose one. A version that holds both: "postpone the permanence decision to the autumn budget preparation and decide once it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course."
