## Source-support findings

**F1: "participants' knowledge" widens "förkunskaper".**
- Missing support: the source says only that the evaluation does not measure *prior* knowledge.
- Case: the course leaders' written summaries, which are a stated basis of the evaluation, could record what participants could do at the end of the course. The source would then hold and the draft would be false. No supplied statement excludes this.
- Repair: "The evaluation does not measure participants' prior knowledge."

**F2: "doubling a course" is unsupported.**
- Missing support: the material gives three rounds in fall 2025 and a proposal for six rounds a year. It gives no current annual number of rounds.
- Case: the course also ran rounds in spring, or three rounds in one term is already a pace of about six a year. Six a year is then not a doubling. Nothing in the material excludes this.
- Repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and expanding it." (Another option: "for expanding a course to six rounds a year.")

**F3: "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect" turns an absence of a claim into an asserted absence.**
- Missing support: the source says "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt". That is a statement about what the association claims, limited to a measurement of its own.
- Case: the association holds some informal measurement it does not put forward. The source then holds and the draft is false. No supplied statement excludes this. (The boundary "Inga effektstudier ... är belagda" concerns what is documented, not what the association has.)
- Repair: "Lyssna Först does not claim any measurement of its own of the course's effect." (Or keep the first person: "We make no claim to any measurement of our own of the course's effect.")

**F4: "Forty-eight people finishing a course" counts people where the evaluation counts completions.**
- Missing support: the evaluation "räknar ... fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger". The draft said so two paragraphs earlier, and now reverts to "people".
- Case: some of the repeat participants completed both times, so the 48 completions belong to fewer than 48 people. The source holds and the draft is false. No supplied statement excludes this.
- Note: the wording comes from Aziz's own permitted quotation ("fyrtioåtta personer"), so this is the brief's own figure-as-worded. The count remains a factual claim whoever supplied it, and the brief allows the quotation to be paraphrased ("refereras").
- Repair: "Forty-eight completions say the course could be finished." Adjust the next sentence to match, for example: "They do not say the course gave participants something they did not already have."

**F5: "meets on April 14 to decide whether" is more than the source says (minor).**
- Missing support: the source says the staff statement was prepared "inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april" and proposes permanence and expansion. It does not say the committee will decide the matter at that meeting.
- Case: the item is raised on April 14 but set for decision at a later meeting before the autumn. The source holds and the draft fails. The association's request to postpone "till höstens budgetberedning" implies a decision before autumn, but not on April 14. No supplied statement excludes this.
- Repair: "A staff recommendation prepared for the April 14 meeting of Rönnvik's education committee proposes making Digitalt grundstöd a permanent course with six rounds a year." The next sentence then reads: "The course should keep running while the decision waits."

## Translation findings (translated quotation)

**T1: unclear referent in "It does not say it gave them something they did not already have."**
- The Swedish separates "Det" (the fact that people finished) from "den" (the course) by grammatical gender. In the English, both are "it".
- The reader can take the second "it" as the finishing itself ("finishing gave them something"), which shifts Aziz's point from the course's value to the act of completing it.
- The supplied context settles the referent: "den" = "kursen".
- Repair: "It does not say the course gave them something they did not already have." (If F4's repair is taken, use its wording.)

Otherwise the rendering keeps Aziz's meaning and his clipped, parallel voice in idiomatic English: "says the course could be finished" renders "säger att kursen gick att gå klart".
