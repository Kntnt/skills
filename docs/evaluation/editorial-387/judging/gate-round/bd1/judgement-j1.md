# Judgement: j-6928913c

## My reading of the focus passage

Draft: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
Material: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."

"Förkunskaper" means prior knowledge: what participants knew before the course. The draft drops "prior", so it says the evaluation measures no knowledge at all, including what participants knew at the end. The material does not say that. It says the evaluation "bygger på närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar", and those written summaries could record end-of-course abilities. Nothing supplied rules that out. So the thing measured has changed, and the draft asserts more than the material carries. The change also blurs the draft's own argument, which turns on the missing baseline: "what those people could do afterward that they could not do before". A finding against this passage is **supported**. The repair is small: "does not measure participants' prior knowledge" or "what participants already knew".

Rulings on the other recurring passages, applied the same way to every report:

- **"meets on April 14 to decide whether"**: false. A tjänsteutlåtande "inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april" that "föreslår att kursen permanentas" is the proposal put to that meeting for decision. The association's request that "nämnden skjuter upp beslutet" presupposes that the decision is otherwise due then. An ordinary reader of the material and of the draft takes both the same way.
- **"doubling a course"**: supported. The material gives "tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025" and "sex omgångar per år", but no current annual rate. "Doubling" asserts a base the material does not give.
- **"Forty-eight people finishing a course"**: disputed. Aziz's permitted wording is "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs", and the draft speaks in his voice. But the evaluation counts "fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer", and the boundary asks "Siffror ska tillskrivas utvärderingen där de bär argumentet". Editors could disagree on whether the sender's own permitted wording carries the figure.
- **"Lyssna Först has no measurement ... and we do not claim one"**: disputed. The disagreement is whether "gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning" is, in an opinion piece, an idiomatic way of saying "we have none". Read that way, the draft is a fair rendering. Read as a hedge limited to "egen", the draft asserts an absence and widens its scope.
- **Closing "postpone ... until it has the cost per round ... and decide then"**: disputed. The brief's thesis is condition-based ("utan först låta förvaltningen komplettera det"), and the previous paragraph gives the autumn date exactly. The proposal itself, however, fixes a time ("till höstens budgetberedning"). The question is whether the ending is read as a restatement of the proposal or as the thesis.
