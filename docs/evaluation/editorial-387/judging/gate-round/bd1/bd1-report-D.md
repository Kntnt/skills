## Findings

All findings are source-support findings. No translation finding stands. The one translated passage from a speaker, C16/C11, keeps the meaning and plain, pointed voice of Aziz's statement in idiomatic English.

### F1. "participants' knowledge" widens "förkunskaper" (source support, crossing languages)

- Draft: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
- Source: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
- What changed: The thing measured. The source excludes only prior knowledge. The draft excludes any measurement of knowledge.
- Case where the source holds and the draft fails: the course leaders' written summaries, which the evaluation "bygger på", record what participants could do at the end of the course. Then the evaluation says something about knowledge, but nothing about prior knowledge. No supplied statement excludes this case.
- Why it matters: The argument depends on the missing baseline, meaning what participants already had. The source supports that exactly.
- Smallest repair: "The evaluation does not measure what participants knew before the course." Alternatively: "...does not measure participants' prior knowledge."

### F2. "doubling" has no support

- Draft: "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
- Source: "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år". The evaluation "gäller tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025".
- What changed: The draft asserts a current annual level of three rounds. The material gives three rounds in one autumn term that was evaluated, and no annual level.
- Case: the course also ran a round in spring 2025, or runs in spring 2026, so the current level is four or five a year and six is not a doubling. It is compatible with "byggs ut", which requires only fewer than six. Nothing in the material states how many rounds run per year outside the evaluated autumn.
- Smallest repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and expanding it to six rounds a year." Alternatively: "...for expanding a course."

### F3. "Forty-eight people finishing" contradicts the count basis the draft has just stated

- Draft: "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished."
- Source: "Utvärderingen räknar anmälningar och fullföljda kurser, inte enskilda personer; några deltagare gick kursen två gånger." The permitted statement from Aziz does read "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs".
- What changed: The subject counted. Forty-eight is a count of completions, and the draft said so in C9. Here it is presented, unquoted and in the author's voice, as 48 people. The figure also bears the argument without attribution to the evaluation, which the brief asks for ("Siffror ska tillskrivas utvärderingen där de bär argumentet").
- Case: some of the participants who took the course twice completed it both times, so the 48 completions belong to fewer than 48 people. The source's "några deltagare gick kursen två gånger" allows this, and nothing excludes it. A figure stays a factual claim even when the speaker supplied it.
- Smallest repair: "Forty-eight completions in the evaluation say the course could be finished." The second sentence can stay as written.

### F4. "has no measurement" turns an unclaimed measurement into a known absence and drops "own"

- Draft: "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt."
- What changed: The certainty and the scope. The source reports that the association makes no claim to a measurement of its own. The draft states as fact that it has none at all, of its own or anyone else's.
- Case: the association has informal member feedback, or holds a third party's figures, and does not claim them as a measurement of effect. The source holds and the draft's first clause fails. Nothing supplied excludes this.
- Smallest repair: "Lyssna Först does not claim any measurement of its own of the course's effect." Alternatively: "We make no claim to a measurement of our own of the course's effect."

### F5. The closing request changes the postponement from a date to a condition

- Draft: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
- Source: "föreslår att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen."
- What changed: The time and scope of the association's proposal. The source ties the decision to the autumn budget preparation, and the information requests run in the meantime. The draft ties the decision to receiving the information.
- Cases in both directions:
  - The administration delivers the figures in June. Under the draft the committee should decide in June. Under the source it decides at the autumn budget preparation.
  - The figures have not arrived by the autumn budget preparation. Under the source the decision comes then. Under the draft it waits further.
  - No supplied statement excludes either case.
- This sentence closes the piece and names the committee's decision, so it restates the association's actual proposal. It is not rhetoric that can stay loose.
- Smallest repair: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision to the autumn budget preparation, ask for the cost per round and how participants reached the course in the meantime, and decide then."

- **C8, "Sixty-two people signed up".** This repeats the source's own wording ("Sextiotvå personer anmälde sig"), and its qualification follows in the next sentence, as in the source. It is not a defect relative to the material. Once F3 is repaired, "Sixty-two registrations and forty-eight completions" would make the two passages consistent. That is an editorial option, not a required repair.
- **C11, "people who started the course could finish it".** Read in context, this says the course was finishable, which matches the author's own statement. The material has no count of starters, only registrations, so the passage should not be read as a claim about all starters.
