# Judgement: Hamngatan opinion draft, reports A–F

## My own reading of the focus passage

Focus passage: "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health."

Material: "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa." The material also lists what the questionnaire did ask: "vad de gjorde på gården, hur de tog sig dit och om de ville att gården skulle vara öppen fler kvällar."

The draft narrows "health" to "mental health". A questionnaire that asks nothing about health also asks nothing about mental health, so the source entails the draft. In context the sentence comes straight after the recommendation's quoted mental-health motive. An ordinary reader takes it as "the survey gives no basis for that claim", and not as a hint that the survey asked about some other kind of health. The complete list of questions rules that hint out anyway. Nothing in the passage is unsupported or changed, so a finding against it would be false. None of the six reports files one. All six clear the passage on the entailment argument.

Two other passages matter. (1) "decides on March 3": the material has a recommendation prepared "inför nämndens möte den 3 mars", so the matter is before that meeting, but the material never says a decision is taken there. (2) "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to": the material has only "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta". The draft adds a statement about what the association actually knows.

| "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent." (S1) | The material gives a meeting and a recommendation prepared for it, not a decision on that date. | disputed | The material has "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas". The disagreement is whether a recommendation prepared for a meeting carries the ordinary scheduling sense of "decides on March 3". Tabling is possible for any scheduled decision, and the brief itself speaks of "nämndens möjliga beslut". A careful editor may soften the verb. Another may call the test hyper-literal. |
| "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to." (S2) | A disclaimer ("gör inget anspråk på att veta") becomes a statement of fact about the association's lack of knowledge. | supported | The material records only "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa". "Does not know" asserts an epistemic state the material does not give. The repair "does not claim to know" is cheap and exact. |

Focus passage: discussed and cleared. Decisive sentence: "Test, source holds and draft fails: impossible. Supported, no finding."
