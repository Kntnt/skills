## Findings

### Source-support findings

**F1: The decision date is stated as fact.**
- Draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- Missing support: the source says only "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas". The meeting and the recommendation are supported. That the committee decides on that date is not.
- Smallest repair: "On March 3, Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee takes up a proposal to make the open youth center on Hamngatan permanent."

**F2: A declined claim is turned into a statement that the association does not know.**
- Draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- Changed support: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa" says only that the association makes no claim to know.
- Smallest repair: "Unga Röster does not claim to know how the center affects young people's health."

### Translation findings

None.

- T1 (checked, no finding): "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn" renders ”motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn” with the same meaning. "mental ill health" is a public-health term that US readers understand. It also keeps the official register of the recommendation it quotes. Nothing concrete blocks an idiomatic reading, and a US-leaning synonym would be a matter of preference.
- T2 (checked, no finding): "The center is needed. But a justification that cannot be supported holds only until somebody reads the file." renders Sandberg's ”Gården behövs. Men en motivering som inte går att belägga håller bara tills någon läser underlaget.” with the same meaning. It keeps her short, blunt two-beat voice and reads as idiomatic English.
