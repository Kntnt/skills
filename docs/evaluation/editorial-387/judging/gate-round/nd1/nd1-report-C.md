# Claim comparison report

Draft language: en_US. Source language: Swedish. The fixture holds the brief, the supplied material ("Underlag") and the boundaries. This report compares every factual and attributed claim in the complete draft with that material.

## Claim accounting

Each item gives the draft passage (D), the source passage (S) and what differs.

1. **Title**: D: "Keep the Hamngatan youth center, and give a reason that holds"
   S: "Tes: kultur- och fritidsnämnden bör permanenta den öppna fritidsgården på Hamngatan, men motivera beslutet med det som faktiskt är undersökt"
   Difference: none of substance. This is the brief's thesis, given as advocacy. Using "youth center" for "fritidsgård" names the same thing here. Supported.

2. **Byline**: D: "By Elin Sandberg, chair of Unga Röster"
   S: "Avsändare är Elin Sandberg, ordförande för föreningen Unga Röster."
   Difference: none. "chair" is gender-neutral, and the draft uses no gendered pronoun for Sandberg anywhere. Supported.

3. D: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
   S: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
   Difference: modality and certainty of the event. The source says only that a recommendation was prepared ahead of the committee's March 3 meeting. The draft states as fact that the committee decides the matter on that date. **Finding 1** (see below).

4. D: "It should. What should not survive the meeting is the reason the staff recommendation gives for it."
   S: "Unga Röster föreslår att nämnden permanentar gården och skriver om motiveringen" / "Motivet i utlåtandet är att ..."
   Difference: none. This is the association's own advocacy. "the reason" (singular, definite) matches "Motivet" (singular, definite). "staff recommendation" names the tjänsteutlåtande, and the source confirms that the utlåtande "föreslår" (proposes). "It" means the committee (singular). Supported.

## Findings: source support

### Finding 1: The decision date is asserted as fact

- Draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- What changed: the source establishes a meeting on March 3 and a recommendation prepared for it. It does not establish that the committee decides the question at that meeting.
- Concrete case: at the March 3 meeting the committee tables the item, or sends the recommendation back to the administration for a new justification, which is exactly what Unga Röster asks for. The source's statement holds and the draft's fails. No supplied statement excludes this case.
- Smallest supported repair: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee meets on March 3 to consider whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent." The following "It should." then stays as written.

### Finding 2: No claim to know becomes not knowing

- Draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa."
- What changed: the source records that the association makes no claim to knowledge. The draft asserts as fact that the association lacks the knowledge. This turns what is unclaimed into what is known to be absent.
- Concrete case: the association's members believe, from their own experience of the center, that it helps young people's well-being, but they make no claim about it because the documents do not show it. The source's statement holds, and the draft's "does not know" fails, or at least goes beyond what the association has said. No supplied statement excludes this case.
- Smallest supported repair: "Unga Röster does not claim to know how the center affects young people's health."

## Findings: translation

None.

- Translated quotation: "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn" renders "motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn". The thing named, the scope (young people in Nyhamn) and the modality (unhedged "motverkar", rendered as unhedged "counteracts") are the same. "Mental ill health" is less usual in American usage than in British usage. However, it keeps the institutional register of "psykisk ohälsa", and a US reader meets no concrete obstruction to understanding it, so I record no finding. The quotation is presented in English without a note that it was translated. That is an editorial choice outside this comparison.
- "the activity", outside the quotation marks, renders "verksamheten". In context it reads as the center's operation, so it names the same thing, and no meaning changes.
- Sandberg's statement (item 21) is referenced, not quoted, and keeps her meaning and her short, blunt voice.

## Outside scope, noted only

"The questionnaire" (item 8) appears before the follow-up and its questionnaire are introduced in the next section. That is an editorial ordering matter, not a support defect.
