## 3. Findings

### Source-support findings

**S1. The draft states that the committee decides on March 3 (opening sentence).**
- Draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- What differs: the source establishes only that a recommendation was prepared for the March 3 meeting. It does not establish that the committee will take its decision at that meeting.
- A case compatible with the material where the draft fails: at the March 3 meeting the committee tables the item or sends it back for further preparation, which is common in a municipal committee. The decision then comes at a later meeting. The source holds and the draft is false. No supplied statement rules this out.
- Smallest supported repair: "When Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee meets on March 3, it has before it a staff recommendation to make the open youth center on Hamngatan permanent." Everything after this sentence can stay as it is. The closing call ("should make … permanent on March 3") is advocacy and needs no change.

**S2. The association's non-claim becomes asserted ignorance.**
- Draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa."
- What differs: the source reports what the association declines to claim. The draft states what the association does not know.
- A case compatible with the material where the draft fails: the association's members hold views or observations about the center's effect on health, but the association chooses not to claim them. The source holds, and "does not know" is false. No supplied statement rules this out. The Claims contract says what is unclaimed is not thereby known to be absent.
- Smallest supported repair: "Unga Röster makes no claim to know how the center affects young people's health." Alternatively, keep the sentence's rhythm with "Unga Röster does not claim to know how the center affects young people's health."

Nothing in the draft relies on that reading, so this is reported as a question: the author may want "drop-in youth center" or to keep the brief's own label.
