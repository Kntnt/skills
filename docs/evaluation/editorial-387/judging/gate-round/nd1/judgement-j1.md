# Judgement: Hamngatan opinion draft (en_US) against the Swedish brief and material

## My reading of the focus passage

Focus passage: "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health."

Material: "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa." It also lists what the questionnaire did ask: "vad de gjorde på gården, hur de tog sig dit och om de ville att gården skulle vara öppen fler kvällar."

The draft narrows "hälsa" (health) to "mental health". The source entails this: a questionnaire that asks nothing about health asks nothing about mental health. In context the sentence comes straight after the recommendation's quoted claim about "mental ill health". An ordinary reader takes it as saying that the recommendation's claim has no survey behind it. It does not suggest that other health questions were asked. Also, the draft later lists the three questions, and none of them is about health. No reading in context makes the draft false while the material holds. A finding against this passage would therefore be **false**. None of the six reports files one. All six discuss the passage and clear it.

My classes for the recurring findings:

- **"decides on March 3"**: disputed. The material says "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas". A staff recommendation prepared for a named meeting puts the matter on that meeting's agenda for decision. The question is whether the scheduled-future present "decides" reads as ordinary news phrasing for "comes up for decision", or as a firm statement that a decision will be made that day, which a tabling would falsify. The draft's own "What should not survive the meeting" supports the agenda reading. The repair is cheap, though.
- **"does not know"**: supported. The material says only "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa". "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health" goes further and asserts the association's actual state of knowledge, which the material never gives. "Inga hälsoeffekter … är belagda" concerns documented effects, not what the association knows. The repair "does not claim to know" is exact.
- **Translation idiom of "mental ill health"** (report D only): false. D itself says "Meaning: preserved". The wording belongs to a quoted official document, and the difference cannot make one statement true and the other false.
