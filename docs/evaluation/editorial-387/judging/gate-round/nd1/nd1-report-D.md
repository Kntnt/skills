### Boundary checks
- Health effects, comparison groups, complaints and cost figures: the draft asserts none of them.
- The draft criticises the justification without saying the center lacks value. It says the opposite ("The center is needed").
- The draft does not say that Sandberg objects to the municipality taking an interest in young people's health, so it keeps "Hon motsätter sig inte att kommunen intresserar sig för ungas hälsa."
- The draft has no commercial call to action.

### Pronoun accounting (person, number, natural gender)
- "It" (row 3, "It should"): refers to the committee's action or the center. Third person singular, non-human, no natural gender. Supported.
- "it" (rows 5, 18: the center; row 13: the follow-up; row 21: the justification, source "den" = motiveringen). Third person singular, non-human. Supported.
- "they" (row 16): the 71 young people, source "de". Third person plural, no gender conveyed. Supported.
- "we" (rows 20, 21): Unga Röster speaking through its chair. First person plural. This fits the byline and the brief ("Avsändare är Elin Sandberg, ordförande för föreningen Unga Röster"), so no finding.
- No pronoun refers to Elin Sandberg, and the draft makes no natural-gender assertion about anybody. The source's "Hon" is not carried over, so no gender information is added or lost.

## Findings

### S1 (source support): the draft turns a recommendation prepared for the meeting into a decision on that date
- Draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- What differs: the event and its time. The source puts a staff recommendation before the March 3 meeting. The draft says the decision is taken on March 3.
- A case where the source holds and the draft fails: the committee tables the matter at its March 3 meeting ("bordläggning") and decides at a later meeting. No supplied statement rules this out. The brief's "nämndens möjliga beslut" supports that the committee decides, but not when.
- Smallest supported repair: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee meets on March 3 with a staff recommendation to make the open youth center on Hamngatan permanent. It should follow it." The closing advocacy in row 24 can stay as written.

### S2 (source support): making no claim to know becomes an assertion of not knowing
- Draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa."
- What differs: modality and what the association asserts about its own knowledge. The source records a refusal to make a claim. The draft also asserts that the association lacks the knowledge.
- A case where the source holds and the draft fails: the association's members have their own observations of how the center affects young visitors but choose not to present them as a claim. No supplied statement rules this out. "Inga hälsoeffekter ... är belagda" concerns documented evidence, not what the association knows.
- Smallest supported repair: "Unga Röster does not claim to know how the center affects young people's health."

### T1 (translation of a quotation): the translated wording is not idiomatic American English
- Draft quotation: "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn"
- Source: ”motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn”
- Meaning: preserved. "mental ill health" keeps the breadth of "psykisk ohälsa", and "counteracts" keeps "motverkar".
- Obstruction: the draft is declared en_US. "Mental ill health" is a chiefly British public-health term, and in American usage "ill health" pairs with physical health. Paired with "counteracts", which in American English normally takes an effect or force as its object, the quoted line reads as translated British officialese rather than as an American rendering of the committee staff's bureaucratic voice.
- Smallest repair that keeps the meaning and the official register: "counters mental health problems among young people in Nyhamn".
- Severity: low. The meaning reaches the reader. The finding concerns only idiomatic target-language speech.
