## Findings

### Source-support findings

**S1 — Row 3: "decides on March 3"**

- Draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- What differs: the source establishes a meeting on March 3 and a proposal prepared for it. The draft states as fact that the committee will make the decision on that date.
- Case: at the March 3 meeting the committee tables the item or refers it back to the administration. The proposal was still prepared for that meeting, so the source holds, but the committee does not decide on March 3, so the draft fails. No supplied statement rules this case out.
- Smallest repair: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee takes up at its March 3 meeting whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent." Alternative: "…has a proposal before it on March 3 to make the open youth center on Hamngatan permanent." Rows 5 and 24 need no change: "survive the meeting" and "should … on March 3" stay correct.

**S2 — Row 20: "does not know"**

- Draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa."
- What differs: the source reports the association's disclaimer, meaning what it does not claim. The draft adds a statement about the association's actual state of knowledge. Something unclaimed has become something stated as unknown.
- Case: the association holds views on the center's effect on health, for example from members' own observations or from its own conversations with visitors, and chooses not to claim them. In that case the source holds and the draft's "does not know" is not established. "Inga hälsoeffekter … är belagda" rules out documented effects in the material. It does not rule out knowledge or belief the association keeps outside the material.
- Smallest repair: "Unga Röster makes no claim to know how the center affects young people's health." Another option keeps the rhythm: "Unga Röster does not claim to know how the center affects young people's health."

### Translation findings

**T1 — Translated quotation, row 7: "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn"**

- Source: ”motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn”.
- Meaning: the same meaning. "counteracts" = "motverkar", "mental ill health" = "psykisk ohälsa", "young people" = "unga".
- Voice: the source phrase is administrative public-health wording. "mental ill health" keeps that register. It sounds more British than American, but a US reader still understands it as the term of art for poor mental health. It is quoted from an official document, where stiff wording belongs to the speaker. No concrete obstruction to the idiomatic reading. **No finding.**

No other passage is a translated quotation. Row 22 renders Elin Sandberg's own position in her own authorial voice without quotation marks. Its meaning is the same (see row 22), and no translation finding arises.
