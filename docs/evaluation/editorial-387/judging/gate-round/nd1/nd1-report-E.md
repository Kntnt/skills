### Implications checked

- The draft never says that the center has no health effect, or that it has one. This matches the brief: "Inga hälsoeffekter ... är belagda". It also matches the association's position that it claims no knowledge of the effect (subject to F1).
- The draft brings in no comparison groups, complaints or cost figures.
- The draft criticises the justification without saying that the activity lacks value ("It should." / "The center is needed.").
- Nothing in the draft says that Elin Sandberg objects to the municipality's interest in young people's health, so it does not contradict "Hon motsätter sig inte att kommunen intresserar sig för ungas hälsa."

### Pronoun accounting

- "they" (#13) is third person plural and refers to the 71 respondents. The source uses "de", which is also plural and conveys no natural gender. Person and number match, and the draft asserts no gender.
- "we" (#15, #16) is first person plural and refers to Unga Röster with the author included. The brief supports this: the sender is the association's chair.
- "it" (#4, #13, #14) refers to the center or the follow-up. It is neuter and asserts nothing about natural gender. Swedish "gården" has grammatical gender only.
- "its own documents" (#18) refers to the committee, a collective noun. It asserts nothing about natural gender.
- The draft never refers to Elin Sandberg with a pronoun, so the brief's "Hon" is neither carried over nor contradicted.

## Findings (source support)

### F1: "does not know" asserts what the source leaves unclaimed

- Draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa."
- What differs: the source reports a disclaimer, namely that the association makes no claim to know. The draft turns this into a factual statement about the association's state of knowledge. Knowledge that is not claimed is not thereby shown to be absent.
- Case where the source holds and the draft fails: the association has members' first-hand impressions of how the center affects them, or some other basis for a view. It still chooses to make no claim to know. The disclaimer is true and "does not know" is false. No supplied statement excludes this case.
- Smallest supported repair: "Unga Röster makes no claim to know how the center affects young people's health." In the first person: "We do not claim to know how the center affects young people's health."

### F2: "decides on March 3" states a scheduled decision the source does not give

- Draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- Source: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- What differs: modality and time. The source establishes a meeting on 3 March and a recommendation prepared for it. It does not establish that the committee will reach a decision on this matter at that meeting.
- Case where the source holds and the draft fails: at the 3 March meeting the committee sends the matter back for further preparation or postpones it, so no decision is made that day. The recommendation was still prepared ahead of that meeting. No supplied statement excludes this case.
- Smallest supported repair: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee meets on March 3 to consider whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent." The closing call to act on March 3 (#19) is advocacy and can stay as it is.

## Editorial question (not reported as a defect)

- Q1: "That is not a small drafting slip." The ordinary reading is that the author refuses to treat the problem as minor. A narrower reading takes the phrase to say that the health justification was an inadvertent slip, which would be a claim about how it came to be written, and nothing supplied supports that. The negation makes the rhetorical reading the natural one, so this is left to the editor. If the editor wants to rule out the narrower reading, one option is "That is not a minor point of wording."

## Translation check (translated quotations and reported speech)

- T1: The quotation "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn" renders ”motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn”. The meaning is preserved. "mental ill health" keeps the breadth of "psykisk ohälsa" better than "mental illness" would. The term is more common in British usage, but US readers understand it without trouble, and its official tone matches the official register of the source. Nothing concrete obstructs the reading, so there is no translation finding. "counteracts" is a faithful rendering of "motverkar" in the same official register.
- T2: The draft renders Elin Sandberg's statement in her own voice, without quotation marks: "The center is needed. But a justification that cannot be supported holds only until somebody reads the file." The two short, blunt sentences keep the meaning and the terse voice of the original. "holds only until" is idiomatic enough for a justification that "holds", and "the file" is an acceptable rendering of "underlaget" in context. There is no translation finding.
