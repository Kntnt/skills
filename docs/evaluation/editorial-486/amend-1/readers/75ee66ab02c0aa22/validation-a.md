# 1. The lead

The lead is the paragraph after “Text: Hedda Lund”, rather than the bold paragraph above the byline:

> De 14 lektionspassen ingår i de 120 som fastighetskontoret räknade. Andelen säger därför något om de räknade passen, inte om varje timme i Björkskolans sex klassrum. Genomgången nedan visar vad värdena räcker till och var gränsen går.

It asserts that the 14 sessions are part of the 120 counted sessions and explains that their proportion describes those sessions, rather than all classroom hours. It promises an account of what the measurements establish and where their explanatory limits lie.

# 2. Support

The lead makes one explicit inference, signalled by “därför”.

- **Premise:** “De 14 lektionspassen ingår i de 120 som fastighetskontoret räknade.” The body supplies the counting rule: “räknar 14 lektionspass av 120 med minst en mätning under den gränsen.”
- **Conclusion:** “Andelen säger därför något om de räknade passen, inte om varje timme i Björkskolans sex klassrum.” A proportion of sessions containing at least one reading below the threshold describes those sessions. It does not establish the temperature during every hour, or the proportion of time below the threshold.
- **Missing information:** No absent premise is needed for that limited conclusion. Extending it to all hours would require information about measurement coverage and time spent below the threshold. The body explicitly says: “Hur länge temperaturen låg under den anger rapporten inte.”

The reference to six classrooms is a factual assertion, not a further inference. The conclusion about sessions versus hours does not depend on the number of classrooms.

# 3. Follow-through

The commitment is:

> Genomgången nedan visar vad värdena räcker till och var gränsen går.

The body fulfils both parts:

- **What the values establish:** “räknar 14 lektionspass av 120 med minst en mätning under den gränsen.” This establishes the occurrence of a below-threshold reading in the counted sessions. Rask also states: “Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare.”
- **Limits of the values:** “Hur länge temperaturen låg under den anger rapporten inte.” The threshold's scope is explained by “Gränsen är kontorets egen, vald för det här försöket.” The limits concerning classroom experience are explicit: “Givarna mätte varken luftdrag eller elevernas upplevelse.” The limit concerning causes is also explicit: “Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då.”

The body additionally identifies a next step: “Rask rekommenderar att temperaturserierna kopplas till rummens användningstider innan styrningen ändras.”

The first two lead sentences already assert and explain the denominator limitation; they do not promise a separate account of every classroom hour. The third sentence promises an account of the measurements' scope and limits, which the quoted passages provide. It does not promise to explain the cause of the cold or supply the missing duration data. No promised account remains absent.

# 4. Class

**supported and fulfilled**. The session-based inference follows from the stated counting rule, and the body provides the promised account of what the measurements establish and what they cannot establish. Neither an unsupported inference nor an unfulfilled commitment applies to this lead.
