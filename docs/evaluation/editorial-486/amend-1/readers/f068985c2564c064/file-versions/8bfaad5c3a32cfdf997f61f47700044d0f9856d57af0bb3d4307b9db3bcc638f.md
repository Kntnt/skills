# 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding edit is “De 14 lektionspassen ingår i de 120 som fastighetskontoret räknade” becoming “De 14 lektionspassen med minst en temperaturmätning under fastighetskontorets arbetsgräns ingår i de 120 som fastighetskontoret räknade”. The stated purpose is “så att de 14 passen går att förstå utan ingressen”.

The standfirst already identifies those periods, while the original lead correctly limits the proportion to the counted periods. Repeating the identification to satisfy an unsupported preference for an independently understandable lead is a rewrite of a clean passage, not a repair of a concrete visible defect. It therefore violates R1's clean-text rule, even though it preserves the claim and the frozen expectation's explicit safeguards.

There are no legitimate removals, rejected claim losses or irreparable findings. The verdict rests solely on the unnecessary non-mechanical addition; it does not penalize proofreading work or attempt unavailable-source verification.
