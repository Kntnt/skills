# Judgement b

## 1. Differences.

- Before: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` → After: `# Björkskolans mätvärden bör kopplas till användningstider`. Class: **change to what a claim says — meaning**. A statement about the measurement trial's explanatory limits becomes a recommendation to link readings to room-use times. The recommendation already appears in the body, but replacing the original headline is an editorial preference, not the repair of a concrete visible defect.

This is the only difference. The headline changes from 48 to 56 characters, while retaining its H1 formatting. Everything after the headline is identical, including Markdown emphasis, headings, paragraph breaks, punctuation and the final newline. Neither file has frontmatter. There are no mechanical corrections.

There are no legitimate removals to record. The rejected loss is the original headline's result and limitation; that claim survives elsewhere, but its removal from the headline still changes a clean text. No irreparable finding is reported, and no claim requires unavailable-source verification for this judgement.

The account also accurately identifies the removed headline claim and its survival elsewhere:

Its statement, “Övrig text är oförändrad”, is accurate. No separate formatting, frontmatter or mechanical change is omitted.

The account's justification is not a valid defect finding under this control:

The headline and standfirst do share the explanatory limitation. However, the standfirst adds the numerical result, duration and purpose of further investigation. This overlap does not establish a visible defect in the conforming anatomy. Thus the account reports the actual edit accurately but wrongly presents an editorial preference as a necessary repair. “Rubriken har reparerats för att komplettera ingressen” repeats that unsupported diagnosis.

## 4. R1.

**Fail.** The deciding passage is:

> Before: Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför
>
> After: Björkskolans mätvärden bör kopplas till användningstider

R1 prohibits rewriting clean texts to satisfy taste. The only asserted defect is that the headline repeats the standfirst's conclusion. The frozen expectation establishes conforming anatomy, and the original standfirst independently adds the quantified result and context. Their shared central limitation is not a concrete visible defect. The edit therefore replaces a working headline claim with a different claim without a legitimate finding to justify it.

The new recommendation is supported by the unchanged body, and the account candidly reports both the substitution and loss. Those facts do not make the unnecessary rewrite permissible. All other claims, quotations, attribution, funding uncertainty and the next step are preserved. No mechanical correction contributes to this failure.
