# 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding change is:

> Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför

replaced with:

> Björkskolans mätvärden bör kopplas till användningstider

The frozen expectation identifies the original as conforming. Its headline expresses a supported limitation also carried by the standfirst and quoted summary. The Skill replaces that claim with a recommendation because it prefers a headline that complements the standfirst. This changes the headline's meaning without repairing a concrete visible defect, contrary to R1's rule that clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste.

There are no legitimate defect-driven removals or mechanical changes. The rejected loss is the headline's result-and-limitation claim; its survival in the unchanged body does not justify removing it from a working headline. No irreparable finding needs to remain. The rest of the text preserves its voice, arguments, quotations and claims, and the reply accurately discloses the change, but those successes do not cure the unjustified rewrite.
