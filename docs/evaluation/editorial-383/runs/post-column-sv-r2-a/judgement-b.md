## 4. R1

**Fail.**

R1 allows the Skill to address concrete visible defects and forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines. The input carried at most two visible defects: the em dash (difference 5) and the ambiguous pro-form (difference 7). Both were fixed, correctly and reportedly. Everything else was added or replaced to satisfy a structural guideline, by the Skill's own statement of motive:

> *"en ingress lagts till och två underrubriker satts in, vilket ger texten de avsnitt och den avslutning som artikelanatomin kräver"*

That is the passage that decides it: the Skill names a template requirement, not a defect in the text, as the reason for the largest changes it made. And the deciding artefact is the sentence it wrote to meet that requirement:

> *"En full kalender är inget kvitto på att något blivit bestämt."*

Nothing in `work/input.md` asserts this in the text's own voice; the input's nearest sentence is bounded by *"Det är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra."* A reviewing Skill that is given one text, sees no source material, and finds *"Inga fynd återstår"* has manufactured a claim to satisfy an anatomy rule. The retitling and the two headings compound it — the working voice of the column is first person throughout, and the deck breaks it into third — while the assurance *"Inget påstående har tagits bort, och inget står kvar med ändrad omfattning…"* is scoped so as not to cover what was added.
