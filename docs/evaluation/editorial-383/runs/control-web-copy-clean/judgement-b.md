**Pass.**

The control is a clean text, and the criterion's decisive requirement here is
that a clean text may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines.
The passage that decides it is the whole returned text being identical to
`work/input.md`, declared by the reply's first sentence: `Ingen ändring
behövdes.` Voice, arguments, claims and structure are preserved entire —
including the two clauses most exposed to a taste-driven edit, `En
intresseanmälan ... är inte en beställning` and `En tid bokas alltså inte när
formuläret skickas in.`, both of which a sales-template instinct would have
softened or replaced with a CTA. There are no before/after claim changes to
compare, no legitimate removals, no rejected losses and no irreparable
findings, and the reply attempts no verification against material the Skill
never saw.

The one shortfall is presentational and outside R1: the reply states the text is
unchanged instead of returning it, so a reader gets the account without the
copy. It does not affect detection or preservation and I do not count it against
the criterion.
