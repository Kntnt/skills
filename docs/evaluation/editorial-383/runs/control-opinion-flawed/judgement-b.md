The deciding passage is the pair of before/after cost sentences. Input: `Handlingarna saknar tidsmätning. Därför kostar det ingenting att behålla telefonbokningen.` Returned: `Handlingarna saknar tidsmätning, och försökets kostnad är inte beräknad.` The broken inference is gone; both qualified facts that bound the claim survive whole; nothing was imported from outside the text to settle it. Against R1's reporting duty: legitimate removals are listed with their reasons, rejected losses are addressed ("Inget förslag från rundan avvisades"), and one finding is left standing and explained. Two blemishes, neither fatal. The run's own standfirst introduced a defect the input did not have, which the reply flags itself, in the text's own terms, rather than letting it pass as delivered work — detection and preservation are scored apart from the final text's quality, and this is a quality cost honestly booked. The unrepaired half of the ending finding — the exhortation still lacking a section of its own — is the one place where the account overstates: "texten uppfyller anatomins krav utan avvikelser" is not borne out by the delivered structure. That is one clause of the anatomy expectation unmet and one sentence of the account unreliable; it does not touch a claim, a quotation or a fact, so it does not overturn R1.
