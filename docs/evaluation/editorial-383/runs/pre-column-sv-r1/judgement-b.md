## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the third heading standing over its own section:

> ## Ett försök är värt en ruta till
>
> Där börjar tvivlet. … Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör våra möten bättre. Kanske blir frågan bara en rad till att fylla i.
>
> Jag vill prova den i alla fall, och behålla båda hållningarna så länge: hoppet om frågan och tvivlet på ännu en ruta.

R1 asks whether the Skill addressed concrete visible defects while preserving voice, arguments and claims outside its findings, and forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines. Of the five findings acted on, exactly one (D7) repairs anything visible in the language of the input; D8 is a legitimate locale mechanic. The other four are conformance work, and the reply says so in its own terms: it re-measured with `article_anatomy.py`, "som nu avslutar med status 0 och rapporterar `conforms: true`", and justifies the additions by word counts ("en ingress på 54 ord", "ett stycke på 38 ord"). A language-clean column was given 54 words of new ingress, three new headings and a new closing imperative to make a script report conformance.

That rewrite did not preserve the claims outside the findings. Six of the nine differences change what the text says (D1, D2, D3, D4, D6, D9): the byline now carries a third-person paraphrase of the author (D2), a promise to the reader (D3), an enlarged account of what the template lacks (D1), an instruction about the reader's own template (D9), and two headings that assert what the body expressly refuses — the generality disclaimed by "jag påstår inget om möten i allmänhet" (D4) and the verdict withheld by "Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör våra möten bättre" (D6). The reply's own diagnosis of D6 is exact — "den här gör texten säkrare än den är" and "Den som skummar de tre underrubrikerna får en prydlig argumentation utan den ovisshet som är kolumnens bärande drag" — and the column's load-bearing uncertainty is precisely what R1 protects as working voice and argument. Reporting a self-inflicted hardening accurately is the right account of a wrong outcome; the defect is in the returned text, where a reader meets it, and the budget rule that left it there does not repair it.

The candour counts in the run's favour and the four preserved disclaimers come back verbatim, so this is a narrow fail rather than a broad one. But on the passage above the returned text asserts more than the input did, in the most conspicuous position on the page, because of a change the Skill itself made.
