## 1. Differences

Five differences. The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`; frontmatter and every body paragraph from the lead to the penultimate paragraph are byte-identical to the input.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Keep the telephone until Lervik knows why it is used`. After: `# Keep the telephone until Lervik has the figures`. The demand therefore changes in meaning, and widens in scope from one named question to data in general.

A municipal pilot counted 96 web bookings and 24 by telephone, and measured nothing about age, ability or digital habit. Öppna beslut wants six months with both routes open first.` Class: **change to what a claim says — scope** (plus added material). Two scope shifts:

   Nothing in the standfirst is unsupported by the input, so this is not fabrication; it is the input's material re-asserted at a wider scope.

3. **Byline form.** Before: `*Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*`. After: `*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*`. Class: **change of taste** (a house byline form; not a mechanical error in the input, and not a claim change — same attribution, same actor).

4. **Byline position.** Before: immediately after the headline. After: after the inserted standfirst. Class: **consequent on difference 2** — the insertion took the slot. Not a claim change.

Not a claim change: the section already ends `asking what that sum is, before the channel goes, is the smaller risk`, so the heading asserts only what the section asserts. No change to what the claim says — same actor, same modality.

Counting 4 and 6 as consequent, there are four independent differences; six lines in all. Two of them (1 and 2) are changes to what a claim says.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the headline. Input: `# Keep the telephone until Lervik knows why it is used`. Returned: `# Keep the telephone until Lervik has the figures`. There is no visible defect in the input headline — no misspelling, no ungrammaticality, no contradiction with the body; it is the body's thesis compressed, and the body says so in terms (`It is a reason to find out why the telephone is still being used`). The reply states the motive for changing it: "This followed from the repair of the headline's word-count norm". That is precisely a rewrite of clean text to satisfy a numerical guideline, which R1 forbids, and here the rewrite did not stop at length — it altered the central claim of an opinion piece, as the Skill's own finding 3 concedes: "The shortened headline no longer names the condition the piece argues for." A claim outside any genuine finding was not preserved.

The inserted standfirst compounds it rather than deciding it: it is added material, not a correction of a visible defect, and it re-asserts the pilot's counts stripped of the two-venue, eight-week bound that the piece exists to insist on — again by the Skill's own admission ("which is the reading the whole piece exists to refuse"). Two of the three findings the Skill carries forward are damage it caused, not damage it found.

What the Skill does well counts in its favour and does not rescue the verdict: the body is preserved exactly, every limiting sentence stands untouched, no claim is removed, no unavailable source is invoked, and the reply reports its own irreparable findings openly and names the trade-off a human must settle. Its one reporting error is the *Claims* line "Nothing else shifted in scope, certainty, attribution, chronology or causality", which its own finding 2 refutes.
