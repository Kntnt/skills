## 4. R1

**Pass.**

The passage that decides it is the second section's body, returned as:

> Enligt kundens försöksanteckning registrerades 31 ärenden under åtta veckor, med akuta
> ärenden undantagna. Tiden till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar jämfört med tre tidigare.
> Anteckningen säger att arbetsbelastningen var olika och att skillnaden inte kan tillskrivas
> programvaran.

The contradiction was cut at its defective end — "Vår programvara orsakade därför hela
förbättringen." is gone — while the sentence it contradicted, the reservation, the exclusion and
the comparison all stand word for word, and both quotations survive with their words untouched.
Every removal is of a claim the text itself could not support: two first-person supplier boasts,
one rescue narrative, one causal overclaim, one proof-claiming heading. Nothing outside the
findings was dropped. The three absences the text cannot fill — byline, call to action, genre
background — are reported and left unfilled, with no invented author, offer or link. No
verification against an unavailable source is claimed. The run's own new text introduced three
faults (a restating lead sentence, a weaker standfirst paraphrase of the reservation, a heading
that pre-echoes the quote), and the reply reports all three against itself, quoting them; under
R1 that is detection and reporting intact, and the corpus scores it apart from the final text's
quality.

The two marks against the account fall short of a failure. The first is the under-reporting of
the subheading defects: both were repaired, but the reply never names why the old ones were
wrong, and never lists "Resultatet bevisar allt" among the claims removed. The second is
difference 3, where the new standfirst recasts the eight weeks as a period of note-keeping and
calls the software "ny", both unreported and both sitting under the reply's blanket assurance
that no other surviving claim changed scope. Neither loses a claim, a quotation, an argument or
a measure from the input; they are gaps in the account of a run whose detection, preservation and
reporting are otherwise complete.
