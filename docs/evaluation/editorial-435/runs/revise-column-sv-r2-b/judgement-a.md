The returned text is wrapped in a ```` ```markdown ```` fence. That fence is part of how the reply is delivered, not a change to the text, so I do not count it.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall frågar inte varför vi möts`.
   - **Class: a change to what a claim says, by meaning.** It is also a change of taste, because no visible defect caused it.
   - The old headline asserts that a box is absent. The body identifies that box as the one "för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta" (for the decision we should be able to make). The ending comes back to the same box ("tvivlet på ännu en ruta").

"Varför vi behöver just varandras tid" is the author's proposal, not an observation of the template.
   - So the headline now states a paraphrased claim that the body never states in those words, and the "missing box" image the column is built on leaves the headline.
   - Scope, certainty, attribution, chronology and causality are not raised. The claim stays on this one library's template, is stated as flatly as the body states its observation, and is still the author's.
2. **Dash.** Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid`. After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid`.
   - **Class: a mechanical correction (locale form).** Swedish uses the spaced en dash as tankstreck, not the em dash.

No difference follows from another one.

- The input is a clean column. Its one visible defect is the em dash, which was correctly repaired.
