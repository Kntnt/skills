## 1. Differences

There are two differences.

1. **Headline (input line 8).**
   - Before: `# Mallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`
   - After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall har en ruta för allt utom poängen`
   - Class: a **change to what a claim says: scope**. The headline's subject changes from a template with no stated referent to one named template, the library's meeting template. The new scope is exactly the one the body sets in its first sentence ("Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall"), so the headline now asserts nothing the body did not already assert. The scope narrows and does not widen.
   - The stated motive is a genre rule: a headline must make sense without the text. That puts the edit on the border between the repair of a visible defect and a change of taste. An unanchored definite form, "Mallen", in a headline is a real if mild defect of standalone legibility, and it can be seen in the input.
   - The edit does not follow from any other change.

2. **Dash (input line 22).**
   - Before: `genom att utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien`
   - After: `genom att utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien`
   - Class: a **mechanical correction** of a locale form. Swedish uses a spaced en dash where the input had an em dash. The spaces were already there in the input and are kept.
   - The edit does not follow from any other change.
