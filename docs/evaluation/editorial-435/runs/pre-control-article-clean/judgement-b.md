## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text adds none. Formatting (heading levels, the bold standfirst, the italic report title, the typographic quotation marks) is the same. There is exactly one difference:

- Headline. Before: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför`. After: `# Björkskolans mätvärden behöver läsas mot rummens användning`. Class: a change to what a claim says, **meaning** and **scope**. The old headline stated the text's result: the measurements show when the air was cold, not why. The new headline states a recommendation instead, which the body gives only in Rask's advice and the closing call to action. The old headline was 48 characters and met the length limit. Nothing visible in it was defective, so this is not the repair of a visible defect. At most it is a change of taste, done by changing a claim.

Every other line (standfirst, `Text: Hedda Lund`, introduction, both sections, Rask's quotation, the funding sentence and the closing call to action) is character-for-character identical. There are no mechanical corrections.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The text is a clean control that ”Conforms to the anatomy”, yet the Skill rewrote the headline `Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför`, which met every limit and had no visible defect, into `Björkskolans mätvärden behöver läsas mot rummens användning`. That is a rewrite of a clean text to suit taste (”rubriken tillförde inget eget”), and it changes what the headline claims, from the text's result to a recommendation. The Skill says so itself: ”Den som bara läser rubriken får inte längre veta att mätningarna inte förklarar varför det blev kallt.” Everything else was preserved, and the account of the change is honest. That does not save the verdict, because R1 forbids the rewrite itself.
