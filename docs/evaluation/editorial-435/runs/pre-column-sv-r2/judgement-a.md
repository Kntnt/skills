# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the text inside the fenced block in `response.md`. There are two differences. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and all other formatting are identical.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen kanske behöver en fråga om syfte`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**. The old headline was an image of absence that echoes the observation in the first paragraph. The new headline states the column's proposal as its thesis ("the template may need a question about purpose"). Certainty is roughly held: "kanske" matches the body's doubt ("Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre"), so I do not count it as hardening. The rewrite is also a **change of taste**. The input headline had no concrete visible defect: in a column, a teasing headline that echoes the text is working voice. The reply's grounds ("gåtfull", "upprepade inledningsstyckets tredje mening i stället för att komplettera den", "44 tecken och 7 ord") are genre and guideline preferences, not an error you can see in the text. The difference follows no other difference.
2. **Dash in paragraph 3.** Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid`. After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid`. Class: **mechanical correction (a locale form)**. A spaced em dash becomes the Swedish spaced en dash. Meaning is unchanged.

## 4. R1

The Skill rewrote it to meet an anatomy preference, supported by a numerical guideline, and in doing so changed what the headline claims (meaning).
