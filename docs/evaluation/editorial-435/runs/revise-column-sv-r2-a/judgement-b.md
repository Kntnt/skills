The new headline says the template leaves out *why we need each other's time*. That is the author's proposal from paragraph 4 ("borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), not the gap she observed in paragraph 1.    - **Why taste.** The input headline has no concrete visible defect. A cryptic, teasing headline is an ordinary choice in a column.

Frontmatter: no difference. Other formatting: no difference. Neither difference follows from the other.
