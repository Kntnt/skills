## 1. Differences

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen hoppar över varför vi behöver varandras tid`.
   - **Class:** a change to what a claim says, in **scope** and **meaning**. It is also a change of taste.
   - **Scope.** The input observes one document, "bibliotekets mötesmall", and its limiting sentence says so. The new headline drops "bibliotekets". A headline read on its own as "Mötesmallen" can mean meeting templates in general.
   - **Meaning.** The old headline names an absence: the box that does not exist. In the body, that box is the one for "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta". The new headline instead states a thesis: the template "hoppar över" (skips) *why we need each other's time*. That wording comes from the author's normative wish in paragraph 4 ("borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid"). Now it is stated as the template's omission, and "hoppar över" frames that omission as a fault.
   - **Hardening, fourth form.** I also flag this as a candidate fourth form. An allusive, non-assertive title becomes a flat assertive headline, without the tentativeness the column ends on ("Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre").
   - **Defect?** No concrete visible defect is present. The old title is grammatical, coherent and deliberately echoes the opening. The rewrite answers the anatomy guideline, not a defect.
2. **Paragraph 3, dash.** Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`.
   - **Class:** mechanical correction. Swedish locale form: an em dash becomes a spaced en dash. The spaces were already there.
   - It follows no other change.

The frontmatter is unchanged. No other formatting differences.
