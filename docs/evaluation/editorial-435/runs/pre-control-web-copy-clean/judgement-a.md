# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

There is no frontmatter in either version. The title, both subheadings, the first two sections and the Markdown formatting are identical. There is one difference.

- Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid."
  After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid."
  Class: a change of taste. The subheading directly above, "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar", already states the response time. The body sentence now repeats it. What the claim says is unchanged: same scope (three working days, still with no starting point), same certainty, same attribution to Svale, and no chronology, causality or meaning is added. It is not the repair of a visible defect, because a short heading that carries a fact the body leaves out is a working pattern in web copy, not a defect.

## 2. The account

- The single difference is reported as finding 1: "Åtgärdat: svarstiden stod bara i underrubriken … Nu står ”inom tre arbetsdagar” också i den första meningen under rubriken. Rubriken är oförändrad." The report is accurate about what changed and where. The "Påståenden" section is also accurate: the claim "har kopierats med samma räckvidd: inte heller brödtexten säger när de tre dagarna börjar räknas". The closing line, "texten bara ändrats på en punkt", is true.
- The reply calls it a defect ("Den som läste avsnittet utan rubriken fick därför inte veta när svaret kommer"). That label is wrong. The reply describes the edit accurately but misclassifies its nature.
- Finding 2 changes nothing in the text. It is correctly reported as unresolved.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page without sales template or CTA."** Met. No CTA, sales framing or template section was added. All three sections survive with their content, for example "En intresseanmälan till Svales genomgång av bokningsrutiner är inte en beställning." and "Inga betalningsuppgifter behövs." The run did not make the named mistake of adding a sales template or CTA.
- **"Short headings and variable section length work."** Not met in judgement, though met in the text's structure. Both headings survive unchanged and no section was lengthened except by the one inserted phrase. But finding 1 treats the short heading as insufficient on its own: "Tidsgränsen på tre arbetsdagar fanns bara i underrubriken … Brödtexten under den angav ingen tid." The run then spent its one correction duplicating the heading's content into the body. This is the rejection the clause guards against: treating a working short heading as a defect.
- **"No invention of destination or function."** Met. Finding 2 notes that the form's location is not given and says explicitly "Uppgiften finns inte i texten och har inte hittats på." No link, destination, URL or form function was added to the returned text. Raising the missing location as an open finding is questionable on a page the expectation calls complete, but it is not invention, and it changed nothing.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the returned line "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid.", together with finding 1, which justifies it. R1 says clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste. This control is a clean text whose short headings work, and the run used its correction budget to repeat the heading's claim in the body. The edit is small, accurately reported, and changes no claim's scope or meaning, and every other part of the text was preserved without invention. Even so, it is a taste rewrite of a clean text presented as a defect repair.
