The returned text reads "Svale svarar via e-post **inom tre arbetsdagar** för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." **Class: a change of taste.** No claim changes. ## 4. R1

**Fail**, narrowly.

The deciding passage is finding 1 together with the change it drove: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget …" The text was clean. The claim was already visible in the heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar", and the frozen expectation states that short headings work. Copying the claim into the body fixes no concrete visible defect. It is a change of taste made to a clean text, which R1 does not allow ("Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste").

The run preserved everything else: every claim, the voice, all three headings, the uneven section lengths, and the absence of a CTA. It reported its one change accurately and invented no destination. The failure rests on that single unneeded edit.
