## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text has none. The reply puts the returned text inside a fenced `markdown` block. That fence is how the reply is packaged, not a change to the text.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena`. After: `# Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta i gemensam reparationslogg`. Class: **a change to what a claim says.** It changes **meaning**: the customer's action (it gathered the cases) becomes the foreman's appraisal. It also changes **certainty**: the text gives Lind's appraisal with a condition ("men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser"), and the headline states it with no condition. It also changes **attribution**: Lind says a shared picture of the cases helps, and the headline says she sees value in the log itself. The input's headline had no visible defect, so this is a change of taste.
2. **Standfirst, last sentence.** Before: `Här är vad gruppen gjorde, vad anteckningarna visar och vad hon skulle ändra.` After: `Här är vad Elm Quays underhållsgrupp gjorde, vad gruppens anteckningar från försöket visar och vad hon skulle ändra.` Class: mostly **a change of taste**, with a small addition (**meaning**) that the notes are from the trial, which the body supports. The definite forms had an implied referent (Elm Quay Housing, the trial), so this was at most a weak defect.
3. **Body, first sentence.** Before: `Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` After: `Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna i en gemensam reparationslogg.` Class: **a change of taste.** The pseudo-cleft is correct Swedish. The rewrite also makes a small change to **meaning**: "sin" becomes "en", so the sentence no longer says the log was the group's own. And "ville få ut av" (the benefit they wanted from the log) becomes "ville kunna se … i" (what they wanted to see in a log).
4. **Second subheading.** Before: `## Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning`. After: `## Mediantiden till tilldelning var kortare, men belastningen skilde sig`. Class: **a change of taste**, and a change of emphasis (**meaning**). The input's heading put the caveat first. The new one puts the improvement first and makes the caveat a "men" clause. Each fact in it is in the section, and it states no cause.
5. **Body, caveat sentence.** Before: `Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning,`. After: `Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastningar,`. Class: **a mechanical correction.** It was made by the final proofreading pass. The singular was already acceptable Swedish. This is noted here and is not counted under R1.

No other line changed. These lines are word for word the same as in the input: the byline `Text: Iris Falk`, both quotations, the numbers paragraph, the first section, the third subheading, the disclosure and the link.
