# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

A line-by-line comparison of `work/input.md` with the fenced text in `response.md` finds three differences. Neither version has frontmatter. The standfirst, the byline, the first section, the numbers section's body, the closing section, the disclosure and the link are identical, and so is all the formatting apart from the heading text below.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena` (36 characters). After: `# Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta av gemensam ärendebild` (55 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, in **meaning** and **attribution**. The headline used to report something the customer did (the organisation gathered the repair cases). Now it reports how the customer's supervisor rates the result (she sees a benefit). It keeps the positive half of Lind's appraisal ("hjälper oss") and drops the qualification that comes with it ("men jag skulle lägga till en vecka"). This also shifts **certainty** towards an unqualified benefit. This was a rewrite for taste, not the repair of a visible defect: the standfirst gives the scope (two houses, eight weeks) straight away.
2. **First body sentence.** Before: `Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` After: `Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få se samma information över skiftgränserna med sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` Class: a change of taste, and at most a light style repair of an awkward cleft sentence. The claim is unchanged: "wanted to get out of" became "wanted to get to see … with", which says the same thing.
3. **Subheading of the numbers section.** Before: `## Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning` (39 characters). After: `## Mediantiden till tilldelning var kortare, men belastningen skilde sig` (69 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, in **meaning**. The heading now puts the improvement ("kortare") into a heading that used to state only the caveat. The caveat stays, and the body's non-attribution sentence is untouched. So scope and causality are unchanged, but the heading now leads with the performance result. This was taste, not a visible defect: the old heading was accurate and matched its section.

**Fail.** The control is a clean text, and R1 says "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste". The Skill rewrote the headline because it "beskrev en åtgärd i stället för kundens nytta". It replaced `Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena` with `Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta av gemensam ärendebild`, which changes what the headline claims and puts forward an unqualified benefit.
