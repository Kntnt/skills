## Findings (all six fixed)

1. **Headline "Unlock your association's full potential".** It promised a "full potential" that nothing on the page supports, used "unlock" in the generic corporate style, and didn't say what's being offered. It's now "A review of one shared room in your housing association."
2. **Opening paragraph.** "Decision-makers in a complex reality face growing complexity" goes in circles and could open any page. "Take the thumb out" is the Swedish idiom *ta tummen ur* translated word for word, and "discover a fantastic journey" is a promise the page doesn't back up. The paragraph said nothing about the offer and has been removed.
3. **"Start" section and the link "Book and pay now".** The label promised booking and payment. The page's own last section says the link opens a form that places no order, asks for no payment details and books no meeting. The heading "Start" didn't say what the section held, and the link came before the reader knew what was offered. The "Start" heading is gone. The link now reads "Open the expression-of-interest form" and sits directly above the sentence that explains it.
4. **Headings "It", "More", "Again", "Things" and "Important".** None of them said what its section contained. Each "It" pointed to an offer the page only named ("the review") in its very last sentence, so a reader landing on any one section couldn't tell what cost SEK 4,800. The first four sections are now under "Price and contents of the review." "Important" is now "How to express interest." The first sentence names "the review", so the "It"s that follow have something to point to.
5. **Four one-sentence sections.** Price, scope, the video call and the written account all describe one offer but were split across four separate sections. They're now one paragraph, in the same order and wording, except that the first "It" became "The review."
6. **"within three working days".** "Working days" is British; a US reader expects "business days", and it has been changed.
