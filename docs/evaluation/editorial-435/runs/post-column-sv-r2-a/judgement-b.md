# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter is identical (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline is identical, and every paragraph except the third is identical. There are two differences.

1. **Heading.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall borde kanske fråga varför vi träffas`. Class: a **change of taste** that is also a **change to what a claim says, by meaning**. The old heading names a fact the text states (a box is missing). The new heading states a hedged normative proposal (the template should perhaps ask why we meet). It also changes what the headline is about: from the missing box to a question the template should ask. The input has no visible defect here. The heading is a deliberate allusive column title that echoes the first paragraph ("Någon ruta … finns inte"). Nothing in it is misspelled, ungrammatical, false or unclear on its face. The new wording is supported loosely by the body ("borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid", "Den säger inte varför vi behöver göra det tillsammans"), and "kanske" carries the author's doubt. So this is not hardening, but the headline now makes a claim that the original headline did not make. The change follows nothing else.
2. **Dash in paragraph 3.** Before: `något annat — att tid i kalendern`. After: `något annat – att tid i kalendern`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. A spaced em dash becomes a spaced en dash, which is the Swedish tankstreck convention. The change follows nothing else.

Formatting: the heading level, paragraph breaks and emphasis are unchanged. No other difference.
