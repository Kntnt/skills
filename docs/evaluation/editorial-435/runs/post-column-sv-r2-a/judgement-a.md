## 1. Differences

1. **Headline (H1).**
   Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`
   After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall borde kanske fråga varför vi träffas`
   Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**, made for reasons of taste or anatomy rather than to repair a visible defect. The old headline is a figurative observation: a box is absent. The new headline is a hedged normative recommendation: the template should perhaps ask why we meet. Scope stays within the text ("bibliotekets mötesmall"), and "kanske" keeps certainty close to the body's hedging. But what the headline asserts changes from a finding to a proposal. It also picks one side of a tension that the column explicitly leaves open ("Hoppet om frågan och tvivlet på ännu en ruta får samsas ett tag till"). The question it names ("varför vi träffas") is also not the question the author proposes ("vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?"). The nearest it comes to the body is paragraph 4 ("varför vi behöver just varandras tid"). The old headline had no visible defect. A figurative column headline that echoes its opening is a normal feature of the genre, not an error. The reply's own justification rests on the headline not stating the angle, on repetition and on a character count from a measuring script, which are taste and numerical guidelines.

2. **Paragraph 3, dash.**
   Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid i kalendern`
   After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid i kalendern`
   Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. A spaced em dash becomes the spaced en dash used in Swedish typography. Nothing else changes.

No difference is consequent on another.
