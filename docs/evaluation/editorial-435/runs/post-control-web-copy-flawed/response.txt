## Findings

**Repaired**

1. **Headline:** "Unlock your association's full potential" was a promise nothing on the page supported, in stock corporate wording ("unlock"). It didn't say what the page offers. It's now "A review of how your association's community room is booked."
2. **Opening paragraph:** It was generic and went in a circle ("a complex reality" with "growing complexity"). "Take the thumb out" is a word-for-word translation of the Swedish idiom *ta tummen ur* ("get your finger out"). "Discover a fantastic journey" was a promise with nothing behind it. The whole paragraph was the problem, so it's gone.
3. **The offer was never stated:** The page only said it was a review, and who provides it, in its last sentence. It now opens with "Svale's review covers one community room…".
4. **Link label and position:** "Book and pay now" contradicted the page's own statement that the form places no order, takes no payment and books no meeting. The link now reads "Open the expression-of-interest form". It sits in the last section, right above the paragraph that explains it.
5. **Vague headings and a dangling "It":** "Start", "It", "More", "Again" and "Things" said nothing about their sections, and each section opened with an "It" that pointed to nothing. Those headings were removed and "It" now refers back to "Svale's review". "Important" became "How to express interest", which says what the section contains.
6. **Fragmented structure:** Four facts about one offer had six headings between them. They're now one paragraph and a two-item list under the headline.
7. **"shared room":** In American English this usually means a room two people share. It's now "community room".
8. **"working days":** This is British usage. It's now "business days".

**Unresolved**

9. **"two board representatives":** The text doesn't say whose board: the housing association's or Svale's. So the reader can't tell who is expected on the call. Fixing this needs a fact the text doesn't contain.

The loop stopped because the budget of one correction was spent while finding 9 was still open.
