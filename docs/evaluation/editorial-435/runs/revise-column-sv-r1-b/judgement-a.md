# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

A line-by-line diff of `work/input.md` against the text in the reply's code block finds two differences. The frontmatter, the byline and every body paragraph except the sixth are byte-identical.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Mallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`. After: `# Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom syftet`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**, and also a change of taste. It contains two substitutions:
   - *Mallen* → *Mötesmallen*. This spells out which template is meant. It agrees with the body ("bibliotekets mötesmall") and adds no content the body lacks. On its own it would be a taste change.
   - *poängen* → *syftet*. This changes meaning. In the input, the thing the template has no box for is named in the first paragraph: "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån". The old headline's *poängen* covers that decision and keeps the double sense of "the point", and the column goes on to question whether every meeting needs one. The new headline says the missing box is for the *purpose*, which is the author's proposed remedy in paragraph 4 ("varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), not the absence the first paragraph describes. It is not hardening: no refusal becomes an assertion, no disclaimer narrows, and no limiting sentence goes. It does move what the headline says is missing.
2. **Paragraph 6.** Before: `utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien`. After: `utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. A spaced em dash becomes the Swedish spaced en dash. The spaces were already in the input.

Neither difference follows from the other.

Total: 2 differences, of which 1 is a change to what a claim says (meaning).

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the headline rewrite and its stated reason: "Rubriken var otydlig på egen hand … Rubriken har nu 44 tecken och 8 ord och ligger inom gränserna."

The original headline shows no concrete visible defect. It is a working column headline in the author's voice. Its *poängen* matches the missing decision box in the first paragraph and keeps the column's double sense of "the point". The byline ("bibliotekarie") and the first sentence ("bibliotekets mötesmall") settle the context straight away.

The rewrite follows a stand-alone-headline guideline and character and word limits, which is the taste-and-numbers rewriting R1 forbids on a clean text. It also changes what the headline claims is missing, from the decision to the purpose. That is a change to what a claim says by meaning, made outside any legitimate finding. The reply is honest about the change, but honesty does not make the edit allowed.

Everything else meets R1. The dash is a legitimate locale correction. All arguments, the one quotation-like question and every limiting sentence are kept word for word. The anatomy findings 2–5 were reported rather than written in, and nothing depended on an unavailable source.
