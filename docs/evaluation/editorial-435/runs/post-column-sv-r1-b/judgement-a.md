## 1. Differences

There are two differences.

1. **Headline (line 8).**
   - Before: `# Mallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`
   - After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall har rutor för allt utom poängen`
   - Class: **a change to what a claim says, by scope.** The headline's subject goes from an unspecified "the template" to "the library's meeting template". This narrows the claim to the one document the body examines. It does not harden anything: the body already says "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall", so the headline asserts nothing the text did not already assert. Inside the same rewrite, the singular "en ruta" becomes the plural "rutor". That is a **change of taste**. The meaning is essentially the same, but it loses a little of the aphoristic ring of the original line ("en ruta för allt"). The plural does not follow grammatically from the new subject. It is a separate choice made in the same edit.

2. **Dash (line 22, paragraph "Där börjar tvivlet").**
   - Before: `utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien`
   - After: `utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien`
   - Class: **mechanical correction (locale form).** A spaced em dash becomes a spaced en dash, which is the Swedish convention for a dash in running text (tankstreck). The spaces were already there in the input.

Nothing else differs: no word, punctuation mark, frontmatter key, heading level or paragraph break.
