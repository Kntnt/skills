## 1. Differences

1. **Headline (H1).**
   - Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`
   - After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall frågar efter tiden men inte syftet`
   - Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning.** The old headline was an image: *the box that does not exist*. The column returns to that image at the end with "tvivlet på ännu en ruta". The new headline is a thesis statement: the template asks about time but not about purpose. The body supports that thesis ("Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om"; "varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), so no body claim gains scope, certainty, attribution, chronology or causality. But the headline now says something different from what it said before, and it drops the "ruta" image that ties the opening to the close. The trigger was not a concrete visible defect. "Rutan som inte finns" is intelligible, and an allusive headline is normal in a column. The Skill judged it too vague because it measured it against article-anatomy expectations. So the motive was **taste**. Not consequent on any other difference.
2. **Dash, paragraph 3.**
   - Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid …`
   - After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid …`
   - Class: **mechanical correction (locale form).** A spaced em dash became the spaced en dash that is Swedish convention for a tankstreck. The spaces were already there in the input. Not consequent on any other difference.

Total: 2 differences, 1 of them a change to what a claim says (meaning).

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is finding 1 together with the headline it produced. "Rutan som inte finns" → "Bibliotekets mötesmall frågar efter tiden men inte syftet", justified as "så vag att läsaren måste läsa texten för att förstå den … den visade inte krönikans vinkel."

Apart from the dash, the input is a clean, working column. Its headline is a deliberate image that the last paragraph returns to ("ännu en ruta"). That is voice, not a concrete visible defect. The rewrite puts a stated thesis where the author had an allusion. The Skill's own finding 5 shows the rewrite created a new defect: the headline now repeats the first paragraph's closing sentence. Findings 2–4 and the closing line ("Texten följer alltså inte artikelanatomin: ingressen, avsnitten och ett eget avslutningsavsnitt saknas") show the Skill judged a column against article-anatomy expectations for a lede, H2s and a closing section. It did not act on those findings, but that same framing drove the one change it made. R1 forbids exactly this: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines."

Everything else is in R1's favour. All quotations, arguments and claims outside the headline are preserved verbatim. Every limiting sentence survives. The dash is a legitimate mechanical correction. The account is complete and accurate. There was no attempt to check against unavailable sources. These do not outweigh an unwarranted rewrite of a working element of a clean text.
