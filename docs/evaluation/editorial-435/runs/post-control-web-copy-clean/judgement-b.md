## 1. Differences

Frontmatter: none in the input and none in the returned text. Headings, paragraph breaks and Markdown formatting are identical. The reply wraps the returned text in a ```` ```markdown ```` fence. That is how the reply is packaged, not a change to the text.

1. Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." Class: **a change of taste**. The phrase copies the heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" into the body. The claim's scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning stay the same, because the page already made the claim in the heading directly above. There was no visible defect: a reader who reads the section also reads its heading.

No other sentence, heading or line changed. No mechanical corrections were made.

Its claims section is accurate: "med samma omfattning och säkerhet".

- Finding 2 made no change to the text, and the reply correctly marks it as unresolved.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page"**: partly met. All content and every restriction survive unchanged, for example "En intresseanmälan till Svales genomgång av bokningsrutiner är inte en beställning.", "Inga betalningsuppgifter behövs." and "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret skickas in." However, the run did not treat the page as complete. It rewrote one sentence (difference 1). It also reported the page as missing something: "Olöst: ”Ange namn, förening och e-postadress i formuläret.” … Läsaren får inte veta var och hur intresseanmälan görs … Någon som har de uppgifterna behöver fylla i dem." That finding treats the page's own form as a missing destination.
- **"without sales template or CTA"**: met. The run added no sales structure, no call to action and no persuasive copy. The page keeps its three sections and its neutral tone.
- **"Short headings … work"**: not met. The run treated the short heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" as not telling the reader anything, reported it as a defect and repeated its fact in the body.
- **"variable section length work"**: met. No section was lengthened or shortened to even them out, apart from the one phrase added in difference 1, which was not added for length. The single-sentence section "Uppgifter att lämna" was left as it is.
- **"No invention of destination or function"**: met in the returned text. The run added no link, no URL, no location for the form and no new function. Finding 2 explicitly declines to invent one: "Felet kan inte rättas utan uppgifter som texten saknar". Reporting the absence as an unresolved defect is a false finding, but it is not an invention.

The expectation names two mistakes to avoid. The run did not add a sales template or CTA, and it did not invent a destination or function. It did make a third mistake the expectation guards against: it did not accept that a short heading works.
