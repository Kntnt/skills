## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the headline rewrite, justified as follows: "Rubriken påstod i stället att mallen har rutor för allt annat än varför. Den som läst klart hade funnit att rubriken överdrev vad mallen innehåller."

"En ruta för allt utom varför" is an ordinary rhetorical hyperbole. It is the column's working voice, and the self-mocking close ("Formuläret blir åtminstone längre") echoes it. The body does not contradict it in any way a reader would take for a factual error. The body never says the template has only two boxes. It says the boxes that exist "räknar tid". Reading the figure literally and then narrowing its scope is not the repair of a concrete visible defect. It rewrites a clean text, one the Skill itself says conforms, and changes what the headline claims.

The loss is small and the reply discloses it honestly and accurately. Every other preservation and rejection clause in the expectation is met. Even so, R1 forbids rewriting a clean text and requires working voice to be kept outside genuine findings, and this finding is not genuine.
