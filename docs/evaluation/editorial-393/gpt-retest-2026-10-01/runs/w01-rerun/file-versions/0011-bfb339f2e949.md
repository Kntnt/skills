## Source-support findings

### S1 — Expansion outcome should remain expressly undecided

Draft:

> “Elm Quay Housing avvaktar en utökning”
>
> “Först efter den kontrollen beslutar teamet om en utökning.”

Source:

> “The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs.”

The source fixes the present absence of expansion and the timing of a future decision. It does not fix the decision's outcome. Read together, the heading and “beslutar … om en utökning” admit the ordinary reading that an expansion is being awaited and will be decided upon affirmatively after the check. The factual implication is stronger than the source's undecided outcome.

Countercase, source true and that draft reading false: the team checks larger repairs and decides that the categories are unsuitable, so it keeps the trial limited or stops it rather than expanding. This is compatible with every supplied statement; no supplied statement excludes a negative decision.

Reverse check: an already approved expansion is merely waiting for a categories check before the team issues its implementation decision. This would fit the affirmative reading of the draft, but would not carry the source's still-future decision about expansion. Nothing supplied establishes such prior approval.

Smallest supported repair that removes both linked implications:

> “Teamet ska ta ställning till en utökning”
>
> “Efter den kontrollen beslutar teamet om försöket ska utökas.”

Keep the preceding sentence about larger repairs, the customer agency and the following qualified quotation unchanged.
