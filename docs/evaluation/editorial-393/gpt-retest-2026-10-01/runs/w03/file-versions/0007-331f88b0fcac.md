## Findings

### T1 — Translation adds an unestablished first-person plural actor

**Draft:** “Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan vi börjar i nästa byggnad.”

**Source:** “I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

**Supported unchanged part:** Lind would reserve the category-agreement time before the next building's start. The conditional “would” and the referent of “that time” are preserved.

**Changed part:** “Vi börjar” supplies a first-person plural group as the actor starting in the next building. The source makes the building the subject and leaves the starting personnel implicit. “We spent more time agreeing on the categories” supplies a group for the earlier preparation experience; it does not establish that the same group carries out the next building's start. “The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs” establishes the team's decision role, not the identity of future implementation personnel.

**Concrete divergence, source to draft:** The original team could agree on categories and decide whether to extend the trial, while a different building team or contractor starts the log in the next building. Lind could reserve category-preparation time before that building starts without her “we” group beginning there. This future arrangement is compatible with the package. The source does not exclude it. The draft's first-person plural start introduces an actor the source need not imply.

**Reverse-direction test:** “Vi börjar i nästa byggnad” also ties the deadline to the original group's own start. In a future arrangement where that group begins its work only after another group has started the building's trial, reserving time before the original group's start need not mean reserving it before the building's trial starts. The source's stated deadline is the building's start. The package gives no future staffing or handover schedule excluding this distinction. This is the same translation defect, not a second finding.

**Smallest supported repair:** “Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan försöket startar i nästa byggnad.” The context establishes that the start concerns this trial; the repair makes that settled activity explicit while leaving the actor unclaimed. It preserves Lind's conditional stance and preparation recommendation.

**Classification:** Translation finding involving an added actor and person/number features. The same addition is also a source-support issue within the translated quotation. No separate defect is counted.
