## Separate pronoun and gender accounting

- **“det”** in “när det prövade”: third-person singular collective team, matching the source's “team” and “It.” Neuter is grammatical agreement with *team*, not members' natural gender. **“det egna underhållsteamet”** uses the definite article; “own” comes explicitly from “its own maintenance team.”
- Quotation **“Vi”** and **“oss”**: first-person plural from “We” and “us.” The customer group remains the actor/beneficiary. Identifying that group does not establish its membership list or gender composition, and the draft asserts neither.
- **“våra”**: first-person plural possession, supplied by “ours”; plural agreement is with categories. **“dem”**: third-person plural categories, supplied by “them.” Neither conveys human gender.
- **“Jag”**: first-person singular directly supplied by “I,” independently of identifying Lind as referent. It conveys no natural gender.
- **“den tiden”**: singular demonstrative from “that time,” referring to time for category agreement. Common grammatical gender follows *tid*. **“nästa hus”** preserves singular “the next building.”
- **“Den redovisar”**: third-person singular note. Common grammatical gender follows *anteckning*; no female human referent is asserted. The original likewise treats the note as singular in “It excludes”.
- **“de boende”**: plural residents, supplied by “residents” and “resident-satisfaction”; no specific count or gender. **“de uppmätta tiderna,” “de första anmälningarna” and “de föregående åtta veckorna”** contain plural definite articles agreeing with intervals, reports and weeks, not human-gender pronouns.
- **“Det saknas”**: impersonal/expletive *det*, corresponding to “There are no ... measurements”; no person is its referent. **“dessa delar”**: plural demonstrative identifying the preceding cost, satisfaction and completion dimensions.
- **“Du,” “dig,” “ditt” and “eget”**: singular reader/user address in the optional invitation and visible byline placeholder. They convey no natural gender and claim no actual reader behavior.
- **“kvällspasset” and “morgonpasset”** remain singular collectives corresponding to “the evening shift” and “the morning shift”; neither means one worker.
- **“arbetsledaren,” “medarbetare,” “personal” and “Maya Lind”** add no natural-gender information. The source uses “Her final quotation,” but the draft adds no *hon* or *hennes*. No gender is inferred merely from the name or job title.

## Findings and smallest supported repairs

### S1 — Source support: narrower ownership of the internal note

Draft: **“teamets interna anteckning”**. Source: **“Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025”**, including the qualification **“the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software.”**

Difference: the draft attributes the note specifically to the maintenance team, while the source attributes it to the company.

Source-true/draft-false case: a central operations analyst independently creates and retains Elm Quay's internal trial note from the log records, with the supplied date, exclusions, count and caveat; it is not an internal note of the maintenance team. No supplied statement excludes this arrangement. **“its own maintenance team decided to trial”** establishes who initiated the trial, not who owns the later note. Reverse direction: a team-internal trial note can also be Elm Quay's internal trial note, so the conflict is the unsupported narrowing, not company affiliation.

Smallest repair: replace **“teamets interna anteckning”** with **“bolagets interna anteckning”**. Keep the supported evidential limitation.

### S2 — Source support: particular team-leadership relationship added

Draft: **“underhållsteamets arbetsledare Maya Lind”**. Source: **“Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind”**. Lind says **“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours”**.

Difference: maintenance supervision becomes supervision of this particular team. The job title and customer-group first person do not independently establish that reporting relationship.

Source-true/draft-false case: Lind supervises contractor maintenance or another subgroup at Elm Quay, while a different supervisor directs the own team running this trial. Lind can use the company's/customer group's “we” and “ours” and hold the supplied job title without leading this trial team. No supplied statement excludes that organizational arrangement. Reverse direction: the trial team's supervisor is a maintenance supervisor, so the additional specific relation is the unsupported step.

Smallest repair: replace **“underhållsteamets arbetsledare Maya Lind”** with **“arbetsledaren för underhåll Maya Lind”**. Preserve the supported “skriver” and email attribution.

### T1 — Quotation translation: the building's unspecified “beginning”

Draft: **“Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan nästa hus börjar.”** Source: **“I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”** Context: **“decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings”** and **“The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs.”**

Conditional stance, individual person, time allocation and next-building scope survive. The obstruction is the absolute Swedish **“nästa hus börjar”**: the physical house has no named action to begin. It can suggest the start of building construction, obscuring the trial start intended in context. This is a translation finding, not an additional source-event assertion.

Reading test in both directions: construction of a next house can begin without that house entering the repair-log trial; a trial can begin in an existing next building without any house construction. The supplied trial context settles the implicit activity sufficiently to express it idiomatically.

Smallest supported repair: **“Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan försöket börjar i nästa hus.”** This retains the conditional recommendation and does not assert that expansion has been approved.
