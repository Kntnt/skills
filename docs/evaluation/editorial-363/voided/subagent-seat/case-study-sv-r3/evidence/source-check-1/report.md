## 2. Source-support findings

Ordered most severe first. Each names a concrete case, compatible with the supplied material, in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails (and, where relevant, the reverse).

### F1 — "Hennes omdöme gäller Elm Quays eget test" narrows her assessment beyond the material

- **Draft (L31):** "Hennes omdöme gäller Elm Quays eget test."
- **Material:** "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company. Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment. It is not an inference from the figures."
- **What differs:** scope of the assessment, and whose assertion the limit is. The material limits her recommendation ("not … to every housing company") and states what her assessment is *not derived from* (the figures). It nowhere confines the assessment's applicability to Elm Quay's own trial. The draft supplies that confinement as a statement of fact about her view.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** Lind's assessment is that a shared log helps any housing company that runs shift handovers, while she still declines to recommend Svale to *every* housing company because some have no shift split and no phone-reporting load. Both material sentences hold; "Hennes omdöme gäller Elm Quays eget test" fails, because her assessment then reaches beyond Elm Quay's own trial. Nothing supplied excludes this: the material's only scope statement is the "not to every housing company" one, which is compatible with a wide but not universal scope.
- **Reverse direction:** where her view really is confined to Elm Quay's trial, both hold; the asymmetry is one-directional, which is enough.
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace with the material's own limiting statement —
  `Hennes omdöme är hennes egen bedömning och bygger inte på siffrorna.`
  (Deleting the sentence outright also removes the defect; the reservation in the preceding sentence survives either way.)

### F2 — "hyresgästerna" / "hyresgästernas" asserts a tenancy the material does not supply

- **Draft (L13):** "höll samtidigt telefonanmälan öppen för hyresgästerna"; **(L21):** "Kostnader, hyresgästernas nöjdhet och tiden till färdigt arbete mättes inte."
- **Material:** "kept telephone reporting open for residents"; "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
- **What differs:** the people named, and with them a claim about the legal relation between them and Elm Quay. *Resident* names whoever lives in the flat; *hyresgäst* names a party to a tenancy agreement. The term is carried into Swedish as if the two could not come apart, and in this context they can.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats that are owner-occupied co-operative flats, and the people living in them are members and owners rather than tenants. "kept telephone reporting open for residents" holds exactly; "höll telefonanmälan öppen för hyresgästerna" fails, because those residents are not *hyresgäster*. The material says only "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats" and "housing company" — it supplies no statement that excludes this case.
- **Smallest supported repair:** `de boende` in both places — `…öppen för de boende` (L13) and `…de boendes nöjdhet…` (L21).

### F3 — "telefonsamtal … sparats" names calls where the material names reports

- **Draft (L7):** "telefonsamtal och mejl hade dittills sparats var för sig"
- **Material:** "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately."
- **What differs:** the thing named and measured. *Telephone reports* are the fault reports that arrived by phone; *telefonsamtal* are the calls themselves. The material's parallel is report-channel against report-channel; the draft's is call against email.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** the phone reports were written down by whoever took the call and those written records were filed in one place while emails sat in a mailbox; the calls themselves were never recorded or stored at all. "telephone reports … were stored separately" holds; "telefonsamtal … hade sparats var för sig" fails, since no call was stored anywhere. Nothing in the material states that calls were recorded.
- **Smallest supported repair:** `telefonanmälningar och mejl hade dittills sparats var för sig`.

### F4 — "underhållsansvarig" claims a responsibility that "maintenance supervisor" does not

- **Draft (L3):** "vad underhållsansvarig Maya Lind skulle göra annorlunda"; **(L15):** "skriver Maya Lind, underhållsansvarig på Elm Quay".
- **Material:** "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind".
- **What differs:** the role asserted, i.e. a circumstantial claim about a named person. *Supervisor* names someone who leads others at work; *underhållsansvarig* names the person answerable for the maintenance function, which in Swedish reads as the single responsible post.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay has a maintenance manager who is *underhållsansvarig* for all 640 flats, and Maya Lind is one of two supervisors leading the shift teams under that manager — exactly the structure the material's own morning/evening shift split suggests is possible. "maintenance supervisor Maya Lind" holds; "underhållsansvarig Maya Lind" fails, because somebody else holds that responsibility. The material supplies no statement of Elm Quay's maintenance hierarchy that excludes this.
- **Smallest supported repair:** `arbetsledare för underhållet` in both places — e.g. `…skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet på Elm Quay, i ett mejlsvar.` (`underhållsarbetsledare` is equally close.)

### F5 — "finns inte" upgrades an availability statement into a non-existence claim

- **Draft (L11):** "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte."
- **Material:** "No comparison with another supplier is available."
- **What differs:** modality and whose knowledge stands behind it. The material reports that no such comparison is at hand for this case; the draft states that none exists. The claims contract asks that what is unknown not be turned into known-not-to-have-happened.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay's team did sit down and compare Svale against one other supplier, but that comparison was never written up and was not supplied with this material. "No comparison with another supplier is available" holds; "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte" fails. Nothing supplied excludes this — the material's sentence is about availability, and "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair" does not say that testing was the only step.
- **Smallest supported repair:** `Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte att tillgå.` (or `…redovisas inte.`)

### F6 — the deck's "från Svale Systems" attaches to the reports, not the log

- **Draft (L3):** "en gemensam logg för felanmälningar från Svale Systems i två fastigheter"
- **Material:** "a shared repair log"; "It chose Svale Systems"; "Svale configured the log".
- **What differs:** on the nearest-attachment reading a Swedish reader takes first, *från Svale Systems* modifies *felanmälningar* — fault reports coming from Svale Systems. The material has fault reports coming from residents through phone and email, never from the supplier. The intended reading (the log is Svale's) is supported; the available one is not.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** every fault report in the trial was made by a resident by phone or email, as "kept telephone reporting open for residents" and "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately" describe. The material holds; a deck read as "fault reports from Svale Systems" fails against it.
- **Smallest supported repair:** move the modifier to the noun it belongs to — `…prövade underhållsteamet på Elm Quay Housing Svale Systems gemensamma logg för felanmälningar i två fastigheter`, or `…en gemensam logg från Svale Systems för felanmälningar i två fastigheter`.

## 3. Translation findings (quoted speech)

Each quotation was read in Swedish alone first, then set beside the source.

**Q1 (L15).** Swedish alone: "Vi ville att kvällspasset skulle se vad morgonpasset redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." Reads in one pass. No verb without a subject that can perform it, no time or place adverbial whose event is missing, no reference the sentence never introduces. Beside the source ("We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."): meaning, stance (matter-of-fact, crediting Svale with help only), certainty (flat past assertions) and the ownership emphasis on *ours/våra* are all preserved, and the *kvällspasset/morgonpasset* pairing keeps the speaker's own shift vocabulary. **No finding.**

**Q2 (L27).** Swedish alone: "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att föra in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan vi drar igång i nästa fastighet." Reads in one pass; the fronted "Den tiden" takes ordinary V2 order and its referent is introduced by the sentence before it. Beside the source: the comparison keeps both of its terms (*agreeing on* → *komma överens om*, *entering* → *föra in*), the conditional *I would set aside* stays conditional, and the shift from *we* to *I* — she reports a team fact, then her own intention — is preserved. **No finding.**

**Q3 (L33).** Swedish alone: "Jag skulle välja att göra om testet. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." Reads in one pass. Beside the source: *I would choose to* keeps its deliberate *välja att*; the present-tense *helps us* stays present-tense *hjälper oss*; the *but* clause keeps the qualification that is the whole point of the quotation, and it is neither strengthened nor softened. **No finding.**

No quotation was welded together from separate remarks, no hedge was removed or added, no self-correction is involved, and all three are used whole as the material supplies them.

## 4. Cases considered and set aside

- **S1 — present tense in "Hon rekommenderar inte Svale Systems till alla bostadsföretag" (L31)** against the material's past "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company." The material states her stance as a standing, qualified assessment ("Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment"), and Swedish reporting of an interviewee's current position takes the present as the unmarked tense. No case separates the two here. Not a finding.
- **S2 — "innan vi drar igång i nästa fastighet" (L27)** supplying an agent the source's elliptical "before the next building starts" leaves out. Swedish needs a verb with a subject here, and the supplied context settles the referent: the maintenance team runs the trial and would start the next building. The register of *drar igång* is a shade more informal than *starts*, but meaning, stance and certainty are unchanged, and a preferred synonym is not an obstruction. Not a finding.
- **S3 — "Underhållspersonalen" in the heading (L9)** for "The maintenance team". The material gives no staff outside the team who could have set categories, and the quotation attributes ownership to the speaker's own group. Not a finding.
- **S4 — "Under testet fanns felanmälningarna i en gemensam logg" (L37)** against "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial." The exclusion attaches to the note's count of 31, not to what the log held; the material never says any report was kept out of the log. Not a finding.
- **S5 — "Kostnader … mättes inte" (L21)** against "There are no cost … measurements." Unlike F5, the material states this absence flatly, with no availability qualifier, so the draft's *mättes inte* adds no certainty the material lacks. Not a finding.
- **S6 — the heading "Kategorierna tog mer tid än de första anmälningarna" (L23)** compressing "more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports". A compression standing immediately above the full quotation; nothing is reversed or re-attributed. Not a finding.
- **S7 — speech dashes for written email answers.** The draft sets the quotations with the Swedish speech dash but attributes them with "skriver … i ett mejlsvar", so the reader is told what the material is. This signals the material honestly rather than pretending to recorded speech; the choice of mark itself is mechanics, outside this comparison. Not a finding.
- **S8 — the forbidden inferences.** Checked individually: no claim that the software caused the shorter assignment time (L17, L21 state the opposite, in the note's voice); no cost or money claim; no resident-satisfaction claim (L21 states its absence); no rescue framing; assignment time is never turned into completion time (L21 keeps them apart); the customer's reservation survives at L31; supplier narration stays in third person throughout (the only *vi/oss* are Lind's, inside quotations); the link describes its destination as a checklist to read. None is a finding.
