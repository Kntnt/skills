## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 — source support: the draft puts *the* repair reports in one shared log, the material puts the log in two buildings

**Draft (line 37):** "Under testet fanns felanmälningarna i en gemensam logg, men Elm Quay har ännu inte utökat den till fler fastigheter."

**Source:** "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." / "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats." / "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."

**What differs — scope.** The definite plural *felanmälningarna*, with no restriction, says that during the trial Elm Quay's repair reports were in one shared log. The material restricts the log to two buildings out of a 640-flat portfolio, and the only count it gives is one that leaves out emergencies and pre-trial work.

**Case where the draft fails and the source holds:** during the eight weeks, a resident in a third Elm Quay building telephones in a leaking radiator, and that report is handled the old way — a phone note filed separately, never in the shared log. The material's statements all remain true; "under testet fanns felanmälningarna i en gemensam logg" is false. Nothing in the material excludes the case: the trial is stated as running "in two buildings", and no sentence says the log covered anything else. The reverse direction holds trivially — the source statement is true of the case, the draft's is not.

**Smallest supported repair:** restrict the subject to the trial's scope, e.g. "Under testet fanns de två husens felanmälningar i en gemensam logg, men Elm Quay har ännu inte utökat den."

### Finding 2 — source support (and, inside the quotation, translation): *fastighet* names a property unit, the material names buildings

**Draft:** title "…i två fastigheter"; deck "…i två fastigheter"; line 7 "…i två fastigheter"; line 25 "till nästa fastighet"; line 27 (quotation) "innan vi drar igång i nästa fastighet"; line 37 "till fler fastigheter".

**Source:** "decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings"; "I would set that time aside before the next building starts"; "The trial has not yet expanded."

**What differs — the thing named.** Swedish *fastighet* is the registered property unit; a building is *hus* or *byggnad*. One *fastighet* can carry several buildings, and one building can be divided between *fastigheter*, so the two terms do not name the same thing in a housing company's portfolio.

**Case where the draft fails and the source holds:** Elm Quay's 640 flats sit on a small number of registered *fastigheter*, and the two buildings picked for the trial — a low-rise and the block beside it — stand on the same *fastighet*. "A shared repair log in two buildings" is true; "en gemensam felanmälningslogg i två fastigheter" is false, and so is "nästa fastighet" for a next building on that same property. The material supplies no statement excluding this: it counts flats, never properties, and never says the buildings are separately registered. Reverse: two *fastigheter* trialling the log could involve more than two buildings, which the source's "two buildings" excludes.

**Smallest supported repair:** use *hus* (or *byggnader*) at every occurrence — "i två hus", "till nästa hus", "innan vi drar igång i nästa hus" — and, at line 37, either "till fler hus" or simply "har ännu inte utökat den", since the material names no unit of expansion.

**Translation part:** inside the quotation at line 27 the same substitution changes what Lind said the next stage is — a building — into a property. The rest of that quotation is faithful and idiomatic; only the noun needs changing.

### Finding 3 — source support: the author's voice presents a next building as settled

**Draft (line 25):** "Lind har en lärdom att ta med till nästa fastighet."

**Source:** "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." / Lind: "I would set that time aside before the next building starts."

**What differs — modality, and whose assertion it is.** Lind's own sentence is conditional and hers; the draft's narration states, unhedged, that there is a next building for the lesson to be carried to. The material has the expansion undecided and pending a check.

**Case where the draft fails and the source holds:** the team checks how the categories work for larger repairs, finds them unusable there, and drops the log; there is no next building. The material's sentences are all still true, and Lind's conditional remains what she said; "en lärdom att ta med till nästa fastighet" is false. No supplied statement excludes the case — "the team will decide after checking" leaves both outcomes open.

**Smallest supported repair:** "Lind har en lärdom att ta med om teamet går vidare." (with the same *hus* correction if a building is named instead).

## 3. Translation check of the quotations (read in Swedish first, then against the source)

- **Quotation 1** — "Vi ville att kvällspasset skulle se vad morgonpasset redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." Reads on one pass in Swedish; *kvällspasset/morgonpasset* for the crews on those shifts is a metonymy Swedish uses itself, so it is not an obstruction. Against the source: motive, ownership of the categories and Svale's helping (not leading) role are all preserved, including the concessive force of the semicolon clause. No finding.
- **Quotation 2** — "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att föra in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan vi drar igång i nästa fastighet." Idiomatic, V2 correct after the fronted object. The added *vi* names an agent the material settles. The noun *fastighet* is the one defect — see Finding 2.
- **Quotation 3** — "Jag skulle välja att göra om testet. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." Idiomatic; nothing a Swedish reader stops at. Both conditionals, the unhedged present-tense benefit and the *men*-reservation sit exactly where the source has them, so the qualified assessment is not upgraded into an endorsement. No finding.

No quotation was welded together from separate remarks, no hedge was removed or added, and no self-correction is involved.
