## 3. Quotation translation check

Each quotation was read in Swedish alone first, then set beside the English.

**Quotation 1 (l. 22).** Swedish alone: "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." One pass, no point at which a Swedish reader must supply something the sentence withholds: both shifts are named, *dem* is settled by *kategorierna*, *loggen* by the deck and body. Against the source: same want, same past perfect in the subordinate clause, same past-tense ownership claim (*var våra* ← "were ours"), same division of labour (*hjälpte oss att lägga in* ← "helped us put them into"). Stance — matter-of-fact ownership, no blame of the supplier — preserved. Certainty unchanged; nothing added or removed. Idiomatic Swedish speech, not English syntax. **No finding.**

**Quotation 2 (l. 36).** Swedish alone: "Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus drar igång." *Lägga tid på* and *avsätta tid* are the ordinary Swedish collocations; the comparative *mer … än på* closes properly; *den tiden* points back to the time just named. *Nästa hus drar igång* is the source's own metonymy ("before the next building starts"), and Swedish has the same figure to reach for — *nästa etapp/nästa hus drar igång* is said in exactly this project sense — so it carries over rather than leaving a verb its subject cannot perform. Against the source: the comparison, the lesson, and the conditional *skulle* all hold; the stance is self-critical about the team's own planning, not about the supplier, and stays so. **No finding.**

**Quotation 3 (l. 40).** Swedish alone: "Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en extra vecka för förberedelser." Reads in one pass; *en samlad bild av anmälningarna* is a single consolidated view of the reports, which is what the log provides, and *men* carries the reservation. Against the source: conditional endorsement ("I would choose") kept conditional; the present-tense benefit ("helps us") kept present and kept hers; the qualification ("but I would leave an extra week for preparation") kept as a qualification, rendered as *lägga till en extra vecka*, which allocates the same week the source sets aside. Nothing sharpened into a recommendation of the supplier. **No finding.**

Across the three: no quotation is welded together from separate remarks, none is a paraphrase presented as speech, no hedge is removed or added, and each contributes experience the surrounding narrative does not carry — the shift problem in its own terms (1), the cost of agreeing on categories (2), and the qualified verdict with its reservation (3). All three are set with the speech dash, which the Swedish guidance makes the default for reported speech.

## 4. Considered and set aside (with the reason)

Recorded so the judgement is visible, not reported as defects:

- **"hösten 2025" (l. 10).** Set aside by "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial…", "The trial lasted eight weeks." and the note dated "4 December 2025": every placement of the eight weeks compatible with those statements falls in the Swedish autumn.
- **"två av sina hus" (l. 10) implying more than two buildings.** Set aside by "before the next building starts" and "The trial has not yet expanded".
- **"byggde" vs "designed" (l. 10).** Set aside by "The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log" — the team's part covers devising them and putting them in, so no reading of *byggde* takes work from the supplier's column or gives it the team's.
- **"redovisas inte" vs "is available" (l. 18).** The draft asserts strictly less than the source; no compatible case makes it fail.
- **"de" in "vad de mätte" (l. 10).** The dominant reading is the customer, and the body attributes the figures to Elm Quay's own note; no measuring claim about Svale is asserted. An editorial question about precision, not a support defect.
- **"arbetena" (l. 30).** Definite plural at first mention, but the sentence is a list of what was not measured about the repairs, and the reading is settled by the paragraph. A wording preference, not an obstruction.
- **Present tense "ger hon inte" (l. 40) against past "did not recommend".** It sits in the same reporting frame as *säger* in the same sentence, which is the ordinary Swedish journalistic present for an interview stance.
- **Length.** The draft runs shorter than the brief's "Approximately 400 words, as material allows" — the direction the contract requires when material is thin, and it adds nothing to reach the figure. Whether to name what further material would close the gap is a drafting decision outside this comparison, not a support defect.
- **Supplier-published status.** The draft nowhere claims independence, so no unsupported claim arises from the material's "it is not independent journalism"; whether to disclose it is an editorial decision outside this comparison.
