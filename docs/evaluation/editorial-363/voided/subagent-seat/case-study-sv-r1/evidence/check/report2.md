## 2. Translation check on the quotations

Each quotation was read in Swedish alone, then set beside the English.

**Q1** — "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Swedish-only reading: no verb whose subject cannot perform it, no adverbial without its event, no unintroduced reference; *dem* takes *kategorierna* from the same sentence. Compounds *kvällsskiftet*, *morgonskiftet* written as one word. Meaning, stance (the categories claimed as the team's, the supplier's role limited to help) and certainty are carried. No finding.

**Q2** — "Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus drar igång."
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
Swedish-only reading: *Den tiden* points back to the time just quantified; the fronted object takes V2 correctly (*Den tiden skulle jag avsätta*). *nästa hus drar igång* was tested as a possible obstruction — a building cannot itself get going. The source has the same metonymy ("the next building starts"), and Swedish reaches for that figure itself in property and construction speech (*när nästa hus drar igång*, *nästa etapp drar igång*), so the reader supplies nothing the language does not supply. *drar igång* is a shade more colloquial than "starts"; it is speech, and it neither adds nor removes meaning, stance or certainty. Not a finding.

**Q3** — "Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en extra vecka för förberedelser."
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Swedish-only reading: idiomatic, no obstruction. *en samlad bild av anmälningarna* renders "one view of the reports" without narrowing or widening it. *lägga till en extra vecka* renders "leave an extra week" — both are scheduling one further week; neither adds a claim. The conditional *skulle* keeps her hypothetical stance in both sentences, and the reservation after *men* survives intact, as the material requires. No finding.

Nothing was added to, removed from or welded between quotations; all three appear whole, in the material's wording, set with speech dashes as Swedish ordinarily sets reported speech.

## 3. Findings

### Finding 1 — source support: *de* in the deck leaves the measuring unattributed, with the supplier as the nearest reading

**Draft:** "Underhållsteamet byggde sina egna kategorier, Svale Systems konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare. Här är vad **de** mätte, vad mätningen inte kan svara på …"
**Source:** "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered." — the figures are Elm Quay's. The material nowhere says Svale measured anything; its supplier sentences are "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions." The brief adds: "The customer, Elm Quay Housing, should remain the acting party."

**What is missing or changed:** *de* is third person plural. The intended referent, Elm Quay or its maintenance team, is not plural in the draft's own text — *Underhållsteamet* is neuter singular (*det*, as the draft itself writes two sections later: "när **det** har kontrollerat"), and the company is singular. The two plural-compatible antecedents actually standing in the sentence are *Svale Systems* (the nearest clause subject, and Swedish does take a named company with *de*) and *sex medarbetare* (the nearest plural nominal).

**Case in which one holds and the other fails:** a reader who takes *de* as Svale Systems reads the deck as promising what the supplier measured. Nothing in the material supports a supplier measurement; the median times and the 31-report count come from Elm Quay's internal note, which is also the party that withholds the attribution to the software. Compatible with the material, a supplier could have run its own measurement and reached a different figure — the material neither describes nor excludes it — and the draft's deck would then be saying something the note cannot support. Taken the other way, a reader who takes *de* as the six trained staff reads the measuring as done by trainees rather than recorded in the company's note. No supplied statement excludes either reading: the material never says who did the measuring, only whose note reports it.

**Smallest supported repair:** name the party. "Här är vad Elm Quay mätte, …" (or "vad Elm Quay Housing mätte"). This keeps the deck's rhythm, keeps the customer as the acting party per the brief, and matches the note's ownership of the figures.

## 4. Editorial questions (not defects, not repairs)

- **Supplier publication is not disclosed.** The material says "The supplier approved publication of this case as supplier-published material; it is not independent journalism." The draft misrepresents nothing — it never claims independence, and the byline is supplied by the invocation — but it also carries no note of who publishes it. Whether a supplier-published case should say so is a publishing decision, not a claim the draft gets wrong, so it is recorded here rather than as a finding.
- **Length.** The brief asks for approximately 400 words "as material allows"; the draft runs shorter. Whether the material allows more is an editorial judgement, and the Claims contract forbids closing such a gap by invention, so no repair is proposed.
