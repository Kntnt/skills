## 2. Quoted speech

**Q1 — "We wanted the rotas settled before the winter. They were, just about. After that the depot stopped ringing round at six in the morning," said depot manager Ruth Calder.**
- Wording: returns unchanged, attribution included.
- One pass: yes, for a British reader. *They were, just about* is the ordinary English ellipsis of *they were settled*, the comma carrying the qualification; *ringing round* is common British idiom for phoning a list of people; *the depot* for its staff is everyday metonymy; *After that* points back to the rotas being settled. Nothing has to be supplied.
- Unchanged and unobstructed, so nothing was owed as a finding.

That is the only passage of quoted speech. The reported-speech sentence about Calder did change — see difference 7 — but it is not quoted speech, and the quotation itself was not touched, so no passage of speech supplies an event, fact, name or figure the rest of the text does not carry.
