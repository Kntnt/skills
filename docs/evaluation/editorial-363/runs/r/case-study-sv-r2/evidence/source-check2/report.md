Draft: `draft.md` (Swedish, `sv`). Material: `material.md` (English). Contracts: `claims.md`, `quotation-guidance.md`, `composition-sv.md`.
Source language English, target language Swedish; quotations are translated, and translation of them is permitted by the material ("Translation of the quotations is permitted, preserving stance and qualification").
The byline "Av Thomas Barregren" comes from the invocation, not the material, and is excluded from this accounting by instruction.

## 1. Claim accounting

Each row: the draft passage (draft line), the supplied passage it rests on quoted in the source's own words, and what differs in thing named or measured, subject, scope, time, modality, certainty or whose assertion it is.

### Title and deck

**1.1 — L1 "Elm Quay samlade felanmälningar från två hus"**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." / "Elm Quay's internal trial note ... says that 31 repair reports were entered."
Differs: nothing load-bearing. Subject Elm Quay (the acting party the brief requires); thing named: reports gathered in the shared log; number two buildings; time past (preterite, matching a trial that had ended before the 4 December note). No causal, evaluative or comparative word. "Felanmälning" and "repair report" name the same thing here: every report in the material is a resident's or a caller's report of something to repair ("Median time from report to assignment", "kept telephone reporting open for residents"), and nothing in this context falls under one term and not the other.

**1.2 — L3 "Telefonanmälningar och mejl lagrades var för sig."**
Source: "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately."
Differs: the deck sentence carries no *tidigare/dittills*. The Swedish preterite plus the following sentence ("prövade ... att samla ... i en gemensam logg") places it before the trial, and L7 states "hade dittills lagrats" explicitly. Scope, subject and time otherwise identical. Not a defect.

**1.3 — L3 "Hösten 2025 prövade Elm Quay Housing under åtta veckor att samla felanmälningar från två av sina hus i en gemensam logg från Svale Systems."**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." + "The trial lasted eight weeks." + "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025 ..." + "It chose Svale Systems".
Differs: "Hösten 2025" aggregates the supplied dates. It is entailed, not added: the decision is September 2025 and the note of 4 December 2025 reports the finished trial, so an eight-week trial must have begun between September and 9 October and ended inside the autumn. Duration "under åtta veckor" exact. "En gemensam logg från Svale Systems" is supported by the choice of Svale plus "Svale configured the log" and the note's reference to "the software"; no claim of effect is attached. Modality: *prövade* (trialled), matching "trial", with no claim that the arrangement was adopted.

**1.4 — L3 "från två av sina hus"**
Source: "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats." / "a shared repair log in two buildings".
Differs: the possessive *sina*. Read beside L7's "som förvaltar 640 lägenheter", *sina hus* is the ordinary Swedish way of saying the buildings in the company's charge, not an assertion of title; the draft never claims ownership in any other place, and it renders "manages" as *förvaltar* rather than *äger*. Defensible as written; recorded, not a finding.

**1.5 — L3 "Här får du veta vad förvaltningens underhållsgrupp gjorde, vad försöksnoteringen visar och vad den inte visar."**
Source: "its own maintenance team"; "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says ..."; "The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."; "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
Differs: *förvaltningens* introduces no new party — the subject of the preceding clause in the same deck is Elm Quay Housing, which the draft elsewhere says *förvaltar* the flats, so the management whose maintenance team this is is Elm Quay's own, as "its own" requires. "Vad den inte visar" is weaker than the source (the material says the measurements do not exist at all, the deck only that the note does not show them); a weaker statement entailed by the source is not a defect. *Du* is the composition contract's default address and carries no claim about the reader beyond the conditional at L29.

## 2. Person, number and natural gender

Identifying a referent is accounted for separately from the features the pronoun asserts.

- **L15 "skriver hon", L27 "Hon rekommenderar"** — third person singular, natural gender female. Asserted by the draft, and supported independently of referent identification by the material's own pronouns: "**Her** final quotation is **her** actual, qualified assessment." No finding.
- **L27 "när den har kontrollerat"** — *den* resumes *underhållsgruppen*. Purely grammatical gender (common-gender noun); it asserts no natural gender and no personhood. No finding.
- **L3, L29 "du", "om du överväger"** — second person singular address to the reader, the composition contract's default; the only claim attached is conditional. No finding.
- **Quotations "Vi", "oss", "våra", "vi börjar"** — first person plural. Source: "**We** wanted", "helped **us**", "The categories were **ours**", "**We** spent". Number and person preserved; see 3.2 on *vi börjar*.
- **Quotations "Jag", "jag"** — first person singular. Source: "**I** would set", "**I** would choose", "**I** would leave". Preserved, including the conditional mood in each.
- **L11 "de boende", L21 "de boendes nöjdhet"** — third person plural, definite. Source "residents", "resident-satisfaction". Number preserved; the definite form scopes them to the buildings under discussion, which is the source's scope.
- **L7 "bestämde sig ... gruppen", L11 "sex medarbetare"** — number preserved (six), no gender asserted of any of them.
- **L13 "Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet"** — source "maintenance supervisor Maya Lind". Name, role and singular number preserved; the apposition asserts no rank beyond "supervisor".

## 3. Translated quotations

Each was first read in Swedish alone, with the English out of sight, and only then set beside the source.

**3.1 — L13**
Swedish-first reading: "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." One pass, no stop. *Kvällsskiftet/morgonskiftet* standing for the people on those shifts is a metonymy Swedish uses itself ("nattskiftet jobbar över"), so it is not an obstruction. *Kategorierna* arrives definite with no prior mention of categories in the article; a Swedish reader bridges it from the log in the same breath ("lägga in dem i loggen"), exactly as the English reader bridges "The categories were ours", so this is not a reference the sentence never introduces.
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Meaning, stance, certainty and voice: the credit claim ("were ours") keeps its emphasis in *var våra*; Svale's role stays help, not authorship; nothing is added, narrowed or sharpened. Attribution *skriver ... i ett mejlsvar* is supported by "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind" plus "All interviews occurred by email", and it signals what the material actually is, as the quotation contract asks. No finding.

**3.2 — L15**
Swedish-first reading: "Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att mata in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan vi börjar i nästa hus." One pass. The fronted *Den tiden* is V2-correct and is a natural spoken topicalisation; *den tiden* resumes the time just named.
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
One rendering decision to record: the source's "before the next building starts" has the building as subject. Swedish has no ready figure for a house that begins; *innan nästa hus börjar* is precisely a verb whose subject cannot perform it, and would be a concrete obstruction. The draft says what the speaker said the way Swedish says it, *innan vi börjar i nästa hus*, and the referent is settled by the supplied context — she speaks throughout as the maintenance team's *vi*, and the material puts the expansion decision with that team. Making it explicit is permitted here and adds no meaning the speaker did not supply. Conditional *skulle* preserves "would". No finding.

**3.3 — L25**
Swedish-first reading: "Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." One pass, no stop.
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Meaning, stance, certainty and voice: the conditional stays conditional in both clauses, the *men* reservation stays where the speaker put it, and the endorsement stays the qualified one she gave rather than a recommendation. "One view of the reports" is rendered *en samlad bild av anmälningarna*; in this context — one log replacing separately stored telephone reports and emails — nothing could fall under one description and not the other, and the Swedish is what a Swedish speaker would say. "Leave an extra week" as *lägga in en extra vecka* keeps the act of allocating time, matching *avsätta* in 3.2 without flattening either. Nothing additive. No finding.

Placement of the three: each quotation carries experience the narration does not — the shift-handover motive, the effort split between agreeing categories and entering reports, and the qualified would-do-it-again. None restates its surroundings.

## 4. Cases tested and set aside

Each case below was constructed as compatible with the material, then excluded by a supplied statement or by the draft's own wording.

- *The buildings are owned by a third party, so "sina hus" fails* — the draft renders "manages" as *förvaltar* at L7 and claims no title anywhere; *sina hus* here reads as the buildings in the company's charge. Set aside.
- *A comparison with another supplier exists but is unavailable, so the draft overstates* — the draft says only *redovisas inte*, which is entailed by "No comparison with another supplier is available". Set aside.
- *Svale, not the customer, kept telephone reporting open* — the draft names no agent, so it asserts neither; the statement it does make is entailed by "The maintenance team ... kept telephone reporting open for residents". Set aside, with the observation at 1.12.
- *Lind has since changed her mind, so the present tense at L27 fails* — excluded by "Her final quotation **is** her actual, qualified assessment", which gives the reservation as standing. Set aside.
- *The two medians are the writer's own computation rather than the note's* — excluded by "the note explicitly does not attribute **the difference** to the software", which places the difference, and so both medians, in the note. Set aside.
- *The heading pair implies the software shortened assignment time* — excluded by the draft's own text: the heading names workload difference rather than improvement, and the express non-attribution stands in the same sentence as the figures. No causal verb and no causal hedge appears anywhere in the draft. Set aside.
- *"Tilldelning" has drifted to completed work* — excluded by L21's own second sentence, which names *tiden till färdigt arbete* as unmeasured. Set aside.

## 5. Findings

**Source support: none.** No draft passage was found that states more, less or other than the material supports in thing named or measured, subject, scope, time, modality, certainty or whose assertion it is, and no passage lacks a source passage. Every qualification the material attaches — the exclusions behind the count of 31, the different workloads, the note's express non-attribution, the three absent measurement types, the absent supplier comparison, Lind's withheld blanket recommendation, and the un-expanded trial with its pending decision — appears in the draft attached to the claim it qualifies.

**Translation: none.** The three translated quotations express the same meaning, stance, certainty and distinctive voice in idiomatic Swedish, and a Swedish reader takes each in one pass. The one place where the source's figure has no Swedish counterpart — a building that "starts" — is resolved by saying what the speaker said in the way Swedish says it, with a referent the supplied context settles. No meaning is changed in crossing languages outside quoted speech either.

**Editorial questions / the brief's own unevidenced statements: none requiring report.** No passage sits on the unsettled line between a standpoint and a factual assertion: the valuations in the draft are all inside quotation marks and attributed to Maya Lind, and the narrated facts are all document- or material-backed.

Two non-defect observations are recorded above for the author's judgement rather than as findings: the dropped agent at 1.12, where the s-passive leaves it open who kept telephone reporting open while the brief asks that the customer remain the acting party; and the definite *Kategorierna* at 3.1, which the article first introduces inside the quotation because the narration omits "The maintenance team designed its categories". Neither changes a claim and neither stops a reader.
