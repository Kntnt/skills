All present, all attached to the claim they qualify, including *uttryckligen*.
- Assignment time is never promoted to completion time: *från anmälan till tilldelning* stays, and the next sentence names *tiden till färdigt arbete* as unmeasured.
- No causal verb, no causal hedge; no cost, satisfaction or rescue claim; no rescued-customer framing.
- The reservation survives twice — inside Q3's *men* and in *Hon rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsföretag*, with the scope of the negation intact.
- *Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör redovisas inte* states less than "No comparison … is available"; unknown is kept apart from absent.
- Publisher stance is stated: *Den här kundberättelsen publiceras av Svale Systems*. The material's added gloss "it is not independent journalism" is dropped, but the operative disclosure stands and no independence is claimed.
- Link text and URL match the supplied destination, described as a checklist to read.
- *Hösten 2025* is bounded by the September decision and the 4 December note, so it is a supported placement rather than an invented date.
- *sina hus* asserts no title the material denies; the same sentence group renders "manages" as *förvaltar*.

**Caution — the agent of the open telephone line.** *Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen. Försöket pågick i åtta veckor, och telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende.* The material says "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents." The agentless s-passive sits two clauses after a sentence whose subject is Svale, and a Swedish reader carries that subject forward, so the placement invites the reading that the supplier kept the residents' phone line open. Nothing in the draft says so, which is why I record this as a caution and not as a false claim, but it is the one place where an act the material assigns to the customer drifts toward the publisher. The same section never states in narration that the team designed its own categories; that fact reaches the reader only inside Lind's quotation.

**Byline.** *Av Thomas Barregren* is an attribution the material does not carry — the brief says "No author name supplied." The reply states it comes from the invocation and tells the user to change the line before publication, so it is disclosed, but on the material alone it is an addition.

**Tense.** *Hon rekommenderar inte Svale* moves the material's "Lind did not recommend" into a standing present. Defensible: the material calls the final quotation "her actual, qualified assessment" in the present, and her own words are present and conditional. Not a finding.

## 3. G2 — pass, with reservations

Headline, standfirst, byline, lead, three sections and an ending, in that order. As a customer account: situation (separate storage of telephone reports and emails), action (the group's decision, the choice after its own test, the eight-week trial, Svale's configuration and training), results (31 reports with exclusions, the two medians with the workload caveat and the note's non-attribution, three absent measurement types), appraisal (Q3), publisher stance, and a call to action built from the one supplied link.

Two parts do not fully earn their separation. The standfirst opens *Telefonanmälningar och mejl lagrades var för sig* and the lead's second sentence repeats it as *Telefonanmälningar och mejl hade dittills lagrats var för sig* — the same fact in the same words inside sixty words, so the lead's middle does no new work. The lead also re-states the 640 flats, the two buildings and the choice of Svale, which the standfirst has already carried; what remains new in it is only the September date and the status test.

The customer is the acting party across the piece — *bestämde sig underhållsgruppen*, *Valet föll på Svale Systems, sedan gruppen testat*, *underhållsgruppen tar ställning* — but the whole setup section is written without a customer subject: an agentless heading (*Så sattes den gemensamma loggen upp*), an agentless passive for the phone line, and Svale as the only named actor. That section reads as the supplier's section in a piece whose subject should be Elm Quay.

Smaller: the close says *Läs implementeringschecklistan* with a definite form for a thing the article has never mentioned and without saying whose it is, and gives the address as a bare URL rather than as a link; the reader meets a definite reference and an unlabelled address in the last line of the piece.

## 4. L1 — pass

Idiomatic Swedish throughout, with no English syntax showing through. V2 holds after every fronted element (*I september 2025 bestämde sig underhållsgruppen…*, *Från anmälan till tilldelning var mediantiden…*, *Hösten 2025 prövade Elm Quay Housing…*). Compounds are single words (*felanmälningar*, *arbetsbelastning*, *mejlsvar*); double definiteness is marked (*den gemensamma loggen*, *de boendes nöjdhet*); the s-passive is the unmarked one; *tillskriver uttryckligen inte programvaran skillnaden* is the correct frame. The headings are finite and carry information (*Noteringen jämför två perioder med olika belastning*, *Nästa hus får vänta på ett besked*), and the last of them is a genuinely Swedish turn of phrase. Attribution is *skriver … i ett mejlsvar*, which names the written channel the material states.

The speech marker is an em dash (—) where Swedish sets a speech dash; that is locale mechanics, not L1, and I do not count it here.

Reader effect: the piece reads quickly and honestly, and the second section in particular leaves an operations manager knowing exactly what the figures do and do not show.

## 5. Quoted speech

**Q1 — `— Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen, skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet, i ett mejlsvar.`**
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Almost one pass. The shift metonymy is one Swedish uses itself and costs nothing. The reader does check at *Kategorierna*: the article has not mentioned categories anywhere before this line, so the definite form is the first mention, and the reader has to supply which categories — *de kategorier gruppen tagit fram* — which the same sentence then hands over with *lägga in dem i loggen*. A recoverable half-beat inside the sentence, not a stop that breaks the reading. Meaning, stance and certainty match: the ownership claim keeps its emphasis in *var våra*, Svale's role stays help rather than authorship, nothing is hedged or sharpened. Voice intact.

**Q2 — `— Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att mata in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan vi börjar i nästa hus, skriver hon.`**
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
One pass, no stop. The fronted *Den tiden* with V2 inversion is ordinary Swedish and resumes the time just named. The source's "before the next building starts" has no Swedish counterpart with the building as subject — *innan nästa hus börjar* would be a verb whose subject cannot perform it — and the draft says it the way Swedish says it, *innan vi börjar i nästa hus*. Making the agent explicit adds no meaning she did not supply: she speaks throughout as the team's *vi*, and the material puts the expansion with that team. The comparison, both its terms and the conditional *skulle* are hers.

**Q3 — `— Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, skriver Maya Lind.`**
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
One pass, no stop. Both conditionals stay conditional, the benefit stays present and first-person plural, and the *men*-clause lands as a real reservation attached to the endorsement rather than stranded after it. *En samlad bild av anmälningarna* renders "one view of the reports" at the level the material establishes and no higher.

**Missing quotations:** none. All three supplied quotations appear, none welded, trimmed or sharpened, and no fourth is assembled. Each carries experience the narration does not — the shift-handover motive, the effort split, the qualified would-do-it-again.

Every passage of quoted speech in this draft is speech a Swedish reader takes on one pass with the source's meaning, stance, reservation and voice intact, the one friction being the definite *Kategorierna*, which the article never introduces outside the quotation and which the same sentence recovers.

## 6. Intermediate

First comparison (`evidence/source-check/report.md`), two findings:

1. Title *Elm Quay samlade felanmälningarna från två hus* and the standfirst's *att samla felanmälningarna* — allegation: the Swedish definite plural claims the two buildings' reports as a closed set, which the material does not support, since it counts 31 entered reports and names emergencies as excluded. Writer dropped the definite in both places. **Supported repair** — the finding is right (out-of-hours emergency calls on the open phone line would falsify the closed set while every supplied statement held), and the repair is the smallest one that closes it.
2. *säger Maya Lind* / *säger hon* / *säger Maya Lind* — allegation: the material says all interviews occurred by email, and an unqualified *säger* asserts spoken delivery. Writer changed all three to *skriver* and named the channel once (*i ett mejlsvar*). **Supported repair**, and it takes the stronger of the two repairs the report offered.

Second comparison (`evidence/source-check2/report.md`): no source-support findings and no translation findings; seven cases constructed and set aside, and two non-defect observations recorded for the author — the dropped agent in *telefonanmälan hölls öppen*, and the definite *Kategorierna* introduced first inside the quotation. I agree with the seven set-aside cases on my own reading of the source.

Both observations in the second report are the two places where I find real, if mild, weakness — see the caution under F1 and the half-beat under Q1 in point 5. So no defect I found went entirely unseen: a checker saw both, but classed each as an observation carrying no repair, and the writer left both as they stood. On the first of them I would have gone further than the checker did, because the identical passive is the one place in the piece where an act the material assigns to the customer can be read as the supplier's, and a one-clause repair (naming the maintenance group as the subject, which would also introduce the categories before the quotation needs them) was available at no cost to any other claim.

## 7. Delivery

**A valid delivery.** Nothing in the draft claims more than the material supports, no caveat is dropped, no forbidden inference appears, the reservation survives, and the three quotations are idiomatic Swedish with stance and qualification intact. Both findings raised against the first draft were real and were repaired at the source, and the fresh comparison that read the repaired prose found nothing to carry forward. What the run left on the table is editorial rather than factual: an agentless setup section that reads as the supplier's, a standfirst and lead that repeat one sentence between them, and a closing line that points at *implementeringschecklistan* without having introduced it.
