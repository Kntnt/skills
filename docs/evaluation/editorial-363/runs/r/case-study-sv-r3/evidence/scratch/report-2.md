# Source-support and translation comparison — case-study-sv-r3

Draft: `scratch/draft.md` (Swedish). Material: `work/source.md` (English, the entire package). Context: `scratch/checker-context.md`.

Method: every factual and attributed claim in the draft — title, deck, byline, headings, body, quotations, closing note and link text — is set beside the source passage it rests on, quoted in the source's own words. Each pair is tested in both directions with a concrete case compatible with the material. Source-support findings and translation findings are reported separately.

## 2. Person, number and natural gender conveyed by pronouns

Accounted separately from referent identification.

- "sina" (deck, "två av sina hus") — 3rd person reflexive possessive, singular possessor (bostadsbolaget). Source: "its own maintenance team … in two buildings". Person and number match the company; the possession of the buildings is discussed at D2. No natural-gender assertion.
- "Det" (body, "Det valde Svale Systems") — 3rd person singular, grammatical neuter, referent "Underhållsteamet". Source: "It chose Svale Systems". Person and number match; the neuter is grammatical agreement with *team*, not a natural-gender assertion. Ambiguity of parse discussed at §5.2.
- "det" ("när det har sett") — same referent, same features, same grammatical neuter.
- "hon" (twice: "vad hon skulle göra", "sammanfattar hon testet") — 3rd person singular, **natural** feminine, referent Maya Lind. The material asserts the same natural gender in its own voice: "**Her** final quotation is **her** actual, qualified assessment." Supported as a natural-gender claim, not merely as a referent identification.
- "Vi", "oss", "våra" inside quotations 1–3 — 1st person plural, the speaker with her team. Source: "We wanted …", "helped **us**", "The categories were **ours**", "We spent …", "helps **us**". Person and number match exactly.
- "jag" inside quotations 2–3 — 1st person singular. Source: "**I** would set that time aside", "**I** would choose", "**I** would leave". Match.
- "du" ("Vill du gå vidare") — 2nd person singular address to the reader. Not a claim about any person in the material; the Swedish composition guidance makes *du* the default address.
- "hon" is the only natural-gender assertion in the draft; no pronoun ascribes gender to Elm Quay Housing, the team, Svale Systems or the note.

## 3. Translated quotations — read in Swedish first, then against the source

Each rendering was read on its own in Swedish, looking for a verb whose subject cannot perform it, a time or place adverbial whose event is missing, or a reference the sentence never introduces. None was found; then each was set against the English.

**Q1.** "— Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Swedish-first reading: idiomatic; the shift metonymy (*kvällsskiftet* for the staff on it) is one Swedish uses itself. "loggen" and "Svale" are both introduced by the surrounding article before the quotation. Against the source: meaning, tense sequence ("redan hade gjort" for "had already done"), the emphatic ownership of "Kategorierna var våra", and the limited role given to Svale ("hjälpte oss att lägga in dem", not *skapade* or *valde*) are all preserved. Nothing added. No finding.

**Q2.** "— Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan starten i nästa hus."
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
Swedish-first reading: idiomatic; the fronted object with V2 ("Den tiden skulle jag avsätta") is ordinary Swedish emphasis, and "starten i nästa hus" names an event the article has set up (the trial ran in two buildings, expansion is pending). Against the source: the comparison of the two time expenditures, the modality "skulle", and the forward-looking remedy are intact. "avsätta" renders "set aside" without adding a recommendation to anyone else. No finding.

**Q3.** "— Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Swedish-first reading: idiomatic and readable on one pass. Against the source: the stance (would do it again), the qualification ("men …"), and the certainty of "hjälper oss" as a plain present are all preserved; "en samlad bild av anmälningarna" renders "one view of the reports" without widening it into a claim about results. The hedged assessment the material insists on ("her actual, qualified assessment") survives. No finding.

No quotation was welded together from separate remarks, none had a hedge removed or added, and no fourth quotation was invented.

## 4. Findings

### Source-support findings

**Finding 1 — the title asserts an achieved effect the material gives only as a want and as the supervisor's assessment, and widens it to all repair reports.**

- Draft: "# Gemensam logg gav överblick över felanmälningarna" (line 1).
- Source, in its own words: "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information."; "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."; "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions."; Lind: "Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."; the note "says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."
- What differs: **modality and whose assertion** — the material has an intention, a capability that was tested, and one qualified first-person assessment; the title states the outcome flatly, in the publisher's voice, in the preterite. **Scope** — "felanmälningarna", definite plural, is the repair reports as a class; the only figure in the material is a count of 31 that explicitly excludes emergencies.
- Case in which the draft fails and the material still holds: during the eight weeks, emergency reports continued to be taken by telephone and dispatched to whoever was on shift without ever being entered in the log, and the log therefore held only the 31 non-emergency reports the note counts. Then a shared log existed and helped the team, as Lind says, yet it did not give an overview of *the repair reports*. Nothing supplied excludes this case: the material ties the exclusion of emergencies to the count ("It excludes emergencies …") and never says that every report went into the log.
- Case in the other direction: if a reader takes the title only as Lind's view, the title as written is not what she said — she said one view "helps us", present tense, alongside a reservation and a refusal to recommend Svale to every housing company; "gav överblick" reports a finished effect with neither hedge nor speaker.
- Smallest supported repair: attribute it — "Arbetsledaren: gemensam logg ger oss en samlad bild av anmälningarna" — or state the tested act instead of the effect: "Gemensam logg prövades i två hus under åtta veckor". Either keeps the deck and body untouched.
- Note: the body is clean on this point. The deck makes no overview claim, and no sentence in the body credits the software with the shorter assignment time; the reservation and the workload difference are both carried in the same paragraph as the figures. Finding 1 is confined to line 1.

## 5. Cases considered and set aside

**5.1 The missing "tidigare" in the opening line.** "Telefonanmälningar lagrades för sig, e-post för sig." carries no time adverbial where the source has "Previously". A reading in which this describes the arrangement *during* the trial would change a claim. It is set aside because the following sentence — "bestämde underhållsteamet i september 2025 att testa en gemensam logg" — supplies the before-state, and the preterite excludes the present. Defensible as written; if the author wants the marker, "Tidigare lagrades telefonanmälningarna för sig, e-posten för sig." keeps V2 and costs one word.

**5.2 "Det valde Svale Systems" as a possible agent reversal.** With V2, a fronted object reading ("Svale Systems chose that") is formally available and would reverse who chose whom. Set aside: there is no choosable propositional antecedent in the preceding sentence, and the non-finite clause "efter att ha prövat om loggen kunde visa status" takes its subject from the matrix subject, which the material fixes as the customer. A Swedish reader lands on the team without stopping.

**5.3 "med 640 lägenheter" versus "manages 640 flats" (deck).** Considered as an ownership claim; set aside because "ett bostadsbolag med 640 lägenheter" covers a managed portfolio in Swedish and the body states "förvaltar" in the material's own terms.

**5.4 "mättes inte" versus "There are no … measurements".** Considered as a strengthening from *not supplied* to *not done*; set aside because the material marks the availability reading explicitly where it intends it ("No comparison with another supplier **is available**") and does not here.

**5.5 Length.** The draft runs roughly three-quarters of the brief's "approximately 400 words, as material allows". That is the material's limit rather than a claim defect, and the contract permits delivering the length the material supports. Outside this comparison; noted only because the contract also asks that the gap be named where it matters.
