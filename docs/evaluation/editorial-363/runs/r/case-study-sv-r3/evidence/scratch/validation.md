# Validation of the two comparison reports

## Report 1 (`report-1.md`) — accounting validated

Coverage: title, standfirst, byline, lead, four subheadings, every body paragraph, the three
quotations, the disclosure and the link each have a pairing with a source passage quoted in the
source's own words. Pronoun person, number and natural gender are accounted separately from referent
identification. No accounting mismatch detected.

Dispositions:

1. **Accepted.** `Valet föll på en logg som kunde visa status` — the material says only that the team
   tested *whether* the log could show status ("after testing whether the log could show the status of
   each repair"); the heading asserted the result. Nothing supplied settles the test's outcome.
   Repaired to `Loggen prövades innan leverantören valdes`, which asserts only the test and the order.
2. **Accepted.** `vad ett eget test skulle kräva` described the destination as a trial guide; the
   material calls it an "implementation checklist" and "a document to read". Repaired to
   `Vill du gå vidare, är nästa steg att läsa …`, which carries the material's "optional next step"
   and its document-to-read character without naming a trial.
3. **Accepted.** `Nästa steg avgörs av de större reparationerna` turned "the team will decide after
   checking how the categories work for larger repairs" into a determinant and dropped the deciding
   party. Repaired to `Utvidgningen är inte avgjord än`, which states the material's "has not yet
   expanded" and nothing about what decides.
4. **Accepted in part.** The title widened Lind's "one view of the reports" to an overview of the two
   buildings. Repaired to `Gemensam logg gav överblick över felanmälningarna`, which narrows the scope
   to the reports. The report's editorial question A (whether a supplier-published case may state its
   customer's benefit unattributed in a headline at all) is not a defect: the genre resource requires
   that "the headline names the customer's benefit or result".
5. **Accepted.** "No comparison with another supplier **is available**" is about what the material
   offers; `finns inte` asserted non-existence. Repaired to `redovisas inte`.
6. **Accepted.** `ärende` in a *logg för felanmälningar* covers a report that needs no repair;
   "each repair" does not. Repaired to `varje reparation`.

Editorial question B (`ställde in loggen` admitting a cancellation reading) was taken up in the same
pass; the subheading was rewritten for other reasons. Editorial question C was set aside with the
report's own evidence: the lead states `förvaltar`.

No translation findings; the three quotations are used whole, with stance, conditional and
qualification intact.

## Report 2 (`report-2.md`) — accounting validated, one finding stands

Coverage: complete, including the four repaired passages. No accounting mismatch detected. The
report's §5 sets aside four candidate findings with supplied evidence, which this validation accepts.

**§5.5 is a counting error and is not carried forward.** The report says the draft "runs roughly
three-quarters of the brief's 'approximately 400 words'". The draft is 411 words as delivered. There
is no length gap to report.

**Finding 1 stands and is unresolved.**

- Draft passage: `# Gemensam logg gav överblick över felanmälningarna`
- The concrete problem: `felanmälningarna`, definite plural, is the repair reports as a class, and the
  title states an achieved effect flatly, in the preterite and in the publisher's voice.
- What the material carries instead: an intention ("The team wanted staff on different shifts to see
  the same information"), a capability that was tested ("after testing whether the log could show the
  status of each repair"), Lind's qualified present-tense assessment ("Having one view of the reports
  helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation"), and a count of 31 entered reports that
  "excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial".
- Why it is not rejected: the report's case — emergency reports taken by telephone and never entered
  in the log, so that the log held only the 31 the note counts — is compatible with the material, and
  no supplied statement excludes it. The material ties the exclusion of emergencies to the note's
  count and never says every report went into the log. There is therefore no exact source evidence
  with which to reject the finding.
- Smallest repairs the checker proposed: `Arbetsledaren: gemensam logg ger oss en samlad bild av
  anmälningarna`, or `Gemensam logg prövades i två hus under åtta veckor`.
- Why it is not repaired: this was the second and final comparison. Prose repaired after it would be
  prose no comparison has read, so the draft is delivered exactly as that comparison read it and the
  finding is reported beside it.
