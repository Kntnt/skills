The body, which had no sections, was sectioned. Every quotation, figure, reservation and limiting sentence survives untouched. The reply names both removals, names all three changed claims, and volunteers that "the maintenance team's supervisor" is "slightly more than the text states" — a level of self-report that deserves saying.

That is thin but not misleading, and neither asserts anything the text lacks.

What fails it is what shipped. The headline was not defective in its claim — "Elm Quay finds value in one view of repair reports" is supported by the standfirst and the quotations. The run diagnosed this precisely and then delivered it anyway, with the budget spent. A review that removes two pre-echoes and installs a misattribution in the headline has made the text less true where it matters most. R1 fails.
