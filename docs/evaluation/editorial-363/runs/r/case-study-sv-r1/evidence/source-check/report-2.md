## 1. Claim accounting

Each entry gives the draft passage, the supplied passage it rests on in the source's own words and language, and what differs (thing named or measured, subject, scope, time, modality, certainty, whose assertion).

### Title and deck

**1. `Elm Quay testade en delad felanmälningslogg` (title)**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." / "The trial lasted eight weeks."
Thing: *delad felanmälningslogg* = the shared repair log; the material establishes the log holds repair reports ("31 repair reports were entered", "whether the log could show the status of each repair"), so no case here falls under one term and not the other. Subject: Elm Quay, by way of "its **own** maintenance team" — the team is internal to Elm Quay, so the company is the acting party. Time: completed past, matching an eight-week trial closed by the 4 December note. Modality: flat, no causal verb, no effect claimed. **Supported.**

**2. `Elm Quay Housing förvaltar 640 lägenheter.`**
Source: "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats." Same measured thing (flats, not buildings), same number, same present tense. **Supported.**

**3. `Under åtta veckor hösten 2025`**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial…", "The trial lasted eight weeks.", "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025". Duration is stated. The placement in autumn is bounded on both sides by supplied dates: it cannot start before the September decision, and it is closed by a note dated 4 December. Every eight-week window inside that bound falls in the Swedish autumn. **Supported.**

**4. `lät bolagets eget underhållsteam två av husen dela på en enda logg för felanmälningar`**
Source: "its **own** maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings"; "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately."
*eget* carries "own". *två av husen* carries "two buildings". *en enda logg* asserts singularity, which the prior separate storage plus "a shared repair log" carries. Subject and agency: the team, as in the source. **Supported.**

**5. `med Svale Systems som leverantör`**
Source: "It chose Svale Systems…"; "Svale configured the log and trained six staff"; brief: "The publisher is the supplier, Svale Systems." Role named is supplier, not consultant or operator. **Supported.**

**6. `Här är hur teamet lade upp arbetet, vad den interna anteckningen redovisar om tiderna och vad arbetsledaren skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång.`**
Source: the setup paragraph; "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says…"; "Median time…"; "I would set that time aside before the next building starts."; "I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Whose knowledge: the times are given as the note's, matching the material. *annorlunda nästa gång* rests on two would-statements that are changes to what was done. **Supported.**

### Opening section

**7. `I september 2025 bestämde sig underhållsteamet hos Elm Quay Housing för att pröva en gemensam logg för felanmälningar i två av bolagets hus.`**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings."
Time, subject, scope and modality (a decision to trial, not a deployment) all carry. *bolagets hus* is read against the draft's own preceding sentence, *förvaltar 640 lägenheter*, which fixes the relation as stewardship, not ownership. **Supported.** (See §5.1.)

**8. `Dittills hade telefonanmälningar och mejl lagrats var för sig.`**
Source: "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately." Background claim; same two channels, same separateness, same anteriority (*dittills* = up to that point). **Supported.**

**9. `Teamet ville att personal på olika skift skulle se samma information.`**
Source: "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information." Motive claim, attributed to the team as in the source, not to the supplier. **Supported.**

### `Valet föll på Svale efter en egen kontroll`

**10. Heading**
Source: "It chose Svale Systems **after** testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
*efter* preserves sequence only; no causal verb and no causal hedge is added, so the Claims prohibition on deriving cause from sequence is respected. *en egen kontroll* attributes the testing to the customer, matching the implied subject of "after testing", which is the same "It" that chose. **Supported.**

**11. `Underhållsteamet prövade om loggen kunde visa status för varje reparation innan det bestämde sig för Svale Systems.`**
Source: same sentence as above. *om loggen kunde visa status* keeps the indirect question — what was tested is whether the capability existed, not that it was proved. Scope: *varje reparation* = "each repair". Order: draft fronts the test and uses *innan*; the source fronts the choice and uses *after*. Same chronology, same absence of cause. **Supported.**

**12. `Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte redovisad.`**
Source: "No comparison with another supplier is available."
State of knowledge: both are statements about the record, not about the world; the draft's *finns inte redovisad* is, if anything, the weaker of the two, and does not turn an absence in the record into a claim that no comparison was ever made. **Supported.**

**13. `Underhållsteamet utformade sina kategorier och höll telefonanmälan öppen för de boende.`**
Source: "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents." Reflexive *sina* carries "its"; the channel stays open rather than being reopened; beneficiaries are the residents. **Supported.**

**14. `Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två utbildningstillfällen.`**
Source: "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions." Count (six), session count (two), and the division of labour (supplier configures and trains; customer designs categories) all carry. The draft does not say whose staff, matching the material's silence. **Supported.**

**15. Attribution frame: `skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet på Elm Quay, i en intervju per mejl`**
Source: "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind"; "All interviews occurred by email."
Verb: *skriver*, not *säger* — correct for an email interview, and it signals what the material is, as the quotation guidance requires. Role and employer carry. **Supported.**

### `Siffrorna finns, men förklaringen gör det inte`

**16. Heading**
Source: "The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."; "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
The heading asserts the presence of figures and the absence of a supplied account of them. It adds no cause and no hedged cause. **Supported.**

**17. `Testet pågick i åtta veckor.`**
Source: "The trial lasted eight weeks." **Supported.**

**18. `Elm Quays interna anteckning från den 4 december 2025 redovisar 31 inlagda felanmälningar, exklusive akuta ärenden och arbeten som beställts före testet.`**
Source: "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."
Whose assertion: the note's, named. Figure: 31, unqualified by any evaluative characterisation (no *bara*, no *hela*), as Claims requires when no comparison or target is supplied. Both exclusions are carried and attached to the same count they qualify in the source. **Supported.**

**19. `Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar under testet och tre under de åtta veckorna dessförinnan.`**
Source: "Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period."
Measured thing: median, not mean; report-to-**assignment**, not report-to-completion — the forbidden conversion is not made. Units: working days, carried across the ellipsis to *tre*. Comparison period: the preceding eight weeks. Modality: flat statement, as in the source, inside a passage the next clause reattaches to the note. **Supported.**

**20. `Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte programvaran skillnaden.`**
Source: "The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."
Scope of negation: *uttryckligen inte* = explicitly refrains (the refraining is explicit), which is the source's reading; *inte uttryckligen* would have been the other and weaker one. Whose assertion: the note's abstention, named as the note's. **Supported.**

**21. `Kostnad, nöjdhet bland de boende och färdigställandetid är inte mätta.`**
Source: "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
All three items carry, with the same existential force the source uses here (contrast its weaker "is available" for the supplier comparison). This sentence is also what keeps the completion-time claim out of the draft. **Supported.**

### `Kategorierna ska prövas mot större reparationer`

**22. Heading**
Source: "the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs."
Thing tested: the categories, against the class of larger repairs. Modality: planned, not done. The agentless s-passive drops no qualification the body does not restore in the next sentence. **Supported.**

**23. `Testet har ännu inte utvidgats.`**
Source: "The trial has not yet expanded." *ännu inte* carries "not yet" — the state of knowledge is preserved, with no implication that expansion was refused. **Supported.**

**24. `Teamet ska bestämma efter att ha kontrollerat hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer.`**
Source: same sentence. Subject (the team), sequence (decide after checking), and the unstated object of the decision are all as supplied; the draft leaves the object implicit exactly as the material does. **Supported.**

**25. `Lind pekar på var tiden gick.`**
Lead-in to the quotation that follows. The fact it presents as given — that her remark is about where the time went — rests on "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports." **Supported.**

### Closing section

**26. Heading `Den samlade bilden hjälper Elm Quay, enligt arbetsledaren`**
Source: "Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Whose assertion: marked as the supervisor's by *enligt arbetsledaren*, so no technical effect is asserted in the draft's own voice. Subject: her *us* is glossed as Elm Quay; "its **own** maintenance team" excludes any reading on which her *us* falls outside Elm Quay. Scope: the heading carries only the first half of a two-part remark, and the reservation survives verbatim in the quotation directly beneath it and again in claim 28. **Supported.**

**27. `Hon rekommenderade inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag.`**
Source: "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company. Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment. It is not an inference from the figures."
Scope of negation: Swedish *inte … alla* is partial negation, matching "not … every"; it does not become "recommended to none". Time: past, tied to the interview. Whose assertion: hers, named. **Supported — this is the reservation the material requires to survive.**

**28. `Den här kundberättelsen är publicerad av Svale Systems och är inte oberoende journalistik.`**
Source: "The supplier approved publication of this case as supplier-published material; it is not independent journalism."; brief: "The publisher is the supplier, Svale Systems." **Supported.**

## 2. Grammatical modifiers and qualifications carried across clauses

- "its **own** maintenance team" → *bolagets eget underhållsteam* (deck) and *underhållsteamet hos Elm Quay Housing* (body). The ownership modifier survives where it matters, and it is what licenses Elm Quay as the acting subject of the title.
- "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial" — the exclusions attach to the 31-count in both texts.
- "two working days … and three" — the unit *arbetsdagar* is elided after *tre* in Swedish exactly as "working days" is elided after "three" in English.
- "not yet" → *ännu inte* (claim 23); "explicitly does not" → *uttryckligen inte* with the negation inside the explicitness (claim 20); "not … every" → *inte … alla* as partial negation (claim 27).
- "but I would leave an extra week" → *men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka* — the adversative that qualifies the endorsement is retained inside the quotation rather than split off.

## 4. Translated quotations

Each was first read in Swedish alone, with the English out of sight, then set beside the source.

**Q1 — `Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort … Kategorierna var våra. Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen.`**
Swedish-only reading: one pass, no stop. Every verb has a subject that can perform it; *dem* is introduced by *Kategorierna* in the immediately preceding sentence; no time or place adverbial is left without its event.
Against "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.": meaning, stance (ownership of the categories asserted, supplier role limited to help) and certainty (flat, unhedged) all match. The source semicolon becomes a full stop — a syntactic rendering choice that changes no meaning and adds nothing. The attribution is placed inside the first sentence and the remaining sentences run on under the speech dash, which is the ordinary Swedish convention the composition scope names. **No finding.**

**Q2 — `Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus kommer i gång.`**
Swedish-only reading: one pass. *Den tiden* is settled by the preceding sentence; the fronted object with V2 inversion (*Den tiden skulle jag avsätta*) is ordinary Swedish; *innan nästa hus kommer i gång* is read against the draft's own preceding sentence *Testet har ännu inte utvidgats*, which supplies the rollout the clause refers to.
Against "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.": the comparison (*mer … än*), its two terms, and the conditional/intentional stance (*skulle*) all match. *avsätta* is "set aside". **No finding.** (See §5.2 on the metonymy.)

**Q3 — `Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser.`**
Swedish-only reading: one pass, no stop; the *men*-clause lands as a real qualification rather than a politeness.
Against "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.": the endorsement stays conditional (*skulle välja*), the benefit stays present-tense and first-person-plural, and the reservation stays attached by *men*. "one view of the reports" → *en samlad bild av felanmälningarna* keeps the sense the material establishes — reports previously "stored separately", now in one log — rather than upgrading it to an analytic insight. Nothing additive: no fact, figure, name or connection the speaker did not supply. **No finding.**

Quote approval is not claimed or treated as settled anywhere in the draft, which is where the guidance leaves it.

## 5. Considered and set aside

**5.1 `i två av bolagets hus` (claim 7).** A genitive could be read as ownership, and the material says only that Elm Quay "manages 640 flats". The case — buildings owned by a third party and only managed by Elm Quay — is excluded for the reader by the draft's own immediately preceding sentence, *Elm Quay Housing förvaltar 640 lägenheter*, which fixes the relation as management before the genitive appears. Defensible as written; not a finding.

**5.2 `innan nästa hus kommer i gång` (Q2).** The source's "before the next building starts" is a metonymy — the building stands for the rollout in it. Swedish reaches for the same figure with *komma i gång* over a process or a unit of work, and the referent is supplied two sentences earlier by *Testet har ännu inte utvidgats*. A reader does not stop to supply anything the language withholds there, so this is not a concrete obstruction; preferring *innan vi drar i gång i nästa hus* would be a synonym preference. Not a finding.

**5.3 `hösten 2025` (claim 3).** Bounded by the September decision and the 4 December note, as set out at claim 3. Not a finding.

**5.4 `Den samlade bilden hjälper Elm Quay` (claim 26).** The widening from her *us* to the named company is excluded by the supplied "its **own** maintenance team", and the claim is carried as hers by *enligt arbetsledaren*. Not a finding.

## 6. Findings

**Source-support findings: none.**

**Translation findings: none.**

Every factual and attributed claim in the draft, including the title, the deck and all four headings, rests on a passage in the supplied material at the same scope, time, modality and certainty, and with the same party standing behind it. The forbidden inferences are all absent: no causal verb or causal hedge links the software to the shorter assignment time, assignment time is never turned into completion time, no cost, satisfaction or rescue claim appears, and the customer's reservation survives twice — once inside Q3 and once in the draft's own sentence at claim 27. Supplier narration stays in the third person throughout; first person appears only inside Elm Quay's quotations.
