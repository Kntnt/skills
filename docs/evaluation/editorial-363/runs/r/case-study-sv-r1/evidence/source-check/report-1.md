## 4. Translated quotations — read in Swedish first, then against the source

Each quotation was read in Swedish with the English out of sight, then set beside it.

### 4.1 L15 — "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra. Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."

*Swedish-only reading:* idiomatic spoken Swedish; V2 holds; *skulle se* is the natural complement after *ville att*. One stop, at **"Kategorierna var våra"**: the definite article promises categories the reader has already met, and the draft's narration has not mentioned any. See Finding 2; the defect is in the narration, not in the rendering of the words.
*Against the source:* meaning, stance and the possessive claim ("were ours") are preserved; *hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen* keeps Svale's role auxiliary, as the speaker had it. Nothing additive. **No translation finding in the rendering itself.**

### 4.2 L29 — "Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus kommer i gång."

*Swedish-only reading:* reads on one pass. The fronted object *Den tiden* with the verb second is ordinary Swedish emphasis, not English syntax. *Enas om* is the natural Swedish for reaching agreement on something. *Innan nästa hus kommer i gång* uses a building for the work in it; Swedish reaches for *komma i gång* with projects and stages of a rollout on its own, so this is a figure the language supplies rather than one imported. No obstruction.
*Against the source:* the comparison, the first-person plural, the conditional *skulle*, and the forward-looking scope ("before the next building starts") are all preserved. Nothing narrowed, widened or sharpened. **No finding.**

### 4.3 L33 — "Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."

*Swedish-only reading:* reads on one pass; the *men*-clause lands as the speaker's own reservation, which is how it must land. No verb without a subject that can perform it, no dangling adverbial, no unintroduced reference (*felanmälningarna* has been established throughout the article).
*Against the source:* "Having one view of the reports" → *Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna* keeps the single-consolidated-view sense; "helps us" keeps the beneficiary; "I would leave an extra week for preparation" → *lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser* keeps the conditional and the amount. The two *skulle* clauses keep her certainty exactly where she put it, and the rendering avoids turning a qualified endorsement into an unqualified one. **No finding.**

Attribution verbs (*skriver*, twice named, once *skriver hon*) correctly signal written email answers rather than speech, and the draft does not claim scene, emotion or remembered dialogue, none of which the material supplies. Quote approval is neither claimed nor treated as a solved problem.

## 5. Cases considered and set aside

**5.1 "hösten 2025" (L3).** The decision falls in September 2025 and the note reporting the finished trial is dated 4 December 2025, so the eight weeks lie inside that window. A start in early October would put the final days in December, outside the meteorological autumn. In ordinary Swedish usage *hösten* covers the whole term through early December, so the frame is defensible as written and the divergence is not one an ordinary reader would register. Not a finding.

**5.2 "Siffrorna finns, men förklaringen gör det inte" (L17).** Considered whether this denies an explanation the material offers, since "The periods had different workloads" names a candidate factor. The material states that factor as a reason not to attribute the difference to the software, not as an established explanation of it, and the heading asserts an absence rather than a cause. Defensible as written; not a finding.

**5.3 "programvaran" (L21).** First mention in the draft, and definite. This is narration, and the source's own first mention ("the software") is equally definite; the referent is settled by the log Svale configured. No meaning changes in crossing languages. Not a finding.

**5.4 "lät … två av husen dela på en enda logg" (L3).** Considered whether *lät* imports a permission relation the material lacks. The material has the team deciding to trial the log in two buildings, which is the same arrangement seen from the team's side. Defensible; not a finding.

**5.5 Third-person supplier narration and the link's destination.** Svale is narrated in third person throughout, and the link is described as a checklist to read, not a booking or a trial. Nothing to report.

## 6. Findings

### Finding 1 — source support: the agent who kept telephone reporting open is lost, and the placement transfers it to Svale
**Draft (L13):** "Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två utbildningstillfällen. Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende."
**Source:** "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents."
**What differs:** whose act it was. The agentless s-passive follows a sentence whose subject is Svale, and a Swedish reader carries that subject forward.
**Concrete case:** Svale had no part in the telephone channel — it configured the log and ran the training, and the maintenance team alone decided to keep residents' telephone reporting open. This case is not merely compatible with the material; the material asserts it. Under it, the reading the draft's placement invites ("Svale kept it open") fails while the source statement holds. In the other direction, a case where Svale kept it open is excluded by the supplied sentence naming the maintenance team.
**Smallest supported repair:** replace "Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende." with "Underhållsteamet utformade sina kategorier och höll telefonanmälan öppen för de boende." (This repair also closes Finding 2.)

### Finding 2 — the definite "Kategorierna" in the first quotation has no antecedent in the draft
**Draft (L15):** "Kategorierna var våra."
**Source:** the narration "The maintenance team designed its categories" precedes the quotation "The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
**What differs:** the draft drops the narration clause, so the quotation's definite noun phrase is the reader's first encounter with the categories.
**Concrete case / obstruction:** a Swedish reader who has read only "Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två utbildningstillfällen. Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende." meets *Kategorierna* and stops to ask which categories — a reference the sentence never introduces. The supplied context does settle it, so restoring it is permitted rather than invented. The rendering of the speaker's words is faithful; the defect is the missing introduction around it.
**Smallest supported repair:** as in Finding 1 — restore "Underhållsteamet utformade sina kategorier" ahead of the quotation.

### Finding 3 — source support: the section heading widens "helps us" to an unrestricted benefit
**Draft (L31):** "Den samlade bilden hjälper, enligt arbetsledaren"
**Source:** "Having one view of the reports helps **us**, but I would leave an extra week for preparation." / "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company."
**What differs:** the beneficiary, and with it the scope. The attribution to the supervisor is correct and preserved; what is not preserved is that her claim is about her own organisation.
**Concrete case:** the consolidated view helps Elm Quay, whose telephone reports and emails were previously stored separately and whose shifts could not see each other's work, and does not help a housing company that already has one channel. The material carries this case explicitly — Lind withheld a recommendation to every housing company, and the material marks that reservation as her actual, qualified assessment rather than an inference. Under this case, "Den samlade bilden hjälper" (unrestricted) fails while "helps us" holds. No supplied statement excludes it.
**Smallest supported repair:** "Den samlade bilden hjälper oss, enligt arbetsledaren" — one word, and it restores the source's beneficiary without touching the voice or the heading's shape.
