## 5. Quoted speech

**Q1 — `– Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort, skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet på Elm Quay, i en intervju per mejl. Kategorierna var våra. Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen.`**
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
One pass, no stop. *Kvällsskiftet*/*morgonskiftet* standing for the people on those shifts is a metonymy Swedish uses itself. *Kategorierna* arrives definite, but the narration two lines above has just said *Underhållsteamet utformade sina kategorier*, so the reference is introduced before it is used; *dem* is settled by it. Meaning, stance and certainty match: ownership of the categories asserted flatly, Svale's role limited to help. The source semicolon becomes a full stop — a syntactic choice that changes nothing. Voice intact.

**Q2 — `– Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus kommer i gång, skriver hon.`**
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
One pass. The fronted *Den tiden* with V2 inversion is ordinary Swedish and resumes the time just named. *Innan nästa hus kommer i gång* is the mild strain in the draft: a house cannot literally get going, but *komma i gång* over a unit of work is a figure Swedish uses, and the preceding *Testet har ännu inte utvidgats* supplies the rollout, so the reader carries on rather than stopping to supply a missing subject. The comparison and both its terms are hers; *skulle* keeps the conditional; nothing is sharpened into a plan.

**Q3 — `– Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, skriver Maya Lind.`**
