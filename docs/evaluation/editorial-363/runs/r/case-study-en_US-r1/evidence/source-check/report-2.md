## 4. Findings

**Source-support findings: none.**

**Translation findings: none** (no translation between languages was performed; American-English rendering checked above).

Two passages were examined as candidates and are reported here as editorial questions rather than defects, because each is defensible as written and neither has a repair that does not cost something supported:

- **Heading "How Elm Quay chose the log and set it up" (line 9).** The material assigns the configuration to the supplier: "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions." The heading is nevertheless a label for the whole setup episode — the decision, the categories the team designed, the training it took — and the brief requires that "The customer, Elm Quay Housing, should remain the acting party." The body two sentences below names Svale as the configurer, so no reader takes the configuration from the supplier. Raised only in case the writer prefers a heading that does not put *set it up* next to Elm Quay; nothing in the material obliges the change.
- **"she says" / "sets out" for emailed answers (lines 13, 29).** The material states that "All interviews occurred by email". *Says* is ordinary attribution for a supplied statement and asserts nothing about a spoken exchange, and the material supplies the exact wording ("complete usable quotations"), so the quotation guidance's paraphrase case does not apply. A house style that prefers *writes* for email answers would change this; the source support does not require it.
