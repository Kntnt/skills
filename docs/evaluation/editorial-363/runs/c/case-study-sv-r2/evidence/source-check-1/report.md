# Source-support and translation comparison — case-study-sv-r2

Draft language: Swedish (sv). Supplied material: English. Quotations in the draft are translations of the three supplied English quotations, which the material explicitly permits ("Translation of the quotations is permitted, preserving stance and qualification").

Scope: source support of every factual and attributed claim, plus, for the three quotations, whether the same meaning and voice are expressed in idiomatic Swedish speech. General editorial and mechanical review is outside this comparison; where such a matter is noted below it is marked as an editorial question, not a finding.

The byline "Av Thomas Barregren" comes from the invocation, not the material, and is no finding.

## 1. Claim accounting

Each row: draft passage → the supplied passage it rests on, quoted in the material's own words → what differs, and whether a case stands in which one holds and the other fails.

### Title and deck

**T1. "Gemensam logg samlade Elm Quays felanmälningar" (l. 8)**
Source: "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately. The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information."; "trial a shared repair log in two buildings"; "31 repair reports were entered"; quotation: "Having one view of the reports helps us."
Differs: **scope**. The genitive "Elm Quays felanmälningar" is definite in Swedish and reads as the company's repair reports as such; the material supports the reports of two buildings over eight weeks, with the note's count excluding emergencies and pre-trial work. Tense ("samlade", completed past) is supported — the trial is over. Causality: "samlade" asserts only consolidation, which is constitutive of a shared log, not an outcome; no forbidden causal claim. → **Finding 1.**

**T2. "Telefonsamtal på ett ställe, mejl på ett annat." (l. 10)**
Source: "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately."
No difference; the tenseless fragment reads as the prior state from the following sentence. Supported.

**T3. "Hösten 2025 testade underhållsteamet hos bostadsföretaget Elm Quay Housing en gemensam felanmälningslogg i två hus under åtta veckor." (l. 10)**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings."; "The trial lasted eight weeks."; "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025".
Differs: **time**, stated as "Hösten 2025" where the material dates the decision (September 2025) and the note (4 December 2025) but not the trial's start. Tested: any eight-week trial beginning no earlier than September and reported on by 4 December falls inside autumn 2025; no compatible case puts it outside. Supported.
"bostadsföretaget" rests on "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats" and the brief's "small housing companies" / "The customer, Elm Quay Housing". Supported.

**T4. "Här är vad teamet gjorde, vad den interna anteckningen från testet visar – och vad arbetsledaren Maya Lind skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång." (l. 10)**
Source: "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that…"; "maintenance supervisor Maya Lind"; "I would set that time aside before the next building starts."; "I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Differs: **modality of the document verb** — "visar" (shows) against "says". Tested: the note does contain the figures and the workload qualification it is credited with here, and the body attributes correctly ("Enligt … anteckning", "Anteckningen påpekar"); no case stands in which the note says these things and does not show them. Supported.
"skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång" is her own forward-looking stance in quotations 2 and 3. Supported.

### Opening section

**B1. "Elm Quay Housing förvaltar 640 lägenheter." (l. 14)** ← "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats." Identical in thing measured, subject, time and modality. Supported.

**B2. "I september 2025 bestämde företagets eget underhållsteam att testa en gemensam felanmälningslogg i två av husen." (l. 14)** ← "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." Supported; "två av husen" presupposes only that Elm Quay has buildings, which its 640 flats carry.

**B3. "Dittills hade telefonanmälningar och mejl lagrats var för sig" (l. 14)** ← "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately." Supported; pluperfect matches "Previously" relative to the September decision.

**B4. "och teamet ville att personal på olika skift skulle se samma information." (l. 14)** ← "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information." Supported, including subject (the team) and the modality of a want rather than an achievement.

### "Elm Quay prövade loggen före leverantörsvalet"

**B5. Heading (l. 16) and "Teamet undersökte om loggen kunde visa var varje felanmälan låg, och valde Svale Systems efter det." (l. 18)**
Source: "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
Differs on two axes, both tested:
- **Subject.** The material's "It" is third person singular with an ambiguous referent — the nearest antecedent is "The team", the alternative is Elm Quay Housing. The draft resolves it to "Teamet". A case in which company management, not the team, made the choice is compatible with the material; but the nearest antecedent is the team, the team is the material's decision-maker elsewhere ("its own maintenance team decided to trial"), and either referent keeps the acting party inside Elm Quay as the brief requires. Defensible as written; no finding.
- **Thing named.** "status of each repair" → "var varje felanmälan låg" (where each report stood). In this context the log is the repair-report log through which a repair's progress is tracked, so nothing here could have a report position without a repair status or the reverse. "Var ärendet ligger" is the ordinary Swedish for status tracking. Same thing in context; no finding.
Order of events ("före leverantörsvalet", "efter det") matches "chose … after testing". Supported.

**B6. "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör redovisas inte." (l. 18)**
Source: "No comparison with another supplier is available."
Differs: **whose knowledge**. The material speaks of availability; the draft speaks of what this text presents. Tested both directions: if no comparison exists, nothing is presented, so the draft holds; if a comparison exists somewhere but is unavailable here, the draft still holds. The draft's form is the weaker one and preserves the state of knowledge required by the Claims section ("what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened"). Supported.

**B7. "Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen." (l. 20)** ← "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions." Supported: agent, count, occasions.

**B8. "Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende." (l. 20)**
Source: "The maintenance team designed its categories and **kept telephone reporting open for residents**."
Differs: **whose act**. The s-passive drops the agent, in a paragraph whose only named agent is Svale and immediately after two Svale actions, so the implication an ordinary reader takes is that the supplier kept reporting open. → **Finding 2.**

**Q1. "– Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att få in dem i loggen, skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet." (l. 22)**
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."; "maintenance supervisor Maya Lind"; "All interviews occurred by email".
Meaning, stance and certainty preserved clause for clause; nothing added; ownership of the categories ("våra") and the limited supplier role ("hjälpte oss") both intact. The attribution verb "skriver" correctly signals the email interview. Speech dash rather than quotation marks, as Swedish prose ordinarily sets reported speech. Supported. Translation checked in §2.

### "Anteckningen jämför två perioder med olika belastning"

**B9. Heading (l. 24)**
Source: "Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period. The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."
That the note sets the two periods beside each other follows from its stating both figures and declining to attribute the difference between them. "Olika belastning" carries "different workloads" (spelled out as "arbetsbelastning" in the body). Supported — except for who states the workload difference, treated at B11.

**B10. "Enligt Elm Quays interna anteckning från den 4 december 2025 registrerades 31 felanmälningar under testet. Akuta ärenden och arbeten som beställts dessförinnan är undantagna." (l. 26)**
Source: "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."
Attribution, date, count and both exclusions carried across, with the exclusions kept as the note's ("är undantagna" in the attributed run). No evaluative characterisation of the count is added, as the Claims section requires. Supported.

**B11. "Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar under testet och tre under de åtta veckor som föregick det. Anteckningen påpekar att perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och den tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden programvaran." (l. 28)**
Source: "Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period. The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."
- Figures, the measured interval (report → assignment, not completion) and both periods: identical. The forbidden inferences — software causing the shorter time, assignment time turned into completion time — are avoided, and the non-attribution is reproduced with its "uttryckligen". Supported.
- Differs: **whose assertion**. The material states the workload difference flatly and attributes only the non-attribution to the note; the draft makes the note the asserter of the workload difference ("Anteckningen påpekar att"). → **Finding 3.**

**B12. "Kostnader, boendenöjdhet och tid till färdigt arbete har inte mätts." (l. 28)**
Source: "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
Differs: existence of measurements → the act of measuring. The material's "There are no … measurements" is flat, so a case in which they were measured is excluded by the supplied statement itself. All three subjects carried. Supported.

### "Beslutet om fler hus dröjer"

**B13. Heading (l. 30)**
Source: "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs."
Tested: does "dröjer" assert lateness against an expectation the material does not establish? "Beslutet dröjer" is the ordinary Swedish headline idiom for a decision still outstanding, and the section beneath it states only the pending check. Defensible as written; no finding.

**B14. "Ännu har försöket inte utvidgats. Teamet ska först kontrollera hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer och bestämmer därefter." (l. 32)** ← "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." Sequence, subject and the unsettled modality all preserved. Supported.

**B15. "Samma kategorier är det Lind pekar på när hon beskriver vad som tog tid." (l. 34)**
The author's own bridge, resting on quotation 2 ("We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports") and on B14's categories. Read as an ordinary reader would: the facts it presents as given are that the categories are what she points to and that they took time. The first is the quotation itself; the second rests on her "I would set that time aside" and "I would leave an extra week for preparation", which treat that time as substantial. A case in which both activities were trivially quick is excluded by her own setting-aside of a week. Supported.

**Q2. "– Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att mata in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus drar i gång, skriver hon." (l. 36)**
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
The comparison is kept as a comparison, not converted into an absolute; "den tiden" keeps the referent the source's "that time" has; the conditional "skulle" keeps it as intention, not a completed act. Nothing added. Supported. Translation checked in §2.

### "Så kan du väga ett eget försök"

**B16. Heading (l. 38)** — a promise about the article, not a claim about the world; "du" is the sanctioned Swedish address. Whether the section delivers a way to weigh a trial is an editorial question, not a source-support matter. No finding.

**B17. "Sitt eget omdöme om testet lämnar Lind med en reservation inbyggd." (l. 40)**
Source: "Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment. It is not an inference from the figures."; "The customer's reservation must survive."
The author's framing of supplied material; the fact it presents as given — that her assessment carries a qualification — is the material's own "qualified assessment". Supported, and the reservation survives into Q3.

**Q3. "– Jag skulle välja att göra om testet. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, skriver hon." (l. 42)**
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
"välja" keeps the endorsement an act of choice; the present tense "hjälper oss" keeps her claim about the shared view where the source has it, with "oss" holding it to her own team rather than to housing companies generally; the adversative "men" and the extra week keep the reservation. Nothing added, no hedge removed. Supported. Translation checked in §2.

**B18. "Någon rekommendation som gäller alla bostadsföretag ger hon inte." (l. 44)**
Source: "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company."
Differs: **scope of the negated object** — "Svale" → "någon rekommendation". Tested: a case in which she recommended something else to every housing company, outside the three usable quotations, is not excluded by any supplied statement, and under it the material holds while a literal reading of the draft fails. Set aside on the contextual reading the instruction requires: the sentence follows her assessment of this trial and precedes Svale's checklist, so the elliptical object an ordinary reader supplies is this approach and this supplier — exactly the endorsement the material bounds. Defensible as written; no finding.

**B19. "Funderar du på samma sak hos dig, kan du börja med att läsa igenom [Svales implementeringschecklista](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist)." (l. 44)**
Source (brief): "The optional next step is the supplied implementation checklist at https://example.invalid/svale/checklist; it is a document to read, not a consultation booking or a product trial."
URL identical; "implementeringschecklista" names a checklist; "läsa igenom" presents it as a document to read and promises neither a booking nor a trial; "Svales" rests on the publisher being the supplier and on the supplied URL. The invitation is conditional and asserts nothing about the reader. Supported.

### Supplier narration

Outside the quotations the draft uses no first person: Elm Quay and its team are the acting parties throughout, Svale appears in the third person ("Svale konfigurerade", "Svales implementeringschecklista"). No finding.

## 3. Findings

### Finding 1 — source support (scope): the title claims company-wide consolidation

- Draft: "# Gemensam logg samlade Elm Quays felanmälningar" (draft.md l. 8).
- Material: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log **in two buildings**."; "The trial lasted eight weeks."; "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."
- What is missing: the scope. In Swedish the genitive makes "Elm Quays felanmälningar" definite, so the title reads as Elm Quay's repair reports as such. The material supports two of the buildings of a 640-flat portfolio, over eight weeks, with the counted set excluding emergencies and pre-trial work.
- Case in which the draft fails and the material holds: Elm Quay's remaining buildings went on storing telephone reports and emails separately throughout the trial, and emergencies stayed outside the counted log. Nothing supplied excludes this; the material affirms the two-building scope and the exclusions. The company's repair reports were then not gathered into one log — only those of two buildings were.
- Mitigation, stated for the record: the deck names "i två hus under åtta veckor" one sentence later. The Claims section nonetheless extends the boundary explicitly — "This boundary holds in the title, summary, headings and body alike. Preserve chronology, scope and qualifications wherever a claim appears."
- Smallest supported repair: carry the scope into the title, e.g. "Gemensam logg samlade felanmälningarna i två hus".

### Finding 2 — source support (whose act): the customer's act is read as the supplier's

- Draft: "Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen. Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende." (draft.md l. 20).
- Material: "**The maintenance team** designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents."
- What is changed: the agent. The s-passive leaves the agent unstated in a two-sentence paragraph whose only named agent is Svale, directly after two Svale actions, so the implication an ordinary reader takes is that the supplier kept telephone reporting open. The material makes it the maintenance team's doing, and the brief requires that "The customer, Elm Quay Housing, should remain the acting party."
- Case in which the draft's implication fails and the material holds: Svale's standard configuration would have closed telephone reporting, and Elm Quay's team kept the channel open over the supplier's preference. Nothing supplied excludes this; the material assigns the act to the team.
- Smallest supported repair: name the agent the material names — "Teamet höll telefonanmälan öppen för de boende."

### Finding 3 — source support (whose assertion): a statement placed inside the note

- Draft: "Anteckningen påpekar att perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och den tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden programvaran." (draft.md l. 28).
- Material: "**The periods had different workloads**, and **the note** explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."
- What is changed: the workload difference is stated flatly in the material, which attributes to the note only the refusal to blame the software; the draft credits the note with pointing the difference out — a claim about the document's contents.
- Case in which the draft fails and the material holds: the note reports the two medians and, without discussing workloads at all, declines to attribute the difference to the software, while the differing workloads are established by Elm Quay's ordinary operational reporting. Nothing supplied excludes this; the material's clause carries no attribution.
- Alternative reading, stated for the record: the surrounding material sentences do recount the note's contents, so reading this clause as the note's too is not unreasonable. The repair costs nothing either way.
- Smallest supported repair: keep the flat statement and attribute only what the material attributes — "Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden programvaran."

### Translation findings

None. The two closest calls, both read in Swedish alone before the source was set beside them:

- "Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan **nästa hus drar i gång**" (Q2) for "before the next building starts". The source's metonymy — the building standing for its rollout — is one Swedish reaches for itself: "dra i gång" is the ordinary Swedish for a thing or a stage getting under way, and in a rollout context "nästa hus" is read as the next building's turn. A reader does not stop to supply a word Swedish withholds here, so this is not a concrete obstruction; "innan vi drar i gång med nästa hus" is a preferred synonym, not a repair.
- "Jag skulle välja att **göra om testet**" (Q3) for "I would choose to do the trial again". "Göra om" has a second, alter-it reading, but with a definite event as its object the repeat reading dominates in Swedish (as in "göra om provet"), and the retained "välja" keeps the source's endorsement rather than a rueful do-over. Stance, certainty and the following reservation are intact.

In all three quotations the Swedish is ordinary spoken Swedish rather than English syntax: V2 is observed throughout ("Den tiden skulle jag avsätta"), the comparison in Q2 keeps its Swedish "på att … än på att" frame, and no hedge is added or removed. The attribution verb "skriver" matches the material's "All interviews occurred by email", and no scene, emotion or remembered dialogue is supplied where the material offers none.

### Editorial questions (not findings, outside this comparison)

- The material states "The supplier approved publication of this case as supplier-published material; it is not independent journalism." The draft neither discloses Svale's publisher role nor claims independence. Nothing in the draft contradicts the material, so this is not a source-support defect; whether the genre contract requires the disclosure is for the genre review, not this comparison.
- Whether the section heading "Så kan du väga ett eget försök" is answered by the section beneath it is a structural judgement, not a claim about the material.
