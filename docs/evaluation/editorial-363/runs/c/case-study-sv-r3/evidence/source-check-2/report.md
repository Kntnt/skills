## 2. Source-support findings

### Finding 1 — "finns inte" turns an absence of evidence into an absence of fact

- **Draft:** "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte."
- **Source:** "No comparison with another supplier **is available**."
- **What differs:** whose knowledge it is, and modality. The material reports the state of the record available to the writer; the draft reports the state of the world.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay's maintenance team did run a short side-by-side against a second supplier before settling on Svale, but that comparison was never written up and was not handed over with this material. "No comparison with another supplier is available" is then true; "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte" is false. Nothing supplied excludes the case — the material says only that the team "chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair", which is silent on what else it looked at.
- **Contract:** "Preserve the state of knowledge: what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened."
- **Smallest repair:** "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte redovisad." (Alternatively "…finns inte tillgänglig.")

### Finding 2 — the count loses "entered", becoming a count of reports rather than of log entries

- **Draft:** "Den interna noteringen från den 4 december 2025 redovisar 31 felanmälningar under de åtta veckorna."
- **Source:** "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports **were entered**."
- **What differs:** the thing measured. The material counts reports entered into the log; the draft counts reports, full stop.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** during the eight weeks 38 fault reports reached Elm Quay, and 31 of them were entered in the log — the other seven came in by telephone late in a shift and were handled without ever being logged. "31 repair reports were entered" is true; "noteringen redovisar 31 felanmälningar under de åtta veckorna" reports a figure for the period that is wrong by seven. The case is compatible with the material, which says the team "kept telephone reporting open for residents" and lists only two exclusions — emergencies, and work ordered before the trial — neither of which covers an unlogged report.
- **Note:** the draft does carry both stated exclusions in the next sentence, which limits but does not repair the shift, because the exclusions are the note's and say nothing about what reached the log.
- **Smallest repair:** "…redovisar 31 felanmälningar i loggen under de åtta veckorna." (Or: "…att 31 felanmälningar registrerades i loggen under de åtta veckorna.")

### Finding 3 — the pending decision becomes a pending step

- **Draft:** "Försöket har ännu inte utvidgats till fler hus. Teamet ska **först** se efter hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer."
- **Source:** "The trial has not yet expanded; the team **will decide** after checking how the categories work for larger repairs."
- **What differs:** modality. The material leaves expansion undecided and names the check as what precedes the decision. The draft names the check as what precedes expansion: *först* answers "first, and then what?" with the expansion of the preceding sentence, so the decision itself disappears and expansion reads as scheduled rather than open.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** the team checks the categories against larger repairs in January, finds they break down on anything involving more than one trade, and decides to drop the log rather than take it into the next building. "The team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs" is true of that course; the draft's "har ännu inte utvidgats … ska först se efter" has told the reader that the check stands between the trial and its extension. Nothing supplied excludes the case: "will decide" is explicitly an open decision, and Lind's "before the next building starts" is her own conditional plan ("I would set that time aside"), not the team's decision.
- **Contract:** "The customer's reservation must survive" (brief); "Keep uncertainty where it exists" (claims).
- **Smallest repair:** "Teamet ska först se efter hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer och därefter ta ställning."

### Finding 4 — "varje ärende" widens "each repair" (minor)

- **Draft:** "Teamet valde Svale Systems efter att ha testat om loggen kunde visa status på **varje ärende**."
- **Source:** "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of **each repair**."
- **What differs:** the thing named. A term carried into another language names the same thing only where nothing in this context could fall under one term and not the other. In Swedish property management *ärende* is the generic word for any logged case; a repair is one kind of case, a report that turns out to need no repair is another.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** the team put the log through a check on repairs only — could it show, for each repair, whether it was assigned, under way or done — and never looked at what the log did with a report closed without a repair. "Testing whether the log could show the status of each repair" is true; "testat om loggen kunde visa status på varje ärende" claims a test that was not run. Nothing supplied excludes it; the material mentions no case type beyond repairs and repair reports.
- **Smallest repair:** "…om loggen kunde visa status på varje reparation."

## 3. Translation findings

Each translated quotation was first read in Swedish alone, with the source out of sight, and only then set beside the English.

**Quotation 1** — "– Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra, och Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Read in Swedish alone, it runs without a stumble; there is no point at which a Swedish reader must supply a word the language does not supply there. Against the source: meaning, stance and the division of labour are intact — the categories stay the team's, and Svale's role stays *helped*, not *made*. The source's semicolon becomes *och*; the juxtaposition's faint concessive edge is slightly flattened, but the sentence still says the categories were theirs and Svale only helped, so no stance has moved. **No finding.**

**Quotation 2** — "– Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus kommer igång."
Read in Swedish alone: fluent. *Innan nästa hus kommer igång* carries the source's metonymy — the building standing for the work in it — and Swedish reaches for that figure itself (*innan nästa etapp kommer igång*, *innan bygget kommer igång*), so it is not a place where a reader stops. A preferred synonym such as *innan vi drar igång med nästa hus* is not a finding. The conditional *skulle* preserves *I would*; the comparison (*mer tid på … än på …*) is exact. **No finding.**

**Quotation 3** — "– Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."
Read in Swedish alone: idiomatic, and the qualification lands where it should — the *men* clause is the last thing the reader holds. Against the source: *I would choose to do the trial again* → *Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket* keeps the conditional and the choosing; *one view of the reports* → *en samlad bild av anmälningarna* is the Swedish figure for the same thing and neither sharpens nor widens it; *helps us* stays *oss*, not generalised to housing companies at large; *I would leave an extra week for preparation* keeps both the conditional and the hedge that is the point of the remark. Stance — willing, with a reservation — is preserved, and nothing additive has entered. **No finding.**

**Speech presentation:** the quotations are set with a speech dash, which the Swedish composition guidance names as what an ordinary Swedish text reaches for first for reported interview speech; quotation marks are used nowhere. Attribution is *säger Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet* on first reference and *säger Lind* / *säger hon* afterwards, the ordinary Swedish pattern. The email origin of the interview is disclosed in the closing line, so *säger* does not misrepresent what the material is.

**No translation findings.**
