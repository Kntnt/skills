## 2. F1 — **Fail**, on four counts, and one of them removes a qualification the brief asks to be kept.

1. **"Teamet ska först se efter hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer."** The material says "the team **will decide** after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." The draft keeps the check and drops the decision. With *först* in front of it and "Försöket har ännu inte utvidgats **till fler hus**" above it, a reader takes expansion as scheduled and the check as a formality on the way. The material makes the expansion an open question, and the heading over the section — "Kategorierna ska prövas mot större reparationer" — repeats the same framing. This is the most consequential of the four: nowhere in the delivered text does the reader learn that Elm Quay has not decided. The second checker found it with the exact repair (*och därefter ta ställning*); it stands.

2. **"Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte."** The material says "No comparison with another supplier **is available**." *Finns inte* asserts that none exists. A compatible case: Elm Quay did compare two suppliers and simply did not supply the comparison. Absent is not the same as unknown, and the draft picks the wrong one. Found by the second checker (repair: add *redovisad*); unrepaired.

3. **"Den interna noteringen … redovisar 31 felanmälningar under de åtta veckorna."** The material says the note "says that 31 repair reports **were entered**." Without the entering, the figure reads as all fault reports received in the period — and the next sentence, "Akuta ärenden och arbete som beställts före försöket är inte medräknade", then makes the exclusions sound like exclusions from what happened rather than from what was logged. Found by the second checker (repair: add *i loggen*); unrepaired.

4. **"status på varje ärende."** The material says "the status of **each repair**." In property management *ärende* covers enquiries, complaints and administrative matters that are not repairs, and the material itself keeps repairs and reports of repairs apart when it counts "31 repair reports". Found by the second checker; unrepaired. The reply itself calls it minor, and it is.

A fifth, which no checker counted: **"ett bostadsbolag med 640 lägenheter"** asserts that the company has the flats, where the material says it "**manages** 640 flats". The draft's own standfirst says *förvaltar* one paragraph earlier, so the piece states the relation two ways within four lines. The second checker examined the phrase and set it aside on the ground that *med* does not assert title; I think it does — a *bostadsbolag med 640 lägenheter* is ordinarily understood to hold them — and the draft's own better wording sits right above it.

What holds:

- Every figure exact and uncharacterised: 640, two houses, eight weeks, six staff, two sessions, 31, two against three working days, 4 December 2025.
- The causal line is not crossed. The heading "Siffrorna säger inget om orsaken" denies the link rather than asserting it, and the body carries the workload difference and the note's explicit refusal ("noteringen kopplar uttryckligen inte skillnaden till programvaran").
- Assignment time is never turned into completion time: *från anmälan till tilldelning*, with *tid till avslutat arbete* only among the unmeasured.
- "Kostnader, nöjdhet hos de boende och tid till avslutat arbete mättes inte" carries all three absences.
- "Hon rekommenderar däremot inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag" keeps the negation's scope; the reservation survives here and inside the final quotation.
- *de boende* for "residents" asserts no tenure. *Telefonanmälningar och mejl sparades var för sig* renders "stored separately" without adding places to store them — the only one of the drafts I have read from this material that resists that embellishment.
- Supplier in the third person throughout; Elm Quay or its team is the subject of every act the material gives it, including "Telefonanmälan höll teamet öppen för de boende", which puts the customer back in the subject slot where a passive would have lost it.

## 3. G2 — **Pass**, with a weak standfirst.

All required parts are present and in order, and the customer account is complete: situation (separate storage), action (own decision, own test of the status display, own categories, telephone kept open), results (31 reports with exclusions, the two medians with the confound and the note's non-attribution, the three unmeasured things), appraisal (the final quotation with its reservation, plus "rekommenderar däremot inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag"). Elm Quay is the acting party. The call to action is built from the one supplied route and describes it exactly as the brief does — "[införandechecklistan] ett dokument att läsa igenom först", a document to read, not a booking and not a trial.

The **publisher stance** is carried and is the best-judged of these drafts: "Texten publiceras av Svale Systems, **som var leverantör i försöket**, och intervjun med Maya Lind gjordes via mejl." It names the publisher, names the stake, and discloses the channel in the same breath. It drops the material's "it is not independent journalism", which the first checker noted; what remains still tells the reader what they need.

The weakness is the **standfirst**. It opens on a bare fact — "Elm Quay Housing förvaltar 640 lägenheter." — which is the lead's work, not a standfirst's, and the lead then repeats both the 640 flats and the eight weeks two lines later. Its last sentence does the real job ("arbetsledaren har redan en sak hon skulle göra annorlunda"), and "Utfallet finns i en intern notering" oversells slightly, since what the note contains is two medians it declines to explain. The parts are distinguishable, so this passes, but the standfirst and lead are doing about one and a half jobs between them rather than two.

## 4. L1 — **Pass.**

The Swedish is clean and unforced. V2 holds at every fronting, including the *om*-less conditional ("Står du inför samma fråga i ditt eget bestånd, **är** införandechecklistan …"), which is a construction only a writer at home in the language reaches for. Compounds are solid and unsplit (*felanmälningslogg*, *arbetsbelastningen*, *mediantiden*, *införandechecklistan*, *kvällsskiftet*). Double definiteness holds (*den interna noteringen*, *de första anmälningarna*, *de åtta veckorna*, *ditt eget bestånd*). *Var för sig*, *dessförinnan*, *är inte medräknade*, *bestånd* are the words the Swedish property trade uses, and *bestånd* in particular is a native touch no translation would produce.

The verb in "noteringen **kopplar** uttryckligen inte skillnaden **till** programvaran" is Swedish, not a calque: *koppla X till Y* is correct, where *tillskriva X till Y* would not have been. "Vad loggen skulle lösa hade teamet redan bestämt" is a fronted object with correct inversion. The one wording I would query is *se efter hur kategorierna fungerar* — a shade domestic for a decision procedure, where *se över* or *kontrollera* would sit better — and that is a preference, not an obstruction.

Locale mechanics are set aside from this verdict.

## 5. Quoted speech

**Q1.** Draft: "– Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra, och Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen, säger Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet." Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
*One pass* — yes. *Meaning, stance, certainty* — intact: the pluperfect with *redan*, the ownership claim, Svale subordinate (*hjälpte oss*). The source's semicolon becomes *och*, which flattens the contrast a little — the semicolon sets "ours" against "Svale helped" and the *och* merely adds — but she still says the categories were theirs and that Svale only helped, so the stance does not move. *Voice* — hers.

**Q2.** Draft: "– Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus kommer igång, säger Lind." Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
*One pass* — yes. **"innan nästa hus kommer igång"** solves the line well: *komma i gång* is a figure Swedish uses for a thing or a stage getting under way, so the source's own ellipsis carries over, the reader supplies nothing, and nothing is supplied for her — the draft does not name what starts, which is right, because she did not. *Meaning, certainty, reservation* — the comparative frame is exact, *skulle* carries *would*, no hedge moved. *Voice* — plain and hers.

**Q3.** Draft: "– Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, säger hon." Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
*One pass* — yes. *Meaning, stance, reservation* — the conditional endorsement is kept with *välja att* rather than smoothed away; the benefit stays present tense and stays hers; the *men* clause is last, so the qualification the material insists on is the thing the reader carries out of the section. The heading above it, "Omdömet gäller inte varje bostadsbolag", frames it as her own qualified assessment rather than as an endorsement — the best framing of this quotation among the drafts I have read from this material.

**Missing quotations:** none; all three permitted quotations appear, none welded, none invented.

**Attribution.** *säger* three times, for answers the material says were given by email ("All interviews occurred by email"). The channel is disclosed — but in the article's last sentence, some thirty lines after the first *säger*. A reader meets "säger Maya Lind" three times taking her to have spoken, and learns otherwise at the end. This is a manner claim the material settles; the first checker did not raise it and the second explicitly cleared it on the ground that the closing line discloses the channel. I record it as a defect in attribution, not in the quoted words themselves, which are untouched.

**In one sentence:** every passage of quoted speech in this draft is speech a Swedish reader takes on one pass with the source's meaning, stance, reservation and voice intact; what has moved is outside the quotation marks, in a reporting verb that says she spoke.

## 7. Delivery

**A delivery that carried more than it should have.** No forbidden inference is present — no causal claim or hedge, no cost, satisfaction or rescue framing, assignment time never turned into completion time, the reservation intact in Lind's words and in a sentence of the draft's own, the supplier in the third person, the link honestly described, the publisher stance carried. The disclosure practice is the best of the drafts I have read from this material: four standing findings, each with its source passage and its minimal fix, and an explicit note that the two checkers disagreed about two of them. An editor with the reply in hand can repair all four in about a minute.

What makes it marginal is finding 3. Three of the four standing defects are small — a preposition, three words, one noun. The fourth deletes the fact that Elm Quay has not decided whether to expand, in a case study published by the supplier, where a reader takes a scheduled rollout as an endorsement the customer has not given. That is the material's own qualification, the brief says the customer's reservation must survive, and a four-word repair was on the table. With that one applied the run would have been a clean delivery; with it standing, the disclosure is doing work the text should have done.
