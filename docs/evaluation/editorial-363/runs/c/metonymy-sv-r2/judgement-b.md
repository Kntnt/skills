## 3. R1 — FAIL

The body of this text was left completely alone, and that is to the run's credit: both quotations, every figure, both comparison periods, the exclusion of the post-eight-o'clock sick calls, the note's refusal to credit the list with the difference, and the sentence listing the measurements that do not exist all came back verbatim. `säger rektor Nina Falk` is a small, correct repair that costs the text nothing. The headline was a genuine defect — present tense, whole school, unqualified — and the run identified it correctly and reported its removal accurately.

But two of the three changes leave the text with a fresh defect in place of the one they removed, and one visible defect was never found at all.

**The subheading swap is a regression.** `Arbetslagen utformade själva kategorierna` was wrong about who did the work; the sentence one line below it gives the right answer, `Skolan tog fram kategorierna för ämne och årskurs`, and that is where the repair was. Instead the section is now headed `Vikarieportalen gick igenom listan med sex medarbetare` — the section's own second sentence, nearly word for word, with the supplier as the actor. In a customer story, and above a section whose point is that the school made its own categories and did not get its routines in place in time, that heading is worse than the error it replaced. The run's own finding 2 says all of this, and says it well.

**The headline trades one fault for two.** `I vikarieförsöket tillsattes halva passen på en kvart` does confine the claim to the trial, which is the repair. It also stops naming the customer in a customer story and opens on a definite noun for something the reader has not been introduced to. Finding 1 states both, correctly. What the account does not state is that the new headline recasts a median as a proportion of shifts and carries none of the qualifications the body attaches to that number — the different sick-leave levels, the excluded emergency calls, the note's refusal to attribute the difference. The claim account says nothing stands with changed omfattning or säkerhet; that is true of the body and not quite true of the headline the run wrote.

**A third defect went unfound.** `## Skolan vill ge rutinerna mer tid` turns Falk's conditional — `jag skulle lägga en vecka till på rutinerna`, said about doing the trial again — into a present intention held by the school, and states the quotation's point directly above the quotation. It survives untouched and unreported.

So the delivered text has one clean small repair, one headline that fixes an overclaim while breaking the headline's own job, one subheading that hands the section to the supplier, and one untouched defect. The removals are both reported and both reported accurately; the changes the repairs introduced are reported for the two faults the run found and not for the claim the new headline makes. A text that arrived with three visible defects leaves with two new ones and one of the originals. Fail.
