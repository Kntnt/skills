## 1. Claim accounting

Every factual and attributed claim in the draft, beside the source passage it rests on, quoted in the source's own words. "Differs" records the thing named or measured, subject, scope, time, modality, certainty, or whose assertion it is.

### Title and standfirst

**1. `# Elm Quay samlade felanmälningarna i två hus`**
Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." / "31 repair reports were entered."

## 2. Person, number and natural gender

Accounted separately from referent identification.

- *det* ("innan det valde", "när det har kontrollerat") — 3sg, refers to *teamet*; Swedish neuter is grammatical gender here and asserts no natural gender. Matches the material's "It chose…", "the team will decide…".
- *vi / oss / våra* inside quotations 1–3 — 1pl, Lind speaking for her team; source "We wanted…", "helped us", "were ours", "We spent", "helps us". Person and number match.
- *jag* in quotations 2 and 3 — 1sg; source "I would set…", "I would choose…", "I would leave…". Match.
- *dem* ("lägga in dem i loggen") — 3pl, refers to the categories; source "put them into the log". Match.
- *du* ("Vill du förbereda… kan du läsa") — 2sg reader address, from `composition-sv.md` ("*Du* is the default address"), not a claim about any person in the material.
- *de boende* — 3pl, "residents". Match.
- **Maya Lind takes no pronoun anywhere in the draft**, so the draft makes no natural-gender assertion about her. Had it done so, "Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment" would have supported a feminine one. No unsupported natural-gender information is conveyed.
- No pronoun is used for Svale Systems; every reference is the name in the third person, and the supplier never narrates as "vi".

## 3. Translated quotations

Each read first in Swedish alone, then set beside the English.

**Quotation 1** — "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. … Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Swedish-only reading: idiomatic throughout; *kvällsskiftet/morgonskiftet* is the ordinary Swedish pairing, the past perfect *redan hade gjort* sits naturally under *skulle se*, and the emphatic *Kategorierna var våra* carries the same insistence a Swedish speaker would put there. Nothing obstructs.
Against the source: meaning, stance ("ours", firmly) and the division of labour (the team's categories, Svale's help) all preserved. Nothing additive; no hedge added or removed. **No finding.**

**Quotation 2** — "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar."
Swedish-only reading: the first sentence is idiomatic and the fronted *Den tiden* obeys V2. The reader stops at *innan nästa hus börjar*: in Swedish a *hus* does not *börja*, and the reader has to supply the word the sentence omits (the work, the rollout, the introduction). → **Finding F3.**
Against the source: content, comparison and conditional are otherwise exact; *avsätta* is the right verb for "set aside". No meaning, stance or certainty changed.

**Quotation 3** — "Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."
Swedish-only reading: idiomatic; *en samlad bild av anmälningarna* is what a Swedish supervisor would say for one shared view of the reports, and *lägga in en extra vecka* is ordinary. Nothing obstructs.
Against the source: the endorsement stays an endorsement, the qualification stays attached by *men*, and the conditional *skulle* keeps her modality. The material's requirement that this be her own qualified assessment rather than an inference from the figures is met, because the draft places it as speech and does not tie it to the median times. **No finding.**

## 4. Findings

### F1 — Source support: the trial's telephone reporting is given a duration the material does not state

Draft (line 24): "Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende **under hela försöket**."
Source: "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents."
What is missing: any statement of how long telephone reporting stayed open. The material gives three durations — "The trial lasted eight weeks", "during two sessions", "the preceding eight-week period" — and none of them is attached to telephone reporting.
Case where the source holds and the draft fails: the team kept telephone reporting open when the log went live and closed it in week six, once residents had got used to reporting through the log. Nothing supplied excludes this; "kept telephone reporting open for residents" states the arrangement, not its end date, and *hela* makes completeness an explicit claim.
Smallest repair: delete the modifier — "Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende."

### F2 — Source support: written email answers are attributed as speech

Draft (lines 20, 26, 36): "**säger** Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet", "**säger** Maya Lind" (twice).
Source: "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind, complete usable quotations" and "**All interviews occurred by email**, and no physical scene, emotion or remembered dialogue is provided."
What is changed: the manner of the utterance. `claims.md` makes circumstantial detail a claim — "duration, manner, motive, absence and background all need support" — and the material states the channel expressly. Calling the material "quotations" establishes that the words are hers; it does not establish that she spoke them.
Case where the source holds and the draft fails: Lind typed all three answers into an email reply and said none of them aloud. The material does not merely permit this case, it states it.
Smallest repair: change the attributing verb in the three tags from *säger* to *skriver* — "skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet", "skriver Maya Lind", "skriver Maya Lind". The words quoted, their order and their placement stay as they are. Whether written answers should then take quotation marks instead of the speech dash is a mechanics question and outside this comparison.

### F3 — Translation: "innan nästa hus börjar" obstructs a Swedish reader

Draft (line 26): "Den tiden skulle jag avsätta **innan nästa hus börjar**."
Source: "I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
What the English does: "the next building starts" is a conventional English project shorthand in which the site stands for the work at it, and an English reader passes over it.
Why Swedish stops: Swedish does not reach for that ellipsis with *börja*. A *projekt*, an *etapp*, a *bygge* or *arbetet* can *börja* or *dra i gång*; a *hus* cannot, and the reader has to supply the missing noun to make the clause mean anything. This is a place where the reader stops for a word the language does not supply there, not a preference between synonyms.
What the context settles: the material's "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs" settles that what would start is the rollout of the log to the next building, so the implicit referent may be made explicit.
Smallest repair: "Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan arbetet i nästa hus börjar." This keeps the speaker's unnamed agent. If naming the agent is acceptable — she says *vi* twice elsewhere in the same interview — "innan vi börjar med nästa hus" is the more colloquial option; either preserves meaning, stance and the conditional.
