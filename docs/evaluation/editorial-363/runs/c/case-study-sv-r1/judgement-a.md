## 2. F1 — **Fail**, on four counts.

1. **"i två av bolagets hus."** The material says the trial ran "in two buildings" and that Elm Quay "**manages** 640 flats". Nothing says the company owns the buildings. The genitive adds that. A compatible case: Elm Quay is a managing agent for associations that own the stock. The second checker found this; it is unrepaired, and the reply names it. Note that the headline and standfirst both say plainly "i två hus", so the draft contradicts its own, correct, wording four lines later.

2. **Heading: "Nästa beslut hänger på de större reparationerna."** The material gives a sequence — "the team **will decide after checking** how the categories work for larger repairs" — and names no criterion, threshold or consequence. *Hänger på* makes the check decisive. A compatible case: the categories pass easily and what actually settles expansion is next year's budget. Both checkers examined this heading and both set it aside on the ground that the body sentence beneath restores the sequence. I do not think that saves it: the claims boundary holds in headings as well as in the body, and a heading is read first and remembered longest. This is a defect, and it is the one my reading and both checkers' part company over.

3. **"om loggen kunde visa status för varje enskild felanmälan."** The material says "whether the log could show the status of **each repair**." A repair and a report of a fault are not the same object: one report can lead to several repairs, and the material itself distinguishes them by counting "31 repair **reports**" separately from the work. *Varje enskild* sharpens it further. The first checker noticed the substitution and set it aside as "the same thing in context"; the piece elsewhere keeps the two apart carefully, which makes the one place it does not stand out.

4. **"Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende."** The material assigns the act to the customer — "**The maintenance team** designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents" — and the brief asks that Elm Quay remain the acting party. The agentless s-passive names nobody, and the next sentence in the same paragraph opens with Svale as subject, so the nearest agent the reader has to hand is the supplier. The second checker found this with the right repair ("Teamet höll telefonanmälan öppen för de boende"); it is unrepaired and the reply names it.

Recorded, not counted:

- **"Vill du förbereda ett eget försök kan du läsa igenom [Svales checklista för införande]."** The second checker calls this a claim about what the checklist does. The link text is accurate and the brief does call the destination the optional next step for this reader; the lead-in is a fair reading of an implementation checklist offered to operations managers. **Disputed caution** — I would not have raised it.
- **"Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte i underlaget."** Accurate about the state of knowledge, and weaker than the material, which is the safe direction. But *underlaget* is the source package, a thing the published article's reader has never heard of; see G2.

What holds:

- Every figure exact and uncharacterised: 640, two, eight weeks, six, two sessions, 31, two against three working days, 4 December 2025.
- The causal line is not crossed. "Mätningen säger inget om varför tiden skilde sig" is a denial, not an assertion, and the body carries both the workload confound and the note's own explicit refusal ("noteringen förklarar uttryckligen inte skillnaden med programvaran").
- Assignment time is never turned into completion time: *från anmälan till tilldelning*, with *tid till avslutat arbete* named only among the things not measured.
- "Lind rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsföretag" keeps the negation's scope; the reservation survives here and in the final quotation. The present tense for the material's past is at the edge and I do not count it.
- "Försöket har ännu inte utvidgats, och teamet tar ställning när det har kontrollerat …" keeps the decision open in the body.
- Author perspective: supplier in the third person throughout; the publisher stance is carried in full, including "inte oberoende journalistik".

## 3. G2 — **Pass**, with two blemishes.

Every required part is present and in order, each doing its own job: headline, standfirst (three promises, no repeat of the headline), byline, lead, four sections, ending. The customer account is complete — situation, action, results, appraisal — Elm Quay acts throughout except at the passive noted under F1, the publisher stance is truthful and complete, and the call to action is built from the one supplied route and describes it as a document to read.

Blemishes:

- **"Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte i underlaget."** This is the article telling its reader about its own source package. An operations manager reading a published case has no *underlag*; they stop for a moment on a word that belongs to the commissioning note rather than to the piece. Two drafts of the same material solve this with *redovisas inte*, which says the same thing from inside the article.
- **The last section carries too much.** Under "Nästa beslut hänger på de större reparationerna" the final paragraph runs the appraisal, the reservation, the pending decision, the call to action and the publisher disclosure together in five sentences. The ending's job and the call to action's job get done, but in a single breath, and the disclosure — the one line the reader most needs to register — arrives last in a crowded paragraph.

Also worth naming: the heading **"Sex medarbetare fick utbildning vid två tillfällen"** puts a supplier action on the banner of a section whose other contents are the telephone channel and the trial's length. It is the weakest of the four, and it is the one place where the section's own job is hard to state.

## 4. L1 — **Pass**, with one grammatical slip.

The Swedish reads as Swedish. V2 holds throughout, including at the fronted conditional ("Vill du förbereda ett eget försök **kan** du läsa igenom …"). Compounds are solid (*felanmälningslogg*, *försöksnotering*, *underhållsteamet*, *arbetsbelastning*, *kvällsskiftet*). Double definiteness holds (*den gemensamma loggen*'s relatives: *de åtta veckorna*, *de boende*, *de första anmälningarna*). *Tar ställning*, *dessförinnan*, *var för sig*, *ingår inte* are the register's own words. The cleft-free, plain sentence rhythm suits a trade case study.

The slip: **"Kostnader, boendes nöjdhet och tid till avslutat arbete mättes inte."** *Boendes nöjdhet* is a bare genitive plural with no determiner; Swedish needs *de boendes nöjdhet*. It is a syntax error rather than a spelling or punctuation one, so it falls inside this criterion, and it sits in the sentence that closes the article's most important paragraph. One slip in a clean text.

Two things I checked and do not count: the semicolon inside Q1 (*Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss …*) is punctuation, which the criterion sets aside, though it is an unusual mark to find in Swedish reported speech; and *noteringen förklarar uttryckligen inte skillnaden med programvaran*, where *förklara … med* is correct Swedish for attributing a difference to a cause and carries the material's meaning without the English preposition.

## 5. Quoted speech

All three quotations are split across an attribution, with the second half continuing after it — the ordinary Swedish dash convention, and it reads as continued speech on one pass in each case.

**Q1.** Draft: "— Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort, skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
*One pass* — yes. *Meaning, stance, certainty* — intact: the pluperfect with *redan*, the flat ownership claim, Svale subordinate. *Voice* — hers; the semicolon is carried over from the English, which is a punctuation choice and not a shift in what she says.

**Q2.** Draft: "— Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första anmälningarna, skriver Maya Lind. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan **arbetet i** nästa hus börjar." Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
*One pass* — yes, and more smoothly than the bare "innan nästa hus börjar" it replaced; a Swedish *hus* does not itself *börja*. *Meaning, certainty, reservation* — the comparative is exact, *skulle* carries *would*, no hedge moved. *Voice* — intact. But the repair does settle something the speaker left open: "before the next building starts" leaves unnamed what starts — the rollout, the logging, the work — and *arbetet i nästa hus* names it as the work in the building. Both checkers judged the supplied context to settle it; I would call it a shade narrower than she said, and the smaller move was available (*innan nästa hus drar i gång*, *kommer i gång*), which is the Swedish figure for a unit coming onstream and leaves the ellipsis where she left it. Not a defect I press, but it is meaning added inside quotation marks to buy fluency.

**Q3.** Draft: "— Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket, skriver Maya Lind. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
*One pass* — yes. *Meaning, stance, reservation* — the marked *välja att* is kept rather than smoothed; the benefit stays present tense and stays hers; the *men* clause comes last and carries her qualification intact. *en samlad bild* is the Swedish figure for a consolidated view.

**Missing quotations:** none; all three appear, none welded, none invented. Attribution is *skriver*, which matches "All interviews occurred by email".

**In one sentence:** every passage of quoted speech in this draft is speech a Swedish reader takes on one pass with the source's meaning, stance, reservation and voice intact — with the single reservation that Q2's *arbetet i* resolves an ellipsis the speaker left open.

## 7. Delivery

**Valid delivery.** The article carries no forbidden inference: no causal claim or hedge about the software, no cost, satisfaction or rescue framing, assignment time kept distinct from completion time, the reservation surviving in two places, the supplier in the third person, the link honestly labelled, the publisher stance complete. The three repairs the first comparison earned were applied and all three were right. What stands unrepaired is named first in the reply, each with its source passage and its minimal fix, and the reply explicitly declines to claim source coverage for those paragraphs.

The reason I would still hold this one at arm's length is not the disclosed residue but the undisclosed part: "hänger på" in a heading and "status för varje enskild felanmälan" in the body are both defects that no checker put to the writer, so no editor reading the reply learns of them. The disclosed items — the passive, the genitive — are each a two-word fix; these two need a reader who goes back to the material.
