# Judgement — w-213109

## 1. Outcome

**Delivered**, with three disclosed unrepaired findings. **Two** full comparisons ran (`evidence/source-check-1/report.md`, `source-check-2/report.md`). The delivered prose is **identical** to the prose the second checker read (`source-check-2/draft.md` matches `delivered.md` from the headline down; only the `kntnt` frontmatter map is added).

## 2. F1 — **fail**

Four items, in descending order of weight.

**(a) "i två av bolagets hus" — ownership the material never gives.** The material says "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats" and locates the trial "in two buildings", whose owner it never names. The genitive *bolagets* makes the buildings the company's own. A managing agent whose 640 flats belong to the associations that engage it fits every supplied sentence and makes the draft's sentence false. The draft's own headline and standfirst already say "i två hus", which is the repair, and the run names it in the reply — and then ships the other wording.

**(b) "Nästa beslut hänger på de större reparationerna" — a sequence turned into a dependency.** The material says "the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." That fixes an order of events and names no criterion, no threshold and no consequence. *Hänger på* says the larger repairs settle the outcome. Nothing supplied says the check decides anything; expansion could turn on budget, on staff, on the two buildings' experience. Both checkers examined this heading and set it aside because the sentence below it restores the sequence. I do not accept that defence: a heading is read first and remembered longest, and the contract that holds scope and modality in the body holds in the heading too. This is the defect in the draft I would want fixed first after (a).

**(c) "om loggen kunde visa status för varje enskild felanmälan" — a changed term.** The material says the team tested "whether the log could show the status of each **repair**". A *felanmälan* is a fault report; a repair is the work. They do not overlay in either direction here: a report can be closed with no repair, and one repair can answer several reports, so status per report and status per repair are different capabilities. The draft's other sentences keep *reparationer* where the material has repairs ("hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer"), which shows the distinction was available. "Enskild" also adds a shade of emphasis the source does not carry.

**(d) "Telefonanmälan hölls öppen för de boende" — the acting party dropped.** The material attributes the decision: "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents." The agentless s-passive names nobody, and the next sentence of the same paragraph makes Svale the subject, so the nearest actor to hand is the supplier. The material's point is that the customer chose to keep the phone line open. The repair is four words, and the run names it.

**Checked and not counted against F1:**

- Headline "samlade felanmälningarna i två hus": the definite plural could be read as every report, and the note's 31 excludes emergencies — but that exclusion is stated of the count, not of the log, so the headline stands.
- "Lind rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsföretag": present tense for the material's past "did not recommend", but the material calls her final quotation "her actual, qualified assessment", which supports a standing position. Scope is right — *inte … alla* negates the universal, not the whole set.
- "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte i underlaget": weaker than "No comparison with another supplier is available", so nothing is added. It does break the fourth wall — see G2.
- "noteringen förklarar uttryckligen inte skillnaden med programvaran", "Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning", "Mätningen säger inget om varför tiden skilde sig": the note's refusal of cause is carried three times over. No causal verb and no causal hedge anywhere.
- Assignment time is never turned into completion time: "från anmälan till tilldelning" throughout, and "tid till avslutat arbete" appears only among the things not measured.
- "Svale Systems har godkänt publiceringen av det här kundcaset, som alltså inte är oberoende journalistik" carries both halves of the material's disclosure, including the half the material states last.
- Byline "Av Thomas Barregren" is not in the material; the reply states it comes from the invocation and must be changed before publication. Not counted.

## 3. G2 — **fail**

The parts are all present and in order: headline, standfirst, byline, lead, four sections, ending with the call to action. Two of them do not do distinct jobs, and one hands the customer's part to the supplier.

**The standfirst and the lead say the same thing.** Standfirst: "Ett underhållsteam hos bostadsföretaget Elm Quay Housing testade under åtta veckor en gemensam logg för felanmälningar i två hus." Lead: "I september 2025 bestämde sig underhållsteamet hos Elm Quay Housing för att pröva en gemensam felanmälningslogg i två av bolagets hus." Same actor, same company, same object, same two buildings, in the same order, forty words apart — and the headline has already said "samlade felanmälningarna i två hus". A reader is told the same fact three times before the piece has told them anything. The lead adds only the date and the previous state of the records; the standfirst adds only its second sentence. The lead should have started somewhere the standfirst did not go.

**"## Sex medarbetare fick utbildning vid två tillfällen"** banners the supplier's training over a section that is mostly about the customer's arrangements, and under it the only named actor is Svale ("Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare"), because the phone-reporting sentence lost its subject. In a customer case where the brief says the customer "should remain the acting party", one section in four has the supplier acting and the customer nowhere. The heading also does not describe its own section, which carries the phone line, the configuration, the training and the duration.

**The ending welds four jobs into one paragraph.** The last paragraph runs the reservation, the pending-expansion status, the call to action and the publisher disclosure together without a break, and the section opens cold on Lind's quotation with no lead-in. The appraisal, the outlook and the next step each deserve their own beat; here they queue.

What does work: situation, action, results and appraisal are all present and in a sensible order; the publisher stance is truthful and third person; the call to action is built from the one supplied offer; no scene, emotion or second voice is invented; the quotations carry experience the narration does not.

## 4. L1 — **pass**

The Swedish is native. V2 holds everywhere, including in the harder constructions ("Innan beslutet kontrollerade teamet om loggen kunde…", "Vill du förbereda ett eget försök kan du läsa igenom…", "telefonanmälningar och mejl hade fram till dess sparats var för sig"). Compounds are closed (felanmälningslogg, statusvisningen, arbetsbelastning, kvällsskiftet). Double definiteness is right ("den gemensamma" is avoided, but "de större reparationerna", "de första anmälningarna", "det här kundcaset" are all correct). The s-passive is used where Swedish uses it ("hölls öppen", "har ännu inte utvidgats", "mättes inte"). Idiom is Swedish, not English wearing Swedish: "bestämde sig för att pröva", "var för sig", "valet föll på", "tar ställning", "hänger på", "läsa igenom". "Noteringen förklarar uttryckligen inte skillnaden med programvaran" uses *förklara … med*, which is the Swedish construction, where a translated text typically reaches for *tillskriver … till*.

Three blemishes, none of them an import from English:

- "Kostnader, **boendes** nöjdhet och tid till avslutat arbete mättes inte." The substantivised *boende* needs its determiner in the genitive: *de boendes nöjdhet*. As written it is a grammatical slip, and it sits in the sentence that carries three of the material's caveats.
- "tid till avslutat arbete" is a paraphrase where Swedish has the compound (*färdigställandetid*); accurate but laboured.
- "försöksnotering" is a coinage. *Notering* pulls towards a jotting or a listed price; *anteckning* is the word for the document the material describes.

Reader effect: an operations manager reads sober, specific Swedish trade prose that is scrupulous about what was and was not measured. The slips above are editor-level, not translator-level.

## 5. Quoted speech

**Q1.** Draft: "— Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort, skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet. Kategorierna var våra; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
One pass: yes. The interrupted attribution after the first sentence is standard Swedish newspaper practice and costs the reader nothing; *kvällsskiftet*/*morgonskiftet* are the ordinary compounds and the shift-for-staff metonymy is native.
Meaning, stance, certainty, voice: intact. The ownership claim keeps its flat insistence, Svale stays in the helping role, the past perfect with *redan* survives. *Skriver* matches "All interviews occurred by email".

**Q2.** Draft: "— Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första anmälningarna, skriver Maya Lind. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan arbetet i nästa hus börjar."
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
One pass: yes — and here the draft has bought that at a price. The source's "before the next building starts" leaves open what starts; the draft supplies *arbetet*. The material settles nothing about it: what would plausibly start in the next building is the log's rollout, which is what she has been talking about, while *arbetet i nästa hus* reads to a Swedish maintenance reader as the repair work there. So the passage runs smoothly and says a little more, and slightly to one side of, what she said. The quotation guidance permits making an implicit referent explicit only where the supplied context settles it, and it does not. A rendering that keeps the opening — "innan vi drar i gång med nästa hus" — would have read just as smoothly without choosing for her.
Meaning, stance, certainty, voice: the comparison, the conditional *skulle* and the fronted emphasis are intact; the referent has been narrowed.

**Q3.** Draft: "— Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket, skriver Maya Lind. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
One pass: yes. *Göra om försöket* after a finished trial takes the repeat reading, and the sentence that follows confirms it.
Meaning, stance, certainty, reservation, voice: intact. The marked "I would choose to" survives as "Jag skulle välja att"; the present-tense "hjälper oss" is neither strengthened nor hedged; the reservation after *men* keeps its position and force, which is what the material insists on.

Missing quotations: none; all three permitted quotations appear, each adding something the narration does not.

**In one sentence:** all three passages read on one pass in Swedish and keep the source's stance, certainty and voice, but Q2 is not quite the source's speech — it supplies a referent the material leaves open, and points it at the repair work rather than the rollout.

## 7. Delivery

**A delivery that should not have happened.** The draft is careful where the material is dangerous — no causal claim, no cost or satisfaction claim, no completion time, the reservation intact in her own words — and the first comparison's three repairs were all worth making. But the delivered text still says the buildings are the company's, still hands the customer's decision about the phone line to nobody and by proximity to the supplier, and still headlines a dependency the material does not carry. The first two were named in the reply with one-word and four-word repairs attached, one of them the wording the draft's own headline already uses. The rule that no prose is changed after the final comparison is coherent, and the disclosure is honest and precise, but what it bought here was a draft delivered with defects its own reply could describe, and a reader who must apply three edits before publishing.
