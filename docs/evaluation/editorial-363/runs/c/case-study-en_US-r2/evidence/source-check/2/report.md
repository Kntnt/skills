## 3. Source-support findings

None.

Every factual and attributed claim in the draft, including those in the title, the deck and the four headings, traces to a passage of the supplied material, at the same strength, scope, time, modality and attribution. Where a compatible counter-case could be constructed — the "late 2025" window (#2), "wrote the categories itself" (#13), "no… measurements were made" (#25), the present tense of "does not recommend" (#30), and the compressed fourth heading (#33) — each is excluded by a supplied statement quoted in the row, so none stands as a finding. The four forbidden inferences are all avoided: no causal claim for the shorter assignment time (#20, #24), no cost or resident-satisfaction claim (#25), no conversion of assignment time into completion time (#26, and #23 keeps the endpoints), and no helpless-customer framing — the customer designs, decides, chooses and keeps its own channel open throughout. The customer's reservation survives in the deck (#7), in a heading (#27) and in the closing quotation (#32). Supplier narration stays in the third person, and the suggested link is described as the document the brief says it is (#38).

Locale-register substitutions outside quoted speech — "flats" → "apartments" (#9), "telephone" → "phone" (#11, #16) — are same-language vocabulary choices that name the same things; they are accounted for above as source-support items, not as translation findings.
