## 3. Findings

**Source-support findings: none.**

**Translation findings: none, and none possible** — material and draft are both English and the
quotations are reproduced untranslated, so no meaning crosses a language boundary either inside or
outside quoted speech.

Every factual and attributed claim in the draft — including the title, the summary, all three
headings, the figures, the dates, the exclusions, the comparison period, the confounder, the
non-attribution, the unexpanded status, the pending check and the link’s description — rests on a
passage in the supplied material, with subject, scope, time, modality, certainty and the holder of
the knowledge preserved. Where the draft departs from the material’s phrasing it departs in the safe
direction (“this account records no comparison”) or renders the same thing in American English
(*apartments*, *test*). The four passages tested hardest are recorded above with the reasoning that
set each aside: “this account records no comparison with another supplier”, “names what it leaves
out”, “entered during the trial” and “The trial produced no measurement …”. None of them adds a
figure, a cause, a name or a certainty the material lacks, and each is defensible as written.

The three claims the material forbids are absent: the draft nowhere says the software caused the
shorter assignment time (it states the non-attribution twice, in the summary and in the body),
nowhere converts assignment time into completion time (it says so explicitly: “Assignment is not
completion.”), and nowhere claims savings, resident delight or a rescued customer.
