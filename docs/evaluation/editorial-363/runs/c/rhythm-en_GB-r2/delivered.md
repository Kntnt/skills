# Kirkwell weighs whether its late buses stay

**Kirkwell's council put four late buses back on the valley route for a six-month trial. It counted 2,140 boardings, 41 per cent of them on Fridays. A decision on a permanent timetable is due in June.**

By The Newsroom

Four late buses ran between Kirkwell and Ardfern from November to April, a six-month trial. They had come off the timetable in 2023. The council paid for the trial out of its transport reserve, changed no fares, and rostered the drivers from the existing depot.

## Fridays carried 41 per cent of the trial's total

Over the six months, the council counted 2,140 boardings on the four buses. Friday accounted for 41 per cent of them. The counts exclude concessionary travel, which the operator records separately.

Passenger numbers on the daytime services did not change over the same period. The council's report does not say whether the late buses drew new travellers or moved existing ones.

## The depot's own drivers covered the four buses

No overtime figure is published.

“We wanted the rotas settled before the winter. They were, just about. After that the depot stopped ringing round at six in the morning,” said depot manager Ruth Calder.

Calder said the four buses had been straightforward to staff once the rotas were settled. She would not say whether that would hold across a full year.
