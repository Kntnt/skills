## 3. R1 — **Fail**

The good first, because it is real. Changes 2 and 3 are genuine Swedish errors, both found without any absent material, and 2 sits inside a bounding sentence and repairs it without touching the claim — the note still explicitly declines to attribute the difference to the software. Every quotation is verbatim. Every figure, date, exclusion and disclaimer stands: the 31 reports, the exclusions, the two medians, the different workloads, the unmeasured cost, satisfaction and completion time, no supplier comparison, no recommendation to every housing company. And where the run did lose something, it said so precisely, naming what went and where the content still stands.

It fails on the rest.

- **Two working Swedish sentences were rewritten to taste, and each cost a claim.** "Sitt eget omdöme om testet lämnar Lind med en reservation inbyggd" uses ordinary Swedish topicalisation — fronting is not a defect in this language — and it does real work, telling the reader the verdict is qualified before the verdict arrives. What replaced it says less and reads flatter. "Samma kategorier är det Lind pekar på …" is heavier, but it carried the connection between two sections; "Lind beskriver vad som tog tid" carries nothing.
- **Neither rewrite achieved its stated purpose.** The reply says the finding was that the bridge announced its quotation in advance. Both replacements still announce their quotations — one says Lind will describe what took time, the other that she will give her verdict. The pre-echo survived; only the content was lost.
- **Changes 1 and 5 are unreported.** The reply names six findings and explains none of them; only the two lost claims get an account. Change 1 is the one that matters: it swaps the section's actual point (the log was tested before the supplier was chosen) for *behöll sina egna kategorier*, a claim about what pre-existed that the text does not make in those terms, in a heading a scanner reads.
- **"Borttagna påståenden. Inga." is wrong on the reply's own showing.** Two body claims are gone, as the next section admits in the words *gick förlorat* and *gick förlorad*. Filing them under "moved" and then declaring no removals leaves a reader of the account with the opposite of what happened, even though the detail beneath is honest.

Two genuine corrections do not pay for two taste rewrites that stripped reader-guiding claims without fixing what they were said to fix, plus two unreported heading changes and a removals line that contradicts the paragraph under it.
