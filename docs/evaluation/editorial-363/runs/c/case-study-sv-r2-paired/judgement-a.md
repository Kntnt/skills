- One pass: yes. A competent Swedish reader takes it whole; *kvällsskiftet*, *morgonskiftet* and *loggen* are established above, and *Kategorierna* is introduced here in the speaker's own definite form, which Swedish carries without strain.

**Passage 2 — "– Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att mata in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus drar i gång, skriver hon."**
- Wording: comes back unchanged, word for word.
- One pass: yes. *Att ett hus drar i gång* is an ordinary Swedish metonymy — the building stands for the work in it, and *dra i gång* is the idiom Swedish uses for a thing getting under way. Nothing has to be supplied. The antecedent for *kategorierna* still stands two sentences above ("hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer"), so the flattened bridge (#4) does not cost the reader the referent, only the text's explicit statement that it is the same categories.
- Unchanged, and a reader does not stop in it.

**Passage 3 — "– Jag skulle välja att göra om testet. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, skriver hon."**
- Wording: comes back unchanged, word for word.

## 3. R1 — **Fail**

There is genuine skill here. Two real Swedish faults were found and fixed correctly, and one of them — the reversed objects of *tillskriva* — sits inside the single most important hedging sentence in the piece, where a garbled construction would have undercut the text's whole claim to precision. A supplier call to action that had been made a subheading over the customer's verdict was removed. All three quotations are verbatim, every figure, date and attribution stands, and every bounding sentence stands: no supplier comparison reported, the count's exclusions, the periods' different workloads, the note's express refusal to credit the software, the unmeasured costs, resident satisfaction and completion time, and the absence of any recommendation covering all housing companies.

It fails on the account and on what was added. The reply announces "Sex fynd, alla åtgärdade i en runda" and never lists the six; it then reports two changed claims and states "Inga siffror, datum, attributioner, citat eller förbehåll i övrigt är ändrade" and "inget är olöst". Four of the six differences are reported nowhere. One of them, #1, adds to the text: *behöll sina egna kategorier* asserts that the categories pre-existed the trial, which nothing in the text supports, and it replaced a heading that had nothing wrong with it. Another, #5, ships the exact defect this reply is elsewhere alert to — a heading that says the quotation before the quotation — while the reply declares the re-review clean. A person reading this reply cannot see what came back changed, and two of the changes they cannot see are the ones that needed their judgement.

**Reporting, item by item.** The two reported changes, #4 and #6, are reported with real precision: each names the finding behind it, states exactly which part of the text's own connective tissue went with it, and says where the surviving claim still stands. That is honest work, and it is the reply's strength. But the reply files both under "Ändrade påståenden" and declares "Borttagna påståenden. Inga.", when what each did was delete a statement the body made — that Lind points to the same categories, and that her verdict carries a reservation — as the reply's own prose concedes ("gick … förlorat"). #1, #2, #3 and #5 are unreported, and #1 is a changed claim the reply expressly denies making.
