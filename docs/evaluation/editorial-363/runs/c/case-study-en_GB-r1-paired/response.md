# Two Elm Quay buildings share one view of repairs

Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats. In 2025 its maintenance team spent eight weeks testing whether two of its buildings could work from a single record of repairs, and an internal note recorded what happened. Here is what that note says, what it declined to conclude, and what the supervisor would change.

By Thomas Barregren

Telephone reports and emails were stored separately at Elm Quay Housing. The company manages 640 flats, and its maintenance team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information. In September 2025 it decided to trial a shared repair log, and it chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair.

## Elm Quay designed the categories, Svale set up the log

The trial covered two buildings and lasted eight weeks. Residents could still report a fault by telephone. Svale Systems trained six members of staff in two sessions.

Maya Lind is Elm Quay's maintenance supervisor. 'We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log,' she says.

## Elm Quay does not credit the software for the shorter time

An internal note dated 4 December 2025 records 31 repair reports entered in the log. The count excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial began.

The same note gives the median time from report to assignment as two working days during the trial, and three working days in the eight weeks before it. The periods had different workloads, and the note does not attribute the difference to the software. There are no measurements of cost, resident satisfaction or completion time.

## Maya Lind would repeat the trial, with one more week

The supervisor is specific about where the work actually went. 'We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts,' she says.

Lind's own assessment is a qualified one. 'I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation,' she says. She did not recommend Svale Systems to every housing company.

## Expansion is still an open question at Elm Quay

The shared log has not gone beyond the two buildings where it started. The team will decide after checking how its categories hold up for larger repairs. Operations managers weighing a similar trial can read [Svale Systems' implementation checklist](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist).
