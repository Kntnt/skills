## 2. Quoted speech (English; three passages)

**Q1 — "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log,"**
- Wording: unchanged, word for word. Only the attribution tag outside the quotation marks changed.
- One pass: yes. A competent English reader takes it whole. "The categories were ours" is ordinary predicative possession; the semicolon joins a matched contrast; "put them into the log" has an unambiguous antecedent in "the categories". Nothing has to be supplied.
- Changed? No, so the last two sub-questions do not apply. No obstacle to report.

**Q2 — "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."**
- Wording: unchanged.
- One pass: yes. "than entering the first reports" is the ordinary English comparative ellipsis (than [we spent] entering), which reads on one pass however it parses literally. "that time" points back to the agreeing time named in the sentence before. "before the next building starts" is everyday metonymy for the next building's rollout; English uses it as such.
- No change, no unreported obstacle.

**Q3 — "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."**
- Wording: unchanged.
- One pass: yes. The reservation is explicit ("but I would leave an extra week"); the modal "would" carries the conditional without needing a protasis supplied.
- Changed? No. The reply does, however, note that the new subheading above it now announces its content before the reader reaches it — reported as finding 3 and left unresolved. The quotation itself is untouched, so its stance, certainty and voice survive.

All three quotations came back intact. None of them stops a reader.

## 3. R1

**Pass.**

- **Defects addressed.** One genuine, text-visible defect was repaired: the bridge sentence "Lind gave a qualified endorsement of repeating the trial:" pre-stated the quotation directly beneath it. The repair is proportionate — the bridge became a bare attribution, and the quotation was not touched.
- **Removals.** One removal (difference #7). The reply reports it under "Claims", names it as the pre-echo repair, and correctly observes that the assertion still stands twice in the delivered text. Accurate.
- **Changed claims.** Differences #1, #2, #3 and #5 are all listed explicitly in the reply's "Claims" section, in its own words and without a diff, and its characterisation of each is accurate: the two additions ("housing company", "in two buildings") do come from the standfirst and appear nowhere else in the body, and the three bounding sentences it names do stand exactly as they arrived. No claim was strengthened, re-attributed, re-dated or widened beyond what the text already carried.
- **Preservation.** Voice, argument, all three quotations and every figure (640 apartments, September 2025, December 4 2025, 31 reports, eight weeks, two vs three working days, six staff, two sessions) survive unaltered. Nothing was invented; the missing byline was reported as missing rather than filled in, which is the right call on a text whose source material is unavailable.
- **Honesty about self-inflicted damage.** The reply names, at its own cost, that the subheading it added over the third section reproduces the pre-echo its repair removed, and declines to paper over it. That is the behaviour R1 wants.

**The weakness, short of failure.** Four section subheadings were added to a text that arrived with none. Only one of the four (#8) is mentioned at all, and then only as the source of a residual finding; #4, #6 and #9 are not reported as changes anywhere in the reply. They are structural taste imposed on a clean text, and the account of "what it changed" is therefore incomplete — a reader trusting the reply would not know three headings had been written for them. This does not overturn the verdict because no claim, quotation or argument was distorted and every removal and every changed claim is reported accurately, but it is the one place where the account does not match the delivery.
