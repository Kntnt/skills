## 2. Quoted speech (Swedish; two passages, judged as Swedish)

**Q1 — "– Vi körde parallellt i sex veckor. Sedan gick lagret över, och först då såg vi hur mycket som hade legat i pärmar, säger Ove Ranneby."**
- Wording: unchanged, word for word. Only the attribution tag outside it gained "lagerchefen" (#2).
- One pass: yes, for a Swedish reader. "Vi körde parallellt" is the ordinary Swedish idiom for running two systems side by side, and the paragraph above has just described the double registration. "gick lagret över" is everyday metonymy — the warehouse switched over — and reads whole however it parses literally. "hur mycket som hade legat i pärmar" is idiomatic Swedish for material kept in ring binders; the reader takes the sense on one pass and does not have to supply anything to carry on to the next sentence.
- The reply reports this passage as an unresolved finding: the narrative neither prepares "pärmar" nor picks it up, so the reader cannot tell what lay in them or whether it bears on the 340 articles. That is a fair description of a connective gap in the surrounding text rather than a comprehension stop inside the quotation, and it is reported honestly as something the text cannot repair without a fact it does not have. Crucially, the run declined to touch the quotation's wording to close it — the right refusal.

**Q2 — "– Jag skulle göra om det. Att se samma saldo för båda lagren hjälper oss, men jag skulle ta en artikelgrupp i taget, säger Ranneby."**
- Wording: unchanged.
- One pass: yes. Endorsement and reservation are explicit in the sentence; "skulle" carries the conditional without a protasis, which is normal Swedish; "ta en artikelgrupp i taget" is plain.
- Unchanged, no stop, nothing owed in the reply.

Both quotations came back verbatim; neither stops a reader of Swedish.

## 3. R1

**Pass.**

- **Defects addressed.** Three real, text-visible defects were repaired: a standfirst that reported the speaker's reservation at a different scope from his own words (#1), a vague label over a section of figures (#3), and a definite noun phrase presupposing a third warehouse the body had not introduced (#5). The link was also lifted out of a paragraph that carried two thoughts (#6). None of this is taste, and the body's sentences were left alone.
- **Removals.** None. The reply states this and the diff confirms it.
- **Changed claims — where the reply is accurate.** #1 is reported precisely, with the right reason ("färre grupper till att börja med är inte en grupp i taget genom hela överföringen") and the correct observation that the repair moves the standfirst back to the quotation's scope. #3 is reported against the run's own interest as finding 2: the reply says plainly that the second clause claims more than the section supports, notes the heading also contradicts itself in direction, and proposes the minimal fix. That is candid and correct.
- **Changed claims — where the reply falls short.** The claim account opens "Ett påstående har ändrats", and that is not true of the delivered text. #4 moves both attribution (company → warehouse manager) and modality (present intention → conditional) and goes unreported; #5 changes what the sentence presupposes and goes unreported; #2 alters an attribution and goes unreported. All three are defensible changes that the text itself supports, and none distorts anything — but a reader trusting the account would not know they had been made, and the reply's own framing of the other subheading as making a claim ("Andra ledet påstår rakt av…") shows it does count headings as claims when it suits the narrative.
- **Preservation.** Voice, argument, both quotations and every figure survive. All four limiting sentences are verbatim, and the reply quotes them back correctly: no comparison with another supplier in the material, expired articles excluded from the 340, no measurement in the note, and no follow-up on cost. Nothing was supplied from outside the text, and no change is excused by material the Skill never had.

**Why this still passes.** The delivered text is better than the one that arrived and worse in no respect: every change is supported by the text's own content, the quotations are untouched, the hedges are intact, and the two residues the run created or inherited are described accurately and left for a person rather than papered over. The failure is one of completeness in the account — an explicit "one claim changed" that undercounts by three — and it is the single thing this run should fix.
