## 2. Quoted speech

**Q1 — "– Vi körde parallellt i sex veckor. Sedan gick lagret över, och först då såg vi hur mycket som hade legat i pärmar, säger Ove Ranneby."**
- Wording: unchanged; only the attribution outside the quotation marks gained his title.
- One pass: yes. "Legat i pärmar" is native Swedish shorthand for records kept on paper, and a Swedish reader takes "hur mycket som hade legat i pärmar" as "how much had been sitting on paper" without supplying anything. What the reader is left without is not comprehension but connection — the surrounding narrative never mentions pärmar before or after, so the discovery is a loose end rather than a stop.
- Changed? No.
- The reply reports that loose end as its finding 1, describing it as an obstacle it cannot repair without a fact the text does not carry, and refusing to touch the quotation's wording to fix it. That is the right refusal; my own reading is that the passage still reads on one pass, so the reply reports rather than under-reports here.
