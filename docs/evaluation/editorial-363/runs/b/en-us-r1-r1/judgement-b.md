- Wording: unchanged, word for word.
- One pass: yes. "the evening shift"/"the morning shift" are ordinary English metonymies for the staff on those shifts and need no unpacking; "The categories were ours" is plain predicative possession; "them" has one available antecedent. Nothing must be supplied.
- No change to the passage itself; the attribution outside it changed (#5). No obstacle to report.
