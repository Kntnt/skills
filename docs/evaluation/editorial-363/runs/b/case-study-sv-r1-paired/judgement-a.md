- **One pass?** Yes. A competent Swedish reader takes it whole. "Kategorierna var våra" is ordinary Swedish for "we defined them ourselves", and the sentence after it ("Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen") confirms the division of labour on the spot. Nothing has to be supplied.

- **Changed — does it supply anything the text does not carry?** No. Every word of the bridge is warranted by the quote beneath it and by the section above it; the identification in it is the input's own, moved intact.
- **Changed — did meaning, stance, certainty, reservation and voice survive?** Yes, entirely. The speaker, her role, the medium (an email) and the claim to have made the categories herself all survive, and "skriver hon" keeps the written-source marker the genre depends on.

## 3. R1 — **Pass**

A disciplined text came back a disciplined text. Every figure, date, exclusion and disclaimer survives untouched: the 640 flats, the 31 reports, the two-versus-three working days, the 4 December note, the exclusion of urgent and pre-ordered jobs, the explicit refusal to attribute the shorter time to the software, the unmeasured cost, satisfaction and completion time, and the closing admission that the case is vendor-published and not independent journalism. All three quotations keep their words, their conditionals and their reservations. Nothing clean was rewritten to suit a taste.

Removals and changed claims, one by one:

- **Removals:** none. The reply says so, and it is right.
- **Changed claims:** none either, on the strict reading. #1 names an owner the body already gives; #2 adds a bridge whose every element is warranted by the quote it introduces; #3 moves an attribution without weakening it; #4 and #5 are locale and agreement. The reply's blanket statement — "ingen uppgift står kvar med ändrad omfattning, säkerhet, källhänvisning, kronologi, orsakssamband eller innebörd" — is, unusually among such statements, true of this delivery.

Two reservations, neither enough to fail it. First, the account is a certificate rather than a report: it says two findings were raised and both repaired, and never says what they were, so a writer cannot see that a sentence was added to their text or that a quotation's attribution line was rebuilt. Those changes were harmless here, but the user learns that from me, not from the reply. Second, the all-clear is a little generous to itself: "Maya Lind rekommenderade inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag" is left as it arrived, and it reads two ways in Swedish — she recommended it, but not to everyone, or she declined to recommend it at all — with a preterite that sits oddly among the present tenses around it. That is a defect visible in the text, and a review declaring no findings remain should have caught it. What the review did do, it did without harming a word that was working.
