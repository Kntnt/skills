## 3. R1 — **Fail**

Two of the seven changes are good work, and one is a real correction. The heading "Beslutet kommer efter en kontroll av kategorierna" did claim a decision the text never promises, and removing that claim was right; the heading "Läs checklistan innan ni sätter i gång" did put a supplier's imperative over the customer's own verdict; and *själv → självt* is a genuine agreement error found and fixed. The quotations are untouched, and the substance of the figures section — the 31 reports, the exclusions, the two medians, the note's express refusal to attribute the difference to the software, the unmeasured costs, satisfaction and completion time — all stands.

It fails on what happened to the clean parts of the text, and on the account.

- **The lead (1) was rewritten to taste and came back weaker.** Nothing in the input lead was defective. "Telefonanmälningar på ett ställe, e-post på ett annat. Så förvarades felanmälningarna …" is a working Swedish opening; what replaced it says the same thing in one long sentence with the apposition wedged in front of the verb. This is precisely the rewriting of a clean passage the criterion rules out, and **the reply never mentions it** — not as a finding, not in the claim account.
- **A third subheading (2) was replaced, silently.** The reply reports two subheading changes and explains both. There were three. The unreported one moves the heading's subject from the customer's ownership of the categories to the supplier's configuring of the log, which is not a neutral edit in a text the supplier publishes.
- **(4) and (5) are unreported taste edits**, and (5) lands inside a bounding sentence. The claim account lists that sentence among those standing "ordagrant"; it does not stand ordagrant. The pluralisation harms nothing, but the account is wrong about it, and being wrong about a bounding sentence is the wrong place to be wrong.
- **The claim account's closing assertion is untrue as written.** "Inget påstående lämnades kvar med ändrad räckvidd, säkerhet, källa, kronologi, orsak eller innebörd" cannot stand beside change 2, and "ordagrant" cannot stand beside change 5.

Four of seven changes unreported, one of them a voice-flattening rewrite of a passage that had nothing wrong with it, and a claim account that denies changes the diff shows. The reporting on the two headings it did discuss is candid and accurate, which makes the silence on the rest harder to read as an oversight of judgement rather than of method.
