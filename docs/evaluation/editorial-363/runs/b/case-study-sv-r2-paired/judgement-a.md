## 3. R1 — **Fail**

There is real work here. Two subheadings genuinely misdescribed their sections — one asserting a decision the text never promises, one turning the customer's verdict section into the supplier's call to action — and both were repaired for the right stated reasons, with the costs of the repairs named honestly and handed to a person. Two Swedish mechanical faults were found and fixed correctly (*själv* → *självt*, the number on *arbetsbelastning*). All three quotations are verbatim, no figure or date moved, and every bounding sentence stands: no supplier comparison reported, the count's exclusions, the periods' different workloads, the note's express refusal to attribute the difference to the software, the absent measurements of cost, resident satisfaction and completion time, and the publisher disclosure.

It fails on two counts. First, the account is materially wrong where it is most definite: "Inget annat påstående togs bort. Inget påstående lämnades kvar med ändrad räckvidd, säkerhet, källa, kronologi, orsak eller innebörd" — but #4 changes what the team is said to have done before choosing the supplier, and four further differences (#1, #2, #3, #5) are not mentioned at all. A reader of this reply cannot see what came back changed. Second, two of the unreported changes were not repairs of anything the reply is willing to name: the lead's distinctive Swedish opening was flattened, and a subheading was swapped for one no better than it, which is a working text rewritten to taste.

**Reporting, item by item.** One removal — the old third subheading's assertion that a decision follows the check — is reported, and reported accurately, with the reason and the evidence from the text. #6 and #7 are reported accurately, including the defects the repairs themselves created and left in the delivered text (a heading that repeats its own first sentence; a heading that pre-empts the quotation under it). #1, #2, #3, #4 and #5 are reported nowhere, and #4 is a changed claim that the reply expressly denies making.
