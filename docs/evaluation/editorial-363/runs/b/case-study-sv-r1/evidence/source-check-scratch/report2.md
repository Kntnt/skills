## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 (source support) — "under hela försöket" adds an unsupported duration

- **Draft (L17):** "De boende kunde fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon under hela försöket."
- **Material:** "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents."
- **What is missing:** the material states that telephone reporting was kept open; it states nothing about for how long. Duration is a claim in its own right.
- **Case, compatible with the material:** telephone reporting is kept open for residents when the trial starts and is closed in week six, once the log is carrying the traffic. "Kept telephone reporting open for residents" still holds; "under hela försöket" fails. Reverse direction: reporting open throughout makes both true, so the draft is strictly stronger. No supplied statement excludes the week-six case — the material never dates or bounds the telephone channel.
- **Smallest supported repair:** delete "hela" → "De boende kunde fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon under försöket."

### Finding 2 (source support) — "därefter" fixes the note after the trial

Reported under the compatible-case test; the alternative reading is strained but the material does not exclude it.

- **Draft (L7):** "Försöket pågick i åtta veckor, och därefter sammanfattade bolaget det i en intern anteckning."
- **Material:** "The trial lasted eight weeks. Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered."
- **What is missing:** the material gives the note's date but never the trial's start or end date, and never says the note was written after the trial ended.
- **Case, compatible with the material:** the team decides in late September and the trial runs 20 October – 15 December; the 4 December note is an interim summary of the weeks completed so far. Every supplied statement still holds — "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial", "The trial lasted eight weeks", the note's date — while "därefter" fails. I can quote no supplied statement that excludes a start date after early October.
- **Smallest supported repair:** drop the sequencing word — "Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och sammanfattades i en intern anteckning." (Authorship is already carried by "intern"; if the company is to stay the subject: "Försöket pågick i åtta veckor, och bolaget sammanfattade det i en intern anteckning.")

### Translation findings

None. The three quotations were checked separately for meaning, stance, certainty, distinctive wording and idiomatic Swedish:

1. "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log." → "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra. Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen." Pluperfect ("had already done" → "redan hade gjort") and the ownership claim both preserved; the semicolon becomes a full stop, which changes no meaning. Svale's role stays subordinate ("hjälpte oss"), as in the source.
2. "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts." → "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar." Comparison, conditional "would" → "skulle", and "the next building" all intact; the fronted object is ordinary V2 Swedish speech.
3. "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation." → "Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser." Both conditionals kept, the "but" reservation kept as a reservation, present tense "helps us" → "hjälper oss". "one view" → "en samlad bild" is the idiomatic Swedish for a consolidated view and names the same thing here.

No implicit referent was made explicit beyond what the material settles; nothing additive appears inside the quotation marks; the speech-dash form and the reporting verb "skriver" match a written email source.
