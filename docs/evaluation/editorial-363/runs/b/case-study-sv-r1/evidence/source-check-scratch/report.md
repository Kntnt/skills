## 2. Translated quotations — meaning, stance, certainty, voice, idiom

Checked separately from source support, per the loaded quotation guidance.

**T1.** EN: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
SV: "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra. Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Meaning, stance and certainty preserved. The ownership emphasis of "The categories were ours" survives as "Kategorierna var våra" with the same marked, unhedged flatness; replacing the semicolon with a full stop changes punctuation only. "kvällsskiftet"/"morgonskiftet" are the established Swedish terms; past perfect "hade gjort" preserves "had already done" including "redan". Idiomatic Swedish speech, V2 throughout. No finding.

**T2.** EN: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
SV: "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar."
Comparative, conditional "skulle"/"would" and the absence of any hedge all preserved. Fronting "Den tiden" with subject–verb inversion is ordinary spoken Swedish emphasis and adds no meaning. "innan nästa hus börjar" reproduces the source's own ellipsis ("before the next building starts"); the material does not settle what exactly starts — a rollout, the logging, the work — so filling it in would add meaning the speaker did not supply. Not an obstruction, no finding.

**T3.** EN: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
SV: "Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."
The marked "I would choose to" is kept as "Jag skulle välja att" rather than smoothed to "Jag skulle göra om försöket" — the distinctive phrasing survives. Present tense "hjälper oss" matches "helps us", and the qualification after "men" is intact and in the same position, so the reservation the material insists on is not weakened. "en samlad bild" renders "one view" (a single, consolidated view of the reports) in idiomatic Swedish; no concrete reading of "one view" available in this material falls outside it. No finding.

No quotation in the draft welds separate remarks together, and no quoted sentence lacks a counterpart in the three supplied quotations.

## 3. Findings

### S1 — source support — the note is dated eight weeks after the decision

**Draft (line 7):** "Åtta veckor senare sammanfattade bolaget försöket i en intern anteckning."
**Material:** "The trial lasted eight weeks." / "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025…"

The preceding sentence anchors the reader in "I september 2025", so "Åtta veckor senare" dates the summarising to roughly eight weeks after the September decision. The material gives the note a date, 4 December 2025, and says nothing about when it was written relative to the trial's end.

**Case in which the material holds and the draft fails:** the team decides on 20 September, the trial runs 1 October – 26 November (eight weeks), and the note is written on 4 December, eight days after the trial ends and about eleven weeks after the decision. Every element is compatible with the supplied material, and no supplied statement excludes it. Under that case "Åtta veckor senare sammanfattade bolaget försöket" is false while every material statement remains true. The draft's own later sentence, "Den interna anteckningen är daterad den 4 december 2025", states the date that makes the eight-week reckoning fail.

**Smallest supported repair:** drop the interval and keep the sequence — "Efter de åtta veckorna sammanfattade bolaget försöket i en intern anteckning."

### S2 — source support — the reservation is widened from Svale to any recommendation

**Draft (line 29):** "Någon rekommendation som gäller alla bostadsbolag ger Maya Lind inte."
**Material:** "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company."

The material's negation is about one thing, Svale, and is in the past ("did not recommend"). The draft negates *any* recommendation holding for all housing companies, in the present.

**Case in which the material holds and the draft fails:** Lind, asked what she would tell other housing companies, says that every housing company should agree its categories before it starts, while still declining to recommend Svale itself to all of them. Nothing in the material excludes this; her second quotation ("I would set that time aside before the next building starts") sits close to exactly that advice, framed as her own practice. Under that case the material's sentence is true and the draft's is false.

**Case in the other direction:** Lind recommends nothing at all to anybody — then both hold. That the draft's claim only sometimes coincides with the material's is the point: it asserts more.

**Smallest supported repair:** narrow the object back to Svale — "Någon rekommendation av Svale till alla bostadsbolag ger Maya Lind inte." (Or: "Maya Lind rekommenderade inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag.")

### S3 — source support — the exclusions belong to the figure, not to the log

**Draft (line 23):** "Under försöket registrerades 31 felanmälningar i loggen, medan akuta ärenden och arbeten som beställts före försöket hölls utanför."
**Material:** "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."

In the material the excluding subject is the note (equivalently, the count of 31). In the draft the exclusion is attached, by the contrastive "medan … hölls utanför", to what was entered in the log — so an ordinary reader takes it that emergencies never went into the log at all.

**Case in which the material holds and the draft fails:** the team enters everything in the shared log, emergencies included, and the note's tally of 31 deliberately counts only non-emergency reports and only work ordered after the trial began, so that the two eight-week periods compare like with like. Nothing supplied excludes this; the material says only that the note excludes them. Under that case emergencies were not "hållna utanför" the log, and the draft's clause is false.

**Smallest supported repair:** move the exclusion onto the figure — "Under försöket registrerades 31 felanmälningar i loggen. Siffran utesluter akuta ärenden och arbeten som beställts före försöket."

### S4 — source support — "säger" asserts spoken answers where the material gives email

**Draft (lines 13, 19, 31):** "säger Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet"; "säger hon" (twice).
**Material:** "All interviews occurred by email, and no physical scene, emotion or remembered dialogue is provided."

The attribution verb is a circumstantial claim about the manner of the statement. The material states the channel outright: the answers were written, not spoken.

**Case in which the material holds and the draft fails:** Lind receives the questions by email and types all three answers; she never utters them. This is not merely compatible with the material — it is what "All interviews occurred by email" describes. Under it, "säger hon" is false as a claim about how she delivered the words, while everything in the material remains true.

The quotations themselves are unaffected: because the material supplies her exact written wording, quoting them is right, and no repair to their content is needed. Only the attribution verb overstates.

**Smallest supported repair:** "säger" → "skriver" in all three attributions — "skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet", "skriver hon", "skriver hon".
