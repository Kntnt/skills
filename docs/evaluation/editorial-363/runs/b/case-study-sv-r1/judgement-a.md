## 5. Quoted speech

**Q1.** Draft: "— Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra. **Q2.** Draft: "— Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar, skriver hon." Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
*One pass* — the comparative frame *mer tid på … än på …* and the fronted *Den tiden* are ordinary spoken Swedish. The one place a reader could slow is **"innan nästa hus börjar"**: a *hus* beginning is, read literally, odd, and the reader supplies the work or the rollout. It is the source's own ellipsis and a Swedish reader resolves it from the sentence it sits in without stopping — *innan nästa hus drar i gång* or *kommer i gång* would run a shade smoother, which is a preference, not an obstruction. Left as it is, it also declines to settle what the speaker left open. *Meaning, certainty, reservation* — conditional *skulle* for *would*, no hedge added or removed.

**Q3.** Draft: "— Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, skriver hon." Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
*One pass* — yes. *Meaning, stance, reservation* — the marked "I would choose to" is kept as *Jag skulle välja att* rather than smoothed to a plain *Jag skulle göra om*; the present-tense benefit stays present (*hjälper oss*) and stays hers, not generalised; the *men* clause is the last thing the reader holds, so the qualification the material insists on survives in position as well as in words. *en samlad bild* is the Swedish figure for a consolidated view and neither widens nor narrows "one view".

**Missing quotations:** none. All three permitted quotations appear, none is welded to another, and no fourth quotation is invented. Attribution is *skriver* and the channel is named at first reference ("i ett mejl"), which matches "All interviews occurred by email".

**In one sentence:** every passage of quoted speech in this draft is speech a Swedish reader takes on one pass, with the source's meaning, stance, reservation and voice intact.
