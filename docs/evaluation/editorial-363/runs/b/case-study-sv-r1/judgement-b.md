## 4. L1 — **pass**

The prose reads as Swedish written by a Swede, not as English rearranged. V2 holds everywhere, including in the harder places: "Fram till dess sparades telefonanmälningar och mejl var för sig", "Inför valet testade bolaget om loggen kunde visa status", "Något beslut om fler hus har försöket ännu inte lett till." Compounds are correctly closed (felanmälningar, arbetsbelastning, boendenöjdhet, färdigställandetid, införandechecklista, kvällsskiftet). Double definiteness is right ("den interna anteckningen", "det egna underhållsteamet", "de första felanmälningarna"). The s-passive is the unmarked choice ("registrerades", "sparades", "redovisas inte", "har inte mätts"). Speech dashes rather than quotation marks, with inverted attribution ("skriver Maya Lind"), is the Swedish newspaper convention.

Idiom: "Valet föll på Svale", "avsätta tid", "ta ställning", "en samlad bild" are all native. The cleft "Förberedelserna är det Maya Lind återkommer till" is ordinary Swedish emphasis.

One clumsy spot: "anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte programvaran skillnaden" stacks three arguments behind the verb and makes the reader hold "skillnaden" to the end; "i anteckningen tillskrivs skillnaden uttryckligen inte programvaran" would read more easily. It is grammatical and does not read as an import from English, so it does not fail the criterion.

Reader effect: an operations manager gets a piece that reads as trade journalism — sober, specific, and noticeably unwilling to claim more than it has.

## 5. Quoted speech

**Q1.** Draft: "— Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra. Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen, skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet, i ett mejl."
Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
One pass: yes. Nothing to supply. "Kvällsskiftet"/"morgonskiftet" are the standard Swedish terms and the shift-for-staff metonymy is native to Swedish. The semicolon becomes a full stop, which is only punctuation.
Meaning, stance, certainty, voice: intact. The flat ownership claim "Kategorierna var våra" keeps the source's unhedged emphasis, and Svale stays subordinate ("hjälpte oss"). Attribution "skriver … i ett mejl" matches "All interviews occurred by email".

**Q2.** Draft: "— Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första felanmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar, skriver hon."
Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
One pass: yes. The fronted object with inversion ("Den tiden skulle jag avsätta") is ordinary spoken Swedish emphasis. "Innan nästa hus börjar" reproduces the source's own ellipsis about what starts; Swedish takes the building-for-project reading without a stumble, and filling it in would add meaning Lind did not supply.
Meaning, stance, certainty, voice: intact. The comparison, the conditional "skulle", and the absence of any hedge all survive.

**Q3.** Draft: "— Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en samlad bild av felanmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser, skriver hon."
Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
One pass: yes.
Meaning, stance, certainty, voice: intact, and this is the quotation that mattered most. The marked "I would choose to" is kept as "Jag skulle välja att" instead of being smoothed to "Jag skulle göra om försöket"; the present-tense "hjälper oss" is not strengthened; the reservation after "men" keeps its position and its full force, so the qualified assessment the material insists on survives as a qualified assessment.

Missing quotations: none. All three usable quotations appear, each in a place where it adds the speaker's own experience rather than restating the narration around it — Q1 gives the motive, Q2 the cost of preparation, Q3 the qualified verdict.

**In one sentence:** every passage of quoted speech in this draft is speech a Swedish reader takes on one pass, with the source's meaning, stance, reservation and voice intact.
