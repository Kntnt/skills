## 3. Cases tested that did not become findings

### 3.1 Headline: "lät två skift dela samma reparationslogg"

Tested case: the log was set up as shared, but through the eight weeks only morning-shift staff actually had accounts, so two shifts never in fact shared it — the material states the cross-shift aim as a want ("The team **wanted** staff on different shifts to see the same information"), never as an outcome.

Set aside by a supplied statement: Lind's own present-tense assertion in the final quotation, "Having one view of the reports **helps us**", asserts that a single shared view exists and is in use by the team she supervises, together with "decided to trial a **shared** repair log" and "trained six staff". The draft's *lät … dela* claims enabling, which those passages carry. A second tested case — that Elm Quay runs three shifts, so naming two under-describes the set — fails because *två skift* excludes no third shift and both named shifts (evening, morning) come from the quotation. No case stands; not a finding.

### 3.2 Editorial question — quotation 3 under the call-to-action heading (l.38–40)

The section headed "Läs checklistan innan ni sätter i gång" opens with Lind's "Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen", so her qualified assessment does work as testimonial for the reader's next step. The material is explicit that "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company" and that her final quotation "is her actual, qualified assessment", not a general endorsement.

Against that: the draft states the reservation in its own paragraph immediately before the heading (l.36), the quotation keeps her hedge ("men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka"), and nowhere does the draft say or imply in words that she recommends the step to the reader. Whether the arrangement recasts a personal, qualified assessment as an endorsement of the reader's trial is a genuinely unsettled line between a standpoint and an assertion. **Reported as an editorial question, with no repair proposed.** If the publisher wants the distance made unmistakable, moving l.36 to sit between the quotation and the call to action would do it — but the draft is defensible as written.

### 3.3 Other passages tested and left alone

- **"redovisas inte" (l.20)** — weaker than "is not available", never stronger; weakening an absence claim introduces nothing.
- **"Så förvarades felanmälningarna" (l.14)** — "telephone reports and emails" are treated as fault reports throughout, which the material's repair-log context carries; the draft does not claim the emails contained nothing else.
- **"utbildade sex medarbetare" (l.18)** — the material does not say whose staff; the draft does not either, and training one's own staff is not a reading the sentence offers.
- **"tillskriver … till" (l.28)** — the Swedish idiom is *tillskriva programvaran skillnaden*, without *till*. Meaning, attribution and the explicit non-attribution are unchanged, so this is not a source-support finding, and it is not quoted speech. General editorial and mechanical review is outside this comparison; recorded here only so it is not mistaken for an omission.

## 4. Translation of quotations

All three are licensed ("Translation of the quotations is permitted, preserving stance and qualification") and all three are set with the Swedish speech dash, which is what the resolved composition guidance says an ordinary Swedish text reaches for when reporting what a person said. **No translation findings.** What was checked:

**Quotation 1 (l.22)** — "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Meaning, stance and the past-perfect sequencing ("redan hade gjort" ← "had already done") are all present; nothing additive. The semicolon is rendered as a full stop, so the contrast (the categories were ours, Svale only helped) sits across two sentences rather than one. Both facts remain adjacent and in the same order, and the contrast still reads; this is a punctuation choice, not a concrete obstruction to the reading. Idiomatic Swedish speech, V2 order intact.

**Quotation 2 (l.34)** — "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
The comparison, the conditional *skulle* and the referent of "that time" are preserved. "drar i gång" for "starts" is a degree more colloquial than the source verb; it is ordinary spoken Swedish for a building's work starting, it adds no meaning, stance or certainty, and preferring "kommer i gång" or "startar" would be a synonym preference rather than a defect.

**Quotation 3 (l.40)** — "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
The three loads that matter all survive: the conditional endorsement ("Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen"), the present-tense benefit claim ("hjälper oss"), and the reservation introduced by *men*. "en samlad bild av anmälningarna" for "one view of the reports" keeps the consolidation that is the point of the phrase; "one view" is not distinctive wording being smoothed away, and no alternative rendering changes what she claims.

No quotation is welded from separate remarks, no hedge is removed or added, no implicit referent is made explicit beyond what the material settles, and no fourth quotation appears.

## 5. Findings

Four. Each is a defect against the material, not a preference; each repair is the smallest one that restores support.

### Finding 1 — "med 640 lägenheter" asserts holding where the material asserts management

- **Draft (l.14):** "ett bostadsföretag med 640 lägenheter"
- **Source:** "Elm Quay Housing **manages** 640 flats."
- **What differs:** the relation between the company and the flats. *Med* attributes the stock to the company; "manages" attributes an activity over it.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay is a managing agent that owns no flats and manages 640 on behalf of their owners. Compatible with the material, which says only "manages" and never names an owner. "Manages 640 flats" is then true and "a housing company **with** 640 flats" misdescribes its holdings.
- **Case where the draft holds and the source fails:** Elm Quay owns 640 flats but has contracted out the management of 200 of them, managing 440 itself. Then "with 640 flats" is true and "manages 640 flats" is false. The two are not interchangeable in either direction, and nothing supplied excludes either case.
- **Missing support:** any statement of ownership. The material supplies none.
- **Smallest repair:** "ett bostadsföretag som förvaltar 640 lägenheter".

### Finding 2 — "hyresgäster" narrows the material's "residents"

- **Draft (l.18):** "lät **hyresgästerna** fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon"
- **Draft (l.28):** "Det finns inga mätningar av kostnader, **hyresgästnöjdhet** eller färdigställandetid."
- **Source:** "kept telephone reporting open for **residents**"; "There are no cost, **resident-satisfaction** or completion-time measurements."
- **What differs:** the population named. *Hyresgäst* asserts a tenancy; "resident" asserts only that the person lives there. A case in this material could fall under one term and not the other, so the terms do not name the same thing here.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay manages a tenant-owner association's 640 flats, so the residents are owner-occupiers, not tenants. Compatible with everything supplied — the material says "manages 640 flats" and "residents", and never uses "tenants", "rent" or "lease". "Telephone reporting open for residents" is then true while "lät hyresgästerna…" names a group that does not exist at Elm Quay.
- **Case the other way:** a landlord's flat is sublet or occupied by the tenant's adult child, who phones in a fault. The caller is a resident but not the *hyresgäst*, so the draft's sentence excludes a report the source's sentence covers.
- **Set aside?** No supplied statement excludes either case; the material never states the tenure.
- **Smallest repair:** "lät **de boende** fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon" (l.18) and "mätningar av kostnader, **nöjdhet bland de boende** eller färdigställandetid" (l.28).

### Finding 3 — heading widens "designed its categories" into deciding the log's contents

- **Draft (l.16):** "## Elm Quay bestämde vad loggen skulle innehålla"
- **Source:** "The maintenance team **designed its categories**"; "**Svale configured the log** and trained six staff during two sessions"; Lind: "The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
- **What differs:** the thing decided. The material grants Elm Quay the categories and puts configuration of the log with Svale; the heading grants Elm Quay the log's contents, which is the wider thing and reaches into what the material assigns to the supplier.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay designed the categories, while Svale's configuration fixed everything else the log holds — the mandatory fields, the status values, whether an assignment timestamp is recorded, what a report record even consists of. Compatible with the material, which says in its own words that Svale configured the log, and with Lind's "Svale helped us put them into the log" (help with placement, not authorship of the log's structure). Elm Quay then did not decide what the log would contain.
- **Set aside?** No supplied statement gives Elm Quay authority over anything beyond the categories; the one sentence about the log's setup names Svale as the configurer.
- **Note:** the heading layer is where the Claims contract holds scope and qualification just as in the body, and this heading also sits against the brief's requirement that Svale's role stay accurately described.
- **Smallest repair:** "## Elm Quay bestämde kategorierna" — which the section's own first sentence, "Underhållsteamet utformade kategorierna själv", then delivers exactly.

### Finding 4 — heading turns a sequence into a dependency

- **Draft (l.30):** "## Beslutet hänger på de större reparationerna"
- **Source:** "The trial has not yet expanded; the team **will decide after checking** how the categories work for larger repairs."
- **What differs:** modality. The source states an order of events — the check comes first, then the decision. The heading states that the larger repairs determine the outcome. A sequence establishes no dependency, just as it establishes no cause.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** the check on larger repairs is a step the team will complete before deciding, but what actually settles expansion is next year's budget, or whether the two buildings' staff want to continue; the categories pass easily and change nothing about the outcome. Compatible with the material, which says only that the decision comes after the check and gives no criterion, threshold or consequence attached to it.
- **Set aside?** No supplied statement makes the check decisive — the material names no outcome the check would produce either way, and the draft's own body sentence (l.32) correctly says only "Teamet ska **först** kontrollera…".
- **Smallest repair:** "## Först ska kategorierna prövas på större reparationer" — or, keeping the decision in view without claiming what settles it, "## Teamet beslutar efter att ha prövat kategorierna på större reparationer".
