## 3. R1

**Pass.**

- **Defects addressed, nothing else touched.** All three changes answer defects a reader can see in this text without any source material: a headline that converted a trial median into a standing capability of the school, a subheading naming an actor the section contradicts, and a subheading putting a school's present-tense intention where the section holds one person's conditional. The clean body was not rewritten to taste — not one sentence of it moved. That restraint is precisely what R1 asks for.
- **Removals.** The only things removed are the two old subheadings and the old headline, each replaced in place. The reply reports all three under "Påståenden som rundan ändrade", names the old wording, and says what changed in it. Accurate in every case.
- **Changed claims, checked one by one.** #1: reported, and the reply's account is right — the median survives unchanged in both standfirst and body, so no figure was lost; its claim that the new headline "säger medianen med medianens egen styrka" is sound, since a 15-minute median does mean half the counted shifts were filled within a quarter of an hour. #2: reported, and the delivered section does name only "Skolan". #3: reported, and the reply names both dimensions that moved — attribution (school → rector) and modality (intention → conditional judgement). It explicitly states that no body sentence was removed or altered, which the diff confirms.
- **Preservation.** Voice, argument, both quotations, every figure and every bounding sentence survive. Nothing was supplied from outside the text; no change leans on material the Skill never had.
- **Residuals declared.** Both standing findings are damage this round produced — a subheading now sharing subject and head word with the sentence directly beneath it, and a title ("Rektorn") that only the standfirst has introduced — and the reply names each, explains which repair caused it, and states the minimal fix rather than spending another round correcting itself. The "Minsta rättningen är att namnge referenten där brödtexten först behöver den" is the right remedy for a body that must read without its standfirst.

**One reservation, not enough to fail.** The new headline's "passen i vikarieförsöket" is marginally wider than the counted base, which excludes acute sick reports after eight; the reply does not flag that residue, though it shows it knows the exclusion. The claim remains far closer to the text than the headline it replaced, and the body states the exclusion plainly.
