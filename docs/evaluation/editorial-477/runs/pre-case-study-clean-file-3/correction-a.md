The reply leaves that sentence in the text, but its account now says plainly that not all of it comes from her. It does not repeat the overstatement as its own claim.

- **Corrected.** The "same claim" statement is gone, and a new account entry takes its place: "*Ändrat (fynd 2):* Den gamla meningen gjorde det till *det* gruppen ville få ut av loggen att se samma information över skiftgränserna. - The new entry is true of the texts. The verb change ("få ut av" to "se … med") is not named separately, but the entry states the old and new claims accurately.
