**1. False statements**

- **"Utöver påståendena ovan gjorde körningen två slags ändringar: rubrikens attribution rättades, och inledningsstyckets första mening skrevs om till svensk ordföljd med subjektet först."** This is about the delivered text. The text has only three differences from the text as it arrived:
  - The headline: "säger Elm Quay" became "säger arbetsledare".
  - The lead's first sentence: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." became "Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna i sin gemensamma reparationslogg."
  - The lead's third sentence: "vad det gav" became "vad som hände".

  The claim account itself already lists all three as changed claims. The headline attribution is its first entry and the first-sentence rewrite is its third. So no change was made beyond the claims listed above. The sentence counts two of those same changes a second time as further changes outside the claims, which is not true.

- **Finding 3: "där en lång infinitivfras stod före subjektet". Repeated as "Meningen börjar nu med subjektet" and, in the account, "skrevs om till svensk ordföljd med subjektet först".** This is about the text as it arrived: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." In this reversed pseudo-cleft, the main clause is "[Att se …] var [vad … ville få ut av …]". The infinitive phrase is the subject of "var", and the vad-clause is the predicative. So the sentence as it arrived already began with its subject. "Underhållsgruppen" is only the subject inside the relative vad-clause. What is true: the rewrite changed the sentence from a pseudo-cleft with an infinitival subject to a plain clause with "Underhållsgruppen" as its subject. It did not move the subject from after a fronted phrase to first position.

**2. Claim changes the account misses**

- **Lead, first sentence.**
  - Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg."
  - After: "Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna i sin gemensamma reparationslogg."

  The pseudo-cleft put focus on, and limited, what the group wanted out of the log: seeing shared information across shifts was *the* thing they sought, the stated purpose of adopting it. The new sentence states this only as one wish the group had about the log. It no longer says this was their aim, or their only aim, in getting the log. The reader loses the "this and nothing else was the goal" framing. That framing set up the trial's yardstick, which the closing quote "Att ha en gemensam bild av ärendena hjälper oss" answers.

  The account says: "Inledningsstyckets första mening säger nu att underhållsgruppen ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna i sin gemensamma reparationslogg. Tidigare sa den att detta var vad gruppen ville få ut av loggen." Elsewhere it calls the change a word-order rewrite ("skrevs om till svensk ordföljd med subjektet först"). It gives both wordings but never says what the reader no longer has, which is the focus on this as the group's single stated purpose. It reports the change as smaller than what it did.
