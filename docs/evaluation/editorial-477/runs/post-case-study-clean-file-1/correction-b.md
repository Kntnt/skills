- **What `response.md` did:** corrected. In its place `response.md` says: "skrivit om brödtextens första mening till en vanlig svensk huvudsats."

The delivered sentence "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg ville underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna." is a main clause with inverted word order (omvänd ordföljd): the adverbial "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg" stands first, then the finite verb "ville", then the subject "underhållsgruppen". "Rak" read as a word-order label therefore contradicts it. The item is also right that the sentence as it arrived ("Att se … var vad …") has its subject before its finite verb.
