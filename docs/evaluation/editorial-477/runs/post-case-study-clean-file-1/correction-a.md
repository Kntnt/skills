## 1. The checker's items

**Item 1.** "skrivit om brödtextens första mening till en rak svensk huvudsats". The checker says this is false of the delivered text, because the sentence has inverted word order: the adverbial comes first, then the finite verb, then the subject.

- **Is the item right about the texts?** Yes. The delivered sentence is "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg ville underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna." The adverbial "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg" comes before the finite verb "ville", and the subject "underhållsgruppen" comes after it. That is omvänd ordföljd (inverted word order), not rak ordföljd (straight word order). The sentence as it arrived, "Att se samma information … var vad underhållsgruppen … ville få ut av …", has its subject before "var", so it was the one with straight word order. The checker also notes that "rak" read as "straightforward" would be a judgement of quality. That caveat is fair.
- **What `response.md` did:** **corrected.** It now says: "skrivit om brödtextens första mening till en vanlig svensk huvudsats". The sentence is a main clause. "vanlig" ("ordinary") is not a word-order label, so it is a judgement of quality, and the text cannot contradict it. The response did not follow the item into a false statement.
