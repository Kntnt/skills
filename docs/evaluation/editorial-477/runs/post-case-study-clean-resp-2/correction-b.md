## 1. The checker's items

`check.md` lists no false statements and one missed claim change.

**Item 1:** "**Inledningens första mening.** Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." After: "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg ville underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna." […] **Why that falls short:** It treats the change as lost emphasis ("utan samma betoning"), not as a claim that lost its "this, and only this" sense. Its wording "gruppens mål" can still be read as the group's single aim, which the delivered sentence no longer says."

- **Is the item right?** Yes. The input sentence (input.md line 7) is a pseudo-cleft. It names seeing the same information across shifts as *what* the group wanted to get out of the log. The delivered sentence (delivered.md line 10) says only that the group wanted to be able to do this with the log, and it does not rule out other aims. The draft's "gruppens mål med loggen, utan samma betoning" describes the change as one of emphasis. It also allows the reading that this was the group's single aim.
- **What response.md did:** **Corrected.** In place of the draft sentence, response.md (lines 18–19) says: "Den tidigare meningen pekade ut detta som just det gruppen ville få ut av loggen, alltså vad loggen skulle ge dem." and "Den nya meningen säger att det var något gruppen ville kunna göra med loggen. Den lämnar öppet om gruppen hade fler mål." Both texts bear this out. The account now carries what the item said it lacked: the shift from "get out of" to "be able to do with", and the fact that other aims are left open. The words "gruppens mål" and "utan samma betoning" are gone.
