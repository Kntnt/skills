**2. Claim changes the account misses**

- **Inledningens första mening.** Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." After: "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg ville underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna."
  - **What the change did:** The old sentence was a cleft ("X var vad Y ville få ut av Z"). It identified seeing the same information across shifts as *the* thing the group wanted to get out of the log. The new sentence makes it one thing the group wanted to be able to do with the log, and leaves room for other aims. That narrows a claim about what the group wanted. The new wording is also no longer about what they expected to *get out of* the log (its yield). It is now about what they wanted to *be able to do* with it.
  - **What the account says:** "Den tidigare meningen framhöll detta som just det gruppen ville få ut av loggen; den nya anger det som gruppens mål med loggen, utan samma betoning."
  - **Why that falls short:** It treats the change as lost emphasis ("utan samma betoning"), not as a claim that lost its "this, and only this" sense. Its wording "gruppens mål" can still be read as the group's single aim, which the delivered sentence no longer says.
