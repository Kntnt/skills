# Material: the Pellbrook homework club

Invented evaluation material. Pellbrook Library and every person named are fictional and describe no real organisation or client.

## 1. From the club's leaflet

- The homework club meets every Saturday in term time, 10:00 to 12:00, in the first-floor study room at Pellbrook Library.
- It is for pupils in years 7 to 11. It is free, and no booking is needed; pupils sign in at the door.
- Two volunteer tutors are there each week, one for maths and science and one for English and humanities.
- Laptops and a printer are available. Pupils bring their own homework.

## 2. Notes from a visit, Saturday 26 September 2026

- 14 pupils signed in.
- Amira, in year 9, has come every Saturday since March. "At home my little brothers are always shouting. Here I get my maths done before lunch." Her mother drops her off on the way to the market.
- The maths tutor, Colin Parry, is a retired teacher. "Most of them just need someone to ask when they're stuck."
