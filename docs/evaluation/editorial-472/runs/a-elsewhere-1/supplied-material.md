# Material: the weekly receipt habit

Invented evaluation material. Larkspur Ledger and every person named are fictional and describe no real company or client.

## 1. User survey, summer 2026

Larkspur Ledger asked its users how long their last Self Assessment return took them. 412 answered. Those who said they matched receipts at least once a week reported a median of 6 hours on the return. Those who said they left receipts until January reported a median of 19 hours. Of the 412, 57 said an accountant prepared their return, and they reported a median of 3 hours of their own time. The figures are self-reported and were not independently checked, and the survey does not show that the weekly habit caused the difference.

## 2. Interview with Priya Nand, maths tutor, Leeds, September 2026

- For four years she kept her receipts in a biscuit tin until January. "The last weekend of January was always gone."
- In March 2026 she moved to a session every Friday: "About ten minutes, with a cup of tea."
- Her steps: she photographs each receipt when she gets it, and on Friday she matches the week's photographs to her bank lines and notes anything odd.
- Two receipts from the previous spring had faded to blank before she ever looked at them, and she could not claim them.
- "January this year was an evening."

## 3. Note from the support team

Till receipts printed on thermal paper often fade within months, faster when kept somewhere warm. A photograph taken while the receipt is new stays readable. The app's receipt scanner reads the date, the supplier and the total from a photograph and suggests the bank line it matches.
