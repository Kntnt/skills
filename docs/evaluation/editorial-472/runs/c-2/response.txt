It is Sunday night, the maths homework is due in the morning, and the kitchen table is covered in books, plates and a younger brother's noise. For many families in Pellbrook, that is where homework gets done.

Amira is in year 9. "At home my little brothers are always shouting," she says. Since March she has spent her Saturday mornings somewhere else: the homework club at Pellbrook Library. Her mother drops her off on the way to the market. "Here I get my maths done before lunch."
