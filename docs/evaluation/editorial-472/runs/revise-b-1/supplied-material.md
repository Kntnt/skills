# Material: fixing it together at the Repair Café

Invented evaluation material. The Hatherley Repair Café and every person named are fictional and describe no real organisation or client.

## 1. From the volunteer handbook

- "We fix things with you, not for you. The owner stays with the item and does as much of the repair as they can."
- Volunteers cover small electrical items, bikes, clothes and textiles, and small furniture.
- "We do not repair microwaves or anything with a gas supply."
- Sessions run on a Saturday morning, 10:00 to 13:00, usually in St Anne's church hall.
- There is no charge. Tea is on the table.

## 2. Notes from the September session

- 31 items came in, among them a toaster, four bikes, two lamps and a pair of jeans.
- Jo Carr brought a standard lamp that had stopped working. With Dev, one of the volunteers, she found a loose wire in the plug and fitted it again herself. "I've never opened a plug in my life. Now I'd do it on my own."
- Two new volunteers joined: a retired electrician and a tailor.
