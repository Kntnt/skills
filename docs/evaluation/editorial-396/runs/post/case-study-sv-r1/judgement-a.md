It still has these defects:

- **The exclusion caveat is narrowed (scope).** The source says: "Elm Quay's internal trial note … says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial." "It" most naturally means the note, so the exclusion may also cover the medians. The draft attaches the exclusion to the count alone: "fördes 31 felanmälningar in i loggen, akuta ärenden och arbeten beställda före provet oräknade". The median sentence that follows carries no exclusion. A reader could take the two-versus-three-day figures as covering all work, emergencies included. This is a real but small scope change.
- **The byline is invented.** The brief says "No author name supplied", and the piece is supplier-published material. The draft prints "Av Thomas Barregren", which names a person as author of Svale's customer case. The material carries no such attribution. The reply discloses it and asks the user to change it, but the published text still asserts it.
- **"Helhetsbedömning" changes the term.** The source calls the final quotation "her actual, qualified assessment". "Helhetsbedömning" means an overall or comprehensive judgement. That is broader than "actual", and "qualified" drops out of the narration (the quotation itself still carries the qualification). A remark Lind made could count as her actual assessment without being her overall judgement. The shift is minor.
- **The tense changes, with a small modality shift.** The source says "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company" (past tense, about the interview). The draft says "hon rekommenderar inte Svale till varje bostadsbolag", in the present tense, which makes it a standing position. This is minor.

Everything else is supported: the chronology (September 2025 decision, eight weeks, note dated 4 December 2025), the six staff trained in two sessions, the telephone route kept open, the categories designed by the team, the email interview, and the choice made after testing whether the log could show each repair's status. The draft makes no claim about any comparison with another supplier.

**L1: pass, with blemishes.** The narration reads as native Swedish: "Hur provet föll ut beskriver …", "Teamet lät telefonen vara kvar som väg in för de boendes felanmälningar", "Siffran gäller alltså …". Two blemishes:

- "innan nästa hus sätter i gång" (quotation 2) copies English syntax. In Swedish a house does not "start". Readers stumble, and some may read it as a construction start.
- "lägga till en extra vecka för förberedelser" (quotation 3) echoes English "add". "avsätta" or "räkna med en extra vecka" would be more native.

The absolute construction "… oräknade" is idiomatic but stiff and official in tone.

2. **Quotation 2.**

   - **Quotation:** "Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att föra in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus sätter i gång"
   - **Why the speech tag is the bridge:** no narrative sentence sits between the subheading and the quotation. The previous sentence ("Siffran gäller alltså …") is under another heading and does not lead into this quotation.
   - **Class (b).** "om förarbetet" names the subject and nothing more.
   - **Other reading:** this quotation has no bridge at all. I chose (b) because the brief counts a speech tag that carries anything beyond the attribution as a bridge.
3. **Quotation 3.**
   - **Bridge:** "Hennes helhetsbedömning är ingen slutsats dragen ur siffrorna, och hon rekommenderar inte Svale till varje bostadsbolag."
   - **Quotation:** "Jag skulle välja att göra provet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en extra vecka för förberedelser"
   - **Speech tag:** "skriver hon".
   - **Class (c).** "ingen slutsats dragen ur siffrorna" and "rekommenderar inte Svale till varje bostadsbolag" are facts the quotation does not carry.
   - **Other reading:** "Hennes helhetsbedömning" names the occasion, which on its own would be (b). The two added facts decide it.
