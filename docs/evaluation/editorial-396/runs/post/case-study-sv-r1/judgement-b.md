## 2. F1: fail

**Unsupported additions and changed terms**

- **Byline "Av Thomas Barregren".** The brief says "No author name supplied", and the material names no writer. The draft credits authorship to a named person the material never mentions. For supplier-published material ("it is not independent journalism"), a personal byline also suggests an independent writer where the source only gives the publisher, Svale Systems. The reply discloses the choice and invites a change, but it stays in the delivered text. This is an invented attribution, and no checker raised it.
- **"Hennes helhetsbedömning är ingen slutsats dragen ur siffrorna".** The source says "Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment. It is not an inference from the figures."
  - "Helhetsbedömning" (overall or holistic assessment) is broader than "actual assessment". It presents one qualified quotation as her verdict on everything.
  - The word "qualified" is dropped from the narration. The quotation itself still carries the reservation ("men jag skulle lägga till en extra vecka"), so the caveat survives in substance.
  - This is a minor change of term. Checker finding S2 raised it, and it was left unrepaired.
- **Scope of the exclusions:** "fördes 31 felanmälningar in i loggen, akuta ärenden och arbeten beställda före provet oräknade".
  - The source's "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial" follows the sentence about the note and the 31 reports. "It" can mean either the count or the whole note.
  - The draft settles on the narrower reading. The median sentence that follows then carries no exclusion, although the material leaves it unknown whether the medians exclude emergencies.
  - This is a small narrowing of an uncertainty rather than an invented fact. Checker finding S1 raised it, and it was left unrepaired.

**Checked and within the material**

- **Figures and dates:** 640 flats, September 2025, two buildings, eight weeks, six staff, two sessions, the 4 December 2025 note, 31 reports, and a median of two against three working days.
- **Causal caveat:** "anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte programvaran skillnaden" is kept, together with the different workloads.

## 4. Bridges into quotations

1. **Quotation 1**
   - **Bridge:** "Det prövade underhållsteamet innan det valde Svale Systems, som också publicerar den här texten." (Before it: "Kunde loggen visa var varje reparation stod?") The tag "skriver Maya Lind" carries only the attribution.
   - **Quotation:** "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra, Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen"
   - **Class (c):** "prövade … innan det valde Svale Systems" and "som också publicerar den här texten" carry the selection test and the publisher's role. The quotation carries neither.
   - **Other reading:** because the sentence does not lead into the quotation's subject (the shifts and the categories), it can be read as no bridge at all. I chose (c) because it is the narrative sentence standing immediately before the quotation.
2. **Quotation 2**
   - **Bridge:** the speech tag "skriver Maya Lind om förarbetet". No narrative sentence stands between the subheading and the quotation.
   - **Quotation:** "Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att föra in de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus sätter i gång"
   - **Class (b):** "om förarbetet" names the subject. The quotation's point (categories took longer than entry, so set time aside) is not given in advance.
3. **Quotation 3**
   - **Bridge:** "Hennes helhetsbedömning är ingen slutsats dragen ur siffrorna, och hon rekommenderar inte Svale till varje bostadsbolag." The tag "skriver hon" carries only the attribution.
   - **Quotation:** "Jag skulle välja att göra provet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en extra vecka för förberedelser"
   - **Class (c):** "ingen slutsats dragen ur siffrorna" and "rekommenderar inte Svale till varje bostadsbolag" are facts the quotation does not carry.
   - **Other reading:** "helhetsbedömning" alone could be read as naming the occasion (b). The two added facts decide it as (c). It is not (a), because the bridge does not say she would repeat the trial or add a week.

No quotation stands without any bridge.

- **Counts:** the draft carries 0 bridges of class (a), 1 of class (b) and 2 of class (c).
- **Interviewer:** no question or utterance is attributed to an interviewer. The two questions in the text ("Hur ser kvällsskiftet …?" and "Kunde loggen visa …?") are the narrator's own rhetorical questions and are not put in anyone's mouth.
- **Unsupported assertion in a bridge:** one. The bridge into quotation 3 calls the quotation "Hennes helhetsbedömning" (her overall assessment), where the material says only "her actual, qualified assessment".
