# Elm Quay trial gives shifts one repair view

Elm Quay Housing's maintenance team spent eight weeks testing a shared repair log from Svale Systems in two buildings. Operations managers planning something similar can see what the preparation took, what an internal note shows and leaves open, and why maintenance supervisor Maya Lind would run the trial again with more time for preparation.

By Thomas Barregren

At Elm Quay Housing, which manages 640 flats, telephone reports and emails used to be stored separately. In September 2025, the company's own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings. How that went is recorded in an internal trial note and described by Elm Quay maintenance supervisor Maya Lind.

## Why Elm Quay wanted one log

The team chose Svale Systems to supply the log after testing whether it could show the status of each repair. Answering questions by email, Lind describes the starting point:

> “We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

## What the setup demanded

Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions. The team also kept telephone reporting open for residents.

Lind describes the setup from the team's side:

> “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

## The note does not credit the software for quicker assignment

The trial ran for eight weeks. Elm Quay's internal note on it, dated December 4, 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered; emergencies and work ordered before the trial are excluded.

Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, compared with three in the preceding eight weeks. The two periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software.

These figures concern assignment, not completion. There are no measurements of cost, resident satisfaction, or completion time.

## Maya Lind looks back on the trial

Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company. Of the trial itself, she writes:

> “I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

## Any expansion waits on larger repairs

The trial has not yet been expanded. Before deciding whether to expand it, the team will check how its categories work for larger repairs.

Operations managers weighing a similar trial can start by reading the [implementation checklist](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist).
