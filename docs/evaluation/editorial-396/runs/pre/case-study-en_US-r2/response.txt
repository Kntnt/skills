# Repair trial gives Elm Quay's team one view

Elm Quay Housing, which manages 640 apartments, tested a shared repair log from Svale Systems in two of its buildings. Its maintenance supervisor would run the trial again, with an extra week for preparation—a lesson for operations managers planning something similar.

By Thomas Barregren

At Elm Quay Housing, telephone reports and emails used to be stored separately. In September 2025 the company's own maintenance team decided to test a shared log in two buildings so that staff on different shifts could see the same information. After the eight-week trial, the team had a figure that Elm Quay's own trial note does not credit to the software, and a qualified verdict from its supervisor.

## Elm Quay tested repair tracking before picking Svale

In an email interview, maintenance supervisor Maya Lind puts the aim concretely: “We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done.”

The team chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair. No comparison with another supplier is available.

## Elm Quay designed the categories, and Svale set up the system

Residents kept the option of reporting repairs by telephone; the team left that route open. Svale Systems configured the log and trained six staff members in two sessions, and Lind is precise about the division of work: “The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

Lind draws a practical lesson from the work: “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

## Median assignment time fell, but the note does not credit software

Elm Quay's internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial.

Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, against three in the preceding eight-week period. The two periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software.

Nor is assignment the same as completion. There are no measurements of completion time, cost, or resident satisfaction.

## The supervisor would repeat the trial with more preparation

Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company. The assessment Lind does give is a qualified one, and it is not drawn from the figures: “I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

## Expansion decision waits on how the categories handle larger repairs

The trial has not yet expanded. The team will decide after checking how its categories work for larger repairs. That leaves Elm Quay with a shared view its supervisor says helps, a figure its note does not credit to the software, and a decision still ahead.
