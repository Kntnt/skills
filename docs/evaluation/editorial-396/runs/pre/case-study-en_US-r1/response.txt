# Elm Quay's trial puts repairs in one view

Elm Quay Housing's maintenance team spent eight weeks testing a shared repair log from Svale Systems in two buildings. Maintenance supervisor Maya Lind would run the trial again, with an extra week of preparation. This case shows what the preparation took, what the trial figures show and what they leave open.

By Thomas Barregren

In September 2025, the maintenance team at Elm Quay Housing, which manages 640 flats, decided to test a shared repair log in two of its buildings. The trial that followed lasted eight weeks. It left the team with a first set of figures, advice about preparation and a question to settle before it goes further.

## Elm Quay wanted its shifts to share one picture

Before the trial, telephone reports and emails were stored separately. “We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done,” maintenance supervisor Maya Lind wrote in an email interview.

The team chose Svale Systems after testing whether its log could show the status of each repair.

## The maintenance team designed its own categories

The team kept telephone reporting open for residents. “The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log,” Lind wrote. Svale also configured the log and trained six staff members in two sessions.

Lind also offered advice about that work. “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

## Median assignment time fell, but the note does not credit software

Elm Quay's internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, says 31 repair reports were entered. The note excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial.

The median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period. The two periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. There are no measurements of cost, resident satisfaction or completion time.

## Lind would repeat the trial, with an extra week to prepare

Lind's assessment is not an inference from the figures. “I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

She did not recommend Svale to every housing company.

## Elm Quay will check its categories against larger repairs

The trial has not yet been expanded. The team will decide after checking how its categories work for larger repairs. Operations managers considering a shared repair log of their own can read the [implementation checklist](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist).
