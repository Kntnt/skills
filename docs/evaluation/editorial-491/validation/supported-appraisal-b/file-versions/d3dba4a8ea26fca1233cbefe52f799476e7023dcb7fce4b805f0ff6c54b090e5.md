# Validation b

Assessment based only on `text.md` and the eight supplied files under `contract/`.

## 1. Headline: supported

> Lättare överlämning, säger Granhamn om loggförsöket

The headline reports Granhamn's appraisal that handover became easier in the logging trial. “säger Granhamn” carries the attribution; “om loggförsöket” bounds the subject. It does not assert that the supplier has proved a general improvement in repair performance.

The customer voice and the experience claimed are explicit in the body:

> – Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen. Den gemensamma ärendebilden gör överlämningen lättare för oss, men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser, säger Holm.

The speaker's relationship to the customer has already been supplied:

> säger Eva Holm, arbetsledare på Granhamn.

The lead bounds the trial:

> Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus.

Using the customer's name for its identified work supervisor's account does not turn her statement into a claim of unanimity. “för oss” supports the customer-level experience. The wish for another preparation week qualifies the experience without contradicting easier handover.

The measured comparison concerns something different:

> Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar. Under de föregående åtta veckorna var den tre.

Its causal limit is explicit:

> Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran.

The headline neither converts this comparison into a software effect nor substitutes it for the customer's appraisal. Its attribution, certainty and trial-specific scope are supported. It also tells a reader seeing only the headline the benefit, the speaker and the subject; another possible headline is not evidence of failure.

## 2. Lead and opening: working

The customer's aim is stated directly:

> För att kunna följa ärendena även efter ett skiftbyte ville underhållsgruppen på Granhamn Bostäder pröva en gemensam reparationslogg.

The group wanted to try a shared repair log to retain visibility of cases across a shift change. The sentence states an aim and an intended trial, not a guarantee that every case would become traceable or that a performance target would be reached.

The reading promise is:

> Vad det krävde, och vad det gav, går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar.

The body supplies concrete implementation work:

> Kategorierna i loggen var underhållsgruppens egna. Leverantören Lärk konfigurerade loggen efter dem och utbildade sex medarbetare.

It also supplies the customer's account of preparation:

> – Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första ärendena. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar, säger Eva Holm, arbetsledare på Granhamn.

Results have an identified documentary basis and an explicit exclusion:

> Enligt Granhamns interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025 registrerades 31 ärenden under åtta veckor. Akuta ärenden och tidigare beställda arbeten ingick inte.

The median comparison, workload qualification and closing appraisal quoted above complete the account. The lead does not promise an exhaustive requirements specification, exact costs or preparation hours, a causal evaluation, or a measured outcome for every aspect of the aim. Their absence therefore does not leave this reading promise unfulfilled. The customer's handover appraisal supplies a result relevant to the stated aim.

The standfirst begins:

> Granhamn Bostäder provade en gemensam reparationslogg i två hus under åtta veckor.

The lead repeats the trial's identity and bounds so the body can stand alone, while adding the previously unstated shift-change purpose and identifying the maintenance group. It advances the account. The shorter statement “pröva en gemensam reparationslogg” might also work, but that possibility establishes no concrete reader loss in the existing wording.

## 3. References: resolved

### Standfirst read independently

> Granhamn Bostäder provade en gemensam reparationslogg i två hus under åtta veckor. Arbetsledaren Eva Holm vill göra om försöket, men skulle avsätta mer tid för förberedelser. Här är vad gruppen gjorde, vad anteckningarna visar och vad hon skulle ändra.

There is no genuinely unresolved reference:

- “försöket” names the trial just described by “provade en gemensam reparationslogg”.
- “gruppen” means the Granhamn participants carrying out that trial. Their precise departmental name is a particular the body supplies, not a missing referent necessary to understand the standfirst.
- “anteckningarna” introduces the trial records as the source of what the article will show. A document date is not needed to understand that reference; the body later supplies “Granhamns interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025”.
- “hon” has the expressly named antecedent “Arbetsledaren Eva Holm”.
- “Här” points to the article presenting that account.

## 4. Other editorial failures: none identified

The customer acts rather than being rescued by the supplier:

> Kategorierna i loggen var underhållsgruppens egna.

The supplier is named in third person, and the publication interest is disclosed:

> Kundcaset publiceras av leverantören Lärk.

The number “31 ärenden” receives its exclusions, without an unsupported high/low appraisal. The sentence about different workloads is necessary: removing it would remove the express limit on attributing the median difference to the software. It is not redundant filler.

The subheadings leave the quotations their distinctive judgements. “Gruppen enades om kategorierna först” orients the reader to establishing the categories before configuring the log; it does not pre-spend the quotation's relative time expenditure or next-time reservation. “Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning” identifies the comparison's setting and limitation. “Arbetsledaren ser tillbaka på försöket” names the occasion for the customer's closing appraisal without stating that appraisal ahead of her.

The closing appraisal answers the opening's question about what the trial gave and ends with a preparation reservation. It functions as the permitted closing reflection, followed by the relevant next step:

> Den som står inför samma förberedelser kan börja i [Lärks checklista för införandet](https://example.invalid/lark/checklist).

It does not open a separate line of argument. The action concerns the preparation the account has described and uses the link present in the supplied text.

The prescribed parts and order are present. Counts meet the limits: headline 51 characters and six words; standfirst 39 words; lead 41 words; all body paragraphs at most 41 words; subheadings 36, 39 and 38 characters; each of the three sections has two paragraphs. The lead and standfirst begin with different words.

**Absent information:** exact preparation hours, cost, a named next house, the previous period's case count and a workload-adjusted effect estimate are not supplied. None is promised as such, and none is necessary to understand the qualified account. Absence does not establish that preparation failed, that handover did not improve or that the software had no effect.

**Contradicted information:** no passage contradicts the headline's attributed handover appraisal or the lead's trial purpose. The workload sentence expressly limits a software-effect attribution that the text does not make.

The remaining chains are accounted for above without using the headings or standfirst.
