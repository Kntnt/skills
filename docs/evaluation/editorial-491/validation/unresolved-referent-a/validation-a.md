# Validation a

## 1. Headline: appraisal, attribution and scope

> Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quay om loggförsöket

The headline says that a shared view of the cases helps, attributes that appraisal to the customer, Elm Quay, and confines it to the log trial. It does not claim a measured software effect or a benefit established for every implementation.

The decisive support is the customer's own quotation:

> – Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen. Att ha en gemensam bild av ärendena hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser, säger Lind.

The text has already identified the speaker as:

> Maya Lind, arbetsledare på Elm Quay.

Attributing the customer appraisal to Elm Quay is supported by its identified supervisor speaking about what helps “oss”. It does not imply a survey or unanimous customer opinion. The trial's extent is supplied in the lead: “Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus.” The headline's “om loggförsöket” preserves that local scope, while “säger Elm Quay” preserves the appraisal's status as customer testimony. The quotation's preparation reservation remains visible in the complete text; the headline does not promise an unqualified implementation success.

The numerical comparison also retains its separate evidential limit:

> Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran.

**The headline is supported in attribution, certainty and scope.**

## 2. Lead: aim, promise and reader effect

> Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg. Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus. Vad det krävde, och vad det gav, går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar.

The customer's aim is shared information across shifts. The lead promises an account of the trial's practical demands and what emerged from it. It does not promise exhaustive technical requirements, a target achieved to a specified degree, or a causal evaluation of the software.

The body supplies implementation work:

> Kategorierna i loggen var underhållsgruppens egna. Leverantören Svale konfigurerade loggen efter dem och utbildade sex medarbetare.

It supplies the customer's assessment of preparation:

> – Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första ärendena. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar, säger Maya Lind, arbetsledare på Elm Quay.

And it supplies documented observations:

> Enligt Elm Quays interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025 registrerades 31 ärenden under åtta veckor. Akuta ärenden och tidigare beställda arbeten ingick inte. Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar. Under de föregående åtta veckorna var den tre.

The different-workload qualification quoted in section 1 prevents the reader from treating that comparison as a software effect. Lind's closing “Att ha en gemensam bild av ärendena hjälper oss” supplies the customer's assessment of the benefit sought.

Read after the standfirst, the lead adds the previously unstated aim, “Att se samma information över skiftgränserna”. Naming the log, customer, duration and extent again gives the independent body its necessary setting. This is the anatomy contract's permitted naming again in passing while the lead advances. “Vad det krävde” can successfully introduce the practical work described here; preferring a narrower expression would offer another working wording without identifying a concrete reader loss.

**The opening is working.** The unidentified alternative discussed below damages one body passage; it does not make the opening's broader promise defective.

## 3. References: standfirst independently, body without paratext

### Standfirst

> Elm Quay Housing provade en gemensam reparationslogg i två hus under åtta veckor. Arbetsledaren Maya Lind vill göra om försöket, men skulle avsätta mer tid för förberedelser. Här är vad gruppen gjorde, vad anteckningarna visar och vad hon skulle ändra.

There is no genuinely unresolved reference in this independent entry point. “Försöket” is the log trial just described; “hon” is Maya Lind. “Gruppen” identifies the customer's trial participants in the context of a repair log and their supervisor, and “anteckningarna” identifies the trial records. The reader understands the promised account without needing the participants' individual identities or the records' bibliographic particulars. The body supplies the more specific descriptions “underhållsgruppen” and “Elm Quays interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025”. Their greater specificity does not establish that the standfirst loses its reader.

### Body

The genuinely unresolved reference is:

> Det andra alternativet avvisades innan försöket började.

Neither the preceding lead nor any later passage identifies a first alternative, a pair of alternatives, or what the rejected second option was. The reader cannot understand the choice being reported. The complete text does **not** supply the information needed to name the option accurately. “Kategorierna i loggen var underhållsgruppens egna” cannot establish what an unspecified alternative contained. A repair that named it would invent information.

The other potentially suspect references are settled in the body itself:

| Passage | Context that settles it |
| --- | --- |
| “Försöket pågick i åtta veckor” | The preceding sentence introduces the group's shared repair log and what it wanted from it. |
| “Kategorierna i loggen” / “konfigurerade loggen efter dem” | The lead introduces the log; “dem” refers to the categories just named. |
| “Den tiden skulle jag avsätta” | The preceding quoted sentence specifies time spent agreeing on categories. |
| “innan nästa hus börjar” | The trial already encompasses two houses; in this discussion of future preparation, the next house means the next house to start using the log. No particular building's identity is needed. |
| “Under de föregående åtta veckorna var den tre” | “Den” refers to “Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning”; “föregående” locates the comparison before the trial's eight weeks. |
| “Perioderna” / “anteckningen” / “skillnaden” | The immediately preceding paragraph supplies both eight-week periods, the dated internal note and the two-versus-three-day comparison. |
| “programvaran” | The shared log that the supplier configured is the software under discussion. |
| “säger Lind” / “hjälper oss” | The first quotation introduces Maya Lind as Elm Quay's supervisor; the lead names the maintenance group. |
| “Den som står inför samma förberedelser” | The quotations describe agreeing on categories and allowing additional preparation time before a further rollout. |

These references remain intelligible with the standfirst and all headings removed. No heading supplies the missing identity of “Det andra alternativet”.

## 4. Other editorial failures and absent information

No additional concrete editorial failure is established under this contract.

The customer's agency is explicit in “Kategorierna i loggen var underhållsgruppens egna”; the supplier appears in third person in “Leverantören Svale konfigurerade loggen efter dem och utbildade sex medarbetare”. The count has exclusions, the comparison has a causal qualification, and the customer supplies an appraisal and reservation. None requires invented praise or an inferred result.

The first subheading, “Gruppen enades om kategorierna först”, does not spend the quotation's distinct comparison of preparation time with entry time or Lind's next-time recommendation. “Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning” names the comparison's setting and limitation. “Arbetsledaren ser tillbaka på försöket” names the speaker and occasion while leaving her appraisal to the quotation. These are working under the headline review guidance.

The final section closes with the customer's appraisal and preparation recommendation, followed by a relevant next step:

> Kundcaset publiceras av leverantören Svale. Den som står inför samma förberedelser kan börja i [Svales checklista för införandet](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist).

It introduces no separate line of argument. The publisher's interest is disclosed. The required anatomy is present in order. The headline is 59 characters and eight words; the standfirst is 40 words, the lead 43, every body paragraph is below 80 words, and every subheading is below 70 characters. Each section has two paragraphs.

The absent information is the identity and framing of the rejected alternative. That absence does not contradict the assertion that an alternative was rejected; it prevents the reader from understanding which choice the assertion reports. No passage contradicts the headline's appraisal or the lead's aim.

## 5. Classifications and contract conflict

- **Headline: supported.** “Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quay om loggförsöket” is carried by the identified customer's “Att ha en gemensam bild av ärendena hjälper oss”. Attribution and trial scope are retained; the text separately withholds causal attribution of the median-time difference.
- **Opening: working.** “Att se samma information över skiftgränserna” adds the customer's aim; categories, configuration, training, preparation testimony, qualified measurements and the customer's appraisal fulfil “Vad det krävde, och vad det gav”. No concrete opening loss is established.
- **Body's references: unresolved.** “Det andra alternativet avvisades innan försöket började” has no identifiable alternative anywhere in the complete text. The other references are contextually resolved.

The unresolved alternative conflicts with the base contract's requirement to keep reference clear and give readers what they need to understand the account, and with the case-study requirement to explain the customer's choices. The contract supplies no licence to invent its identity: its review guidance requires reporting a finding when the necessary information is absent. No further contract conflict is established.
