# Validation a

This review uses only `text.md` and the eight existing files under `contract/`. It judges passages in their reading order, the standfirst independently, and the body without headings or the standfirst.

1. **Headline: supported.**

   > Lättare överlämning, säger Granhamn om loggförsöket

   The headline reports Granhamn's appraisal of handover during the log trial. The customer carries that appraisal through its named foreman, rather than through the supplier or the writer:

   > Den gemensamma ärendebilden gör överlämningen lättare för oss, men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser, säger Holm.

   Her identity is established in the first quotation's attribution:

   > säger Eva Holm, arbetsledare på Granhamn.

   “Säger Granhamn” preserves the status of a customer judgement. “Om loggförsöket” bounds its subject; the body supplies the trial's dimensions:

   > Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus.

   Neither headline nor quotation claims a measured improvement throughout the customer's housing stock. Nor does the headline attribute the recorded assignment-time difference to the software. The body explicitly bounds that separate comparison:

   > Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran.

   Attribution, certainty and scope are supported. A headline naming Holm instead would also work, but the existing attribution causes no reader loss.

2. **Opening: working.**

   The lead states the customer's aim precisely:

   > För att kunna följa ärendena även efter ett skiftbyte ville underhållsgruppen på Granhamn Bostäder pröva en gemensam reparationslogg.

   This is an aim to try a shared log for continuity across shifts. It does not assert that the trial proved continuity quantitatively or that the software caused faster assignment. The lead then promises an account of the trial's demands and returns:

   > Vad det krävde, och vad det gav, går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar.

   The body supplies concrete implementation demands:

   > Kategorierna i loggen var underhållsgruppens egna. Leverantören Lärk konfigurerade loggen efter dem och utbildade sex medarbetare.

   > Vi lade mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första ärendena.

   It supplies recorded results with their exclusions:

   > Enligt Granhamns interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025 registrerades 31 ärenden under åtta veckor. Akuta ärenden och tidigare beställda arbeten ingick inte. Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar. Under de föregående åtta veckorna var den tre.

   The workload qualification quoted above and Holm's appraisal supply the limits and the practical return. “Vad det krävde” promises this account, not an exhaustive specification or quantified budget. There is no unmet promise requiring such additions.

   The standfirst establishes the trial and offers “vad gruppen gjorde, vad anteckningarna visar och vad hon skulle ändra”. The lead advances by introducing the shift-change problem. Its repetition of “åtta veckor” and “två hus” names the necessary setting in passing so the body works independently. It does not merely repeat the standfirst. More specific introductory wording would be another successful choice, not a remedy for a concrete loss.

3. **References: one unresolved expression in the body; none in the independent standfirst.**

   The unresolved passage is in the first quotation:

   > Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar, säger Eva Holm, arbetsledare på Granhamn.

   “Nästa hus börjar” leaves the activity that begins unstated. A house is used as the acting party, but the quotation gives “börjar” no completion. The reader must supply the step to which the preparation should precede. This is a missing connection in the quoted wording, rather than a demand to identify the particular property.

   The complete text supplies enough context to make the general log activity explicit: the lead names the aim to “pröva en gemensam reparationslogg”, and the preceding paragraph names configuration and staff training. It does not supply a decision about a new trial versus routine rollout, a property name or a start date; a repair must not add those particulars or turn Holm's conditional remark into an established plan.

   The following references are settled in context and are not findings:

   - In the independent standfirst, “försöket” refers to “provade en gemensam reparationslogg i två hus under åtta veckor”; “hon” refers to “Arbetsledaren Eva Holm”. In “vad gruppen gjorde”, the group is the customer team conducting that trial. The exact department name is unnecessary to understand the standfirst; the body subsequently names it as “underhållsgruppen”.
   - In the body, “Kategorierna i loggen” follows the lead's “gemensam reparationslogg”. In “Leverantören Lärk konfigurerade loggen efter dem”, “dem” refers to the immediately preceding categories.
   - “Den tiden” refers to “mer tid på att enas om kategorierna än på att lägga in de första ärendena”. It does not require an unstated number of hours.
   - In “Under de föregående åtta veckorna var den tre”, “den” refers to “Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning”. The trial's eight weeks supply the comparison period's temporal anchor.
   - “Perioderna” refers to those two eight-week periods; “anteckningen” refers to “Granhamns interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025”. Neither needs the subheading.
   - “Holm” has already been introduced as “Eva Holm, arbetsledare på Granhamn”; “för oss” refers to her customer team.
   - “Den som står inför samma förberedelser” follows the discussion of category agreement and Holm's “en vecka för förberedelser”. The link text “Lärks checklista för införandet” consequently concerns introducing the shared log.

4. **No other concrete editorial failure found.**

   The customer acts through its own categories and its decision to try the log. Lärk is named in third person, and publication interest is disclosed:

   > Kundcaset publiceras av leverantören Lärk.

   The ending combines the customer's qualified appraisal with a relevant next step:

   > Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen.

   > Den som står inför samma förberedelser kan börja i [Lärks checklista för införandet](https://example.invalid/lark/checklist).

   The standfirst's “vill göra om försöket” conveys the preference expressed by “Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen”; it does not announce a scheduled repeat. The first subheading, “Gruppen enades om kategorierna först”, gives the preparation setting supported by the customer's categories and configuration “efter dem”. It leaves the quotation's comparison of effort and recommendation for next time to the quotation. “Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning” states the comparison's qualification. “Arbetsledaren ser tillbaka på försöket” names the occasion without spending her appraisal.

   Absent information is distinct from contradiction: the omitted completion of “nästa hus börjar” is the reference finding above. Exact preparation hours, detailed categories and a future rollout schedule are not supplied, but the text does not promise them. No passage contradicts the customer's appraisal or the numerical comparison with its stated limits.

   The required parts and order are present. The headline has 51 characters and six words; the standfirst has 39 words; body paragraphs have 11–41 words; subheadings have 36–39 characters. All three sections have two paragraphs. No dimension failure is present.
