## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the standfirst edit together with its finding: "”innan den ena stängs” framställde det som givet att en av bokningsvägarna ska stängas efter försöket. Brödtexten håller den frågan öppen … Jag strök bisatsen."

This is a clean control text, and the defect the finding names is not visible in it. The clause "innan den ena stängs" says the same thing as the thesis ("bör skjuta upp bytet") and the headline ("Avskaffa inte telefonbokningen på ett antagande"). The author asks for a trial before the planned closure, not for the closure to be dropped. The body's "Först då går det att väga alternativen" does not contradict that. Striking the clause changed a claim in the standfirst on the basis of a misreading, which is exactly the kind of rewrite R1 forbids for clean texts: "Clean texts may not be rewritten." Everything else the expectation requires is preserved, and the reply reports the change openly. Neither point undoes an unwarranted change to a claim in a text that should have come back unchanged.
