# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

The frontmatter, formatting, headline, standfirst, byline, subheadings and every paragraph are identical except this one:

- Introduction, second sentence. Before: "Pröva först båda bokningsvägarna i alla sju lokaler under ett halvår och mät vad de kräver." After: "Pröva först båda bokningsvägarna, digital bokning och telefonbokning, i alla sju lokaler under ett halvår och mät vad de kräver." Class: a change of taste presented as a repair. It adds an appositive that names the two booking paths. The claim keeps its scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning. The two paths were already clear in context: the headline directly above names "telefonbokningen", and the sentence before this one says "bytet till enbart digital bokning", where "enbart" implies a second, non-digital path. No reader meets the introduction without the headline, so the text had no visible defect here. The addition also makes the paragraph slightly redundant, since "digital bokning" now appears in two sentences in a row.

## 4. R1

**Fail**, narrowly. Preservation is complete, and the run avoided every rejection the expectation names. But this control text conforms to the anatomy, and the run rewrote it anyway: "Pröva först båda bokningsvägarna, digital bokning och telefonbokning, i alla sju lokaler …". The finding behind that change addresses no visible defect. The headline "Avskaffa inte telefonbokningen på ett antagande" and the preceding phrase "enbart digital bokning" already identify both paths. So the edit is a rewrite of a clean text to satisfy a rule applied more literally than the text required, which R1 forbids. The change harms nothing, but R1 does not allow a clean text to be rewritten at all, however small the change.
