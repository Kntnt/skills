## 4. R1

**Fail.** The decision rests on this passage:

> **Fynd (åtgärdat):** … Att kalla försöket ”kort” är en värdering som behöver stöd i en jämförelse eller i någons bedömning. Texten ger inget sådant stöd, eftersom den bara anger längden, fyra veckor.

This is a clean control text, and "ett kort försök" is not a concrete visible defect. The author's own judgement is that a four-week measurement trial is short. That judgement is plain and does not need a source. It also fits the text's argument that a trial of this kind shows when the air was cold but not why. The sentence does not say the trial's length caused the limitation, so the Skill's second reason ("lade ordet … försökets begränsning i dess längd") gives the sentence a meaning it does not have. The rewrite removes the author's characterization and narrows the sentence to this one trial. That is the kind of rewriting of a clean text on editorial taste that R1 rejects, and it is a loss of a working claim. The Skill reported the change transparently and accurately, and everything else the expectation lists was detected correctly or preserved. The change is a single word and minor, but it is still an unwarranted change of meaning in a text that should have been returned unchanged.
