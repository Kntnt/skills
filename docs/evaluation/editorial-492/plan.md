Each run preserves exact invocation, input, response, captured file delivery, complete private-root inventories and deltas, native parent and child traces, and transient Markdown/text file versions captured externally at 100 ms. Captures do not change the Skill's process or its cleanup obligations. The native process and runner each have their own process group, are immediately registered through session-cleanup with state confined to assigned scratch, and are waited on. Failure to provide that supported native seat or capability stops this evaluation; it is neither non-reproduction nor a product finding. The exact smoke prompt and outcome are retained.

Runs use random neutral packet names in assigned scratch. A builder-only mapping links each packet to its matrix row. Runs of one input execute sequentially; at most four inputs may be in flight. Preserve the early proposal to delay the self-service transition, the trial of both alternatives, the attribution and loan counts, the distinction between loans and visitors' needs, the relevant staffing-cost objection, the unknown trial cost, the council's named next step and the polemical final sentence. Reply-visible false findings, locale and side effects remain answerable in the response run.

**Split rule copied from #479/#480:** R1 and C1 pass when both judges pass, fail when both fail; a split fails unless the deciding difference is accurately covered by the reply's claim account or closing summary, in which case both readings are preserved and the split meets that criterion. This rule does not decide the Target: an accurate account never establishes that a clarification was needed. Both historical and new splits retain both readings and their evidence.
