# Reading validation

The assessment below uses the headline and body in their order, with the bold standfirst covered.

## Alternatives

The lead asks the reader to consider retaining staffed lending alongside self-service, or moving to self-service alone. The headline, “Ta inte bort bibliotekarien på ett antagande”, identifies the librarian whose service would be removed. The lead names the proposed change: “övergången till enbart självbetjäning på Lindby bibliotek”. Together, these identify what “båda alternativen” refers to through ordinary contextual inference.

The subsequent body confirms the two lending routes: “400 lån i automaten och 100 lån vid den bemannade disken”. It later describes visitors choosing “disken eller automaten”. This does not require the reader to infer a staffed-only library as the other alternative.

No additional naming or expansion is needed. The standfirst’s “bemannad utlåning på Lindby bibliotek ersättas av enbart självbetjäning” states explicitly a relationship already available from the headline and lead.

## Unresolved referents in the lead

None remain unresolved with the standfirst covered. There are therefore no missing referents for which a standfirst quotation and a minimal clarification are required.

In particular, “båda alternativen” has the contextual referents identified above. “Besökarna” denotes the visitors to the named library. “Som föreslås till december” describes the transition already named in the sentence. The implicit addressee of “Pröva” and “undersök” is the “Kulturnämnden” addressed in the preceding sentence. The definite “övergången” is specified by “till enbart självbetjäning på Lindby bibliotek”. “Under våren” gives the proposed trial period; the standfirst supplies no additional year or temporal anchor.

## Classification

**working**

The deciding passage is the headline “Ta inte bort bibliotekarien på ett antagande”, read together with the lead: “Kulturnämnden bör skjuta upp övergången till enbart självbetjäning på Lindby bibliotek, som föreslås till december. Pröva först båda alternativen under våren och undersök vad besökarna behöver.” The staffed service and the self-service alternative are identifiable before any dependence on the standfirst arises. Repeating their names would be an editorial preference, not a repair of a missing referent.

## Other visible contradictions or broken inferences

None identified. Postponing the proposed December transition allows a spring trial. The text consistently distinguishes counts of loans from evidence about visitors’ needs, and it acknowledges that retaining both lending routes entails work whose cost must be assessed.
