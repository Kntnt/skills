**Technique:** for the `report` genre I suggest **PAC** (premise, analysis, conclusion), which is the genre's usual technique. The board is checking something rather than being carried along, and PAC lets them trace the path from the facts through the analysis to the conclusion. For your information, the genre advises against ABT: told as a problem and its consequence, a report hands a story to readers who came for a result they can act on and argue with.
