The angle is recorded: what the September sign-in sheets show about where the overrun time goes.

---

**Question 6 of 12: What is the hook?**

The hook is the tension that makes the reader want to read to the end: the question, conflict or surprise the text promises to resolve. It carries the angle, and in a PAC structure it becomes the premise. The hook isn't the same as the opening, although in short texts the two often coincide. The reader needs to understand what's at stake before the hook arrives.

In a report, the hook is usually the question the board asked, put in their own terms.
