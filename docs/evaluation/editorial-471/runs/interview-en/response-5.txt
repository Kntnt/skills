**Question 5 of 12: What is the text's angle?**

The angle is the one particular perspective you take on the topic. Choose only one. The most common mistake is trying to cover the topic from every direction. The result says a little about a lot and is hard to take in. If you see several good angles, each can be its own text. Choose the angle that lies closest to the reader and can carry the message. The angle governs the hook, the structure and the conclusion.
