# Swap seeds with the Hollin Seed Circle

Come with a single packet of leftover seed, or with none at all. Either way, you can leave our seed swap with seed for the season.

We are the Hollin Seed Circle, a volunteer association of home gardeners. The Seed Circle is open to anyone who grows something, not only to experienced gardeners. If this is your first swap, you are welcome whether or not you bring seed.

## What to bring

If you have leftover seed, bring it in a labelled envelope. If you have none, come anyway.

## What happens at the swap

The seed is set out on tables by kind of plant. Choose from the tables, and note on the newcomers' sheet what you take.

That is all we ask of newcomers. The swap runs on what our members share, which is why you can leave with seed even if you arrived without any.

## Spring swap: 14 March 2027

Bring that leftover packet if you have one, or come without. Either way, you can still leave with seed for the season.

Come to the spring swap on 14 March.
