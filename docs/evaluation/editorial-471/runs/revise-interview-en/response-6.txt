**5. Angle:** What the September sign-in sheets show about where the overrun time goes.

That's a single angle, so we can move on.

---

**6. What is the hook?**

The hook is the tension that makes the board want to read to the end: the question, conflict or surprise the report promises to resolve. It carries the angle. The reports genre is normally written with PAC, and in PAC the hook becomes the **premise**.

The hook isn't necessarily the opening. The reader should understand what's at stake before the hook arrives. In a report, that usually means the question the board asked, stated in their own terms.
