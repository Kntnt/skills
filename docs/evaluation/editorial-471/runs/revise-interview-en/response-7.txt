**7. How should the text be structured?**

A report is normally written with **PAC (Premise, Analysis, Conclusion)**. The board is checking something rather than being carried along, and PAC lets them trace the path from the facts, through the analysis, to the answer. The genre explicitly advises against ABT, because a story built around a complication gives the board a narrative when what they came for is a result they can act on and argue with. The choice is still yours.

Two things to note about the report genre:
- **The answer comes first.** The report opens by saying what it covers, what it's for and which period it speaks to (September). The finding goes at the top, before the evidence.
- **Headings name what each section found,** not just what it contains. For example, "Setup takes the first half-hour" rather than "Analysis".
