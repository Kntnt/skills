That gives us both question 3 and question 4.

**3. Message:** The volunteer coordinators have the sessions under control and can fix the overruns themselves.

**4. Reader:** The library's seven board members. They know the sessions exist but not how they run. They care about the volunteers' wellbeing and about the terms on which the library rents its premises. They asked for this report and will read it before the November board meeting, where the session schedule is decided. That meeting is the "why now", and it should show in the structure.

---

**5. What is the text's angle?**

The angle is the one perspective the report takes on the evening repair sessions. Choose just one. The most common mistake is trying to cover the topic from every direction, which leaves a text that says a little about a lot. If you see several good angles, each can become a report of its own.

Pick the angle that lies closest to the board and can carry the message. The angle then shapes the hook, the structure and the conclusion.
