# Judgement: brief run, draft mode

**B1, mode and language: pass.** The run was asked "Gör ett utkast till en brief från anteckningarna" and delivered one draft from `notes.md` in a single turn, in Swedish ("Jag har skrivit utkastet till `brief.md`. Det bygger på `notes.md` …"). As a draft, it asks no questions one at a time, which is correct.

**B2, the headings and markers: pass.** `brief.md` has the twelve headings in template order and in Swedish, from "## 1. Vad är uppdraget?" to "## 12. Vilka källor känner du till, och vad behöver du ta reda på?". The only markers are `[MISSING: …]` and `[SUGGESTED: …]`, both in English.

**B3, message and conclusion: pass.** Question 3 holds the sender's message ("[SUGGESTED: Med söndagsöppet blir Norrmyra bibliotek en mötesplats för hela orten, också på söndagar.]"). Question 9 holds a separate conclusion ("[SUGGESTED: Från söndag 1 november 2026 har Norrmyra bibliotek öppet på söndagar klockan 11–15, så att ortens invånare kan träffas där också på söndagen.]"). The chain check has the line "*Är slutsatsen det strukturen landar i, bär den budskapet, och ligger den inom mandatet?*".

**B4, no grading, few follow-ups: pass.** No answer is marked or graded as weak. A draft asks no follow-ups, and I counted 0. The reply makes one offer of an interview and repeats no suggestion.

**B5, no demand for evidence: pass.** The run asks for no links or evidence and writes no unsubstantiated-claim marker. What needs to be found out is listed as "Öppna frågor" under question 12, for example "Stämmer det att det är första gången på tio år …?", and the reply says they "hindrar inte skrivandet". One reservation: the brief makes the ten-year fact conditional ("Den tas med bara om den bekräftas", question 8). That comes close to making a supplied fact depend on evidence. I judge it under B7 instead, because it is an unmarked decision of the run and not a demand for a source.

**B6, readiness: pass.** My own reading: condition 1 fails, because the angle (5), the message (3) and the conclusion (9) are all unconfirmed `[SUGGESTED: …]`. Condition 2 holds: question 1 has genre and language, and every other question has at least a suggested answer or a partial answer. Condition 3 holds, because the angle line and the conclusion line both say "Delvis" and neither says no. So the brief is not enough to write. The run agrees ("Är briefen tillräcklig för att skriva? Inte än.") and names exactly the three blocking questions (3), (5) and (9). It lists the other gaps separately, as "Luckor utöver dem", and does not present them as blocking.

**B7, the structure question: fail.** Question 7 has the one sentence as `[SUGGESTED: …]` and a nine-step sketch. The sketch invents no facts: the quote, the time 11–15, the date and the contact all come from the notes, and the missing facts go to the open questions. But step 4 contains an unmarked decision that the material does not carry: "Uppgiften om första söndagsöppet på tio år tas med om den bekräftas." The notes give the ten-year fact as news and only say where it comes from ("kommer från bibliotekschefens minne"). They do not say to include it only if it is confirmed. The same unmarked decision is repeated in question 8 ("Den tas med bara om den bekräftas"). The draft rule requires every step the material does not carry to be marked `[SUGGESTED: …]`.

**B8, metadata: pass.** The map holds `genre: pressrelease` and `language: sv`, and has no `technique`. That is what I expected: the notes settle both the genre and the language ("Det ska bli ett pressmeddelande på svenska"). The technique is correctly left out, because the genre's own form is only suggested ("[SUGGESTED: Pressmeddelandets egen form, utan någon teknik.]").

**B9, the chain check: pass.** In a draft the chain check must be written as `[SUGGESTED: …]`, and it is: "**Kontroll av kedjan:** [SUGGESTED: …]". It has all six lines, each answered ja or delvis with a reason. RIV is also written as `[SUGGESTED: …]`.

**B10, review and offers: pass.** This is a draft, so the criterion asks for an offer of a gap interview. The run makes it: "Vill du att jag intervjuar dig om luckorna, en fråga i taget?"

**F1, truth: pass.** No fact, figure, source or brand detail is invented. The brand, the promise, the quote, the contact and the main effect are all in the notes. The run's inferences are marked, for example the reader profile, the mandate, the tone and the length of 200–350 words. The unmarked inclusion condition in B7 is a handling decision and not a supplied fact, so it does not fail this criterion.
