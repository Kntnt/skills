# Judgement

Mode: interview. Working language: Swedish.

**B1, mode and language: pass.** The run worked as an interview in Swedish from the start ("Jag ställer frågorna i mallens ordning, en i taget"). Each turn opened one question, 1 to 12 in order. Where the user answered ahead (hook in turn 7, technique in turn 8, effect in turn 11), the Skill recorded the answer and still took the questions in template order ("Du gav redan ett svar på den här frågan … Ska jag föra in det som kroken").

**B2, the headings and markers: pass.** `brief.md` has all twelve questions as Swedish headings in template order, from "## 1. Vad är uppdraget?" to "## 12. Vilka källor känner du till, och vad behöver du ta reda på?". It uses no marker of any kind. Every answer was supplied or confirmed, so none was needed, and no invented marker appears.

**B3, message and conclusion: pass.** Question 3 holds the sender's message, "Cykelköket är stället där vem som helst kan lära sig ta hand om sin cykel". Question 9 holds a separate conclusion, "Den som tvättar och smörjer kedjan regelbundet klarar vintern utan att byta kedja". The chain check has the line "Är slutsatsen det strukturen landar i, bär den budskapet, och ligger den inom mandatet? Delvis."

**B4, no grading, few follow-ups: pass.** There are two follow-ups, each on a permitted question and each asked once. The first is on the angle in turn 6 ("**Följdfråga:** Vilken enda vinkel ska den här texten ha?"), where it rides on the several-angles suggestion. The second is on the conclusion in turn 10 ("**Följdfråga:** Vilken mening ska slutsatsen vara?"), where it rides on the topic-not-claim suggestion. The Skill made no suggestion twice and graded nothing as weak. It also declined to reopen the partly lines: "Jag frågar inte om delvis-raderna igen".

**B5, no demand for evidence: pass.** The Skill asks for no evidence, marks no claim as unsubstantiated, and says "Inget i briefen behöver vara belagt. Kravet gäller den färdiga texten." The open questions appear as plain text under question 12. One borderline point: the question 12 prompt says "Numrera dem, och länka där det går". That is an optional formatting request for sources the user already knows, not a demand that any answer be sourced, so I did not count it as a fail. A stricter reading of "asks for no links" would.

**B6, readiness: pass.** My own check of the three conditions:

1. The angle, the message and the conclusion are all the user's own unmarked answers.
2. Questions 1 to 11 are all answered. Question 1 gives genre "artikel" and language "sv", and the sketch and chain check were confirmed.
3. The chain check answers "Ja" on the angle line and "Delvis", not no, on the conclusion line.

So the brief is enough to write. The Skill agrees: "**Briefen räcker för att skriva.** … ingen rad om vinkeln eller slutsatsen fick ett nej. Delvis-raderna stoppar inte skrivandet."

**B7, the structure question: pass.** Question 7 has the one sentence ("Salt och slask sliter på kedjan … därför visar vi handgreppen genom att gå igenom dem ett i taget") and a four-step ABT sketch, which the user confirmed ("Skissen fungerar, jag bekräftar den"). The Skill invented no facts for the steps: "Om det finns fler handgrepp än de två säger ditt material inget om, och jag hittar inte på några". It sent the missing details (what the reader needs, how often, the dates of the open evenings) to question 12 as open questions.

**B8, metadata: pass.** The map holds `genre: article`, `technique: abt` and `language: sv`, which is what I expected. The user settled "artikel" and "ABT" explicitly and gave "sv" in question 1 as the only version. No suggested value appears in the map.

**B9, the chain check: pass.** In turn 11 the Skill proposed RIV and the chain check from the answers as suggestions ("Alla svar nedan är förslag tills du bekräftar dem") and asked for confirmation ("Bekräftar du RIV-svaren och kedjekontrollen …?"). The user confirmed in turn 13 ("Jag bekräftar RIV och kedjekontrollen som du föreslog"), and the brief was written after that, so the unmarked lines are correct. There is one wobble. In turn 12 the user said only that they would confirm, and the Skill declared early "det tar jag nu som bekräftat". This did not affect the brief. Also, the in-chat proposal was labelled in prose rather than wrapped in the literal `[SUGGESTED: …]` token.
