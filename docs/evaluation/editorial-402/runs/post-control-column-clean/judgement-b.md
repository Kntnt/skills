# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

There is no frontmatter in either text. Formatting (the bold standfirst, the `Text:` byline, two `##` subheadings, paragraph breaks) is identical. The body, the byline and the second subheading are unchanged, word for word. There are three differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom varför` (44 characters). After: `# Mötesmallen mäter tiden men frågar inte varför` (46 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, in **scope** (from "a box for everything except why" to "measures the time"), with a loss of meaning as well: the image of the *ruta* (box) is gone. That image runs through the text in "rutorna" (paragraph 1) and "Ännu en ruta" (second subheading). The original headline states the column's rhetorical premise. It shows no concrete visible defect, so this is a change of taste presented as a repair.
2. **First subheading.** Before: `## Det är inte bara besluten jag vill åt` (37 characters). After: `## Jag vill att förståelse får plats bredvid besluten` (50 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, in **meaning**. The original is a negated contrast ("it is not only the decisions I am after"). The new one is a positive statement of a wish, and it introduces "förståelse" before the section uses the idea. It is a change of taste, not the repair of a visible defect. The subheading also grows by 13 characters, well past the 37 characters that the frozen expectation names as the text's subheading length.
3. **Standfirst verb.** Before: `och misstänker samtidigt min egen lösning`. After: `och misstror samtidigt min egen lösning`. Class: borderline. "Misstänka" with a thing as its object is unidiomatic in Swedish, and "misstro" is the idiomatic verb, so this can pass as the repair of a small visible (idiomatic) defect. It is not a spelling, grammar or punctuation correction. The shift from "suspect" to "distrust" is slight, and the admitted doubt survives.

## 2. The account

1. **Headline:** reported, in "Påståenden som ändrats" item 1, which says correctly that the claim was narrowed ("snävare nu"). The justification does not hold up. "Rubriken sa mer än texten" treats a column's figurative premise as an over-claim. The account also leaves out that the *ruta* thread is lost.
2. **First subheading:** reported, in item 2, which says correctly that the claim now says something different. The reasons are strained. The heading did not "förutsätta beslut som ämne": the section is about decisions from its first paragraph on. Reading "vill åt" as "wants to attack" is far-fetched. The account does not mention that the heading grew from 37 to 50 characters.
3. **Standfirst verb:** reported, under "Vad körningen ändrade utöver påståendena", with an accurate description ("misstänker" → "misstror", "lät översatt").

The account has one inconsistency of its own. It says "rättat tre saker" but also "Rättelsebudgeten var 1 och räckte". It also calls the result "Inga fynd återstår" while declaring that the text follows the anatomy. On the reply's own account, both of its findings sit outside the anatomy.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The frozen expectation says this text conforms and is a control. R1 says "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste." The run rewrote two working claims that showed no concrete visible defect. The headline "Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom varför" became "Mötesmallen mäter tiden men frågar inte varför", which narrows its scope and drops the *ruta* image the text returns to. "Det är inte bara besluten jag vill åt" became "Jag vill att förståelse får plats bredvid besluten", which changes its meaning. The reply's findings justify both changes ("rubriken sa mer än texten", "förutsatte beslut som ämne", "vill åt kunde … läsas som att skribenten ville angripa besluten"). These are editorial preferences dressed as defects. Everything else was preserved, and none of the named rejections occurred. The one-word standfirst change counts as an idiomatic repair and does not decide the verdict.
