# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text adds none. Formatting (bold standfirst, H2 subheadings, italic report title, the `Text:` byline line, the typographic quotation marks) is identical. Every other line is byte-identical. There are two differences:

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` (48 characters, 7 words). After: `# Björkskolans mätningar visar när det var kallt, inte orsaken` (60 characters, 9 words). Class: **a change of taste**. The original headline is a complete, working antithesis ("the trial shows when, not why"), and the standfirst directly below it gives the result it refers to. Nothing in it is wrong or unreadable. The rewrite keeps the claim's content ("när det var kallt" is supported by the body, and "orsaken" means the same as "varför"). But it makes the headline 12 characters longer and pushes it past the eight-word norm, which the reply admits.
2. **Standfirst, last sentence.** Before: `Här är vad ett kort försök kan säga, och vad som behövs för att komma vidare.` After: `Här är vad försöket kan säga, och vad som behövs för att komma vidare.` Class: **a change to what a claim says: meaning, and with it scope**. The author's description of the four-week trial as *kort* (short) is deleted. The generic "ett kort försök" (a short trial) is narrowed to "försöket" (this trial). Calling a four-week trial short is an ordinary, supportable description. The text states the duration ("Fyra veckors mätningar") and nowhere contradicts it, so this is the loss of a working claim rather than the removal of a defect.

## 2. The account

1. **Headline:** reported as finding 1 ("Rubriken gick inte att förstå på egen hand", that is, the headline could not be understood on its own) and in the opening note that the headline now has nine words, one over the guideline. The account is accurate about what changed. Its diagnosis is not accurate: the original headline did say what it concerned when read with the standfirst it sits above, and "när" (when) was not obscure. Calling the change a repair overstates it. The reply does not mention that the headline grew from 48 to 60 characters, although it asserts that all counted limits held.
2. **"kort" removal:** reported as finding 2, and again under "Påståenden som ändrats" (claims changed) as both **Borttaget** (removed) and **Ändrad räckvidd** (changed scope). The account of the change itself is accurate and complete, and it correctly classes the change as a scope change. The justification is not sound. It claims that "kort" implied a longer trial could have explained the cold, but the text itself says only that the next trial needs more than sensors. "Kort" is the author's own reading of the trial, and it does not contradict that.

The reply also states that the closing proofreading pass changed nothing. That is consistent with the diff: there is no mechanical difference.
