## 1. Differences

A line-by-line diff of `work/input.md` against the returned text (the fenced block in `response.md`) shows exactly two differences. There are no frontmatter differences because neither version has frontmatter. There are no formatting differences: the standfirst's bold, the italic report title and the `”…”` quotation marks are all unchanged.

1. **Headline.**
   - Before: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` (48 characters).
   - After: `# Björkskolans temperaturer bör läsas mot när rummen används` (58 characters).
   - Class: a change to what a claim says, changing **meaning** and **attribution**. The old headline stated the trial's result, a descriptive finding: the measurements show when, not why. The new one states a recommendation, which is a normative claim. In the body, that recommendation belongs to Rask ("Rask rekommenderar …") and to the closing instruction. The headline now asserts it in the publication's own voice about "Björkskolans temperaturer", with no attribution. The result the old headline carried is no longer in the headline. It survives only in the standfirst and the quotation.
2. **First subheading.**
   - Before: `## En gräns för det lokala försöket` (32 characters).
   - After: `## Värdena mäts mot kontorets gräns och fångar inte elevernas upplevelse` (69 characters).
   - Class: a change of taste. The old subheading was accurate and within limits. The new one summarises the section's content and adds no new claim, although it names only "elevernas upplevelse" and leaves out "luftdrag". It is close to a 70-character ceiling that the old one was nowhere near.

Everything else is word for word the same, including the standfirst, byline, introduction, body, quotation, second subheading and ending. There were no mechanical corrections; the reply says the proofreading pass found nothing.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

R1 says: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste." The frozen expectation says this control conforms to the anatomy, and the reply agrees: "Texten följer artikelns anatomi utan avvikelser". The run still rewrote the headline and the first subheading on three findings of taste. The deciding change is the headline:

> `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` → `# Björkskolans temperaturer bör läsas mot när rummen används`

This rewrite changed a claim outside any legitimate finding. The headline went from the trial's reported result to a recommendation that the body attributes to Rask, now stated without attribution. Everything the expectation names for preservation survives, and none of the specifically rejected findings was made. That is not enough to pass, because R1 also forbids rewriting a clean text.
