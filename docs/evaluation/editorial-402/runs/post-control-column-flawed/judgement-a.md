Neither text has frontmatter. The byline line and the paragraph formatting are unchanged, apart from the paragraphs that were deleted.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Möten förändrar allt`. After: `# Mötesmallen rymmer tiden men inte förståelsen`. Class: the repair of a visible defect. The old headline claims a change that the column never makes, and it names no subject of its own. It adds no new claim, so it changes no scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning that the body does not already carry.

After: "Vår mötesmall har plats …". Class: the repair of a visible defect, namely a generic opening plus an empty tautology. It is a legitimate removal of an unsupported claim of importance.
3. **Final paragraph deleted.** Before: "Sammanfattningsvis är möten viktigare än någonsin i dagens snabbt föränderliga värld." After: nothing. The text now ends on "… gör möten bättre." Class: the repair of a visible defect, a generic ending that repeats the opening. It is a legitimate removal.
