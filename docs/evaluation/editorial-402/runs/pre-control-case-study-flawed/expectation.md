# Frozen expectation

Detect first-person supplier praise/rescue, pre-echoed quote, duplicate standfirst/lead and causal contradiction. The anatomy adds: no byline, reported as missing and left unfilled; two subheadings, neither describing its section, the second claiming a conclusion the note itself declines to draw; and no ending section or call to action — the text supplies no offer, link or contact route, so the missing action is reported rather than invented. Keep the customer's reservation and factual measures; report unavailable support.
