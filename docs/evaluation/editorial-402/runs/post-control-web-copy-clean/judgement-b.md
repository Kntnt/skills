# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text has none. The headings (`#`, `##`, `##`), the paragraph breaks and the Markdown formatting are the same. The returned text differs from the input in two places, both in body sentences.

1. **Before:** "Ange namn, förening och e-postadress i formuläret." **After:** "Ange namn, förening och e-postadress i formuläret för intresseanmälan." **Class:** a change of taste. It adds a qualifier to "formuläret". The page title ("Så fungerar intresseanmälan") and the opening paragraph already make clear which form is meant, because the page has only one form and it is about the interest registration. The page has only three short sections, and the referent is not ambiguous in any of them. The claim stays the same: no change of scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning.
2. **Before:** "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." **After:** "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." **Class:** a change of taste. It copies the time limit from the heading directly above ("Svar inom tre arbetsdagar") into the body sentence. The reader sees the fact already, because the heading states it, so the input had no defect here. The claim stays the same: the heading already made the three-working-day promise, so neither scope nor certainty changes.

The run made no mechanical corrections. Its reply says the proofreading pass found nothing.

## 4. R1

**Fail**, narrowly. The deciding passage is "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av…". This edit copies the heading's fact into the body. Together with "i formuläret för intresseanmälan", it is a rewrite of a clean control text to suit an editorial preference, not the repair of a visible defect. R1 says: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste". Both edits keep the meaning, the voice is intact, and the account is honest and complete. That is why the failure is narrow and not a harmful one. But the control expects the complete page to be kept as it is, and the run changed half of its body sentences.
