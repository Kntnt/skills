# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. There is no frontmatter in either file, and the headings, paragraph breaks and all other lines are identical byte for byte.

- Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." Class: a change of taste. The time frame was already stated on the page, in the heading directly above the sentence ("## Svar inom tre arbetsdagar"). The body now repeats it. The page's claim is unchanged in scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning. Only the place where it is stated has changed: it is now in both the heading and the body. No visible defect was repaired, because a reader of the section sees the heading.

## 2. The account

- The one difference is reported, as finding 1 ("Åtgärdat") and again under "Påståenden" ("Ändrat"). The report is accurate: it quotes the new sentence exactly, says the heading is unchanged, and says correctly that the claim is no stronger and no wider because the heading already stated it. The closing line says that this is the only difference and that proofreading changed nothing. That matches the diff.
- The reply's description of the difference is accurate. Its reason is not: it calls the change the repair of a defect ("den som läste stycket fick aldrig veta tidsramen"), but the reader of that section gets the time frame from its heading.
- Finding 2 (the definite form "formuläret", with no statement of where the form is) is reported as unresolved and matches no difference. The text was left unchanged, as the reply says.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page"**: partly met. All three sections, every claim and the boundary sentence are kept ("En intresseanmälan … är inte en beställning", "Inga betalningsuppgifter behövs", "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret skickas in"). But the run treated the page as incomplete twice. Finding 1 says the body lacks the response time. Finding 2 says the reader "får alltså inte veta var anmälan faktiskt görs", and asks someone to "komplettera meningen eller bädda in formuläret". On the first point it acted and rewrote a sentence. So the page was not preserved as it stood.
- **"without sales template or CTA"**: met. The run added no call to action, sales framing or template section. The output has no CTA.
- **"Short headings … work"**: not met. The run did not change the heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar", but it treated the heading as unable to carry its own fact. Finding 1 says "svarstiden stod bara i mellanrubriken", and the body was changed to repeat it. That is the judgement this clause says not to make.
- **"variable section length work"**: met. The run did not lengthen, shorten or even out any section beyond the three added words, and it made no remark about section length.
- **Rejection, "No invention of destination or function"**: the run did not make this mistake. Finding 2 names the missing destination of "formuläret" and explicitly declines to invent one: "För att åtgärda det behövs ett faktum som texten saknar … Därför lämnades meningen orörd." Nothing about where the form lives or what it does was added to the text.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding clause: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste." This control is a clean page, and its expectation says the short headings work. The Skill rewrote the body sentence under "## Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" to repeat the heading ("Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar …"). Its justification was that the time frame "stod bara i mellanrubriken". A fact stated in the heading directly above a paragraph is not a concrete visible defect. The change moved no claim, it was reported accurately, and it was declined on the unavailable-source point (finding 2). But it is still a taste rewrite of a clean text, and that is the one mistake R1 forbids on a control.
