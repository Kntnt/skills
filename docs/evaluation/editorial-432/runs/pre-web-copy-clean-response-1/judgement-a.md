# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text has none. Headings, heading levels, paragraph breaks and all other sentences are identical. There is one difference.

1. Section "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar", first sentence.
   - Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid."
   - After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid."
   - Class: a change of taste. The time limit was already stated in the section's own heading, which sits directly above the sentence. Nothing a reader needed was missing, so this does not repair a visible defect. It does not change what a claim says. The heading's claim is copied into the body, and its scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning stay the same.

## 2. The account

1. Difference 1 is reported as finding 1 ("Åtgärdat, avsnittet ”Svar inom tre arbetsdagar”"), and again under "Påståenden" ("Kopierat, inte ändrat eller tillagt"). The account of what changed is accurate: the words were added only in the body, and the heading was left as it was. The account of why is not accurate. The reply says that a reader using a screen reader "fick inte veta när Svale hör av sig", but a screen reader reads headings, and the heading states the time limit. The defect it reports does not exist in the text.

The reply also reports finding 2 (unresolved: the page mentions "formuläret" but does not say where the form is). No text change goes with it, and none is claimed. The reply's statement that nothing else changed is accurate.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page"**: The page's content is preserved. All three sections and every claim are still there: "är inte en beställning", "Inga betalningsuppgifter behövs", "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret skickas in." But the run does not treat the page as complete. It treats the page as defective twice: once in the fix to the time limit, and once in unresolved finding 2, which says "Läsaren vet vad som ska fyllas i men inte var."
- **"without sales template or CTA"**: Met in the text. No CTA, sales framing or link was added. The reply comes close, though. Finding 2 asks someone to "lägga in formuläret eller en länk med en etikett som säger vad den gör", which in effect recommends adding an action element to a page the expectation says is complete without one. The run did not make that change.
- **"Short headings … work"**: Not respected. The heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" is exactly the kind of short heading that carries its section, and the run judged it insufficient. Its reason is "Bara rubriken sa när svaret kommer, inte brödtexten". It then repeated the heading's content in the body.
- **"variable section length work"**: Met. No section was padded to even out lengths. The two short middle sections are unchanged, apart from the three words added to the last one.
- **"No invention of destination or function"** (a rejection): The run did not make this mistake. It explicitly declined to invent where the form is: "Formulärets plats framgår inte av texten och går inte att hitta på." It added no URL, link target or new function.

## 4. R1

**Fail**, narrowly.

The passage that decides it is finding 1 together with the edit it justifies: "Bara rubriken sa när svaret kommer, inte brödtexten … Nu står ”inom tre arbetsdagar” även i brödtexten." This is a clean control. The time limit was visible in the heading directly above the sentence, and the expectation says that short headings work. The added words therefore repair no concrete visible defect. They rewrite a clean text to satisfy a rule that a section's body must stand without its heading, and R1 forbids that. The screen-reader reason given for it is factually wrong.

What counts for the run: it preserved every claim, restriction and the voice; it made no change of scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning; and it did not invent a destination. What counts against it: it made a change of taste to a clean text. Unresolved finding 2 is also a false detection, because it treats a complete page as missing a form link, but it changed no text.
