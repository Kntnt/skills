# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text adds none. The heading levels, the order of the headings, the heading wording, the paragraph breaks and the Markdown formatting are the same in both. The returned text is wrapped in a four-backtick `markdown` fence in the reply. That fence is packaging for the reply, not a change to the text.

The two texts differ in one place:

- Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid."
  After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid."
  Class: **a change of taste**. The claim that Svale replies within three working days was already on the page, in the section heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar". The edit copies that claim into the body text below it. The claim's scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning stay the same. Only where it appears changes, and it now appears twice. The input had no visible defect here. A short heading that carries the time frame, followed by a body that gives the rest of the information, is a normal and working pattern on an information page.

Nothing else changed: no mechanical corrections, no headings, no CTA, no link, no form location, no frontmatter.

## 2. The account

- The one difference is reported, accurately, in Anmärkning 1 ("Nu står ”inom tre arbetsdagar” även i brödtextens första mening. Mellanrubriken är oförändrad."). It is also reported in the Påståenden list under "Kopierade", where the claim is correctly described as having the same scope and certainty. The account describes the change exactly.
- The account calls it a repaired defect ("brödtexten bar inte det innehåll rubriken utlovade"). That classification is the Skill's own, and I do not share it. The account does not misdescribe the edit. It labels a change of taste as a repair.
- The closing summary ("körningen bara ändrat en sak") is accurate, and so is the statement that the proofreading pass found nothing.
- Anmärkning 2, the form's location, is reported as irreparable and left unchanged in the text. That matches the returned text.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page"**: partly met. All three sections, their headings and their claims are kept, and nothing is removed. The page still received one edit that it did not need. The Skill treated the page as incomplete in two ways. It "fixed" the first, the time frame appearing only in the heading. It flagged the second without changing any text: "Kvarstår: formuläret har ingen angiven plats … Någon med den uppgiften behöver komplettera texten." The page is preserved in substance, but not left as it was.
- **"without sales template or CTA"**: met. No CTA, no benefit list and no sales framing were added. The returned text ends as the input did: "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret skickas in."
- **"Short headings … work"**: met as far as the headings themselves go. All three headings are unchanged: "Så fungerar intresseanmälan", "Uppgifter att lämna" and "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar". The Skill did, however, treat content carried by a short heading as a gap in the body, with the reasoning "En läsare som bara läste brödtexten, eller fick sidan återgiven utan rubriker, fick därför ingen tidsram". So it did not fully accept that the short heading works.
- **"variable section length work"**: met. The sections keep their lengths of two, two and two sentences, and nothing was padded or balanced. The body of the last section grew by three words.
- **"No invention of destination or function"**: met. The Skill added no link, URL, form location or new function. It explicitly refused to add one: "Formulärets plats eller länkmål finns inte i texten och kan inte läggas till utan att hittas på." Reporting a missing destination is not inventing one.
- **Detection clauses**: the expectation names nothing for the run to detect. Both findings the Skill reports come on top of a clean control. Finding 1 led to a change in the text. Finding 2 changed nothing.

## 4. R1

**Fail (narrow).**

The deciding passage is the returned sentence "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid.", together with the Skill's reason for it in Anmärkning 1. This is a clean control, and the expectation says short headings work. The input's pairing of a heading that states the time frame with a body that explains the rest was not a concrete visible defect. The Skill added the phrase for a hypothetical reader who reads without headings. That is a rewrite of a clean text for taste, which R1 does not allow.

Much else was done right. The edit changes no claim, the account reports it accurately, nothing was invented, no CTA or sales template was added, and the form-location concern was reported without altering the text. The failure is the single unwarranted edit, not any loss or distortion of content.
