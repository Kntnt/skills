# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

`work/input.md` and `work/output.md` have no frontmatter. Headings, paragraph breaks and Markdown formatting are identical. The body differs in two lines.

1. Line 7. Before: "Ange namn, förening och e-postadress i formuläret." After: "Ange namn, förening och e-postadress i formuläret för intresseanmälan." Class: **a change of taste**. The page is titled "Så fungerar intresseanmälan" and opens by defining the intresseanmälan, so "formuläret" already had a clear referent. What the claim says does not change.
2. Line 11, first sentence. Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." After: "Svale svarar på intresseanmälan via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." Class: **a change of taste**. The heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" already stated the deadline for this section, and "på intresseanmälan" repeats the page's subject. The claim does not change in scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning. The only difference is that the deadline now appears twice.
3. Line 11, second sentence. Before: "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret skickas in." After: "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret för intresseanmälan skickas in." Class: **a change of taste**, same as difference 1. The claim does not change.

There are no mechanical corrections. None of the three differences repairs a visible defect.

## 2. The account

- Difference 1: reported, under finding 2 ("Båda hänvisningarna säger nu ’formuläret för intresseanmälan’") and under "Ändrade". The reply describes the edit accurately. It is wrong to call it a repair: it says a reader who began at "Uppgifter att lämna" could not tell which form was meant, but the page is a three-section page about one form.
- Difference 2: reported, under finding 1 and under "Ändrade" ("Tidsgränsen fanns redan i rubriken och har bara förts in i brödtexten"). The reply describes the edit accurately, including that the heading already held the deadline. It still calls this a defect: "så den som läste brödtexten utan rubriken fick inte veta svarstiden".
- Difference 3: reported, under finding 2 and under "Ändrade". The description is accurate.
- The reply contradicts itself on one point. It says "Rättningsbudgeten var 1 och har gått åt", but it lists two findings as corrected, finding 1 as "Rättat" and finding 2 as "Delvis rättat". Both corrections are in the output.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page"**: **not met.** The run treated the page as defective and changed two of its three body paragraphs. It also reports the page as incomplete in an unresolved finding: "texten innehåller inte formuläret och säger inte heller var det finns … Någon som vet måste lägga till en länk eller en beskrivning av var formuläret finns." The page itself survives, and every claim in it is intact, for example "En intresseanmälan till Svales genomgång av bokningsrutiner är inte en beställning." But it was not left alone as a clean text.
- **"without sales template or CTA"**: **met.** The run added no call to action, no selling phrasing and no link. The unresolved finding asks for a link but does not insert one.
- **"Short headings and variable section length work"**: **not met.** This is the rejection the clause guards against, and the run made it. It treated the short heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" as not enough to carry the deadline, and copied the deadline into the body because a section should be readable "fristående" ("brödtexten i två avsnitt fått de sammanhang som saknades när ett avsnitt läses fristående"). No heading was changed. Section lengths were changed only slightly, by the added words.
- **"No invention of destination or function"**: **met.** The run did not invent a URL, a location for the form, or any new function. It says so directly: "Uppgiften finns inte i texten och har därför inte lagts till." The added words "för intresseanmälan" and "inom tre arbetsdagar" come from the page's own title and heading, so they are not invented.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** R1 says clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste. This control is a clean, complete information page. The run rewrote it to make each section readable on its own, which is a matter of taste. The passage that decides the verdict is the added "Svale svarar på intresseanmälan via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar …", given under the heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar". The reply admits the heading already said this ("Tidsgränsen fanns redan i rubriken") and still reports it as a corrected defect. The repeated "formuläret för intresseanmälan" is the same kind of change. The run did preserve every claim, invented nothing and reported every change accurately. Those points count in its favour, but they do not outweigh an unneeded rewrite of a clean text.
