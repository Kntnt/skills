# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

`work/input.md` and `work/output.md` differ in one line only. There is no frontmatter in either file. Headings, paragraph breaks and all other formatting are identical.

1. Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." Class: **a change of taste** that the Skill presented as the repair of a visible defect. The body sentence now states the response time, which the section heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" already stated. Measured against the page as a whole, no claim changes: the scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning are the same, because the heading already made the three-working-day promise. Measured against the body sentence alone, the sentence gains a time limit it did not have, which is a **scope** change to that sentence. But the heading had that time limit in the original, so the sentence contains nothing that the page did not already say.

No mechanical corrections were made.

## 2. The account

- Difference 1 is reported as finding 1 ("**Löst.** ... Nu står svarstiden även i brödtexten. Mellanrubriken är oförändrad.") and again under **Påståenden** ("**Ändrat:** ... Mellanrubriken sa redan detsamma, så påståendet har en motsvarighet i originalet och har inte fått större räckvidd."). The report of what changed is accurate: the wording, the location, and the note that the heading was left unchanged all match the file.
- The reply is not accurate about why the change was needed. It says that a reader who read the body without the heading, "till exempel med skärmläsare", missed the response time. But screen readers announce headings, and a heading is part of the text the reader gets. So the defect it describes is not a visible defect in this text.
- The statement "Övriga ändringar: Utöver svarstiden i brödtexten skiljer sig den levererade texten inte från originalet" is accurate: the diff shows no other change.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page without sales template or CTA."** Met. The Skill added no sales structure, no call to action, no benefit list and no closing push. The page still ends with "En tid bokas alltså inte när formuläret skickas in." All three sections are there, and the opening paragraph is unchanged: "En intresseanmälan till Svales genomgång av bokningsrutiner är inte en beställning. Den hjälper Svale att avgöra om genomgången passar föreningens behov."
- **"Short headings and variable section length work."** Partly violated. The Skill did not change the headings or the section structure, and the sections keep their different lengths. But finding 1 treats a short heading that carries a fact as a defect: "svarstiden stod bara i rubriken". The Skill then used its only correction to repeat the heading's content in the body. The expectation says this pattern works. The run treated it as something that needed fixing.
- **"No invention of destination or function."** Met. Finding 2 notes that the text does not say where the form is. The Skill left that finding unresolved and explicitly declined to invent a location: "Det går inte att rätta utan att hitta på en plats, och ingen sådan finns i texten." No link, URL, button or new form behaviour appears in `work/output.md`. Finding 2 itself is doubtful, because an information page like this one usually sits next to the form it describes. But reporting a finding without changing the text breaks no rule of the expectation.
- **Rejections named.** The expectation rejects three things: adding a sales template or CTA, changing short headings or section length, and inventing a destination or function. The run made none of these changes to the text. It did, however, rewrite a clean body sentence because it misread a working short heading as a defect.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is finding 1 and the change it drove:

> "Under mellanrubriken ”Svar inom tre arbetsdagar” stod svarstiden bara i rubriken. ... Nu står svarstiden även i brödtexten."

This control is a clean text, and R1 says a clean text "may not be rewritten to satisfy taste". The frozen expectation says that short headings work. The Skill found no concrete visible defect: the response time was stated on the page, in the heading, which a screen reader announces like any other text. The Skill still changed a body sentence so that it repeats the heading. The change is small, preserves meaning and is reported accurately, and the Skill correctly refused to invent a destination for the form. But the one correction it spent is a taste-driven rewrite of a clean text, and R1 forbids exactly that.
