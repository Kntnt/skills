# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

The input has no frontmatter, and the returned text adds none. All three headings (`#`, `##`, `##`), the paragraph breaks and the paragraph order are the same in both. Two of the three body paragraphs are identical word for word. There is one difference:

- Before: "Svale svarar via e-post för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." After: "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att stämma av uppdraget och föreslå en mötestid." Class: **a change of taste**. The reply presents it as the repair of a visible defect, but it is not one. The heading directly above the paragraph, "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar", already states the response time, and the heading is visible on the page. Copying it into the body adds no claim and removes none, so no claim changes in scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning. The effect is only that the page now states the same fact twice, which is redundant.

## 2. The account

- The one difference is reported, as finding 1 ("Åtgärdad: svarstiden stod bara i mellanrubriken"), and again under **Påståenden** ("kopierad från mellanrubriken med samma innebörd"). The report describes the edit accurately: what was added, where, and that the heading was left as it was. Its reason is weak. It says a reader who reads the paragraph "utan rubriken" does not learn the response time. A heading on an information page is part of what the reader sees, so the missing time in the body is not a defect.
- Finding 2 (the text says "formuläret" but does not say where the form is) is reported as unresolved and changes nothing in the text. The reply's closing statement, that the response time is the only difference, is accurate.

## 3. The frozen expectation

- **"Preserve a complete information page without sales template or CTA."** Met. The returned text adds no call to action, sales template, benefit list or closing prompt. The page's three statements survive: the notice of interest is not an order, only name, association and email are needed with no payment details, and Svale answers by email and no time is booked when the form is sent. One sentence has a phrase added, described under heading 1.
- **"Short headings and variable section length work."** Partly violated. The headings stay short and unchanged, and the sections keep their uneven lengths. However, the one edit exists because the run treated the short heading "Svar inom tre arbetsdagar" as not enough to carry the fact it states: "Den som läste stycket utan rubriken fick alltså inte veta svarstiden." The run acted as if the short heading does not work and made the body say it again. That is the judgement the expectation rules out.
- **"No invention of destination or function."** Met. The run did not invent where the form is, a link, a URL or anything the form does. Finding 2 flags that the form's location is not given and explicitly declines to supply it: "Det går inte att rätta utan uppgifter som texten saknar." Whether this is worth reporting at all is arguable, because a form on an information page like this is normally on the same page. But it is a report, not an invention, and the text is not changed because of it.

## 4. R1

**Fail**, though the failure is narrow. The text is a clean control, and R1 says: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste." The only change to the text is a matter of taste, presented as the repair of a defect: "Svale svarar via e-post för att …" became "Svale svarar via e-post inom tre arbetsdagar för att …". The heading directly above it already gives the fact, so no visible defect existed. The edit makes the page repeat itself, and it goes against the expectation that short headings work. Everything else passes: voice, claims and structure are preserved, no destination or function is invented, and the account is complete and accurate.
