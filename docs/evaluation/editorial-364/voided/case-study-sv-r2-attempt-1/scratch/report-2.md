## 2. Findings

### Finding A — source support: an unavailable comparison reported as a non-existent one

- **Draft:** "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte." (body, first section)
- **Source:** "No comparison with another supplier is available."
- **What differs:** whose knowledge is at stake. The material reports the state of the evidence; the draft reports the state of the world.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay's team informally weighed Svale against a competitor in a meeting in August 2025 and kept no record, so nothing comparative was supplied for this case. Then "No comparison with another supplier is available" is true and "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte" is false. Nothing in the material excludes this: it says only that no comparison is available. The material's own wording elsewhere shows the distinction is live — it writes flat non-existence when it means it ("There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements").
- **Case where the draft holds and the source fails:** none; non-existence entails unavailability.
- **Smallest repair:** "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör redovisas inte." (Alternatively: "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte att tillgå.")

### Finding B — source support: a scoped denial widened into a general one

- **Draft:** section heading "Anteckningen mäter tilldelningstiden men tillskriver ingen orsak"
- **Source:** "The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."
- **What differs:** the scope of what the note declines to do. The material names one candidate cause the note rules out of its account; the heading says the note assigns no cause at all.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** the trial note's closing line reads "the shorter median in the trial period is most likely explained by the lighter workload in those weeks; it should not be attributed to the software." Then the material's statement is true — the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software — while the heading's "tillskriver ingen orsak" is false, because the note does attribute a cause, namely workload. The material is compatible with this: it reports that the periods had different workloads and states only what the note refuses to attribute.
- **Case where the draft holds and the source fails:** none; attributing no cause entails not attributing this one.
- **Note:** the body sentence under the heading is exact ("anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden till programvaran"), so only the heading overreaches — and the contract holds headings to the same boundary as the body. The error is in the cautious direction, which does not make it supported.
- **Smallest repair:** "Anteckningen mäter tilldelningstiden men tillskriver inte programvaran skillnaden".

### Finding C — source support: a supervisor given responsibility for maintenance, and a singular view put in the plural

- **Draft:** "Maya Lind är underhållsansvarig på Elm Quay" (body); "vad underhållsansvariga skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång" (deck)
- **Source:** "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind"
- **What differs:** (i) the position named. "Supervisor" names who directs the work; "underhållsansvarig" names who carries responsibility for maintenance at the company. A person can fall under one and not the other, so the term does not carry across on its own. (ii) In the deck, the bare plural "underhållsansvariga" reads as maintenance managers in general, where one named person's view is what the material supplies.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** Elm Quay employs a property manager who holds formal responsibility for maintenance across all 640 flats and signs off the budget, while Maya Lind supervises the six-person maintenance crew and ran the trial day to day. Then "maintenance supervisor Maya Lind" is true and "Maya Lind är underhållsansvarig på Elm Quay" is false. The material is compatible with this: it says the team decided, designed the categories and will decide on expansion, and never says Lind holds the responsibility.
- **Case where the draft holds and the source fails:** a maintenance manager whose remit is responsibility rather than day-to-day supervision of the crew — the deck's generic plural reading likewise covers managers whose views were never supplied.
- **Smallest repair:** body — "Maya Lind är underhållsledare på Elm Quay" (or "arbetsledare för underhållet på Elm Quay"); deck — "vad underhållsledaren skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång".
- **Confidence:** the deck's number is the weaker half of this finding: the bare plural can also be read as a loose definite reference to the one supervisor. The role term itself is the substantive part.

## 3. Examined and not reported

**3.1 The title's count without its exclusions.** "Elm Quay samlade 31 felanmälningar i en logg" against "31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial." A case can be constructed — emergencies were phoned in and logged too, so the log held more than 31 items while the note counted 31 — but the deck routes the figures to the note ("vad den interna testanteckningen redovisar") and the body carries the attribution, the date and both exclusions in full, two sentences apart. A headline count whose exclusions are stated at first use in the body is defensible as written, and the count itself is not overstated in either direction. Recorded here rather than as a finding.

**3.2 "Så går Elm Quay vidare".** See row 29: the supported next step is itself a proceeding, and the section states the decision is open.

**3.3 Tense shift in "Lind rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsföretag".** See row 30: present tense for a standing position, with the negation's scope intact.

**3.4 Forbidden inferences.** None present. No causal claim for the software (the draft denies one), no money saved, no delighted residents, no rescued customer; assignment time is never turned into completion time, and the absence of completion-time measurement is stated; the customer's reservation survives in both the narration and the quotation; supplier narration stays in the third person throughout; the link is described as a checklist to read.

**3.5 Out of scope.** General editorial and mechanical review, including the government of *tillskriva* in row 25 and the article-less adjectival noun in the deck, except where number or reference bears on a claim (Finding C).
