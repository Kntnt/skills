## 2. Findings

All four are source-support findings. No translation findings: each of the three quotations preserves meaning, stance, certainty and voice in idiomatic Swedish, and no obstruction to the source reading was identified in any of them.

### F1 — The title states as accomplished what the material gives only as an aim

- **Draft:** "Elm Quay lät skiften se samma felanmälningar" (title).
- **Source:** "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information." — and, from the interview, "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done."
- **What differs:** modality and whose knowledge. The material carries an intention plus an installed and used log ("Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions"; "31 repair reports were entered"). It reports no observation that staff on different shifts saw, or were able to see, the same reports. The subject also widens from the maintenance team's two-building trial to "Elm Quay".
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** all six trained staff worked the morning shift; the evening shift was never given log access and went on working from the old email folder for the whole eight weeks, while the 31 reports were entered by day staff. Every supplied statement remains true — the team still wanted the evening shift to see the morning shift's work, the log was still configured, six staff were still trained, the medians still stand — and the title is false. No supplied statement excludes it: the material never says who used the log, or that both shifts did.
- **Smallest supported repair:** make the title the trial rather than its result, e.g. "Elm Quay prövade en gemensam logg för skiften" — or keep the aim as an aim: "Elm Quay ville låta skiften se samma felanmälningar".

### F2 — "Därefter" asserts an order the material does not give

- **Draft:** "Därefter drog de åtta veckorna i gång i de två husen."
- **Source:** "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions. The trial lasted eight weeks."
- **What differs:** time. The material lists configuration, training and duration; it does not place the two training sessions before the start of the eight weeks.
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the first training session was held the week before the trial and the second in week three, once staff had reports of their own to practise on. Both sentences of the material remain true; "därefter" is false. Nothing supplied excludes a session during the trial — "during two sessions" fixes the count, not the dates.
- **Smallest supported repair:** drop the chronological adverb — "De åtta veckorna genomfördes i de två husen." (or simply delete the sentence, since #9 already gives the duration).

### F3 — The heading compares more than the quotation compares

- **Draft:** "Kategorierna tog mer tid än de första anmälningarna" (heading).
- **Source:** "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports."
- **What differs:** the thing measured, on both sides. "Agreeing on the categories" becomes the categories as such, and "entering the first reports" becomes the first reports as such — which a reader takes to include handling them.
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** agreeing on the categories took two working days; entering the first reports took under an hour; but dealing with those first reports — dispatching, doing and closing the repairs — consumed several staff-weeks. Lind's sentence is true, the heading is false. Nothing supplied excludes it; the material says nothing about how long the reported repairs took, and indeed states that completion time was not measured.
- **Smallest supported repair:** restore the two activities — "Att enas om kategorierna tog mer tid än att registrera de första anmälningarna".

### F4 — "Stored separately" becomes one location per channel

- **Draft:** "Underhållsteamet på Elm Quay Housing hade telefonanmälningar på ett ställe och e-post på ett annat."
- **Source:** "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately."
- **What differs:** scope, and the subject. The source says the two kinds were kept apart from each other; the draft says each kind sat in a single place, and supplies the maintenance team as the party that held them.
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** telephone reports were written in a paper ledger at each of the two caretakers' desks and emails sat in two separate inboxes, none of them shared with the other channel. They were stored separately, as supplied; they were not in "one place and another". Nothing supplied excludes it.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Telefonanmälningar och e-post förvarades var för sig." (If the team as holder is wanted, "Underhållsteamet … förvarade telefonanmälningar och e-post var för sig.")
