# Elm Quay gave its shifts one repair log

Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats. In an eight-week trial, its maintenance team put repair reports from two buildings into one shared log, designed the categories itself, and kept the phone line open for residents. What the team counted afterwards is worth reading before you run a trial of your own.

By Thomas Barregren

Telephone reports went one place and emails another. For a maintenance team looking after 640 flats, that meant two separate stores of what had been reported. In September 2025, the team at Elm Quay Housing decided to try a shared repair log in two of its buildings, and to see what it would take.

## Elm Quay tested the status view before choosing Svale

The decision was the maintenance team's own. It tested whether the log could show the status of each repair, and chose Svale Systems after that test. No comparison with another supplier is available for this account.

Maya Lind, the maintenance supervisor, puts the problem in terms of the working day. “We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

## Elm Quay built the categories and Svale set up the log

The team designed its own categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale Systems configured the log and trained six staff in two sessions. The trial ran for eight weeks.

Lind's advice for the next building is about the calendar rather than the software. “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts,” she says.

## The note counts 31 reports and claims no cause

The numbers come from Elm Quay's internal trial note, dated 4 December 2025. It records 31 repair reports entered during the trial, excluding emergencies and work ordered before the trial began.

Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, against three in the preceding eight-week period. The two periods carried different workloads, and the note explicitly declines to attribute the difference to the software.

There are no figures for cost, for resident satisfaction, or for how long repairs took to complete: assignment is where the note's timing stops.

## Elm Quay will check the categories before it decides

The trial has not expanded yet. The team will look at how the categories work for larger repairs, and then decide; Lind, for her part, did not recommend Svale to every housing company. “I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”
