## 2. Findings

Ranked most severe first. Each is a defect against the material, with the smallest supported repair.

**Finding 1 — "on that basis" turns a sequence into a ground for the decision (line 11).**
Draft: "It tested whether the log could show the status of each repair, and chose Svale Systems **on that basis**."
Material: "It chose Svale Systems **after** testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
Case in which the material holds and the draft fails: the team ran the status test as a pass/fail precondition that several suppliers met, and then chose Svale on price and availability. That is compatible with the material, and nothing supplied excludes it; under it "after testing" is true and "on that basis" is false. Since no comparison with another supplier is available, the draft cannot know the test was the basis.
Repair: "It tested whether the log could show the status of each repair, and chose Svale Systems after that test."

**Finding 2 — the heading asserts that only one thing was tested (line 9).**
Draft: "Elm Quay tested **one thing** before choosing Svale"
Material: "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
Case: the team also checked that the log worked on the caretakers' phones, and the material simply names the status test as the one that settled the shortlist. Compatible with the material; no supplied statement excludes further testing. What is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened.
Repair: "Elm Quay tested the status view before choosing Svale".

**Finding 3 — "Nothing else was measured" generalises three named absences into all (line 27).**
Draft: "**Nothing else was measured.** There are no figures for cost, for resident satisfaction, or for how long repairs took to complete…"
Material: "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
Case: the note also records how the 31 reports split between the two buildings, or the hours spent in the two training sessions. Compatible with the material, which denies three specific measurement types and no others.
Repair: delete "Nothing else was measured." The sentence that follows already states the supported absences.

**Finding 4 — "and stops there" says the note holds nothing beyond the count, which the draft then contradicts (line 21).**
Draft heading: "The trial note counts 31 reports **and stops there**"
Material: the same note carries the medians and "the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software" — which the draft cites at line 25.

## 3. Editorial questions (not findings)

Reported here rather than as defects, because each is defensible as written.

**A. "and to see what it would take" (line 7).** The material gives the decision and its motive (shifts seeing the same information) but not this appraisal purpose. Reading it as an ordinary description of what a trial is leaves it supported; reading "what it would take" as effort or cost appraisal makes it an added motive, and the note has no cost figures. Author's framing of a trial, so not reported as a defect.

**B. "repair reports from two buildings" (line 3).** The material places the log in two buildings and counts 31 entered reports without saying both buildings contributed. This describes the trial's design rather than asserting a split, so it is defensible; a draft that said "reports from both buildings" as a result would not be.

**C. "The trial has not expanded" (line 31).** The material's "has not **yet** expanded" is a qualification; the draft drops "yet" but the next sentence ("The team will decide once it has checked…") restores the pending state, so no expectation is lost.

**D. "is the document to read before you set a date" (line 33).** Advocacy addressed to the reader, resting on the brief's own next step and correctly describing the destination as a document to read rather than a booking or a trial. The brief's standpoint, carried as the publisher's; no external evidence needed.
