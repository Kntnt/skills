# Source-support comparison — case-study-sv-r1

Draft language: Swedish. Source language: English. Quotations in the draft are translations.
Scope: source support and quotation translation only. General editorial and mechanical review excluded.
Method: each draft claim is set beside the source passage it rests on, quoted in the source's own words. Where the two diverge, a concrete case compatible with the supplied material is named in which one statement holds and the other fails. A case is set aside only where a supplied statement excludes it.

## 4. Findings (source support)

### Finding 1 — the median-time heading drops the interval that was measured

- **Draft (line 15):** "## Mediantiden låg på två arbetsdagar under testet"
- **Source:** "Median time **from report to assignment** was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period." And: "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."
- **What is missing:** the scope of the measurement. The body carries it one paragraph later, but the claim in the heading stands unqualified, and the contract holds this boundary "in the title, summary, headings and body alike".
- **Concrete case:** the median time from report to *completion* during the trial was twenty working days. Nothing in the material excludes this — it states outright that no completion-time measurement exists, so completion time is unknown and may take any value. In that case the source's claim holds and the heading, read as an ordinary reader reads a bare *mediantiden* under an article about repair reports, fails. This is the turning of assignment time into completion time the material names as a forbidden inference.
- **Smallest repair:** `## Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning låg på två arbetsdagar under testet`

### Finding 2 — the heading names larger repairs, not the categories, as what is to be tested

- **Draft (line 29):** "## Större reparationer ska prövas först"
- **Source:** "the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs"
- **What is changed:** the thing to be examined. In the source it is the category scheme, examined against the case of larger repairs; in the heading it is larger repairs themselves that are to be put to the test.
- **Concrete case:** the team sits down with its category list and a list of larger repairs already carried out, decides on paper that the categories do not fit them, and drops the matter — no larger repair is ever entered, tried or tested in the log. Compatible with the material, which says only that the categories will be checked. In that case the source's statement holds and "Större reparationer ska prövas" fails.
- **Smallest repair:** `## Kategorierna ska prövas mot större reparationer först`

### Finding 3 — an exhaustive absence of measurement beyond the three the source names

- **Draft (line 21):** "Mer än så mättes inte."
- **Source:** "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements." And: "No comparison with another supplier is available."
- **What is missing:** support for the universal. The material names three kinds of measurement that do not exist; it nowhere says that nothing else was measured, and the contract holds that "what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened".
- **Concrete case:** the internal trial note also recorded how many of the 31 reports were duplicates of a report already in the log. Nothing in the material excludes it; the trial note's contents are given only in part. In that case every sentence of the source holds and "Mer än så mättes inte" fails.
- **Smallest repair:** `Mer än så redovisas inte.` — a claim about what the material reports rather than about what was measured. Deleting the sentence outright also works, since the enumeration that follows carries the point.

### Finding 4 — the wish is the maintenance team's, not the company's

- **Draft (line 3):** "Ett bostadsbolag med 640 lägenheter ville att personal på olika skift skulle se samma information."
- **Source:** "In September 2025 **its own maintenance team** decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings. … **The team** wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information."
- **What is changed:** whose wish it is. The source attaches it to the team and stresses that the initiative was the team's own; the draft attaches it to the company. Company-level naming of an *act* is warranted by the brief ("The customer, Elm Quay Housing, should remain the acting party") and is used correctly in the title and in the section heading on line 9; a want held inside one team is not thereby a want of the organisation.
- **Concrete case:** Elm Quay's management never considered shift handover a problem and formed no view on it; the maintenance team alone wanted it and acted on its own, which is what "its own maintenance team decided" allows. In that case "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information" holds and the draft's sentence fails.
- **Smallest repair:** `Underhållsgruppen i ett bostadsbolag med 640 lägenheter ville att personal på olika skift skulle se samma information.`

### Finding 5 — the reservation loses the supplier it is about

- **Draft (line 31):** "och Maya Lind lämnar ingen rekommendation som gäller alla bostadsbolag"
- **Source:** "Lind did not recommend **Svale** to every housing company."
- **What is changed:** the scope of the denial. The source withholds one specific recommendation; the draft withholds every recommendation that would apply to all housing companies.
- **Concrete case:** Lind holds, and says, that every housing company ought to keep its repair reports in one shared view, while declining to recommend Svale's product to all of them. Nothing in the material excludes it — the supplied quotations are the complete usable ones, not the complete record of what she said, and the material's own sentence is specifically about Svale. In that case the source holds and the draft's broader denial fails.
- **Smallest repair:** `och Maya Lind rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag`

### Finding 6 — the eight weeks are placed inside the September decision

- **Draft (line 7):** "I september 2025 bestämde sig underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing för att prova en gemensam logg för felanmälningar i två hus **under åtta veckor**."
- **Source:** "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." And, separately: "The trial lasted eight weeks."
- **What is changed:** the eight weeks become part of what was decided in September, that is a planned length, where the source gives them only as the length the trial turned out to have. The deck states the same figure correctly, as duration.
- **Concrete case:** the team decided in September to run the log open-endedly and see how it went, and wound the trial up after eight weeks when it had enough to judge by. Compatible with the material, which says nothing about a term being set at the decision. In that case "The trial lasted eight weeks" holds and the draft's sentence fails.
- **Smallest repair:** end the sentence at "i två hus" and state the length separately: `… för att prova en gemensam logg för felanmälningar i två hus. Testet kom att pågå i åtta veckor.`

## 6. Editorial questions and notes (not defects, no repair proposed)

- **E1 — what the first quotation adds.** The deck ("personal på olika skift skulle se samma information") and the section heading ("Elm Quay stod för indelningen, Svale för uppsättningen") state in advance both halves of the quotation on line 13. The quotation is accurately translated and accurately attributed, so this is not a source-support defect; whether it carries experience beyond the surrounding narrative — one of the things the material asks to be judged — is an editorial question for the owning Skill.
- **E2 — the pending decision.** The source has "the trial has not yet expanded; **the team will decide** after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." The draft keeps "ännu inte" and "först" but not the decision point, so the section can be read as an expansion awaiting a formality. The draft asserts no expansion, and "not yet" is the source's own word, so this is defensible as written; naming the decision (for instance "… innan gruppen tar ställning till fler hus") would close the implicature.
- **E3 — publication status.** The material states: "The supplier approved publication of this case as supplier-published material; it is not independent journalism." The draft makes no claim of independence, so nothing in it is unsupported. Whether a supplier-published case carrying a personal byline should disclose that status is an editorial and policy question, not a defect in the draft's claims.
