## 4. Findings

Four. Each is a defect against the supplied material, not a preference, and each is given with the smallest supported repair.

### F1 — "som går igenom stegen" describes a document the material never describes

- **Draft** (line 36): "Väger ni samma beslut kan ni läsa [checklistan för införande](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist), **som går igenom stegen**."
- **Support in the material:** none. The brief supplies only: "The optional next step is the supplied implementation checklist at https://example.invalid/svale/checklist; it is a document to read, not a consultation booking or a product trial." Nothing states what the checklist contains or how it is organised.
- **What is missing:** the relative clause is a claim about the destination's contents — that it walks through *the steps* (definite: the steps of an införande). It is not the author's own argument or reflection resting on supported material; it is a statement of fact about a document, presented to the reader as given.
- **Concrete failing case (compatible with the material):** the checklist is a list of questions to put to a supplier and conditions to confirm before deciding — no sequence, no steps. The brief's statement ("a document to read") holds; "som går igenom stegen" fails. Nothing supplied excludes this case; the brief describes the checklist's purpose and what it is *not*, never its structure.
- **Why it matters here:** the brief names this explicitly among the judging criteria — "whether the suggested link accurately describes its destination."
- **Smallest supported repair:** delete the relative clause.
  `Väger ni samma beslut kan ni läsa [checklistan för införande](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist).`
  (*Checklistan för införande* is itself supported by "implementation checklist" and needs no change; *läsa* correctly presents it as a document rather than a booking or a trial.)

### F2 — "Åtta veckor senare" places the trial note and the assessment at a time the material does not support

- **Draft** (line 14): "**Åtta veckor senare** fanns både en testnotering med siffror och en arbetsledare med en bedömning."
- **Support in the material:** "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial…"; "The trial lasted eight weeks."; "Elm Quay's internal trial note, **4 December 2025**, says that 31 repair reports were entered." The date of the customer interview is not supplied at all.
- **What is changed:** the material dates the note; the draft instead locates it eight weeks after the September start, and asserts that the supervisor's assessment existed at that same moment.
- **Concrete failing case (compatible with the material):** the team decides in early September and starts the trial on 1 September; eight weeks later is 27 October. On that date the note dated 4 December does not yet exist, and nothing supplied says the interview had happened either. "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025" holds; "Åtta veckor senare fanns … en testnotering … och en arbetsledare med en bedömning" fails. Nothing supplied excludes this case — no supplied statement fixes the start date within September, and none says the note was written at the trial's close. (Only a start around 9 October would make the sentence come out true, and nothing supplied requires that either.)
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace the offset with the sequence the material does carry.
  `Efter de åtta veckorna fanns både en testnotering med siffror och en arbetsledare med en bedömning.`
  (The note's exact date is already given correctly in its own section, so nothing is lost.)

### F3 — "finns inte" turns an unavailable comparison into a non-existent one

- **Draft** (line 14): "Teamet valde Svale Systems efter att ha prövat om loggen kunde visa status för varje ärende, och någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör **finns inte**."
- **Support in the material:** "No comparison with another supplier **is available**."
- **What is changed:** a statement about what this account has to hand becomes a statement about what exists in the world. The Claims section is directly on point: "Preserve the state of knowledge: what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened."
- **Concrete failing case (compatible with the material):** the maintenance team did sketch an informal comparison against a second supplier in an internal email thread, and that comparison was simply not made available for this case. "No comparison with another supplier is available" holds; "någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte" fails. Nothing supplied excludes this case. That the material means the narrower thing is visible in its own usage elsewhere: where it does intend absolute absence it writes "There **are no** cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements", which the draft renders correctly at line 30.
- **Smallest supported repair:** restore the availability reading with two words.
  `…, och någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte att tillgå.`

### F4 — the heading widens "categories" to "the content"

- **Draft** (line 16), heading: "Leverantören konfigurerade, teamet bestämde **innehållet**."
- **Support in the material:** "The maintenance team designed **its categories** and kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions."; Lind: "**The categories** were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
- **What is changed:** the team's decision rights are extended from the category scheme to the log's content. The Claims section holds this boundary in headings as in the body.
- **Concrete failing case (compatible with the material):** Svale's configuration supplies the log's mandatory fields, status labels and report templates as standard, and the team settles only the category scheme within them. "The maintenance team designed its categories" holds; "teamet bestämde innehållet" fails. Nothing supplied excludes this case — the material grants the team the categories and the telephone channel and nothing further, and Lind's own quotation has Svale involved in putting even the categories in. On a second reading of *innehållet*, the log's content is the 31 reports themselves, which the team did not decide at all.
- **Smallest supported repair:** name what the material grants them.
  `## Leverantören konfigurerade, teamet bestämde kategorierna`

## 5. Observations — not findings

Recorded because the comparison touched them; each is defensible as written, and none carries a repair.

- **O1 — Title scope.** "Elm Quay fick en gemensam bild av felanmälningarna" states the shared view of Elm Quay without naming the two buildings; reports from the rest of the 640 flats stayed split between telephone and email. The deck immediately below scopes it ("prövade hösten 2025 … i två av husen"), which is how an ordinary reader takes a headline, so this is not reported as a defect.
- **O2 — Whose note.** The material says "**Elm Quay's** internal trial note"; the draft says "den interna testnoteringen" without an owner, in a case study whose publisher is the supplier. The note is introduced in a paragraph wholly about Elm Quay's trial, which settles the referent, so no claim comes out false. If an editor wants the attribution explicit, "Elm Quays interna testnotering från den 4 december 2025" costs two words.
- **O3 — *tillskriva* construction.** "tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden **till** programvaran" follows the English "attribute … to"; the Swedish verb takes the thing credited directly (*tillskriver inte programvaran skillnaden*). The meaning is unchanged, so this is mechanical rather than a source-support matter, and mechanical review is outside this comparison.
- **O4 — Tense of Lind's reservation.** The material reports "Lind **did not** recommend Svale to every housing company"; the draft uses the present, "rekommenderar inte". The present is the ordinary Swedish register for a standing assessment (the draft says *säger* throughout), and the shift does not weaken the reservation the brief requires to survive — if anything it extends it. Not reported as a defect.
- **O5 — Referent of *det*.** In "teamet har ännu inte utvidgat testet: först ska det se hur kategorierna fungerar", *det* agrees with both *teamet* and *testet*. Only the team reading is coherent, and the material settles it ("the team will decide after checking"), so no claim is changed; a reader is briefly slowed.
- **O6 — *du* then *ni*.** The deck addresses the reader as *du*, the closing paragraph as *ni*. `composition-sv.md` makes *du* the default and warns that *ni* to a single reader reads as a translation artefact; here *ni* plausibly addresses the reader's organisation. No factual claim is affected, and the clause is conditional, so it is raised as a composition matter, not a source-support finding.
- **O7 — *säger* for email answers.** The material states "All interviews occurred by email." *Säger* with a speech dash is the ordinary Swedish attribution for a quoted statement regardless of channel, and the draft honours what the email origin actually forbids: no physical scene, no emotion and no remembered dialogue appear anywhere. Whether to write *skriver* instead is an editorial question, not a defect.
- **O8 — No supplier-published disclosure.** The material says "The supplier approved publication of this case as supplier-published material; it is not independent journalism." The draft carries no such disclosure. This is an omission rather than an unsupported claim, so it is outside the findings; it is noted because it bears on how O2 reads.
