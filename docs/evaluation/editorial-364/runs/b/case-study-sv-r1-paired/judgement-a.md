## 2. Bridges into quotations

### Quotation 1 — "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra – Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen…"

- **Bridge, input:** "Kategorierna i loggen tog underhållsteamet fram själv, och de boende kunde fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon. Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen. Testet pågick i åtta veckor."
- **Bridge, delivered:** "De boende kunde fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon. Svale utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen. Testet pågick i åtta veckor."
- **Class before: (a).** "Kategorierna i loggen tog underhållsteamet fram själv" is the quotation's own point — "Kategorierna var våra" — delivered first, so the reader meets the quotation as the bridge said again. The other clauses are class (c) material, but the leading clause settles the class.
- **Class after: (c).** "de boende kunde fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon", "utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen", "Testet pågick i åtta veckor" — none of these is in the quotation, and nothing in the bridge pre-empts it any longer.
- **Named by the reply?** Yes, twice and precisely: finding 2 and the removals list ("Borttagna, båda av rättningen av anmärkningen om att berättartexten sade citatet i förväg"), naming both removed clauses by their words.
- **New material?** None. Every clause left in the bridge was already in the input; nothing supplies an event, fact, name or figure the rest of the text does not carry.
- **Lost?** Yes — "Svale konfigurerade loggen", a fact the quotation does not carry and which now appears nowhere. The reply states this ("Den har ingen motsvarighet kvar i texten") and calls it unresolved. The other loss, "Kategorierna … tog underhållsteamet fram själv", survives in Maya Lind's own words, as the reply says.
- **Speech tag:** "skriver Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet på Elm Quay Housing" — unchanged, attribution plus role only, class (b) before and after.

### Quotation 2 — "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna…"

- **Bridge, input:** no narrative bridge; the preceding paragraph is quotation 1. The only lead-in is the trailing speech tag "skriver hon".
- **Bridge, delivered:** identical.
- **Class before: (b).** Bare attribution. **Class after: (b).** Unchanged.
- **Named by the reply?** No change to name. No new material, nothing lost.

### Quotation 3 — "Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen…"

- **Bridge, input:** "Testet har ännu inte utvidgats. Teamet vill först se hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer, och Maya Lind rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsföretag." Tag: "skriver hon".
- **Bridge, delivered:** identical, word for word.
- **Class before: (c).** "har ännu inte utvidgats", "större reparationer", "rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsföretag" are facts the quotation does not carry; the quotation's own content — that she would run the test again and would add a preparation week — is nowhere in the bridge.
- **Class after: (c).** Unchanged.
- **Named by the reply?** No change to name. No new material, nothing lost.

## 4. R1 — **pass**

The input was not clean, and the defects the run went after are visible in the text alone: a back-reference ("efter den prövningen", "ett eget test") to an evaluation the text never describes; a narrative clause that delivered the first quotation's point before the quotation did; a heading echoed almost verbatim by the ingress; and a section heading, "Mediantiden var lägre under testet", broader than the body it labels, which says only time to assignment was measured. Each was addressed. Nothing was invented: no event, name, figure or date entered the text, no quotation was touched, every hedge and every negative finding came through, and no change is excused by material the Skill did not have.

Removals and changed claims, and how the reply treats them:

- **"i två av husen" out of the ingress (difference 1).** Reported, as finding 1 and again under "Flyttade — Omfattning", and reported accurately: the reply states that the scope survives in the heading and the opening paragraph but not in the ingress read alone, and that the loss arose in its own correction round. Accurate, and the weakest point of the delivery — the fix for the heading echo cost a scope qualifier it did not have to cost, and paid for it by adding "åtta veckor", which the reply itself then files as finding 4 (the duration now stated three times).
- **"Svale konfigurerade loggen" (difference 6).** Reported, in the removals list and in finding 2, accurately including the point that matters: the quotation's "hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen" is narrower and does not replace it. This is a fact lost outright, and the report does not soften it.
- **"Kategorierna i loggen tog underhållsteamet fram själv" (difference 5).** Reported, accurately: the fact survives in the quotation.
- **Chronology "Efter den prövningen" (difference 3).** Reported under "Flyttade — Tidsordning", accurately, and correctly as the point of the fix rather than collateral.
- **Heading 2 rewrite (difference 4).** Reported, and self-critically: finding 3 states the replacement covers its section worse than what it replaced and that the section now opens on a fact the heading does not concern.
- **Heading 4 narrowing to "Mediantiden till tilldelning" (difference 8).** Not reported, and the reply's closing "Inga andra påståenden ändrades" is therefore overbroad. The change is in the right direction — it brings the heading into line with what the body measures — but a heading's scope is a claim, and it went unnamed.
- **Dash normalisation (7) and the CTA rewrite (9).** Not reported. Mechanical and taste respectively; neither moves a fact, and the second leaves url and sponsorship disclosure untouched.

Against the criterion, the run corrected real defects, left voice and argument standing, altered no quotation, and disclosed its two substantive losses in its own words rather than hiding them behind the fixes that caused them. What it should not have done: rewrite a working heading into one it then had to file as worse, and buy a repetition fix with a scope qualifier. Those are damage, but disclosed damage inside its own findings, and they do not reach the text outside them. Two unreported changes (8, 9) and one overbroad closing sentence keep the pass from being clean.
