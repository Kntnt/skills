## 2. Bridges into quotations (every quotation in the input, in order)

**Q1 — `"We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log,"` (speech tag: `writes Maya Lind, Elm Quay's maintenance supervisor, who answered questions by email`)**

- **Bridge before:** `The team designed the categories itself and kept telephone reporting open for residents.`
- **Bridge after:** `The team kept telephone reporting open for residents.`
- **Class before: (a).** `The team designed the categories itself` delivers, in the text's own voice, exactly what the quotation is there to deliver — `The categories were ours`. A reader who has read the bridge meets that half of the quotation as the bridge said again. (The remaining clause is class (c) material, but the pre-echo is what fixes the class.)
- **Class after: (c).** `kept telephone reporting open for residents` is a fact the quotation does not carry; the quotation speaks about shift handover and who owned the categories, neither of which the bridge touches.
- **Named by the reply?** Yes — open finding 3 names the change precisely, states that the bridge no longer prepares its quotation and that the paragraph now holds two unconnected things, and says which repair caused it. The removed clause is also listed under *Claims the round removed*.
- **New fact supplied?** No. The after-bridge's content (`kept telephone reporting open for residents`) is verbatim from the input.
- **Anything lost that the quotation does not carry?** The text's own assertion that the team *designed* the categories. The quotation asserts ownership (`The categories were ours`), which is adjacent but not identical — *ours* can be read as authorship or as possession. This is a real, if small, loss of a narrative claim; the reply reports it accurately and does not overstate the quotation's coverage.
- **Speech tag:** unchanged. It carries more than bare attribution (`who answered questions by email`), and that method disclosure survives intact.

**Q2 — `"We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts,"` (tag: `she writes`)**

- **Bridge before:** `Lind would plan the next building differently.`
- **Bridge after:** identical — unchanged.
- **Class before: (a). Class after: (a).** `would plan the next building differently` states what the quotation then says (`I would set that time aside before the next building starts`); the reader meets the quotation as the bridge said again. This class (a) bridge was in the input and the run left it alone.
- No change, so nothing to report, nothing supplied, nothing lost.

**Q3 — `"I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation,"` (tag: `she writes`)**

- **Bridge before:** `Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company, and her own appraisal carries a condition.`
- **Bridge after:** identical — unchanged.
- **Class before: (c). Class after: (c).** `did not recommend Svale to every housing company` is a fact the quotation does not carry — the quotation is confined to Lind's own repeat decision and her one reservation, and says nothing about what she declined to recommend to others. (The trailing clause `her own appraisal carries a condition` is class (a) flagging of the quotation's `but`; the non-recommendation fact is what sets the class.)
- No change, so nothing to report, nothing supplied, nothing lost.

## 4. R1 verdict

**Pass.**

Defects addressed were concrete and visible in the text alone, not matters of taste: a heading restating its own first sentence (twice), a sentence restating the one before it with a pronoun that resolved to the wrong noun, and a bridge that pre-said its quotation. Nothing was rewritten to satisfy preference — four of the five sections' prose is untouched, every quotation is verbatim, and every sentence that bounds what the text asserts survives exactly: `No comparison with another supplier is available`, the exclusions on the count of 31, `the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software`, `Cost, resident satisfaction, and completion time were not measured`, `Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company`, and the publisher disclosure. No claim's strength, subject, scope, attribution, chronology or modality moved. Nothing was excused by absent material, and nothing was invented: every word in a changed heading or sentence is supported by text that was already on the page.
