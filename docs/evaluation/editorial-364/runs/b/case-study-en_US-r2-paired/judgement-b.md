## 4. R1 verdict — **pass**, with two blemishes named

Removals and changed claims, and whether the reply reports them accurately:

- **Removal of "The team designed the categories itself"** — reported, accurately, in the claim account, including where the claim still stands (Lind's words and the first subheading). Legitimate: a pre-echo removal that loses no fact.
- **"manages" → "is responsible for"; "Elm Quay Housing" → "the company"** — reported, but described as "the same relation named differently". That is a shade generous: being responsible for 640 apartments is not the same assertion as managing them. Small drift, disclosed rather than hidden.
- **"telephone reports and emails had been stored separately" → per-repair filing** — not named individually; it rides inside a change the reply does name, and it preserves the substance. Minor reporting gap.
- **Subheading rewrite** — reported at length, in the right terms, and correctly refused as unrepairable on the spent budget, with the reason (the run's own repair created it) stated plainly. The reply even prescribes the fix. This is the honest handling of a self-inflicted defect, but the defect still ships: the delivered text carries "fell by a day" over a section that supplies two medians and then bounds them, and the scanning reader takes away a result the note declines to give. The input's own heading was merely repetitive — the lesser fault — so reverting its own change at zero budget cost was available and was not taken.
- **Locale corrections (1, 5, 7, 8)** — reported as a group and accurately characterized; each is a genuine en_US mechanic, and none touches meaning.

Everything outside the findings is intact: voice, argument order, all three quotations, all three bridges unchanged in wording and class, and every limiting sentence — no supplier comparison, the note not attributing cause, "Assignment is not completion", the absent cost/satisfaction/completion figures, the non-generalization, and the un-expanded trial. No taste rewriting of clean prose. Nothing was excused by material the Skill never had. The claim account is complete enough that a reader can reconstruct the diff from it, which is what the criterion asks for; the two blemishes are a softened characterization of one claim change and an overstated heading left standing rather than reverted.
