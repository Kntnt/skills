## 3. Inferences tested and set aside

Each of these is a place where the draft is tighter than the material's literal wording. For each, a concrete case was constructed and then tested against the supplied statements; none stands as a defect.

### 3.1 "two of its buildings" (C2)

Case: Elm Quay Housing manages buildings owned by third parties, so the two trial buildings are not "its" buildings.
Set aside: the material says "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats" and calls the team "its own maintenance team"; a possessive in this register denotes the buildings in its management, which the material establishes. The draft claims no ownership anywhere.

### 3.2 "over eight weeks" / "entered during the eight weeks" (C3, C21)

Case: the log stayed in use after the trial, and the note of 4 December 2025 counts everything entered up to that date, so a few of the 31 fall outside the eight weeks.
Set aside: the count is introduced by the material as the content of "Elm Quay's internal trial note" and is immediately qualified by "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial" — an exclusion that binds the count to trial work. Reading the note's count as the trial's count is what the material's own framing states, not an addition to it. The draft also attributes the figure to the note ("records"), so the note's own scope, whatever its edges, is what the draft reports.

### 3.3 "Repair reports reached its maintenance team by telephone and by email" (C6)

Case: the "emails" stored separately were correspondence about repairs rather than reports, so not every report channel was as described.
Set aside: the material's sentence sets "telephone reports and emails" in parallel inside a paragraph whose subject is repair reporting, and confirms that residents report by telephone ("kept telephone reporting open for residents"). The draft asserts only the two channels and the separate storage the material states, and adds no volume, share or procedure.

### 3.4 "six of Elm Quay's staff members" (C17)

Case: some of the six trained were contractors rather than Elm Quay employees.
Set aside: the material describes a supplier training staff at the customer during that customer's trial ("Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions"), which is a statement about the customer's staff; "six" and "two sessions" are carried unchanged, and no role, seniority or department is added.

### 3.5 "were not measured" (C25)

Case: cost or satisfaction was measured somewhere but not written into the trial note.
Set aside by the supplied statement "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements", which denies the existence of such measurements rather than their presence in the note.
