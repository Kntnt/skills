# Source and quotation comparison — case-study-sv-r1, draft 1

Draft: `scratch/source-check/draft-1.md` (Swedish, `sv`).
Material: `work/source.md` (English brief + material).
Context: `scratch/source-check/context.md` (resolved language, Claims section, quotation guidance, Swedish composition guidance).

Scope: source support for every factual and attributed claim, including headings, deck, attribution tags and the closing call to action; plus a separate fidelity check of the three translated quotations. General editorial and mechanical review is out of scope. The byline `Av Thomas Barregren` comes from the invocation, not the material, and is not assessed.

## 1. Claim accounting

Each entry gives the draft passage, the source passage it rests on quoted in the source's own words, what differs, and the verdict. Findings are carried forward to section 4.

### 1.1 Title — "Elm Quay testade gemensam logg i två hus"

Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." / "The trial lasted eight weeks."

Differs: the draft names Elm Quay as the actor where the material names its maintenance team; the team is Elm Quay's own team ("its own maintenance team"), and the brief requires the customer to remain the acting party. The title reports the trial as having happened, which the eight-week duration and the 4 December trial note establish. No causal or outcome claim in the title. **Supported.**

### 1.2 Deck — "Elm Quay Housing förvaltar 640 lägenheter."

Source: "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats."

Differs: nothing. *Förvaltar* renders *manages* and asserts no ownership. **Supported.**

### 1.3 Deck — "Hösten 2025 testade underhållsteamet en gemensam logg för felanmälningar i två av husen."

Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." / "The trial lasted eight weeks." / "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025 …".

Differs: the draft dates the trial (*hösten 2025*) where the material dates the decision (September 2025). An eight-week trial decided in September and reported on in a note of 4 December 2025 falls inside autumn 2025, so the period is entailed rather than added. *I två av husen* ("in two of the buildings") presupposes Elm Quay has more than two; "the next building starts" in Lind's second quotation and the unexpanded trial both carry that. V2 order is correct after the fronted adverbial. **Supported.**

### 1.4 Deck — "vad underhållsansvariga Maya Lind skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång"

Source: "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind"; "I would set that time aside before the next building starts."; "I would leave an extra week for preparation."

Differs: **the role named.** *Underhållsansvarig* asserts that Lind is the person responsible for maintenance; the material calls her a *maintenance supervisor*, one who supervises. Concrete case, compatible with the material: Elm Quay has an underhållschef above Lind, and Lind supervises one shift of the maintenance team. Nothing in the supplied material excludes it — she is introduced only as an interviewee, and the material never places her at the head of the function. Under "maintenance supervisor" the statement holds; under *underhållsansvarig* it fails. The reverse case also stands: a person can hold responsibility for maintenance without supervising staff. "Skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång" is carried by both of her forward-looking quotations. **Finding F1** (role). Remainder supported.

### 1.5 Deck — "vad teamet valde, vad den interna testnoteringen visar"

Source: "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair." / "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered."

Differs: nothing. **Supported.**

### 1.6 "Hos underhållsteamet på Elm Quay Housing, ett bostadsföretag med 640 lägenheter, lagrades telefonanmälningar och mejl var för sig."

Source: "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately." / "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats."

Differs: **where the storing happened.** The material states that the reports were stored separately; it does not state who stored them or where. The draft places the storing at the maintenance team. Concrete case, compatible with the material: Elm Quay's central service desk took the telephone reports into one system and kept the emails in a shared mailbox, passing work to the maintenance team, which stored nothing itself — the team would still have had the shift-visibility problem the material describes. No supplied statement excludes it; "The maintenance team … kept telephone reporting open for residents" says who kept the channel open, not who stored what came in. **Finding F6** (locative). The priorness of that state is carried by the following sentence's date, so "Previously" survives without a word for it. Supported otherwise.

On "ett bostadsföretag med 640 lägenheter": the material's verb is *manages*, and a company can have flats it does not manage and manage flats it does not have. The deck three lines above states *förvaltar 640 lägenheter*, which fixes the relation for the reader before the appositive compresses it, and the brief itself addresses "small housing companies". Recorded as examined; see section 6.

### 1.7 "I september 2025 bestämde teamet sig för att testa en gemensam logg för felanmälningar i två av husen."

Source: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings."

Differs: nothing of substance. Date, actor, act (decision, not start), object and place all match. *Felanmälan* is the established Swedish term for a resident's report of a fault, and the draft uses it consistently for "repair report" here and at the count of 31. **Supported.**

### 1.8 "Syftet var att personal på olika skift skulle se samma information."

Differs: whose state it is — a want of the team, rendered as the purpose of the trial. Placed immediately after the team's own decision to trial, *syftet* reads as that team's purpose and no other party's; the draft attributes the aim to nobody else and adds no further aim. **Supported.**

### 1.9 Heading — "Elm Quay valde efter ett eget test"

Differs: nothing. The testing was the customer's own ("It chose … after testing"; "its own maintenance team"), the order (test, then choice) is preserved, and the heading claims no outcome for that test. **Supported.**

### 1.10 "Underhållsteamet ville veta om loggen kunde visa status för varje reparation. Efter den prövningen valde teamet Svale Systems."

Source: "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."

### 1.11 "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte."

Source: "No comparison with another supplier is available."

Differs: **certainty / whose knowledge.** "Is available" states that no comparison is at hand; *finns inte* states that none exists. Concrete case, compatible with the material: Elm Quay's maintenance team wrote an internal memo comparing Svale with a second supplier, and that memo was not released for this case. The source statement holds of that case; the draft's does not. No supplied statement excludes it — the material says only that no comparison is available, and the Claims section holds that "what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened." **Finding F4.**

### 1.12 Heading — "Svale konfigurerade, teamet bestämde indelningen"

Differs: nothing. Each party's act is attributed to that party, and the supplier stays in the third person. **Supported.**

### 1.13 "Kategorierna i loggen tog underhållsteamet fram själv, och de boende kunde fortsätta anmäla fel per telefon."

Source: "The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents."

Differs: the second clause states the resulting state (residents could go on reporting by telephone) where the material states the team's act of keeping the channel open. The state is what the act consists in; the draft does not transfer the agency to another party, and the first clause keeps the categories as the team's own work (*själv*, matching "its categories"). **Supported.**

### 1.14 "Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen. Testet pågick i åtta veckor."

Source: "Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions." / "The trial lasted eight weeks."

Differs: nothing. Six, two and eight all match; *vid två tillfällen* renders "during two sessions" without claiming anything about their length. **Supported.**

### 1.15 Attribution — "säger Maya Lind, underhållsansvarig på Elm Quay Housing" and "säger hon" (twice)

Source: "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind" / "All interviews occurred by email, and no physical scene, emotion or remembered dialogue is provided."

Differs: **the manner of the utterance**, and **the role named**. The material states that all interviews occurred by email, so Lind's words were written, not spoken; *säger* asserts speech. Concrete case: the material's own case — Lind typed these answers into an email reply and never uttered them. That case is not merely compatible with the material, it is what the material states. Under "All interviews occurred by email" *skriver* holds and *säger* does not. The Claims section makes circumstantial detail, including manner, a claim in its own right. The speech dash itself is not at issue: the resolved Swedish guidance sets reported speech with a speech dash, and the draft follows it. **Finding F5** (manner), **Finding F1** (role, second instance). The rest of the tag — Lind's employer — is carried by the material's framing of the interview as the customer interview.

### 1.16 Heading — "Mediantiden var lägre under testet – orsaken är öppen"

Source: "Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period. The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."

Differs: the heading names no measure, and the second half characterises the state of knowledge in the draft's own words. *Lägre* is an evaluative comparative, and the Claims section permits one where a comparison exists — two working days against three is that comparison, supplied in the material. The heading asserts no cause, hedged or otherwise: *orsaken är öppen* denies that a cause has been settled rather than proposing one, and the material establishes no cause for the difference anywhere. The measure is named in full in the first line of the section beneath it (*från anmälan till tilldelning*), so the forbidden slide from assignment time to completion time is not made. **Supported**; see section 6 for the judgement call.

### 1.17 "Elm Quays interna testnotering från den 4 december 2025 redovisar 31 registrerade felanmälningar."

Source: "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered."

Differs: nothing. The figure is given to the note rather than asserted flat, the date matches, and the genitive takes a bare *s* on a name not ending in a sibilant, as the resolved guidance requires. **Supported.**

### 1.18 "Akuta ärenden och arbeten som beställts före testet räknas inte in."

Source: "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial."

Differs: the material attributes the exclusion to the note; the draft's agentless s-passive leaves the excluder implicit, immediately after naming the note. The count is given with its exclusions, as the Claims section requires. **Supported**; the paragraph boundary that separates this from the median figures is recorded in section 6.

### 1.19 "Tiden från anmälan till tilldelning var i median två arbetsdagar under testet och tre under de åtta veckorna dessförinnan."

Source: "Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period."

Differs: nothing. The modifier *i median* is placed so that it governs both figures, as "median" does in the source across both clauses; *tre* carries *arbetsdagar* by the same ellipsis the source uses; *tilldelning* is assignment and not completion; the comparison period keeps its length and its position before the trial. Double definiteness is marked on *de åtta veckorna*. **Supported.**

### 1.20 "Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning, och noteringen avstår uttryckligen från att tillskriva programvaran skillnaden."

Source: "The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software."

Differs: *avstår uttryckligen från att tillskriva* renders "explicitly does not attribute"; both make the non-attribution a deliberate act of the note rather than an omission, which is what "explicitly" carries. The confound and the non-attribution are both kept, and neither is weakened into a hint. **Supported.**

### 1.21 "Kostnader, de boendes nöjdhet och tid till avslutat arbete mättes inte."

Source: "There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements."

Differs: the material states that no such measurements exist; the draft states that these things were not measured. The source's assertion is flat — unlike the comparison at 1.11, it is not qualified by availability — so the two coincide. All three absences are kept, and the completion-time absence is stated in the same breath as the assignment figure it could otherwise be confused with. **Supported.**

### 1.22 Heading — "Beslutet om fler hus dröjer" and "Testet har ännu inte utvidgats."

Source: "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs."

Differs: the heading names the decision's subject matter (*fler hus*) where the material leaves the decision's object implicit. Expansion of a trial run in two buildings is expansion to further buildings, and Lind's "before the next building starts" fixes the unit. *Dröjer* says the decision has not yet come, which "will decide after checking" carries; it asserts no lateness against any deadline, and no deadline is supplied. *Har ännu inte utvidgats* keeps "not yet" and so keeps the possibility open rather than closing it. **Supported.**

### 1.23 "Teamet vill först se hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer"

Source: "the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs."

Differs: a stated plan rendered as a wish. The object of the checking, its subject and its position before the decision are all preserved, and the draft claims no further condition on the decision. **Supported.**

### 1.24 "och någon rekommendation till alla bostadsföretag ger Maya Lind inte"

Source: "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company." / "Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment. It is not an inference from the figures."

Differs: **what was not recommended.** The material withholds a recommendation of one named thing, Svale, from the universal class of housing companies. The draft withholds *någon rekommendation* — any recommendation at all — from that class. Concrete case, compatible with the material: Lind would recommend every housing company keep a shared view of its repair reports in some form, while declining to recommend Svale to every one of them. Nothing supplied excludes it; her final quotation says "Having one view of the reports helps us", which is about her own organisation and leaves her general view on shared logs unstated, and the material's only statement on the scope of her recommendation is the one about Svale. Under the source the statement holds; under the draft's wider negation it fails. The placement before her final quotation correctly frames that quotation as the qualified assessment the material says it is. **Finding F2.**

### 1.25 "Planerar du ett liknande test?"

Source: brief — "Write a journalistic customer case for operations managers at small housing companies."

Differs: this is address, not assertion; it presupposes nothing about the reader beyond the brief's audience. *Du* is the default address the resolved Swedish guidance prescribes. **Supported.**

### 1.26 "Svale Systems implementeringschecklista är ett dokument att läsa igenom innan arbetet börjar: https://example.invalid/svale/checklist."

The draft adds that it is read before the work begins. Concrete case, compatible with the material: the checklist is a step-by-step list worked through during an implementation, item by item as each stage is reached, and reading it before starting would tell a reader little. No supplied statement excludes it; "The optional next step is the supplied implementation checklist" places the checklist after reading this case, not before an implementation. **Finding F3.** The rest is accurate about the destination: a document, not a booking and not a trial, and the URL matches character for character. The compound is written as one word and the genitive on a name ending in *s* takes nothing, both as the resolved guidance requires.

## 3. Translated quotations

Checked separately from source support: same meaning, stance, certainty and distinctive voice, expressed in idiomatic Swedish speech rather than English syntax.

**Q1.** Source: "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."
Draft: "Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna var våra – Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen."
Meaning, both shifts, the pluperfect "had already done" and the ownership assertion "were ours" are all carried. The semicolon becomes a speech dash; in Swedish the dash links the two halves as the semicolon does, and the second half remains a subordinate acknowledgement of Svale's help rather than becoming a concession or a contrast. *Lägga in dem i loggen* is what a Swedish speaker says for putting categories into a system. No addition: Svale *helped*, and the draft does not let Svale do the work. **No finding.**

**Q2.** Source: "We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts."
Draft: "Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus drar i gång."
The comparison, its two terms and their order survive. *Avsätta* is exactly "set aside" in the sense of reserving time. The fronting of *Den tiden* is what Swedish V2 order does with a topicalised object and carries emphasis, not new meaning. *Drar i gång* is idiomatic and slightly informal where "starts" is neutral, and it reproduces the source's own ellipsis — a building does not literally start in either language, and the reading in both is that work on the next building begins. That is a register choice inside the permitted latitude, not an obstruction to the source reading. **No finding.**

**Q3.** Source: "I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation."
Draft: "Jag skulle välja att göra testet igen. Att ha en samlad bild av anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga in en extra vecka för förberedelser."
The conditional *skulle välja* keeps the qualified endorsement as an endorsement, the present-tense *hjälper oss* keeps the benefit scoped to her own organisation, and *men* keeps the reservation attached to the endorsement rather than following it as an afterthought. *En samlad bild av anmälningarna* renders "one view of the reports": both say that the reports are seen together in one place, which is the reading the first quotation's evening-shift/morning-shift contrast settles. *Lägga in en extra vecka* is allowing an extra week, as "leave" is here. Nothing is sharpened: she does not say the trial succeeded, and the draft does not make her. **No finding.**

The attribution verb that introduces Q1–Q3 is a claim of the draft's narration, not of the quotations, and is handled at 1.15 as F5.

## 4. Source-support findings

**F1 — Lind's role is raised from supervising to being responsible for maintenance.**
Draft: "underhållsansvariga Maya Lind" (line 3) and "säger Maya Lind, underhållsansvarig på Elm Quay Housing" (line 19).
Missing support: the material's only statement of her role is "Customer interview with maintenance supervisor Maya Lind". *Underhållsansvarig* names the holder of responsibility for the function; *maintenance supervisor* names someone who supervises within it, and the material places nobody at the head of Elm Quay's maintenance. A person can be either without being the other, so the two terms do not name the same thing here.
Smallest repair: use the supervising role in both places — line 3 "vad arbetsledaren Maya Lind skulle göra annorlunda nästa gång"; line 19 "säger Maya Lind, arbetsledare för underhållet på Elm Quay Housing".

**F2 — the reservation is widened from Svale to any recommendation at all.**
Draft: "och någon rekommendation till alla bostadsföretag ger Maya Lind inte" (line 31).
Changed support: "Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company." The material withholds a recommendation of Svale; the draft withholds every recommendation. It also loses the named product from the customer's reservation, which the brief requires to survive.
Smallest repair: name what she does not recommend — "och Svale rekommenderar Maya Lind inte till alla bostadsföretag".

**F3 — the checklist is given a time of use the brief does not supply.**
Draft: "är ett dokument att läsa igenom innan arbetet börjar" (line 35).
Missing support: "it is a document to read, not a consultation booking or a product trial." The brief settles what the destination is and rules out two things it is not; it says nothing about when it is read, and an implementation checklist may well be worked through during the implementation.
Smallest repair: delete the added clause — "är ett dokument att läsa igenom: https://example.invalid/svale/checklist".

**F4 — an unavailable comparison becomes a non-existent one.**
Draft: "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte." (line 13).
Changed support: "No comparison with another supplier is available." The source reports the state of the material; the draft reports the state of the world. The Claims section requires that "what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened".
Smallest repair: keep it about availability — "Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte tillgänglig."

**F5 — written answers are attributed as speech.**
Draft: "säger Maya Lind" (line 19), "säger hon" (lines 21 and 33).
Missing support: "All interviews occurred by email, and no physical scene, emotion or remembered dialogue is provided." Lind's answers were written, so the manner *säger* asserts is one the material has already ruled out. The Claims section makes manner a claim that needs support like any other. The speech dash itself stands: the resolved Swedish guidance sets reported speech with a dash, and email answers rendered readable fall under it.
Smallest repair: change the verb in the three tags — "skriver Maya Lind, …", "skriver hon", "skriver hon".

**F6 — the separate storing is placed at the maintenance team.**
Draft: "Hos underhållsteamet på Elm Quay Housing, ett bostadsföretag med 640 lägenheter, lagrades telefonanmälningar och mejl var för sig." (line 7).
Missing support: "Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately." The material states the state of the records, not who held them or where. Elm Quay's central service desk could have held both while the maintenance team held neither, and the team's shift-visibility problem would be unchanged.
Smallest repair: raise the locative to the company the material names — "Hos Elm Quay Housing, ett bostadsföretag med 640 lägenheter, lagrades telefonanmälningar och mejl var för sig."

## 6. Examined and not reported

Recorded so that the judgement is visible rather than silent. None of these is a finding.

- **"ett bostadsföretag med 640 lägenheter" (line 7)** against "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats." *Med* is looser than *förvaltar* and a company can have flats it does not manage. The deck four lines earlier states *förvaltar 640 lägenheter*, so the relation is fixed for the reader before the appositive compresses it, and the brief itself calls the audience "small housing companies". Defensible as written.
- **"orsaken är öppen" (line 23)** against "the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software." The heading denies that a cause has been established rather than proposing one, and the material establishes none: the differing workloads are supplied as a confound, not as the cause. The reading that would make this a defect — that it keeps the software in play as the reader's inference — is an editorial question about a supplier-published case, not a claim the draft makes, and the section beneath it carries the note's non-attribution in full. Defensible as written.
- **The paragraph break between the count and the median figures (lines 25–27).** "It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial" is the note's exclusion and so governs its figures; the draft's *räknas inte in* sits in the paragraph with the count of 31, which could leave a reader unsure whether the medians exclude emergencies too. This under-informs rather than misinforms, and the Claims section's requirement — give the count with its exclusions — is met. Defensible as written.
- **"Syftet var att …" (line 7)** against "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information." A want rendered as a purpose, immediately after the team's own decision, with no other party's aim in view.
- **"Teamet vill först se …" (line 31)** against "the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." A plan rendered as a wish, with the object of the checking and its position before the decision both preserved.
- **"drar i gång" (Q2)** against "starts". A register lift of about one step, reproducing the source's own ellipsis; a preferred synonym rather than a concrete obstruction to the reading.
- **Length.** The draft runs shorter than the brief's "approximately 400 words, as material allows". Under-length is what the Claims section prescribes where material is thin, and it is a matter for general editorial review rather than this comparison.
