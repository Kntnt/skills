- **Draft:** "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log and chose Svale Systems after testing whether it could show the status of each repair."

- **What differs:** time. In the material the date attaches to exactly one event, the decision to trial. In the draft the adverbial "In September 2025" governs both conjoined predicates, so it also dates the choice of supplier, and with it the testing that the choice followed. The material gives no date for either, and the sequence it does give (decision, then a test, then a choice) leaves the test and the choice free to fall later.
- **Both directions tested.** Source true, draft false: the team decides on 29 September 2025, runs its status test through October, and chooses Svale on 6 November 2025. Every supplied statement still holds — the decision is in September, the choice follows a test, the trial runs eight weeks and the note is dated 4 December 2025 — while the draft's dating of the choice is wrong. Draft true, source silent: if all three fell in September the draft holds, but nothing supplied asserts it, so the draft is strictly the stronger statement. No supplied statement excludes the case; the material dates only the decision, and "No comparison with another supplier is available" speaks to comparison, not to timing.
- **Smallest supported repair:** end the sentence after the decision and let the choice stand undated, as the material has it — "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log. It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."

### Finding 2 — the pronoun moves what was tested from the log to the supplier

- **Draft:** "…and chose Svale Systems after testing whether **it** could show the status of each repair."

- **What differs:** the thing tested. In the draft clause the nearest and most available antecedent for "it" is "Svale Systems", the noun phrase immediately before it; on that reading the team tested the supplier, or the supplier's system as a whole, rather than the log. The material says the log.

- **Smallest supported repair:** write the antecedent out — "after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair". This is the same edit the Finding 1 repair already makes, so one rewritten sentence closes both.
