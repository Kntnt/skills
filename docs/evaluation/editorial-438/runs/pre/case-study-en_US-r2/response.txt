# Elm Quay's supervisor finds one repair view helps

Elm Quay Housing's maintenance team tested a shared repair log in two buildings for eight weeks. Its trial note, its supervisor's assessment and the preparation it needed show operations managers at small housing companies what such a trial involves, and what it can and cannot prove.

By Thomas

At Elm Quay Housing, which manages 640 flats, repair reports that came in by telephone were stored separately from those that came in by email. In September 2025 the company's own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings. The trial produced an internal note with figures, and a decision the team has not yet made.

## Why Elm Quay's team wanted one log

Maya Lind, the maintenance supervisor, explains:

“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

The team chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair. This case does not compare Svale with other suppliers.

## Getting two buildings ready for the trial

The team kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale configured the log and trained six staff members in two sessions.

Looking back on that preparation, Lind says:

“We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

## The note records assignment times but does not credit the software

The trial lasted eight weeks. Elm Quay's internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered; the note excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial.

The median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period. The two periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. No measurements of cost, resident satisfaction or time to completion are available.

## Lind weighs the trial

In the email interview, Lind writes:

“I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

She did not recommend Svale to every housing company. The trial has not yet expanded beyond the two buildings. The team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs.
