## 2. F1: Fail (minor, but real)

Unsupported additions and implications:

- **Byline "Av Thomas".** The brief says "No author name supplied." The writer took the name from the machine's user name, and the reply says so. It is still an author attribution that the material does not carry. It is also a personal byline on material the source calls "supplier-published … not independent journalism" (see G2).
- **Standfirst "hur arbetsledaren Maya Lind ser på förberedelserna inför nästa hus".** In the narrator's voice this presupposes that there will be a next building. The source says "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs." Lind's own "before the next building starts" is conditional ("I would set that time aside"). The standfirst drops that condition. The draft's own last section says the question is still open ("Frågan om att vidga provet är ännu öppen"), so the reader gets a small contradiction.
- **Headline "Elm Quays arbetsledare skulle göra om provet".**
  - The source says "I would choose to do the trial again". The headline drops "choose".
  - In Swedish, "göra om provet" first reads as "redo / retake the test", which is what you do after failing. That moves her positive, qualified appraisal towards an implied failure.
  - The definite genitive "Elm Quays arbetsledare" can read as Elm Quay's only supervisor, or as its supervisors in the plural ("arbetsledare" has the same plural form). The source names only "maintenance supervisor Maya Lind".
  - The conditional "skulle" survives, so certainty is not raised.
- **Exclusion scope narrowed.** The source says "It [the note] excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial." The draft attaches "Akuta ärenden och arbeten som beställts före provet är inte medräknade" to the 31-report count only. A reader can take the median of 2 vs 3 working days to include emergencies.
- **Term check, "repair reports" → "felanmälningar" (minor).** In Swedish housing usage, "felanmälan" is a fault reported by a tenant. A staff-entered repair report falls under the source's term but not under the draft's. The reverse gap is negligible. Elsewhere the draft uses the neutral "anmälningar". I count this as a slight narrowing, not a failure on its own.
- **"Teamet lät samtidigt de boende fortsätta att anmäla fel per telefon."** "samtidigt" can read as "meanwhile/also". The source's "kept telephone reporting open for residents" is compatible with it. Not a defect.

- **Missing measurements.** "Mätningar av kostnader, de boendes nöjdhet eller tiden tills reparationerna var klara finns inte" states what does not exist, which is correct. It does not treat those measurements as unknown.
- **Assignment time vs completion time.** The draft never turns one into the other.

- **Facts carried correctly:** the date 4 December 2025, the eight weeks, the 640 flats, the two buildings, September 2025, six staff and two sessions.

- **Weakness.** Nothing tells the reader the piece is supplier-published. Journalistic form plus a personal byline ("Av Thomas") lets a reader who meets it away from Svale's own channel take it for independent reporting. The draft never claims independence, and the publisher's channel will usually make the stance plain, so I do not fail it.
- **Minor ordering point.** Under "Teamet valde Svale efter ett test av loggen", the quotation about shifts and categories comes before the sentence that explains the test. The reader meets the heading's claim, then an unrelated quotation, and only then the explanation.

**L1: Pass, marginal.**

- **What works.** The body reads as competent Swedish news prose:
  - dash-led quotations with "skriver Lind", as Swedish papers do;
  - natural verb-second word order in most sentences ("Hittills har provet bara gällt de två husen");
  - idiomatic "Någon jämförelse … finns inte att tillgå".
- **Blemishes:**
  - **Headline "göra om provet".** A literal rendering of "do the trial again". In Swedish, "prov" plus "göra om" calls up retaking a failed exam. "Pilot", "försöket" or "göra provet igen" would avoid it.
  - **"provanteckning".** A calque of "trial note"; it is not an established Swedish word.
  - **"Någon rekommendation av Svale till alla bostadsbolag ger Lind inte".** Contorted object-first order, and "av Svale" is momentarily ambiguous.
  - **"anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden programvaran".** Heavy and stiff, with the double object after the negation.
  - **"programvaran pekas inte ut".** "peka ut" leans towards "blame". It carries no agent, so it reads as though nobody links the software to the change.
- None of these stops comprehension. The headline is the costliest, because it is the most-read line.
