## 2. F1: fail (minor)

Most of the draft stays close to the source. It keeps the figures, the exclusions, the different-workloads caveat, the note's explicit refusal to credit the software, "assignment, not completion", and the missing cost, satisfaction and completion measures. It also keeps both of Lind's reservations and "did not recommend Svale to every housing company". It commits none of the forbidden inferences. Three items are defects or borderline:

1. **Invented author attribution: "By Thomas".** The brief says "No author name supplied", and the material names no author. The byline puts a named person on a text the material describes as supplier-published. That is an attribution from outside the material, and it affects author perspective, because a personal byline on a journalistic case suggests an independent writer. The reply discloses it, but it is still in the text.
2. **Changed scope in a subheading: "Expansion waits on how the categories hold up".** The source says the team "will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs". The subheading drops "for larger repairs", so a narrow check becomes a general test of whether the categories "hold up". "Waits on" also suggests the categories are the deciding factor. The source makes them only the thing the team checks before it decides. The body sentence below it is correct, so the damage is limited to the heading.
3. **Borderline: "The note does not tie the faster assignment to the software".** The source supplies a comparison: a median of two against three working days, over a restricted set of reports. "Faster assignment" states that difference as a general property of assignment. That is a mild overreach, but the section immediately gives the median, the exclusions and the workload caveat. I record it and do not count it as a separate fail.

- "The eight-week trial has ended" is supported: the trial "lasted eight weeks", and the note is dated after it.

Unknown is kept apart from absent throughout ("has yet to decide", "has not yet made its decision").
