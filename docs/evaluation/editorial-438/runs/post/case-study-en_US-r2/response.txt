# Elm Quay tests one repair log across shifts

A housing company's maintenance team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same repair information. Here is how Elm Quay Housing ran an eight-week trial of software from Svale Systems, what its own trial figures can and cannot show, and how its maintenance supervisor judges the result.

By Thomas

Elm Quay Housing manages 640 apartments, and its telephone repair reports and emails used to be stored separately. In September 2025 the company's own maintenance team decided to test a shared repair log in two buildings. The eight-week trial has ended, and the team has yet to decide whether to expand it.

## Elm Quay tested the log before choosing Svale

Maya Lind, Elm Quay's maintenance supervisor, answered questions about the trial by email. “We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done,” Lind wrote. “The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

Before choosing Svale Systems, the team tested whether the log could show the status of each repair. Svale configured the log and trained six staff members in two sessions, while the team kept telephone reporting open for residents.

## Expansion waits on how the categories hold up

Lind also wrote about the preparation: “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

The team will decide whether to expand the trial after checking how the categories work for larger repairs.

## The note does not tie the faster assignment to the software

An internal trial note from Elm Quay, dated December 4, 2025, says 31 repair reports were entered. The note excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial. Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period.

The two periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. The figure covers time to assignment, not to completion. Completion time, cost and resident satisfaction were not measured.

## Lind weighs the trial as a whole

Lind's overall assessment is not drawn from those figures. “I would choose to do the trial again,” Lind wrote. “Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

Lind did not recommend Svale to every housing company.

## Other teams can begin with a checklist

Elm Quay has not yet made its decision on expansion.
