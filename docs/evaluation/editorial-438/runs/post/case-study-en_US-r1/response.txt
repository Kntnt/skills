# Elm Quay's maintenance shifts trial a shared log

For eight weeks in two of its buildings, Elm Quay Housing entered repair reports into a log from Svale Systems. This account follows how the maintenance team set it up, what its internal figures do and do not show, and what its supervisor would do differently.

By Thomas

Telephone repair reports and emails used to be stored separately at Elm Quay Housing, which manages 640 flats. In September 2025 its maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings. An internal trial note and the maintenance supervisor's own assessment record how it went.

## Why Elm Quay chose a shared log

Before settling on Svale Systems as its supplier, the team tested whether the log could show the status of each repair.

Maya Lind, the maintenance supervisor, was interviewed by email. "We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done," she says. "The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log."

## Six staff learn the log in two sessions

Residents could still report repairs by telephone. Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions, and the trial ran for eight weeks.

"We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports," Lind says of the preparation. "I would set that time aside before the next building starts."

## The trial note does not credit the software

Elm Quay's internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, counts 31 repair reports entered, excluding emergencies and work ordered before the trial began. Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, against three in the preceding eight weeks.

The two periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. Costs, resident satisfaction and completion times were not measured.

## Lind looks back on the eight weeks

"I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation," Lind says.

She stops short of recommending Svale to every housing company. The trial has not yet been extended beyond the two buildings; the team will decide after checking how its categories work for larger repairs.

## Read the checklist before a trial of your own

The eight weeks left Elm Quay with figures its own note does not credit to the software and a qualified assessment from its supervisor.
