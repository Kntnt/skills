## 4. L1: fail (narrow)

Most of the prose reads as professional Swedish: "Elm Quay Housing förvaltar 640 lägenheter", "Leverantören konfigurerade loggen och höll två utbildningstillfällen för sex medarbetare". The dash-led quotations follow Swedish press convention. Several constructions are imported from English, though:

- **"anteckningen tillskriver uttryckligen inte skillnaden till programvaran":** "tillskriva … till" is a calque of "attribute to". Idiomatic Swedish is "tillskriver inte programvaran skillnaden". The draft's own subheading uses the correct form ("tillskriver inte loggen den kortare tiden"), so the text is also inconsistent with itself.
- **"Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus startar":** a literal rendering of "before the next building starts". In Swedish, a house that "startar" suggests building works starting. A reader stumbles or misreads.
- **"Någon rekommendation av Svale till alla bostadsbolag ger hon inte":** "rekommendation av Svale" is an English-shaped noun phrase, and in Swedish "av Svale" can be read as a recommendation made *by* Svale. "Hon rekommenderar inte Svale till alla bostadsbolag" would be native.
- **Smaller points:**
  - "Innan teamet valde Svale testade det om …": "det" for "teamet" is clumsy.
  - "Hennes omdöme är inte en slutsats ur siffrorna utan hennes egen bedömning": "slutsats ur" and the omdöme/bedömning pair read as translated.
- **Excluded as mechanics:** the comma splice in quotation 1 ("Kategorierna var våra, Svale hjälpte oss").

Reader effect: a Swedish operations manager gets the facts, but at three points the text sounds translated, and one of those points invites a wrong reading.
