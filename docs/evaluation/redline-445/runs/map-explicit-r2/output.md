# Visit the repair desk

Bring your broken lamp to the Oak Room on Saturday, October 10, between 10 am and noon. Fen Repair volunteers will help you assess whether it can be repaired. Bring its cable too.

The desk offers an assessment, not a promise of repair. Book a slot by emailing desk@example.org.
