# Article — review guidance

Read the opening as a sequence: does the lead begin explaining or reporting after the standfirst has introduced the piece, or does the text start twice? Where a part of the opening is there but does not do its work, repair it from existing content, without treating necessary orientation as waste; where a part is missing, report it and leave it unwritten.

Follow the promised angle through the sections and ending. Topic accumulation, a concept explained after the reasoning needs it, or a sales close unrelated to the explanation gives the reader a specific problem; a different successful opening does not. Keep relevant technical substance when improving navigation.

Apply the full form to a complete article, not an explicitly requested excerpt. Report a required part the text does not have rather than writing it, even where the text's own content could supply it.
