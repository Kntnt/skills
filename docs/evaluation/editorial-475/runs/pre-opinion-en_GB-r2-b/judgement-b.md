That narrowing is small and consistent with the text's own argument.
4. Ending. Before: "It can adopt the September removal". After: "The municipal executive board can adopt the September removal". Class: repair of a visible defect. The pronoun's only antecedent was the subheading. The actor is the same board, so attribution does not change.

No difference is consequent on another.
