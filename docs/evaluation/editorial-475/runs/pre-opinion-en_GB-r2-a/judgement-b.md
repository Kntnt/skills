# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter, headings, formatting and every other paragraph are identical. There are two differences.

1. Byline. Before: `*Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*`. After: `*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*`. Class: **change of taste**. A byline that gives a name and a role without "By" is not a visible defect in British English, and adding "By" is not a spelling, punctuation or grammar correction. The attribution stays the same: the same author, with the same role. No claim changes.
2. First sentence of the last section. Before: `It can adopt the September removal on the material in front of it.` After: `The board can adopt the September removal on the material in front of it.` Class: **repair of a visible defect**. The pronoun's only antecedent is in the subheading ("What the board can decide on 18 June"). The body sentence just before it ends on "the people who use it", so a reader of the body alone meets a subject with no referent. Naming the actor the heading already names does not move the claim to a different actor, so this is not a change of attribution. The following "Or it can postpone…" now refers back to "The board". That is consequent on this change and needs no edit of its own.

Changes to what a claim says: **0**.
