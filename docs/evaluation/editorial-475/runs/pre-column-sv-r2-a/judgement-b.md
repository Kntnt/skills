# Judgement b

Compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks and all body sentences except one dash are identical. There are two differences.

## 1. Differences

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen har ingen ruta för varför vi ses`. Class: **a change of taste** that is also **a change to what a claim says, by meaning** (and marginally by scope). The old headline withheld which box was missing; the text resolved that in its first paragraph ("Någon ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta … finns inte"). The new headline names the missing box as one "för varför vi ses", which merges paragraph 1's decision box with the "varför" of paragraphs 2 and 4. It also drops "bibliotekets", so on its own the headline reads as a claim about meeting templates in general rather than one library's template. The body supports the new wording, since the template lists no purpose field, and it fits the author's later limit that not every conversation must end in a decision. So it is not hardening. But it is not the repair of a visible defect. The old headline was a working, deliberately withholding column title in the author's voice, and it was changed to meet an anatomy guideline ("names no subject", "inom anatomins gränser").
2. **Dash, paragraph 3.** Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: **mechanical correction** (a Swedish locale form: the spaced en dash is the Swedish tankstreck). It depends on no other change.

No other differences, including in the frontmatter and formatting.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the headline change: `# Rutan som inte finns` → `# Mötesmallen har ingen ruta för varför vi ses`, justified in finding 1 by "nämnde inget ämne" and "Rubriken ligger inom anatomins gränser". Apart from its form, this column is clean: one dash is its only mechanical flaw. Its title is a working part of its voice, and paragraph 1 resolves it at once. Replacing it to satisfy an anatomy guideline is the rewrite of a clean text to satisfy taste or guidelines, which R1 forbids. It also changes what the headline says by meaning, and slightly by scope ("bibliotekets" dropped). The rest of the run preserves arguments, quotations, claims and every limiting sentence. It also reports its change honestly and leaves the missing standfirst and sections unwritten rather than inventing them. The dash fix is legitimate. The single unwarranted edit falls on the most visible line of the text, and R1 does not allow it.
