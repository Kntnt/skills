## 1. Differences

The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`, lines 4–25. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and every body sentence except one are identical to `work/input.md`. There are two differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen har ingen ruta för varför vi ses`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**, with a secondary change of **scope**.
   - Meaning: the input's headline is a figurative image ("the box that does not exist"). The new headline is a literal thesis. It names the missing box as one for *why we meet*, while the body's only explicit statement of what is missing names a box for *det beslut vi ska kunna fatta*. The body does support the new reading indirectly ("Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om"; the agenda "säger inte varför vi behöver göra det tillsammans"; "formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), so the new claim is not invented. It does, however, pick one of the text's two framings and state it as the headline's claim.
   - Scope: "bibliotekets mötesmall" becomes "Mötesmallen". The body still says "bibliotekets" in its first sentence, so the change is slight.
   - The change also replaces the author's allusive column title with a descriptive one, which is also a change of taste.
   - It is not consequent on another difference.
2. **Paragraph 3.** Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. The spaced em dash becomes a spaced en dash, the Swedish form. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. It is not consequent on another difference.

The fenced wrapper around the returned text is packaging, not a change to the text.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the headline change together with the Skill's reason for it.

"Rutan som inte finns" is not a concrete visible defect. It is a working allusive column title in the author's voice, and the whole piece turns on the box that does not exist. The Skill replaced it on a genre-anatomy ground: a reader "i en lista eller ett sökresultat" cannot see the subject, and the new headline "ligger inom anatomins gränser". Under R1, that is rewriting a clean text to satisfy a guideline. The replacement also changes what the headline claims, by meaning (the missing box becomes a box for "varför" rather than for "beslut") and slightly by scope ("bibliotekets" is dropped).

The rest of the run meets R1 well:
- The only other edit is a legitimate locale dash.
- Arguments, quotations and every limiting sentence are untouched.
- The missing standfirst and missing sections are reported as irreparable within a review and are left unwritten rather than invented.
- The account is complete and accurate.

The failure rests on that single, well-reported change of voice.
