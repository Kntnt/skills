What decides it is R1's clause "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste." This is a control that conforms to the anatomy, yet the run rewrote two sentences for reference preferences rather than for concrete visible defects:

- "Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus." became "Loggen prövades i ett försök som pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus." The original sentence refers to the trial that the headline and standfirst have just introduced, and the reader loses nothing through it.

Both edits are small and faithful to the text's own facts, so this failure is the narrow kind: the run rewrote a clean text for taste, and the harm stops there.
