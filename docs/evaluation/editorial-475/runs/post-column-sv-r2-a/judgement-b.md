I compared the text inside the reply's fenced block with `work/input.md` line by line. The frontmatter, the byline and five of the six body paragraphs are identical. The reply puts the returned text inside a ```` ```markdown ```` fence. That is packaging in the reply, not a difference in the text. There are two differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar en ruta för varför vi ses`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**, with a secondary shift in **certainty**.
   - Meaning: the old headline names a box without saying which one, and the text answers it twice. One answer is the box the template lacks (paragraph 1).    - Certainty: "saknar" together with a named purpose invites the reader to conclude that the template ought to have this box. The body leaves that question open.
   - This is not a mechanical correction. Whether it repairs a visible defect is assessed under section 4.
2. **Paragraph 3, dash.** Before: `något annat — att`. After: `något annat – att`. Class: **mechanical correction** (locale form). Swedish typography uses a spaced en dash here, not an em dash.

No difference follows from another.
