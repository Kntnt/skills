# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` with the text inside the reply's fenced block, line by line. Frontmatter, byline, paragraphing and every body sentence except the one in D2 are identical. There are two differences.

- **D1. Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar en ruta för varför vi ses`. Class: **a change of taste that is also a change to what a claim says, by meaning.** The input has no visible defect here. A column may use a figurative headline, and this one echoes the closing doubt ("tvivlet på ännu en ruta"). The new headline makes a claim the body does not make in those words. The body says the missing box is one "för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta" and that the template does not ask "Vad tiden ska leda till". The headline says it is a box "för varför vi ses", which merges the observation with the author's proposal ("borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid"). There is a secondary shift in **certainty**. As a declarative headline, "saknar" presents the lack as a finding that ought to be fixed, but the body leaves open whether a further box would help ("Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre"). The input's headline allowed both readings. The new one settles on one. The headline's voice also changes, from a figurative line to a label.
- **D2. Dash, paragraph 3.** Before: `något annat — att`. After: `något annat – att`. Class: **mechanical correction** (locale form: Swedish uses a spaced en dash, not a spaced em dash).

Neither difference follows from the other.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** D1 decides it. The input is a clean column with no concrete visible defect in its headline. "Rutan som inte finns" is a deliberate figurative headline, and it leaves the question open in the same way the closing line does ("Hoppet om frågan och tvivlet på ännu en ruta får samsas ett tag till"). The Skill's reason for replacing it is how the headline would work in a list or search result: "Den som bara ser rubriken, till exempel i en lista eller ett sökresultat, kunde inte förstå att texten handlar om en mötesmall". That is a guideline about how headlines should be packaged, not a defect in the text. Following it rewrote a clean text's most prominent line to suit taste. The rewrite also changed what that line says: the missing box went from the decision to "varför vi ses", and a doubt the text holds open was settled. The Skill's own reply concedes both points ("Den dubbla betydelsen är borta"; "Brödtexten lämnar dock frågan öppen").

Everything else meets R1. The dash fix is legitimate. All body claims, quotations and limiting sentences survive verbatim. The Skill wrote nothing in answer to the other three "missing parts" findings. The account is complete and accurate.
