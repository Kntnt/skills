# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. The frontmatter (there is none), the headline, the standfirst, the byline, every paragraph and all formatting are the same byte for byte.

- **Before:** the paragraph "Frågan jag vill lägga till är enkel: … lösningen på ett formulär är ett längre formulär." was followed directly by the paragraph "Prova ändå frågan nästa gång du bokar ett möte, …", with both under "## Ännu en ruta, och ändå vill jag prova".
  **After:** a new subheading, "## Om mallen blir bättre vet vi inte förrän vi har provat", with a blank line after it, now stands between those two paragraphs. The final section has become two sections.
  **Class:** a change of taste presented as the repair of a structural defect. It also changes what claims say:
  - **Certainty and meaning:** the new heading adds a claim that the text did not make, "vi vet inte" whether the template gets better until it has been tried. The text only said what the writer "kan lova".
  - **Meaning and causality:** "ändå" in the invitation no longer follows the objection "lösningen på ett formulär är ett längre formulär" directly. It can now be read as "trots att vi inte vet", and the heading gives a new reason to try the question: that a trial will settle whether the template improves.
  - **Scope:** the heading "Ännu en ruta, och ändå vill jag prova" now covers only the proposal paragraph, so its "vill jag prova" no longer sits over the paragraph where the reader is asked to try.

  The new subheading is 54 characters long.

## 2. The account

The reply reports the difference as Finding 1: "Slutstycket står nu oförändrat under en ny mellanrubrik, 'Om mallen blir bättre vet vi inte förrän vi har provat'."

The report is accurate and complete about the claim effects:
- It lists the new heading's claim under *Tillagt*, and it separates that claim from the writer's "kan lova".
- It lists the shift in the referent of "ändå", and the new reason to try, under *Ändrat*.
- It reports that the old heading no longer stands over the invitation.
- It states that no claim was removed.

What is not accurate is the premise. The reply presents the change as the repair of a defect ("Artikelanatomin kräver att slutet är ett eget avsnitt med en egen mellanrubrik"), but the standard says the text already conforms to the anatomy (see 3). The reply also says the final proofreading pass changed nothing, and the diff agrees: there is no mechanical difference.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The text is clean: by the standard, it conforms to the anatomy. R1 says a clean text may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or a guideline. This run inserted "## Om mallen blir bättre vet vi inte förrän vi har provat" to repair a defect the text does not have.

That insertion is not neutral. It adds a claim of uncertainty that the writer never made. It also changes what "ändå" answers in the ending's invitation: before, "Prova ändå frågan …" answered "lösningen på ett formulär är ett längre formulär" directly, and now it follows the new heading.

The reply reports these claim changes honestly. Honest reporting does not make the change legitimate. It is a change to what claims say, outside any valid finding, in a text that needed none.
