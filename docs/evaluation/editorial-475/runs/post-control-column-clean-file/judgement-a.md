# Judgement a

Files read: `work/input.md`, `work/output.md`, `response.md`.

## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. The frontmatter (there is none), the headline, the standfirst, the byline, the opening, both original subheadings and every paragraph are unchanged, word for word.

- **Before:** the last section, "## Ännu en ruta, och ändå vill jag prova", holds two paragraphs: the proposal paragraph ("Frågan jag vill lägga till är enkel: … lösningen på ett formulär är ett längre formulär.") and then the closing paragraph ("Prova ändå frågan nästa gång du bokar ett möte … innan vi har provat.").
  **After:** a new subheading, "## Om mallen blir bättre vet vi inte förrän vi har provat", comes between those two paragraphs. The closing paragraph now forms a third section of its own.
  **Class:** a change to what a claim says, **meaning**. It is also, at root, a change of structure that the text did not need (see 3 and 4). The new subheading asserts something the text did not say: that "we" do not know whether the template gets better until the question has been tried. The nearest source is the closing sentence, "Det är den enda förbättring jag kan lova innan vi har provat". That sentence is about what the writer can promise, not about what anyone knows, so the change also moves the **attribution** from "jag" to "vi". The insertion also changes what the closing paragraph's "ändå" points back to. Before, "Prova ändå" followed straight after the admitted objection "lösningen på ett formulär är ett längre formulär", in the same section. Now a heading stands between them, so "ändå" can be read as "even though we do not know".

The run made no mechanical corrections.

- It also lists the changed reference of "ändå" (*Ändrat:* … "”ändå” kan läsas som ”trots att vi inte vet”"). It notes that the heading adds a reason to try that the text did not have, and that the old subheading no longer stands over the invitation.

The account is honest and complete. What is wrong is the finding behind the change, not the reporting of it.

Per the standard this is a false finding, and the run changed the text because of it.

The reply also contradicts itself. It ends by saying "Texten följer artikelanatomin", yet that is true only of the text it delivered. The same reply also lists a paragraph-count deviation that its own repair created: "Förslagsavsnittet och slutet består nu av ett stycke var."

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the inserted heading "## Om mallen blir bättre vet vi inte förrän vi har provat", made on the finding "Artikelanatomin kräver att slutet är ett eget avsnitt med en egen mellanrubrik".

The standard says this text conforms to the anatomy, so it is a clean text. R1 says clean texts may not be rewritten. The run nonetheless:

- restructured the text on a false finding;
- added a claim the text did not make, shifting the attribution from what "jag kan lova" to what "vi vet";
- altered what "Prova ändå" answers.

The reply reports all of this accurately, and the run avoided every named rejection. Neither fact rescues it: the change itself is the failure.
