# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the text in the fenced block of `response.md`. There are two differences. The frontmatter, byline, paragraphing and every body sentence are otherwise identical.

1. Headline. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen har ingen ruta för varför vi ses`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**. The old headline said only that some box is absent. The new one names the template and says which box is absent: one for why we meet. The body supports this. It says "Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om" and "borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid". One nuance: the box the body first names as missing is for "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta". The headline swaps that for "varför vi ses", which matches the author's later move away from decisions ("Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut"). So the change moves the headline toward the text's thesis. It does not add a new claim. It is also partly **a change of taste**: a figurative column headline is replaced by a descriptive, declarative one. Whether the old headline was a visible defect is discussed under R1.
2. Paragraph 3. Before: `något annat — att tid` (spaced em dash). After: `något annat – att tid` (spaced en dash). Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. A spaced en dash is the Swedish convention for a parenthetical dash. This change does not follow from any other.

The spaces were already in the input, so "med mellanslag" describes the result, not an added change. The reply's claim that only this one proofreading fix was made in the body is true.

The reply also says "Utöver rubriken har inget påstående strukits, ändrats eller lagts till." That is true. Findings 2 and 3 (no standfirst, no subheadings) are reported as unresolved. No prose was written for them, and the returned text matches that.

## 4. R1

**Pass, narrowly.** The deciding passage is the headline, the only change of substance. The old headline, "Rutan som inte finns", does not name its subject when read alone. The reply's reason ("Den som bara såg rubriken, i en lista eller ett sökresultat, kunde inte avgöra att texten handlar om en mötesmall") can be checked from the input alone. The replacement reuses the text's own words ("ruta", "ses"). It claims no more than the body does, and the reply states the change of meaning openly. Beyond the headline, the voice, arguments and claims are untouched, and every limiting sentence survives verbatim. The one other edit is a correct Swedish dash form. The two anatomy findings the Skill could not fix without inventing prose (standfirst, subheadings) were left unresolved and reported as such. It did not satisfy the numerical guideline by writing new text. The reservation: in a column, an allusive headline is a legitimate part of the working voice. Replacing it comes close to a change of taste, so this is a pass on a defensible repair, not a clear one.
