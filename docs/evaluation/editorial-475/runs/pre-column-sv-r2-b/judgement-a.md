# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

The returned text (the fenced block in `response.md`) differs from `work/input.md` in two places. The frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks and all other sentences are identical.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen har ingen ruta för varför vi ses`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning (and scope)**. It is also a **change of taste**. The input headline is a figurative column title: it says that a box does not exist and leaves the reader to find out which one. The new headline names the missing box as one "för varför vi ses". The body names the missing box differently: "Någon ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån finns inte". It reaches "varför" only through the agenda ("Den säger inte varför vi behöver göra det tillsammans") and the author's wish ("borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid"). The new headline is a fair synthesis of the argument and can be supported from the text. Even so, it replaces a figure in the author's voice with a descriptive label and states the missing box more narrowly and literally than any one sentence in the body does. A column title that teases is normal for the genre and is not a visible defect.
2. **Paragraph 3, dash.** Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. Swedish uses a spaced en dash as tankstreck. The spaces were already there in the input.

Neither difference depends on the other.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the headline: `# Rutan som inte finns` → `# Mötesmallen har ingen ruta för varför vi ses`.

The body is a clean column. Apart from the dash, the Skill correctly left it alone and kept every argument, quotation, hedge and limit. The headline, though, is not a concrete visible defect. It is a deliberate figurative title in the author's working voice, typical of the column genre. The Skill replaced it for findability ("Den som bara såg rubriken, i en lista eller ett sökresultat, kunde inte avgöra …"), which is a guideline-driven change of taste. The new title also specifies the missing box as "varför vi ses", while the body's first-named missing box is a *beslut* box, so what the headline says changed in meaning. The criterion forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste or guidelines, and asks that working voice be preserved. The headline is the most prominent place in the piece where voice was lost.

The reply is transparent: it reports the change and labels it a changed claim. That transparency does not make the change legitimate. The dash correction and the two findings left unresolved (ingress, subheadings), which the Skill correctly declined to write, count in its favour but do not outweigh the headline.
