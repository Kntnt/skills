# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter, the headline, all five subheadings and every paragraph after the lead are identical. There are three differences. The first and third are halves of one move.

1. **Byline inserted under the headline.**
   Before: (nothing between the headline and the lead)
   After: `By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut` as its own paragraph, followed by a blank line, directly under the H1.
   Class: **change of taste** (placement and form of the author line). The Skill presents it as the repair of a visible defect: the reader meets "I" and "Öppna beslut proposes…" before learning who is writing. That reading is defensible but weak. A closing sign-off is a standard form for an opinion piece, and "Öppna beslut proposes" already names the proposer in the sentence before "We make no claim". No claim changes. The author, the role and the organisation are the same words, so the attribution of the text and of every claim in it is unchanged. The dash became "By", which is formatting that follows from the move.

2. **Lead names the council.**
   Before: `On 18 June the council executive board is asked to remove phone booking…`
   After: `On 18 June Lervik's council executive board is asked to remove phone booking…`
   Class: **change to what a claim says: meaning**. The board's identity, which the input left implicit, is now stated. The headline ("Lervik's phone booking") and "Lervik's residents" in the second section already entail it, so the returned text asserts nothing the input did not already carry. The Skill presents this as the repair of a visible defect ("Lead relied on the headline"). I do not find a defect a reader would stumble on, because the lead sits directly under a headline that names Lervik. It is closer to taste, but because it adds an explicit assertion I class it by meaning.

3. **Closing sign-off removed.**
   Before: a blank line followed by `— Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut` at the end of the text.
   After: (absent)
   Class: **consequent on difference 1**. It is the other half of the byline move, a change of taste.

Total: 3 differences. 1 is a change to what a claim says (meaning), and 0 are mechanical corrections.
