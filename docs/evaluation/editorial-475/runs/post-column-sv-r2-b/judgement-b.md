# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline, the paragraph breaks and all Markdown formatting are identical. There are two differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Rutan som inte finns i mötesmallen`. Class: **change to what a claim says, by scope and by meaning.** It is also a change of taste; see below.
   - *Scope:* the headline now places the missing box "i mötesmallen", in the definite form without "bibliotekets". Read on its own, the headline makes a claim about meeting templates in general. The body limits itself to one document, the library's template: "Det här är en iakttagelse av ett dokument".
   - *Meaning:* the input headline could be read two ways. It could mean the box is missing from the template, or that the box does not yet exist anywhere, which is the question the writer proposes in the last paragraph and doubts. The new headline keeps only the first reading.
   - I can see no concrete defect in the original headline. In a column, a headline that evokes rather than labels is a normal choice, so the edit serves a guideline ("a standalone headline that names its subject"). It does not repair a visible fault.
2. **Dash in paragraph 3.** Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form).** The spaced em dash becomes the spaced en dash that Swedish uses. The spacing was already there in the input.

Neither difference follows from the other.

Total: 2 differences. 1 is a change to what a claim says (scope, meaning).

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The input is a clean column. It has no spelling, grammar or factual defect that the text itself exposes, apart from the dash, which the Skill corrected legitimately. The Skill still rewrote the headline, the one element of the text that carried the writer's wordplay. It did this to satisfy a guideline, not to repair a visible defect: "En läsare som bara ser rubriken, i en lista eller ett sökresultat, kan inte avgöra att texten handlar om en mötesmall."

Its own reply shows that the edit costs claims outside any real defect:

- "Bredare än texten: … En läsare som bara ser rubriken kan därför ta den som ett påstående om mötesmallar i allmänhet. Brödtexten gäller bara bibliotekets mötesmall och kallar sig uttryckligen en iakttagelse av ett dokument."
- "läsningen ”finns inte alls” är borta."

So the edit widens scope past the text's own limit, and it removes a meaning the writer's voice carried. Both are losses R1 should reject. "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines" decides it.

The Skill did well elsewhere. It left findings 2–4 unresolved rather than inventing a standfirst, subheads or a call to action, it kept every limiting sentence, and it reported everything accurately. None of that repairs the headline edit.
