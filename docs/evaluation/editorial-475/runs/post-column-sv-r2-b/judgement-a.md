# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

A line-by-line diff of `work/input.md` against the text in `response.md` finds two differences. The frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks and all other formatting are identical.

1. Headline. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Rutan som inte finns i mötesmallen`. Class: **a change to what a claim says**. It changes **scope**, and with it **meaning**. The original headline does not say where the box is missing, so it covers two things: the box missing from the library's template, and the question the writer proposes in the last paragraph, which does not exist anywhere yet. The new headline ties it to "mötesmallen". That drops the second reading (meaning). Read on its own, the bare definite form, without "bibliotekets", can also be taken as a claim about meeting templates in general (scope). The body limits itself to one library's template and calls itself "en iakttagelse av ett dokument". The Skill presents this as the repair of a visible defect: a headline that names no subject. The input has no such defect. A column headline that teases a subject the first paragraph resolves works in this text. The change is driven by a genre rule (a headline that stands on its own in a listing), which makes it at most **a change of taste**, and the change also alters the claim.
2. Paragraph 3. Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid` (em dash, spaced). After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid` (en dash, spaced). Class: **a mechanical correction**, in the locale form. Swedish uses a spaced en dash as its tankstreck. It does not follow from difference 1.

No change to the frontmatter or formatting.

The closing statement "Frontmatter och all övrig text är oförändrade" is true. So is "Inga påståenden har tagits bort eller lagts till, och ingen avgränsning har strukits": no body claim was removed or added, and no limiting sentence was struck.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The input is a clean text. The only visible defect it has is the Swedish dash form, and the Skill corrected that properly. The Skill then used its single correction budget on the headline, a working element of the column. It gave a genre-anatomy reason ("En läsare som bara ser rubriken, i en lista eller ett sökresultat, kan inte avgöra att texten handlar om en mötesmall"). That is not a concrete defect visible in the text. The rewrite changes what the headline claims, in the Skill's own words: "läsningen ”finns inte alls” är borta" and "kan därför ta den som ett påstående om mötesmallar i allmänhet". R1 says: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines". It also asks that claims be preserved. The headline change breaks both, even though it is reported openly.

Findings 2 to 4 (lead, subheadings, call to action) were left unresolved and not written in. That restraint keeps the voice, arguments and every limiting sentence intact, and it is to the Skill's credit. It does not rescue the one change the Skill did make.
