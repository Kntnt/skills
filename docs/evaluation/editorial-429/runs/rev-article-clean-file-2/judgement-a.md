# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. The files have no frontmatter. Every other line, including the headline, the byline, the subheadings, the quotation, the bold standfirst markup and the italic report title, is byte-identical.

- Standfirst, third sentence. Before: "Här är vad ett kort försök kan säga, och vad som behövs för att komma vidare." After: "Här är vad mätningarna kan säga, och vad som behövs för att komma vidare." Class: a change to what a claim says, in its **meaning**. The author's description of the trial as short (*kort*) is gone. It is not the repair of a visible defect. Calling a four-week trial short is the author's own judgement. It fits the headline ("visar när, inte varför") and the ending, which points to the next trial. Nothing in the text contradicts it. The change is not mechanical.

## 2. The account

The reply reports the difference, as finding 1 and again under **Påståenden** → **Ändrat**. It quotes the before and after text accurately, and it correctly says that the sentence no longer calls the trial short. The reply also says nothing else changed ("Utöver ändringen i ingressen har inget ändrats"). That is accurate.

The account gives a flawed reason for the change. The reply says "Brödtexten lägger begränsningen någon annanstans, i vad givarna mätte och inte mätte". The body never says whether the trial's length limits what it can show. It lists other limits, but it does not rule out length, so the two do not conflict. The reply also says "Texten ger inget stöd för att fyra veckors mätningar är ett *kort* försök", which treats the author's own judgement as a claim that needs outside support. The report of the change is accurate. The reason for making it is not.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the standfirst edit: "Här är vad ett kort försök kan säga" → "Här är vad mätningarna kan säga". This is a control text that conforms to the anatomy. The Skill changed one of the author's claims in the standfirst because of a finding that points to no concrete visible defect. It treats a judgement the text is entitled to make as an unsupported claim. It also rests on a conflict with the body that does not exist. R1 says clean texts may not be rewritten for taste, and that working voice and claims must be kept. This change removes a meaning the author chose. The Skill reported the change fully and accurately, kept everything else, and avoided every rejection the expectation lists. That does not make the edit legitimate. The failure is narrow: one word, one sentence.
