## 1. Differences

1. **Headline.** Before: "Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena". After: "Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta i gemensam reparationslogg". Class: change to what a claim says: **meaning** (a statement of what the organisation did becomes a statement of what the manager thinks), with a shift of **attribution** to the manager.
2. **Standfirst, third sentence.** Before: "Här är vad gruppen gjorde, vad anteckningarna visar och vad hon skulle ändra." After: "Här är vad Elm Quays underhållsgrupp gjorde, vad gruppens anteckningar från försöket visar och vad hon skulle ändra." Class: repair of a visible defect (the standfirst names no group before "gruppen"). The added attribution of the notes to the group matches the body's "gruppens egna anteckningar", so no new claim.
3. **Opening, first sentence.** Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." After: "Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna i sin gemensamma reparationslogg." Class: repair of a visible defect (the pseudo-cleft "var vad" is a recognised anglicism in Swedish), with a small **meaning** shift: what the group wanted to *get out of* the log becomes what it wanted to *be able to see in* the log.
4. **Opening, second sentence.** Before: "Försöket pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus." After: "Loggen prövades i ett försök som pågick i åtta veckor och omfattade två hus." Class: repair of a visible defect (definite "Försöket" with no antecedent in the body). Defensible, though minor.
5. **Opening, third sentence.** Before: "Vad det krävde, och vad det gav, går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar." After: "Vad det krävde går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar." Class: change to what a claim says: **scope** and **meaning** (the claim that the notes show what the trial yielded is removed).
6. **Second subheading.** Before: "Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning". After: "Väntan på tilldelning var i median en arbetsdag kortare än tidigare". Class: change to what a claim says: **meaning**. The heading layer stops carrying the workload caveat and instead states the favourable difference. The new statement is true to the section's numbers (two against three working days) and asserts no cause. But a reader who skims now gets the result without the caveat.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The rejections named in the expectation were all avoided, and every preservation it names holds. But R1 also says "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste", and it asks for claims to be preserved except where a concrete visible defect justifies the change. The deciding passage is difference 5, together with finding 4. "Vad det krävde, och vad det gav, går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar." lost "och vad det gav" on the ground that the note does not attribute the difference to the software. The text never claimed that it did: the notes do record what the trial yielded, and the text's own caveat already qualified it. That is a rejected loss of a claim, not a defect repair. Difference 6 compounds it: the only heading that carried the workload caveat now states the favourable result instead. The reply admits that skimmers no longer see the caveat. Difference 1 replaces a working headline for a slot preference. Differences 2–4 are defensible small repairs and would not on their own decide a fail.
