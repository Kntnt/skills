Class: a change of taste that changes what a claim says, in **attribution** and **meaning**. The run avoided every named rejection and kept the body intact. But this is a clean control, and the run rewrote two headings on grounds of taste, each of which changed what a claim says. The deciding passage is the Skill's own record of the second rewrite:

> "Mellanrubriken påstår nu allmänt att beslut inte är det enda skälet att träffas. Tidigare uttryckte den skribentens hållning, att hen är ute efter mer än bara besluten."

It swaps the column's deliberate hyperbole, "en ruta för allt utom varför", for a narrower literal claim, which is a change of scope made for taste. R1 forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste, and the text needed no correction at all.
