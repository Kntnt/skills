# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

Neither file has frontmatter. The reply puts the returned text inside a Markdown code fence. That is how the reply is packaged, and the text itself is not changed by it. Headings, bold standfirst, byline, quotation dashes, the link and the paragraph breaks are all unchanged. There are three differences:

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena`. After: `# Elm Quay samlade reparationsärenden på försök`. Class: a change to what a claim says, in **scope**. The definite plural (all "the" repair cases, by the organisation) is narrowed to repair cases gathered on a trial. The narrowing matches the body's own limits: eight weeks, two houses, urgent and pre-ordered work excluded. It fixes a mild overclaim, so it counts as the repair of a visible defect. The headline grows from 36 to 45 characters.
2. **Standfirst, third sentence.** Before: `Här är vad gruppen gjorde, …`. After: `Här är vad underhållsgruppen gjorde, …`. Class: the repair of a visible defect. "gruppen" has no antecedent inside the standfirst. The change also makes the **attribution** explicit by naming the maintenance group, which is the body's own term. The word count is unchanged at 40, because one word replaces one word.
3. **First sentence of the body.** Before: `Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` After: `Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna med sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` Class: a borderline case, between the repair of a visible defect (a pseudo-cleft modelled on English, "var vad … ville få ut av") and a change of taste. The claim changes slightly in **meaning**: the original framed shared visibility as *the* thing the group wanted from the log, and the rewrite states it as a wish. The goal, the actor and the instrument are all kept.

There are no mechanical corrections.
