## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is finding 3. The run rewrote the lede of a text that conforms to the anatomy:

> Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen … ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.

became

> Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information … med sin gemensamma reparationslogg.

The only grounds given were that the construction "lät översatt". That construction is grammatical Swedish, so this is a rewrite for taste. The reply itself concedes that the rewrite changed what the sentence says: "Den betoningen att det var själva målet är borta."

R1 bars clean texts from being rewritten to satisfy taste. Two more points add to that:
- The headline change narrows a claim in scope on a control that needed no narrowing.
- The unresolved finding asserts that the headline fails the genre, which the frozen expectation rules out.

In the run's favour, every protected element survives word for word, none of the named wrong findings was made, and the account of all three changes is complete and honest.
