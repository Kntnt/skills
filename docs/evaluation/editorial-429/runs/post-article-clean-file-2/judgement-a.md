## 4. R1

**Fail.**

Deciding passage: the standfirst sentence ”Här är vad ett kort försök kan säga” was rewritten to ”Här är vad försöket kan säga”.

- The frozen expectation treats this text as conforming and names the standfirst as something to preserve. Calling a four-week trial ”kort” is the author's own modest, working judgement. It sits naturally with the text's calm framing of what the measurements can and cannot show. Nothing in the text contradicts it, and the Skill's objection needs no outside source to test. It is not a visible defect.

- The change was reported accurately. Everything else was preserved, and none of the rejected mistakes were made. Still, R1 does not allow a clean text to be rewritten for taste, and here a claim was changed outside any real finding.
