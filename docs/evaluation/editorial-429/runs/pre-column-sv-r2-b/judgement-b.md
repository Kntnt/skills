# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared the text line by line. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline and five of the six body paragraphs are identical. There are two differences.

1. **Title.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall frågar när och vem, inte varför`.
   Class: **a change to what a claim says (meaning)**, made on grounds of taste. The old title names an absence ("the box that does not exist") and asserts nothing more. The new title makes a proposition: the template asks when and who, but not why. The body supports this proposition. Paragraph 1 has "Mallen vill veta när vi ses och vilka som kommer. Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om", and paragraph 4 has "varför vi behöver just varandras tid". So the proposition is not new to the text, but the title now makes a claim it did not make before. The new title also turns "vad tiden ska leda till" (what the time should lead to, the missing box for a decision) into "varför" (why). That narrows the author's framing slightly but does not contradict it. The title gains no scope, certainty, attribution, chronology or causality beyond what the body asserts, and it stays with the document ("mötesmall"), not a meeting. Nothing is hardened. The old title's working metaphor, which paragraph 1 pays off, is lost. That is a loss of voice.
2. **Dash in paragraph 3.** Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid…` (spaced em dash). After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid…` (spaced en dash).
   Class: **mechanical correction (locale form).** Swedish uses the spaced en dash. This difference does not depend on difference 1.

Total: two differences. One changes what a claim says (meaning).
