## 1. Differences

I diffed `work/input.md` against the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and all other formatting are identical. There are two differences.

1. Headline. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall saknar en ruta för mötets syfte`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning and scope**. The old headline named no box and made no claim beyond an image of absence. The new one says the missing box is a box for the meeting's *syfte*. The body names the missing box as the box for *beslutet* ("Någon ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån finns inte"). "Syfte" is wider than "beslut", and the column says outright that it does not want every conversation pushed toward a decision. The new headline is also a flat declarative in place of the author's ironic image, which the last sentence echoes ("tvivlet på ännu en ruta"). So it is a change of voice as well. It follows no other difference.
2. Paragraph 3, dash. Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. A spaced em dash becomes the spaced en dash that Swedish uses.

Total: 2 differences. 1 is a change to what a claim says (meaning, with scope), and 1 is mechanical.
