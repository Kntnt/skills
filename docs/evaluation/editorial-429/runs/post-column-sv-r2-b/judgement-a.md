# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

A line-by-line diff of `work/input.md` against the fenced text in `response.md` finds two differences. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and every other sentence are identical.

1. Heading, line 8. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall saknar en ruta för mötets syfte`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning and scope.** The old heading pointed at an unnamed box, and the first paragraph identifies it as the box "för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån". The new heading names that box as one "för mötets syfte". Purpose is a wider category than decision, so the claim now covers more. The Skill presents this as the repair of a visible defect: the heading "does not stand on its own". But the old heading is a working column headline in the writer's voice, a riddle that the first paragraph answers. Replacing it is a change of taste made to satisfy a genre guideline, and it also changes the claim.
2. Paragraph 3, line 16. Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: **a mechanical correction** of a locale form. The Swedish dash (tankstreck) is a spaced en dash, so the em dash was a locale error. This difference does not follow from the first one.
