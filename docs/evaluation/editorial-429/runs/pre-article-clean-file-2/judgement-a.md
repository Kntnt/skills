# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

The two files differ on one line only. The file has no frontmatter, and its formatting is unchanged.

- Headline. Before: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` (48 characters, 7 words). After: `# Björkskolans givare visar när luften var kall, inte orsaken` (59 characters, 9 words). Class: **a change of taste**, which also changes what the claim says:
  - **attribution**: the thing that "visar" is now the sensors ("Björkskolans givare"), not the trial ("Mätförsöket").
  - **scope/meaning**: the bare "när" becomes "när luften var kall", and "varför" becomes "orsaken".

  The old headline had no visible defect. "visar när, inte varför" is an ordinary, readable elliptical headline, and the standfirst below it resolves it at once. This is not a mechanical correction and it is not the repair of a visible defect.

No other difference. The standfirst, byline, opening, both sections, the quotation and the ending are identical byte for byte.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** R1 says: "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines." This control is a clean text that conforms to the anatomy, and the Skill rewrote its headline:

> `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` → `# Björkskolans givare visar när luften var kall, inte orsaken`

The stated reason ("'när' saknade objekt") is a matter of taste, not a visible defect. The rewrite also moves the claim's attribution from the trial to the sensors. It makes the headline longer, 9 words against the Skill's own eight-word norm, which the reply admits. By the reply's own account it also does not remove the overlap it was meant to address. Everything else in the text was preserved, and the account is honest. That does not cure the unwarranted rewrite.
