## 1. Differences

- Line 19, the last subheading. Before: `## Besluta om försöket, inte om antagandet`. After: `## Besluta om försöket, inte om ett antagande`. Class: a change of taste that touches **meaning** a little. In the original, the definite form "antagandet" refers back to the headline's "ett antagande" ("Avskaffa inte telefonbokningen på ett antagande"). This is ordinary Swedish anaphora: a thing is named first in the indefinite and then in the definite. The body also says what the assumption is: that booking counts from two venues over eight weeks show who can manage without phone booking (lines 3, 11 and 13). So the definite form points at the particular assumption the article has already named. The indefinite form makes it a general one. That is not a visible defect. The subheading also grew from 39 to 42 characters, which is outside the 33–40 range the frozen expectation gives for this text's subheadings.
