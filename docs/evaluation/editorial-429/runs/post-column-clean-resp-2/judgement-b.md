## 4. R1

**Fail.** The decisive passage is the one finding:

> "”Vill åt” kan läsas på två sätt. Den som bara ser rubriken kan tro att skribenten går åt besluten. … Rubriken sa dessutom bara vad avsnittet inte enbart handlar om. Den är nu omskriven till ”Ett möte kan ge mer än ett beslut”."

This is a control that conforms to the anatomy, so any rewrite has to rest on a concrete visible defect. The alleged ambiguity is not one. In "Det är inte bara besluten jag vill åt", the words "inte bara" force the reading "I'm after more than the decisions", and the section's own closing line, "Jag är alltså inte ute efter färre beslut", confirms that reading. A hostile sense of "vilja åt" does not survive the sentence. The reply's second reason, that the heading only says what the section is not only about, is a preference about how subheadings should be phrased. It is not a defect.

The fix cost the text a claim outside any real finding. The writer's first-person statement of her own aim became a general, unowned claim about meetings (a change of attribution and meaning). It also blunts the link that "alltså" in "Jag är alltså inte ute efter färre beslut" made back to the heading. The reply disclosed the change accurately, and it avoided every rejection the expectation names. But R1 forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste, and that is what this one correction did.
