## 4. R1

**Fail.** The case is narrow.

Everything the expectation says to preserve is preserved, and none of the rejected findings was made. The run nonetheless rewrote a clean control text on the strength of a finding that is not a concrete visible defect.

The deciding passage is the finding's premise: "Brödtexten sade aldrig vad som antogs. Mellanrubrikens bestämda form ”antagandet” syftade på något som varken brödtexten eller ingressen hade nämnt." This premise does not hold. The headline "Avskaffa inte telefonbokningen på ett antagande" introduces the assumption, and the body names its content: "Siffrorna visar varken … hur många som skulle klara sig utan telefonbokning". The subheading's "antagandet" therefore refers back to the headline, and a reader can recover what is assumed from the text itself.

The repair is also not neutral:

- It adds a new claim in the author's voice ("vore att anta att den inte behövs").
- It stretches a crisp, parallel subheading to 65 characters ("… inte om antagandet att telefonen inte behövs").
- It shifts the object from "telefonbokningen" to "telefonen".

Both edits are fully and honestly reported, which counts in the run's favour. Under R1, though, "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste", and this is a rewrite for explicitness, not the repair of a visible defect.
