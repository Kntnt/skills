  **After:** "… eller hur många som skulle klara sig utan telefonbokning. Att de som valde telefonen skulle klara sig utan den är därför ett antagande."
  **Class:** a change to what the text claims (**meaning**, with a side effect on **scope**). The paragraph before it only said what the figures do not show. The new sentence also narrows the assumption that the article argues against, which is that the telephone channel can be removed, to a claim about the people who chose the telephone. It is not a mechanical correction. It does not repair a visible defect either (see heading 4), so it is a change of taste that adds a claim.
