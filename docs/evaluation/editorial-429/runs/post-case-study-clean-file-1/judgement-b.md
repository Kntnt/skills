## 1. Differences

There is no frontmatter in either file. The formatting (bold standfirst, `Text:` byline, H2 levels, the dash-led quotes and the link) is unchanged. There are three differences, all in wording.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena` (36 characters). After: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quays arbetsledare` (58 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, in **attribution** and **scope**, and it loses the qualification.
   - The headline used to state an action by the customer. It now states an appraisal, attributed to Lind.
   - The appraisal loses her "oss", so it reads as a general claim that a shared picture helps.
   - It also loses her "men jag skulle lägga till en vecka", so the headline presents the appraisal without its qualification.
   - The input's headline had no visible defect, so this is also a change of taste.
2. **First body paragraph, first sentence.** Before: `Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` After: `Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville använda sin gemensamma reparationslogg för att se samma information över skiftgränserna.` Class: a change of taste, bordering on repair.
   - The "var vad" pseudo-cleft is at most a mild anglicism. The original sentence is grammatical Swedish.
   - The meaning is essentially kept. There is a slight shift from "ville få ut av" (the outcome they wanted) to "ville använda … för att" (the use they intended).
3. **Second subheading.** Before: `## Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning` (39 characters). After: `## Tilldelningen tog i median två arbetsdagar under försöket` (58 characters). Class: a change of taste. It also changes **meaning** at heading level.
   - The only heading that carried the caveat now carries the result instead.
   - The caveat survives only in the body sentence "Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning…". That sentence is unchanged.
   - The number itself is stated correctly.

Nothing else differs. The standfirst, the byline, all quotes, the numbers (31, two and three working days, 4 December 2025, six staff), the disclosure sentence and the checklist link are identical.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The control is clean ("Conforms to the anatomy"), and R1 forbids rewriting a clean text for taste. The run rewrote the headline, a subheading and an opening sentence, none of which had a visible defect.

The headline decides the verdict. `Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena` became `Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quays arbetsledare`. The new headline:

- drops Lind's "oss" and her "men … en vecka för förberedelser" qualification, so the claim changes in scope and loses its qualification;

The reply admits these losses. So the run added a real, reported overclaim in order to remove a speculative one.

The subheading rewrite moves the caveat out of the headings. The first-sentence rewrite is taste, and the reply mislocates it in the standfirst.

What kept the run from worse: it rejected every trap the expectation names, and it preserved the numbers, the disclosure, the link and the body quotes verbatim.
