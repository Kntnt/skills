## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. There is no frontmatter in either file. Formatting, the standfirst, the byline, both subheadings and all body paragraphs are byte-identical.

- Headline, line 1. Before: `# Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom varför` (44 characters). After: `# Mötesmallen frågar när men inte varför` (38 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, changing its **scope**. It also changes the **meaning** of a figure of speech. The original is the idiomatic hyperbole "a box for everything except why". The new headline makes the narrower, literal claim that the template asks *when* but not *why*. The change also drops the word "ruta", which the text returns to on purpose in "Det är inget fel på rutorna" and "## Ännu en ruta, och ändå vill jag prova".

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the reply's finding: "Rubriken ”Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom varför” sa att mallen har rutor för allt annat än varför. Texten säger tvärtom att mallen bara har plats för starttid och sluttid … En läsare som bara såg rubriken fick alltså motsatt bild av textens poäng. Rubriken lyder nu ”Mötesmallen frågar när men inte varför”."

This is a clean control that conforms to the anatomy. The original headline is a working figure of speech in the author's voice, and the text's own point is that time is the only thing the template counts. It is not a visible defect. By rewriting it, the Skill spent its correction budget on a clean text, narrowed the headline's claim and dropped the "ruta" echo. R1 says clean texts may not be rewritten to suit taste. Everything else is preserved exactly, and none of the named rejections occurred. The single unwarranted rewrite of the headline is what decides the verdict.
