## 1. Differences

I compared the body in the reply's fenced block with `work/input.md` line by line. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline, the paragraph breaks and all Markdown formatting are identical. There are two differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Mallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`. After: `# Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`. **Class: repair of a visible defect, and only just.** Read on its own, the headline's definite noun "Mallen" has nothing earlier in the text to point to. You can see that from the input alone. The fix names the same object as the body's first sentence ("bibliotekets mötesmall"), so the claim keeps its scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality and meaning. It is **not** a change to what a claim says. It sits close to a change of taste, because a column headline can be allowed to lean on its opening, and the wordplay still works either way.
2. **Dash in the paragraph that begins "Där börjar tvivlet".** Before: `utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien`. After: `utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien`. **Class: mechanical correction (locale form).** Swedish uses a spaced en dash, not an em dash.

Neither difference follows from the other. There are no changes to what a claim says.
