# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. From line 2 to the end, the two files are byte-identical. That covers the standfirst, the byline, the body, the subheadings, the quotations, the numbers, the disclosure and the link. There is no frontmatter, and no formatting changed.

- Before: `# Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena` (36 characters). After: `# Elm Quays arbetsledare skulle pröva reparationsloggen igen` (58 characters).
  - Class: a change to what a claim says. It changes **meaning**: the headline stops stating what the group did (it gathered its repair cases) and states the customer's appraisal instead (the supervisor would try the log again).
  - It is not a repair of a visible defect. The Skill calls the old headline a **scope** overstatement. The standfirst directly beneath it, however, sets the scope at once: two houses, eight weeks, a trial. A 36-character headline in the definite form, scoped by its own standfirst, is ordinary headline practice, not a defect. The Skill's second reason is that the headline named only what the group did, not what the trial gave or what the customer thought. That is a preference about what a headline should put first, so it is a change of taste.
  - The new headline also drops Lind's condition, "men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser". The standfirst and the body still carry that condition.

## 2. The account

- The headline change: reported, as finding 1 and under "Vad som hände med påståendena". The report is accurate as a description: it gives the old and new text correctly, and it correctly states that the rest of the text is unchanged character for character. Its characterisation is not sound. It presents the change as the repair of a scope overclaim, but the old headline was not an overclaim once the standfirst is read with it (see 1). The report also says the new headline "bygger på Maya Linds eget villkorade omdöme", yet the headline leaves out the condition that made the appraisal qualified. That is not stated.
