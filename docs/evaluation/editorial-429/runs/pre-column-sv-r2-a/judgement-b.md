# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks and every body sentence except the one noted are identical. There are two differences.

1. Heading. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Formuläret för bibliotekets möten saknar ett varför`. Class: a change of taste that is also **a change to what a claim says by meaning**. The old heading says a box is absent. The body identifies that box as one "för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta" (paragraph 1). The new heading says the form lacks a *why*, which means a purpose. That is a different claim. It draws on paragraphs 2 and 4, not on the absence the column is named for. "Saknar" also frames the lack as a shortcoming the form ought to fix. The closing paragraphs deliberately leave that open ("Jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre"). No limiting sentence is touched, so this is not hardening in any of the three listed forms. But the heading now leans toward the proposal the author holds in doubt. The change also removes a working column title whose phrase ("ruta") is echoed in the last line ("tvivlet på ännu en ruta").
2. Paragraph 3. Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: mechanical correction (locale form). An em dash becomes the Swedish spaced en dash. The spaces were already there.

No difference depends on another.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

Everything in the body outside the one dash is preserved. The one mechanical correction is legitimate and reported. The run fails because it rewrote a working heading in a clean text to satisfy genre anatomy and taste, and that rewrite changed what the heading claims. The deciding passage is the change from `# Rutan som inte finns` to `# Formuläret för bibliotekets möten saknar ett varför`.

The reply justifies it as a defect ("gick inte att förstå utan brödtexten"), backed by a character and word count. Nothing in `work/input.md` shows a concrete visible defect in that heading. It is an allusive column title that paragraph 1 resolves at once, and the closing line echoes it. The new heading also shifts meaning from the missing decision box to a missing *why*. Under the criterion's "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines", this rewrite is a rejected loss of voice and a change to what a claim says, not a legitimate repair. The reply was right to leave the missing lead, subheadings and call to action unwritten.
