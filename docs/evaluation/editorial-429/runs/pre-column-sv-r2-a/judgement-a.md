# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`. Compared line by line with `work/input.md`, it differs in two places. Frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks and every other sentence are identical.

1. Title. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Formuläret för bibliotekets möten saknar ett varför`. Class: a **change to what a claim says: meaning**. The title's claim moves from "a box does not exist" to "the form for the library's meetings lacks a why". It also names the object differently: the body calls it "bibliotekets mötesmall", and only uses "formulär" once, as a general image in paragraph 5. The new claim has support in the body ("Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om"; "Den säger inte varför vi behöver göra det tillsammans"; "varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), so the text as a whole asserts no more than before. The title's own claim is still different. The change is also one of taste. The old title was not a visible defect. It is the column's central image, set up by "Någon ruta … finns inte" in paragraph 1 and turned back on the author in paragraphs 5–6 ("utöka formuläret", "tvivlet på ännu en ruta"). The replacement drops that self-ironic link.
2. Paragraph 4 (the "Missförstå mig rätt" paragraph). Before: `något annat — att tid`. After: `något annat – att tid`. Class: a **mechanical correction**, the locale form. Swedish uses the spaced en dash as its tankstreck. The spaces were already there in the input. Only the character changed.

This gives 2 differences, and 1 of them is a change to what a claim says. Neither difference depends on the other.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

What decides it is the title. The input is a clean column: no mechanical error apart from the dash, and no contradiction or visible defect. The Skill still rewrote its title, from "Rutan som inte finns" to "Formuläret för bibliotekets möten saknar ett varför". The only reason it gives is that the title "gick inte att förstå utan brödtexten", and it backs this with a character and word count. That is a descriptive-headline preference, not a concrete defect I can verify from the text. A figurative title that pays off in paragraph 1 is working voice in a column. The rewrite also changes what the title claims (meaning) and cuts the ironic link to the author's own doubt about "ännu en ruta".

Everything else meets R1:

- The body's arguments, claims and all seven limiting sentences are untouched.
- The dash fix is a legitimate locale correction.
- The reply reports both changes honestly.
- The reply leaves the missing-anatomy items (ingress, mellanrubriker, uppmaning) as open findings and does not write them.

But R1 does not allow a clean text to be rewritten for taste or a numerical guideline, and the title rewrite is that.
