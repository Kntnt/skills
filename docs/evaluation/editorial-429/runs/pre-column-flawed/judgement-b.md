## 1. Differences

There is no frontmatter in either text. The heading level (`#`), the bare-name byline "Nora Vik", the opening paragraph and the third paragraph are unchanged. The Skill made four changes:

The old headline claimed a change the column never makes. 3. **Second paragraph, second sentence.** Before: "Det är viktigt att vara viktig." After: removed. Class: this repairs a visible defect (an empty filler sentence). It removes a claim with no content (**meaning**). The removal is legitimate.
4. **Final paragraph.** Before: "Sammanfattningsvis är möten viktigare än någonsin i dagens snabbt föränderliga värld." After: removed. Class: this repairs a visible defect (a generic summary ending that repeats difference 2). It removes the repeated claim (**scope**). The removal is legitimate. As a result, the body now ends on the writer's doubt ("men jag vet inte om ytterligare en ruta gör möten bättre."), and the body falls from four paragraphs to three.

The Skill made no mechanical corrections. The reply says the proofreading pass found nothing to correct, and the two texts agree.
