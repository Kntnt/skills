## 2. Findings

### 2.1 F1 — the denial of measurement is broadened from prior knowledge to knowledge

- Draft: "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
- Source: "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."

What differs: the thing measured, and with it the scope in time. "förkunskaper" is knowledge held beforehand — the prerequisites participants arrive with. The draft's "participants' knowledge" carries no such restriction and denies measurement of their knowledge at any point, including at the end of the course.

Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails: the course leaders' written summaries, which the evaluation is built on, record for each round what participants could do by the end of the four weeks, while nothing at all is recorded about what they could do at the start. The evaluation then measures participants' knowledge — at the close of the course — yet still does not measure their förkunskaper. Nothing supplied excludes this: the material states only what the evaluation rests on ("närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar") and what it does not measure ("förkunskaper"), and says nothing about the content of those summaries.

Case in the other direction: none. If the evaluation measures no knowledge whatever, it measures no prior knowledge either, so the draft's statement entails the source's.

The broadened denial also does work in the argument: the following paragraph and §1.15 rest on the absence of a before-and-after baseline, which is exactly what "förkunskaper" names, so the narrower statement is the one the argument actually needs.

Smallest supported repair: "The evaluation does not measure participants' prior knowledge."

### 2.2 F2 — "doubling" states a change in scale the material does not give

- Draft: "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
- Source: "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år"; "gäller tre kursomgångar vid vuxenutbildningen i Rönnvik under hösten 2025."

What differs: the thing measured. The source gives the proposed volume (six rounds per year) and, separately, the number of rounds the evaluation covers (three, during the autumn of 2025). It nowhere states how many rounds run in a year now. "Doubling" asserts that six per year is twice the present rate.

Case in which the material holds and the draft's claim fails: three rounds also ran in the spring of 2025, so the course already runs six rounds a year and the proposal changes the volume not at all, or changes only its permanence. The evaluation would still "gälla tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025", since it covers one term. Nothing supplied excludes this; the material fixes the evaluation's coverage, not the course's annual schedule.

Case in the other direction: if the course ran only those three rounds in the whole year, the draft's claim holds while the material still says only what it says — which is why the material does not settle it.

Note that "thin basis" itself is the author's own evaluation of the documentation, supported by §1.13, and should be preserved.

Smallest supported repair: "That is a thin basis for making a course permanent and running it six times a year." (Any wording that states the proposed volume instead of a ratio to an unstated present volume will do.)

### 2.3 F3 — a non-claim is reported as a non-existence, and "egen" is dropped

- Draft: "Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt."

What differs: whose assertion it is, and the scope of the denial. The material establishes what the association claims — namely, nothing — and does not establish that no such measurement exists. It also restricts the non-claim to an own measurement ("egen mätning"); the draft's first clause drops that restriction. The second clause of the draft reproduces the source faithfully; the first clause goes beyond it.

Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's first clause fails: the association had a handful of former participants describe what changed for them, judged the exercise too slight to rely on, and therefore makes no claim to have a measurement of the course's effect. The material states what the association claims, not what it possesses, so nothing supplied excludes this.

Case in the other direction: none for the first clause — having no measurement is consistent with claiming none.

Smallest supported repair: "Lyssna Först makes no claim to have a measurement of the course's effect of its own." The sentence keeps its rhetorical work — conceding that the association has not measured the effect either — while asserting only what the material gives.

## 3. Editorial questions (not reported as defects)

### 3.1 The closing condition for the postponement

- Draft: "But the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then."
- Source: "skjuter upp beslutet till höstens budgetberedning och under tiden ber förvaltningen redovisa kostnaden per omgång och hur deltagarna hittade till kursen."

The source sets the postponement to an occasion (the autumn budget preparation) and the two figures to the interval before it. The close instead conditions the decision on having the figures, which would in principle permit a decision as soon as they arrive. The two come apart in a case where the administration reports in May. Against that: §1.17 has already stated the association's proposal in the source's own terms two sentences earlier, "and decide then" points back to the postponed occasion named there, and the boundary "Slutet ska namnge nämndens möjliga beslut" asks the ending to name the decision open to the committee, which this does. The line between a loose restatement of the association's own ask and a changed proposal is genuinely unsettled here, so it is reported as a question for the editor rather than as a defect with a repair. Tightening it to "postpone the permanence decision to the autumn budget preparation, and decide then, with those figures in hand" would remove the question without loss.

## 4. Translation of referenced speech

- Voice: preserved. The source's plain, almost clipped repetition of the same verb is kept by repeating "finish", and the second sentence keeps the flat understatement of "något de inte redan hade" in "something they did not already have".
- No obstruction to the reading was found, and no implicit referent needed clarifying: "it" in the second sentence resolves to the course, as "den" does in the Swedish.
