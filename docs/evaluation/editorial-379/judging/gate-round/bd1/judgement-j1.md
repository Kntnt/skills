# Judgement — bd1-broadened-denial (judge j1)

Fixture: `docs/evaluation/editorial-379/fixtures/bd1-broadened-denial.md`. Six reports judged: A, B, C, D, E, F. Classes are my own, formed from the fixture; each report's reasoning was weighed as argument, not taken as evidence.

## 1. My reading of the focus passage

**Draft:** "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
**Material:** "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."

*Förkunskaper* is prior knowledge — what a participant brought to the course. The draft denies measurement of participants' knowledge with no restriction in time, so it takes in what participants knew at the end of the four weeks as well. That is strictly more than the material denies, and the material leaves the wider denial open rather than supporting it: it says only that the evaluation "bygger på närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar" and never says what those summaries contain. An evaluation whose course-leader summaries describe what participants could do by the final week, with nothing recorded at intake, satisfies "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper" and falsifies the draft's sentence. Nothing supplied excludes that state of affairs; the boundary "Inga effektstudier ... är belagda" rules out effect studies, not an end-of-course description.

The broadening also does work in the argument. Two sentences later the draft says completion "does not tell the committee what those people could do afterward that they could not do before" — a before-and-after point that needs only the missing baseline, which is precisely what *förkunskaper* names. So the draft asserts more than the material carries, in a sentence load-bearing for the article's charge against the decision basis, and the repair is one word. **A finding against the focus passage is correct**, and the supported repair is "participants' prior knowledge".

## 2. Other passages I formed a view on before reading the reports

- **"That is a thin basis for doubling a course."** The material gives one round count, "tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025", scoped to what the evaluation covers in a single autumn term, and the proposal "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år". No present annual volume is supplied, so "doubling" fixes a ratio the material does not fix; *byggs ut* establishes only that the present annual figure is below six. "Thin basis" itself is the author's evaluation of two supplied absences and stands.
- **"It tells the committee that people who started the course could finish it."** Read in context — the sign-up and completion figures stand two sentences above, and the same point is restated as "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished" — this is a generic completability claim in the author's own voice, not a claim that every starter finished.
- **"Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."** The first clause converts "gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning" into non-possession and drops *egen*; the second restores the source's form exactly. Whether a first-person concession by the party about its own holdings, running against its own interest, needs repair is a question competent editors can answer either way.
