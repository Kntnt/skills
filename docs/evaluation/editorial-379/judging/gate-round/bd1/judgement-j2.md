# Judgement — bd1-broadened-denial (judge j2)

Fixture: `docs/evaluation/editorial-379/fixtures/bd1-broadened-denial.md`. Six reports judged: A, B, C, D, E, F. Judged from the fixture; each report's reasoning was weighed as argument, not taken as evidence.

## 1. My own reading of the focus passage

**Draft:** "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
**Material:** "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."

*Förkunskaper* is prior knowledge — what a participant brought to the course. The draft's sentence carries no temporal restriction at all: an ordinary reader takes it to say the evaluation measures participants' knowledge at no point, before, during or after the four weeks. That is strictly more than the material denies.

The difference can make one statement true and the other false. The material says the evaluation "bygger på närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar" and never says what those summaries contain. If a course leader's summary recorded what participants could do by the final week, with nothing recorded at intake, then "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper" is true and the draft's sentence is false. Nothing supplied excludes that case: the only statement about what the evaluation does not measure is the narrow one, and the boundary "Inga effektstudier, jämförelsegrupper, kostnadssiffror eller klagomål är belagda" concerns effect studies, not an end-of-course description. The entailment runs one way only — the draft's denial entails the material's, not the reverse — so this is a one-directional broadening.

It is also load-bearing rather than incidental. The draft's next paragraph argues that completion "does not tell the committee what those people could do afterward that they could not do before" — a before/after gap that the narrow, supported denial establishes on its own. The broadened form therefore claims more against the decision basis than the material licenses, in a text whose boundary is that the author may criticise the documents sharply without attributing what is not in them.

**Verdict: a finding against the focus passage is correct.** The smallest supported repair is "The evaluation does not measure participants' prior knowledge."

## 2. Other passages I checked on my own reading

Two further passages bear on the reports' findings.

- **"That is a thin basis for doubling a course."** The material gives a proposed level ("byggs ut till sex omgångar per år") and one round count ("gäller tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025"), which is the evaluation's coverage for one autumn term, not the course's annual output. "Doubling" fixes a ratio to a present annual volume the material never states, and it compares three-in-a-term with six-in-a-year. A finding here is correct; "a thin basis" itself is the author's licensed evaluation of two stated absences and should stand.
- **"Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."** The material states a non-claim ("gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning"), not a non-existence. The clauses come apart in principle, but this is the sender's own disclaimer about its own organisation in its own signed piece, and the second clause restores the source's modality exactly. Genuinely contested ground.
