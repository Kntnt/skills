## 2. Findings (source support)

### F1 — The denial about the questionnaire is narrowed from health to mental health

- **Draft (§ "The recommendation gives a reason the file does not carry"):** "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health."
- **Source:** "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa."
- **What differs:** scope of the denial. The supplied statement denies that the questionnaire asks about the participants' health at all. The draft denies only the narrower mental-health subset, and so leaves standing the possibility that the questionnaire asked about health in some other respect.
- **Test, direction 1 (draft holds, source fails):** suppose the questionnaire had included an item on how often the respondent exercises, or whether they eat an evening meal at the center — health questions that are not mental-health questions. Then the draft's sentence would be true while "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa" would be false. Nothing in the supplied material excludes this case: the material lists three topics the questionnaire asked about and then states the health exclusion, but the listing is introduced with *och frågade*, not as an exhaustive enumeration, and no supplied statement says those were the only items.
- **Test, direction 2 (source holds, draft holds too):** the source statement entails the draft's, so no case separates them in this direction. The defect is one-sided: the draft asserts strictly less than the material supports.
- **Why it is a defect rather than a preference:** the sentence is load-bearing. It sits immediately after "The documents contain nothing to support that statement" and is the draft's only evidence that the *tjänsteutlåtande*'s motive is unexamined. Narrowed to mental health, it reads as though the questionnaire's coverage of health were the open question, and it weakens a supported claim in the one place the argument needs it at full strength. The brief's thesis ("motivera beslutet med det som faktiskt är undersökt") turns on what was and was not investigated.
- **Smallest supported repair:** delete the narrowing modifier —
  > The questionnaire does not ask about participants' health.

### F2 — A disclaimer of any claim is turned into an assertion of ignorance

- **Draft (§ "Write the reason the evidence supports"):** "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to."
- **Source:** "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa."
- **What differs:** modality, and whose knowledge is asserted. The material records what the association *claims* — namely, nothing on this question. The draft's first clause instead asserts, as fact, the association's actual state of knowledge. Declining to make a claim and not knowing are different states; the material carries only the first. The second clause ("we do not claim to") is exactly supported.
- **Test, direction 1 (source holds, draft fails):** suppose a member of Unga Röster's board has read the research literature and is personally satisfied that she knows how such centers affect young people's health, but the association has decided, as a matter of discipline about this file, to advance no claim of its own on the question. Then "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta…" holds and "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health" fails. The material nowhere excludes this: it states only what the association claims, and it adds that Sandberg "motsätter sig inte att kommunen intresserar sig för ungas hälsa," which is consistent with the association having views it declines to press.
- **Test, direction 2 (draft holds, source fails):** an association that genuinely does not know might still assert that it does; then the draft's first clause holds while the source statement fails. Both directions separate, which confirms the two are not the same statement.
- **Why it is a defect rather than a preference:** the paragraph's rhetorical force comes from the association holding itself to the standard it demands of the committee, and the material's careful *gör inget anspråk på att veta* is precisely that standard. Replacing it with a factual assertion about the association's knowledge commits the author, in a piece whose whole argument is against unsupported assertions, to an assertion of the kind it criticises. It is a modality change in the one sentence where modality is the point.
- **Smallest supported repair:** drop the added clause and keep the disclaimer —
  > Unga Röster makes no claim to know how the center affects young people's health.

### F3 — A scheduled meeting with a proposal before it is reported as a decision taken on that date

- **Draft (opening):** "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- **Source:** "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas." Boundaries: "Slutet ska namnge nämndens möjliga beslut."
- **What differs:** modality and time of the event. The material establishes that there is a committee meeting on 3 March and that a staff proposal to make the center permanent stands before it. It does not state that the committee decides the matter at that meeting, and the boundaries describe the outcome as a *möjligt beslut*.
- **Test, direction 1 (source holds, draft fails):** suppose the committee takes the item up on 3 March and returns it for further preparation, or defers it to a later meeting without deciding. The meeting happened, the *tjänsteutlåtande* was prepared ahead of it and proposed permanence — the supplied statement holds in full — while "the committee decides on March 3 whether the center becomes permanent" fails. Nothing in the supplied material excludes deferral; no supplied statement says a decision falls at that meeting.
- **Test, direction 2 (draft holds, source fails):** a committee could decide the question on 3 March on a member's motion with no staff proposal before it; then the draft's sentence holds while the source's does not. The statements separate in both directions.
- **Severity:** minor. This is the mildest of the three findings, and the draft's closing sentence uses the correct modality ("The committee should make the Hamngatan center permanent on March 3"). It is reported because the opening presents a future event as settled where the material presents a possibility, and because the same piece elsewhere holds the committee to exactly this distinction between what the file establishes and what it merely proposes.
- **Smallest supported repair:** state what the material carries — the item is before the committee — and leave the decision as possible:
  > Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee has the open youth center on Hamngatan before it on March 3, with a staff recommendation to make it permanent.

  If the sentence's forward motion is wanted, an equally small repair is: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee can make the open youth center on Hamngatan permanent at its meeting on March 3."

## 3. Translation check (separate from source support)

The draft is en_US; the material is Swedish. Checked here: that translated quotation expresses the same meaning and distinctive voice in idiomatic target-language speech. Meaning changes outside quoted speech are reported in §2 as source-support findings; none was found beyond those listed there.

**T1 — Quoted motive from the *tjänsteutlåtande*.** "motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn" → "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn". Meaning is preserved exactly: *motverkar* → "counteracts", *psykisk ohälsa* → poor mental health as a condition rather than an illness diagnosis, *bland unga* → "among young people", place carried. Observation, not a defect: "mental ill health" is a compound more current in British and Nordic English than in en_US, where "poor mental health" is the ordinary phrase. It is not a concrete obstruction — an American reader parses it without difficulty, and rendering an official document's wording literally is defensible in a piece whose argument is about that exact wording. Recorded as an observation only; no repair is required, and the choice is the author's.

**T2 — Sandberg's standpoint.** "Gården behövs. Men en motivering som inte går att belägga håller bara tills någon läser underlaget." → "The center is needed. But a justification that cannot be supported holds only until somebody reads the file." Meaning and voice are preserved: the two-beat structure, the concession followed by the sharp second sentence, and the plain register all survive, and *håller bara tills* → "holds only until" keeps the metaphor of something that stands up for a limited time. Observation, not a defect: *underlaget* is the evidentiary basis, while the draft renders it "the file", the same phrase it uses two sections earlier for *handlingarna* (the case papers). The two are not identical in Swedish. The reading survives, because the *underlag* is what a reader of the case papers would find missing, so the point lands either way, and the repetition of "the file" ties the sentence to the earlier claim rather than obstructing it. No repair required; flagged only so the choice is visible.

No translation finding rises to a defect. The two observations above are recorded for transparency and are defensible as written.
