## 2. Source-support findings

### Finding 1 — the denial of health questions is narrower than the material's

- **Draft:** "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health."
- **Source:** "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa." The listed content of the questionnaire ("vad de gjorde på gården, hur de tog sig dit och om de ville att gården skulle vara öppen fler kvällar") contains no health question of any kind.
- **What differs:** the scope of the denial. The material denies health questions altogether; the draft denies only mental-health questions.
- **Both directions.** A questionnaire that asked "how would you rate your physical health?" alongside the three listed questions would satisfy the draft's sentence and falsify the supplied one. That case is set aside by the supplied statement "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa", so the draft's sentence is entailed by the material and nothing in it is false. The reverse case does not exist. What the narrowing changes is not truth but the certainty delivered: the sentence is the draft's evidence for "The documents contain nothing to support that statement", and a reader who meets the narrower denial in that position has to weigh the contrast the wording invites — mental health named, health at large not — against a file that in fact contains no health question at all. The material warrants the flat denial; the draft withholds it at the one point where it does the most work.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' health at all." (Alternatively, keeping the draft's word order: "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' health, mental or otherwise.")
- **Note on classification.** This is reported as a defect in strength, not as a false statement. A reader who disagrees may reasonably hold the sentence defensible as written; nothing in it misstates the material.

### Finding 2 — a scheduled decision asserted as certain

- **Draft:** "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- **Source:** "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- **What differs:** modality and scope. The material establishes that a meeting falls on March 3 and that a staff recommendation proposing permanence lies before it. It does not establish that the committee settles the question at that meeting.
- **Both directions.** A committee that takes the item up on March 3 and refers it back for further preparation, deciding at a later meeting, is compatible with everything supplied: the recommendation still lies before the March 3 meeting, so the source statement holds, while the draft's statement fails. No supplied statement excludes that case. The reverse direction raises nothing: whenever the committee does decide on March 3, the source statement holds too.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee takes up on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent." (Or: "is asked on March 3 to make ... permanent".) The closing sentence, "The committee should make the Hamngatan center permanent on March 3", needs no change: it is advocacy about what the committee should do, not a prediction that it will act that day.

## 3. Translation findings

No translation findings. Checks performed:

- **T1 — the quoted motive.** "motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn" is rendered "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn". Same meaning, same scope (young people in Nyhamn), same present tense, same unhedged causal force, which is correct here because the draft's argument is precisely that the recommendation asserts it without support. "Mental ill health" is a calque of "psykisk ohälsa" and reads more British than American; "poor mental health" or "mental health problems" would be the commoner en_US phrasing. It is no obstruction to the reading, it is current public-health usage, and it preserves the register of an official staff document, so it is recorded as an observation and not as a finding.
- **T2 — Elin Sandberg's statement.** "Gården behövs." becomes "The center is needed." — same impersonal construction, same flat delivery. "Men en motivering som inte går att belägga håller bara tills någon läser underlaget." becomes "But a justification that cannot be supported holds only until somebody reads the file." "Går att belägga" is "can be substantiated"; "cannot be supported" carries it, and the draft's use of "support" here matches "underlag ... finns inte" two paragraphs earlier, which is a consistency, not a drift. "Underlaget" is the supporting documentation; "the file" is the word the draft has already used for "handlingarna", and since the follow-up is part of the case papers the referent is settled by the draft's own context. The dry, clipped voice of the original survives. Idiomatic en_US throughout.
- **T3 — terms carried across.** "fritidsgård" to "youth center", "öppen fritidsgård" to "open youth center", "kultur- och fritidsnämnden" to "culture and leisure committee", "tjänsteutlåtandet" to "the staff recommendation" / "the recommendation prepared for the March 3 meeting", "uppföljning" to "follow-up", "besöksräkning vid entrén" to "a count at the entrance", "enkät" to "questionnaire", "handlingarna" to "the documents". In this context nothing could fall under one term of a pair and not the other: the referent of each is a single named object in the material, and the draft fixes it by name or by date on first use. "Youth center" is broader than "fritidsgård" in the abstract, but the draft always attaches it to Hamngatan, so no divergence arises here.
- **T4 — figures and dates.** "3 240" to "3,240", "9 januari 2026" to "January 9, 2026", "den 3 mars" to "March 3", "läsåret 2024/2025" to "the 2024/2025 school year", "71" spelled out as "Seventy-one" at the head of a sentence. All correct for en_US, and no value is altered.
- **T5 — person, number and natural gender.** The draft speaks as "we" (first person plural) for Unga Röster while bylined to its chair; the brief makes her the sender for the association ("Avsändare är Elin Sandberg, ordförande för föreningen Unga Röster") and gives the association's own proposal, so both the person and the number are carried. The source's one third-person singular feminine pronoun, "Hon motsätter sig inte att kommunen intresserar sig för ungas hälsa", is not rendered; the draft uses no pronoun for Elin Sandberg and so neither asserts nor loses a natural-gender feature. "Ungdomar" and "unga" become "young people": plural, no gender asserted in either language, and the Swedish here carries grammatical gender only. No natural-gender assertion is introduced anywhere in the draft.

## 4. Editorial questions (not defects, no repair proposed)

- **E1 — "does not know" for "gör inget anspråk på att veta".** The draft opens its third section with "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to." The material supplies the second clause ("Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa") but not the first: an association that had formed a private view from what its members say, and declined to press it as knowledge, would satisfy the material and not the draft's first clause, and nothing supplied excludes that. Set against this, the sentence is the sender speaking in the first person about its own state of knowledge, which is the kind of position the brief carries as the commissioning party's own, and the disclaimer is if anything more self-denying than the material's. The line between a standpoint and a factual assertion is genuinely unsettled here, so it is recorded as the brief's own unevidenced statement rather than reported as a defect. Should an editor want it tightened, "Unga Röster makes no claim to know how the center affects young people's health" is the material's own wording.
- **E2 — the subject of the visit figure.** "The school year recorded 3,240 visits" moves the subject from the counting to the period, where the material has an agentless passive ("Under läsåret registrerades 3 240 besök"). The count is attributed to the follow-up by the two sentences before it and the exclusion follows immediately after, so the boundaries' attribution requirement is met. Raised only in case the arm's editor prefers the figure to name its source in its own sentence.
- **E3 — an omission, recorded for completeness.** "Hon motsätter sig inte att kommunen intresserar sig för ungas hälsa" is not carried into the draft. An omission is not a finding on its own, and nothing in the draft implies the contrary: the criticism is aimed throughout at the evidentiary basis of the justification, never at the municipality's interest in young people's health.
