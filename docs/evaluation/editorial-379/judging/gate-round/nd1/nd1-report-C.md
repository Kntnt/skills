## 4. Divergences tested and set aside

### 4.1 "decides on March 3" (C2)

Case tested, draft fails while the material holds: the committee meets on 3 March, takes up the item, and refers it back for further preparation, so that no decision on permanence is taken that day.

Weighing: the material supplies only "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas", which places the proposal before that meeting. It also supplies, as a judging boundary, "Slutet ska namnge nämndens möjliga beslut", which establishes the committee as the deciding body on precisely this matter and 3 March as the only occasion the material names. Read as an ordinary reader would read a debate article, "the committee decides on March 3 whether … becomes permanent" says that the question is before the committee at that meeting, which is exactly what the material supplies; it does not warrant that no procedural outcome other than a decision is possible, and the draft's closing sentence is framed as what the committee *should* do, not as a prediction of what it will do. The divergence is a matter of how tightly the agenda item is described, not a claim the material contradicts, so it is defensible as written. Not a finding.

### 4.2 "mental health" for "hälsa" (C5)

The two terms do not name the same thing in the abstract: a question about sleep, diet or physical complaints would fall under "hälsa" and not under "mental health". Both directions therefore have to be tested.

Direction one — draft holds, source fails: the questionnaire asks nothing about mental health but does ask about the young people's physical health. This case is excluded by a supplied statement, quoted: "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa." The itemised list of what it did ask — "vad de gjorde på gården, hur de tog sig dit och om de ville att gården skulle vara öppen fler kvällar" — excludes it a second time. Set aside.

Direction two — source holds, draft fails: none can be named. A questionnaire that asks nothing about health asks nothing about mental health in particular, so the draft's statement follows from the supplied one in every case compatible with the material.

The draft's denial is therefore narrower than the material's and fully entailed by it: it claims less, not more, and claims nothing the material does not carry. In its context the narrowing is also the pertinent one — the sentence's work is to show that the one instrument that could have produced evidence for the quoted motive about "psykisk ohälsa" did not ask after it, and the sentence is restricted to that topic by the two sentences before it. No reader is left believing anything about the questionnaire that the material contradicts, and no qualification that changes a claim is lost. Widening the denial to "participants' health" would track the source wording more closely, but that is a preference about the draft, not a defect in it. Not a finding.

### 4.3 "what the young people using it want next" (C11)

Case tested: many distinct young people use the center across the school year, while the questionnaire reached only those present on three evenings in May 2025 and was answered by 71 of them, whose wishes need not be the wishes of the wider group.

Weighing: the draft claims only that the follow-up "says something about" what they want next, which is true of any answered questionnaire, and the same paragraph has already fixed both limits the reader needs — that the questionnaire was handed out "over three evenings in May 2025" (C7) and that 3,240 is a count of visits "not individual young people" (C9). No proportion, majority or result is attributed to the respondents or generalised from them. Defensible as written. Not a finding.

### 4.4 "does not know" for "gör inget anspråk på att veta" (C12)

Case tested: the association privately holds a well-founded view about how the center affects young people's health but advances no such claim, because the view has no support in the municipality's file. On that case "gör inget anspråk på att veta" holds and "does not know" would fail.

Weighing: the sentence is the association's own statement about its own position, written by its chair in the first person, and the material carries that position as the party's own ("Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta …"). A party is entitled to state where it stands on its own knowledge, and the sentence's operative half — "and we do not claim to" — reproduces the supplied wording exactly. This is the commissioning party's standpoint expressed as theirs, not an assertion about an external state of affairs that would require evidence. Not a finding.

## 5. Translation check of quoted and referenced speech

Checked separately from source support, for the same meaning, the same distinctive voice, and idiomatic en_US.

- C3, the recommendation's quoted motive: "motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn" → "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn". Meaning is exact, and the register is faithful: the Swedish is bureaucratic officialese, and "counteracts mental ill health" reads as officialese too, which serves the draft's point that this is a phrase from a file. "Mental ill health" is more common in British than American public-health usage, but it is transparent to an American reader and the alternatives that read as more natively American — "prevents mental health problems", "improves mental health" — each shift the claim. No concrete obstruction to the reading; a change here would be a preferred synonym at the cost of accuracy. Not a finding.
- C14, Elin Sandberg's position, referenced rather than quoted: "Gården behövs." → "The center is needed." — plain, flat, unhedged, as in the Swedish. "Men en motivering som inte går att belägga håller bara tills någon läser underlaget." → "But a justification that cannot be supported holds only until somebody reads the file." — "går att belägga" is rendered as "can be supported", which keeps the evidentiary sense; "håller" is kept as "holds", preserving the dry, load-bearing metaphor that gives the line its voice; "underlaget" as "the file" is idiomatic for the documentation before a committee and is consistent with "the documents" and "the file" used elsewhere in the draft. The sentence rhythm — short assertion, then the qualifying "But" — survives intact. Not a finding.
- Terms carried across: "kultur- och fritidsnämnden" → "culture and leisure committee"; "tjänsteutlåtandet" → "the staff recommendation" / "the recommendation prepared for the March 3 meeting"; "öppen fritidsgård" → "open youth center"; "uppföljning" → "follow-up"; "besöksräkning vid entrén" → "a count at the entrance"; "enkät" → "questionnaire"; "läsåret 2024/2025" → "the 2024/2025 school year". In this context nothing could fall under one member of any of these pairs and not the other. No meaning is changed in crossing languages outside quoted speech.
