# Judgement — nd1-narrowed-denial (judge j2)

Fixture: `docs/evaluation/editorial-379/fixtures/nd1-narrowed-denial.md`
Reports judged: A, B, C, D, E, F (gate round, nd1).
Focus passage: "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health."

## 1. My own reading of the focus passage

The material says, flatly and without qualification: "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa." The draft
says: "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health." The draft's denial is narrower
than the material's, and it is *entailed* by it: a questionnaire that asks about no health at all asks about
no mental health in particular. No case compatible with the supplied material makes the material's sentence
true and the draft's false. The contrary case — a questionnaire silent on mental health but asking about
health in some other respect — is excluded twice over: once by the supplied denial itself, and again by the
supplied enumeration of what the questionnaire did ask ("vad de gjorde på gården, hur de tog sig dit och om
de ville att gården skulle vara öppen fler kvällar"), which contains no health item of any kind.

So the passage asserts nothing the material does not carry. The one serious argument on the other side,
made in different words by D, E and F, is pragmatic rather than logical: a reader meeting a denial pitched
at *mental* health may infer by contrast that health was on the questionnaire in some other form, and that
inference is false to the material. I weigh that argument and do not accept it here, for two reasons. First,
the salient alternative in this paragraph is not "health at large" but the recommendation's own phrase,
"motverkar psykisk ohälsa bland unga i Nyhamn" — the sentence is placed to rebut that motive precisely, and
a denial cut to the shape of the claim it rebuts reads as targeting, not as conceding. Second, the draft
itself defeats the inference two paragraphs later by listing all three questions the questionnaire asked,
none of them about health.

What remains is a real editorial observation — the wider denial is available, costs one word, and is the
stronger sentence — but it is a strengthening available to the author, not a repair owed before delivery.
The material supports the passage as written.

**Verdict: no finding is warranted against the focus passage.**

Two other passages recur across the reports and are classed the same way wherever they appear:

- **"decides on March 3"** (draft: "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the
  open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent"). The material gives "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens
  möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas" and the boundary "Slutet ska namnge nämndens **möjliga**
  beslut". It fixes the meeting date, the deciding body and the proposal before it, but does not state that
  the question is settled that day; a deferral is compatible with everything supplied. Against that, an
  ordinary reader of a debate article takes "decides on March 3 whether X" to mean the question is up for
  decision at that meeting, which is exactly what a staff report prepared "inför" the meeting establishes.
  I class every finding on it as **disputed**: the disagreement turns on whether that clause asserts an
  outcome on the date or only that the item is up for decision then.
- **"does not know"** (draft: "Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and
  we do not claim to"; material: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att veta hur gården påverkar ungas
  hälsa"). Declining to claim knowledge and lacking it are different states, and the first clause has no
  counterpart in the material. Against that, the sentence is a first-person avowal by the sender about her
  own association — the thing she is the authority on — and the boundaries state that no health effects are
  established at all, so there is nothing in this file to know. **Disputed**; it turns on whether a party's
  statement about its own state of knowledge is a factual assertion needing support or its own standpoint.
