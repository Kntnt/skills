## 5. Findings

### Finding 1 — the March 3 meeting is turned into the decision itself

- **Draft:** "Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent."
- **Source:** "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars föreslår att gården permanentas."
- **What differs:** time and certainty. The material establishes that a staff recommendation stands *ahead of* a committee meeting on 3 March and that it proposes permanence. It does not establish that the committee decides the question at that meeting.
- **Case in which the source statement holds and the draft fails:** at the 3 March meeting the committee takes up the recommendation, sends it back for the evidence Unga Röster says is missing, and decides the permanence question at its next meeting in April. Nothing in the supplied material excludes this; "inför nämndens möte den 3 mars" places the paper before the meeting and says nothing about what the meeting resolves, and the boundaries ask only that the ending "namnge nämndens möjliga beslut" — a possible decision, not a scheduled one.
- **Case in the other direction:** a case where the draft holds and the source fails would need the committee to decide on 3 March with no recommendation before it, which the material's "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför nämndens möte den 3 mars" excludes. The asymmetry is what makes the draft the stronger of the two statements.
- **Why it matters here:** the opening sentence sets the piece's clock, and the whole argument is about what the committee may lean on when it does decide. If the decision slips, the draft's factual frame is wrong while its argument is untouched.
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace "decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent" with "meets on March 3 with a proposal before it to make the open youth center on Hamngatan permanent". Everything after it, including the closing call to act on 3 March, stands unchanged.

### Finding 2 — the denial about the questionnaire is narrowed from health to mental health

- **Draft:** "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health."
- **Source:** "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa."
- **What differs:** the scope of the denial, and with it the state of knowledge the reader is left in. The material denies health as a whole ("hälsa"); the draft denies only the mental part of it. The narrowing is an addition by the draft — the word "mental" has no counterpart in the source sentence — and the draft renders the same Swedish word without narrowing elsewhere, at "how the center affects young people's health" for "hur gården påverkar ungas hälsa".
- **Case in which the source statement holds and the draft, read as an ordinary reader reads it in context, fails:** the questionnaire asks nothing whatever about the respondents' health. That is the actual state of affairs the material supplies. A denial pitched at the narrower term, placed immediately after "The documents contain nothing to support that statement", is read as drawing a contrast — the questionnaire is silent on mental health, whatever it may have asked about health otherwise — and that contrast fails, because there is no "otherwise" in the questionnaire. The three topics the material lists ("vad de gjorde på gården, hur de tog sig dit och om de ville att gården skulle vara öppen fler kvällar") contain no health question of any kind. So what the sentence conveys in this position is false to the material even though the words, taken in isolation, are entailed by it.
- **Case in the other direction:** a questionnaire that asked about sleep, diet or physical health but not about mental health would satisfy the draft while contradicting the source. That case is set aside by the supplied statement "Enkäten frågar inte om deltagarnas hälsa" — which is precisely the sentence the draft has narrowed, and precisely the reason the narrowing gives away ground the author holds.
- **Why it matters here:** the draft's case is that the file contains nothing on health and that the committee should say only what the file will carry. The unnarrowed denial is the stronger and the supported one; the narrowed denial concedes, by implication, a health question that was never asked, and invites the reply that the questionnaire covered health in some other respect. The repair costs one word and strengthens the argument.
- **Smallest supported repair:** delete "mental" — "The questionnaire does not ask about participants' health." The surrounding sentences need no change; the quoted motive keeps "mental ill health", which is correct, because that is what the recommendation says.
