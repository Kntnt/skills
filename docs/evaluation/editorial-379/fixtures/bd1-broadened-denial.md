# Rönnvik should not make Digitalt grundstöd permanent on this evidence

By Milad Aziz, chair of Lyssna Först

Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 to decide whether Digitalt grundstöd becomes a permanent course with six rounds a year. The course should keep running while that decision waits. What should not happen is a permanent commitment made on paperwork that does not answer the question the committee is being asked.

## What the evaluation counted

The municipality's evaluation of February 5, 2026, covers three rounds of the four-week course for job seekers at Rönnvik's adult education service during the fall of 2025. It rests on attendance lists and the course leaders' written summaries. Sixty-two people signed up and forty-eight completed the course. Those are registrations and completions, not individual people: some participants took the course twice. The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge.

Completion is worth knowing. It tells the committee that people who started the course could finish it. It does not tell the committee what those people could do afterward that they could not do before.

## The staff recommendation rests on one number

The recommendation prepared for the April 14 meeting gives the high completion rate as its reason for making the course permanent and expanding it to six rounds a year. The documents contain no comparison with job seekers who did not take the course, and no cost per participant.

That is a thin basis for doubling a course. Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished. It does not say it gave them something they did not already have.

## Ask for the missing figures first

Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one. We are asking the committee to postpone the decision to the autumn budget preparation and, in the meantime, to ask the administration for the cost per round and for an account of how participants found their way to the course.

We are not asking for the course to close. Keep it running. But the committee should postpone the permanence decision until it has the cost per round and knows how participants reached the course, and decide then.
