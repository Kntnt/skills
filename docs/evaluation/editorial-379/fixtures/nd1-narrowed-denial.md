# Keep the Hamngatan youth center, and give a reason that holds

By Elin Sandberg, chair of Unga Röster

Nyhamn's culture and leisure committee decides on March 3 whether the open youth center on Hamngatan becomes permanent. It should. What should not survive the meeting is the reason the staff recommendation gives for it.

## The recommendation gives a reason the file does not carry

The recommendation prepared for the March 3 meeting proposes making the center permanent because the activity "counteracts mental ill health among young people in Nyhamn." The documents contain nothing to support that statement. The questionnaire does not ask about participants' mental health.

That is not a small drafting slip. It is the sentence that would be quoted back at the committee the first time somebody disagreed with it.

## What the follow-up does show

The municipality's follow-up of January 9, 2026, covers a trial during the 2024/2025 school year. It rests on a count at the entrance and a questionnaire handed out on site over three evenings in May 2025. The school year recorded 3,240 visits. Those are visits, not individual young people. Seventy-one young people answered the questionnaire, which asked what they did at the center, how they got there, and whether they wanted it open more evenings.

That is a real basis for a decision. It says the center is used. It says something about how it is used, and about what the young people using it want next.

## Write the reason the evidence supports

Unga Röster does not know how the center affects young people's health, and we do not claim to. We are asking the committee to make the center permanent and to rewrite the justification so that it rests on the visit figures and the questionnaire answers.

The center is needed. But a justification that cannot be supported holds only until somebody reads the file. A committee that gives a reason its own documents do not carry invites the next reader to ask what else in the file was written the same way.

The committee should make the Hamngatan center permanent on March 3 and adopt a justification drawn from the visit count and the questionnaire answers.
