## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — the denial is broadened from prior knowledge to knowledge (source support)

- **Draft:** "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge."
- **Source:** "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper."
- **What differs:** the thing measured, and with it the scope of the denial. The source denies measurement of one thing, what participants knew before the course. The draft denies measurement of participants' knowledge without restriction, which takes in what they knew after it as well. The draft therefore asserts more than the material states about the evaluation's content.
- **Test, source holds and draft fails:** the course leaders' written summaries — one of the two bases the evaluation rests on, per "Utvärderingen bygger på närvarolistor och kursledarnas skriftliga sammanfattningar" — record for each round what the participants could do by the end of it, while nobody recorded anything about what they arrived knowing. On that state of affairs "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper" is true and "The evaluation does not measure participants' knowledge" is false. The case is compatible with everything supplied: the material names the two bases of the evaluation but never says what the summaries contain, and it denies measurement only of förkunskaper.
- **Test, draft holds and source fails:** none available; the broader denial entails the narrower one.
- **Can the case be set aside?** Only by a supplied statement excluding it. There is none. "Den mäter inte deltagarnas förkunskaper" is the sole statement about what the evaluation does not measure, and it is the narrow one. The boundary "Inga effektstudier, jämförelsegrupper, kostnadssiffror eller klagomål är belagda" says no effect studies are established; it says nothing about what the course leaders' summaries describe, and a summary of what participants could do at the end is not an effect study.
- **Why it matters beyond wording:** the broadened denial is doing argumentative work. It stands immediately before "It does not tell the committee what those people could do afterward that they could not do before", and a reader takes the second sentence to follow from the first. The narrow, supported denial supports that conclusion on its own — with no record of the starting point, no gain can be shown whatever was measured at the end — so the argument loses nothing by being corrected.
- **Smallest supported repair:** insert the missing restriction.
  > The evaluation does not measure participants' prior knowledge.

  Nothing else in the draft needs to change. The following paragraph continues to stand on the repaired sentence.

### Finding 2 — "doubling" asserts a ratio the material does not supply (source support)

- **Draft:** "That is a thin basis for doubling a course."
- **Source:** "föreslår att kursen permanentas och byggs ut till sex omgångar per år"; the only count of rounds anywhere in the material is "gäller tre kursomgångar vid vuxenutbildningen i Rönnvik under hösten 2025".
- **What differs:** the thing measured, and the time scope over which it is measured. "Doubling" asserts that the proposal takes the course to twice its present annual size, which requires a present figure of three rounds a year. The material gives three rounds in a single autumn term, and gives that figure as the coverage of the evaluation rather than as the course's annual output. The proposal's own wording, "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år", establishes only that the present number is fewer than six a year.
- **Test, source holds and draft fails:** the course ran the three autumn 2025 rounds the evaluation covers and two further rounds in the spring of that year, five in the year. The proposal to move to six a year is then an increase of one round, not a doubling: "byggs ut till sex omgångar per år" is still true and "doubling a course" is false. The case need only be compatible with the material, and it is — the material states what the evaluation covers, never what the course's yearly schedule is, and an expansion to six is consistent with any present figure below six.
- **Test, draft holds and source fails:** none; if the present rate is three a year, both hold.
- **Can the case be set aside?** Not by any supplied statement. "gäller tre kursomgångar ... under hösten 2025" scopes the three rounds to the evaluation and to one term; it does not fix the annual total, and it is the only count supplied.
- **Note on what is not at issue:** "That is a thin basis" is the author's own evaluation of the absences he has just listed, and the brief licenses a sharp criticism of the decision basis. It needs no external support and should be preserved. Only the arithmetic characterisation is unsupported.
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace the unsupported ratio with the expansion the documents actually propose.
  > That is a thin basis for expanding a course to six rounds a year.

  The sentence keeps its force and its position; only the multiplier goes.

## 5. Passages examined and not reported as findings

Recorded so the accounting is complete and so a later reader can see what was weighed rather than missed.

**"It tells the committee that people who started the course could finish it."** Set against "Att fyrtioåtta personer gick klart en kurs säger att kursen gick att gå klart." Read in isolation as a generalisation about everyone who started, the sentence would overreach, since fourteen registrations did not end in completion and the material never says why. Read as an ordinary reader would in its context, it does not generalise: the sentence two lines above has just given the reader both figures, "Sixty-two people signed up and forty-eight completed the course", and the following sentence refers back to "those people", the completers. In that setting the clause states what the completions show, which is the source's point, and the draft restates the same point in the source's own precise form later — "Forty-eight people finishing a course says the course could be finished." Defensible as written; reported here as an observation rather than a defect.

**"Lyssna Först has no measurement of the course's effect, and we do not claim one."** Set against "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha någon egen mätning av kursens effekt." The source states the absence of a claim; the draft's first clause states the absence of the measurement itself, and its second clause restores the source's claim-form. Strictly these come apart — an association could possess a measurement and decline to invoke it — but the statement is the chair's own disclaimer about his own association, made in his own voice in a text the brief assigns to him, and the draft's second clause preserves the source's formulation intact. The effect on the reader is the source's effect: Lyssna Först rests nothing on a measurement of its own. Defensible as written.

**"Ask for the missing figures first."** A section label. Of the two things requested, the cost per round is a figure and the account of how participants found the course is not. The body states both items exactly, and the heading orients rather than asserts. Defensible as written.

**"Rönnvik's education committee meets on April 14 to decide whether ..."** The material describes the utlåtande as prepared "inför utbildningsnämndens möte den 14 april" and has the association proposing "att nämnden skjuter upp beslutet", which presupposes a decision before that committee. No year is attached to the date in the source; the draft supplies none either, and the surrounding dates place it after February 5, 2026. Defensible as written.

**Absence of an imputed motive.** The boundary "Författaren ska kunna kritisera beslutsunderlaget skarpt utan att tillskriva förvaltningen motiv som inte finns i handlingarna" was checked against every passage about the administration and the utlåtande. The draft criticises what the documents contain and omit, and reports only the motive the material states. No finding.
