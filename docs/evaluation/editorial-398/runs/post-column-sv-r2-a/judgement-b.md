# Judgement b

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the text in the fenced block of `response.md`. There are two differences. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and every other sentence are identical.

1. Headline. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar frågan om varför vi träffas`. Class: a **change to what a claim says** by **meaning**. The old headline says that one box is missing. It does not name the box, and it sets up the "ruta" motif that the last sentence resolves ("tvivlet på ännu en ruta"). The new headline says that the template lacks a *question*, "why we meet". The body supports this reading: paragraph 1 says "Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om", paragraph 2 says "Den säger inte varför vi behöver göra det tillsammans", and paragraph 4 says "varför vi behöver just varandras tid". So the new claim is not invented. It is still a different claim from the one the headline made. It moves the headline from "box" to "question", from the decision box of paragraph 1 to "why we meet", and from teaser to thesis. I found no visible defect in the old headline. It is short, idiomatic and tied to the text. Replacing it is also a **change of taste**, justified in the reply by informativeness and character counts.
2. Paragraph 3 (body paragraph "Missförstå mig rätt …"). Before: `något annat — att tid` (spaced em dash, U+2014). After: `något annat – att tid` (spaced en dash, U+2013). Class: a **mechanical correction** (Swedish locale form: tankstreck is a spaced en dash). It does not follow from any other difference.

Of the two differences, one changes what a claim says.

Only the dash character changed.

The reply also says: "Brödtexten, bylinen och frontmattern är i övrigt oförändrade" and "Inga andra påståenden har tagits bort, ändrats eller lagts till." The diff confirms both statements. The three unresolved findings (ingress, subheadings, reader call to action) are reported as unresolved, and none of them was applied to the text.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the headline change: `# Rutan som inte finns` became `# Mötesmallen saknar frågan om varför vi träffas`.

The body, quotations, arguments and every limiting sentence are preserved, and the account is honest and complete. But the one budgeted correction rewrote a working, defect-free column headline on grounds of taste and length ("20 tecken, 4 ord" against "46 tecken och 7 ord"; "kunde ha stått över en helt annan text"). That changed what the headline claims (meaning) and dropped the "ruta" motif that the closing sentence pays off. R1 says a clean text "may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines". A deliberately allusive headline is working voice, not a concrete visible defect.

The en-dash change is a legitimate mechanical correction.
