# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and every body sentence are identical except for the two differences below.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar frågan om varför vi träffas`. Class: **a change of taste** and **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**. The old headline names an absent *box* ("ruta"). That image ties the column together, because the last sentence echoes it with "tvivlet på ännu en ruta". The new headline swaps the image for a thesis about a missing *question of why we meet*. That thesis paraphrases paragraph 4 ("varför vi behöver just varandras tid") more closely than paragraph 1, and paragraph 1 names the missing box as the one for "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta". So the headline no longer says the same thing, even though the body supports each half of the new wording. It does not harden the text: the body already makes the new headline's claim. It does not add scope or certainty to any claim in the body, and the body's hedges stay as they were.
2. **Dash in paragraph 3.** Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid` (spaced em dash). After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid` (spaced en dash). Class: **mechanical correction (a locale form)**. Swedish sets the tankstreck as a spaced en dash. Neither difference depends on the other.

Total: 2 differences. 1 of them is a change to what a claim says (meaning).

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is the headline rewrite, together with the finding that justifies it: "Rubriken sa inte krönikans vinkel. ”Rutan som inte finns” (20 tecken, 4 ord) kunde inte förstås utan texten … den kunde ha stått över en helt annan text."

The genre is `column`. In a column, a figurative headline that the text pays off is part of the working voice, and this one is paid off: "ruta" runs through paragraphs 1, 5 and 6 and closes the piece. So the headline is not a concrete visible defect: nothing in it is wrong, broken or contradicted. The rewrite applies an article-anatomy guideline, argued partly through character and word counts. That is a taste or numerical-guideline rewrite of a clean part of the text, and R1 forbids it.

It also changes what the headline says (meaning): "a box that does not exist" becomes "the template lacks the question of why we meet".

It left the missing ingress, subheads and reader prompt as reported findings rather than writing them in, and it did not claim to verify against any source.
