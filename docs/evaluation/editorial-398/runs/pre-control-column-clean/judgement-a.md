## 1. Differences

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom varför` (44 characters). After: `# Mötesmallen frågar när men inte varför vi träffas` (49 characters). Class: **a change to what a claim says (scope, meaning)**. The original is an ironic overstatement: a box for everything except *why*. The new headline narrows it to a literal *asks when but not why*. It also drops the word "ruta", which the text repeats in "Det är inget fel på rutorna" and "Ännu en ruta, och ändå vill jag prova". The original headline is not a visible defect, so this is also a change of taste, not a repair.
2. **New section break.** Before: the last paragraph ("Prova ändå frågan nästa gång du bokar ett möte …") followed directly on the paragraph "Frågan jag vill lägga till är enkel …" in the section "Ännu en ruta, och ändå vill jag prova". After: a new subheading `## Vad en extra rad ger får ett försök visa` (40 characters) goes before that last paragraph, so the text has three sections, not two. Class: **a structural change of taste** that the reply presents as the repair of an anatomy defect. The section it split had two paragraphs, as did the first section. After the split, the second and third sections have one paragraph each.

No mechanical corrections were made. The reply says the proofreading pass found nothing, and the diff confirms that.
