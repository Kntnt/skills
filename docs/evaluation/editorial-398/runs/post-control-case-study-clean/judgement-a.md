## 1. Differences

There is no frontmatter in either text. The byline, the section order, the bold standfirst, the quotation dashes, the link and every paragraph break are unchanged. The only formatting difference is that the reply puts the returned text inside a fenced `markdown` block, which wraps the text and does not change it. There are five textual differences:

1. **Headline.** Before: "Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena" (36 characters). After: "Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta i gemensam ärendebild" (54 characters). Class: a change of taste that also changes the claim. The **meaning** changes from something the organisation did (it gathered its repair cases) to a benefit appraisal. The **attribution** moves from Elm Quay to its supervisor. The new headline claims a benefit and leaves out Lind's reservation about preparation time.
2. **Standfirst, sentence 2.** Before: "Arbetsledaren Maya Lind vill göra om försöket, men skulle avsätta mer tid för förberedelser." After: "Arbetsledaren Maya Lind skulle göra försöket igen, men avsätta mer tid för förberedelser." Class: a change to a claim in **certainty**. "Vill" (wants to) becomes the conditional "skulle" (would), which matches her quote "Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen." At most this repairs a slight overstatement against the quote. The control treats the original as a sound qualified appraisal.
3. **Standfirst, sentence 3.** Before: "vad gruppen gjorde, vad anteckningarna visar". After: "vad underhållsgruppen gjorde, vad gruppens egna anteckningar visar". Class: a change of taste presented as a stand-alone repair. The change of "anteckningarna" to "gruppens egna anteckningar" is a small **attribution** change. It agrees with the lead ("gruppens egna anteckningar") and with the body's "Elm Quays interna försöksanteckning".
4. **Lead, sentence 1.** Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." After: "Med sin gemensamma reparationslogg ville underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing att alla skift skulle se samma information." Class: a change of taste (word order and idiom). One could defend it as repairing an English-shaped pseudo-cleft and the calque "över skiftgränserna". The meaning is the same: the sentence still states a goal, not an outcome.
5. **Last subheading.** Before: "Kunden vill ge förberedelserna mer tid" (38 characters). After: "Maya Lind ser tillbaka på försöket" (34 characters). Class: a change of taste that removes a claim from the heading (**meaning**). The claim ("vill", which is sharper than her "skulle") is no longer in the heading, and the quote below still carries it. The heading now names a person instead of "Kunden".

For reference only: the standfirst goes from 40 words to 41 and the headline from 36 characters to 54. The reply says it measured lengths with a script and that the text conforms. I do not know the limits and do not rule on them.

## 4. R1

**Fail.** The deciding passage is finding 1 and its repair. The run rewrote the conforming headline "Elm Quay samlade reparationsärendena" into "Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta i gemensam ärendebild" because "Rubriken sa bara vad kunden gjorde, inte vad det gav". It also adds 18 characters to a headline that met its limit. The last subheading and the standfirst were reworked in the same way on a text the corpus defines as conforming. Every change is reported honestly. The numbers, the disclosure, the link, the byline and the quotations all survive. None of the named mistakes was made. But R1 does not allow rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste or genre guidance, and this run rewrote five places in one.
