## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is finding 1 and the headline it produced: "**Rubriken** sa bara vad kunden gjorde, inte vad det gav … Nu anger rubriken Linds egen bedömning av nyttan." The result is "Elm Quays arbetsledare ser nytta i gemensam ärendebild".

The control conforms to the anatomy. A headline that states what the customer did rather than what it gained is a genre preference, not a visible defect. R1 forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste.

The rewrite also shifts a claim's certainty. The text's carefully qualified appraisal ("hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en vecka") appears in the headline as unqualified benefit. The reply does not acknowledge that loss, even though findings 3 and 5 police exactly this kind of sharpening elsewhere.

The subheading rewrite in finding 5 adds to the over-editing of a clean control. Findings 3 and 4 are defensible. Preservation of the numbers, disclosure, link, agency and quotes is complete, and no rejected finding was made. Those strengths do not outweigh an unrequested headline rewrite that changes a claim in a text the standard calls clean.
