## 1. Differences

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar ett varför`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning, with a narrowing of scope.** The original headline is figurative: a box that does not exist. The body picks this up twice, first as the missing decision box (para. 1) and then as the author's doubt about adding "ännu en ruta" (last line). The new headline turns this into a plain thesis. It names the object (the meeting template) and changes what is missing from "a box" to "a why". The claim does not become more certain. It comes from the body's own lines "Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om" and "Den säger inte varför vi behöver göra det tillsammans", so it adds no fact the text lacks. But the headline now says something different from what it said before, and the double sense is lost: the missing box is also the box the author doubts she should add.
2. **Dash, para. 3.** Before: `Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid i kalendern…`. After: `Det som retar mig är något annat – att tid i kalendern…`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form).** An em dash becomes the spaced en dash used in Swedish. The spaces were already there, so only the character changed. This change does not follow from difference 1.

Total: 2 differences. 1 is a change to what a claim says (meaning, with scope).
