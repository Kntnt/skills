# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the text inside the fenced block in `response.md`. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline and all six body paragraphs are identical except as listed. I found two differences.

1. Headline. Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar ett varför`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**, and a change of taste. The old headline names one absent thing, the box the first paragraph describes ("Någon ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta ... finns inte"). The new headline states a verdict about something else: the template as a whole lacks a "why". The body supports that reading (paragraph 1: "Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om"; paragraph 2: "Den säger inte varför"; paragraph 4: "formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), so the new claim is not unsupported. But what the headline says has changed. The rewrite also breaks a frame the text built on purpose: the "ruta" motif opens in the headline and closes in the last sentence ("tvivlet på ännu en ruta"). You could also argue for scope, since the claim widens from one missing box to a missing purpose, but meaning is the main element.
2. Paragraph 3, the dash. Before: `något annat — att tid` (a spaced em dash). After: `något annat – att tid` (a spaced en dash). Class: **mechanical correction (a locale form)**. Swedish uses the spaced en dash as its tankstreck. The spaces were already in the input, so only the character changed. This difference does not depend on the first.

None of the differences is a change to formatting or to the frontmatter.

## 4. R1

**Fail**, on a narrow margin. The Skill kept every argument, quotation, limiting sentence and claim in the body, and did not write the lede or sections it found missing. The deciding passage is the headline rewrite, `# Rutan som inte finns` to `# Mötesmallen saknar ett varför`, which spent the whole budget of one correction.

The defect the Skill names ("nämnde varken möten, mallen eller vad som saknas") is not a concrete visible defect in this text. It is a guideline that a headline should stand alone in a feed, applied to a column whose allusive headline is working voice. That headline is tied to the body: "Rutan" is the missing decision box of paragraph 1, and the motif closes in the final sentence, "tvivlet på ännu en ruta". The rewrite breaks that frame and replaces the author's ambivalent image with a thesis verdict. That is a change to what the headline claim says, by meaning. R1 says "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines," and that clause decides the verdict. The transparent reporting in finding 3 and under Påståenden counts in the Skill's favour, but it does not make the change legitimate.
