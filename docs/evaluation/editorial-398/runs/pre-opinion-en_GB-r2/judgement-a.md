7. Same paragraph. Before: "remove, change or keep a channel". After: "remove, change or keep a route". Class: a change of taste. It follows difference 4 (the same term-consistency change).
8. Same paragraph. Before: "before the channel goes". After: "before the route goes". Class: a change of taste. It follows difference 4.

In total there are eight differences. One is a change to what a claim says (difference 5: meaning and scope), one repairs a visible defect (difference 6), and six are changes of taste. There are no mechanical corrections.
