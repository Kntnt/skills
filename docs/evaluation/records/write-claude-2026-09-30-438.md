## Run conditions

Fourteen `Write` runs in two arms of seven, over the three rows [`../editorial-438/plan.md`](../editorial-438/plan.md) froze before the first run: `case-question-en_GB` three times, `case-study-en_US` twice and `case-study-sv` twice. Arm `pre` is the collection at `2afeb95e`, unchanged; arm `post` is the candidate committed as `2eca70b3`. It is a control and not a reproduction: the ticket makes shipping conditional on two totals not being higher than the pre-change arm's, and the plan says so. The measurement, the candidate and the ticket's conclusion are in [`../editorial-438/results.md`](../editorial-438/results.md); every packet, with its two judgements, is under [`../editorial-438/runs/`](../editorial-438/runs/).

**How a run was made.** Each run is one top-level `claude --print` session started by [`../editorial-388/harness/staged_run.py`](../editorial-388/harness/staged_run.py) with `--model=claude-opus-5-5 --effort=high`, which stages `skills/editorial/{write,redline,proofread,unslop}` and `skills/kntnt` from the arm's commit with `git archive` into a private root with its own `HOME`, types the Formal Invocation and nothing else, keeps the whole Harness trace and before-and-after inventories of that root in the packet, and removes the root on exit. The invocation names the installed genre, `casestudy`. The three `case-question-en_GB` runs of an arm went one after another in one lane and the four control runs in another, so no two runs on one input overlapped; the `post` arm started after the last `pre` run had finished.

**Judging.** Two judges per draft, blind to the arm, the plan, the hypothesis and the model, under [`../editorial-396/write-judge-brief.md`](../editorial-396/write-judge-brief.md), used as it stands. Each judge read only `work/source.md`, `response.md` and `delivered.md`, the last being the draft as the reply carries it with Write's fence and delivery account removed. The plan's two counts are per-arm totals: every draft's bridges classed (a), and every subheading over a quotation counted once as a pre-echo where either judge classes it so. Both are 0 in both arms, so no judge split on either.

**Side effects.** Read from each packet's `filesystem-changes.json`, which compares the whole private root before and after: no file was removed or changed in any run, and no run changed `work/` or the staged Skills. What each run created is the Harness's own configuration, caches and transcripts under the run's `HOME`, and the root holding all of it was removed (`cleanup.json`). One run left a file of its own: `post/case-study-sv-r2` created `scratch/tmp/write-NHfb/draft.md` and `report.md` and could not remove them, because the Harness stopped its removal command; its reply says so under *Städning misslyckades* and names both files. It is recorded here and not filed: Write reported it, the private root was removed at the end of the run, and the other thirteen runs removed their own scratch. Nothing outside the root is claimed either way: the runner isolates rather than sandboxes, and the working copy was not inventoried.

**`T1` and `R2`** are not criteria here and are recorded `skipped`. Every packet keeps its complete trace, so both can be judged from it later.

**Findings owned elsewhere.** The byline, which every draft carries (`By Thomas`, `Av Thomas` or `Av Thomas Barregren`) against a brief that supplies no author, is recorded and not filed, as the #364 and #396 records did. A true finding delivered disclosed rather than repaired at the final comparison is the shape `editorial-364` assigned to [#376](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/376); it is recorded under that number.
