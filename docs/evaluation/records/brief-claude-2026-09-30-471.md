The criteria and the per-run table are in [`../editorial-471/results.md`](../editorial-471/results.md); each packet, with its two judgements, is under [`../editorial-471/runs/`](../editorial-471/runs/).

**Judging.** Two blind judges per run under [`../editorial-471/brief-judge-brief.md`](../editorial-471/brief-judge-brief.md), each reading only `conversation.md`, `brief.md` and the run's material. A criterion is met only where both pass it. `T1` and `O1` were read by this session from `trace-index.json` and `filesystem-changes.json`; `G1` and `G2` were checked against the repository, and every judge scored them `skipped`. The editorial-quality matrix's criteria are not this evaluation's and are not scored.

**Side effects.** In every run `filesystem-changes.json` shows only `work/brief.md` created outside the Harness's own files under the run's `HOME`, the material unchanged, and the private root removed (`cleanup.json`).
