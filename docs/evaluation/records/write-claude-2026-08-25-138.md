That is a real defect and it is written down here rather than absorbed; it belongs in a ticket of its own against Write, filed by whoever integrates this work, and it is not something #138 either fixed or claimed to.

Here, no run reported anything of the kind: each said what it had not written and what it would need, which is what makes the two residual findings above findable at all.
