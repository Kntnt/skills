## 3. Findings (source support)

Ranked most severe first. Each states the support that is missing or changed and the smallest supported repair.

### Finding 1 — L24, heading: "Ingen har mätt vad det dubbla arbetet kostar" (scope)

*Category: scope.*

- **Draft:** "## Ingen har mätt vad det dubbla arbetet kostar"
- **Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna."

**What differs:** scope. The material's claim is about one document set — the handlingar before the 18 June meeting. The heading makes it a claim about everyone, everywhere, at any time: nobody has measured it.

**Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails:** the förvaltning ran an internal time study of double entry in 2025, decided it was too thin to publish, and left it out of the handlingar. Then "Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna" is true while "Ingen har mätt vad det dubbla arbetet kostar" is false. Nothing supplied excludes this case: the material scopes the absence explicitly with "i handlingarna", and never says no measurement exists anywhere. The converse direction also separates them: if nobody anywhere had measured it, the documents would necessarily lack it, so the heading is strictly the stronger claim.

**Why it is a defect and not a preference:** the contract requires that the boundary holds "in the title, summary, headings and body alike" and that scope and qualifications are preserved "wherever a claim appears". The draft's own body (L28) states the correctly scoped version, so the heading is out of step with the article it heads.

**Smallest supported repair:** `## Handlingarna saknar mätning av vad det dubbla arbetet kostar`

### Finding 2 — L34: "Vi har inte kostnadsberäknat försöket" (state of knowledge)

*Category: state-of-knowledge.*

- **Draft:** "Vi har inte kostnadsberäknat försöket och gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat det."
- **Source:** "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."

**What differs:** modality and whose assertion. The material records what the association does *not claim*; the draft converts the costing half into an assertion about what it has *not done*. The financing half is carried over correctly, which makes the asymmetry visible within the sentence itself.

**Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails:** the association's treasurer made a rough internal costing of the six-month trial but the association chose not to put any figure into the debate, because it holds that the cost is the kommunstyrelse's to weigh. Then "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha … kostnadsberäknat försöket" is true while "Vi har inte kostnadsberäknat försöket" is false. Nothing supplied excludes this: "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" assigns the decision, not the arithmetic, and no supplied sentence says the association has done no costing.

**Why it is a defect and not a preference:** the contract states that "what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened". The material's "gör inget anspråk på" is precisely an unclaimed, not a negative fact, and the draft turns it into one.

**Smallest supported repair:** "Vi gör inget anspråk på att ha kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket."

### Finding 3 — L10, standfirst: "siffror på vad varje bokningsväg kostar" (the thing measured)

*Category: thing-measured.*

- **Draft:** "Öppna beslut vill se ett halvår med både telefon och webb, och siffror på vad varje bokningsväg kostar."
- **Source:** "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg och be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb." / "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till."

**What differs:** the thing measured, and whose task it is. What the association asks the trial to produce is time per booking route; cost is what the material hands to the kommunstyrelse to take a position on. Time becomes cost only through rate and overhead assumptions the association is nowhere said to supply.

**Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails:** during the trial the förvaltning records six minutes per phone booking and one minute per web booking, attaches no krona figure to either, and the association regards its proposal as fulfilled. The supplied proposal is then carried out in full while no "siffror på vad varje bokningsväg kostar" exist. Nothing supplied excludes this case; the proposal names only "tidsåtgång per bokningsväg". Tested the other way: a costing drawn from invoices would give per-route cost figures without any time registration, so the two are not the same measurement.

**Why it is a defect and not a preference:** the draft's own body keeps the distinction carefully — L28 asks for a tidsmätning, L34 says the cost "måste kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" — so the standfirst states a demand the article does not make and shifts the cost question away from the body the material assigns it to. The boundary "med kostnadsosäkerheten bevarad" is at stake. "Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna" supports the article's argument about work cost, but it does not make cost figures per booking route part of the association's proposal.

**Smallest supported repair:** "…, och siffror på hur lång tid varje bokningsväg tar."

### Finding 4 — L18: "Rapporten är tydlig med vad siffrorna är" (added property of the document)

*Category: attribution.*

- **Draft:** "Rapporten är tydlig med vad siffrorna är: bokningar, inte unika personer."
- **Source:** "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer."

**What differs:** whose assertion. The material states what the report counts; the draft additionally states that the report itself is explicit about it — a property of the document's disclosure, and a mild credit to it in a passage that otherwise criticises the beslutsunderlag.

**Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails:** the report tabulates "Bokningar: webb 96, telefon 24" under a methodology section that never addresses the booking/person distinction, and a reader has to work the limitation out. Then the report still "räknar bokningar, inte unika personer" while it is not "tydlig med vad siffrorna är". Nothing supplied excludes this: the material describes what the report counts and what it does not measure, never what it discloses about its own unit.

**Why it is a defect and not a preference:** circumstantial and evaluative characterisation needs support like any other claim. The substance after the colon is fully supported; only the clause before it is not.

**Smallest supported repair:** "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer."
