## 3. Considered and not reported

These were tested and found defensible as written; they are recorded so the judgement is visible, not raised as findings.

**3.1 "when the air dropped below a chosen limit" (lead, #2).** A case can be imagined in which the air in a room is generally above 20 while the sensor by the exterior wall reads 19.8, so that "a reading fell below the limit" holds and "the air [of the room] dropped below the limit" fails. But the subject of the draft sentence is "Temperature sensors in six classrooms … can tell", so an ordinary reader takes "the air" to be the air those sensors measured, and the draft states the point explicitly two sections later ("the temperature of the air at the point where it is mounted"). No room-wide claim is made.

**3.2 "A sensor reports the temperature of the air" (#18).** As a generic about sensors in general this would fail for an instrument measuring operative temperature, which the material presents as a measurable quantity ("Operativ temperatur är ett annat mått"). Read in its context — a paragraph about this trial's *temperaturgivare*, immediately contrasted with operative temperature — the generic is about the trial's sensors, and "försöket mätte bara lufttemperatur" supports it.

**3.3 "with the sensors placed as before" (#35).** The definite article could be read as the same physical units, where "likadant placerade givare" specifies only the manner of placement. The sentence's force is on the placement, and the material names no replacement, so no case was pressed.

**3.4 "could have hidden variation within lessons" (#27).** The shift from the source's general present ("kan dölja") to a past counterfactual about this trial was tested: a dataset flat across lesson time would defeat the specific claim while leaving the general one standing. The trial's own data are not flat — 14 of 120 periods contain a sub-limit reading and the rest do not — so no compatible case survives.

**3.5 Length.** The brief gives "Cirka 450 ord … orientering, inte utfyllnadsmål". Length is a composition matter; nothing in the draft is invented to reach it, which is the only aspect of a stated length this comparison covers.

## 4. Findings

Three, all source-support. None is a translation finding.

### F1 — "The report makes no claim about any legal requirement." (source support)

**Draft:** "Twenty degrees was the office's own working limit for the trial. The report makes no claim about any legal requirement."

**Source:** "Gränsen 20 grader var kontorets egen arbetsgräns för försöket, inte ett påstående om ett rättsligt krav."

**What is changed:** subject and scope. The Swedish denies that *the 20-degree limit* was an assertion about a legal requirement. The draft breaks the qualification off into its own sentence and re-attaches it to *the report as a whole*, turning a statement about one threshold into a statement about everything the document says.

**Test, both directions.** A case compatible with the material: the report's background section notes that a statutory minimum of 18 degrees applies to school premises, while the 20-degree figure used in the trial remains the office's own working limit. Under that case the source statement holds in full and the draft's sentence fails. Nothing supplied excludes it — the material enumerates several things the report does not say ("Den säger inte hur länge…", "Den säger heller inte om eleverna frös") and legal requirements are not among them, and the judging note "Ingen … lagregel följer av materialet" constrains what the *article* may infer, not what the *report* contains. In the other direction, a report that mentions no legal requirement anywhere satisfies both statements, so the draft is strictly the stronger claim.

**Smallest supported repair:** fold the qualification back onto the limit, as the source has it —

> Twenty degrees was the office's own working limit for the trial, not a claim about a legal requirement.

### F2 — "Each sensor logged a value every five minutes" (source support)

**Draft:** "Each sensor logged a value every five minutes for the whole period."

**Source:** "Värden registrerades var femte minut under fyra veckor."

**What is changed:** subject and distribution. The Swedish is an impersonal passive about the values: readings were recorded at five-minute intervals. The draft distributes that interval over each of the six sensors individually, which is a claim about the sampling rate per sensor that the material does not make.

**Test, both directions.** A case compatible with the material: a single logger polls the six sensors in rotation, writing one value every five minutes, so each sensor contributes a value every thirty minutes. "Värden registrerades var femte minut" holds exactly; "Each sensor logged a value every five minutes" fails. No supplied statement excludes it — the material describes the placement of the sensors and the recording interval but never the per-sensor rate. In the other direction, six sensors each logging every five minutes also satisfies the source sentence, so the draft is again the stronger claim. That the distributive reading is the likelier engineering arrangement does not supply the support.

**Smallest supported repair:** restore the source's subject —

> Values were logged every five minutes for the whole period.

### F3 — "the trial never compared them against one another" (source support)

**Draft:** "Two stood near exterior walls and four on interior walls; the placements were documented, but the trial never compared them against one another."

**Source:** "Givarnas placering dokumenterades men jämfördes inte experimentellt."

**What is changed:** a qualification that governs the negated clause is dropped. "Experimentellt" limits what did not happen: no experiment was set up to compare the placements. The draft's "never compared them against one another" negates comparison of any kind, and moves the subject of the negation from the placements to the trial.

**Test, both directions.** A case compatible with the material: after the four weeks, the office reads off the series and notes on paper that the two exterior-wall sensors sat lower than the four interior ones — a comparison, but not an experimental one, since nothing was varied or controlled and the placements were never assigned to test the difference. "Jämfördes inte experimentellt" holds; "the trial never compared them against one another" fails. No supplied statement excludes such a reading-off; the material says only that the placements were documented. In the other direction, a trial in which no comparison of any kind occurred satisfies both, so the draft again claims more than the source.

**Smallest supported repair:** carry the modifier across —

> … the placements were documented, but they were never compared experimentally.
