# Source-support comparison — article-pac draft against its brief and material

Target language en_GB; source language of brief and material sv. The whole draft was compared, including frontmatter, title, lead, byline, four section headings and body. Source passages are quoted in Swedish, in the material's own words. The byline "By Thomas Barregren" is excluded per the supplied context.

Verdict: **3 source-support findings**, all small and all repairable in a few words. **0 translation findings.** 1 editorial question reported without a repair.

---

## 1. Claim accounting

Each entry gives the draft passage, the source passage it rests on, and what differs. "Supported" means no case compatible with the material makes the source statement hold and the draft's fail.

### Title and lead

**1.1** — "A four-week trial shows when to look closer"
Source: "Rapporten ... anger att 14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius."; "Gränsen 20 grader var kontorets egen arbetsgräns för försöket"; Rask: "Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare."
Differs: whose assertion. The title states in the article's voice what Rask states in hers. **Supported anyway**, independently of Rask: the office set 20 °C as its own working limit, and the report identifies the periods that breached it, so "which periods to look at" follows from the office's own limit plus the report. Modality "shows" is proportionate to a report that states the count. No finding.

**1.2** — "Temperature sensors in six classrooms can tell a property manager when the air dropped below a chosen limit"
Source: "sex klassrum i Björkskolan"; "Värden registrerades var femte minut"; "innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius"; "En givare mäter temperaturen där den sitter."; "Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen."
Differs: **the thing measured, and scope.** The source's subject is the temperature at the point where a sensor sits, sampled at five-minute intervals; the draft's subject is "the air" — the room's air — and "when ... dropped below" asserts a crossing, where the material supports only which sampled readings were below. **Finding F1.**

**1.3** — "and nothing about why anyone felt cold"
Source: "Den säger heller inte om eleverna frös."; "Ventilation, luftdrag och upplevd temperatur mättes inte."; Rask: "Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då."; forbidden: "orsak till kyla".
Differs: "why anyone felt cold" carries a weak presupposition that somebody did, supported by "klagomål på kall luft"; the negative-polarity "anyone" does not assert it of the pupils, which the report explicitly does not establish. Supported.

**1.4** — "This is what a four-week trial at one school established, what it did not measure, and what to record before the controls are changed."
Source: "under fyra veckor"; "Björkskolan"; "Ventilation, luftdrag och upplevd temperatur mättes inte."; "Hon rekommenderar att koppla temperaturserier till användningstider innan man ändrar styrningen."
Differs: the third clause is Rask's recommendation carried in the article's voice, but as a roadmap of what the article reports, not as advice issued. "established" is proportionate to "Rapporten ... anger". Supported.

### Opening section

**1.5** — "In January 2026 the property office in Lervik fitted sensors in six classrooms at Björkskolan"
Source: "Fiktiva Lerviks fastighetskontor satte i januari 2026 temperaturgivare i sex klassrum i Björkskolan." Supported. ("Fiktiva" is the corpus's fiction marker, not part of the name.)

**1.6** — "and left them there for four weeks"
Source: "Värden registrerades var femte minut under fyra veckor."
Differs: **duration of what.** The material dates four weeks of recording, not four weeks of installation. **Finding F3.**

**1.7** — "Complaints about cold air had come in, and the office wanted to know whether they coincided with low temperatures during lesson time."
Source: "Kontoret ville veta om klagomål på kall luft sammanföll med låga temperaturer under lektionstid."
Differs: "had come in" adds nothing about who complained or to whom, matching the material's silence. Present perfect is the en_GB form for a recent past bearing on the trial. Supported.

**1.8** — "The readings answer a narrower question than that one."
Author's transition, resting on the limitations that follow ("Den säger inte hur länge..."; "Den säger heller inte om eleverna frös."). No complaint timings are supplied anywhere in the material, so the office's question is indeed not answered. Supported as the author's own argument on supported material.

### "Fourteen of 120 lesson periods held a reading below 20 degrees"

**1.9** — the heading itself
Source: "14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius."
Differs: drops "registrerade" and "Celsius"; both are restored in the body two sentences later. "held a reading below" preserves "minst en" — it asserts containment, not exactness — and the heading does not claim 120 is the total of all lesson periods held, only that 14 of those 120 held such a reading. No overreach. Supported.

**1.10** — "Values were logged every five minutes for the whole period."
Source: "Värden registrerades var femte minut under fyra veckor." Supported.

**1.11** — "Two of the sensors stood near exterior walls and four on interior walls"
Source: "Två givare stod nära ytterväggar, fyra på innerväggar." Supported; the near/on distinction of the original is preserved.

**1.12** — "the placements were documented, but they were never compared experimentally"
Source: "Givarnas placering dokumenterades men jämfördes inte experimentellt."
Differs: "never" for "inte". In an English past-passive clause of this shape "never compared" is read as "were not compared", not as an added claim about all future time; no case compatible with the material separates them. Supported.

**1.13** — "The report *Mätförsök i Björkskolan*, dated 12 March 2026, states that 14 of the 120 recorded lesson periods contained at least one reading below 20 degrees Celsius."
Source: "Rapporten Mätförsök i Björkskolan, daterad 12 mars 2026, anger att 14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius."
Attribution ("states"), scope ("recorded"), strength ("at least one") and the unit all carried across. Supported. Keeping the report title in Swedish is correct — it names an actual document.

**1.14** — "Twenty degrees was the office's own working limit for the trial, not a claim about a legal requirement."
Source: "Gränsen 20 grader var kontorets egen arbetsgräns för försöket, inte ett påstående om ett rättsligt krav." Supported; this is also the forbidden inference "lagregel", correctly closed off.

### "Ventilation, draught and radiant heat went unrecorded"

**1.15** — the heading
Source: "Ventilation, luftdrag och upplevd temperatur mättes inte."; "försöket mätte bara lufttemperatur."
Differs: the heading substitutes radiant heat for perceived temperature in the triad. Both are supported, but by different source sentences: radiant heat by "mätte bara lufttemperatur", ventilation and draught by "mättes inte". Supported.

**1.16** — "A sensor reports the temperature of the air at the point where it is mounted, and nothing else about the room."
Source: "En givare mäter temperaturen där den sitter."; "försöket mätte bara lufttemperatur."
Differs: "of the air" is not in "temperaturen där den sitter", but is settled by "mätte bara lufttemperatur". "nothing else about the room" restates the point-versus-room contrast the source sentence makes; it is the author's gloss on it, not an added measurement claim. Supported.

**1.17** — "Operative temperature — a measure that takes in both the air temperature and the heat radiating from the surrounding surfaces — is a different quantity, and the trial recorded air temperature only."
Source: "Operativ temperatur är ett annat mått som tar hänsyn till både luftens temperatur och värmestrålningen från omgivande ytor; försöket mätte bara lufttemperatur."
Both components of the definition and the restriction "bara" are carried. Supported. This also satisfies the brief's "Förklara relevanta begrepp före användning utan att kasta bort tekniken."

**1.18** — "Ventilation, draught and perceived temperature were not measured at all."
Source: "Ventilation, luftdrag och upplevd temperatur mättes inte." Supported; "at all" is emphasis on a flat negative, adding no scope.

**1.19** — "What the air did as it moved through a room, and what anyone sitting in it experienced, is therefore outside the data."
Author's inference drawn from 1.18: ventilation and draught are what the air does as it moves; perceived temperature is what an occupant experiences. Supported as the author's own restatement.

### "A count of periods is not a measure of how cold it got"

**1.20** — the heading
Source: "Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen."; "innehöll minst en mätning". A count of periods containing at least one sub-limit reading is indeed neither a depth nor a duration. Supported.

**1.21** — "The report does not say how long any reading stayed under the limit"
Source: "Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen."
Differs: **the subject of the silence.** The source's silence is about the temperature; the draft's is about "any reading", which is narrower and leaves an aggregate duration apparently known. **Finding F2.**

**1.22** — "and it does not say whether the pupils were cold"
Source: "Den säger heller inte om eleverna frös." Supported; definite plural "eleverna" → "the pupils"; the state of knowledge is preserved as unknown, not as denied.

**1.23** — "Fourteen out of 120 counts the periods with at least one reading under the limit, and nothing beyond that."
Source: "14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius." Supported; "minst en" preserved exactly.

**1.24** — "Nor does the trial supply anything to set that figure against — no norm, no target, no capacity — so it is a count with its exclusions rather than a verdict."
Source: judging note "14 av 120 saknar normjämförelse: kalla det inte högt/lågt."
Differs: "no target" could seem to collide with "kontorets egen arbetsgräns", but the working limit is the threshold that generates the count, not a comparator for 14-out-of-120; no acceptable number of periods is supplied anywhere. The draft declines the high/low characterisation the material forbids. Supported.

**1.25** — "A mean for the whole day could have hidden variation within lessons, and the report therefore presents lesson periods instead of one figure per day."
Source: "Ett medelvärde för hela dagen kan dölja variation under lektionerna. Rapporten redovisar därför lektionspass i stället för ett enda dygnsmedelvärde."
Differs: modality only — "kan dölja" (generic present) → "could have hidden" (counterfactual about this trial). Both express the same possibility; the causal "därför/therefore" is the material's own, not added. Supported.

### "Pair the readings with usage times before changing the controls"

**1.26** — the heading
Source: "Hon rekommenderar att koppla temperaturserier till användningstider innan man ändrar styrningen."
Differs: whose assertion — an imperative in the article's voice for what the material gives as Rask's recommendation; attributed to her in the body three sentences later. See §5, editorial question E1.

**1.27** — "Which lesson periods to examine, then, is the narrower question these four weeks answer."
Source: "14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius."; Rask: "Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare." Author's synthesis resting on both. Supported.

**1.28** — "Operations technician Elin Rask went through the ventilation and heating times with the school's caretaker afterwards"
Source: "Driftteknikern Elin Rask gick efter försöket igenom tiderna för ventilation och värme med skolans vaktmästare."
Differs: "afterwards" for "efter försöket"; the preceding clause names the four weeks, so the referent is settled. Supported. "vaktmästare" → "caretaker" is the en_GB term for the role.

**1.29** — "and no adjustment has yet had its effect measured"
Source: "Inga justeringars effekt har ännu mätts."
Differs: the coordinating "and" places the two facts in the same sentence where the material has them in adjacent sentences, but asserts no causal link between the walkthrough and any adjustment. Both source and draft carry the same presupposition that adjustments exist, and both preserve "ännu" — unmeasured so far, not unaffected. Supported; this is also the forbidden inference "effekt av givarna", correctly closed off.

**1.30** — "Before the controls are changed, she recommends pairing the temperature series with the times the rooms are used."
Source: "Hon rekommenderar att koppla temperaturserier till användningstider innan man ändrar styrningen." Attribution, scope and ordering all carried; "styrningen" → "the controls". Supported.

**1.31** — "The next trial is planned for November, with the sensors placed as before and notes on when the rooms are used; the funding has not been decided."
Source: "Nästa försök planeras för november med likadant placerade givare och noteringar om när rummen används; finansiering är inte beslutad."
Differs: the draft's definite "the sensors" could be read as the same physical units where "likadant placerade givare" says only identically placed ones; in context "the sensors" is read as those of the next trial, and the assertion carried is about placement, which matches. No year is attached to November in either. "har inte beslutats/är inte beslutad" → "has not been decided" preserves the open state rather than a refusal. Supported.

- Better health ("bättre hälsa"): none; 1.22 explicitly leaves the pupils' state unknown.
- Cause of the cold ("orsak till kyla"): none; 1.3 and the quotation both leave it open.
- Effect of the sensors or of any change ("effekt av givarna"): none; 1.29 states the effect is unmeasured.

## 3. Quotation: translation check (separate from source support)

Material: ”Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare. Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då.” — given as a verbatim interview statement, expressly permitted to be translated idiomatically with the meaning preserved.

Draft: 'We know when we need to look more closely. We still don't know why it got cold just then,' Rask said.

- **Meaning** — "titta närmare" → "look more closely" is the idiomatic en_GB equivalent; "det blev kallt just då" → "it got cold just then" preserves the impersonal subject and the pinpointing of a particular occasion. Nothing narrowed, widened or sharpened.
- **Stance** — the speaker blames nobody and the translation blames nobody; the second sentence stays an admission of an open question rather than a deflection.
- **Certainty** — "ännu inte" is rendered "still don't", which keeps both the negation and the not-yet, i.e. the expectation that the answer may come. "don't yet know" would be an alternative of equal fidelity; neither obstructs the reading, so this is a synonym choice, not a finding.
- **Distinctive voice** — the antithesis that makes the remark memorable ("Vi vet ... / Vi vet ännu inte ...", when against why) survives intact as "We know when ... / We still don't know why ...". The plain, flat register is preserved; it has not been smoothed into the article's own prose.
- **Nothing additive** — no name, figure, cause or connection is added; the implicit "Vi" is not expanded, correctly, since the material does not settle it.
- **Idiomatic target-language speech** — the contraction "don't" is natural spoken en_GB, and neither sentence follows Swedish word order.

**No translation findings.**

Other Swedish terms carried into en_GB were checked for cases where one term could cover something the other does not: "arbetsgräns" → "working limit"; "operativ temperatur" → "operative temperature" (the standard en_GB term, defined in the draft before use); "luftdrag" → "draught"; "upplevd temperatur" → "perceived temperature"; "lektionspass" → "lesson period"; "styrningen" → "the controls"; "vaktmästare" → "caretaker"; "driftteknikern" → "operations technician"; "finansiering" → "funding". None admits such a case here. The report title "Mätförsök i Björkskolan" is left in Swedish, which is correct for an actual document.

## 4. Findings

### F1 — Lead: the sensors are said to report the room's air and the moment it crossed the limit
**Source-support finding.** Severity: the largest of the three.

Draft (lead): "Temperature sensors in six classrooms can tell a property manager **when the air dropped below a chosen limit**".

Support that is missing or changed: the material says "En givare mäter temperaturen där den sitter" and that the report states only "14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll **minst en mätning** under 20 grader Celsius", with "Värden registrerades **var femte minut**" and "Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen." Nothing supports the sensors reporting the room's air, or a crossing of the limit as opposed to a sampled reading.

Concrete case in which the source holds and the draft fails: in one classroom the sensor sits near an exterior wall — the material puts two of the six there. During one lesson period the 10:05 sample reads 19.6 °C while the air across the rest of the room is at 21 °C, and between the 10:10 and 10:15 samples the temperature at the sensor drops under 20 and returns above it unseen. Every supplied statement holds on this case: the period contained at least one reading below 20, the sensor measured the temperature where it sat, and the report says nothing about duration. The draft's statement fails on it twice over — the air of the classroom never dropped below the limit at 10:05, and the sensors cannot say when it dropped below at all between samples.

No supplied statement excludes the case; the material points the other way ("där den sitter", "bara lufttemperatur", "minst en mätning").

Why it matters here in particular: the draft's own third section makes exactly this distinction — "A sensor reports the temperature of the air at the point where it is mounted, and nothing else about the room" — so the lead promises more than the article itself then allows. The body is right and the summary is not; the Claims contract binds the summary alike.

**Smallest supported repair:** replace "when the air dropped below a chosen limit" with "when a reading fell below a chosen limit". Six words changed; the lead keeps its shape, its emphasis and its em-dash contrast, and the claim becomes exactly what the five-minute log supports.

### F2 — The report's silence about duration is narrowed from the temperature to a reading
**Source-support finding.** Severity: moderate.

Draft: "The report does not say **how long any reading stayed under the limit**".

Source: "Den säger inte **hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen**."

What differs: the subject of the silence. The material says the report gives no duration for the temperature being below the limit — nothing at all about how long. The draft says the report gives no duration for any individual reading, which is a narrower silence and leaves an aggregate figure apparently available.

Concrete case in which the source holds and the draft's implication fails: a manager reads the draft and concludes that the report does state a total — say, that the temperature was below 20 °C for 40 minutes across the fourteen periods — and merely omits per-reading durations, and so quotes that total in a request for funding. The material excludes any such total ("Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen"), so the draft has left open a reading the material forbids. Nothing supplied restores the total.

This is a loss of a qualification that changes the claim: the section's whole point is that a count of periods carries no information about duration, and the narrowed wording concedes part of that point back.

**Smallest supported repair:** replace "any reading" with "the temperature" — "The report does not say how long the temperature stayed under the limit". Two words; the sentence's rhythm and its pairing with the following clause are untouched.

### F3 — Four weeks is attached to the sensors' stay rather than to the recording
**Source-support finding.** Severity: the smallest of the three; a circumstantial-detail overreach.

Draft: "the property office in Lervik fitted sensors in six classrooms at Björkskolan and **left them there for four weeks**".

Source: "Fiktiva Lerviks fastighetskontor satte i januari 2026 temperaturgivare i sex klassrum i Björkskolan." and, separately, "Värden registrerades var femte minut **under fyra veckor**."

What differs: what the four weeks measure. The material dates four weeks of recording; it never says when the sensors were installed relative to the recording, nor when they were taken down. Duration is a claim in its own right under the Claims contract.

Concrete case in which the source holds and the draft fails: the office fitted the sensors in January 2026 and has left them in place ever since — they are still on the walls in September, which is why "Nästa försök planeras för november med likadant placerade givare" is easy to arrange — but only the first four weeks were logged and reported. Every supplied statement holds; the draft's "left them there for four weeks" is false on it. Nothing supplied excludes the case, and the mention of a November trial with identically placed sensors is at least as compatible with it as against it.

**Smallest supported repair:** "and recorded there for four weeks" in place of "and left them there for four weeks". Two words; the sentence keeps its length and its cadence, and the following paragraph's "Values were logged every five minutes for the whole period" then attaches to a period the material actually dates.

## 5. Editorial question, reported without a repair

### E1 — A heading issues Rask's recommendation as the article's own advice
Draft heading: "Pair the readings with usage times before changing the controls".
Source: "**Hon rekommenderar** att koppla temperaturserier till användningstider innan man ändrar styrningen."

The material gives this as Rask's recommendation and the draft attributes it to her three sentences into the section ("she recommends pairing the temperature series with the times the rooms are used"). The heading, read alone, is the article recommending it.

This sits on the unsettled line between a standpoint carried in the article's voice and a factual assertion. It is not reported as a defect: an imperative heading summarising a recommendation attributed in the body immediately beneath it is an ordinary convention of instructional prose, the brief asks for a "pedagogisk" article with "mellanrubriker" and a "användbar avslutning", and the advice itself is the only recommendation in the material and is not among the forbidden inferences. Whether the article should adopt it in its own voice or mark it as Rask's ("Rask: pair the readings with usage times") is an editorial choice for the author, not a repair this comparison prescribes.
