## 3. F1 — **fail**

Two passages state more than the material carries. Both are small, and both are named in the delivery account with a repair, which satisfies the reporting obligation; neither is thereby removed from the text.

1. **Standfirst — "Temperature sensors in six classrooms can tell a property manager when the air dropped below a chosen limit".** The material supports only sampled readings at the points where sensors sit: "En givare mäter temperaturen där den sitter", "Värden registrerades var femte minut", "14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius", "Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen." Two strengthenings ride on this sentence. *Scope*: "the air" is the room's air, where the source gives the air at one point — two of the six sensors stood "nära ytterväggar", so a sensor may read under 20 while the room does not. *Modality and thing measured*: "when … dropped below" asserts a crossing and its time, where the material supports only which lesson periods contained at least one sub-limit sample; a dip between two five-minute samples is never recorded. Reader effect: the standfirst promises the property manager exactly the resolution the article's own third section then withdraws — "A sensor reports the temperature of the air at the point where it is mounted, and nothing else about the room" — in a text whose whole angle is what a short trial can and cannot say. This is the defect that matters most, because it lands in the one part most readers read alone.

2. **Lead — "fitted sensors in six classrooms at Björkskolan and left them there for four weeks".** The material dates four weeks of *recording*, not four weeks of installation: "Fiktiva Lerviks fastighetskontor satte i januari 2026 temperaturgivare i sex klassrum i Björkskolan" and, separately, "Värden registrerades var femte minut under fyra veckor." Nothing says when the sensors came down; "Nästa försök planeras för november med likadant placerade givare" is as compatible with their still being up as against it. Duration is a circumstantial claim in its own right. Small, but unsupported as written.

Checked and **not** faulted, with the reason:

- "The report does not say how long any reading stayed under the limit" — the source's silence is broader ("Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen"), so the draft's sentence is *entailed* by the material; it claims a smaller absence, not a larger one. The concern is an implicature the material does not settle. See the class in §8.
- "Nor does the trial supply anything to set that figure against — no norm, no target, no capacity" — extends "14 av 120 saknar normjämförelse" to target and capacity, but the material is declared complete ("Treat them as the complete reporting material"), so no supplied target or capacity is being denied. It also correctly declines the forbidden high/low characterisation.
- "A mean for the whole day could have hidden variation within lessons" — a past counterfactual about this trial for the source's general "kan dölja"; the trial's own data are not flat (14 of 120 periods differ from the rest), so the specific reading holds where the general one does.
- "A sensor reports the temperature of the air at the point where it is mounted" — read as a generic about all instruments this would fail against operative temperature, which the material presents as measurable; read in its context, about this trial's *temperaturgivare*, "försöket mätte bara lufttemperatur" supports it.
- Every forbidden inference is closed off, not committed: no saving, no health claim, no cause of the cold ("and nothing about why anyone felt cold"; "We still don't know why it got cold just then"), no effect of the sensors ("no adjustment has yet had its effect measured"), no legal rule ("not a claim about a legal requirement"), and no high/low verdict on 14 of 120.
- Unknown is kept apart from absent throughout: "it does not say whether the pupils were cold" reports the report's silence, and does not report that the pupils were warm.
- The quotation's translation adds nothing and drops nothing: "Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare. Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då." → "'We know when we need to look more closely. We still don't know why it got cold just then,'". *ännu inte* survives as "still don't", the *Vi vet … Vi vet ännu inte* antithesis survives, the impersonal *det blev kallt* stays impersonal, and the implicit *Vi* is left unexpanded, as the material leaves it.
- Terms checked in both directions: "arbetsgräns" → "working limit" (not a norm or a legal minimum), "operativ temperatur" → "operative temperature", "luftdrag" → "draught", "upplevd temperatur" → "perceived temperature", "lektionspass" → "lesson period", "styrningen" → "the controls", "vaktmästare" → "caretaker", "driftteknikern" → "operations technician", "finansiering" → "funding". None admits a case falling under one term and not the other here. The report's title stays Swedish, correctly, since it names an actual document.
