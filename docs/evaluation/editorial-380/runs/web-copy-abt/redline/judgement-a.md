## 1. Changes

Every difference between `redline/work/input.md` and `redline/delivered.md`. There are four; the frontmatter, the headline, all five headings, the bullet list, the price sentence, the CTA link label and its destination are byte-identical.

1. **Standfirst, first sentence.** Before: "…och sammanställer rutinen skriftligt." After: "…och sammanställer rutinen skriftligt, med de oklarheter som kommer fram." — **a change to what a claim says (scope)**: the summary's promised contents are widened in the standfirst from "rutinen" to "rutinen … med de oklarheter som kommer fram". The added scope is already carried by the second bullet under "Det här ingår" ("nuvarande steg, oklarheter och två möjliga förenklingar"), so no new fact enters the text; the passage it was made to was not defective, so it is also **a change of taste**.
2. **"Det här ingår inte", first sentence.** Before: "Genomgången omfattar inte juridisk rådgivning…" After: "Uppdraget omfattar inte juridisk rådgivning…" — **the repair of a visible defect**, and with it **a change to what a claim says (subject, and thereby the scope of the exclusion)**. The input named the same offering "Uppdraget" in the standfirst, in "Uppdraget består av två delar" and in "Uppdraget gäller en lokal i en förening", and "Genomgången" only here, in the one sentence that fixes what the exclusions cover; a reader could not tell whether the exclusions bound the whole engagement or only the video meeting.
3. **"Pris och förutsättningar", second paragraph, subject.** Before: "Styrelsen behöver kunna beskriva…" After: "Ni behöver kunna beskriva…" — **a change of taste** (second-person consistency with "ni får en gemensam bild", "Så anmäler ni intresse", "I formuläret anger ni"), which is also **a change to what a claim says (subject)**, though to the same referent: the page's addressee is the board ("Uppdraget vänder sig till styrelser i mindre bostadsrättsföreningar"). Not reported in the account; see `R1`.
4. **"Pris och förutsättningar", second paragraph, object.** Before: "…och visa den nuvarande instruktionen till de boende." After: "…och visa Svale den nuvarande instruktionen till de boende." — **the repair of a visible defect**: the input sentence read both as "show Svale the instruction that goes to residents" and as "show the instruction to the residents", and only the first is a prerequisite the board can be asked to meet. The repair also **changes what the claim says (scope)** by settling the recipient.
5. **CTA microcopy, dash.** Before: `) — länken öppnar` (U+2014, em dash). After: `) – länken öppnar` (U+2013, en dash with surrounding spaces, the Swedish tankstreck). — **a mechanical correction**, and a locale one; see `L2`.
