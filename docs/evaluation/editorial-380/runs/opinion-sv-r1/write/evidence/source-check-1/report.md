## 3. Findings

### Finding 1 — deck relocates a documented absence from the papers to the trial

- **Draft (line 10):** "Bakom förslaget ligger åtta veckors försök i två av sju lokaler, **utan mätning av arbetstid och utan besparingsberäkning**."
- **Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i **handlingarna**."
- **What differs:** scope, and whose knowledge. The material records an absence in the documents before kommunstyrelsen. The deck's modifier attaches to "försök" and so asserts that the trial itself was conducted without any measurement of working time.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** the förvaltning did log staff time per booking during the eight weeks, but the log was never written up in the papers put before kommunstyrelsen. This is compatible with the supplied material, which speaks only of what is in "handlingarna"; no supplied statement excludes it. In that case "Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna" is true and "åtta veckors försök … utan mätning av arbetstid" is false.
- **Case in the other direction:** none needed; one direction suffices, and the draft's own body states the claim correctly ("Men handlingarna innehåller varken tidsmätning eller beräkning av vad borttagandet sparar"), which is the model for the repair.
- **Smallest supported repair:** end the sentence after "lokaler" and re-attach the absence to the papers — "Bakom förslaget ligger åtta veckors försök i två av sju lokaler, och handlingar utan tidsmätning och besparingsberäkning."

### Finding 2 — a withheld claim becomes a positive denial about the association's own conduct

- **Draft (line 38):** "Vad ett halvår med båda vägarna kostar **har vi inte räknat på**, och **vi finansierar det inte**."
- **Source:** "Föreningen **gör inget anspråk på** att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."
- **What differs:** modality and whose assertion. "Gör inget anspråk på att ha X" declines to claim X; the draft asserts not-X as a fact about the association, and adds a forward-looking commitment ("vi finansierar det inte") that the material does not supply in any tense.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** the association made a rough internal cost estimate of the six-month trial but deliberately puts nothing forward, precisely because the costing is the committee's job. Compatible with the material, which reports only what the association claims, not what it has done; no supplied statement excludes it. Then "gör inget anspråk på att ha … kostnadsberäknat" is true while "har vi inte räknat på" is false.
- **Note in the draft's favour, and why it does not dispose of the case:** the brief's boundary "med kostnadsosäkerheten bevarad" is met either way, and the draft plainly intends the disclaimer the material asks for. But the contract requires preserving the state of knowledge, and what is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Vi gör inget anspråk på att ha kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat ett halvår med båda vägarna." (Retain the following sentence unchanged.)

### Finding 3 — the deck names associations as the party that will be confined to web booking

- **Draft (line 10):** "Från september ska **föreningar i Lervik** enligt tjänsteutlåtandet bara kunna boka lokaler på webben."
- **Source:** "Tjänsteutlåtandet … föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september." The material names the affected people only as "**invånarna**" ("hur stor andel av **invånarna** som inte kan boka digitalt") and "**användarna**" / "de [som] väljer telefon eller webb". It never states who books the premises.
- **What differs:** the subject. "Föreningslokaler" names the kind of premises, not the class of bookers; the draft converts the former into the latter in the article's most prominent factual sentence.
- **Case in which the source holds and the draft fails:** associations in Lervik hold their premises under separate seasonal or annual agreements with the förvaltning, and the telephone/web booking channels serve residents booking a föreningslokal for single occasions — which is why the report's relevance is stated in terms of "invånarna". Compatible with the material; no supplied statement says associations use the booking channels. In that case telephone booking is removed exactly as the material says, while "föreningar … ska bara kunna boka lokaler på webben" is false.
- **Case in the other direction:** none required.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Från september ska föreningslokaler i Lervik enligt tjänsteutlåtandet bara kunna bokas på webben." (Body line 14 already says "telefonbokningen av föreningslokaler" and needs no change.)

## 4. Editorial questions (not defects; no repair proposed)

- **A. "Dubbelarbetet är verkligt" (line 24) / "Den invändningen är verklig" (line 26).** The material supplies "Förvaltningens **verkliga** invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är **dubbel administration**, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra." That settles what the objection *is* and that it is sincerely held; whether it also establishes that staff in fact enter data twice is genuinely unsettled, because the same sentence functions as a guard against imputing false motives rather than as a verified operational fact. Read in context the body sentence is a concession that the objection is genuine — defensible as written, and the brief's warning against "tandlös neutralisering" tells against weakening it. The heading is the weaker of the two, since it predicates "verkligt" of the double work rather than of the objection. Reported here as the brief's own unevidenced statement, an editorial question for the commissioning party, not a defect.
- **B. Quotation handling.** The draft contains no quotation marks. The one passage the material marks as quotable is used as the author's own prose, which is licit because the author is the speaker and "Detta får citeras eller refereras." Nothing in the draft claims or implies quote approval. Nothing to report under the quotation guidance.
