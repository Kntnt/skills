## 2. Translated quotation

Source: ”Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare. Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då.”
Draft: 'We know when we need to look more closely. We still don't know why it turned cold just then.'

- Meaning: both sentences map clause for clause. "titta närmare" → "look more closely"; "det blev kallt" → "it turned cold", keeping the Swedish impersonal subject as the English impersonal "it" without supplying a referent the material does not settle.
- Certainty: "ännu inte" is preserved by "still don't", which keeps the not-yet reading rather than a flat denial. No hedge added or removed.
- Stance: the contrast between what is known and what is not is preserved in the same order, and no blame is introduced.
- Distinctive voice: "just då" is kept as "just then" rather than smoothed into the surrounding register.
- Person and number: first person plural in both, and the speaker's institutional "we" is not narrowed to her.
- Idiom: contracted "don't" is ordinary British speech; nothing reads as Swedish syntax carried over. Outer single quotation marks match the document's own choice at "'At least one reading'".

No translation finding.

## 3. Findings

### Finding 1 — "a second trial" asserts an ordinal the material does not give (minor)

- Draft: heading "The office reports by lesson and **plans a second trial**", and "**A second trial** is planned for November".
- Source: "Nästa försök planeras för november" — *the next trial*, not the second.
- What is missing: the material nowhere states that the January 2026 trial was the office's first, so "second" adds a count of trials it does not support. Compatible case: the office ran an earlier measurement trial at Björkskolan in 2024; "Nästa försök planeras för november" still holds, while "a second trial" is false. No supplied statement excludes it.
- Smallest repair: "plans another trial" in the heading, and "Another trial is planned for November". "Nästa"/"the next" would serve equally.

### Finding 2 — "why Björkskolan's classrooms turned cold" widens the cold beyond what Rask says (moderate)

- Draft: "So the trial does not say why Björkskolan's classrooms turned cold."
- Source: ”Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt **just då**.”
- What is changed: the denial of cause is supported, but the presupposition it carries is not. The only assertion that it turned cold is Rask's, and hers is restricted to the occasions in question ("just då"). The draft states it in the narrator's own voice, across Björkskolan's classrooms generally and with no time restriction. Compatible case: the readings below the office's own 20-degree working limit fell in two of the six rooms and only on a handful of January mornings; Rask's "det blev kallt just då" holds, while "Björkskolan's classrooms turned cold" — as a statement about the school's classrooms — fails. Nothing in the material says which rooms or which periods the 14 came from, and "Mätningarna omfattar bara dessa sex rum och dessa fyra veckor" excludes no such case.
- Smallest repair: restore the time restriction and drop the generalisation — "So the trial does not say why it turned cold when it did." The following quotation then carries the fact in the speaker's own voice, where the material puts it.
