## 3. Findings

### F1 — The lead dates the measurement period, not the placement, to January 2026

- **Draft:** "Lervik's property office measured six classrooms at Björkskolan every five minutes for four weeks **from January 2026**."
- **Source:** "Fiktiva Lerviks fastighetskontor **satte i januari 2026** temperaturgivare i sex klassrum i Björkskolan." — "**Värden registrerades var femte minut under fyra veckor.**"
- **What is missing or changed:** January 2026 is supplied as the date the sensors were *put in place*. The four-week logging period is supplied with a duration and no start date. The draft's "measured … for four weeks from January 2026" transfers the date to the measurement.
- **Concrete case, draft → source:** the office fits the sensors in the last week of January 2026, commissions them, and logs from 2 February to 1 March 2026. Both source sentences hold — sensors were put in place in January 2026, and values were recorded every five minutes for four weeks — while "measured … for four weeks from January 2026" fails.
- **Concrete case, source → draft:** logging starts on the day of installation, 12 January 2026, and runs to 8 February. Both statements hold. So the two are not equivalent in one direction only.
- **Nothing in the material excludes the first case:** no supplied sentence dates the start or end of the logging period, and the only other date given is the report's, "daterad 12 mars 2026", which is compatible with either.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Lervik's property office put sensors into six classrooms at Björkskolan in January 2026 and recorded a value every five minutes for four weeks." (The draft's own body sentence, row 8, is already correct; this brings the lead into line with it.)

### F2 — The second trial's sensors are promised in "the same positions", where the material promises the same manner of placement

- **Draft:** "A second trial is planned for November, with **the sensors in the same positions** and notes on when each room is used".
- **Source:** "Nästa försök planeras för november **med likadant placerade givare** och noteringar om när rummen används; finansiering är inte beslutad."
- **What is missing or changed:** two things. *Likadant placerade* says the sensors will be placed *in the same way* — it does not fix the positions. And *givare* is indefinite; the draft's "**the** sensors" makes them the same physical devices as the first trial's.
- **Concrete case, draft → source:** the November trial uses six newly bought sensors in six classrooms, two near external walls and four on internal walls as before, but in different rooms of Björkskolan (the six trial rooms are in use for exams that month). The sensors are *likadant placerade* — placed in the same manner — so the source's sentence holds, while "the sensors in the same positions" fails on both counts.
- **Concrete case, source → draft:** the same six devices are refitted at the identical mounting points. Both statements hold.
- **Nothing in the material excludes the first case:** the material names no rooms for the November trial and says nothing about which devices are used. "Mätningarna omfattar bara dessa sex rum och dessa fyra veckor" scopes the *first* trial's measurements, not the second trial's plan.
- **Why it matters here:** the draft has just spent a section on what sensor placement does and does not settle, so a reader takes "the same positions" as a comparability claim about the two trials that the material does not make.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "…with sensors placed the same way and notes on when each room is used".

Both findings are source-support findings (F1 within one language; F2 a meaning changed in crossing from Swedish to English, outside quoted speech). There are no translation findings.

## 4. Editorial question (not a finding, no repair proposed)

"So the trial does not say **why Björkskolan's classrooms turned cold**." The sentence's *assertion* — that the trial establishes no cause — is exactly the material's own "Ingen … orsak till kyla … följer av materialet", and is fully supported. Its *presupposition*, that the classrooms turned cold, is also the material's: Rask's "varför det **blev kallt** just då", the office's motive "klagomål på **kall luft**", and the forbidden-inference note's own phrase "orsak till **kyla**". The passage immediately attributes the thought to Rask ("and Rask said as much"), so the presupposition sits where the material puts it.

The question for an editor, rather than a defect: the draft has argued two paragraphs earlier that 14 of 120 is "neither a high count nor a low one" and that the report is silent on whether the pupils were cold. A reader may take the author's-voice "turned cold" as a grading of the same result the draft has declined to grade. This is the line between a standpoint the material carries and a factual assertion about the rooms, and it is genuinely unsettled — reported here rather than as a defect.
