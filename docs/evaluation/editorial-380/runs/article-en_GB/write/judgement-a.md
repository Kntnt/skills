## 3. F1 — **fail**

The draft is unusually disciplined about the material's stated silences: it carries the report's silence on duration, its silence on whether the pupils were cold, the six-room and four-week exclusion, the unmeasured ventilation, draught and perceived temperature, the undocumented placement comparison, the office's ownership of the 20-degree limit, and the unmeasured effect of any adjustment. None of the forbidden inferences appears — no saving, no health effect, no cause of the cold, no effect of the sensors, no legal rule (it is explicitly denied: "not a claim about a legal requirement"), and the high/low characterisation is explicitly withheld ("so it is neither a high count nor a low one — it is a count, from six rooms, over four weeks"). Two assertions nevertheless leave the material.

**F1.1 — an unsupported ordinal.** Draft, in the subheading and in the body: "plans a second trial" / "**A second trial** is planned for November, with sensors placed the same way and notes on when each room is used; its funding is not decided." Material: "**Nästa försök** planeras för november med likadant placerade givare och noteringar om när rummen används; finansiering är inte beslutad." *Nästa* names the next trial relative to this one; nothing supplied says the January 2026 trial was the office's first. A prior trial at Björkskolan in, say, 2024 leaves every supplied sentence true and makes "a second trial" false. This is an addition of a fact the material does not carry, in the narrator's voice, twice. It is not an *unknown* treated as *absent*: the material is silent on whether earlier trials exist, and the draft closes that silence.

**F1.2 — a widened presupposition, and a dropped restriction.** Draft: "So the trial does not say **why Björkskolan's classrooms turned cold**." The denial of cause is exactly the material's own stated non-inference ("Ingen … orsak till kyla … följer av materialet") and is fully supported. What the sentence presupposes is not. The only assertion in the material that it turned cold is Rask's, and hers is restricted in time: "Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt **just då**." The office's motive supplies "klagomål på kall luft" — complaints, not a finding — and the report is expressly silent on whether the pupils were cold ("Den säger heller inte om eleverna frös"). The draft states it in the author's own voice, across Björkskolan's classrooms as a class and with no time restriction. Two things are lost: the author perspective (Rask's assertion becomes the narrator's) and the chronological caveat (*just då* is dropped). The material names neither which of the six rooms the 14 periods fell in nor when within the four weeks, so a case in which readings below the office's limit fell in two rooms on a few January mornings satisfies Rask and defeats the draft. The defect is sharpened by the draft's own care: two paragraphs earlier it declines to grade the same result and records the report's silence on whether pupils were cold, then asserts the cold outright.

Term checks in both directions found nothing further. *Operativ temperatur* → "operative temperature" is exact, and the gloss "the measure that combines air temperature with radiant heat from the surrounding surfaces" reproduces "tar hänsyn till både luftens temperatur och värmestrålningen från omgivande ytor" without narrowing or widening. *Driftteknikern* → "operations technician", *vaktmästare* → "caretaker" (common gender in Swedish, no natural gender asserted in either text), *styrningen* → "the control system", *dygnsmedelvärde* → "a single daily mean" all hold. "Ventilation, draught and what anyone in the room felt" glosses *upplevd temperatur* loosely, but the list's own members fix the reading as thermal and the same fact is named exactly elsewhere; defensible, noted, not a finding. "Nobody measured that" rests on the material's agentless "upplevd temperatur mättes inte" in material declared complete; defensible. "Before changing anything in the control system" is marginally wider than "innan man ändrar styrningen", but it stays inside Rask's recommendation and asserts no new fact. The quotation is faithful: "still don't know" preserves *ännu inte*, "just then" preserves *just då*, the first-person plural is not narrowed, and the impersonal *det* stays impersonal as "it".

Both defects were reported beside the draft, which satisfies the reporting obligation — the rejection for unresolved mandatory findings *not* reported is not triggered. The text nevertheless carries them, and F1 asks about the text.
