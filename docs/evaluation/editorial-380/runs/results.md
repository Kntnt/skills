## What the evaluation found

- Sixteen invocations and thirty-two judgements, every run and every judge a fresh `claude-opus-5` subagent at high deliberation. Every row carries every criterion the matrix applies to its side.
- **All eight Write runs delivered, and on all eight the prose delivered is the prose the final comparison read** — the same bytes on five rows, and on the other three the same bytes apart from the `kntnt` frontmatter block the delivery wrapper prepends. #362's Claude-family evaluation had one delivery that repaired prose after its last comparison and shipped it unread; nothing of that kind happened here.
- **The outcome [#376](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/376) decided is what these runs do.** Six of the eight ended with a finding their last comparison left standing, and each of the six delivered the prose unchanged and reported every remaining finding beside the draft, with the passage, what the material carries instead and the checker's proposed smallest repair. All six open the reply by saying the draft is delivered with a known defect or defects before saying anything else about it; `article-abt` and `opinion-sv-r1` have one and say `en känd brist`, the other four say `kända brister` or `known defects`.
- **`F1` fails on five of the eight Write rows, and on every one of the five the passage it fails on is a residual the delivery account named.** Two rows carry a second failing passage on one judgement only: `article-pac`'s lead on judge A's, which the account also reports as a residual, and `web-copy-abt`'s `formuläret`, which judge A alone fails on and which the account carries as an editorial question rather than as a finding. No unsupported passage was found that no checker saw and no account named at all, so no Write row earns a defect ticket of its own.
- **The anatomy is not where these texts fail.** The six `article` and `opinion` drafts all measure `conforms: true` with no norm departure, before and after review. The two `web-copy` texts measure `conforms: false` for an absent byline and an absent lead, which is description rather than failure: the corpus binds that skeleton to the four article genres only.
- **Redline's side is one failure in sixty-seven judged lines.** Seven of the eight reviews are clean on every criterion; `opinion-sv-r2` fails `R1` for deleting a clause from a conforming text and reporting the deletion under a claim of equivalence that does not hold, with two further edits unreported. That is [#383](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/383), already open.
- **Half the reviews lost something the material required.** In four of the eight rows a source-blind change removed or weakened a formulation `source.md` warranted. Twice it was a causal connective the source itself carries — `article-sv` and `article-pac`, the same sentence in two languages. Once it was a subheading that was the text's only carrier of a stated limit (`article-abt`), and once a clause bounding what an objection claims (`opinion-sv-r2`). Two of the four lost a second thing as well: `article-sv`'s new headline no longer carries the brief's forbidden-inference caution in its own words, which both judges name and the run itself declared, and judge B records that `opinion-sv-r2` weakened the document's attributed act from `föreslår` to `vill ha`. Redline cannot know any of this by contract, and no judge counted it against `R1`; it is filed as its own question, [#392](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/392).
- **Contract loading and Redline's closing Proofread pass stayed unobserved in this family.** `T1` and `R2` are `skipped` on all sixteen entries: both are answerable only from a Harness trace, and a session cannot read the transcript of a subagent it started. So this evaluation does not settle whether the scoped loading happened, and **it does not settle whether Redline's closing Proofread pass ran** — the question the ticket opens with stays open. The trace-bearing harness is [#388](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/388).
- **`T2` was exercised for the first time in this family.** All three explicit technique rows carry their technique in the delivered metadata and in the account, both judges pass `T2` on both sides of each, and no technique was inferred on any of the five rows that selected none.
- Nothing was written outside each run's own directory. In all sixteen before-and-after pairs the staged install's eighty-four files carry the same `sha256`, and each matches the repository at `8a37e57e`; every `work/source.md` and `work/input.md` carries the same `sha256` before and after; and no file under any `work/scratch/` appears in any inventory.

## What the two sides did to each other

- **Redline repaired one of Write's own residuals.** On `article-en_GB` the single change it made was the second residual above, `why Björkskolan's classrooms turned cold` → `why the temperature in Björkskolan's classrooms fell below the limit`, found from the text alone because the article refuses that characterisation twice in its own voice. It reported the change accurately and left the other residual standing.
- **Redline kept every other residual.** The `article-abt`, `article-pac`, `web-copy-abt`, `web-copy-sv` and `opinion-sv-r1` residuals all survive review untouched, which is what a source-blind pass can be expected to do: each of them needs the material to see.
- **Twice a causal connective went out that the source licensed.** `article-sv` removed `därför` from `Rapporten redovisar därför lektionspass i stället för ett enda dygnsmedelvärde`, and `article-pac` removed the `therefore` of the same sentence in English, both diagnosing it as a reason the text does not support. The source states it: `Rapporten redovisar därför lektionspass i stället för ett enda dygnsmedelvärde`. Four judgements name it independently.
- **Twice a bounding clause went out.** `article-abt` replaced the subheading `Effekten av eventuella justeringar är inte mätt`, which was the text's only carrier of the source's `Inga justeringars effekt har ännu mätts`; `opinion-sv-r2` deleted `inte om att de som ringer skulle vara lata eller dyra`, which carries the source's `Förvaltningens verkliga invändning … är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra`.

## Method

Eight things about how this wave was made, declared here rather than inferred from the artefacts.

1. **The invocation strings are asserted, not preserved.** Neither `write-turn.md` nor `redline-turn.md` carries the command the run was given; each says only that "the invocation the user typed" is in the message that dispatched the run, and those messages are not in the tree. So the eight `/write …` lines and the eight `/redline --output=response input.md` lines in [`plan.md`](plan.md) and in the two records cannot be verified from the run artefacts. What the artefacts do corroborate is what each run resolved: every delivered `kntnt` map carries the genre, technique and language the plan's table names, and each Write reply states where each value came from — `article-pac`'s says "all four from the invocation you typed", `web-copy-abt`'s "`abt`, angiven i anropet". The output target and the file argument are corroborated on neither side, and on the Redline side the replies state that genre, technique and language were read from the text's own map rather than from the invocation, so the invocation itself leaves no trace there at all.
2. **One rolling wave.** The eight Write runs were dispatched together; each row's Redline run was dispatched as soon as that row's Write reply had been saved, extracted and measured, and each pair of judges as soon as its artefact was complete. The scope-3 and scope-4 inventories were therefore taken once, before the first dispatch and after the last judgement, as `wave-1-before.txt`, `wave-1-after.txt`, `wave-1-before-repo.txt` and `wave-1-after-repo.txt` beside `runs/`.
3. **The concurrency ceiling shaped the order, not the content.** The Harness runs at most twenty subagents at once and a Write run holds its checkers, so several judges were queued and dispatched as slots freed. No run and no judgement was affected; only the order they were started in.
4. **Wall time is the evaluator's measurement**, taken from the dispatch timestamp to the modification time of the reply the run saved as `response.md`. A Write row's clock starts at the single wave dispatch, `2026-09-21T16:00:17Z`, the stamp `wave-1-before-repo.txt` carries; a Redline row's starts at its own `start-epoch.txt`, which each row's `inventory-after.txt` records but which is not itself kept in this tree. The sixteen wall times are therefore the evaluator's measurement and are not recomputable from the artefacts.
5. **Each judge was pointed at a byte copy of its frozen brief by path**, in the evaluator's own directory in the staging area, rather than having the text pasted into its turn, plus one evaluator sentence: if the judgement file write is refused, reply with the whole judgement. No judge's write was refused, and every judgement in this tree is its judge's own.
6. **`delivered.md` was extracted from `response.md` mechanically**, by taking the longest fenced code block, which the delivery contract makes the whole Text Artifact. The throwaway script that did it is not kept: every `delivered.md` sits beside the `response.md` it came from, so the extraction is checkable by eye against the rule stated here. For `web-copy-sv`'s Redline reply, which is a short no-change status carrying no fence, `delivered.md` is a byte copy of `work/input.md` and the entry says `no change`.
7. **Two checkers could not write their report files**, because the staging area sits under a `.git` directory their sessions treat as protected — `article-sv` says so in its account, and `web-copy-abt`'s first checker's report never reached `evidence/` at all, its reply reaching the run truncated at the start. Both runs saved what they received and both comparisons carry a completion status, so both count as complete; `web-copy-abt`'s first claim accounting cannot be reviewed, and the record says so. This is the staging area's doing, not the Skill's. The process table was sampled repeatedly while the runs were in flight and held no `until [ -f … ]` wait loop at any point, so the shape of [#391](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/391) did not appear here.
8. **The repository working copy was untouched.** `wave-1-before-repo.txt` and `wave-1-after-repo.txt` each carry the same commit, `8a37e57eca854fea39dd25e5d2483e831edfc599`, and each carries that and nothing else; a `git status --porcelain --untracked-files=all` reading was taken beside it but is not preserved in the tree, so the HEAD reading is the whole of what can be checked here. This ticket's own working tree is byte-identical across the wave: `wave-1-before.txt` and `wave-1-after.txt` hold the same 10,583 paths under it, none of them added, removed or changed. The Skills, the measuring script and the fixtures were taken from `8a37e57e` with `git archive` and `git show`, never from a working copy, and each of the staged install's eighty-four files matches the repository at that commit after the wave as before it.

`O1` and the `side effects` field in both records were read by the evaluator from each run's `inventory-before.txt` and `inventory-after.txt`, never from a run's report of itself. In all sixteen the diff adds `response.md`, the `evidence/` files each Write run wrote on the evaluator's instruction, and the evaluator's `inventory-after.txt`, with `start-epoch.txt` besides on every Redline row; nothing is removed, the staged install appears in no diff, and the only path whose hash moves is `inventory-before.txt`, which hashed itself while still empty. The evaluator's `delivered.md` and the two anatomy JSONs were written after the after-inventory and appear in neither file. The wave-level diff adds 227 paths and removes none, all of them under this ticket's scratch directory. Outside the run tree it holds ten: the evaluator's own helpers `agents.txt`, `queue.txt`, `post-write.sh`, `post-write.sh.bak`, `post-redline.sh`, `start-redline.sh`, `wave-1-dispatch-epoch.txt` and the extraction script `evaluator/extract.py`, plus `wave-1-after.txt` and `wave-1-before-repo.txt`. One further path changes hash, `wave-1-before.txt`, which hashed itself while still empty.
