## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 — the report is credited with an account of its own limits (source support)

- **Draft (line 7):** "Rapporten Mätförsök i Björkskolan, daterad 12 mars 2026, redovisar vad givarna registrerade – och vad de inte kunde registrera."
- **Material:** "Rapporten Mätförsök i Björkskolan, daterad 12 mars 2026, anger att 14 av totalt 120 registrerade lektionspass innehöll minst en mätning under 20 grader Celsius. Den säger inte hur länge temperaturen låg under gränsen. Den säger heller inte om eleverna frös."
- **What differs:** whose assertion. The material records what the report *fails to say*; the draft converts that failure into something the report itself sets out. The material also says only that ventilation, draught and perceived temperature "mättes inte" — it never says the report discusses them, nor that the sensors were incapable ("inte kunde").
- **Concrete case, compatible with the material:** the report consists of the logged series, the 14-of-120 figure and the working-limit note, with no section on what the sensors did not capture. The material is fully satisfied by such a report; under it the draft's clause is false. Nothing supplied excludes this case — the only statements about the report's coverage of limits are the two "Den säger inte …" sentences, which point the other way.
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace the em-dash clause with the report's supplied silence, e.g. "… redovisar vad givarna registrerade – men inte hur länge temperaturen låg under arbetsgränsen." (Deleting the clause outright also removes the defect.)

### Finding 2 — "experimentellt" dropped, widening the exclusion (source support)

- **Draft (line 9, heading):** "Placeringen dokumenterades men jämfördes aldrig"
- **Draft (line 13):** "Var givarna satt är dokumenterat, men placeringarna ställdes aldrig mot varandra i försöket."
- **Material:** "Givarnas placering dokumenterades men jämfördes inte experimentellt."
- **What differs:** scope. The material excludes one kind of comparison; the draft excludes all comparison, in the heading without restriction and in the body across the whole trial.
- **Concrete case, compatible with the material:** the report tabulates the two outer-wall series beside the four inner-wall series, or notes in passing that the outer-wall sensors read lower, without any experimental design — sensors were never swapped, moved or paired for a controlled contrast. That is a comparison of the placements made within the trial, and the material's "jämfördes inte experimentellt" is still satisfied. Under that case the heading and the body sentence are both false. No supplied statement excludes it; the material qualifies its denial precisely with "experimentellt".
- **Reverse direction:** with the qualification restored, nothing the material supports is lost — the draft's own conclusion ("Vad väggen betydde för ett enskilt värde går därför inte att läsa ut") follows from the absence of an *experimental* comparison, so the repair costs the argument nothing.
- **Smallest supported repair:** heading → "Placeringen dokumenterades men jämfördes aldrig experimentellt"; body → "… men placeringarna jämfördes aldrig experimentellt i försöket."

### Finding 3 — "schemat" substituted for "användningstiderna" (source support)

- **Draft (line 41):** "… med schemat bredvid kurvan, inte med en justering av styrningen."
- **Material:** "Hon rekommenderar att koppla temperaturserier till användningstider innan man ändrar styrningen." and, for the next trial, "noteringar om när rummen används".
- **What differs:** the thing named. "Användningstider", glossed by the material as "när rummen används", is actual use; "schemat" is planned use. The material treats notes on actual use as something to be added in the next trial, which is what makes the distinction live here rather than cosmetic.
- **Concrete case, compatible with the material:** a room stands empty during a scheduled lesson, or is used for an evening activity that appears in no timetable. The schedule beside the curve then names the wrong hour and the recommendation as supplied is not followed; the advice as written holds while the supported advice fails.
- **Note:** the section heading one line above ("Lägg användningstiderna bredvid temperaturserien") and the preceding sentence both get this right, so the defect is confined to the final clause.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "… med användningstiderna bredvid kurvan, inte med en justering av styrningen."
