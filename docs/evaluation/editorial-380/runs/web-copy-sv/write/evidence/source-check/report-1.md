## 1. Claim accounting

### C1 — Title (draft line 1)

> Genomgång av bokningsrutinen för föreningens gemensamma lokal

Source:
> Svale erbjuder en avgränsad genomgång av rutinerna för bokning av en gemensam lokal.
> Uppdraget gäller en lokal i en förening.

Differences: *thing named / scope.* Source "rutinerna" (plural) → draft "bokningsrutinen" (definite singular); source "en gemensam lokal" (indefinite) → draft "föreningens gemensamma lokal" (definite, possessed by the association).

- Plural → collective singular: "bokningsrutinen" is idiomatic Swedish for the booking procedure taken as a whole, and the body restates the scope broadly ("hur bokningen … fungerar"). No case separates them that is not merely a preference about number. Not a finding.
- Definite "föreningens gemensamma lokal" presupposes one shared premises per association. A case is nameable (an association with both a party room and a hobby room, the engagement covering one of them), and no supplied statement excludes it — "Uppdraget gäller en lokal i en förening" limits the engagement, it does not say the association has only one. But the lead and the price section both carry the indefinite ("en gemensam lokal", "en lokal i en förening"), so the ordinary reader in context takes the title's definite as generic, not as a uniqueness claim. **Genuinely unsettled → reported as an editorial question (Q1), not a defect.**

## 3. Findings

### F1 — "I formuläret" adds an unsupported means of giving the details (line 28)

Draft:
> I formuläret anger du namn, förening och e-postadress.

Supplied support, complete:
> Intresseanmälan sker på https://example.invalid/svale/intresse och efterfrågar namn, förening och e-postadress.

Missing support: the material says the notification *is made at* that address and *asks for* three items. It nowhere says the means is a form. Manner is a claim under the loaded Claims section ("Circumstantial detail is a claim too: duration, manner, motive, absence and background all need support").

Case in which the source holds and the draft fails: the destination page carries the line "Mejla namn, förening och e-postadress till intresse@svale.example" — the notification is then made at that address and asks for exactly those three items, while there is no form and nothing is entered in one. Compatible with the material; no supplied statement excludes it ("Intresseanmälan sker på …" fixes the location, not the mechanism).

Case in the other direction: a form exists but, say, pre-fills the association from a code in the link, so nothing is "angett" by the reader for that field — the draft's sentence fails while "efterfrågar namn, förening och e-postadress" still holds.

Smallest supported repair (also removes the 2sg/2pl mismatch noted in Q2):

> Intresseanmälan efterfrågar namn, förening och e-postadress.

### F2 — "två förenklingar" drops "möjliga" in the outcome sentence (line 10)

Draft:
> … och två förenklingar att ta ställning till.

Supplied support, complete:
> Beskriv nyttan i termer av vad styrelsen faktiskt får: en gemensam bild av rutinen och två **möjliga** förenklingar.
> … och två **möjliga** förenklingar. (offer description)

Changed support: the material qualifies the pair as *possible* simplifications in both places where it names them, and the draft itself keeps the qualifier in the bullet at line 8. At line 10 the qualifier is gone, so the outcome sentence asserts that what the board receives are two simplifications.

Case in which the source holds and the draft fails: the summary proposes merging two booking steps and moving the key handover to the caretaker; on examination the second proposal shifts work rather than reducing it and the board rejects it. "två möjliga förenklingar" still holds — both were candidates — while "två förenklingar att ta ställning till" fails, since only one of the two turned out to be a simplification. Compatible with the material; nothing supplied excludes it, and "Ingen garanti lämnas för en viss tidsbesparing eller färre konflikter" bears on outcomes of adopting a change, not on whether each proposal simplifies. "att ta ställning till" marks the proposals as undecided, not as merely possible, so it does not carry the dropped qualifier.

Case in the other direction: a proposal could be a genuine simplification the board is nevertheless not asked to decide on — then the draft's phrase fails on the decision clause while the source's holds.

Smallest supported repair:

> … och två möjliga förenklingar att ta ställning till.

### F3 — The instruction is renamed from one addressed to residents to one residents hold (line 24)

Draft:
> … och visa den instruktion som de boende har i dag.

Supplied support, complete:
> Styrelsen behöver kunna beskriva sina befintliga bokningsregler och visa nuvarande instruktion till de boende.

Changed support: "nuvarande instruktion till de boende" identifies the document by its currency and its addressees. The draft identifies it by the residents' present possession of it. These are different identifying conditions, and the precondition the reader must satisfy changes with them.

Case in which the source holds and the draft fails: the current instruction to residents is a laminated notice on the door of the shared premises and a page on the association's website; no resident has been given or holds a copy. "visa nuvarande instruktion till de boende" is satisfiable — the board can show that notice — while "den instruktion som de boende har i dag" picks out nothing, so a board reading the draft concludes it does not meet the precondition. Compatible with the material; no supplied statement says the instruction was distributed to residents or is in their possession.

Case in the other direction: the board revised the instruction to residents last month but has not yet posted it; residents still go by the old sheet. The draft's precondition then points at the superseded sheet, while the source's points at the current instruction — the two name different documents.

Smallest supported repair (restores the source's identifying condition, leaves the sentence's voice and structure intact):

> Styrelsen behöver kunna beskriva föreningens befintliga bokningsregler och visa nuvarande instruktion till de boende.

## 4. Editorial questions (not defects, no repair proposed)

- **Q1 — The title's definite "föreningens gemensamma lokal" (line 1).** The definite singular presupposes one shared premises, where the material has "en gemensam lokal" and "Uppdraget gäller en lokal i en förening". A multi-premises association is compatible with the material and not excluded by it, but the definite is idiomatic generic Swedish and the lead and price section both state the indefinite scope, so an ordinary reader in context is not misled about the engagement's extent. Whether the title should match the indefinite scope wording is an editorial call for the author, not a source-support defect.
- **Q2 — "du" at line 28 against "ni/er" at lines 3, 26 and 30.** Both are supported (§2); the mixture is address consistency, which belongs to general editorial review rather than to this comparison. F1's repair happens to remove it.
