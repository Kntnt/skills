## 4. R1

**Fail.**

R1 says that clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste. This text is a control that conforms to the anatomy. Its headline, "Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför", is a deliberate elliptical headline, and the result-bearing standfirst directly below it completes the thought. That is a stylistic choice, not a concrete visible defect. The run rewrote it anyway, and the rewrite:

- widened the scope from six classrooms to "Björkskolans luft";
- dropped the attribution to a single trial ("Mätförsöket" became "Mätningar");
- created a deviation from the anatomy that did not exist before, which the reply concedes: "rubriken har 9 ord, mot normen tre till åtta".

The disclosure is honest and every other element is preserved. Even so, an unforced change of taste to a clean text, one that alters a claim's scope and attribution and introduces a deviation from the norm, fails R1.
