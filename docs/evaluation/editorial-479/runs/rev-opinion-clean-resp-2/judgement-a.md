  - After: "…bokning av föreningslokaler, ett byte som kan ske i september och som tar bort telefonbokningen."

It does not change any claim's scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning: "kan ske" keeps the switch conditional, and that the switch removes phone booking is already the text's own claim. Its only effect is a third mention of the phone channel within four lines.
