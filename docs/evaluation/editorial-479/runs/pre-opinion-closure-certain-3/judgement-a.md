## 1. Differences

The texts differ in two places. The headline, the `Text:` byline, the subheadings, the paragraph breaks, the bold standfirst markup and every other line are byte-identical.

1. Standfirst, last sentence.
   - Before: "Här är skälen att pröva båda bokningsvägarna ett halvår till innan telefonbokningen ändå stängs för gott."
   - After: "Här är skälen att pröva båda bokningsvägarna ett halvår till."
   - Class: repair of a visible defect, by removing an unsupported claim. It changes what the claim says, in **meaning** and **certainty**: the outcome is no longer presented as settled. The standfirst now has 35 words instead of 41.
2. First section, first paragraph.
   - Before: "96 webbokningar"
   - After: "96 webbbokningar"
   - Class: mechanical correction (spelling of a compound). Under current Swedish rules, all three consonants are kept when a compound is formed. This is the proofreading pass's work and is not counted under R1.

## 2. The account

1. Standfirst clause. **Reported accurately.** Finding 1 quotes the deleted subordinate clause and gives the reason: the clause contradicts the lead and the ending. It states the repair: "Bisatsen är struken, så meningen slutar nu '… ett halvår till.'" The section on claims also records the removed claim and what a reader of the standfirst alone no longer gets. The reply gives the new standfirst length as "35 ord", which matches my count.
2. webbokningar → webbbokningar. **Reported, accurately but without naming the word.** The reply says: "korrekturläsningen på slutet rättade ett stavfel i ett sammansatt ord." Exactly one such correction was made, and it is one compound word.

The reply claims nothing beyond these two differences, and the returned text contains nothing beyond them.

## 4. R1

**Pass.** The deciding passage is the single substantive change. The run cut "innan telefonbokningen ändå stängs för gott" from the standfirst and reported it as Finding 1, with the removed claim recorded under "Borttaget". This repairs a concrete visible defect, an internal contradiction, from the text's own content. It is a legitimate removal of the claim that was itself the finding. Every other argument, quotation, attribution and the voice are left untouched. The run made no taste edits. It raised no numerical-guideline findings against limits the text meets. It attempted no verification against unavailable sources. The only other change, webbokningar → webbbokningar, is mechanical.
