## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the standfirst rewrite, "innan den ena stängs" → "innan det avgörs om den ena ska stängas", which the reply justifies with: "förutsatte att en av bokningsvägarna kommer att stängas … Det motsäger textens eget krav att alternativen ska vägas först."

The text gives no concrete, visible defect here. The headline, "Avskaffa inte telefonbokningen på ett antagande", objects only to closing *on an assumption*. Accepting that a closure may follow once the trial has produced evidence is fully consistent with that headline and with "Först då går det att väga alternativen". The supposed contradiction depends on one contestable reading of an ordinary temporal clause. The original sentence was working argument in the author's voice: its concession that one channel will probably go makes the request for a delay more credible.

R1 forbids rewriting a clean text on taste ("Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines"). The rewrite changed the certainty of the author's stated position, and it is a loss R1 asks a judge to reject. Detection and preservation are otherwise excellent: there are no false anatomy findings, and everything the expectation names outside the standfirst is preserved verbatim. That makes this a narrow fail, decided by one unwarranted change to a claim.
