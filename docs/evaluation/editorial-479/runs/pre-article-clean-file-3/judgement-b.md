## 1. Differences

There is one difference between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. It is in the headline (line 1). The frontmatter, the formatting, the standfirst, the byline, the body, the quotation and the ending are all byte-identical.

- Before: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför` (48 characters, 7 words)
- After: `# Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när luften var kall, inte varför` (64 characters, 10 words)
- Class: a change of taste that also changes what a claim says, in **meaning** and **scope**. The original elliptical "när" can be read two ways, and the text supports both: when the air was cold, and when to look closer (standfirst: "pekar ut tillfällen att undersöka närmare"; quote: "Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare"). The new headline keeps only the first reading. It also states outright, without qualification, that the trial shows "när luften var kall". The body bounds that claim twice: by sensor placement ("luften där den sitter") and by the office's own 20-degree threshold. This is not the repair of a visible defect. The original is a well-formed, common Swedish headline ellipsis, and the standfirst directly below it resolves it. It is also not mechanical.
