## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the headline rewrite. "Mätförsöket i Björkskolan visar när, inte varför" is a conforming headline: 7 words and 48 characters, read directly above a standfirst that resolves "när" and "varför" in its first two sentences. Its ellipsis is not a concrete visible defect. It is a matter of headline taste. R1 says "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste."

The rewrite also changed what the headline claims (meaning). The trial is replaced by the sensors, and the reply admits the headline no longer tells the reader that the measurements came from a trial. The rewrite also moved the headline out of the anatomy's word norm, and the reply then presents that as a deviation found in the text.

Everything else the expectation asks to preserve was preserved. None of the named rejections was made in form.
