## 4. R1

**Fail.**

This is a control text that conforms to the anatomy. The Skill changed one of the author's claims there, and no visible defect justified the change. The deciding passage is the deleted clause "… ett halvår till innan den ena stängs". The Skill calls it a contradiction with the body. The body does not support that.

- The reply's own first support is that the introduction "vill att bytet skjuts upp, inte att det stoppas". To postpone the switch is to let it come after the trial. That matches "innan den ena stängs".
- The introduction's "Pröva först …" has the same order: first the trial, then the switch.
- The standfirst's first sentence ("I september kan telefonbokningen … försvinna") presents the closure as the decision on the table. Read that way, "innan den ena stängs" refers to that planned closure. It is not a concession by the author.
- Only "Först då går det att väga alternativen" leaves the outcome open. A reading where the standfirst presupposes a closure is possible, but the text does not make that a concrete visible defect. It is an interpretive preference.

The change removed the standfirst's chronology (trial, then closure) and narrowed what it says. The Skill reports this itself as a lost claim. R1 requires a clean text to keep its claims outside genuine findings, so this counts as a rejected loss, not a legitimate removal.

Everything else the expectation asks for holds:
- Every named passage is preserved.
- Nothing was flattened or hedged.
- None of the rejected findings was made.
- The account is complete and honest.

R1 fails on this single unwarranted change to a claim.
