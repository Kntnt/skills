## 1. Differences

A line diff of `work/input.md` against the returned text, which is the fenced block at the end of `response.md`, finds exactly one difference. The frontmatter (there is none in either file), headline, standfirst, byline, both subheadings, formatting and every other sentence are byte-identical.

- **Before:** "En temperaturgivare mäter luften där den sitter. Det gör placeringen viktig när fastighetskontoret ska tolka fyra veckors mätningar i Björkskolans sex klassrum."
  **After:** "En temperaturgivare mäter luften där den sitter. Det är utgångspunkten när fastighetskontoret ska tolka fyra veckors mätningar i Björkskolans sex klassrum."
  **Class:** a change to what a claim says, of type **meaning**. The original claim is that the sensor's placement matters when the readings are interpreted. That is an inference the text draws from its own first sentence, and it needs no outside fact. The new sentence drops that inference and replaces it with a vaguer framing ("utgångspunkten"). It also weakens the link to the later detail "likadant placerade givare": the reader is no longer told why placement is worth mentioning. This is not the repair of a visible defect (see section 4). It is a change made on an editorial judgement about the text's angle.
