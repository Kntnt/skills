## 2. R1: fail

**Preservation.** Two sentences were removed. Neither was a concrete visible defect. Both are grammatical, plain, in the author's voice, and load-bearing: each one states what the text is *not* claiming. The account itself concedes that no repair was attempted — "I båda fallen pekade fyndet ut hela meningen som defekten, och borttagningen fick därför stå" — which is an admission that the finding was defined so as to consume the whole sentence rather than to fix anything within it. Removing a hedge from an otherwise clean text is a change of taste that lands as a change to a claim, and R1 forbids exactly that: claims outside findings must be preserved, and clean texts may not be rewritten to taste.

**Reporting.** To the Skill's credit, the account is honest and accurate on both counts:

- Removal #2 is reported under "Borttagna påståenden", quoted verbatim and correctly paraphrased ("påståendet att det som beskrivs är ett dokument och inte en scen från ett bestämt möte"). Reported, and reported accurately.
- Removal #4 is likewise quoted verbatim and correctly paraphrased ("påståendet att iakttagelsen är skribentens egen reflektion och inte något hon har mätt hos andra"). Reported, and reported accurately.
- Change #3 (em dash to en dash) is not itemised, but the account does state that a closing mechanical pass was run. Acceptable for a mechanical correction.
- Difference #1 (broken frontmatter delimiter) is not reported at all.

The account closes "Inga fynd kvarstår", so nothing is declared irreparable; both removals are presented as legitimate. They are not. The reporting is truthful, but truthful reporting of an unjustified loss does not convert it into a legitimate removal. R1 fails on preservation, not on disclosure.
