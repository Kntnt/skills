## Cross-cutting checks

- **Invented particulars.** The boundaries forbid real meetings, journeys, childhood memories, dialogue lines and bodily sensations. The draft contains none: no dated or located meeting, no named colleague, no quoted speech, no remembered scene, no physical sensation. "Timmen är bokad" (row 13) is voiced reasoning, not an occasion; "en verklig dagordning" (row 23) is a hypothetical, not a described one.
- **Statistics and trends.** The boundaries state that none is established. The draft asserts no count, proportion, frequency or trend, and row 14 carries over the explicit disclaimer "inte något jag har mätt hos andra".
- **Evaluative characterisations.** No count is called high, low or modest anywhere in the draft; the only evaluations ("kan vara viktigare", "komiken blir inte mindre") are the author's own, rest on supported material, and are marked as hers.
- **Causation.** No causal verb is applied to any sequence or correlation. "Samtal kan skapa förtroende" (row 16) carries the source's own modal and verb; "alltså har något hänt" (row 13) is the criticised inference, not an endorsed one.
- **Person, number, natural gender.** Draft pronouns: "jag/mig/min" (1sg, the author — the brief marks her female, and the draft asserts no gender for her); "vi/varandras" (1pl, author plus colleagues, as in the notes); "de" (3pl, = kolleger, asserting number only); "den" in rows 9 and 23 (utrum, = dagordningen, frågan — grammatical gender, no natural-gender assertion); "andra" (row 14, matching the source's "andra"). No pronoun introduces a person, number or natural-gender feature the material does not carry.
- **Attribution.** No unnamed study, expert or survey is invoked. Every attributed view is the author's own and is marked as such.
- **Length.** The brief sets "Cirka 350 ord om underlaget bär det"; the draft body runs roughly 295 words. The shortfall follows from the extent of the material and is the correct response under the Claims section ("deliver the length the material supports"), not a licence taken. Closing the gap would require further material — for example, a second concrete observation of the template, or of another library form — which the notes do not supply. No invention was used to reach length.
- **Headings.** The brief leaves subheadings optional; the draft uses none, so no heading-level claim exists beyond the title (row 1).
