## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 — "Twenty-four calls" asserts a count the report does not make

- **Draft:** "Twenty-four calls therefore cannot tell us what share of Lervik's residents are unable to book digitally."
- **Source:** "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt." With: "Under försöket gjordes 96 bokningar via webb och 24 via telefon. Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer."
- **What differs:** the thing counted. The material counts twenty-four *phone bookings*; the draft counts twenty-four *calls*.
- **Test, both directions:** one caller books two venues in a single telephone call and the rest are one booking per call — twenty-three calls, twenty-four phone bookings. The source statement holds; "Twenty-four calls" fails. In the other direction, a call that produces no booking would put calls above bookings. Nothing supplied excludes either case: the material says only that bookings, not people, are counted, and says nothing whatever about calls.
- **Why it bites here:** the sentence before it has just told the reader the report counts bookings rather than unique people, and this sentence then converts the booking count into a call count.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Twenty-four phone bookings therefore cannot tell us what share of Lervik's residents are unable to book digitally."

### Finding 2 — "nobody has yet put a number on" widens the absence beyond the documents

- **Draft:** "The board is being asked to close a channel for good on the strength of an inconvenience nobody has yet put a number on."
- **Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna."
- **What differs:** scope, and whose knowledge. The material states an absence *in the documents before the board*; the draft states an absence in the world.
- **Test, both directions:** the administration timed both routes internally during the pilot and left the measurement out of the papers for the 18 June meeting. The source statement holds — nothing of the kind is in *handlingarna* — while "nobody has yet put a number on it" fails. Nothing supplied excludes an unpublished measurement; the material's negative is explicitly attached to *handlingarna*, and the judging boundary says only that no savings are *belagda*, not that none exist. If in fact nobody has measured it, both hold.
- **Note:** the immediately preceding sentence gets the scope right ("the documents contain no measurement…"), so the widening is unforced; the argument does not need it.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "…on the strength of an inconvenience the documents put no number on."

### Finding 3 — "per booking" narrows what the proposed trial would record

- **Draft:** "During it, the administration records the time each route takes per booking…"
- **Source:** "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg…"
- **What differs:** the unit of measurement. *Tidsåtgång per bokningsväg* is time taken per *booking route*; the draft makes it time per *booking* on each route.
- **Test, both directions:** the administration logs total staff time on the phone route and on the web route across the six months and reports the two totals, never dividing either by the number of bookings. That satisfies *registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg*; it does not satisfy "the time each route takes per booking". Nothing supplied fixes the unit below the route.
- **Why it matters:** the draft's closing argument leans on this measurement ("set the work each route costs staff against what it is worth"), so the added unit is load-bearing rather than incidental.
- **Smallest supported repair:** delete the two added words — "the administration records the time each booking route takes".

### Finding 4 — "We have not costed that trial" converts a withheld claim into a denial

- **Draft:** "We have not costed that trial and we make no claim to be paying for it."
- **Source:** "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."
- **What differs:** modality, and whose assertion. The material reports what the association *does not claim*; the draft's first clause asserts as fact that the costing was not done. The source's careful formulation covers financing and costing alike; the draft keeps it for financing and drops it for costing.
- **Test, both directions:** the association has an internal estimate of what the trial would cost but deliberately rests nothing on it, because the costing is the board's to make. "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha … kostnadsberäknat försöket" holds exactly as written; "We have not costed that trial" fails. Nothing supplied excludes this: the material states the absence of a *claim*, never the absence of the act.
- **Secondary shift in the same sentence:** *ha finansierat* (to have financed) becomes "to be paying for it", moving completed financing to an ongoing one. The repair carries the source's aspect as well.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "We make no claim to have funded or costed that trial."

Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används.” — is rendered as "A booking made by phone is not proof that somebody cannot use the web. No implicit referent has been resolved beyond what the material settles. No obstruction to the intended reading was found in any other translated passage; the shifts recorded in Findings 1–4 are source-support defects, not rendering defects.
