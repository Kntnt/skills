### 2.1 Cases tested and set aside

Recorded so the reasoning is auditable; these are not findings.

1. **"On 18 June the council executive board is asked to remove …" (A1).** Case tested: the item is withdrawn from the 18 June agenda, so the report proposed removal ahead of that meeting but the board was never asked on that date. Set aside by "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september" — a tjänsteutlåtande submitted *inför* a named meeting is the proposal put to that meeting, so what the board is asked there is removal.
2. **"gives one reason" (A2).** Case tested: the report gives a second reason the material does not report. Set aside by the definite singular in "Motivet i utlåtandet är att …", which identifies the motive rather than one among several.
3. **"no measurement of how long either route takes staff" (C2).** Case tested: the documents contain a time measurement of something other than route duration. Set aside by "Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna", which excludes any time measurement; the draft's narrower claim is entailed.
4. **"how used to digital services people are" (B3).** Case tested: the report measures general digital experience but not digital-services familiarity. The draft's negative is the weaker one and is entailed by "Den mäter inte … digital vana"; it asserts nothing unsupported.
5. **"the administration records the time … and asks users" (D2).** Case tested: read as present indicative fact, this would assert the administration is already doing so. Set aside because "During it" binds both verbs to the not-yet-existing trial proposed in the preceding sentence, so the proposal-internal *ska* is the only available reading.
6. **"it will be a better one for being taken with the cost of both routes on the table" (E4).** Case tested: the trial records time per route but nobody converts it into a cost, so the later decision is taken without either route's cost before the board. The sentence is the author's advocacy for the sequence, and what it presents as given — that the trial exists to let the work cost be weighed against user value — is supplied directly: "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" and "kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna". No cost figure is asserted, so the cost uncertainty survives.
7. **"When the trial has produced figures" (E3).** Case tested: the trial runs but yields no usable figures, so *därefter* holds while the draft's condition does not. The draft attaches a condition the author advocates rather than reporting one the material states; the condition is the trial's own design (D2), and the option set and the decision-maker are unchanged.
8. **Heading E1 against E3's later decision.** Case tested: the heading implies the removal/change/keep decision itself falls on 18 June. Set aside because E3 states its own timing ("When the trial has produced figures"), and a single 18 June resolution — to postpone, and to decide after the trial — is the ordinary reading of the pair.
9. **"community venues" for *lokaler* / *föreningslokaler*.** Case tested: a community venue that is not a *föreningslokal*, or the reverse. Set aside because the referent is fixed by count in both places — "all seven community venues" against "alla sju lokaler", "two venues" against "två lokaler" — and by the report title kept in Swedish at B1.
10. **"the council executive board" for *kommunstyrelsen*, "the council" for *kommunen* (D7).** The draft keeps the two bodies distinct with two distinct English terms, so no claim about one is transferred to the other.
11. **Omission of *Kommunens* at B1 and *föreningen* at F1.** Omissions that assert nothing contrary to the material; the figures remain attributed to the report as the brief requires.
