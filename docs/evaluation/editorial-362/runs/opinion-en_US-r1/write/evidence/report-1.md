## 2. Findings

Five findings, all source-support. No translation findings; see §3.

### Finding 1 — bookings by phone reported as calls (§1.3)

- **Draft:** "Those 24 calls therefore cannot tell anyone what share of Lervik's residents are
  unable to book online."
- **Source:** "Under försöket gjordes 96 bokningar via webb och 24 via telefon." /
  "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan
  boka digitalt."
- **What differs:** the thing counted. The source counts 24 bookings made by phone. The draft counts
  24 calls. Nothing in the material connects bookings to calls one for one.
- **Case in which one holds and the other fails:** one resident telephones once and books three
  slots for her choir. The report records three phone bookings; one call was made. The source's "24
  bokningar via telefon" holds and the draft's "24 calls" fails. The material does not describe this
  case and need not; no supplied statement excludes it, and the paragraph's own point — that the
  report counts bookings and not people — is precisely that a booking count does not carry over to
  anything else. The converse also stands: a caller who has to phone back after a dropped call
  produces two calls and one booking.
- **Support missing:** any statement in the material about the number of calls.
- **Smallest repair:** "Those 24 phone bookings therefore cannot tell anyone what share of Lervik's
  residents are unable to book online."

### Finding 2 — an unquantified saving taken as an existing one (§1.4)

- **Draft:** "A permanent change is being proposed on a saving nobody has yet put a figure on."
- **Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna." /
  "Inga lagkrav, protester, diskrimineringsfall, partipolitiska motiv eller besparingar är belagda."
  / "Motivet i utlåtandet är att personalen ska slippa föra in uppgifter i två flöden."
- **What differs:** certainty, and the state of knowledge. The source establishes that no saving is
  demonstrated and none is calculated. The draft's "a saving nobody has yet put a figure on" takes
  the saving to exist and to be merely unquantified — "yet" places it as a figure awaiting
  arithmetic. The Claims contract holds that what is unclaimed is not thereby known, and the
  boundary states that no savings are established.
- **Case in which one holds and the other fails:** removing the phone route yields no saving at all.
  Staff stop entering details twice, but the calls still arrive and are now spent talking residents
  through the web form, so the hours are unchanged or higher. Every supplied statement still holds:
  the memo's stated motive is the double entry, not money, and no savings are established. The
  draft's sentence fails, because there is no saving for anyone to put a figure on.
- **Support missing:** any statement that a saving exists.
- **Smallest repair:** "A permanent change is being proposed before anyone has shown what it would
  save." This keeps the criticism at full strength and keeps the saving unestablished.

### Finding 3 — an absence of claim reported as an absence of the act (§1.6)

- **Draft:** "The trial costs something, and we have neither funded nor costed it."
- **Source:** "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."
- **What differs:** whose assertion, and therefore the state of knowledge. The source is about what
  the association claims: it makes no claim to have funded or costed the trial. The draft is about
  what the association did: it has done neither. An unmade claim is not a demonstrated absence.
- **Case in which one holds and the other fails:** the association's treasurer has worked up a rough
  cost for the six months and set aside a small sum towards it, but the association advances no
  claim about either in this material and rests its case on the board's own weighing. The source
  statement holds exactly; the draft's "we have neither funded nor costed it" is false.
- **Support missing:** any statement that the acts did not occur, as opposed to being unclaimed.
- **Smallest repair:** "The trial costs something, and we make no claim to have funded or costed
  it." The next sentence, "That weighing is the board's to do", is unaffected, and the cost
  uncertainty the boundary requires is preserved.

### Finding 4 — the measure changed from per route to per booking (§1.5)

- **Draft:** "During that time the administration records how much time each route takes per booking
  ..."
- **Source:** "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg ..."
- **What differs:** the thing measured. "tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" is the time each booking route
  takes. The draft adds a normalisation the proposal does not specify: time per booking, within each
  route.
- **Case in which one holds and the other fails:** the administration logs the hours its phone
  handling and its web handling consume over the six months and reports two totals, without dividing
  either by the number of bookings it handled. It has recorded "tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" as
  proposed; it has not recorded how much time each route takes per booking. No supplied statement
  excludes this design, and the proposal is the association's own, so the draft is describing its
  own proposal with a specification the material does not carry.
- **Support missing:** any statement that time is to be recorded per booking.
- **Smallest repair:** delete two words — "records how much time each route takes and asks users
  ...".

### Finding 5 — labour cost narrowed to staff time in an attributed position (§1.5)

- **Draft:** "I am against deciding before the municipality can weigh the staff time a channel costs
  against what it is worth to the people who use it."
- **Source:** "Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för
  användarna."
- **What differs:** the thing measured, inside a position attributed to a named person.
  "arbetskostnaden" is the labour cost; the draft substitutes the hours. A change of meaning in
  crossing languages, outside quoted speech, is a source-support matter, which is why it sits here
  and not in §3.
- **Case in which one holds and the other fails:** phone bookings take more staff time per booking
  than web bookings, but are handled at the reception desk by an assistant on a lower grade, while
  the web queue is cleared by the booking officer. Weighed in hours, the phone route is the more
  expensive; weighed in labour cost, it is the cheaper. Her stated position decides the question one
  way and the draft's decides it the other. Nothing in the material fixes the two routes to one pay
  grade; "personalen ska slippa föra in uppgifter i två flöden" establishes that staff do the
  entering, not what their time costs. The same divergence follows from any overtime or bought-in
  switchboard time on one route only.
- **Support missing:** any statement equating the labour cost with staff hours. The trial's
  "tidsåtgång" is what would be recorded, but the material states her test as the cost, not the
  recording.
- **Smallest repair:** "... before the municipality can weigh the labor cost a channel carries
  against what it is worth to the people who use it." (Spelling per the resolved en_US.) The lead's
  "figures on what each route costs" already uses the source's own term and needs no change.

## 3. Translation check on permitted quoted material

Her released position — "Detta får citeras eller refereras" — is carried in the draft as her own
first-person prose rather than inside quotation marks, which the release permits and which the
byline makes natural. Checked for the same meaning and distinctive voice in idiomatic American
speech:

- "Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben." →
  "A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone cannot use the web." Meaning, scope
  ("en bokning" as any one booking) and the flat denial of proof are intact. "comes in by phone"
  renders "går via telefon" in ordinary American office register rather than Swedish syntax. No
  hedge added, none removed.
- "Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används." → "It is a reason to find
  out why the phone is still being used." The distinctive move of the remark — the same fact refused
  as proof and accepted as a reason to inquire — is kept, along with the parallel that carries it.
  "a reason", not "the reason", matches "ett skäl". The agentless passive matches "används" and
  keeps her from naming who is using it.
- "Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning." → "I am not against digital booking." Stance preserved
  as a denial of opposition, not converted into endorsement.

No obstruction to an ordinary American reader's reading was found in any of these, and no implicit
referent was made explicit beyond what the material settles. No translation findings.
