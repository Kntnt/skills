## 3. Cross-language term tests

Each case below was tested in both directions and set aside by a quoted supplied statement; none is a finding.

**(a) "the same details in two separate flows" for "föra in uppgifter i två flöden".** Case in which the draft fails and the source holds: staff enter *different* particulars in each flow, so information goes into two flows but nothing is entered twice. Set aside by "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är **dubbel administration**" — administration performed twice is duplicated work, which is what "the same details" names. "separate" adds nothing to "två flöden" beyond their being two.

**(b) "online" / "the web" / "book online" for "via webb" and "boka digitalt".** Case in which the draft fails: a third, non-web online channel (e-mail, an app) carried further bookings during the trial, so more than 96 bookings were made online. Set aside by "ett permanent byte till **enbart digital bokning**" (brief) read with "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla **båda kanalerna**" and "med **båda bokningsvägarna** kvar" — the material fixes exactly two channels and equates the digital one with the web, since removing "telefonbokning" is what leaves "enbart digital bokning". Reverse direction ("boka digitalt" rendered "book online"): the same quotations exclude a digital-but-not-web route.

**(c) "ability" for "funktionsförmåga".** Case in which the draft fails: the report measures some ability of bookers other than functional capacity (needing assistance, language), so it does not measure "nothing about ability". Set aside by "**Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer.** Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana." — a report that counts bookings rather than people records no person-level attribute at all, so no ability of bookers is measured. The three-item list is preserved item for item, and in that list ("age, ability or digital habits") an ordinary reader takes "ability" in the functional sense the Swedish term carries.

**(d) "digital habits" for "digital vana".** No case: "vana" is accustomedness to digital use, and the claim is a negative one about what the report measures, which the draft carries unchanged.

**(e) "labor cost" for "arbetskostnaden", "what it is worth to the people who use it" for "värdet för användarna".** No case: both terms are carried with the same referents and the same weighing relation, and the draft does not convert either into money or into a figure.

## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — source support: "figures on what each route costs" names a measurement the proposal does not produce

**Draft (lead, paragraph 1):** "Six months would give the board what it does not have today: figures on what each route costs and what it is for."

**Supplied material:** "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera **tidsåtgång per bokningsväg** och be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb." And, on what is absent today: "Någon **tidsmätning** eller **ekonomisk besparingsberäkning** finns inte i handlingarna."

**What differs — the thing measured.** The proposal's measure is time spent per booking route. The draft names a different measured thing: what each route *costs*. The material keeps these two apart in its own words, listing "tidsmätning" and "ekonomisk besparingsberäkning" as two separate missing items; a measurement of the first is not a production of the second.

**Case in which the draft fails and the source holds (compatible with the material):** over the six months the administration logs, say, six minutes of staff time per phone booking and two per web booking and nothing else, no hourly rate or overhead being applied and no channel-level running costs (telephony, system licence) being gathered. The board then has time figures per route and still has no figure for what either route costs. "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" is satisfied in full; "figures on what each route costs" is not. No supplied statement excludes this case: "Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna" states her aim, not that the recorded time will be converted into cost, and "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" concerns the cost of the trial, which the draft correctly leaves to the board.

**Reverse direction:** a board handed costed figures for each route would satisfy the draft while going beyond what the proposal undertakes to record.

This is the draft's own account of what its proposal yields, presented as given, so it is not shielded as reasoning or transition. The paired body sentence ("the administration records how much time each route takes") is correct; only the lead's compression changes the thing measured.

**Smallest supported repair** — keep the sentence, its structure and its advocacy, and name the measure the proposal actually specifies:

> Six months would give the board what it does not have today: figures on what each route takes in staff time and what it is for.

("staff time" is carried by "tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" together with "personalen"; the author's weighing of "arbetskostnaden" against "värdet för användarna" then still stands in paragraph 5, unchanged.)

## 5. Translation of quoted speech

The one passage the material marks as quotable — ”Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben. Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används.” ("Detta får citeras eller refereras.") — appears in the draft without quotation marks, in the author's own first-person section: "A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone cannot use the web. It is a reason to find out why the phone is still being used." The draft contains no quotation marks at all, so nothing is presented as another person's exact words and no assembled or unapproved quotation exists.

Checked as a rendering nonetheless, since the words are hers:

- **Meaning.** Both sentences map one to one. "är inte ett bevis för" → "is not proof that"; "någon inte kan använda webben" → "someone cannot use the web"; "ett skäl att ta reda på" → "a reason to find out"; "varför telefonen fortfarande används" → "why the phone is still being used", with the passive and "fortfarande" both kept. Nothing narrowed, widened or sharpened.
- **Stance and certainty.** The flat declarative and the absence of hedging are hers in both languages; no hedge added, none removed. No blame is introduced.
- **Distinctive wording.** The move from a denial to a redirection ("not proof ... it is a reason to find out") is the passage's shape and is preserved. "comes in by phone" for "går via telefon" is idiomatic American for a booking arriving through a channel and does not flatten her phrasing into the surrounding register.
- **Nothing additive.** No fact, figure, name or connection appears that she did not supply; the implicit referents ("the phone", "the web") are settled by the supplied context.

No translation finding.
