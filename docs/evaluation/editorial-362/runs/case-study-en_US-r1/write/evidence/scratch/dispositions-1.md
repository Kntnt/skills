# Validation of report-1.md

## Accounting check

The report covers the whole draft: title, ingress, all four section headings, every body paragraph, all three quotations, the call to action and the publication note. Each pairing sets a draft passage beside a quoted source passage.

Accounting mismatches detected:

- The heading "One picture of the work" is not paired anywhere. It is an evaluative-sounding heading over the section that states the team's aim. Support: "The team wanted staff on different shifts to see the same information" and Lind's "Having one view of the reports helps us". It reads as the aim, not as an achieved outcome, so I treat it as supported, but the gap in the accounting is recorded.
- The pairing for the bridge "Lind's advice for the next building concerns that preparation" does not address the referent of "that preparation", which in the draft's sentence order can attach to Svale Systems' training rather than to the categories work. Not a source-support defect, but the wording is repaired for accuracy.

## Dispositions

**F1 — title fixes the number of shifts at two. ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material names a morning and an evening shift only inside Lind's quotation about what the team wanted, and otherwise says "staff on different shifts". No supplied statement caps the count at two. The title asserted it in its own voice.
Repair made: title becomes "Elm Quay Housing gave two buildings one repair log" — two buildings is stated by the material ("a shared repair log in two buildings").

**F2 — "the team's own note". ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material says "Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025". No statement names the maintenance team as the note's author. "The team's own note" both names an author the material does not supply and adds a self-reporting frame.
Repair made: "Elm Quay's internal note records …".

**F3 — the count and the comparison without their scope and qualification. ACCEPTED IN PART.**
Rejected in part, with evidence: the proposition "31 reports entered" is the material's own ("31 repair reports were entered"), and the report's own concrete case (48 arriving, 17 emergencies never entered, 31 entered) is one in which the draft's statement and the source statement both hold, so it does not show the draft failing. The defect the case does establish is scope: a reader meets the figure without knowing what it leaves out.
Accepted: the exclusions are carried into the ingress, and the median comparison is anchored to "the preceding eight weeks" rather than left open. The workload qualification stays in the body; the ingress carries the stronger qualification, the note's non-attribution.
Repair made: "records 31 reports entered, emergencies and earlier work excluded, and a median time from report to assignment shorter than in the preceding eight weeks — a difference the note does not attribute to the software."

**F4 — "the same reports" narrows "the same information". ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material's aim is "to see the same information"; Lind's "see what the morning shift had already done" is status, not report text. "Reports" is the narrower thing.
Repair made: ingress now reads "could see the same information", matching the body.

**F5 — "Chosen on one test" asserts a sole basis. ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material says the team "chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair" — one test performed, not one test relied on. The absence of a supplier comparison in the material is an absence of material, not a statement that no other criterion applied.
Repair made: heading becomes "Chosen after a status test".

**F6 — the workload qualification attributed to the note. ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material asserts "The periods had different workloads" in its own voice and attributes only the non-attribution to the note ("the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software"). The draft placed the workload fact inside the note.
Repair made: "The two periods carried different workloads, and the note does not attribute the difference to the software."

**F7 — Lind's qualified assessment presented as the team's verdict. ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material says "Her final quotation is her actual, qualified assessment" and records no other person's view. The heading "The team's own verdict" over an unattributed quotation made one supervisor's judgement collective.
Repair made: heading becomes "After eight weeks", and the quotation is attributed in place: "Lind's own assessment of the eight weeks:".

**F8 — "not been extended" changes what is denied and drops "yet". ACCEPTED.**
Evidence: the material says "The trial has not yet expanded". Extension in time and expansion in reach are different denials, and "yet" carries the openness the following sentence depends on.
Repair made: "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide once it has checked how the categories work for larger repairs."
