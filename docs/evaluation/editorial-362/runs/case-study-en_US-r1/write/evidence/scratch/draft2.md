# Elm Quay Housing gave two buildings one repair log

For eight weeks, Elm Quay Housing's maintenance team ran a shared repair log in two buildings so that staff on different shifts could see the same information. Elm Quay's internal note records 31 reports entered, emergencies and earlier work excluded, and a median time from report to assignment shorter than in the preceding eight weeks — a difference the note does not attribute to the software.

## One picture of the work

Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats. Before the trial, telephone reports and emails were stored separately. In September 2025 the maintenance team decided to try a shared repair log in two buildings, so that staff working different shifts would have the same information in front of them.

Maya Lind, the maintenance supervisor, explains the aim and the division of work:

“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

## Chosen after a status test

The team chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair. No comparison with another supplier is available for this account.

The categories were the team's own, and residents could still report a fault by telephone. Svale Systems configured the log and trained six staff over two sessions. Lind's advice for the next building concerns the work on those categories:

“We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

## What the note records

Elm Quay's internal trial note of 4 December 2025 records 31 repair reports entered, excluding emergencies and work ordered before the trial. The median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period. The two periods carried different workloads, and the note does not attribute the difference to the software. Cost, resident satisfaction, and time to completion were not measured.

## After eight weeks

Lind's own assessment of the trial:

“I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

Lind did not recommend Svale Systems to every housing company. The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide once it has checked how the categories work for larger repairs.
