# Measure first, then remove the phone

*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

On June 18 the municipal executive board takes up a proposal to make all seven association facilities bookable online only from September. Öppna beslut asks the board to postpone that switch, keep both booking paths for six months, and measure what each one takes in staff time.

## What the pilot report can and cannot tell us

The municipality's pilot report *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, dated April 8, 2026, covers an eight-week trial in two facilities. It records 96 bookings made online and 24 made by phone. It counts bookings rather than people, and it measures neither age, nor ability, nor digital habit.

So those 24 phone bookings cannot tell us what share of Lervik's residents are unable to book online. They cannot tell us the opposite either. A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone can't use the web. It is a reason to find out why the phone is still being used.

## A removal proposed without a single time measurement

The staff memo before the June 18 meeting proposes that phone booking be dropped in all seven facilities from September. The reason it gives is that staff should not have to enter the same details in two flows. That objection is real: double administration is work, and the administration is entitled to say so.

But the documents hold no measurement of the time either path takes and no calculation of what removing one would save. The board is being asked to close a channel for the whole municipality on the strength of an inconvenience the documents put no number on.

## Six months, both paths, and figures worth having

Öppna beslut proposes a six-month trial in all seven facilities with both booking paths kept open. During it, the administration records how long each path takes and asks users to state voluntarily why they chose the phone or the web.

That yields the two things the current documents lack: what the double handling costs in staff time, and what the phone is doing for the people who use it. We make no claim to have costed this trial, and what it costs is for the board to weigh against what it would learn.

## What the board can do on June 18

Postpone the permanent switch, run the six months, and take a position on what that trial costs. I am not against digital booking. I am against settling the question in June on documents that contain no measurement of what it costs. After those six months the board can remove the phone channel, change it, or keep it — and say why.
