## 2. Translation check on the rendered speech

Sanna Ek's supplied words: "Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben. Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används."

Draft: "A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone can't use the web. It is a reason to find out why the phone is still being used."

- **Meaning**: "inte ett bevis för" → "not proof that" — same denial of probative force, neither narrowed nor widened. "ett skäl att ta reda på" → "a reason to find out" — one reason among possible others, not *the* reason; the indefinite is preserved.
- **Stance**: she declines to draw a conclusion and asks for an inquiry; the draft does the same and blames nobody.
- **Certainty**: no hedge in the source, none added; no confidence added either — "not proof" is not upgraded to "no evidence".
- **Distinctive wording**: the two-beat construction (flat denial, then redirection to a question) survives intact, including the pointed "fortfarande" → "still".
- **Idiomatic American speech**: "comes in by phone", "can't use the web", "find out why" are natural spoken American English rather than calqued Swedish syntax. I can identify no concrete obstruction to reading this as speech, and the contraction "can't" suits the register.
- **Implicit referents**: none required clarification; "the web" and "the phone" are settled by the surrounding material as the two booking paths.

Her reported stance "Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning" → "I am not against digital booking": same stance, same scope, no added endorsement, idiomatic.

No translation findings.

## 3. Findings

### Finding 1 — Title commits the author to an outcome the material leaves open (source support)

- **Draft**: "# Measure first, then remove the phone"
- **Source it would need**: none exists. The material's closing position is "Därefter kan den avgöra om en kanal ska tas bort, ändras eller behållas", and the boundary requires "Slutet ska namnge kommunstyrelsens möjliga beslut, med kostnadsosäkerheten bevarad". Nothing in the material has the author asking for removal at any point, before or after measurement.
- **What differs**: modality and the state of knowledge. The source keeps all three outcomes open after the trial; the title's sequencing imperative presents removal as the step that follows measurement. The claims contract states this boundary holds in the title as much as in the body.
- **Concrete case, both directions**: after the six months the recorded time shows phone handling costs little and the voluntary statements show it serves people who need it, and Öppna beslut argues to keep the channel. That case is fully compatible with the supplied material — indeed "behållas" is one of the three outcomes the material names — and in it the title's instruction fails while every supplied statement still holds. Conversely, if the phone is removed after measurement, both hold. No supplied statement excludes the keep case; the material names it explicitly. The case therefore stands, and it also sets the title against the draft's own closing sentence, which correctly preserves all three outcomes.
- **Smallest supported repair**: `# Measure first, then decide about the phone` — keeps the imperative, the sequencing and the sharpness, and restores the openness the material and the final paragraph both carry. (`# Measure first, then decide` is equally supported if the shorter line is preferred.)

No other findings. Every remaining factual and attributed claim in the draft is paired above with a supplied passage that carries it at the same scope, time, modality and certainty, and no fact, figure, motive, legal requirement, protest, discrimination case, party-political motive or saving absent from the material has been introduced.
