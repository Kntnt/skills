## 2. Findings (source support)

### Finding 1 — "measure what each one costs" measures something the proposal does not produce

- **Draft:** "Öppna beslut asks the board to postpone that switch, keep both booking paths for six months, and measure what each one costs."
- **Source:** "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg och be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb." And: "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till."
- **What differs:** the thing measured. The proposal records elapsed time per path; it produces no cost figure for either path. The material keeps *kostnad* as an open question for the board, distinct from the *tidsåtgång* the trial records.
- **Case in one direction:** the six months run exactly as proposed, the administration logs minutes per booking on each path, and no hourly rate, overhead or telephony charge is ever applied to those minutes. The source statement holds; "measure what each one costs" fails, because nothing has measured what either path costs.
- **Case in the other:** a costing of each path is produced from existing finance data without any per-booking timing — the draft's claim would hold while the material's does not.
- **Nothing supplied excludes the first case:** the only stated measurement instruction is "registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg".
- **Smallest repair:** "…keep both booking paths for six months, and measure what each one takes in staff time." (The body already uses this frame: "what the double handling costs in staff time".)

### Finding 2 — "without a single measurement" widens an absence the draft itself contradicts

- **Draft (heading):** "A removal proposed without a single measurement". Second location, closing paragraph: "documents that contain no measurement of it".
- **Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna."
- **What differs:** scope of the absence. The material denies two specific things — a time measurement and a financial savings calculation — in the documents. The draft denies measurement as such.
- **Case:** the documents include the pilot report, and that report measures 96 online and 24 phone bookings over eight weeks. That is a measurement, and it is in the decision material; the draft itself states it two paragraphs earlier ("It records 96 bookings made online and 24 made by phone") and later concedes the documents contain more than nothing ("the two things the current documents lack"). So the material's statement holds while "not a single measurement" fails. No supplied statement excludes the case; the material asserts it.
- **Smallest repair:** heading → "A removal proposed without a single time measurement". Closing → "…on documents that contain no measurement of what it costs." Both keep the sharpness the boundaries invite ("skarpt … utan tandlös neutralisering") while denying only what the material denies.

### Finding 3 — "nobody has put a number on" moves the absence from the documents to everyone

- **Draft:** "The board is being asked to close a channel for the whole municipality on the strength of an inconvenience nobody has put a number on."
- **Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i **handlingarna**."
- **What differs:** whose knowledge. The material reports what the documents do not contain. The draft reports what no person has ever done.
- **Case:** a booking officer has timed the double entry on her own and holds an internal figure that was never written into the staff memo. The documents still hold no time measurement — the material's statement holds — while "nobody has put a number on" fails. Nothing supplied excludes it; the material speaks only about *handlingarna*.
- **Smallest repair:** "…on the strength of an inconvenience the documents put no number on."

### Finding 4 — "We have not costed this trial" asserts an absence the material leaves unclaimed

- **Draft:** "We have not costed this trial, and what it costs is for the board to weigh against what it would learn."
- **Source:** "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."
- **What differs:** modality and whose assertion. Making no claim to have costed something is not the same as stating that it was not costed. The state of knowledge in the material is that the association puts no costing forward; the draft converts that into a positive fact about what the association did.
- **Case:** Öppna beslut has a rough internal estimate it considers too soft to publish, and therefore advances no costing. "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha … kostnadsberäknat försöket" holds; "We have not costed this trial" fails. No supplied statement excludes it — "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" says whose decision the cost is, not what the association has or has not calculated.
- **Smallest repair:** "We make no claim to have costed this trial, and what it costs is for the board to weigh against what it would learn."

### Finding 5 — "Six months from now" dates the end of the trial from publication

- **Draft:** "Six months from now the board can remove the phone channel, change it, or keep it — and say why."
- **Source:** "Öppna beslut föreslår ett sex månader långt försök …"; "**Därefter** kan den avgöra om en kanal ska tas bort, ändras eller behållas."
- **What differs:** time. The material dates the decision from the end of the six-month trial. The draft dates it six months from the moment of writing, which precedes the June 18 meeting at which the trial would be decided at all.
- **Case:** the board postpones on June 18 and the trial begins with the autumn season in September, ending in March. "Därefter" holds; "Six months from now" fails by roughly three months. The material fixes no start date for the trial, so nothing excludes the case.
- **Smallest repair:** "After those six months the board can remove the phone channel, change it, or keep it — and say why."

## 3. Translation check (speech rendered in her voice)

One passage of supplied speech is carried into the draft: "Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben. Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används." → "A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone can't use the web. It is a reason to find out why the phone is still being used."

- **Meaning:** preserved. *Inte ett bevis* → *not proof*; the negated ability *inte kan använda* → *can't use*; the purposive *ett skäl att ta reda på* → *a reason to find out*.
- **Stance and certainty:** preserved. The denial is of proof, not of the possibility; the draft adds no hedge and removes none. Her surrounding framing ("Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning") is rendered without strengthening as "I am not against digital booking".
- **Distinctive voice:** preserved. The two-beat structure — a flat denial followed by a redirection to the open question — survives intact, and the passive "why the phone is still being used" keeps her refusal to name who is using it, matching *används*.
- **Idiomatic American English:** natural. *Can't* is the ordinary spoken contraction here; *find out why* is the plain American idiom for *ta reda på varför*. No source-language syntax is carried over, and no implicit referent needed clarifying beyond what the supplied context settles (*webben* → *the web*, the material's only non-phone path).
