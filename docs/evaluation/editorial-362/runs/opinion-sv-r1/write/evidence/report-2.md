## Findings

### Finding 1 — the absence at the close is widened from the documents to the present state of the world (source support)

**Draft:** "Men innan en väg in stängs måste kommunen kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna, och det underlaget finns inte i dag."

**Source, in full:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna."

**What differs:** the scope of the negation, and whose holdings it covers. The source places the absence inside the decision documents. The draft's clause places it in the present generally, and the sentence's own subject is "kommunen", which includes the förvaltning and not only the papers before the committee. Earlier the draft scopes the same absence correctly ("Men i handlingarna finns varken en tidsmätning eller en beräkning av vad kommunen sparar", #17), so this is a widening at the close rather than a consistent framing.

**Case in which the two come apart, compatible with the material:** the förvaltning has completed a time-per-channel measurement but did not include it in the handlingar inför mötet. Then "Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna" holds while "det underlaget finns inte i dag" fails. Tested in the other direction: there is no case in which the underlag is absent today but present in the handlingar, so the draft's clause is strictly the stronger claim. Nothing supplied excludes the case — the material states only where the measurement is not, never that none exists anywhere, and the Claims contract is explicit that what is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened.

**Smallest supported repair:** replace "i dag" with the supported location, leaving the sentence otherwise untouched:

> "Men innan en väg in stängs måste kommunen kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna, och det underlaget finns inte i handlingarna."

This keeps the sentence's force, its rhetoric, its position and the author's voice, and matches the scoping the draft already uses at #17 and in the heading "Det här saknas i beslutsunderlaget".
