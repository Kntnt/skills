- The person shift *hon* → *jag* is accounted separately for person, number and natural gender, and rejected as a finding on the byline. Verified: the brief names Sanna Ek as the sender, so the first person is the supplied author's own voice, and *jag* asserts nothing about natural gender.

## Finding 1 — ACCEPTED

Draft: "Vi har varken kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket. Det påstår vi inte heller."
Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."

Evidence for acceptance: the source asserts something about what the association claims, not about what it did. The draft turned that into a factual denial. The base contract's Claims section is explicit that what is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened, so the draft asserted more than the material carries, about a different subject matter (the association's work rather than its claims). The checker's concrete case — a member's rough estimate the association chose to put no weight on — is compatible with the material and is excluded by nothing supplied.

Repair applied, exactly as proposed: "Vi gör inget anspråk på att ha kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket." The now-redundant "Det påstår vi inte heller." is removed with it, since the replacement sentence says what that clause said. Nothing else in the paragraph changed.

## Finding 2 — ACCEPTED

Draft: "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte personer."
Source: "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer."

Evidence for acceptance: the modifier is the source's own and it bounds the negation. Without it the draft denies that persons are counted at all, which the material does not say — the material's stated measurement gaps are age, ability and digital habit. The word is also the one the paragraph's later inference rests on, since the *andel av invånarna* argument turns on repeat bookers rather than on headcount.

Repair applied, exactly as proposed: "unika" restored. No other wording changed.
