## Finding 1 — REJECTED, with evidence

Report's claim: "och det underlaget finns inte i dag" widens an absence the material places "i handlingarna" to the present state of the world. Its concrete case: the förvaltning has completed a time-per-channel measurement but left it out of the handlingar.

**The mismatch is in the report's reading of the draft.** "det underlaget" is anaphoric to the weighing named in the same sentence — "väga **arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna**" — so it covers both quantities, not the tidsmätning alone. The report pairs it against "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna" and then tests only that half. Its case therefore supplies the work-cost side and leaves the user-value side untouched, and with the user-value side missing the weighing still cannot be made. Under the report's own case the draft's clause remains true, so the case does not stand.

**The material excludes the case for the remaining half, in the report's own reading of it.** Sanna Eks supplied stance: "Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används." The proposal is that the förvaltning during the trial "be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb", and the report itself, at row #12 of its accounting, sets aside exactly this case for the draft's "inte varför": "the case in which reasons are already known is excluded by Ek's own supplied words, which treat the reason as still to be found out". The report reaches the opposite conclusion about the same absence twenty rows later without new evidence.

**What the material carries about the value side.** "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer. Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana. Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt." Nothing supplied establishes what the phone route is worth to its users, and the one instrument the material names cannot establish it.

The rejection is this writer's evidenced decision, not the checker's approval. No prose change follows from it, so the checked prose stands exactly as compared.
