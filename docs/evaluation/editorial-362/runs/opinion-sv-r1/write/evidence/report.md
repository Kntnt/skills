## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 (source support) — "Vi har varken kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket" (draft l. 23)

**Support that is changed.** The material says only: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."

**What differs.** Whose assertion, and the modality. The source is a statement *about the association's claims* — it makes none. The draft is a statement *about the facts* — it denies the work was done. Preserving the state of knowledge, what is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened.

**Case in which the source holds and the draft fails.** The association had a member make a rough cost estimate of a six-month dual-channel trial and decided to put no public weight on it. The source sentence stays true — Öppna beslut makes no claim to have costed the trial — while "Vi har varken kostnadsberäknat … försöket" is false. No supplied statement excludes this: "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" says where the decision sits, not what the association did or did not compute.

**Smallest supported repair.** Replace "Vi har varken kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket. Det påstår vi inte heller." with:

> Vi gör inget anspråk på att ha kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket.

This keeps the paragraph's function (the association disclaims cost authority before handing the cost question to the styrelse), leaves the following sentence intact, and asserts exactly what the material asserts.

### Finding 2 (source support) — "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte personer" (draft l. 9)

**Support that is missing.** The material says: "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte **unika** personer." The draft drops the modifier.

**What differs.** The scope of the negation. The source denies only that the count is of *distinct individuals*; the draft denies that persons are counted at all.

**Case in which the source holds and the draft fails.** The pilot report records, for each of the 120 bookings, how many people the booking was made for, and reports that headcount — so it does count persons, just never deduplicated across repeat bookers. The source sentence holds; "räknar bokningar, inte personer" is false. Nothing supplied excludes this: the material's only stated measurement gaps are "ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana".

**Why this is not harmless compression.** The inference the draft draws two sentences later — "Av de 24 telefonbokningarna går det därför inte att utläsa hur stor andel av invånarna …" — turns on repeat bookers, that is, on uniqueness. The dropped word is the one carrying the argument.

**Smallest supported repair.** Restore the modifier:

> Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer.
