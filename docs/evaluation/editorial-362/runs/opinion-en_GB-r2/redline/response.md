# Keep the telephone until Lervik knows why it is used

*Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

On 18 June the municipal executive board has before it a proposal that telephone booking of Lervik's association venues disappear in September. It should not settle that yet: keep both routes for six months, in all seven venues, and decide afterwards, with figures in hand.

## The pilot counted bookings, not people

The municipality's pilot report on the booking of association venues, dated 8 April 2026, covers an eight-week trial in two venues: 96 bookings were made on the web and 24 by telephone. The report counts bookings rather than unique people, and it measures nothing about age, ability or digital habit.

Those 24 bookings therefore settle nothing about the residents behind them. They cannot show what share of residents are unable to book digitally; nor do they show the opposite, that the telephone survives out of habit. The report does not measure it.

## The proposal rests on an inconvenience the papers do not measure

The officers' report before the June meeting proposes removing telephone booking in all seven venues from September. The reason given is that staff should not have to enter details into two flows. Double administration is the administration's genuine objection to keeping both channels, not any view that telephone users are lazy or expensive.

What the papers do not contain is any measurement of the time that second flow costs, or any calculation of the saving. A permanent change for seven venues is being proposed on the strength of a nuisance the papers put no number on.

## Six months would produce the figures

Öppna beslut proposes six months in all seven venues with both routes kept open. The administration would record how long each route takes and would ask users to state, voluntarily, why they chose the telephone or the web. We have not costed that trial; what it would cost is for the board to weigh.

That a booking comes in by telephone is not proof that somebody cannot use the web. It is a reason to find out why the telephone is still being used. I am not against digital booking. I am against deciding before the cost of the work can be weighed against what each route is worth to the people who use it.

## What the board can decide on 18 June

The board can adopt the September removal on the material in front of it. Or it can postpone that decision, run the trial in all seven venues, and afterwards remove, change or keep a channel with time figures and users' own reasons on the table. The trial costs something we have not costed; asking what that sum is, before the channel goes, is the smaller risk.
