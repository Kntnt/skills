## 2. Findings (source support)

### Finding 1 — "Those 24 calls" counts a different thing from the source

- **Draft:** "Those 24 calls therefore settle nothing about the residents behind them."
- **Source:** »Under försöket gjordes 96 bokningar via webb och 24 via telefon.« and »Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer.«
- **What differs:** the thing measured. The material establishes 24 telephone *bookings*, not 24 calls. Nothing equates the two — indeed the draft's own preceding paragraph says the report counts bookings.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** one resident rings once and books two rooms in the same call; 24 bookings then arrive in fewer than 24 calls. Compatible with the material, which reports only a booking count and no per-call data. No supplied statement excludes it.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "Those 24 bookings therefore settle nothing about the residents behind them."

### Finding 2 — "nothing in the papers" widens a statement about the administration's objection

- **Draft:** "nothing in the papers calls telephone users lazy or expensive, and nor do I."
- **Source:** »Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra.«
- **What differs:** scope, and whose assertion. The material rules out laziness or expense as *the administration's objection*; the draft rules it out of the whole body of papers.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** a background annex in the papers describes telephone handling as a costly habit, while the administration's stated objection remains double administration. Compatible: the material excludes only a *tidsmätning* and an *ekonomisk besparingsberäkning* from *handlingarna*, not a characterisation.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "the administration's objection is double administration, not that telephone users are lazy or expensive — and nor do I say they are."

### Finding 3 — "nobody has measured / put a number on" widens an absence in the papers

- **Draft:** heading "The proposal rests on an inconvenience nobody has measured"; body "on the strength of a nuisance nobody has put a number on."
- **Source:** »Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna.«
- **What differs:** whose knowledge. The material places the absence in the papers; the draft places it in the world. The Claims contract holds this boundary in headings as in the body.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** an officer times both flows on a spreadsheet that is never appended to the *tjänsteutlåtande*. Compatible with the material, which speaks only of what *handlingarna* contain.
- **Smallest supported repair:** heading "The proposal rests on an inconvenience the papers do not measure"; body "…on the strength of a nuisance the papers put no number on."

### Finding 4 — "We have neither funded nor costed that trial" turns an unmade claim into a fact

- **Draft:** "We have neither funded nor costed that trial; what it would cost is for the board to weigh."
- **Source:** »Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket.«
- **What differs:** modality and state of knowledge. The material declines to claim funding or costing; it does not state that neither happened. What is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** the association has a rough internal costing it does not put forward here. Compatible — »Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till« says the board must take a position on the cost, which an unpublished association estimate does not contradict.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "We make no claim to have funded or costed that trial; what it would cost is for the board to weigh."

### Finding 5 — "the working time" replaces the labour cost in Sanna Ek's position

- **Draft:** "I am against deciding before the working time can be weighed against what each route is worth to the people who use it."
- **Source:** »Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna.«
- **What differs:** the thing measured. *Arbetskostnaden* is the cost of the work; the draft substitutes the time it takes. The trial records *tidsåtgång*, which is a proxy for that cost, not the cost.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** the trial yields minutes per route, the board weighs those minutes against users' stated reasons, and never converts them into a labour cost. The draft's condition is then satisfied while the position she is given in the material is not. Compatible with the material; nothing in it equates time taken with cost.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "…before the cost of the work can be weighed against what each route is worth to the people who use it."
- **Note:** this sits outside the quoted sentences (»Att en bokning går via telefon…«), so it is a source-support finding, not a translation finding.

### Finding 6 — "nobody has yet worked out" what only the association has not costed

- **Draft:** "The trial costs something nobody has yet worked out; asking what that sum is, before the channel goes, is the smaller risk."
- **Source:** »Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket. Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till.«
- **What differs:** whose knowledge. The material withholds a costing from the association only; the draft asserts that no one has one. The draft's own earlier sentence ("We have neither funded nor costed that trial") keeps the subject right, and this closing line widens it.
- **Case where the source holds and the draft fails:** the administration holds a cost estimate for a six-month parallel trial that is not yet before the board. Compatible; nothing supplied excludes it.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "The trial costs something we have not costed; asking what that sum is, before the channel goes, is the smaller risk."

## 3. Findings (translation)

**None.** The one passage rendering a person's words — »Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben. Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används.« — reaches the draft as "That a booking comes in by telephone is not proof that somebody cannot use the web. It is a reason to find out why the telephone is still being used." Meaning, stance, certainty and the distinctive two-beat construction (denial, then redirection) are all present in idiomatic British speech, with no source-language syntax carried over and nothing added. *någon* stays an indefinite *somebody*, neither narrowed to a named person nor widened to *people*. No implicit referent needed settling. »Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning« → "I am not against digital booking" changes person, not stance or strength; the brief's naming of Sanna Ek as *avsändare* warrants the first person. No concrete obstruction to a British reader taking any of this as the speaker meant it.

The cost/time substitution in Finding 5 is a meaning change outside quoted speech and is recorded above as a source-support finding, per the instruction.
