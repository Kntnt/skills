# Validation of report.md — accounting and dispositions

Accounting coverage: the report pairs title, byline, lead, all four section headings and every body sentence against the material, plus a separate pronoun accounting. No draft passage is left unpaired. One accounting mismatch detected, recorded below.

## Dispositions

1. **"Those 24 calls" — accepted.** The material establishes »24 [bokningar] via telefon«, a booking count only; the draft's own preceding sentence states the report counts bookings. Nothing supplied equates one call with one booking. Repaired to "Those 24 bookings".

2. **"nothing in the papers calls telephone users lazy or expensive" — accepted.** »Förvaltningens verkliga invändning … är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra« constrains the administration's objection, not the whole of *handlingarna*. Repaired by attributing the exclusion to the administration's objection and keeping the author's own refusal to allege such a motive.

3. **"nobody has measured" / "nobody has put a number on" — accepted.** »Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna« places the absence in the papers. Heading and body both repaired to say the papers.

4. **"We have neither funded nor costed that trial" — accepted.** »gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat« declines a claim; it does not assert the negative. Repaired to "We make no claim to have funded or costed that trial".

5. **"the working time can be weighed" — accepted.** »väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna« is the cost of the work; *tidsåtgång* is what the trial records, which is not the same quantity. Repaired to "the cost of the work".

6. **"something nobody has yet worked out" — accepted.** Same widening as 3, applied to the trial's cost, which the material withholds from the association only. Repaired to "something we have not costed"; »Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till« remains carried by the surrounding sentence.

## Accounting mismatch detected

The report set aside the lead's "the municipal executive board decides whether …" on the reading that a *tjänsteutlåtande* before a named meeting is that meeting's decision item. That reading is an inference the material does not state; the material says only that the proposal stands »inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni«. The pairing is therefore not established, and the lead was repaired to say the board has the proposal before it rather than that it decides on that date. The closing section continues to name the board's possible decisions, as the material's boundaries require.

Also changed: the title, from "…knows who is using it" to "…knows why it is used". The report treated the original as supported advocacy, but the proposed trial asks »varför de väljer telefon eller webb«, not who the users are; the new title matches what the trial would establish.

All changed passages go to a second comparison with a fresh checker.
