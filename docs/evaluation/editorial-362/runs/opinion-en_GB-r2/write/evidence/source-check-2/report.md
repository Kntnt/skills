## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 — "The pilot was not built for that question." (line 11)

- **Draft:** "The pilot was not built for that question."
- **Source:** ”Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer. Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana. Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt.”
- **What differs:** the thing asserted. The material states what the report *does not measure*; the draft states what the pilot *was designed to do* — a claim about purpose. The base contract makes motive and background claims that need support in their own right.
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the pilot was set up precisely to establish how many residents cannot book digitally, and simply failed to collect the attributes that would have shown it, so the report counts bookings only. Every supplied statement about the report remains true; the draft's sentence is false. Nothing in the material excludes this case — it describes the report's contents and never states the trial's purpose or design intent.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "The pilot did not measure it." (or "The report does not measure it.") — keeps the paragraph's force while asserting only the supplied absence.

### Finding 2 — "the same details into two flows" (line 15)

- **Draft:** "The reason given is that staff should not have to enter the same details into two flows."
- **Source:** ”Motivet i utlåtandet är att personalen ska slippa föra in uppgifter i två flöden.”
- **What differs:** scope of the thing measured. The source says details are entered into two flows; the draft adds that they are *the same* details, sharpening the officers' stated reason.
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the two flows take overlapping but non-identical field sets — the telephone flow records a caller's name and a callback number, the web flow an account reference and a venue code — so staff do enter details into two flows without entering the same details twice. ”Dubbel administration” remains an apt name for the double handling, so the material's own characterisation survives. No supplied statement excludes this case; the material never says ”samma uppgifter”.
- **Smallest supported repair:** delete "same" — "…should not have to enter details into two flows."

### Finding 3 — present-tense rendering of a proposed arrangement (line 21)

- **Draft:** "Öppna beslut proposes six months in all seven venues with both routes kept open. **The administration records** how long each route takes **and asks** users to state, voluntarily, why they chose the telephone or the web."
- **Source:** ”Under försöket **ska** förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg och be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb.”
- **What differs:** modality. The source puts these acts inside the proposal (”ska … under försöket”); the draft's bare present indicative states them as what the administration does.
- **Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the administration does not at present record time per booking route at all — which the draft itself asserts two paragraphs earlier ("What the papers do not contain is any measurement of the time that second flow costs") and which ”Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna” supports. Read as a statement of present practice the sentence is false and contradicts the draft's own supported claim; only the proposal reading survives, and the sentence does not mark itself as the proposal.
- **Smallest supported repair:** "The administration would record how long each route takes and would ask users to state, voluntarily, why they chose the telephone or the web." (Alternatively "Under it, the administration records…".)

## 3. Tested and not findings

Recorded so they are not re-opened.

- **"any calculation of the saving" (line 17).** The definite article could be read as presupposing an established saving, which ”Inga … besparingar är belagda” would not support. In context the phrase names the calculation that is absent, and the same sentence denies the papers contain it; an ordinary reader takes "the saving" as "what would be saved". Defensible as written ("a saving" is a free tightening, not required).
- **"what each route is worth to the people who use it" (line 23).** The source has ”värdet för användarna”, without per-route disaggregation. This is the author's own advocacy in her own first person, and the per-route framing is the material's own — ”registrera tidsåtgång **per bokningsväg**”, ”ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb”, ”avgöra om **en kanal** ska tas bort, ändras eller behållas”. Defensible.
- **"a nuisance" / "an inconvenience" for ”dubbel administration” (lines 13, 17).** Evaluative, but opinion-genre advocacy about an objection the draft explicitly calls genuine; the Boundaries licence sharp criticism of the decision material. It does not contradict ”verkliga invändning”. Defensible.
- **"ability" for ”funktionsförmåga” (line 9).** Broader term inside a negative claim, so tested for overreach; ”Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer” excludes the report carrying any attribute of individuals, which sets the case aside.
- **"municipal executive board" for ”kommunstyrelsen”, "officers' report" for ”tjänsteutlåtandet”, "association venues" for ”föreningslokaler”, "routes" for ”bokningsvägar”.** In this material nothing could fall under one term and not the other: each names a single referent the material identifies uniquely.
- **First person "I"/"we" for the source's ”Hon”/”Föreningen”.** The brief makes Sanna Ek the sender of a bylined debate article, so the person shift changes neither the referent, the number, nor whose assertion it is.
- **Absence of protests, legal requirements, discrimination cases, party motives and established savings.** Checked line by line: the draft introduces none. ”Inga lagkrav, protester, diskrimineringsfall, partipolitiska motiv eller besparingar är belagda” is respected throughout.
