## 1. Technical — contradictions, term drift, dangling references

**Nothing in `SKILL.md`, `help.md` or the frontmatter contradicts the new task.** Step 7 (`SKILL.md:64`) still describes the comparison as "source support and idiomatic translation of quotations; general editorial review and mechanics remain separate", which the task's last four sentences repeat. `help.md:67` still describes a fresh checker comparing draft with material and a writer validating "the report's claim accounting and findings" — all still performed. The `subagents` capability (`SKILL.md:12`, `help.md:122`) is untouched and still required. `quotations.md:7` ("Make an implicit referent explicit only when the supplied context settles it") and the task's "clarify an implicit referent only where the supplied context settles it" still agree. `delivery.md` is unaffected: the scratch-removal duties at `source-check.md:13` and `SKILL.md:66` are unchanged and still satisfy `delivery.md:9`.

**Must fix — one term drifted.** The checker is now told to "Write the **paired accounting** and findings to the report path" (line 7), while the writer is told to "validate its **claim accounting**" and to "Record detected **accounting mismatches**" (line 9), and `help.md:67` also says "claim accounting". Before the change the two paragraphs used one name ("Write the complete claim accounting"/"validate its claim accounting"). The report now has a section name nothing else in the chain uses. Pick one name and use it in both paragraphs and in `help.md:67`.

**Should fix — "carry over" lost its object.** The old task had a target to carry modifiers *into*: "Reconstruct each independently checkable proposition as a meaning-equivalent standalone statement, carrying over grammatical modifiers and qualifications that apply across clauses." The new sentence keeps the verb but removes the destination: "record the draft passage beside the source passage it rests on … and carry over grammatical modifiers and qualifications that apply across clauses" (line 7). When the checker records passages rather than reconstructing statements, there is nothing for a modifier to be carried over into, and a fresh reader has to guess whether this means *quote enough context that the modifier is visible*, *state the modifier beside the pair*, or nothing at all. `p2` (the #344 chronology case, where "In September 2025" governs two clauses) rides on this sentence and passed 3/3, so it is not broken in practice — but it is the one instruction in the paragraph whose action is undefined. Give it an object, e.g. "and state, beside the pair, the modifiers and qualifications that apply across clauses".

**Optional — "that proposition" survives from the removed method.** Line 9 reads "First establish what the draft actually asserts in its full context, then whether the supplied material supports that proposition …". With "reconstructed proposition and negation" gone from the preceding sentence, "that proposition" now takes its antecedent from "what the draft actually asserts", which reads correctly. It is a survivor of the old vocabulary rather than a dangling reference, and I would leave it unless the term-drift fix above is done as a sweep.

**Should fix — an accounting gap the evidence already exposed.** The task tells the checker to record a pair "For every factual and attributed claim", and separately says "A claim with no source passage is a finding unless it is the author's own argument, reflection or advocacy resting on supported material" (line 7). It never says how such a legitimately unpaired passage is entered in the accounting. Line 9 then asks the writer to "Verify that the complete draft is covered and that every pairing sets the draft passage … beside the source passage it rests on". Coverage of the unpaired-but-legitimate passages is therefore unverifiable by construction, and the validation diagnostic recorded exactly this as noise: "The accounting mismatches the readers did find are bookkeeping: pronoun counts, an unpaired `technique: none`, two unpaired headings" (`validation/results.md`, Reading). One clause in the task — that a passage carried as the author's own argument is recorded as such, with the supported material it rests on — closes it.

**Optional — the cross-language rule outruns what line 5 supplies.** "A meaning changed in crossing languages outside quoted speech is a source-support finding" and "A term carried into another language names the same thing only where nothing in this context could fall under one term and not the other" (line 7) now apply to every translated draft. Line 5, unchanged, supplies the resolved composition guidance and names source and target languages only "Where quotations are translated". A Swedish-source/English-draft run with no quotations gets the general cross-language rule without being told, in the prompt, which language the source is in. The checker can read it off the material, so this is not a defect; it is an asymmetry worth one clause if line 5 is touched anyway.

**Nothing else in the unchanged paragraphs dangles.** Lines 11, 13 and 15 speak of "comparison", "findings", "accepted defects", "unresolved material claims" — all still defined by the new task. Line 13's stop condition and line 15's "preserve the checked prose exactly" are unaffected by the method change. No other file in the repository asserts the removed method: a grep for "negation", "reconstruct", "counterexample" and "claim accounting" outside `docs/adr/`, `CHANGELOG.md` and the frozen `docs/evaluation/` evidence returns only `help.md:67` (handled above) and unrelated hits.

## 2. Editorial — reading the task as the fresh checker

I read line 7 as a checker with nothing but the fixture, the Claims section and the resolved language, in the staging `checker/candidate-3-task.md` reproduces.

**None of the five named defects is present.** No canonical example sentence; no prohibition that depends on an example; no fixed synonym list (the equivalence test is stated as a condition, not as a list, which is what the ticket asked for); no ban on conclusions, metonymy or pronouns — pronouns are *accounted for*, not banned, and the author's-own-argument clause explicitly protects reasoning and transitions. There is no demand for verbatim restatement of the draft: the two verbatim demands ("quoted in the source's own words and language"; "Set a case aside only by quoting a supplied statement that excludes it") both fall on the *report's evidence*, not on the draft's wording, and the ticket's prohibition is on requiring the draft to reproduce the source.

**Must fix — "test both directions" names only one case.** "Where the two differ, test both directions: name a concrete case, compatible with the supplied material, in which one statement holds and the other fails" (line 7). "Both directions" asks for two things; the colon then delivers one indefinite case, and "one statement … the other" is symmetric, so a checker who names a single case in either direction has satisfied the sentence as written and can consider both directions tested. This is the operative sentence of the whole new method, and it is the one place where the instruction and its execution clause disagree about arity. The `opinion-en_US-r1` judge read it as two directions and checked both ("The reverse direction fails too"), which shows the intended reading is available — not that it is compelled. Say it plainly: name a case in which the draft's statement holds and the source's fails, and a case in which the source's holds and the draft's fails, or say that one suffices.

**Should fix — the difference checklist omits causation and quantity.** "For each pair, state what differs: the thing named or measured, the subject, scope, time, modality, certainty, or whose knowledge or assertion it is." A fresh checker reads a colon-introduced list as the set of dimensions to report. The base contract supplied alongside it singles out precisely a dimension the list omits: "A sequence or correlation establishes no cause. A causal verb such as *shortened* needs causal support" (`base.md:27`). Quantity and number are likewise absent, and `opinion-sv-r1`'s real finding was a dropped "unika" — a quantity qualifier, caught under "scope" only by generosity. Either open the list ("among them") or add the causal relation and quantity to it.

**Residual tension, mitigated by evidence, no change demanded.** Three sentences pull against each other, and a reader meets them in this order: (a) "If a case stands, that is a finding, however natural or cautious the draft's wording"; (b) "A claim with no source passage is a finding unless it is the author's own argument, reflection or advocacy … test the facts … it presents as given, not the reasoning or transition itself"; (c) "A finding is a defect that must be repaired before delivery; do not report what is defensible as written". (a) pushes a constructed case into a finding regardless of how the passage reads; (b) exempts exactly the reflective transitions where (a) is most tempting; (c) is a judgement call with no operational test, and (c) is the shape of the baseline's original failure — a real difference noticed and then waved through. This is not an internal contradiction in the strict sense: (b) is scoped to passages with no source passage, and (c) restates (a)'s negative half. It is an ordering and calibration risk, and the checker-level evidence is the reason to accept it: `n1` (the #357 column reflection this tension is about) passed 3/3, and the false-finding rate across 21 runs was 3. I flag it so the next author does not "simplify" (c) away.

**One more calibration note.** "A term carried into another language names the same thing only where nothing in this context could fall under one term and not the other" sets a universal-negative bar, and the only sanctioned escape is a quoted supplied statement that excludes the case. Sources rarely carry statements that exclude term-scope cases. Strictly applied, every cross-language pair becomes a difference to be tested and only rarely dismissible, and the sole counterweight is (c). The measured consequence is visible and small (3 false findings in 21 runs, against candidate 1's 0), and the `p1` dissent recorded in `checker/results-rounds-2-3.md` ("A dissent on `p1`, kept as it stands") is the same disagreement in a judge. Nothing to fix now; worth watching in the next real-run batch.

**Everything else is executable.** "The case need only be compatible with the supplied material; the material need not describe it or make it likely" is the clearest sentence in the paragraph and answers the observed miss shape directly. "Judge source support." is terse but unambiguous beside the two exclusion sentences that follow it. The report contract (report path, completion status, unresolved findings, 150-word reply) is unchanged and complete. The checker's tool constraints live at line 5, where the writer applies them, exactly as before the change.
