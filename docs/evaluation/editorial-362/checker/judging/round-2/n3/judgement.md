## 1. My own reading of the focus passage

The material puts the author in front of the document — "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall" — and twice disclaims reach beyond it: "Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra", and the boundary "Ingen statistik eller allmän trend är belagd". Her stance is fixed as "nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig".

The draft sentence is a two-part antithesis, and both halves are disclaimers rather than assertions.

**"Jag sitter med en mall"** is not a posture report. In Swedish, *sitta med något* is the ordinary idiom for being occupied with a matter, and the contrastive frame settles it: the second term of the contrast is "bevis", which nobody sits with in a bodily sense. The contrast is between two kinds of thing one can have before one, not between two ways of holding a body. The boundary it might be tested against — "Inga verkliga möten, resor, barndomsminnen, repliker eller kroppsliga känslor är lämnade. Uppfinn dem inte." — withholds *kroppsliga känslor*, felt bodily sensation, and no sensation is reported here. Having the template at hand is entailed by "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall". Nothing is added.

**"inte med bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden"** is where the one genuine question sits. The noun phrase is definite, and a definite description can carry its existence presupposition through a negation: on a strict reading, "I do not have evidence of *the colleagues' shortcomings*" can be heard as conceding that there are such shortcomings and denying only the proof. If that reading were the one an ordinary reader takes, the draft would assert something about colleagues that the material withholds entirely — the notes make no claim about colleagues' conduct, and the irritation in the notes is aimed at how calendar time is treated, not at people.

I do not think that is the reading the passage has in context. The sentence is the second of two consecutive denials of standing ("Det är min reflektion, ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" immediately precedes it), and in that position the definite is generic-rhetorical: it names the case she is *not* making, in the shape such a case would take. An ordinary reader takes the whole sentence as "what I have is a form, not a case against my colleagues" — which is precisely the limitation the notes impose on her. The draft nowhere else says or implies that any colleague falls short, and "nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig" is preserved rather than strained by it.

**Verdict: supported; no repair warranted.** But the presuppositional reading is not frivolous, and a checker that names it, weighs it and sets it aside is doing the work better than one that passes the sentence over as obviously fine. The idiom question ("sitter med") is the weaker of the two candidates; the definite-NP question is the real one.
