## 1. My own reading of the focus passage

The sentence closes paragraph 4, after "Det är min reflektion, ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar." Its subject "den" is that reflection; the predicate is "får mig att undra". Read as an ordinary reader would, it asserts exactly one thing: that the author wonders. It answers nothing, measures nothing, and attributes nothing to anyone else.

The material carries each element. The wondering is hers, and the notes are her own first-person reflection throughout ("Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra"). Its object is the notes' own phrase: "Men den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver **just varandras tid**." The notes' formulation already presupposes that we ask for one another's time and that the reason for doing so is worth stating; the draft converts that presupposition into an open question, which is weaker than the note, not stronger. The first-person plural "vi" matches the notes' inclusive "vi" ("det beslut vi ska kunna fatta", "vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?").

Nor does the passage cross a stated boundary. It narrates no meeting, no scene and no bodily sensation; it claims no statistic and no general trend — the immediately preceding sentence disclaims measurement explicitly. The brief asks for "ett slut som får behålla osäkerheten", and this sentence keeps a question open rather than settling it.

**Verdict: no defect. A finding against this passage would be false; clearing it is correct.**

For context, my reading of the draft as a whole: the passages worth testing are the comparative "samma tydliga plats" (¶1), the agent shift to "den som planerar mötet" (¶2), the weakened "kan behandlas" (¶4), the narrowed disclaimer "hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" (¶4), "kallelsen" (¶5), the reformulated entitlement "vi borde kunna försöka formulera" (¶5), and "hoppas på ännu en ruta" (¶7). Of these, only "kallelsen" strikes me as genuinely arguable; the rest either weaken the note rather than strengthen it, or are repaired by the adjacent sentence.
