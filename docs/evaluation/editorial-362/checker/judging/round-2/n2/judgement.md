## My reading of the focus passage

> "Min invändning gäller att kommunen föreslås ta bort telefonen innan den har underlag för den avvägningen."

Read in context, this asserts three things, each carried by the material. That removal is at proposal stage: the passive "föreslås" matches "Tjänsteutlåtandet … föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september", and nothing in the sentence says the kommunstyrelse has decided. That there is a weighing at issue: "den avvägningen" points back to the sentence the piece opens with and to "Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna". That the basis for that weighing is missing: the immediately preceding paragraph localises the gap where the material localises it — "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna."

Beyond those, the sentence asserts nothing. "Min invändning gäller att …" frames it as the signed author's own objection, and the byline "Sanna Ek, talesperson för föreningen Öppna beslut" is the brief's own sender, so the first person adds no attribution the material does not carry ("Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning" sits directly behind the preceding sentence). The one reading that could break it — that the förvaltning holds an unpublished time study, so the kommun has a basis although the handlingar do not show one — is not the reading an ordinary reader gives the passage after a paragraph that has just said what the handlingar contain; the basis at issue is the basis before the committee on 18 June. A finding against this passage would therefore be **false**, and clearing it is correct. More generally, my own pass over the whole draft found nothing warranting repair: counts, dates, scope, the proposal's modality, the voluntariness of the users' reasons, the funding and costing disclaimer, and the three open outcomes are all carried, and the remaining sentences are the author's advocacy resting on supported material.
