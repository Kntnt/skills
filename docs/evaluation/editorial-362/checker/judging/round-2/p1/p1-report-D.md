The draft itself fixes "staff" as the administration acting in its official capacity two paragraphs earlier, where "the staff recommendation" renders "Tjänsteutlåtandet" — the document of the förvaltning, not of any individual employee. Under that established usage the case does not separate the two readings. Not a finding.
- **"The staff recommendation" for "Tjänsteutlåtandet".** Candidate case: a tjänsteutlåtande that lays out options without recommending one. Excluded by the supplied statement "Tjänsteutlåtandet ... föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort", which makes this one a recommendation. Not a finding.
- **"book online" for "boka digitalt"; "online-only" for "enbart digital".** Candidate case: a digital channel that is not the web, so that a resident could book digitally without booking online. The supplied material names exactly two routes throughout — "både telefon och webb", "båda bokningsvägarna", "varför de väljer telefon eller webb" — so in this context nothing falls under one term and not the other. Not a finding.
- **"community venues" for "föreningslokaler".** The draft immediately fixes the referent as the municipality's own seven venues ("all seven venues"), the same set the material counts. No case separates them here. Not a finding.
- **"before the board decides whether to remove either option" (¶1).** Candidate case: the board changes a channel rather than removing one, which ¶1 does not mention. The closing paragraph states all three outcomes in the source's own order, so the draft as a whole does not withhold the alternatives. Not a finding.
- **"a real objection that our proposal must address".** Candidate case: the objection is the administration's actual one yet trivial in size, so that conceding it is "real" overstates it. The concession is the author's own assessment, and her supplied position already grants labour cost standing to be weighed; the draft attaches no magnitude to it and the next paragraph states that no time measurement exists. Not a finding.

The one retained Swedish string, the report title *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, is left untranslated and italicised, and is the title the material gives. No translation-of-speech check applies.

## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — source support (meaning changed in crossing languages, outside quoted speech)

- **Draft:** "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
- **Source:** "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."
- **What differs:** the thing measured. "Digital vana" is habituation — how accustomed a person is to doing things digitally, from use. "Digital proficiency" is skill or competence. The two are distinct constructs measured by different questions, and the material nowhere states that the report does not measure skill.
- **Case in which the source statement holds and the draft's fails:** the pilot report includes a single self-rated item on how confident users feel filling in the online form — a proficiency measure — but never asks how often or how long they have used digital services. Then "Den mäter inte ... digital vana" is true and "The report does not measure ... digital proficiency" is false. Nothing in the material excludes this: it says the report "räknar bokningar" and lists three things it does not measure, not that these are all it does not measure.
- **Case in the other direction:** the report records how regularly each respondent uses the municipality's e-services — a habit measure — while asking nothing about competence. Then the draft's claim would be true and the source's false.
- **Why it matters here:** the article's argument turns on separating inability from habit — "Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben." Rendering "vana" as an ability word puts a second ability term beside "functional ability" and drops the habituation the argument relies on.
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace "digital proficiency" with "digital habits" — "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital habits." ("How accustomed users are to digital services" is an equally supported longer form.) No other wording changes.
