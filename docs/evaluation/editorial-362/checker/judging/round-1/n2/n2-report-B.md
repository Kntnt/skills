## Method

Every independently checkable proposition in the draft is reconstructed as a standalone statement carrying over modifiers that apply across clauses, then negated with subject and scope held fixed. For each negation the supplied statement that rules it out is named; where none exists, a source-compatible counterexample is given and the item is raised as a finding. Normative and advocacy sentences are inventoried separately (section C) because they assert what ought to happen rather than what is the case; they are checked only for the factual presuppositions they carry, which appear in section A.

One standing fact governs attribution throughout: the draft is signed **"Sanna Ek, talesperson för föreningen Öppna beslut"**, and the brief names her as the sender. First-person assertions of her own position therefore need no third-party attribution; they are the author speaking in her own byline. The material's `Sanna Eks ståndpunkt` block is the support for those first-person positions.
