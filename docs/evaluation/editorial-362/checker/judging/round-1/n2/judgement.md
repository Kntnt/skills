## My reading of the focus passage

Read in context, the sentence carries two assertions and one piece of scoping. The first assertion is that removal of phone booking is *proposed* — the passive `föreslås` keeps it a proposal and imputes no decision and no wish to the municipality. The material carries this: "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september." The draft's shift from the document as proposer to `kommunen föreslås ta bort` is the ordinary Swedish way of naming who would act under the proposal, not a change of proposer; the draft has already named the tjänsteutlåtande as the proposing document four sentences earlier, so no ordinary reader takes `kommunen` to be the author of the proposal.

The second assertion is that the municipality does not yet have `underlag för den avvägningen`. `Den avvägningen` is anaphoric to the weighing the draft has just set up and which the material attributes to Ek: "Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna." Both sides of that weighing are stated in the material to be unmeasured — the cost side by "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna", the value side by "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana" and "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt."

The scoping is `Min invändning gäller att …`, which narrows the author's own objection immediately after "Jag motsätter mig inte digital bokning" — itself supported by "Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning." The sentence is the signed author's own argument, resting on supported material, and asserts nothing beyond it. `Telefonen` for `telefonbokningen` is an elliptical shorthand the draft and the material both already use ("varför telefonen fortfarande används"; brief: "både telefon och webb"), and it changes nothing. **No repair is warranted.** Any finding against this passage would be false; the only reading on which it fails is one in which `kommunen` is taken as the proposer, and the immediately preceding paragraph forecloses that reading.

For the record, the draft as a whole raises no source-support defect I can find: the counts carry their exclusions, no share is derived from 96/24, `frivilligt` survives into the trial description, the three closing outcomes stay open, and none of the five unestablished categories (lagkrav, protester, diskrimineringsfall, partipolitiska motiv, besparingar) is asserted. So a report with zero findings is, on this fixture, the correct outcome rather than a failure to look.
