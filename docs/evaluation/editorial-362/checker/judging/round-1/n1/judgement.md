Focus passage: "Ändå får den mig att undra vad vi ber om när vi ber om varandras tid."

## My reading of the focus passage

The sentence closes paragraph 4, immediately after "Det är min reflektion, ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar." Its "den" is that reflection, so an ordinary reader takes the sentence as the author reporting where her own thinking goes next: her irritation at calendar time leaves her wondering what is actually being asked for when time is asked for. It asserts no fact about anyone else, no measurement, no trend and no effect; it is a state of wondering, and its object is in the material verbatim — "varför vi behöver just varandras tid" (Nora Viks anteckningar, ¶3). The only feature that could look like a claim is the causal verb "får ... att undra", but the cause and the effect are both inside her own mind, which the notes are the authority on, and the sentence introduces a question rather than answering it. It also does the structural work the notes license: it is the hinge from ¶2 of the notes (the irritation, disclaimed as unmeasured) to ¶3 (each other's time).

**Not a defect.** A checker that raises a finding here is reaching; a checker that clears it is right, and clearing it is the correct outcome for all five reports.
