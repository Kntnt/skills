## B. Findings

### Finding 1 — the central normative claim is narrowed and its qualification absorbed (source support)

**Draft (paragraph 5):** "Men vi borde kunna försöka formulera varför vi behöver undersöka något tillsammans."

**Source (N3):** "Men den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans."

**What differs.** Three things at once, in the sentence that carries the column's argument:

1. *The thing to be formulated.* Source: "varför vi behöver **just varandras tid**". Draft: "varför vi behöver **undersöka något tillsammans**". These are different objects of justification — the claim on these particular people's time, versus the need for the inquiry itself.
2. *The qualification.* In N3, "också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans" is a concession: the demand holds *even* for exploratory meetings. The draft absorbs that clause into the object, so the concession disappears and the exception becomes the rule.
3. *Modality and agent.* Source: "den som fyller i en mall **borde få** formulera" — the template-filler should be *given room* to state it. Draft: "**vi borde kunna försöka** formulera" — a capability and an attempt, held by an unspecified "vi".

**Concrete cases, both directions.**
- Draft holds, source fails: a planner writes "Vi behöver undersöka den nya låneprocessen, eftersom låntagarna hör av sig om den." Why the inquiry is needed is formulated; why it needs *these people's time* — rather than a note circulated for comment — is nowhere stated. The draft's norm is met; N3's is not.
- Source holds, draft fails: the template gives the filler a field that asks why exactly these colleagues' time is needed, and it is filled in for an exploratory meeting whose subject is still open, so nothing is said about why anything must be *investigated together*. N3's norm is met; the draft's is not.
- On modality: the template offers no field at all, and colleagues nevertheless try to say why they need to look into something together in the agenda box. The draft's "borde kunna försöka" is satisfied; N3's "borde få formulera" — room granted by the form — is not.

**Missing support.** Nothing in the material asks anyone to justify joint inquiry. The material asks that the person filling in the template be allowed to say why *each other's time* is needed, and adds that this holds also when the purpose is joint inquiry. The draft turns the protected case into the demand, which reverses which of the two N3 is defending.

**Smallest supported repair.** Restore the object and the concession, keeping the draft's "vi":

> Men vi borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans.

(Closer to N3 still, if the agent is to match: "Men den som fyller i mallen borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans.")

No other finding.
