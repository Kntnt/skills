## Findings

### Finding 1 — Time: the date of the decision is extended to the choice of supplier

- **Draft:** "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log and chose Svale Systems after testing whether it could show the status of each repair."
- **Source:** "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." and, as a separate sentence with no date, "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
- **What differs:** the time of one event. "In September 2025" is a sentence-initial adverbial governing both coordinated verbs, so the draft dates the supplier selection to September 2025 as well. The source dates only the decision and places the selection after a test of unstated duration.
- **Both directions:** a case compatible with the material in which the source holds and the draft fails — the team decides in September 2025, tests status display through October, and selects Svale on 3 November 2025. A case in which both hold — decision and test both completed within September 2025. Since the first case exists, the draft asserts more than the material supports, however natural the coordination reads.
- **Smallest supported repair:** split the coordination so the date governs only the decision.
  > In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log. It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair.

  This also restores "the log" as the tested object, at no cost to the sentence.
