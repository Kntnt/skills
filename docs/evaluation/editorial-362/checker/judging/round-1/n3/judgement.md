## My reading of the focus passage

The sentence is a two-part denial in the author's own voice: what she has in front of her is the template, and what she does not have is evidence about colleagues. Both halves are carried by the material. The first restates "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall"; in Swedish, *sitta med något* is the idiom for having something on one's hands, not a report of posture, so the boundary "Inga … kroppsliga känslor är lämnade" is untouched. The second matches the notes' own disclaimer "Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra" and the boundary "Ingen statistik eller allmän trend är belagd."

Read in context, the sentence asserts nothing about colleagues at all: it denies having grounds for a charge, and a denial of evidence leaves the state of knowledge exactly where the notes leave it. The phrase "kollegernas tillkortakommanden" names the charge being disowned; naming what one disclaims is not asserting it, and the disclaimer pushes the stance towards the "nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig" posture the boundaries ask for, rather than away from it. The passage needs no repair. A finding against it is therefore false — including a hedged one, since the hedge does not change what the report asks for.

For completeness, my own reading of the whole draft finds no passage that warrants repair. The places that differ most from the notes' wording — "kan behandlas" for "behandlas" (weaker), "ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" (a narrower denial, entailed by the broader one), "mallen" for "en mall" (narrower), "måste finnas utrymme" (advocacy about a template, not an upgrade of the notes' "kan"), the relocated comedy, and "den" in "Jag vet inte om den gör möten bättre" (with the box doubt restated in the next sentence) — all say the same or less than the material, and the remaining sentences are the author's own argument resting on supported material.
