# Judgement — p1-opinion-variable, round 1

Fixture: `docs/evaluation/editorial-362/checker/fixtures/p1-opinion-variable.md`. Reports judged: A, B, C, D, E (all files in this directory). Judged from the fixture; each report's reasoning was weighed as argument, not taken as evidence.

## My reading of the focus passage

Focus passage: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."

The material's sentence is "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana." The first two items carry over exactly. The third does not. *Vana* is habituation — how accustomed a person is to doing something — while "proficiency" is skill or competence. The sentence is a negative claim about the report's instrument, and that is precisely where the two come apart: the material lists what the report omits, not everything it contains, so a pilot report that put a short self-rated skill question to bookers and asked nothing about how habitually they use digital services would make the material's sentence true and the draft's sentence false. The draft therefore asserts an absence the material does not establish, and drops the one it does.

It matters in context rather than being a synonym quibble. The list already contains "functional ability", so "proficiency" names capability twice and leaves habituation unnamed — and habituation is the attribute the article's own question turns on two sentences later ("a reason to investigate why someone chose that option"), and the one the material makes load-bearing for "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt."

**The focus passage is defective; a repair is warranted before delivery.** A report that raises a finding against it is right to do so.

For reference, my own reading of the rest of the draft: every other factual and attributed claim is carried by the material at the right strength — the counts with their attribution to the report and the "bookings, not individual people" exclusion, the June 18 recommendation and its stated motive, the two absences "i handlingarna", the association's non-claim about funding, the preserved cost question, and the three-way closing decision. The evaluative passages ("essential questions unanswered", "an inadequate basis for a permanent change") are the sharp criticism of the decision basis that the boundaries expressly license.

Evidence:

- B-S1: the draft says the board is asked to act "with a pilot covering two", not that the recommendation is based on the pilot; read in context the sentence inventories what is before the board, which is the reading B itself calls "the natural one".
- B-S2: the immediately preceding sentence fixes the scope — "the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings" — and the rubric reads passages in context, not in isolation; B's own repair is marked optional.
- B-T1: as in my reading above; "Den mäter inte … digital vana" is habituation, and the draft's "digital proficiency" states an absence the material does not carry.
- B-T2: "varför telefonen fortfarande används" loses both the temporal marker and the aggregate subject in "why someone chose that option" — yet the material independently licenses the individual framing ("be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb"), so editors can reasonably split on whether time and subject are changed or only emphasis is.
- B-T3: the material permits quoting *or* referencing ("Detta får citeras eller refereras"); declining to express one half of a quotable statement asserts nothing unsupported, and the draft's aggregate sentence is carried verbatim by U1.
- B-T4: the material assigns the duty to "förvaltningen", and a case exists where the administration logs times automatically and no staff member records anything; against that, "Staff would record the time spent" is an ordinary English way of saying the municipality's own organisation would do it, and no claim in the piece turns on who holds the stopwatch. The disagreement turns on whether "staff" narrows an organisational duty to individual personnel in a way that can be false while the material is true.
- B-T5: "this trial" is the immediately preceding noun phrase and an association's "cost" is not at issue anywhere in the material; the alleged binding is not a reading the sentence has in context.
- B-T6: in this material the *föreningslokaler* are exactly the seven bookable premises at issue, and no case compatible with the material falls under one term and not the other. B itself labels this "(low, observation)" with an optional repair while still carrying it forward as unresolved; it is listed here for that reason.

**Focus passage:** raises a finding, after partially clearing it on support. Decisive sentences: "Support: supported as to the three absences. The third item's rendering is a translation finding, not a support failure — see **T1**: *digital vana* is habit or familiarity, not proficiency." T1 then demands a repair: "Smallest repair: '…age, functional ability, or how familiar people are with digital tools.'"
