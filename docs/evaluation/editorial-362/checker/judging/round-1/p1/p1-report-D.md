## Findings

### Finding 1 — "digital proficiency" names an attribute the material does not name (source support)

- **Draft:** "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
- **Source:** "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."
- **What differs:** the thing measured. "Digital vana" is habituation — how used to digital services someone is. "Digital proficiency" is skill or competence. In this context something falls under one and not the other: a resident can be entirely capable of filling in a web form yet have no habit of booking that way, and someone accustomed to digital services can still be unskilled at them. The two terms do not name the same thing, so the carry-over is not warranted.
- **Case in each direction:** suppose the pilot report includes a short digital-skills question but records nothing about how accustomed respondents are to booking online. That is compatible with everything the material states — the material lists what the report does not measure, not everything it does. In that case the source sentence still holds (no measure of "digital vana") while the draft sentence fails (proficiency was measured). Conversely, suppose the report logs only whether each booker had used the web portal before: habituation is then measured while proficiency is not, so the draft sentence holds and the source sentence fails. The two statements come apart in both directions.
- **Why it matters here:** this list is the load-bearing support for the next sentence's "therefore". The unmeasured attribute the argument needs is habituation — the reason someone still reaches for the phone — and substituting skill also shifts the item toward overlapping with "functional ability", already named in the same list.
- **Smallest supported repair:** replace the third item with the material's own attribute — "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital habit." If a more natural English noun phrase is wanted, "how used to digital services people are" or "familiarity with digital services" carries "vana" without adding skill.
