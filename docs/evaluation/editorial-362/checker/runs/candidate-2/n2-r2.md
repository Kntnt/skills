## 2. Checks that produced nothing to report

Recorded so that their absence is a result rather than an omission.

- **Invented specifics.** No fact, quotation, source, scene, personal experience or opinion appears that the material does not carry. There is no unnamed study or expert: every figure is attributed to the named report, and the only quoted position is the one the material supplies and licenses.
- **Forbidden content per the boundaries.** `Inga lagkrav, protester, diskrimineringsfall, partipolitiska motiv eller besparingar är belagda` — the draft asserts none of the five. Nothing is claimed about protests, discrimination, legal duty, party motives, or savings.
- **Evaluative characterisation of counts.** The draft reports 96 and 24 without calling either high, low or modest, without a ratio, and without converting bookings into persons or shares.
- **Causation.** No causal verb is applied to the pilot figures. `Att hänvisa till dubbel administration besvarar därför inte …` is an inference about what the documents establish, not a claim that one thing caused another, and no causal hedge smuggles an attribution in.
- **State of knowledge.** What the material leaves unknown stays unknown in both directions: the draft does not claim that telephone users cannot use the web, and does not claim that they can. It does not claim that double administration costs nothing, only that its size is undocumented.
- **Length.** The brief asks for `ungefär 400 ord`; the draft runs somewhat under that. Nothing has been invented to reach the figure, which is the only length question this comparison covers. Whether to close the remainder with further material is an editorial matter outside it.
