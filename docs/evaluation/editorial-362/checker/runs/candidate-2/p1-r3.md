## Considered and not reported

Recorded so a reader can see the tests that were run and why they did not yield findings.

**A. "digital proficiency" for "digital vana" (#11).** *Vana* is habit or familiarity; *proficiency* is skill, and the two can come apart — a person may be skilled but out of the habit. The claim, however, is negative in both texts: the report measures neither. For this to become a finding I would need a case, compatible with the material, in which the report measures proficiency but not habit, so that the source's denial holds while the draft's fails. The material describes a booking-count pilot that measures no user characteristic at all, and the enumerated list is what the report does not capture about its users, so no such case is available. Defensible as written.

**B. "Staff would record" for "ska förvaltningen registrera" (#26).** *Förvaltningen* is the administration as a body; the draft's preceding paragraph uses "staff" for *personalen*, the employees who would otherwise key data twice, so the same word now covers two source terms. I tested the case in which the administration captures time centrally — automatic timestamps compiled by a back-office unit — so that the administration records the time while front-line staff do not. That case is compatible with the material, but it does not make the draft's sentence false: in ordinary English, "staff would record the time" names the municipality's employees carrying out the administration's measurement, which is what happens in that case too. It also does not inflate the burden the draft asks the board to weigh, since the draft attributes the recording to the trial design rather than adding a new task for booking staff. Defensible as written.

**C. "community venues" for "föreningslokaler" (#3) and "online" for "digitalt" (#3, #12).** *Föreningslokaler* are premises booked by associations; "community venues" could in principle be read more widely. In this context the referent is fixed: the municipality's seven venues, two of which were in the pilot, and nothing in the material could fall under one term and not the other. Likewise, the only digital channel in this context is the web ("webb"), which the draft names as such in every other sentence, so "book online" and "boka digitalt" pick out the same channel here. Defensible as written.

**D. "a real objection" for "verkliga invändning" (#21).** Swedish *verklig* here means actual as against pretextual; English "real" can also be read as valid. I tested whether the draft concedes validity the material does not establish — that is, a case in which duplicate administration is genuinely what the administration objects to yet costs so little that the objection carries no weight. In that case the draft's sentence still holds, because it claims only that the objection is genuine and must be answered, and the very next sentence records that no measurement of that cost exists. Defensible as written.

**E. Lead compression of the post-trial decision (#5).** Reported in the accounting rather than as a finding: the three-way outcome is stated in full at #31, and "whether to remove" admits a negative answer, so no case arises in which the lead holds and the source fails.
