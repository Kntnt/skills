## Findings

### Finding 1 — the selection of the supplier is dated to September 2025 (source support, time)

- **Draft:** "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log and chose Svale Systems after testing whether it could show the status of each repair."
- **Source:** "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings. It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
- **Missing support:** the material dates only the decision to trial. The selection of Svale is placed after a period of testing and is given no date at all. Coordinating the two verbs under one "In September 2025" carries the date onto the selection and onto the testing that preceded it.
- **Concrete case, compatible with the material:** the team decides in September 2025 to trial a shared log, spends October testing whether candidate logs can show the status of each repair, and chooses Svale on 3 November 2025. The source statement holds; the draft statement fails. In the other direction, if the whole sequence fell inside September, both hold — so the difference is real and one-sided.
- **Smallest supported repair:** split the sentence and restore the source's noun. "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log. It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair." This also settles the "it" of row 10 without adding anything.

### Finding 2 — the title asserts an unscoped, present, achieved gain (source support, scope and time)

- **Draft:** title, "Elm Quay gains a shared view of repair reports".
- **Source:** "decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings"; "Elm Quay Housing manages 640 flats"; "The maintenance team ... kept telephone reporting open for residents"; the note "excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial"; "The trial lasted eight weeks"; "The trial has not yet expanded"; and, for the gain itself, only Lind: "Having one view of the reports helps us".
- **Missing or changed support:** the title drops the scope (two buildings, the trial's own reports) and the time (a finished eight-week trial that has not expanded), and states as the publication's own finding what the material supports only as the supervisor's assessment. The body carries all of these qualifications; the title does not.
- **Concrete case, compatible with the material:** emergency call-outs and repairs in the other buildings continue to arrive by telephone and email and stay outside the log, and the trial's two buildings hold a small share of the 640 flats. Elm Quay therefore has no shared view of its repair reports; it has one for a subset, during a trial that has ended. The source statements all hold; the title fails. Conversely, were the log in use for all of Elm Quay's repair reports, both would hold.
- **Smallest supported repair:** name the trial in the title rather than a gain, e.g. "Elm Quay tries a shared repair log in two buildings" — or, if the gain is to stay, scope it: "Elm Quay gains a shared view of repair reports in two buildings". Either keeps the body untouched.
