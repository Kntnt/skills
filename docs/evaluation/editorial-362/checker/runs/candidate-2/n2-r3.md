- The draft's alternation between "Vi" (association positions) and "Jag" (personal stance) tracks the material's own split between "Öppna beslut föreslår" / "Föreningen gör inget anspråk" and "Hon menar" / "Hon motsätter sig inte". It is a voice choice, not a source-support matter.

## Considered and rejected

- **C1 — "Då kan nästa beslut bygga på kunskap om både arbetet och användarnas skäl" (row 22).** Tested as a possible overclaim about a future result, since the users' reasons are given voluntarily and could be withheld. Rejected: the modality is possibility, the voluntariness is stated in the immediately preceding sentence, and the passage is the author's argument for her own proposal resting on the proposal as the material describes it.
- **C2 — "måste" for "behöver" (row 24).** Tested as a strengthening of modality. Rejected: both express necessity, the actor and object are unchanged, and in a signed opinion piece the demand is the author's own; no fact about the cost is asserted either way.
- **C3 — "Siffrorna" for "Telefonbokningarna" (row 9).** Tested as a widening of scope. Rejected on the two-direction test: no case compatible with the material lets the counts establish a share of inhabitants lacking digital ability.
- **C4 — "Förvaltningens motiv" for "Motivet i utlåtandet" (row 14).** Tested as an attribution shift from document to actor. Rejected: the material states förvaltningens real objection and it is the same ground.
- **C5 — Invention to reach length.** The brief sets roughly 400 words. Every factual sentence in the draft traces to a passage above; nothing was added to fill the length, and no qualification was dropped to fit it.
