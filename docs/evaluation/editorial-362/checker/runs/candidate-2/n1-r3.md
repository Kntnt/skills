### 2.1 "vi borde kunna försöka formulera varför vi behöver undersöka något tillsammans" (5d)

Source: "Men den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras
tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans."

Two differences are real and worth the both-direction test.

*Subject.* Source: "den som fyller i en mall" should be *permitted* ("borde få"). Draft:
"vi borde kunna försöka". Case in which they come apart: a template that permits its filler
to state a purpose, used by a team that never tries to. The source's permission is satisfied;
the draft's collective attempt is not. The shift is from a permission to an attempt — but
both are normative, both are the author's own stance in her own notes, and neither asserts
that anything has or has not happened. Nothing is presented as a given fact.

*Object.* Source: "varför vi behöver just varandras tid", with joint inquiry as a
*concessive* case ("också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans"). Draft: "varför vi
behöver undersöka något tillsammans" — joint inquiry as the object itself. Case in which they
come apart: a team can say why a topic needs joint investigation ("ingen har gått igenom
alternativen") without saying why *these* colleagues' time is needed.

Why this is nonetheless not a finding: the very next sentence is "Vad behöver just de här
människorna varandra till?", which restores the source's emphatic "just" and its
each-other element, and paragraph 4 has already asked "vad vi ber om när vi ber om varandras
tid". Read as an ordinary reader would in context, the passage delivers the source's point
across the two sentences rather than replacing it. The difference is one of distribution
across sentences, not of claim.

### 2.2 "den som planerar mötet" (2b)

The roles can come apart, but the clause sits inside a hypothetical the draft withdraws in
the following sentence, and asserts nothing about who fills in this library's template.

### 2.3 "kallelsen" (5c)

Names a document the notes do not name, but only inside a withheld demand, and in context
reads as the filled-in template whose "dagordning" box the material establishes.

### 2.4 "ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" (4c)

A narrower disclaimer than the source's. A narrower *denial* cannot create an unsupported
positive claim, and "arbetsdagar" is asserted of nothing.

### 2.5 "hur lätt tid i kalendern kan behandlas" (4a)

Hedged below the source's flat "behandlas". Weakening an assertion the author is entitled to
make preserves the state of knowledge; it does not overstate, and it does not drop a
qualification that changes a claim.
