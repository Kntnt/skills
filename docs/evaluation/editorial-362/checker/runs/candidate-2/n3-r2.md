## Passages examined as possible findings and released

Each was tested in both directions for a concrete case, compatible with the supplied material, in which one statement holds and the other fails. None yielded one that survives the draft's own context.

1. `ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar` narrows the note's `inte något jag har mätt hos andra`. Because both are denials of having measured, a narrower denial makes no positive claim; there is no case in which the draft asserts knowledge the note withholds.
2. `Jag vet inte om den gör möten bättre` shifts the note's uncertainty from the box to the question. One can imagine asking the question aloud without adding a box — but the sentence claims only ignorance, the material asserts no knowledge on either side, and the same paragraph restates the doubt about the box verbatim. Nothing is over-claimed in either direction.
3. `Också när vi ännu inte vet vad undersökningen kommer att leda till` for `också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans`. The clause is additive and permissive, so it cannot exclude a case the note admitted; and it attributes no outcome, duration, motive or result to any investigation.
4. `Jag vill inte skriva en mötesmall där...` might be read as asserting that she writes templates. In context she is inspecting one template and proposing an addition to it, and the sentence is a preference about what she would write, not a report of a role she holds.
5. `Tiden har fått två rutor` is a count. The base contract requires counts with their exclusions and without unsupported evaluative characterisation; this count is exhaustive over the fields the note lists and is offered bare, with no `få`, `många` or other assessment attached, so no comparison or capacity is needed.
6. `Det retar mig hur lätt tid i kalendern kan behandlas` could be read as a claim about workplaces generally. It is the note's own generalisation, at the note's own strength, and the next sentence disclaims measurement — the state of knowledge is preserved, not converted into an established trend.
7. Sequence and cause: the draft never says the missing box causes anything. `Det retar mig hur lätt...` reports her reaction, not a mechanism; no causal verb or causal hedge appears connecting the template to meeting outcomes.
