## 3. Cases considered and set aside

Each is tested in both directions, as required: a concrete case compatible with the supplied material in which one statement holds and the other fails. Where no such case stands, it is not a finding.

### 3.1 "Tiden har fått två rutor. Beslutet har ingen." (row 6)

Candidate case against the draft: the template also holds a third time-related box — a duration, a time zone, a break — so that time has three boxes and "två" fails while "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning" still holds, since that sentence does not say "endast".

The case does not stand. The supplied statement that excludes it is the notes' own construction: "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån." The "men ingen ruta för …" clause is an inventory contrast, and it only functions as one if the preceding list is the inventory. Read as an ordinary reader would read it in context, the source states what the template's boxes are, and exactly two of them are times. The draft's second sentence, "Beslutet har ingen", restates the source's own explicit absence claim. No finding.

### 3.2 "ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" (row 9)

Candidate case against the draft: she has measured something about others that is not their use of workdays, so the draft's narrower denial holds while the source's blanket "inte något jag har mätt hos andra" would fail — i.e. the draft leaves room the source closes.

This is the reverse of a defect. A narrower denial asserts less, not more: it states no measurement, no trend and no finding about anyone. The noun "arbetsdagar" appears only inside the denial and carries no assertion that any particular person's workday contains anything. The Boundaries' constraint — "Ingen statistik eller allmän trend är belagd" — is not breached by a sentence whose whole work is to deny having such evidence. No finding.

### 3.3 "Jag sitter med en mall" (row 10)

Candidate case against the draft: she reviews the template standing at a shelf, or scrolling on a phone; then "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall" holds while a literal "sitter" fails — and the Boundaries forbid inventing bodily detail ("Inga … kroppsliga känslor är lämnade. Uppfinn dem inte.").

The case does not stand as a claim about posture. In Swedish, "sitta med något" is the ordinary idiom for being occupied with a matter — as in "jag sitter med bokföringen" — and an ordinary reader takes it as "what I have in front of me is a template", not as a report of a seated body. The contrastive frame "Jag sitter med en mall, inte med bevis" confirms that reading: the contrast is between two kinds of thing one can have, not between postures. Nothing bodily is felt, no room is described, and no scene from a meeting is narrated, which is what the Boundaries actually withhold. No finding.

### 3.4 "Det finns något komiskt i tilltron till ännu en ruta." (row 22)

Candidate case against the draft: somebody could think expanding the form is comic without holding any confidence in the extra box, so the source's comedy (in the act) holds while the draft's comedy (in the confidence) fails.

The case does not stand as a defect of support. The draft attributes the confidence to no one but the author, and the very next sentence measures her own: "Min egen tilltro är heller inte större än att jag vill prova." The source supplies both halves — the joke about solving a form problem with more form, and the retained doubt about "ännu en ruta". The draft asserts no one else's state of mind. No finding.

### 3.5 Normative force in "Det måste finnas utrymme …" (row 16)

Candidate case against the draft: the source only says conversations "kan skapa förtroende", so a reader might take "måste" as asserting that such conversations reliably do so.

The case does not stand. "Det måste finnas utrymme att …" states what the author wants a template to allow, not what conversations achieve. It is her advocacy resting on supported material, and the Boundaries confirm the stance: "hon medger att icke-beslutande samtal har värde." The facts presented as given inside the sentence — that conversations may investigate together, create trust and surface an unforeseen question — are the source's own words. No finding.
