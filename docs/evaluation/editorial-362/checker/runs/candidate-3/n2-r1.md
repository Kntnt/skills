## 3. Differences tested in both directions

For each difference identified above, I looked for a concrete case, compatible with the supplied material, in which one statement holds and the other fails. A case is set aside only by quoting a supplied statement that excludes it.

1. **#10, "Siffrorna" for "Telefonbokningarna".** Sought a case in which the phone bookings cannot establish the proportion of residents unable to book digitally, yet the figures as a whole can. That would require the web count, alone or with the phone count, to fix that proportion. Excluded by two supplied statements: "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer" and "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana". A count of bookings that identifies no persons and records no ability fixes no proportion of residents in either channel. The reverse direction — the figures do not show it but the phone bookings do — is excluded by the same two sentences a fortiori. No case stands; the draft's wider subject is the material's own reasoning applied to its own scope, and it moves the claim toward less knowledge, not more. Not a finding.

2. **#16, "Förvaltningens motiv" for "Motivet i utlåtandet".** Sought a case in which the document states that motive while the administration's own motive is a different one — for instance staff convenience or an unstated saving. Excluded by the supplied statement "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra." Entering data in two flows is what that sentence calls double administration, so the material settles that the administration's real objection and the document's stated motive are the same one. The reverse direction — the administration holds that motive but the document states another — is excluded by "Motivet i utlåtandet är att personalen ska slippa föra in uppgifter i två flöden." No case stands. Not a finding, and the draft's wording is what keeps the criticism free of the false motive the boundaries forbid.

3. **#25, the unnamed agent in the imperative.** Sought a case in which the association's proposal holds as the material describes it but the draft's sentence fails — for instance the kommunstyrelse, not förvaltningen, being made to record the time. The draft sentence names no agent; it is a recommendation addressed to the body deciding, within a paragraph that opens "Öppna beslut föreslår ett sex månader långt försök i alla sju lokaler." Nothing in it asserts who performs the recording, so there is no proposition that can fail while the material's holds. The qualification that could have changed a claim, "frivilligt", is preserved. No case stands. Not a finding.

4. **#28, "måste" for "behöver".** Sought a case in which the kommunstyrelse needs to take a position on the cost but it is not the case that it must. Both forms express necessity of the same kind, and the sentence is advocacy addressed to the named body rather than a report of someone's assertion, so no modal gap opens between them. The direction that would matter — a draft claiming the cost has been settled, or that the association has covered it — is contradicted by the adjacent "Vi gör inget anspråk på att ha kostnadsberäknat eller finansierat försöket." No case stands. Not a finding.

5. **#4 and #30, "ett halvår" for "sex månader".** Sought a case in which a six-month trial is proposed but a half-year trial is not, or the reverse. The two name the same span, and the brief itself uses "ett halvår" for the same proposal. Not a finding.

6. **#31, "bokningsväg" for "kanal".** Sought a case in which something is one of the two channels without being one of the two booking routes, or the reverse. The material applies both terms to the same pair — "med båda bokningsvägarna kvar", "att behålla båda kanalerna", "registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" — and the only two things in question are telephone and web. Nothing in this context could fall under one term and not the other. Not a finding.

7. **#12, quoted position used unquoted.** Sought a case in which Ek holds that position but the draft's unattributed assertion of it misstates whose assertion it is. The draft is signed by Ek and the material clears reference as well as quotation: "Detta får citeras eller refereras." No third party's assertion is invoked. Not a finding.

8. **#5 and #21, third person to first person.** Sought a case in which Ek holds the view the material reports while the draft's first-person statement fails. The byline establishes that the draft's speaker is Ek. Not a finding.
