## 3. Findings

### F1 (source support) — a document the material does not supply

**Draft:** "Då kan vi knappast begära att den färdiga frågan ska finnas i kallelsen." (paragraph 5)

**Support that is missing:** the supplied material names exactly one document, the library's meeting template: "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall. Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån." Nothing in the brief, the notes or the boundaries mentions a *kallelse*, a notice sent to participants, or any relation between the template and such a notice. The draft's sentence presents that document as given background — the place where a question would be expected to stand — and background is a claim like any other.

**Case, compatible with the supplied material, in which the statements come apart:** the *mötesmall* is an internal planning form that the planner completes and keeps, with the agenda passed on verbally or in a calendar entry that has no question field at all. In that case every supplied statement about the template remains true and the draft's sentence names a document that plays no part in the process it is reasoning about. Tested the other way: the material equally permits the template to be exactly what is sent out — but permitting it is not supplying it, and no supplied statement excludes the first case. The case therefore stands.

**Smallest supported repair:** replace the unsupported document with the one the material supplies.

> Då kan vi knappast begära att den färdiga frågan ska finnas i mallen.

This is a one-word change. It keeps the sentence's function in the argument (the finished question cannot be demanded in advance), keeps the modality "kan vi knappast begära", keeps the rhythm and the author's voice, and rests only on the template the notes describe. "i rutan" would serve equally and ties the sentence to the box the column is arguing about; either is supported, and "i mallen" is the smaller change in sense.

No other finding.
