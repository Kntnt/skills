## Considered and not reported

These are the closest calls. Each was tested in both directions for a concrete case, compatible with the supplied material, in which one statement holds and the other fails. None yields one, so none is a finding.

**A. "Jag sitter med en mall" as an added scene.** Read literally, this adds a posture the notes do not supply, and the boundaries forbid inventing scenes. Read as an ordinary Swedish reader reads it in this context, *sitta med något* is the idiom for having a thing on one's hands, not a report of being seated; the sentence's own contrast ("en mall, inte ... bevis") is about what she has to go on, not where her body is. No setting, room, time of day or occasion is supplied around it, so no scene is built. Not a finding.

**B. "bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden".** The phrase names a shortcoming the notes never establish. But the sentence denies having evidence of it, and the denial is the whole point: it withdraws the standing her irritation might otherwise claim. A denial of evidence asserts neither that the shortcoming exists nor that it does not, which is precisely the state of knowledge the notes leave ("inte något jag har mätt hos andra"). The stance stays self-ironic rather than meeting-hostile. Not a finding.

**C. Where the comedy is located.** The notes put the comedy in the act — solving a form problem by extending the form. The draft (#22) puts it in the *tilltro* to one more box. Tested: a case where one holds and the other fails would be someone proposing the extra box with no confidence at all that it helps — the act is still comic, but there is no confidence for the comedy to attach to. The draft does not stand in that case, however, because it supplies the confidence in the next sentence and measures it: "Min egen tilltro är heller inte större än att jag vill prova." The wish to try is the confidence, the notes state it ("Ändå vill jag prova"), and the draft immediately caps it. Read in context, the two sentences together say what the note says. Not a finding.

**D. What "den" is uncertain about (#25).** The notes say she does not know whether *ytterligare en ruta* improves meetings, and separately assign hope to *den frågan* and doubt to *ännu en ruta*. In the draft, "den" resolves to *frågan* of the preceding line, so the uncertainty is voiced about the question. Tested for a case in which the notes hold and the draft fails: she would have to know the question improves meetings while doubting the box. The notes rule that case out — she *hopes* for the question, and hoping for an outcome entails not knowing it obtains. Tested in the other direction: the draft would fail the notes if it dropped the doubt about the box; it does not, keeping it in the same sentence-group and attached to the same object ("med tvivlet på ännu en ruta kvar"). Both of the source's attitudes survive, each on its own object. Not a finding.

**E. Length against the brief.** At 331 words the draft sits under "cirka 350 ord om underlaget bär det" and reaches that length without adding to the material: every paragraph traces to one of the four note paragraphs, and the added sentences are her own hedges and self-objections, not new facts. The brief's conditional ("om underlaget bär det") is honoured rather than filled out. Not a finding.
