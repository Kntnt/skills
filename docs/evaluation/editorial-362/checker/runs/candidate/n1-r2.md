## Findings

### Finding 1 — the proposal's object and its qualification are exchanged (source support)

**Draft (paragraph 5):** "Men vi borde kunna försöka formulera varför vi behöver **undersöka något tillsammans**."

**Source (Nora Viks egna anteckningar, paragraph 3):** "Men den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera **varför vi behöver just varandras tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans**."

**What differs.** In the material the thing to be formulated is *why we need precisely each other's time*, and "att undersöka något tillsammans" is a qualification marking the hard case in which that same demand still applies ("också när"). The draft makes the investigation itself the thing to be justified, and the qualification disappears as a qualification. The scope of the proposal changes with it: the note's demand reaches every meeting, including exploratory ones; the draft's demand is about stating a reason for exploring together.

**Both directions, with concrete cases compatible with the material.**
- A case where the draft's sentence holds and the source's fails: the template is filled in for a meeting whose purpose is plainly exploratory, and the planner writes "vi behöver undersöka den nya låneprocessen tillsammans". The draft's requirement is satisfied. The note's requirement is not: nothing has been said about why *these* colleagues' time, rather than anyone's, is needed.
- A case where the source's sentence holds and the draft's fails: the planner writes "jag behöver Samis katalogkunskap och Idas erfarenhet av lånedisken i samma rum". The note's requirement is satisfied and its "också när" clause is honoured, while the draft's requirement — a stated reason for investigating something together — has not been answered at all.

**Why the following sentence does not settle it.** "Vad behöver just de här människorna varandra till?" restores the mutual-need element, but it is posed as an open question rather than as the thing the template asks a planner to formulate, and it leaves the "också när" qualification unrecovered. The sentence at issue is the one that states the proposal, and it states a different proposal from the one in the material.

**Smallest supported repair.** Restore the object and the qualification, keeping the draft's own modality and its "vi":

> Men vi borde kunna försöka formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans.

This leaves the paragraph's rhythm, the surrounding sentences and the closing question untouched.
