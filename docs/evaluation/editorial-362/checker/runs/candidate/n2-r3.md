## Checks that produced no finding

- **No unsupported causal claim.** The draft's argumentative verbs are *besvarar inte*, *räcker inte*, *kan bygga på*. Nowhere does it say the digital switch would exclude anyone, that telephone use is caused by inability, or that the change would save or cost anything. The source's own denial (row 12) is preserved as a denial.
- **No evaluative characterisation of the counts.** 96 and 24 appear with their exclusions (bookings not persons; two venues; eight weeks) and without being called many, few, high, low or modest, so no comparison, target, capacity or speaker assessment is owed.
- **No invented material.** No law, protest, discrimination case, party-political motive or saving appears, in line with the boundaries note. No unnamed study, expert or official is invoked; every attributed claim names the pilot report, the tjänsteutlåtande, förvaltningen, Öppna beslut or the author.
- **Length did not drive invention.** The brief asks for roughly 400 words and the draft runs somewhat under that. Nothing is padded: there is no filler statistic, no speculative example and no added scene or personal experience. The shortfall is the material's, and it is handled by leaving the gap unfilled rather than by inventing to fill the form.
- **Pronouns.** *Hon* → *jag* (row 20) changes speaker, not asserted content, and drops rather than adds natural-gender information. Every *den* in the draft refers to *kommunen*, *kommunstyrelsen*, *telefonbokningen* or *arbetskostnaden* and is grammatical utrum, not a natural-gender assertion. *Vi* / *vårt* refer to Öppna beslut, which the byline entitles the author to speak for.
- **Translation.** Draft and material are both Swedish; the one passage taken from quoted speech (row 12) is presented unquoted as the author's own referenced position, which the material expressly allows. Nothing crosses a language boundary, so there is no translation finding to report.
