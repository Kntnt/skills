## Findings

### Finding 1 — Title asserts a standing, organisation-wide gain (scope and time)

- Draft: "Elm Quay gains a shared view of repair reports"
- Support in the material: "decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings"; "The trial lasted eight weeks."; "The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs."
- What is missing or changed: the material supports a shared view for the maintenance team, in two buildings, during an eight-week trial. The title's subject is Elm Quay (640 flats), its aspect is a completed acquisition, and it carries no time limit and no trial. Concrete case compatible with the material, in which the source holds and the draft fails: the trial ends, the team pauses the log while it checks how the categories work for larger repairs, and the remaining buildings continue on separate telephone and email records — nothing in the material excludes this, yet "Elm Quay gains a shared view of repair reports" would then be false. The reverse case (Elm Quay holding a standing, company-wide shared view) is not supported anywhere. The summary's "in two buildings" does not repair the title, since the boundary holds in the title as well.
- Smallest supported repair: "Elm Quay's repair-log trial gives two buildings one view of reports" — or, keeping the draft's wording more nearly intact, "Elm Quay's maintenance team gains a shared view of repair reports in two buildings".

### Finding 2 — September 2025 is extended to the supplier choice (chronology)

- Draft: "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log and chose Svale Systems after testing whether it could show the status of each repair."
- Support in the material: "In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings." and, as a separate undated sentence, "It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
- What is missing or changed: the material dates only the decision. By coordinating "decided" and "chose" under one date adverbial, the draft dates the supplier choice — and the testing that preceded it — to September 2025 as well. Concrete case compatible with the material, in which the source holds and the draft fails: the team decides in late September, tests the status display through the first days of October, and chooses Svale in October, with the eight-week trial starting mid-October and the note following on 4 December 2025. The material excludes nothing in that sequence, yet the draft's sentence would be false. The reverse case (a choice made within September) is equally compatible, which is the point: the material leaves the date open and the draft closes it.
- Secondary defect in the same sentence: "whether it could show the status of each repair" leaves "it" attachable to "Svale Systems", whereas the material tests "whether the log could show the status of each repair". The supplied context settles the referent, so naming it is a clarification rather than an addition.
- Smallest supported repair: split the sentence and restore the material's noun — "In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log. It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair."
