## Cross-cutting checks

- **Unsupported additions.** No fact, quotation, source, scene, personal experience or opinion appears that the material does not carry. In particular the draft adds no legal requirement, protest, discrimination case, party-political motive and no claimed saving — each of which the boundaries mark as unestablished.
- **Evaluative characterisations.** None attaches to the counts. The figures 96 and 24 are given with their exclusions (bookings not persons; no age, ability or digital-habit measurement) and without being called high, low or modest, so no comparison, target, capacity or speaker assessment is owed.
- **Causation.** No causal verb and no causal hedge is applied to the figures or to the proposal. The draft's strongest evidential claim, "besvarar därför inte frågan", is about what the documents establish, not about what caused what. The title, headings and body are consistent on this.
- **State of knowledge.** The draft repeatedly says what the material does *not* show (8, 11, 15, 16) and nowhere converts that silence into a claim that the opposite is known — e.g. it never says that no resident is unable to book digitally, and never says that double administration costs nothing.
- **Attribution.** Every figure that carries the argument is attributed to the pilot report; the removal proposal to the tjänsteutlåtande; the trial proposal to Öppna beslut; the cost decision to kommunstyrelsen. No unnamed study or expert is invoked.
- **Person, number and natural gender.** First-person singular ("jag", "min") = Sanna Ek; first-person plural ("vi", "vårt") = Öppna beslut, the association she speaks for per the byline. "Den" (kommunen, kommunstyrelsen) and "de" (användarna, siffrorna) carry grammatical gender/number only and assert no natural gender. The source's "hon" for Sanna Ek is the material's only natural-gender assertion; the draft neither contradicts nor relies on it.
- **Length.** The brief asks for roughly 400 words and the draft is somewhat under that. Nothing has been invented to reach the length, and no qualification has been dropped to fit a limit, which is what the Claims section governs; word count as such is general editorial review and outside this comparison.
- **Translation.** Not applicable — draft and material are both Swedish. No meaning crosses a language boundary, so there are no translation findings and none of the pairs above is reclassified as one.
