## 2. Findings

Two findings. Both are source-support findings; neither is a translation finding.

### Finding 1 — the absence is widened from the decision to everything the time is for

- **Draft**: "Tiden har fått två rutor. Vad vi ska kunna göra med den har inte fått samma tydliga plats." (paragraph 1)
- **Source**: "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån."
- **What is missing or changed**: the material withholds a box from one thing only — the decision to be made on leaving. The draft withholds "samma tydliga plats" from *what we shall be able to do with the time*, which is a larger class. The template's own "dagordning" box, which the draft names in the preceding sentence, is a field for what will be done with the time; so the draft's generalisation is undercut by the enumeration it rests on. The claim is also evaluative and comparative ("samma tydliga plats" as the two time boxes), and the comparison target for that judgement is the decision box that the material says is absent, not the agenda box that it says is present.
- **Both directions**: a template whose agenda field reads "Vad ska vi göra under mötet?" satisfies the source statement (still no decision box) while failing the draft statement (what we shall do with the time does have a clear place). Conversely, a template with a decision box but no agenda field satisfies the draft statement while failing the source statement. The two come apart in both directions, and the first case is the one the supplied material describes.
- **Smallest supported repair**: narrow the second sentence to the decision the material names, e.g. "Beslutet har inte fått någon ruta alls." The paragraph's rhythm and the two-boxes-for-time contrast survive unchanged.

### Finding 2 — a document the material does not supply

- **Draft**: "Då kan vi knappast begära att den färdiga frågan ska finnas i kallelsen." (paragraph 5)
- **Source**: the material names one document only, "bibliotekets mötesmall", filled in by "den som fyller i en mall". The word *kallelse*, and any sending-out of an invitation, appear nowhere in the brief, the notes or the judging boundaries.
- **What is missing or changed**: the background fact that this library's meetings are convened through a kallelse, and that the planner's formulation would land there, has no support. A *mall* is the form; a *kallelse* is a notice issued for a particular meeting. Something can fall under one and not the other, so the terms do not name the same thing here, and the substitution quietly moves the argument off the document the column is actually examining.
- **Both directions**: a library that fills in the template as an internal planning record and convenes meetings by a calendar entry with no kallelse satisfies everything in the material while making the draft's sentence false. Conversely, a kallelse written from scratch without any template satisfies the draft's sentence while falling outside the material. Compatible cases exist in both directions.
- **Smallest supported repair**: replace "i kallelsen" with "i mallen". One word changes; the sentence's point — that a question one does not yet have cannot be demanded in advance — is untouched, and it re-anchors the paragraph to the template the next paragraph returns to.
