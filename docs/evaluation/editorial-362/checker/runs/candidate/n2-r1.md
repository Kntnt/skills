## 2. Claims specifically tested and cleared

- **No evaluative characterisation of the counts.** The draft reports 96 and 24 without calling either high, low, few or modest, and without a comparison, target or capacity that the material does not supply. The exclusions travel with the figures (bookings not unique persons; eight weeks; two premises).
- **No causal claim.** No passage converts the sequence *phone still used → people cannot use the web* into a cause; #10 explicitly denies that inference, in the source's own words.
- **State of knowledge preserved.** What the material says is unmeasured stays unmeasured: the draft nowhere converts the absence of a time measurement into a claim that the administrative burden is small, nor the absence of a savings calculation into a claim that there are no savings, nor the unmeasured age and ability data into a claim about who can or cannot book digitally.
- **Excluded material stays out.** Nothing is asserted about legal requirements, protests, discrimination cases, party-political motives or savings — the five things the material marks as unestablished.
- **No false motive, no toothless neutralisation.** The criticism of the decision basis is sharp (#11, #15, #16, #19) while the administration's motive is reported as the material gives it (#13).
- **Length.** The brief asks for roughly 400 words and the draft runs somewhat under that. The draft does not pad the gap with unsupported detail, which is the behaviour the contract requires when material is finite; the shortfall is a matter of the material's extent and not a source-support defect. No further material is needed to close a support gap, because no claim lacks support.
- **Pronouns and person.** Accounted for at #3, #18, #19, #23 and in the voice note above. No natural-gender assertion is made beyond the source's *hon* for Sanna Ek; every other *den*/*det* is grammatical.
