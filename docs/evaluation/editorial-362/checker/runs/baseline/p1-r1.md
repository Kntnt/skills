## C. Source-support findings

### C1 — "a real objection" shifts *which objection is genuine* into *the objection is valid* (medium)

- Draft: "Duplicate administration is a real objection that our proposal must address."
- Supplied: "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra."
- What is changed: "verklig invändning" identifies the administration's *actual* objection, in contrast to motives the author must not impute. English "a real objection" instead grades the objection as substantive — an assessment nothing supplied makes. The supplied contrast (not laziness, not expense), which is what enforces J's ban on false motives, disappears with it, and the sentence no longer attributes the objection to the administration at all.
- Smallest supported repair: "Duplicate administration is the administration's actual objection to keeping both channels, and our proposal must address it."

### C2 — the pilot is placed as the recommendation's evidence base (low)

- Draft: "The board is being asked to remove a booking option across seven venues with a pilot covering two and no measured account of the labor involved."
- Supplied: T gives the recommendation's motive as sparing staff two workflows; nothing supplied says the recommendation rests on, or cites, the pilot report. R fixes the pilot at two venues; T fixes the proposal at seven and the absence of any time measurement.
- What is missing: support for the implicature that the two-venue pilot is the evidential basis of the seven-venue proposal. Every component of the sentence is supported; only the implied dependence is not.
- Smallest supported repair: "The board is being asked to remove a booking option across seven venues when the only trial so far covered two, and with no measured account of the labor involved."

### C3 — the board's duty regarding cost is softened to an invitation (low)

- Draft: "Its cost remains a question for the board to consider."
- Supplied: "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till."
- What is changed: the supplied sentence places an obligation on the board to take a position; "a question for the board to consider" states only that the question exists. The cost uncertainty J requires is preserved either way, so this is a strength shift, not an invention.
- Smallest supported repair: "Its cost is for the board to take a position on."

## D. Translation findings (Swedish source → en_US draft)

Each entry names a concrete obstruction to reading the passage as the source's meaning in idiomatic en_US, not a preferred synonym. The draft contains no direct quotation of S; S is referenced in the author's own voice, which the material permits, so the quotation-fidelity check applies to that paraphrase (D2).

### D1 — "digital vana" rendered as "digital proficiency" (medium)

- Draft: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
- Source: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."
- Obstruction: "vana" is habituation — how used to something a person is — and the supplied list keeps it distinct from "funktionsförmåga" (ability). "Proficiency" is a skill level, which restates the ability item and drops habit from the list. The distinction is load-bearing for this piece: its whole argument separates *cannot use the web* from *still uses the phone*. As rendered, the report is said not to have measured capability twice and habit not at all.
- Repair: "…age, functional ability, or how used to digital services people are." ("digital habits" is the idiomatic short form.)

### D2 — "fortfarande" dropped from the referenced position (low)

- Draft: "But a phone booking is a reason to investigate why someone chose that option."
- Source: "Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används."
