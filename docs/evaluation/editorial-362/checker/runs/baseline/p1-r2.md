## 4. Findings — translation into en_US

The draft contains no direct quotation. U4's quotable sentences are rendered as indirect speech in Ek's own first-person voice, which the material permits ("Detta får citeras eller refereras"). The findings below concern whether that rendering carries the same meaning and voice in idiomatic American English.

**T1 — "digital proficiency" for *digital vana* (moderate)**
- Passage: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
- Obstruction: *vana* is habit, familiarity, or being used to something — what a person routinely does. *Proficiency* is skill level — what a person is able to do. The English list therefore names ability twice ("functional ability", "proficiency") and never names habit. That matters here and not merely as a synonym preference: the article's central question two sentences later is why the phone is *still* used, which is a question about habit and circumstance, not competence. As written, the report's stated blind spot no longer lines up with the question the article says the trial should answer.
- Smallest repair: "…age, functional ability, or how familiar people are with digital tools." (Or "digital familiarity", if the list should stay parallel.)

**T2 — loss of *fortfarande* ("still") (moderate)**
- Passage: "But a phone booking is a reason to investigate why someone chose that option."
- Obstruction: U4's "varför telefonen **fortfarande** används" carries the argument's pivot — the web exists and is the majority channel, and the phone persists anyway; that persistence is the thing worth explaining. "Why someone chose that option" reduces an ongoing pattern to one person's single past decision, and the rhetorical point that something survives despite the alternative disappears. This is a dropped qualifier, not a stylistic preference.
- Smallest repair: see T3, which fixes both in one sentence.

**T3 — the quotation's first half is not expressed (moderate)**
- Passage: the same sentence, plus "The phone bookings therefore cannot tell us what share of Lervik's residents cannot book online."
- Obstruction: U4's position has two halves in a deliberate parallel — "is **not proof** that someone cannot use the web" / "**It is a reason** to find out why the phone is still used." The draft renders the second half and replaces the first with an aggregate claim from U1 (what share of residents cannot book online). The two are not the same proposition: U1's is about a population share; U4's is about what a single booking does and does not prove about a single person. The individual-level point — the one that actually answers the "phone users can't cope" inference a reader may be carrying — is absent, and the parallel construction that gives the position its voice is lost with it.
- Smallest repair (fixes T2 and T3 together, replacing the draft's "But a phone booking is a reason to investigate why someone chose that option."): "A booking made by phone is not proof that the caller could not have used the web. It is a reason to find out why the phone is still being used."

**T4 — "Staff" for *förvaltningen* (low)**
- Passage: "Staff would record the time spent on each booking method…"
- Obstruction: the draft uses "staff" twice for two different bodies — *personalen* (the staff who key in bookings, in the tjänsteutlåtande's rationale) and *förvaltningen* (the administration, which would run the trial's data collection). American English distinguishes these readily. As written, the trial's recording duty appears to fall on the same front-line staff whose workload is the objection, which quietly weakens the answer to that objection.
- Smallest repair: "The administration would record the time spent on each booking method…"

**T5 — antecedent of "Its" (low)**
- Passage: "Öppna beslut has not claimed to have funded or costed this trial. Its cost remains a question for the board to consider."
- Obstruction: the preceding sentence's subject is "Öppna beslut", so "Its cost" can momentarily bind to the association rather than to the trial. The supplied context settles the intended referent (U3's "Kostnaden" follows the trial's description and is what the board must take a position on), which is the condition under which clarifying an implicit referent is warranted.
- Smallest repair: "The trial's cost remains a question for the board to consider."

**T6 — "community venues" for *föreningslokaler* (low, observation)**
- Passage: title-adjacent and lead: "online-only booking for community venues".
- Obstruction: *föreningslokaler* are premises booked by associations and clubs. "Community venues" is idiomatic and readable but broadens the user group toward the general public, which slightly loosens the fit with "Öppna beslut", an association, as the speaker. The report title is kept in Swedish, so no contradiction arises on the page.
- Smallest repair, if precision is wanted: "booking of the municipality's club and association venues" at first mention only, with "venues" thereafter. Optional.
