**F2 — Success verb attributing an unobserved explanatory act (C16). Severity: medium.**
- Draft passage: “Ortiz **explained** the distinction …”
- Changed support: the material has Ortiz *sending* three complete quotations in an email headed “notes for publication,” and states there was no Q&A transcript and no observation. “Explained” asserts both an act of explanation and the correctness of what was said, neither of which the material carries.

- Note: F1 and F2 sit in the same six-word clause; the single replacement “Ortiz drew the distinction in the email:” closes both.

**F3 — Title asserts a present, unattributed, unqualified practice (C1). Severity: medium.**
- Draft passage: “# Briar County volunteers share records for new donations”
- Missing/changed support: the material gives a past capability (“could consult the same records”), reported by the note rather than observed, and bounded by an eight-week pilot whose sequel the archive has not yet decided. The title converts capability into practice, past into present, and reported into flat.
- Smallest repair: “# Briar County volunteers tested a shared catalog for new donations” — every element directly supported by “the archive’s collection team chose to test a shared catalog for incoming donations.” If present tense is wanted for headline convention, “share one catalog” must still be given the pilot’s bound, e.g. “… in an eight-week pilot.”

**F4 — Attribution applied asymmetrically to the two note figures (C2, C15). Severity: low.**
- Draft passages: “cataloged 47 incoming donations during an eight-week pilot” (flat) vs. “The archive’s May 6 pilot note records that 11 records retained uncertain dates” (attributed).
- Changed support: both figures come from the same self-reported note; the material frames the 47 as what the note “records.”
- Smallest repair: either attribute both, or, if the lead should stay flat, leave it — the count is the archive’s record of its own operations and no competing figure exists. Recorded for the author’s decision rather than as a defect requiring change.

**F5 — Paraphrase widens the coordinator’s condition (C4). Severity: low.**
- Draft passage: “with further testing before bringing in older collections”
- Changed support: the quotation says “test the descriptions on more posters” and “the older collection” (singular, definite).
- Smallest repair: “with more testing of the descriptions before bringing in the older collection.”
