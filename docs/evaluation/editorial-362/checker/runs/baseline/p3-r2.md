Two low-severity observations, neither a defect:

**O1 — Dropped modifier in the survey sentence (C14).**
Draft: "nine said they would consider using a room on weekday evenings." Material: "would consider using a rehearsal room on weekday evenings."
The modifier "rehearsal" is dropped. The inference holds on that reading, and the claim errs conservatively.
