## 4. Findings — source support

### S1 (primary). "när jag saknar rutan för beslut" attributes to the author a wish the material does not give her

- Draft passage (¶4): "Så redan när jag saknar rutan för beslut behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv."
- Proposition tested (C16): *Nora Vik misses, i.e. wants, the template's absent box for the decision.*
- What the material gives: N1 records the missing decision box as **an observation of the document** — "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall… men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån. Det är en iakttagelse av dokumentet." N3 states the opposite-leaning intention: "Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut." N4's proposal is a box for a **question**, not for a decision.
- Missing support: no supplied statement says she wants a decision box. The negation ("she does not want the template to have a box for the decision") is ruled out by nothing; a source-compatible counterexample is that she notes the two-to-zero asymmetry only to show that the template records duration where it should record purpose, and wants the *question* box instead.
- Why it matters here rather than being a word choice: the sentence is the hinge of the draft's argument. If she is reported as wanting a decision box, the objection she then makes to herself ("Jag vill inte att varje samtal ska pressas fram till ett avgörande") is an objection to a position the material never gives her, and the column's middle becomes a retreat from an invented demand. It also puts her a step closer to the meeting-hostile stance BJ rules out.
- Smallest supported repair: replace the wish with the observation, leaving the rest of the sentence intact —
  "Så redan när jag noterar att beslutet inte har någon ruta behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv."
  (Alternative of the same size: "Så redan när jag pekar på den saknade rutan…".) The self-objection still lands, because it now answers the complaint implicit in ¶1's "Beslutet har ingen" rather than an unsourced demand.

### S2. The disclaimer in ¶2 misstates what was not measured

- Draft passage: "Det är min reflektion, ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar."
- Source: N2 — "Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra."
- What changed: the source denies having measured *the phenomenon just described* (calendar time treated as a result) in others. The draft denies having measured *how others use their workdays* — a different and broader object, and "arbetsdagar" appears nowhere in the material.
- Direction of risk: the denial adds no positive claim, so the state of knowledge is preserved; what it does is frame the column as being about other people's workday habits, which the material does not establish as her subject.
- Smallest supported repair: "Det är min reflektion, inget jag har mätt hos andra."

### S3. "bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden" imports a frame the notes do not contain

- Draft passage: "Jag sitter med en mall, inte med bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden."
- Source: N2's disclaimer of measurement; BJ's "nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig".
- What changed: nothing in the material raises the idea that colleagues have shortcomings that evidence could establish. It appears only inside a denial, and it works in BJ's favour by disowning hostility, so it is not an unsupported positive claim. It is recorded because it is the one place where the draft introduces an adversarial frame of its own, and because the denial's force depends on a premise the notes do not supply.
- Smallest supported repair (optional; the sentence is defensible as written): "Jag sitter med en mall, inte med siffror på hur andra gör." — or delete the clause, leaving C11 to carry the disclaimer.

### S4. "mallen" narrows N3's generic "en mall"

- Draft passage (¶5): "Däremot vill jag att den som fyller i mallen får formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid."
- Source: N3 — "den som fyller i **en** mall borde få formulera…"
- What changed: the source's wish is general (anyone filling in any meeting template); the draft attaches it to the library's template. A narrowing, not an inflation, so no over-claim; recorded for completeness because it is a qualification that applies across the clause.
