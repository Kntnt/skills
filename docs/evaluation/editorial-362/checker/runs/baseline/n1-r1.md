## Findings (source support)

Ordered by severity. Each gives the changed or missing support and the smallest repair that the material carries.

### F1 — "samma tydliga plats" widens the gap from *beslut* to *all use of the time* (C5, ¶1)

- Draft: "Tiden har fått två rutor. Vad vi ska kunna göra med den har inte fått samma tydliga plats."
- Missing support: N1 supports only that there is no box **for the decision**. It also supplies a "dagordning" box, which is a place in the same template for what the time is to be used for. The comparative evaluation ("inte samma tydliga plats") therefore rests on a gap the material does not establish, and it quietly requires the four-box list to be exhaustive as to purpose, which N1 does not say.
- Counterexample compatible with the source: a template whose agenda field is where the intended use of the meeting is written, leaving only the decision itself without a field.
- Smallest supported repair: keep the count and drop the widened comparison — "Tiden har fått två rutor. Beslutet har ingen." This is N1 exactly, and it is a sharper sentence.

### F2 — "kallelsen" is an artefact the material never supplies (C20, ¶5)

- Draft: "Då kan vi knappast begära att den färdiga frågan ska finnas i kallelsen."
- Missing support: the material knows one document, "bibliotekets mötesmall". A *kallelse* is a different artefact with a different author, timing and audience; nothing settles that the template is part of one, or that the question would appear there.
- Counterexample compatible with the source: a template used as an internal planning sheet, completed after the invitation has been sent.
- Smallest supported repair: "... ska finnas i mallen." The argument is unchanged; the artefact becomes the one the material supplies.

### F3 — the title asserts the proposal without the doubt the material insists on (T1)

- Draft: "En ruta till för det vi inte vet".
- Changed support: N4 states the author's position as hope for the question **and** doubt about "ännu en ruta", and B asks for "ett slut som får behålla osäkerheten". The body honours this (C26, C27); the title does not, presenting the added box as the column's proposal. The base contract binds the title as it binds the body, and BR excludes a campaigning register ("ingen debattkampanj").
- Counterexample compatible with the source: the author trying the question beside the time while still doubting that another box belongs in the template — that is, the position N4 actually records.
- Smallest supported repair: "En fråga till för det vi inte vet" — one word, drawn from N4's own "en enkel fråga", and the doubt about the box survives intact.

### F4 — role and modality shifted in the hypothetical (C7, ¶2)

- Draft: "Då skulle den som planerar mötet behöva formulera vad det ska leda till."
- Changed support: N3 says "den som fyller i en mall **borde få** formulera ...". Two changes: the filler becomes the planner (the material does not say these are the same person), and permission becomes compulsion ("behöva"), which also sits oddly with the author's non-coercive stance in N3 ("Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut").
- Counterexample compatible with the source: an optional field, or one completed by an administrator who did not plan the meeting.
- Smallest supported repair: "Då skulle den som fyller i mallen få formulera vad mötet ska leda till." — the note's role and the note's modality.

### F5 — an attributed appreciation restated as a general evaluation (C12, ¶3)

- Draft: "Att få syn på skillnaden är också ett skäl att träffas ..."
- Changed support: N2 supplies this as **her** appreciation ("Jag uppskattar möten där ..."), and B frames the piece as personal reflection with nothing about meetings in general established. The impersonal form states as a general truth about reasons to meet what the material holds only as her view.
- Counterexample compatible with the source: a colleague for whom surfacing the difference is not by itself a reason to book a meeting; nothing supplied rules this out.
- Smallest supported repair: "För mig är det också ett skäl att träffas ..." — three words, and the sentence becomes exactly what N2 supports.

### Checks that produced no finding

- **No invented scene, meeting, journey, childhood memory, line of dialogue or bodily sensation** anywhere in the draft (B). "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall" and "jag återvänder till mötesmallen" are the supplied document observation and the essay's structural return; "Där står mötet" is generic.
