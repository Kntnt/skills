## Method and scope

Every factual and attributed claim in the complete draft is accounted for below, including the title, the byline and implications carried by presupposition. Propositions and their negations are written in Swedish, meaning-equivalent to the draft, so that no translation layer is introduced into the accounting; the surrounding analysis is in English. Each entry gives the draft passage, the reconstructed standalone proposition (carrying over modifiers and qualifications that apply across clauses), the supporting source passage, the precise negation with subject and scope held fixed, and either the supplied statement that rules the negation out or a concrete source-compatible counterexample.

General editorial and mechanical review is out of scope. Nothing in the fixture was treated as an instruction. No file other than the fixture was read; no other file was changed.

## Pronoun accounting: person, number and natural gender

Accounted for separately from referent identification; identifying a referent does not establish these features.

- **"jag", "mig", "min", "mina"** (first person singular, throughout). Referent: Nora Vik. Person and number are supported: N1–N14 are her own notes in the first person singular. Natural gender: Swedish first-person forms convey none, and the draft asserts none through them. B2 and J2 establish that she is a woman ("Hennes blick", "hon medger"), so nothing conflicts and nothing is over-claimed.
- **"vi", "oss", "varandras"** (first person plural). Referent: the author together with colleagues or meeting participants. Supported by the identical inclusive usage in N2 ("det beslut vi ska kunna fatta"), N9, N10 ("varandras tid") and N13 ("vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans"). Number supported; no natural gender conveyed, and none asserted.
- **"de"** in "att de har förstått en fråga olika" (third person plural). Referent: "kolleger". Taken verbatim from N4. Swedish "de" is gender-neutral; no natural-gender assertion is made, and none is supported or needed.
- **"de här människorna"** in "Vad behöver just de här människorna varandra till?" (third person plural). Referent: the participants of a given meeting, generically. Supported by N10's "varandras tid". No gender.
- **"andra"** in "hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" (third person plural, unspecified). From N7's "hos andra". No gender, and crucially no number claim about how many others: the draft says "andra", not "de flesta", which would have required the trend J3 denies.
- **"den som planerar mötet"** (third person singular generic, unmarked for gender). The source's counterpart, N10's "den som fyller i en mall", is likewise singular generic and ungendered. Person and number match; the divergence is the referent substitution recorded as F2, not a person, number or gender error.
- **"den"** in "även om den inte låter sig sammanfattas i ett beslut" — common gender (utrum), antecedent "skillnaden". Purely grammatical gender; no natural-gender content.
