## 2. Finding dispositions

### Finding 1 — "digital proficiency" for "digital vana" — **accepted**

Draft passage (fixture line 42): "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
Source passage (fixture line 13): "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."

Evidence for accepting:

1. *The two terms do not name the same thing in this context.* "Vana" is habituation — how accustomed and practised in a service a person is. "Proficiency" is skill or capability. A resident can be highly proficient with digital services and still use them rarely (high proficiency, low vana); the two attributes come apart. Under the term-carrying test, a term carried into another language names the same thing only where nothing in this context could fall under one term and not the other, and here something plainly can.

2. *The draft's proposition, established in full context, is not supported.* The sentence is a negative existence claim about the pilot report's contents: it asserts that a particular attribute was not measured. The supplied material asserts the non-measurement of three attributes and names them exhaustively as a list; "digital proficiency" is not among them. The immediately preceding item, "funktionsförmåga", is carried accurately as "functional ability" and in this Swedish policy register means functional capacity or disability, not digital skill, so it does not absorb the proficiency claim.

3. *The report's concrete case stands and no supplied statement excludes it.* The case named — a pilot report that checks whether a booker completed the online form unaided while recording nothing about how routinely they use digital services — is compatible with the material. "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer" states what the counts represent, not that the report holds no attribute data; on the contrary, the following sentence's list of unmeasured attributes presupposes that measuring such attributes was possible and therefore worth ruling out one by one. "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt" limits what the phone-booking count can show about residents; it does not assert the report contains no ability measure. I can quote no supplied statement that excludes the case, which is the only way the task permits setting it aside.

4. *This is not a merely conceivable stronger reading of the draft.* The defect is in the draft's own plain reading: in its ordinary contextual sense the sentence asserts the absence of a proficiency measurement, which no supplied passage supports, and drops the habit dimension the material does name — the dimension the proposed trial exists to investigate ("be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb"; "varför telefonen fortfarande används"). The gap is one of source support, not of preferred wording or of qualifications present only in the source.

5. *Classification is correct.* The draft contains no quoted speech, so this is a source-support finding — a meaning changed in crossing languages outside quoted speech — not a translation finding. §4's vacuous translation check is right: Sanna Ek's supplied quotation is referenced rather than quoted, which the material permits ("Detta får citeras eller refereras").
