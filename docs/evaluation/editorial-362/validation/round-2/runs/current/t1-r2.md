## 2. Finding dispositions

### Finding 1 — "digital proficiency" for "digital vana" — **accepted**

Evidence.

- Draft, exact: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
- Source, exact: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."
- The draft's list maps the source's list item for item. "age" takes "ålder" and "functional ability" takes "funktionsförmåga", so the third slot can only rest on "digital vana"; no other supplied phrase is available to support it. The whole supplied material contains no statement that the report omits a measure of skill or capability.
- "Digital vana" is habituation — how accustomed and practised a person is in digital services. "Digital proficiency" is skill or capability. Something falls under one and not the other in this context: a resident may be capable of completing the booking form yet rarely use digital services, or use them constantly without being able to complete this particular form.
- The report's concrete case stands and is compatible with the supplied material: the pilot report contains a check of whether participants could complete the online booking unaided, and records nothing about how routinely they use digital services. Then "Den mäter inte … digital vana" is true and "The report does not measure … digital proficiency" is false. I tried to set this case aside by quoting a supplied statement and could not. The nearest candidate, "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt", limits what the phone-booking *count* shows about the *share of residents*; it does not state what the report does or does not contain. "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer" speaks to the counting unit only.
- This is not a merely conceivable stronger reading of a defensible sentence. In its full context the sentence is an inventory of what the report omits, and the draft's inventory differs from the supplied one in one entry: it asserts an omission the material does not state, and drops the omission the material does state — the habit dimension that the proposed trial is built to investigate ("be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb").
- The report's classification is correct: meaning changed in crossing languages outside quoted speech, so a source-support finding, not a translation finding. The draft contains no quoted speech, so §4's nil return is right.
- The proposed repair is the smallest supported one: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital habits."
