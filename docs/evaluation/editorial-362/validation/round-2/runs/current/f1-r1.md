## 2. Dispositions

### S1 — rejected

Allegation: "när jag saknar rutan för beslut" attributes to the author a wish for a decision box that the material does not give her.

What the draft asserts in full context. ¶1 closes "Tiden har fått två rutor. Beslutet har ingen." ¶4 opens "Så redan när jag saknar rutan för beslut behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv. Jag vill inte att varje samtal ska pressas fram till ett avgörande." The sentence does not present a wish for a decision box as the author's position; it names a pull she registers and, in the same clause, marks as needing an objection from herself — which the next sentence supplies. Nowhere does the draft propose adding a decision box: the only addition it proposes is the question box (¶7, "Bredvid tiden skulle jag vilja ställa frågan: vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?").

Source support for that proposition. N1: "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för **det beslut vi ska kunna fatta** när vi går därifrån." The note's own words give the decision a normative standing — a decision we are to be able to reach — so registering the absence of its box is the note's own movement, not an addition. N3 then performs precisely the self-objection the draft performs: "Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut." That disclaimer is only intelligible because the ¶1 observation carries a pull toward the decision; the notes' sequence (observe the missing decision box → disclaim wanting every conversation pressed to a decision) is the sequence the draft reproduces.

The report's counterexample does not stand. It offers that "she notes the two-to-zero asymmetry only to show that the template records duration where it should record purpose, and wants the *question* box instead". That case is compatible with the draft as written rather than excluded by it: the draft does want the question box, and says so in ¶7. The case therefore does not name a situation in which the draft fails and the material holds.

The qualifier the report leans on is excluded by its own text. N1's "Det är en iakttagelse av dokumentet, inte en scen från ett visst möte" contrasts a document observation with a meeting scene. BJ confirms that this is the axis at issue: "Inga verkliga möten, resor, barndomsminnen, repliker eller kroppsliga känslor är lämnade. Uppfinn dem inte. … En konkret dokumentiakttagelse är en fullgod öppning." Nothing in that qualifier denies that she feels the absence, and the draft narrates no meeting.

This is the author's own reflection resting on supported material, read as an ordinary reader would read it in context. The proposed repair ("när jag noterar att beslutet inte har någon ruta") is a wording preference of the same size, not a missing support relation.

### S2 — rejected

Allegation: "ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" denies a broader object than N2's "inte något jag har mätt hos andra", and "arbetsdagar" appears nowhere.

The passage is a denial and adds no positive claim; the report concedes this ("the denial adds no positive claim, so the state of knowledge is preserved"). The wider denial is itself supplied: BJ states "Ingen statistik eller allmän trend är belagd", so no measurement of others' time use exists behind this column at all, and N2 states "Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra." Together these support the draft's denial at the scope it uses.

The object is not foreign to the context either. The sentence the disclaimer governs is "Det retar mig hur lätt tid i kalendern kan behandlas som ett resultat i sig. Som om en starttid och en sluttid tillsammans vore ett svar på varför vi behöver träffas." Calendar time treated as a result is how working time is spent; "hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" is a contextual gloss of that same object, not a new subject. The report's stated ground is that the wording "frames the column as being about other people's workday habits" — a framing preference, not a concrete mismatch of subject, scope, time, modality or certainty. The proposed repair restores the source's wording without changing what is asserted.

### S3 — rejected

Allegation: "bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden" imports a frame the notes do not contain.

The report's own entry establishes the rejection: "It appears only inside a denial, and it works in BJ's favour by disowning hostility, so it is not an unsupported positive claim… Defensible as written; repair optional." The draft asserts that she has a template before her and no evidence about colleagues — "Jag sitter med en mall, inte med bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden." N1 supports the first half ("Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall"), N2 the second ("inte något jag har mätt hos andra"), and BJ's "Författarens hållning är nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig" is served, not strained, by a clause that disowns an adversarial reading. No proposition about colleagues having shortcomings is asserted anywhere in the draft. An allegation the report itself declines to press is not established.

### S4 — rejected

Allegation: the definite "mallen" narrows N3's generic "en mall".

N3 reads "Men den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid." The general permission entails the particular instance: if whoever fills in any template should be allowed to state why the participants' time is needed, then whoever fills in the library's template should be. The draft's clause is therefore supported with its subject, scope and qualifications intact, and the definite article also reads generically in this Swedish construction ("den som fyller i mallen"). The report classifies the change itself: "A narrowing, not an inflation, so no over-claim." A claim weaker than and entailed by the source is not a defect; a qualification present only in the source does not settle a finding.
