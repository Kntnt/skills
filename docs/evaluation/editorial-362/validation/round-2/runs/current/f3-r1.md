# Validation of comparison report — p1-opinion-variable (baseline/p1-r2)

## 1. Detected accounting mismatches

**A1 — the third section heading is never paired.** §5 states that "three section headings" were reconstructed and tested. Headings 1 and 2 have entries (P08, P21); the heading "Run a six-month trial, then decide" has none. §3 of the accounting opens directly at P28 ("the trial's design"). The heading asserts an ask (run six months, then decide) that B and U3 do support, but the report has not shown the pairing it claims to have made. Coverage is otherwise complete: every other draft sentence, the title, the byline and both remaining headings appear in P01–P33.

**A2 — misdirected cross-reference at P06.** P06 says "the closing (P30) restores all three" of U3's decision branches. P30 is Öppna beslut's funding/costing disclaimer; the three-way decision is P33 ("removed, changed, or retained"). The substantive point is right, the citation is wrong.

**A3 — translation/source-support split is misapplied.** §4 itself records that "The draft contains no direct quotation." The task filed under §4 therefore covers no quoted speech, and a meaning changed in crossing languages outside quoted speech is a source-support finding. T1, T2, T4 and T6 are all of that kind and belong in §3. This misfiling does not change any disposition below, but it makes §5's "Verdict on source support: no material defect" unsafe: T1 is a source-support defect.

**Not a mismatch:** the gender note at pronoun row 3 (Swedish "Hon" → English first person) is correctly classed as information the source pronoun carries and the draft neither asserts nor denies. The pairing is sound and no repair is owed.

## 2. Dispositions

**S1 — "with a pilot covering two" implies the staff recommendation rests on the pilot — rejected.**
Draft, in full context: "The board is being asked to remove a booking option across seven venues with a pilot covering two and no measured account of the labor involved." The paragraph's subject, fixed by the preceding sentence ("Yet the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings"), is the state of what is before the board, not the provenance of the tjänsteutlåtande. The proposition the draft asserts is that the removal covers seven venues while the only trial on record covered two — each component exact against U2 ("telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler") and U1 ("ett åtta veckor långt försök i två lokaler"). The report concedes the point itself: "the second reading is the natural one in context, and the sentence's other two components are exact." A stronger reading that the sentence merely admits in isolation does not establish a defect. No repair.

**S2 — scope "in the documents" dropped at second appearance — rejected.**
Same sentence. The scope is set one sentence earlier by "the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor…" (U2: "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna"), and the clause itself is governed by "The board is being asked" — what the board has before it. Read in context, the draft asserts an absence from the papers, not from the world. The report states this and then files it anyway: "the scope is present in the paragraph; only the sentence in isolation loses it… Optional; the paragraph as written is defensible." Defensible as written is not a defect. No repair.

**T1 — "digital proficiency" for *digital vana* — accepted (as a source-support defect).**
Draft: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency." Source, U1: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana." *Vana* is habit or familiarity — what a person is used to doing; *proficiency* is skill. Concrete case compatible with the material: a pilot report that put users through a short skills check, recording their digital proficiency, while recording nothing about how accustomed they are to booking digitally. Under that case U1's statement holds — the report measures no *digital vana* — and the draft's sentence fails. Nothing supplied excludes the case; U1 names *funktionsförmåga* separately, which is functional ability, not digital skill, and its only other exclusion concerns what the phone-booking count can show. The mismatch runs both ways in effect: the absence the material does state (habit) is asserted nowhere in the draft, while the draft's list names ability twice. The report's supporting observation is correct — the article's own question two sentences on is why the phone is used, a question about habit. Smallest supported repair, as proposed: "…age, functional ability, or how familiar people are with digital tools."

**T2 — loss of *fortfarande* ("still") — rejected.**
Draft: "But a phone booking is a reason to investigate why someone chose that option." U4: "Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används", explicitly citable or referable. The draft asserts that a phone booking is a reason to find out why that option was taken; U4 asserts that it is a reason to find out why the phone is used. The draft states nothing the source does not carry, and *fortfarande* is a qualification present only in the source, which settles neither kind of finding. As a target-language reading the sentence is idiomatic and unobstructed; the report's stated ground is the loss of a "rhetorical point" and an "argument's pivot", which is preferred emphasis, not a concrete obstruction. No repair.

**T3 — the referenced position's first half is not expressed — rejected.**
The report's own words: the first half is "not expressed", i.e. absent. Absence of a source point is not a claim without a source passage, and the sentence the report says replaced it — "The phone bookings therefore cannot tell us what share of Lervik's residents cannot book online" — is itself exactly supported by U1 ("Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt"), scope and modality intact, as the report grants at P14 ("supported. Scope … and modality … both preserved"). The draft contains no direct quotation, so no translated quotation's fidelity is at issue. A defect must be something the draft asserts without support or contrary to the material; the report identifies neither. Coverage of the material is the writer's editorial choice, expressly preserved. No repair.

**T4 — "Staff" for *förvaltningen* — accepted (as a source-support defect).**
Draft: "Staff would record the time spent on each booking method and invite users to explain, voluntarily, why they chose phone or web." Source, U3: "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg och be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb." U3 assigns the duty to *förvaltningen*, the administration as a body. Concrete case compatible with the material: the administration discharges the duty centrally, drawing timings from the booking system's own records, with no front-line staff recording anything. U3's statement holds; the draft's fails. Nothing supplied excludes it — the material never places the recording on *personalen*, and the draft has already used "staff" for exactly those people one paragraph earlier ("staff would no longer have to enter information through two workflows", U2's "personalen"). The same word thus carries both the objection and its answer, which is a concrete obstruction, not a synonym preference. Smallest supported repair, as proposed: "The administration would record the time spent on each booking method…"

**T5 — antecedent of "Its" — rejected.**
Draft: "Öppna beslut has not claimed to have funded or costed this trial. Its cost remains a question for the board to consider." The preceding sentence ends on "this trial", and its predicate is "funded or costed **this trial**" — the cost in play has just been named as the trial's. Binding "Its cost" to the association would attribute a cost to Öppna beslut, which no supplied statement raises; U3 has "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" directly after the trial's description. The report concedes that "The supplied context settles the intended referent." A reading the English momentarily admits, contradicted by the sentence's own object and by the material, does not establish a defect. No repair.

**T6 — "community venues" for *föreningslokaler* — rejected.**
The report files it as an "observation" and its own repair as "Optional". *Föreningslokaler* are the municipality's premises for associations; the material itself treats residents as the people who book them — U1 measures what share "av invånarna" cannot book digitally, and B addresses "invånarna i … Lervik". No concrete case is named, or available, in which "community venues" holds and *föreningslokaler* fails in this context: the seven venues are the same seven throughout, and the report title is left in Swedish. This is a preferred synonym. No repair.
