## 3. Finding dispositions

### S1 — rejected

Allegation: ¶4's "när jag saknar rutan för beslut" attributes to the author a wish the material does not give her.

Evidence against the allegation:

- Draft, ¶1: "Men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån. Tiden har fått två rutor. Beslutet har ingen." Source, N1, verbatim: "men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån." The note's own words are normative about the decision — "det beslut vi ska kunna **fatta**" — so the material itself, in her voice, presents the absent decision box as an absence worth registering. The draft's "saknar" registers exactly that absence and adds nothing to it.
- Draft, same sentence: "behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv", followed by "Jag vill inte att varje samtal ska pressas fram till ett avgörande." Source, N3: "Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut." N3's disclaimer is itself a correction of a reading the note anticipates — that noticing the missing decision box means wanting conversations pressed to decisions. The draft reproduces that movement: impulse registered, then immediately qualified. The proposition the draft asserts in full context is therefore *she registers the missing decision box and at once checks herself against it*, which N1 and N3 support jointly.
- The report names no supplied statement that excludes this reading, and its own counterexample (M2) is the draft's continuation, not an alternative to it. Under the standard that a merely conceivable stronger reading does not establish a defect, "saknar" read as a standing demand for a decision box is such a reading, and it is contradicted by the main clause of the very sentence it is drawn from.

The proposed repair ("när jag noterar att beslutet inte har någon ruta") is a preferred wording, not a restoration of missing support.

### S2 — rejected

Allegation: "ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" misstates what N2 says was not measured.

Evidence against the allegation:

- Draft: "Det retar mig hur lätt tid i kalendern kan behandlas som ett resultat i sig. … Det är min reflektion, ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar." Source, N2: "Samtidigt retar jag mig på att tid i kalendern lätt behandlas som ett resultat i sig. … Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra." The draft's disclaimer sits in the same position, after the same irritation, and names the same object under a description the source's own subject matter supplies: time in the calendar is time in a working day.
- The passage asserts only that she has not measured something. A denial of a broader measurement entails the source's narrower denial and adds no positive claim about anyone. No source-compatible case was named — nor can one be drawn from these notes — in which she *has* measured how others use their workdays: N2 disavows measurement of others without restriction, and no material records any measurement by her.
- The report's remaining ground, that the wording frames the column's subject, is a framing preference, not a changed or missing support relation (M4).

### S3 — rejected

Allegation: "bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden" imports a frame the notes do not contain.

Evidence against the allegation:

- Draft: "Jag sitter med en mall, inte med bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden." The clause occurs inside a denial of possession. Denying that one holds evidence of a thing asserts neither that the thing exists nor that anyone alleges it; the sentence's asserted content is that what she has before her is the template, which N1 supports verbatim ("Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall").
- Source, N2: "Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra." BJ: "Författarens hållning är nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig." The disowning of an adversarial reading serves both and contradicts neither.
- The report itself states the clause "is not an unsupported positive claim" and is "defensible as written", which is the report's own acknowledgement that no defect was established (M3).
