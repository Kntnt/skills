# Validation of report `n3-r1.md` against fixture `n3-column-withheld.md`

## 1. Detected accounting mismatches

Coverage itself is complete: the draft's title, byline and all eight body paragraphs are accounted for, and every draft sentence maps to an entry (¶1 → C3–C7 + C8; ¶2 → C9–C12; ¶3 → C13–C15; ¶4 → C16–C19; ¶5 → C20–C22; ¶6 → C23–C25; ¶7 → C26; ¶8 → C27–C29). The mismatches are in individual pairings, not in extent.

**M1 — C16 states a gloss in place of the draft passage, and drops the clause that fixes its meaning.** The draft sentence is "Så redan när jag saknar rutan för beslut behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv." C16's proposition is "*Nora Vik misses (wants) the template's absent box for the decision, and this wish calls for an objection from her against herself*". The parenthetical "(wants)" is not a restatement of "saknar" but a strengthening of it, and it is then carried unexamined into S1, where the proposition tested has become plainly "*Nora Vik misses, i.e. wants, the template's absent box*". The sentence's subordinating structure ("redan när … behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv") marks the registered lack as a pull the author immediately argues against — a qualification that applies across the clause and that the pairing does not carry over.

**M2 — the C16/S1 pairing omits the qualifying words of its own source passage.** N1 reads "men ingen ruta för det beslut **vi ska kunna fatta** när vi går därifrån." The report quotes the clause in S1 but weighs only "ingen ruta", never "vi ska kunna fatta", which is the note's own statement of an expectation the template fails to meet. The pairing therefore sets the draft passage beside a truncated reading of the source it rests on.

**M3 — internal inconsistency between C6/C7 and C16.** C6 ("Tiden har fått två rutor") and C7 ("Beslutet har ingen") are cleared as neutral arithmetic that gives "the count, not a judgement". C16 treats the same registered asymmetry, restated in ¶4, as an unsourced wish. The same draft content is paired against the same source clause with two different readings, and the report does not reconcile them.

**M4 — C11's pairing establishes the opposite of what it concludes.** C11 and S2 record that the draft's denial names a broader unmeasured object than N2's, while stating in both places that "the denial adds no positive claim, so the state of knowledge is preserved". A denial whose object is broader than the source's is entailed by the source's disclaimer, not unsupported by it; the entry records a scope difference and then reports it as a support gap without testing the entailment direction.

**M5 — S3 and S4 are entered as findings by a standard the report itself disclaims.** S3 concludes "it is not an unsupported positive claim … the sentence is defensible as written"; S4 concludes "a narrowing, not an inflation, so no over-claim". Both are then carried forward in §6 as unresolved findings. Under the comparison task a finding is a defect that must be repaired before delivery, and what is defensible as written is not to be reported.

**M6 — §2 lists draft forms that do not occur in the draft.** The pronoun table's first plural row gives "vi, oss (implied), vårt use of 'varandras'". Neither "oss" nor "vårt" appears anywhere in the draft; only "vi" and the reciprocal genitive "varandras" do. The person/number/gender accounting for that row is nonetheless correct for the forms that are present.

**M7 — word count off by one.** §1's header and §3's "Length" both give 331 words including title and byline. Counted: body 319 (correct as stated), title "En ruta för det vi behöver förstå" 7, byline "Av Nora Vik, bibliotekarie" 4 — 330. Immaterial to any finding; recorded for completeness.

**M8 — C28 double-classified in §3.** It is listed both under "First-person stance, preference and feeling" and under "Advocacy". No consequence for its disposition.

## 2. Dispositions

### S1 — "när jag saknar rutan för beslut" — **rejected**

The report has not established its allegation. Two independent grounds.

*Concrete mismatch between the report's reading and what the draft says in context.* The draft nowhere asks for a decision box. The only addition it ever proposes is the question: "Bredvid tiden skulle jag vilja ställa frågan: vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?" (¶7). The ¶4 sentence is built as a self-objection — "Så redan när jag saknar rutan för beslut **behöver jag göra en invändning mot mig själv**" — and the objection that follows is "Jag vill inte att varje samtal ska pressas fram till ett avgörande." A sentence that registers a lack and in the same breath argues against the pull of that lack does not assert a wish for the box; the report's "misses, i.e. wants" is a stronger reading the passage does not carry, and a merely conceivable stronger reading does not establish a defect.

*The supplied statement carries the draft's claim with subject, scope and qualifications intact.* N1: "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för det beslut **vi ska kunna fatta** när vi går därifrån." The note does not record a neutral absence; it records the absence of a box for a decision the author says we *should be able* to take. That is exactly the felt lack the draft's "saknar" expresses, in the author's own voice and at the same strength. The movement the draft's ¶4 performs is the notes' own: N1 registers the missing decision box, and N3 opens by objecting to it — "Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut."

The report's counter-case (she notes the asymmetry only to show that the template records duration instead of purpose, and wants N4's question box instead) is compatible with the material, but it is also compatible with the draft as written: on that reading she still finds the decision box wanting, and the draft still proposes only the question box. A counter-case that leaves the draft's proposition true does not defeat it. No accepted defect; no repair.

### S2 — "ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar" — **rejected**

The draft passage is a denial: "Det är min reflektion, ingen mätning av hur andra använder sina arbetsdagar." N2 carries both halves with scope intact: "Det här är min reflektion, **inte något jag har mätt hos andra**." The source denies having measured *anything* in others, which entails not having measured how they use their workdays. The draft's object is narrower than the source's denial, not broader than its support, so the draft cannot be true where the source is false: there is no case compatible with the material in which N2 holds and the draft's sentence fails.

The report's stated residual worry is that the wording "frames the column as being about other people's workday habits". That is a topic-framing preference, not a support relation, and the frame is in the material in any case: the passage it disclaims is about "tid i kalendern" treated as a result, and BR addresses "yrkesverksamma som planerar möten". The report's own text ("the denial adds no positive claim, so the state of knowledge is preserved") concedes that no claim has been added. Confidence and preferred wording settle nothing. No accepted defect; no repair.

### S3 — "bevis för kollegernas tillkortakommanden" — **rejected**

The proposition is again a denial: she has the template before her and no evidence of colleagues' shortcomings. "Inte med bevis för X" does not assert or presuppose that X obtains; it disclaims evidence. N2's "inte något jag har mätt hos andra" and N1's "Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall" carry both halves, and BJ's "Författarens hållning är nyfiken och självironisk, inte mötesfientlig" is served rather than strained by the disavowal. The report names no case, compatible with the material, in which the draft's sentence fails, and states outright that the sentence is defensible as written with repair optional. What is defensible as written is not a finding. No accepted defect; no repair.

### S4 — "mallen" for N3's "en mall" — **rejected**

N3: "Men den som fyller i **en** mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid." A wish held generally over any template entails the wish held of the particular template under discussion, so N3 carries the draft's claim with subject, scope and qualifications intact; the draft's "just varandras tid" reproduces the source's words exactly. The report classifies the change itself as "a narrowing, not an inflation, so no over-claim" and records it "for completeness". A qualification present only in the source — here the generality of "en mall" — settles no finding. No accepted defect; no repair.
