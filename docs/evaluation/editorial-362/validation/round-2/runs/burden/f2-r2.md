Report: `gpt-column-report-first.md`. Draft and material: `n1-column-reflection.md`.

## Coverage check

The report's entries 1–32 plus pronoun groups A–H cover the title, the byline and every sentence of the eight draft paragraphs; no passage is left unaccounted for. The draft contains no quotations, so the absence of a translation section is correct.

## Detected accounting mismatches

1. **Entry 7** states the draft's proposition as a comparison of "mallens hela angivelse av vad tiden ska användas till". The draft sentence is "Vad vi ska kunna göra med den har inte fått samma tydliga plats", standing immediately after "Men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån". In context "vad vi ska kunna göra med den" is that decision, not every possible use of meeting time. The pairing substitutes a broader proposition for the contextual one.

2. **Entry 8** renders "Det är frestande att börja där" as "Nora känner en frestelse". The draft's construction is impersonal — it marks a move in an argument, not a reported feeling. The pairing personalises what the draft does not assert.

3. **Entries 9 and 22** pair hypothetical and inferential sentences ("Då skulle …", "Då kan vi knappast begära …") with source passages as though they were reports of fact, and then demand source passages for steps in the author's own reasoning. Entry 22's counter-case also contradicts the draft's own antecedent (see F5).

4. **Entry 19** pairs a reflective transition ("Ändå får den mig att undra …") with S2/S3 as though the draft asserted a documented causal origin for the question.

5. Group **D** carries entry 9's mismatch into the pronoun accounting: it tests "den som planerar mötet" against S3's "den som fyller i en mall" although the draft's sentence is about who would fill in a box that the draft itself proposes hypothetically.

No mismatch affects coverage; the entries that contain no finding pair draft and source correctly.

## Dispositions

### F1 — "generalisering från beslutsruta till all tidsanvändning" — rejected

Evidence, mismatch: the report's reading is broader than the draft's sentence in context. The paragraph names the boxes, notes the missing one, then contrasts "Tiden har fått två rutor" with "Vad vi ska kunna göra med den har inte fått samma tydliga plats". The referent of the contrast is the decision named in the preceding sentence.

Evidence, source: S1 — "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån." This carries the draft's proposition with subject, scope and qualification intact and with room to spare: the source says the decision has *no* box at all, which entails the draft's weaker "inte … samma tydliga plats" as against two time boxes. The report's counter-case (an agenda box carrying a very clear instruction on what the time is for) does not touch the decision, which S1 places outside the template altogether; and "dagordning" is the list of items to be taken up, which is the very thing the note distinguishes from what the meeting should leave people able to do.

### F2 — "tillskrivet tankeförlopp" — rejected

Evidence, mismatch: the draft does not report a felt temptation or a micro-scene. "Det är frestande att börja där: lägg till en ruta för beslutet" is impersonal and hypothetical, and "Men redan när jag tänker det behöver jag hejda mig" is the author pulling back from a position she has just entertained. That is the reasoning and transition itself, which the comparison is not to test, and it rests on supported material.

Evidence, source: S3 — "Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut." The note itself raises the decision-forcing idea in order to disown it, which is exactly the movement the draft performs. S4 — "Jag vill behålla både hoppet om den frågan och tvivlet på ännu en ruta" — carries the same coexistence of pull and check, and "Det finns en komik i att försöka lösa ett problem med formulär genom att utöka formuläret" presupposes that adding a box is the tempting first move. The report's counter-case ("Nora har hela tiden föredragit förståelsefrågan och aldrig velat införa en beslutsruta") is excluded by S3's own framing and by S4's stated doubt about "ännu en ruta", which is doubt about a box she is drawn to.

### F3 — "krav och rollbyte" — rejected

Evidence, mismatch: "Då skulle den som planerar mötet behöva formulera vad det ska leda till" is the consequent of a conditional whose antecedent is the draft's own hypothetical box. The draft asserts neither that such a box exists nor that any actual template imposes a requirement; "skulle" keeps the whole sentence inside the supposition. The report reads it as a factual claim about template mechanics.

Evidence, source: the counter-case "beslutsrutan är frivillig och kan lämnas tom" is a stipulation about a box no supplied statement describes, so it cannot show that one statement holds while the other fails — it merely redescribes the author's own hypothetical. On the role, S3 — "den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid" — is about whoever fills in the template for a meeting; the draft's "den som planerar mötet" occupies that place in the draft's hypothetical, and nothing in the material says the planner is someone other than the filler. The report's assistant case is an added stipulation, not a quoted exclusion, and it does not make the draft's conditional fail.

### F4 — "ny personlig orsak" — rejected

Evidence, mismatch: "Ändå får den mig att undra vad vi ber om när vi ber om varandras tid" is a transition within the author's own reflection and is presented as such; the preceding sentence has just labelled the passage "min reflektion". The report reads it as a documented account of how the question arose.

Evidence, source: S2 — "Samtidigt retar jag mig på att tid i kalendern lätt behandlas som ett resultat i sig. Det här är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra" — supplies the reflection with its knowledge qualification intact, and S3 — "den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid" — supplies the question as hers and as live. "Samtidigt" in S2 already sets the irritation beside the surrounding thought. What the draft adds is the author moving from one of her own supported thoughts to another, which is the reasoning the comparison is told not to test. No concrete case is named in which the reflection holds and her wondering fails.

### F5 — "nytt ställningstagande om kallelsen" — rejected

Evidence, mismatch: the sentence begins "Då", drawing an inference from the immediately preceding premise that a conversation can help "upptäcka en fråga vi inte visste att vi behövde ställa". "Den färdiga frågan" is that discovered question. The report's counter-cases both contradict the draft's own antecedent: a "preliminärt färdig undersökningsfråga" is a different question from the one the draft names, and an organiser who already knows the question is excluded by the draft's "vi inte visste", whose inclusive "vi" the report itself accepts in group B.

Evidence, source: S3 — "Samtal kan skapa förtroende och upptäcka en fråga vi inte visste att vi behövde ställa. Men den som fyller i en mall borde få formulera varför vi behöver just varandras tid, också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans." The note sets the same contrast: what may be asked of the form in advance is the *why*, expressly also when the purpose is joint inquiry — that is, not the finished question. The draft's "knappast begära" is the author's own hedged advocacy resting on that material, and "kallelsen" is the filled-in template in context, not a new subject.
