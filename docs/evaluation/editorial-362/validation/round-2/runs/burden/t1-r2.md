## 2. Finding dispositions

### Finding 1 — "digital proficiency" for "digital vana" — **accepted**

Draft: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
Source: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."

What the draft actually asserts, in context: a negative claim about the pilot report's contents — that it contains no measurement of a named third dimension. A negative claim about a document's contents is supported only by a supplied statement that names that same dimension.

- **No supplied statement carries the draft's claim with its scope intact.** The only candidate is "Den mäter inte … digital vana". *Vana* is habituation — how accustomed and practised a person is in digital services. Proficiency is capability. In this material the two come apart: a resident may be fully capable of booking online and still be unaccustomed to doing so, and that person falls under *vana* but not under proficiency. The rule that a carried term names the same thing only where nothing in the context could fall under one and not the other is therefore not satisfied.
- **The report's counter-case stands and I cannot set it aside.** Suppose the pilot report included a short check of whether bookers could complete the online form unaided, while recording nothing about how routinely they use digital services. Then the source's "Den mäter inte … digital vana" is true and the draft's "does not measure … digital proficiency" is false. To reject the finding I would have to quote a supplied statement that excludes that case. None does. "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer" describes what is counted, and does not state that the measurement list is exhaustive. "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt" limits what the *phone-booking count* can establish about the share of *residents*; it remains true even if the report contained a per-booker ability check, since two venues' bookers are not the residents. What the material makes likely — that the report measured little about persons — is not support.
- **No mismatch between the report's reading and the draft in context.** The draft's preceding "functional ability" and following "cannot tell us what share … cannot book online" both already occupy the ability register, so reading the third item as an ability term is the ordinary reading, not a strained stronger one. On that reading the supplied non-measurement of habit is dropped and a non-measurement the material never states is asserted in its place — and habit is precisely the dimension the argument goes on to rely on ("varför telefonen fortfarande används").
- **Repair check.** The proposed smallest repair — "digital habits" — restores exactly the supplied dimension and touches nothing else; "familiarity with digital services" is equally supported. Voice, sharpness and the surrounding sentences are untouched.

Classification also verified: this is a source-support finding, not a translation finding. The draft contains no quoted speech — Ek's supplied quotation is referenced, which "Detta får citeras eller refereras" permits — so §4's nil return is correct and the finding's placement in §2 is right.

FINDING Finding 1: accepted
