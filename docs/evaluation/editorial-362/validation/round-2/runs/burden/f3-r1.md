## Dispositions

**S1 — "with a pilot covering two" implies the staff recommendation rests on the pilot — rejected.**
Evidence: concrete mismatch between the report's reading and the draft in context. The sentence is "The board is being asked to remove a booking option across seven venues with a pilot covering two and no measured account of the labor involved." The paragraph's subject, fixed by the preceding sentence ("Yet the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings"), is the state of the evidence before the board; "with" reads as "while having only". The report itself concedes "the second reading is the natural one in context". A merely conceivable stronger reading does not establish a defect. The supported component is carried by U1: "gäller ett åtta veckor långt försök i två lokaler."

**S2 — scope "in the documents" dropped at second appearance — rejected.**
Evidence: the draft passage must be read in its full context, and the report states the scope "is present in the paragraph; only the sentence in isolation loses it." The clause is the third conjunct of a sentence whose subject is what the board is being asked to act on, immediately after "the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor…". U2 carries the proposition with scope intact: "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna." The draft asserts no absence beyond the papers before the board.

**T1 — "digital proficiency" for *digital vana* — accepted.**
Evidence: no supplied statement carries the draft's claim with its object intact. U1 states "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana" — *vana* is habit or familiarity, what a person routinely does, not skill level. A concrete case compatible with the material: the pilot report records a self-rated digital-skill question but no measure of how routinely respondents use digital services. Nothing supplied excludes it; U1's list names habit, not skill, and "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt" is about what cannot be inferred, not about what was measured. In that case the source's sentence holds and the draft's fails, and the draft additionally names ability twice ("functional ability", "proficiency") while never naming the blind spot the material states. What the material makes likely is not support. Smallest supported repair: "…age, functional ability, or digital familiarity."

**T2 — loss of *fortfarande* ("still") — rejected.**
Evidence: a qualification present only in the source settles no finding, and the draft's proposition is carried intact by U4: "Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen ... används." The draft asserts that a phone booking is a reason to investigate why that option was chosen; U3 supplies the same individual-level reason-seeking — "be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb." "Fortfarande" adds rhetorical emphasis on persistence; its absence leaves nothing the draft asserts unsupported and introduces no reading the material excludes.

**T3 — the quoted position's first half is not expressed — rejected.**
Evidence: an omission is not an unsupported claim, and the report shows no claim the draft makes that the material fails to carry. The report's reading — that the draft "replaces" U4's individual-level half with U1's aggregate claim — mismatches the draft in context: the two sentences are separate, each with its own support (U1's "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas…" for the aggregate limit; U4's second sentence for the reason), and the draft nowhere asserts that a phone booking proves inability; it twice states the opposite limit and adds "Using them to make that claim would overstate the evidence." The material permits reference rather than quotation ("Detta får citeras eller refereras"), so no quotation fidelity obligation is engaged. Lost parallelism is a preferred wording, not a concrete obstruction to the target-language reading.

**T4 — "Staff" for *förvaltningen* — rejected.**
Evidence: U3 carries the claim with actor and duty intact — "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg och be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb." The draft asserts that the municipality's people would record time per booking route; the administration acts through its staff, and no case is named in which the draft's sentence holds and U3's fails or the reverse. The report's stated obstruction is an effect on the argument's force ("quietly weakens the answer"), which is a preferred wording, not an obstruction to reading.

**T5 — antecedent of "Its" — rejected.**
Evidence: the report concedes "the supplied context settles the intended referent". In the draft's own context the preceding sentence ends "…funded or costed this trial", and an association's own cost is not what U3 assigns to the board: "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till." A momentary alternative binding is a conceivable reading, not a concrete obstruction; the draft's assertion in context is unambiguous.

**T6 — "community venues" for *föreningslokaler* — rejected.**
Evidence: in this context the phrase denotes the same set — the municipality's bookable venues, "alla sju lokaler" in U3 and U2 — and the draft asserts nothing about who may book them. The report files it as an observation with an optional repair and names no case in which the draft's statement holds and the source's fails. Breadth of a term with no claim attached is a preferred wording.
