## 2. Finding dispositions

### Finding 1 — "digital proficiency" for "digital vana" — **accepted**

Draft: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency."
Source: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."

Evidence for accepting:

- **No supplied statement carries the draft's claim with its scope intact.** The only candidate is the paired sentence itself, and its third item is *digital vana* — habituation to and routine use of digital services — not skill or capability. The draft's third item, read as an ordinary reader would in this context (a list of what the pilot report did not record about bookers), asserts that the report recorded no measure of *how able* bookers are with digital services. Nothing supplied asserts that. "Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt" limits what the phone-booking *count* can show about the *share of residents*; it does not state that the report contains no ability measure, so it cannot be quoted to carry the draft's claim.
- **The two terms come apart in this context.** A booker can be proficient without the habit (skilled with digital forms but never a user of the municipality's services) and habituated without proficiency (books the same venue weekly by rote, unable to complete an unfamiliar form). So the carried term does not name the same thing here.
- **The report's counter-case is compatible with the material and is not excluded.** A pilot that checked whether participants could complete the online form unaided, while recording nothing about how routinely they use digital services, makes "Den mäter inte … digital vana" true and the draft's sentence false. *Funktionsförmåga* is functional capacity in the disability sense, not form-completion skill, so the supplied list does not exclude the case. The material need not make the case likely; it need only be compatible, and it is.
- **The mismatch is not merely a preferred synonym.** The draft both asserts an unsupported non-measurement and drops the supplied one. The dropped dimension is load-bearing for the argument: the draft's own case rests on finding out "why someone chose that option" and the proposed trial asks users "varför de väljer telefon eller webb" — that is the habit question the source's list withholds a claim about.
- The report's proposed repair ("digital habits", or "familiarity with digital services") is the smallest change that restores the supplied scope, and it touches nothing else.

Disposition recorded as the writer's evidenced decision: the finding stands as a defect to repair before delivery.
