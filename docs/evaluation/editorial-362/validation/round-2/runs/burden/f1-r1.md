## 2. Dispositions

### S1 — "när jag saknar rutan för beslut" — **rejected**

Evidence of a concrete mismatch between the report's reading and what the draft says in context:

- The draft never asserts a wish for a decision box. What ¶1 establishes is the document observation, in the note's own terms: N1 — "Där finns rutor för starttid, sluttid, deltagare och dagordning, men ingen ruta för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån." The draft's ¶1 reproduces exactly that ("Tiden har fått två rutor. Beslutet har ingen."), and ¶4's "redan när jag saknar rutan för beslut" refers back to that registered absence.
- The source itself frames the absence as a lack, not as neutral inventory: N1's "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån" says the decision is something the participants *ought to be able* to reach, and the contrastive "men ingen ruta" marks its omission. Noticing that omission and feeling it is exactly what N1 supplies.
- N3 supplies the self-objection that follows and presupposes the same pull: "Min tanke är inte att varje samtal ska pressas till ett beslut." That disclaimer exists in the material only because the author's own thinking leans toward decisions; the draft's ¶4 reproduces the disclaimer in her voice, which BJ endorses ("Författarens hållning är nyfiken och självironisk").
- The report's stated harm — "the column's middle becomes a retreat from an invented demand" and "puts her a step closer to the meeting-hostile stance BJ rules out" — depends on the draft proposing a decision box somewhere. It proposes none; the only proposal in the draft is N4's verbatim question box (C26). The stronger reading is conceivable but the context settles against it, so it does not establish a defect.
- The proposed repair ("när jag noterar att beslutet inte har någon ruta") states what the passage already says in context, so nothing supported is added by it.
