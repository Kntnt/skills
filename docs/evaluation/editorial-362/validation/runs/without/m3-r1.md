## Findings — dispositions

The report reports **no findings**, so there is no finding to accept or reject. The five "Considered and not reported" entries (A–E) are explicitly not findings; each was re-tested below against the draft's contextual meaning, and none establishes a defect.

- **A, "digital proficiency" for "digital vana" (#11).** Draft: "The report does not measure age, functional ability, or digital proficiency." Source: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana." and, in the same paragraph, "Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer." The draft's proposition is a denial, and the material establishes that the report captures no user characteristic whatever, so no case is available in which the source's denial holds while the draft's fails. No defect.
- **B, "Staff would record" for "ska förvaltningen registrera" (#26).** Draft: "Staff would record the time spent on each booking method". Source: "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg". In context the recording is an element of the proposed trial design, stated in the conditional; naming the municipality's employees as the ones who record does not add a task or a burden beyond "förvaltningen registrera". No defect.
- **C, "community venues" / "online" (#3, #12).** Draft: "online-only booking for community venues"; "cannot tell us what share of Lervik's residents cannot book online." Source: "enbart digital bokning av föreningslokaler"; "hur stor andel av invånarna som inte kan boka digitalt." The referents are fixed by the material — the seven municipal venues, and the web as the sole digital channel ("Under försöket gjordes 96 bokningar via webb och 24 via telefon") — so the terms pick out the same things here. No defect.
- **D, "a real objection" for "verkliga invändning" (#21).** Draft: "Duplicate administration is a real objection that our proposal must address." Source: "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning … är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra." The draft asserts genuineness, not magnitude, and the immediately following sentence — "Yet the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings." — keeps the magnitude open, matching "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna." No defect.
- **E, lead compression (#5).** Draft: "before the board decides whether to remove either option". Source: "Därefter kan den avgöra om en kanal ska tas bort, ändras eller behållas." "Whether to remove" admits a negative answer, and the closing sentence restates all three outcomes: "It can then decide whether a booking option should be removed, changed, or retained." No defect.
