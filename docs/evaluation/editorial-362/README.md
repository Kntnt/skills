# Source comparison for #362: what was changed, shown and not shown

Everything here was run on 2026-09-20 in the Claude family: Claude Code 2.1.278, every checker, validator, run and judge a fresh `claude-opus-5` subagent at high deliberation, started from a `claude-fable-5-1` session. The protocol forbids a Claude session from driving a Codex harness, so the GPT-family failures that opened [#362](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/362) were **not retested and stay failed**. What follows says what holds in this family and what a GPT-family session still has to establish.

## The change

One file ships differently: `skills/editorial/write/references/source-check.md`, 810 words before and 988 after.

- **The comparison task.** The checker no longer rebuilds every claim as a standalone proposition with its negation. It pairs each draft passage with the source passage it rests on, quoted in the source's language, states what differs, and tests a difference in both directions by naming a concrete case in which one statement holds and the other fails. A case may be set aside only by quoting a supplied statement that excludes it. The author's own argument, reflection or advocacy is tested for the facts it presents as given, not for its reasoning or transitions. What is defensible as written is not reported. A meaning changed in crossing languages, outside quoted speech, is a source-support finding. The pronoun accounting (#356), the carried-over modifiers (#344), whose knowledge a claim describes, and the translated-quotation check are kept word for word.
- **The writer's validation paragraph.** One sentence is reworded to name the pairing the task now produces, and "a faulty reconstruction" becomes "a faulty pairing". Nothing else in the file changes: two comparisons at most, no unchecked prose after the last, a stop where accepted defects remain.

## Why: the diagnosed cause

The baseline checker in this family does notice real faults (7 of 8). What it does around them is the problem. The negation ledger produced about two findings a run that a blinded judge classed false, 27 in 14 runs, beside each real one. It filed the variable swap as a *translation* finding both times, once after first clearing the claim "on support"; that is the class the writer is told to test against idiom and not to settle on "a preferred synonym". Twice a checker saw a real difference and reasoned it away. That is the situation in which the GPT-family writer accepted all five column findings and no step pressed the swap: a real fault in a list of false ones, at the same or a lower severity. [`checker/results-round-1.md`](checker/results-round-1.md).

## The acceptance criteria, one by one

1. **Old failures, reports and disagreements kept; semantic faults told apart from report faults and disputed cautions.** Met. Nothing under `editorial-329/` is touched. Every finding here is classed supported, false or disputed, and where two judge panels disagreed both classes are recorded.
2. **A demonstrated cause motivates the change; unchanged US and GB opinion sources yield repeated delivered drafts without the variable swap; the Swedish control, sharp criticism and the unknown/absent boundary survive; the term must be expressed to count.** Partly met. The cause is demonstrated. No English draft wrote "proficiency" (4 of 4 rendered habit or familiarity), both British drafts were delivered, the Swedish control and its sharp criticism passed, and the unknown/absent repair was made in five runs. **Not met: both American English runs stopped**, validly, on a real defect found at the second comparison. A stop is not a delivered draft.
3. **Repeated column and Swedish opinion runs keep supported reflection, with intermediate reports and dispositions judged.** Partly met. The column ran twice and kept it both times: no finding against a reflective transition, no struck passage, no stop. The Swedish opinion ran **once**, not repeatedly, and passed. One column run's report files never reached disk.
4. **The same method still detects real dating, document-scope and personal-attribute faults, on established controls.** Met at checker level (chronology 9/9, personal attribute 9/9) and in the case-study run. **One fixture is not fixed:** the document-scope fault was missed once in every arm, under four wordings, and the shipped task does not change that.
5. **Source-blind Redline pairs, valid stops, comparison counts and unchanged last-checked prose documented.** Documented. It shows two things that are not good: `opinion-en_GB-r2` delivered prose no checker had seen, and Redline failed R1 in two of four pairs.
6. **Independent whole-chain review and real evaluation of the integrated candidate; reading, latency and process change reported.** Partly met. The reviews were done and the real runs made, with one caveat stated in the records: three words of the task changed after the runs, to the wording the writer's paragraph already used. **Coverage the ticket asks for and this evaluation does not have:** only F1, G2, L1 and R1 were judged, not the corpus's G1, P1, W1, L2, T1, T2, R2 and O1, so nothing here says whether Redline's closing Proofread pass ran; `source-check.md` loads for every genre, and `article`, `web-copy` and every technique case got no run; and no new contrast fixture was written before the change, since every fixture is an established one from the #329 evidence.
7. **Documents consistent, catalogue regenerated, the four checks pass.** Met.

## A judgement call in what ships

The first candidate wording tied the shipped one on detection (11 of 12) with no finding judged false and 28 s less per comparison. The shipped wording adds two rules to it: a case is set aside only by quoting a supplied statement that excludes it, and a standing case is a finding however natural or cautious the draft's wording. At the checker those rules did not measurably help; the fixture they were written for is still missed one run in three. They ship for one measured reason: in validation round 2 the validators who accepted a true finding, 10 of 10, and rejected the false F4, 4 of 4, gave those rules as their reason ("no supplied statement excludes the checker's case"). Both arms of that round carried the shipped task, so it shows the rules in use, not that they are needed. The real runs were made with the shipped wording and not with the first candidate. "Test both directions" still asks for one case; the "ability" observation below suggests it is applied one way under a negation, and it was left alone because there was nothing to test a rewording against.

## What is not solved, and candidate tickets

Each is a defect or a decision this evaluation exposed and did not settle. Three are filed for triage: [#376](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/376) the two-comparison cap and the gate, [#377](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/377) Redline and the sentences that limit a claim, [#378](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/378) the checker's report file. The rest stay with #362, which remains open.

- **Defect A in the GPT family is unverified.** The shipped task detects the swap 3 of 3 here and files it as source support, but here the swap never arises in a real draft, and one earlier candidate wording missed it once. A Codex session has to rerun `opinion` in `en_US` and `en_GB` against this revision.
- **The two-comparison cap turns a late true finding into a stop or a breach.** Three of nine runs met a real defect at the second comparison: two stopped, one repaired and delivered unchecked. The ticket says both that a stop is never an approved draft and that never delivering is no solution; the contract as written cannot give both. This needs a decision, not a wording.
- **Validation cannot catch a wrongly set-aside difference** (0 of 12). A comparison that misses is only caught by a second comparison, which runs only when the first led to a change or a dispute.
- **The both-directions test is applied one way under a negation.** Three of four English drafts render "funktionsförmåga" as bare "ability", and two checkers cleared it by asking only whether something could be the one without being the other. No judge flagged it; it is the evaluator's observation.
- **Redline undoes Write's fidelity repairs.** In three of four pairs the sentence it changed or deleted was one the comparison had made careful on purpose: two source caveats deleted, a no-claim hardened into a denial, *förvaltningen* renamed. It cannot know, by contract. One pair also made nine unreported changes of taste to a clean text.
- **Checkers' report files did not reach disk in two runs**, because the harness refused the write. The writer read the reports as text. `source-check.md` assumes the file.

## Cost

`source-check.md` grows by 178 words, on top of the 884 the ticket already counts. For an `opinion` run the mandatory reading (`SKILL.md`, `base.md`, the genre, `web-craft.md`, `delivery.md`, `source-check.md`, before the language scope and any source) goes from about 5 680 to 5 860 words, 3 % more. A comparison is shorter and quicker than before, but a `/write` run in this family took 418–1 191 s, median 896 s, and eight of nine runs used both comparisons. This evaluation used about 155 subagent runs.
