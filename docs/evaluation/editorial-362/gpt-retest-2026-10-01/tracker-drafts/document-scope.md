Write: measure whether an independent source comparison reduces document-scope misses

## What to build

This is a proposal for Thomas's decision on the question parked in #362, not an authorization to add another checker. The commissioned GPT retest on the unchanged `p3-document-scope` fixture detects the document/knowledge-scope shift correctly in four of six completed trials. The first and sixth discuss the shift but wrongly clear it. No other, false or disputed formal finding appears. Two earlier service-capacity interruptions are void, retained and excluded.

The supplied package says it does not state actual consent status. The draft says the underlying association submission does not establish whether consent exists. A submission establishing a current refusal, omitted from the supplied summary, is compatible with the material but contradicts the draft. Both missed reports use package completeness to rule out or accept that possibility; neither is a missing-pronoun problem or an assertion that consent is absent.

The historical Claude measurement missed one of three, and the current GPT measurement misses two of six. That equal fraction triggers the parked owner question. Different seats/revisions and the lack of a fresh pre-change arm prevent a causal comparison. No existing measurement establishes that a second independent checker helps, that its misses are independent, or that its added cost is acceptable. Current standalone reports take 579.60–817.28 seconds; the proposed study must measure actual pipeline cost rather than borrow the old two-to-four-minute estimate.

If Thomas chooses to proceed, stage a bounded diagnosis with criteria fixed before runs, testing whether an independently fresh second source comparison reduces this miss while preserving supported prose and avoiding additional false findings. Preserve the normal comparison budget and output contract unless an expressly commissioned candidate measures a justified change. Do not prescribe a canonical consent sentence or add a checker merely because the same fraction recurred.

Evidence: `docs/evaluation/editorial-362/gpt-retest-2026-10-01/runs/p3-document-scope-r1/` through `p3-document-scope-r6/`, the complete neutral input and blind judgment in `judges/p3-document-scope/`, and `docs/evaluation/records/source-check-gpt-2026-10-01-362.md`.
