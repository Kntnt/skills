# Source comparison 1

## Scope and method

I read the complete draft and the complete supplied Swedish brief, source material and boundaries. This report compares claims, attribution, qualifications and implications. It does not review prose quality or mechanics. The material is synthetic and fictional; no external evidence was used. The target language is British English. There are no speech quotations in the draft, so there are no translated-quotation findings. Changes of meaning in unquoted translation are treated as source-support findings.

The commissioning brief establishes the proposed position: “Avsändare är Sanna Ek, talesperson för föreningen Öppna beslut. Tes: kommunstyrelsen bör skjuta upp ett permanent byte till enbart digital bokning av föreningslokaler och först pröva ett halvår med både telefon och webb.” Advocacy and evaluative reasoning expressed in this commissioned voice are distinguished below from assertions about what the documents establish.

## Source-support findings

### F1: The heading turns an unreported matter into a pilot finding

**Draft:** “The pilot leaves users’ reasons unanswered”.

**Source:** “Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana.” The proposed later procedure is “be användarna frivilligt ange varför de väljer telefon eller webb”. Neither states that the earlier report has no explanation of users’ reasons. Sanna’s supported position is “Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används.” That position supports an inquiry, not a factual description of all the pilot’s contents.

**Difference:** Scope and state of knowledge. The source specifies three measurements not made and says why further inquiry is advocated. The heading asserts the outcome/content of the pilot in relation to users’ reasons.

**Concrete case:** The pilot could have recorded telephone users’ stated reasons in an open comments field, while measuring none of age, functional ability or digital familiarity. Its report could explain those reasons, and the association could still advocate collecting reasons in a wider trial. Every supplied statement would remain true, but the heading would fail. No supplied passage excludes that case.

**Reverse-direction check:** A pilot without any reasons data but with measured ages would leave reasons unanswered while violating the source’s actual omission statement. This case is excluded by “Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana.” It therefore provides no independent reverse finding. There is no supplied source assertion about reasons that is equivalent to the heading.

**Smallest supported repair:** “The pilot figures leave users’ reasons unanswered”. This confines the claim to the supplied booking totals, which identify channels and counts rather than reasons. The report could contain further information without making that repaired heading false.

### F2: “Local” adds an unsupported qualification to the associations

**Draft:** “all seven premises for local associations”.

**Source:** “telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september”, in a source describing “föreningslokaler”.

**Difference:** The draft adds the geographical/organisational qualification “local” to the associations served by the premises. The source identifies association premises but does not identify the associations as local or state their eligibility.

**Concrete case, source holds and draft fails:** The seven premises could be available to national membership associations or visiting national federations while local associations are not eligible to book them. They would still be “föreningslokaler”, and all the dates, counts, pilot procedure and proposed board action would remain as supplied. They would not be premises for local associations. No supplied statement excludes that case; addressing the town’s residents does not establish who may book its premises.

**Reverse-direction check:** Premises for local associations are also association premises in this context. I can name no compatible case in which the draft’s association-purpose description holds but the source’s generic association-purpose description fails. The unsupported narrowing is the finding.

**Smallest supported repair:** Replace “all seven premises for local associations” with “all seven association premises”.

### F3: The comparison changes labour cost into work/effort

**Draft:** “the board should be able to weigh the work involved against the value for users”.

**Source:** “Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna.”

**Difference:** The thing to be assessed is changed. “Arbetskostnaden” names the cost of labour; “the work involved” names the work, effort or burden itself. In a source that separately names financial savings and an unresolved trial cost, these are not interchangeable. The user-value side of the comparison and its priority are correctly preserved.

**Concrete case, source requirement satisfied but draft requirement not necessarily satisfied:** The board could compare calculated paid labour cost for the two routes with user value without assessing how demanding or onerous staff find the work. It would have the cost comparison Sanna requires but not necessarily an assessment of “the work involved” in the draft’s broader effort sense. A comparison can price task time without assessing the qualitative work burden. Nothing in the source excludes this distinction.

**Concrete case, draft requirement satisfied but source requirement not satisfied:** The board could assess the additional tasks, effort and time alongside users’ convenience, while leaving wages, staffing costs and the monetary cost of that labour unknown. That would permit weighing “the work involved”, but would not establish “arbetskostnaden”. It is compatible with Sanna having the supplied stance and with the present documents lacking measurements; her stance need not already have been fulfilled. No supplied statement says work burden and paid labour cost are identical.

**Smallest supported repair:** Replace “the work involved” with “the labour cost”: “the board should be able to weigh the labour cost against the value for users.”
