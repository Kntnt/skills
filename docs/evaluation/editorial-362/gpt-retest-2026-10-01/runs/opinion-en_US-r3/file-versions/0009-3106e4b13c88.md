## Source-support findings and smallest supported repairs

### F1 — Added locality of the associations

Draft: “two venues for local associations” and “all seven venues for local associations.”

Source: “föreningslokaler” and “alla sju lokaler.” The source identifies association premises, but does not locate or restrict the associations themselves.

Difference: geographical scope of the beneficiaries. A municipality could offer association premises to nationwide associations with no local membership or branch. That remains compatible with every supplied statement and satisfies the source term while failing the draft's added description. Conversely, premises intended for local associations are association premises, so that direction does not produce a counterexample. No supplied statement excludes the standing case.

Smallest repair: replace “venues for local associations” with “association venues” in both occurrences.

### F2 — The summary changes the counting subject

Draft: “An eight-week pilot … counted bookings rather than individual users.”

Source: “Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer.”

Difference: subject and scope. The pilot could have collected a distinct-user count in an internal dataset while the published report counted only bookings. The source holds and the draft's characterization of the pilot as counting bookings rather than users fails. Conversely, a pilot could collect bookings only while a report additionally reconstructs a unique-person count; the draft would hold while the source's report-only-count statement would fail. These are tests of the semantic scopes, not allegations about what occurred. Nothing supplied excludes the first case.

Smallest repair: “The report on an eight-week pilot in two association venues counted bookings rather than unique people.”

### F3 — The heading turns a report limitation into a pilot-wide absence

Draft heading: “The pilot does not measure digital familiarity.”

Source: “Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer. Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana.” “Den” refers to the report.

Difference: subject and the scope of known absence. A pilot could collect an unpublished digital-familiarity survey, with no such measurement in the report. The source would hold and the draft heading would fail. Conversely, a pilot without that measurement could be described in a report that incorporates an independent digital-familiarity survey; the draft would hold while the source's report absence would fail. No supplied statement excludes the standing first case.

Smallest repair: “The report does not measure digital familiarity.”

### F4 — The draft narrows the permanent digital offering to an online-only offering

Draft: “postpone a permanent switch to online booking alone.”

Source: “skjuta upp ett permanent byte till enbart digital bokning av föreningslokaler.” The described temporary trial separately specifies “både telefon och webb.”

Difference: the channel set described as the permanent proposal. Digital booking can include an offline electronic kiosk or locally operated booking terminal. A future arrangement retaining web booking and such a digital terminal while removing phone booking satisfies the source's description of solely digital booking but fails “online booking alone.” The temporary trial's two named routes do not exclude another digital route in the permanent arrangement. In the other direction, ordinary online booking is digital booking; no contextual case makes an online-only arrangement non-digital. No supplied statement excludes the standing case.

Smallest repair: “postpone a permanent switch to digital booking alone.” This preserves the source's exact scope without weakening the author's position. This issue is a source-support finding outside quotation, not a quoted-speech translation finding.

### F5 — The workload sentence changes a documentary gap into an unqualified measurement gap

Draft: “An unmeasured workload is an inadequate basis for a permanent switch.”

Source: “Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna.”

Difference: location and scope of absence. Staff could have measured the workload in a notebook or internal timesheet without including any time measurements in the decision documents. The source would hold while the workload would not be “unmeasured.” Conversely, the documents could include measurements of some different task while the two-route workload remained unmeasured; the draft's workload description could hold while the source's document-wide absence would fail. No supplied statement excludes the standing first case. The inadequacy judgment itself is supported advocacy and should remain sharp.

Smallest repair: “A workload argument without time measurements in the decision documents is an inadequate basis for a permanent switch.”
