## Cross-language and implication checks

The source-support comparison covers translations outside quotation marks as well as direct factual restatement. There are no translated quotations to review separately for quotation voice.

- **Digital/web booking:** The material identifies telephone and web as the two booking routes, describes ending telephone booking, and gives the thesis as a permanent switch to digital booking. Within this supplied proposal, the draft's references to a web-only switch identify the same described change. A separate app, SMS system, kiosk, or third booking route would be additional material absent from the proposal; the draft does not assert the existence or removal of such a route. No concrete contextual contradiction is established by the title.
- **Föreningslokaler / association venues:** The English term identifies places for association activities. It does not state that an association owns the premises, and the surrounding municipal context makes no new ownership claim. Both source and draft use two premises for the pilot and seven for the proposal.
- **Kommunstyrelsen / municipal executive board:** The English names the same municipality-level body throughout, without implying a national cabinet, elected mayor, or different municipal committee.
- **Tjänsteutlåtande / staff memorandum:** This is the staff document presented before the board meeting and stating the proposal and rationale. The draft makes no claim about its legal effect, signatory, internal confidentiality, or document-production procedure.
- **Digital vana / digital experience:** In the personal-characteristic list, “digital experience” denotes familiarity gained using digital tools. It is not read as a digital product's user experience or as a single interface event. The supplied lack of measurement is retained without claiming that the report established lack of familiarity.
- **Arbetskostnad / administrative costs / staff costs:** In context, the draft's administration-cost phrase refers to the staff work in the two workflows, with the later “staff costs” making that referent explicit. The draft does not claim a figure for overhead, software, buildings, or aggregate expenditure. The passage is advocacy about what the board should compare.
- **Bookings/residents/unique persons:** The heading's population-count contrast is clarified immediately as a failure to establish how many different people used each route. It does not turn bookings into residents, identify user residence, or assert that no users are residents. The source's no-unique-person-count statement supports the heading and body limitation.
- **Administrative rationale and benefits:** The draft preserves the actual stated rationale and confines the absence of measurement to the documents. It neither claims there are no savings nor invents motives about user laziness or expense. The author's reasoning calls for measurement rather than asserting a causal effect of either route.
- **Cost uncertainty and chronology:** The closing preserves the association's limited claim and the explicitly required cost uncertainty. It leaves the cost for the board and preserves possible removal, change, and retention. A trial is proposed before a permanent switch; neither its start date nor a later decision date is invented.
