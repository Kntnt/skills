## Cross-language support checks and countercase testing

No directly quoted speech appears in the draft. Consequently there are no direct-speech translation findings. The source statement attributed to Sanna Ek is paraphrased as her own argument, and its meaning is accounted for in items 8, 13 and 26.

The following potentially material terminology and scope differences were checked in both directions. They do not establish defects in this draft’s contextual meaning.

- **“Föreningslokaler” / “association premises”:** Both identify the accommodation available for association booking in this municipal proposal. A possible difference would be municipally owned rooms used by associations rather than property owned by an association. The Swedish source allows that case, but the English draft also allows it: “association” specifies the use/category of these bookable premises and asserts no owner. Conversely, association-owned accommodation would not be excluded by the Swedish term alone, and the draft adds no such ownership assertion. Multiple separately bookable rooms in a single building are compatible with the source; “premises” does not require separate freestanding buildings or include a stated area of land. The draft does not convert the two or seven bookable locations into a count of buildings, owners or associations. No concrete compatible case establishes a contextual mismatch.
- **“Enbart digital bokning” / “online-only booking”:** In abstraction, digital and online can have different technological extensions. A hypothetical offline digital booking method would illustrate that abstract difference. This material, however, identifies the relevant two channels as telephone and web: “först pröva ett halvår med både telefon och webb” and “Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är dubbel administration”. The draft’s phrase identifies the remaining web booking route in that same two-route proposal; it does not separately claim that every digital transaction requires internet connectivity. Adding a separate offline digital route does not make the draft assert a different result about the two routes under discussion. Conversely, online-only booking in this context is digital booking by the web route. No concrete case in which the contextual claims differ was identified.
- **“Funktionsförmåga” / “functional ability”:** Both name functional capacity as the unmeasured dimension. A case in which someone has a diagnosed disability but retains capacity to make a booking does not create a difference: neither passage substitutes disability status for ability. Conversely, impaired functional ability without a diagnosis is within both terms. No narrower diagnostic or bodily category is introduced.
- **“Digital vana” / “digital familiarity”:** Both concern familiarity/experience with digital use. Being unfamiliar while nevertheless capable of completing a booking, or experienced but unable to complete a particular booking, remains compatible with both passages. Neither passage treats this measure as the same as capacity, diagnosis or age. No contrary contextual case was identified.
- **“Unika personer” / “individual users”:** A repeat user making many bookings illustrates the central distinction in both passages: the figures count booking events, rather than distinct individuals. A report might additionally contain an unrelated count of organisations without changing what the 96 and 24 mean; the draft’s “These” is limited to those figures. It does not assert that no other information appears anywhere in the report. No contrary case is created by that possibility.
- **“Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna” / no “measurements of staff time” in the documents:** The English statement is limited to the staff time relevant to the objection. A possible English-true/Swedish-false case would be documents containing only measurements of users’ waiting time. It is excluded by the supplied “Någon tidsmätning … finns inte i handlingarna.” The reverse case—no time measurement of any kind but a staff-time measurement—cannot hold because staff time is time. Thus the draft’s supported narrower claim is not a defect.
- **“Gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat” / “does not claim to have funded or costed”:** Financing by the association and its estimating the trial’s cost are the corresponding actions. If someone else had funded or costed the trial, both passages could still be true. If the association had done either action without claiming it, both passages could likewise still be true. Neither supplies the stronger assertion that financing or costing never occurred. No epistemic change was found.
