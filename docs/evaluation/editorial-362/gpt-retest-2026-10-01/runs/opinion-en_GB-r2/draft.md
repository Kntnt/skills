# Lervik should trial telephone and web bookings

Removing telephone booking would settle how Lervik’s association premises can be booked while important questions remain unanswered. The pilot’s figures and the gaps in the cost evidence explain why a further trial is needed.

By Sanna Ek

As spokesperson for Öppna beslut, I am asking Lervik’s municipal executive committee to postpone a permanent move to online-only booking of association premises. We propose six months with both telephone and web bookings at all seven premises. The committee should first be able to weigh staff costs against value for users.

## Telephone bookings do not prove users cannot book online

The municipality’s report, *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, dated 8 April 2026, records 96 web bookings and 24 telephone bookings during an eight-week trial at two premises. These are bookings, not individual users.

The report did not measure age, functional ability or digital familiarity. Its telephone figures therefore cannot tell us what proportion of residents cannot book online. A telephone booking gives us a reason to investigate why that route is still used; it does not establish that someone cannot use the web.

## Duplicate administration deserves a measured answer

The officers’ report for the committee’s 18 June meeting proposes ending telephone bookings at all seven premises from September. Its stated reason is to spare staff from entering information through two workflows.

That objection deserves to be taken seriously. But the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor calculations of financial savings. The committee needs evidence to assess the workload alongside the value to users. A wish to simplify administration cannot supply that evidence.

## A wider trial would inform the decision

During our proposed trial, the administration would record the time spent on each booking route and ask users to volunteer their reasons for choosing telephone or web. This would give the committee a basis for considering both sides of the decision.

Öppna beslut does not claim to have funded or costed the trial. The executive committee must consider its cost. I do not oppose digital booking; I want the choice of booking routes to be informed.

## The committee can remove, change or retain a booking route

The committee should postpone online-only booking and first approve a six-month trial at all seven premises, keeping telephone and web bookings available and addressing the unresolved cost. Afterwards, it can decide whether either route should be removed, changed or retained.
