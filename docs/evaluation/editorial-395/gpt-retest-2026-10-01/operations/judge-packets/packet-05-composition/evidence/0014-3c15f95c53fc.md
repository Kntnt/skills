## Difference tests and qualifications

The wording changes that could affect source support were tested in both directions. No supported counterexample remains:

- **Customer and team:** A company can act through its own maintenance team, as this source explicitly establishes: “its own maintenance team decided to trial” and “The maintenance team ... kept telephone reporting open.” The draft's organizational summaries do not require a different individual or outside team to have acted.
- **New log:** A log could be an established supplier product while being new to this reporting arrangement. That would satisfy both the source and the draft's contextual meaning; the draft does not assert a newly created product. The source establishes the customer's trial choice against its previous separate storage, not the product's age.
- **Shared view:** A team could have one shared view and still handle excluded emergencies or previously ordered work elsewhere. That is compatible with both the title and the source. The draft does not say the view covers every repair or every resident report.
- **Purpose clause:** Some staff might fail to consult the log even though the team chose the trial so shifts could share information. Both the source's “wanted” and the draft's purpose clause remain true. The draft does not assert every staff member's actual use.
- **Date, training preposition, and flat/apartment wording:** These are a date-order conversion, ordinary temporal wording for the same sessions, and the en_US residential-unit term. The numbers, date, activity, and housing-unit type are preserved. No contextual concrete counterexample establishes a meaning difference.
- **Count exclusions:** The source says, “It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial.” The draft attaches this qualification to the note's reported count. It does not claim no emergencies occurred, no earlier work existed, or exactly 31 total repairs occurred.
- **Completion measurements:** The source paragraph is about repair reports and report-to-assignment time. Adding “repair” to “completion time” identifies that established subject. The draft does not assert an unmeasured completion statistic or treat assignment as completion.
- **Time difference and software:** Both passages leave open a software contribution and other possible explanations. “The note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software” supports an absence of that attribution, not a finding that the software had no effect. The draft keeps this distinction.
- **Availability of comparisons and measurements:** The draft repeats the supplied absence of available supplier comparison and measurements. It does not infer that no comparison was conducted, that no savings occurred, or that residents were dissatisfied.
- **Heading tense:** “Elm Quay keeps telephone reporting open” functions as case-story narration of the trial action immediately explained in the body. A subsequent closure would not falsify that narrated trial action; no continuing policy is asserted beyond the narrated case.
- **Editorial invitation:** The final heading and conditional address invite the intended audience to consider the case. They do not assert an actual population's intentions, adoption, or results. The checklist's identity, optional status, and reading purpose are separately supported by the brief.
