## Source-support finding

**F1 — Training sessions become separate occasions.**

Draft: “Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare vid två tillfällen.”

Source: “Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions.”

The acting supplier, configuration and six-person count are supported. The source counts sessions; “vid två tillfällen” counts occasions and ordinarily indicates two separate times. Those are not necessarily the same unit.

A concrete source-compatible case is a single training occasion at which two simultaneous sessions are held, with three of the six staff in each. There are six staff and two sessions, but only one training occasion. The source supplies no statement excluding simultaneous sessions or requiring two separate occasions. In the other direction, two training occasions with two sessions on each occasion would satisfy the draft's occasion count but not the source's two-session count; the draft does not constrain the session count. This is a unit-of-count change, not a preferred synonym.

Smallest supported repair: “Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare under två utbildningspass.”
