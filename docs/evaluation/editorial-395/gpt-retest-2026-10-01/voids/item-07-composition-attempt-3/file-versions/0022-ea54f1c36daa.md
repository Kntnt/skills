## Source-support finding

### S1 — An unavailable comparison becomes an absence of comparison material

**Draft:** “Det finns inget underlag för en jämförelse med någon annan leverantör.”

**Source:** “No comparison with another supplier is available.”

**Difference:** The source identifies what is unavailable: a comparison. The draft identifies a different thing as absent: a basis or material from which a comparison could be made. Lack of an available comparison does not establish lack of underlying information. The draft also leaves that absence unqualified by whose files, knowledge or access are meant.

**Concrete case, source true and draft false:** Elm Quay could have raw supplier descriptions or testing notes capable of supporting a comparison, while no comparison itself has been prepared or made available. That is compatible with the supplied package: “It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show the status of each repair” describes a test of Svale; “No comparison with another supplier is available” excludes an available comparison but says nothing that excludes unused raw information. The supplied material does not exclude this case. Identifying the entire provided package as the writer's available source does not justify the draft's unbounded claim that comparison material does not exist.

**Reverse-direction test:** An ungrounded comparison could exist despite there being no adequate “underlag” for it. That would make the draft sentence true and the source sentence false. In this particular package that reverse case is excluded by “No comparison with another supplier is available”; it nevertheless confirms that the two terms do not name the same thing. The first case remains sufficient for the finding.

**Smallest supported repair:** “Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte tillgänglig.” This restores the source's object and availability claim without adding a comparison, alleging another supplier was tested, or inventing missing knowledge.
