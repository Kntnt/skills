## Pronoun and natural-gender accounting

- Introductory “den” in “utan att tillskriva den programvaran” refers to the singular difference. The common grammatical gender agrees with skillnad; it conveys no natural gender. The source's “the difference” is likewise singular. No person information is added.
- “Bolagets eget” and the later possessive “Elm Quays” refer to the same organisation. Eget is grammatical agreement with the neuter teamet. It is not a natural-gender claim.
- “De boende” is a plural human designation corresponding to source residents. It conveys plurality, not male, female or mixed composition.
- Quotation 1: “Vi” is first-person plural, matching source “We”; “våra” is first-person plural possession, matching “ours”; “oss” is first-person plural object, matching “us.” The supplied description that the maintenance team designed the categories supports the team referent in context. None of these pronouns identifies the natural genders or exact membership of the group. “Dem” refers to plural categories, matching “them”; it supplies no natural gender.
- Quotation 2: “Vi” remains first-person plural; “Jag” remains first-person singular, matching “I.” “Den” in “den tiden” refers to the singular amount of time, matching “that time”; its common grammatical gender is not a natural-gender assertion. “Nästa hus” remains one next building; this singular referent does not establish how many individual people would participate there.
- Quotation 3: both occurrences of “Jag” match first-person singular “I”; “oss” matches first-person plural “us.” The draft adds no male or female pronoun. Although the complete source's narrative uses “Her” for Lind, the Swedish role titles and first-person pronouns convey no natural gender. This omission does not change the quoted claims.
- “Sin” in “Maya Lind ger sin bedömning” is a third-person reflexive singular possessive whose antecedent is Lind and whose common grammatical form agrees with bedömning. The source expressly attributes the assessment to Lind. Sin establishes no natural gender.
- “Du” in the closing heading and recommendation is second-person singular address to an individual reader. It is an authorial invitation, not an assertion about an actual population of readers, and needs no sourced reader identity or demographic features. “Den” in “Läs den” is a singular common-gender reference to checklistan; it conveys no natural gender.
- Swedish definite suffixes and common-gender role terms in “arbetsledaren,” “underhållsarbetsledaren” and “medarbetare” do not assert male or female staff. “Teamet” is grammatically singular and neuter but represents the supplied collective group, without a claim about natural gender or its unreported membership count.

## Findings and smallest supported repairs

### F1 — source support: the measurement's actor

Draft: “Under ett åtta veckor långt försök i två hus mätte Elm Quay Housing tiden från anmälan till tilldelning av reparationsärenden.”

Support: “Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered. It excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial. Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period.”

Difference: the source identifies the customer as the owner of the note. The draft identifies the customer as the actor that measured the intervals. Reporting a statistic does not establish who measured it. The period to which the result applies is established; I do not separately treat the sentence's ordinary period framing as a defect about when the calculation was performed.

Concrete counterexample: an external analyst measures or extracts every interval and calculates the medians, then sends the figures to Elm Quay, which reproduces them in its internal note. Elm Quay itself performs no measuring. The shared trial, report count, exclusions, differing workloads, non-attribution and all Lind quotations can still be exactly as supplied. No supplied statement excludes this division of work. Thus the supported report exists while the added measuring agency fails.

Reverse test: Elm Quay could itself measure the same variable and produce the stated note; that also fits the supplied material. The point is that the material permits both actors and does not choose the draft's actor. The draft does not misname the variable, unit or interval endpoint.

Smallest clear repair that retains customer action without assigning an unsupported measurement actor:

“Elm Quay Housing prövade en gemensam reparationslogg i två hus under åtta veckor.”

The next sentence can remain, because it accurately attributes the reported difference to the company's note.

### F2 — source support: when category work occurred

Draft: “I mejlintervjun beskriver Maya Lind arbetet inför starten:”

Support: “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.” Also: “The maintenance team designed its categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents.”

Difference: the first source sentence retrospectively compares time spent on two activities. The second is Lind's conditional recommendation to reserve that time before the next building starts. The draft's introduction presents the original category work as work before the start. Neither quoted source statement dates the original agreement before the original trial's start.

Concrete counterexample: the eight-week trial begins, and the team uses some of its opening days to agree on the categories before entering or while entering its first reports. Agreement takes longer than first entry; Lind therefore recommends reserving that time before the next building starts. The categories remain the team's, Svale can configure the log and train the six staff, and every supplied measurement and quotation remains true. Category agreement occurred within the original trial, rather than before its start. The source contains no express timing statement that excludes this case. Listing category design and configuration before a sentence stating the trial's duration does not date the agreement before the trial began.

Reverse test: agreement before the original start is also compatible with the source. The material permits both chronologies and does not establish the one used by this introduction. The forward-looking recommendation in the quotation itself remains fully supported and should stay.

Smallest repair:

“I mejlintervjun beskriver Maya Lind arbetet med kategorierna:”

### F3 — source support: who provides the checklist

Draft: “Svale Systems tillhandahåller en [checklista för införande](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist).”

Support: “The publisher is the supplier, Svale Systems.” “The optional next step is the supplied implementation checklist at https://example.invalid/svale/checklist; it is a document to read, not a consultation booking or a product trial.”

Difference: the source identifies Svale as the case publisher and supplies a document destination. It does not identify the checklist's provider. Supplied does not by itself mean supplied by Svale, and the path component svale does not identify a provider.

Concrete counterexample: an independent housing organisation provides the implementation checklist; the commissioning brief supplies its link for the supplier-published case, using the specified URL. Svale publishes the case and directs readers to the independent document, while the other organisation provides the document. The source's document type, destination and optional reading action all remain true. The source contains no statement excluding such a third-party document. Readers ordinarily understand the draft's sentence as crediting the checklist service to Svale, beyond merely crediting Svale with publishing an article containing a link.

Reverse test: Svale could provide this same checklist; that also fits the source. The material does not settle which provider is responsible. No defect is found in the document description, URL, optionality or suggestion to read it.

Smallest repair:

“Läs [checklistan för införande](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist) om du vill gå vidare med förberedelserna.”

This replaces the two closing sentences with the same authorised next step and removes only the unestablished provider attribution.
