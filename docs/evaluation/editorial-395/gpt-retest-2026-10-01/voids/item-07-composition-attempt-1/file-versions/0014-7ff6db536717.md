## Finding F1: completion is presented as an established past event

**Type:** Source-support finding in the author's explanatory narrative, outside quoted speech.

**Draft passage:** “Siffrorna beskriver alltså tilldelningen av ärenden, inte när reparationerna blev klara.”

**Actual support:** “Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial and three in the preceding eight-week period.” “There are no cost, resident-satisfaction or completion-time measurements.”

**What differs:** The source establishes the measured endpoint as assignment and establishes the absence of completion-time measurements. It supplies no statement that the repairs were completed. The finite past clause “reparationerna blev klara,” embedded under “inte när,” presents completion as a past event whose time the numbers do not describe. Negating the numbers' description of that time does not remove the embedded event. The unsupported element is the completion event, not the valid distinction between assignment and completion metrics.

**Source-holds/draft-fails case:** All 31 entered reports could have been assigned, with the stated assignment-time medians, while all of the associated repairs remained unfinished at the time covered by the case. The source would still hold: reports were entered, assignment was measured, and no completion-time measurements existed. The draft's representation of “when the repairs became complete” would present an event that did not occur. Nothing in the supplied source excludes this case: no completion statement is provided, and the actual relevant statement is “There are no ... completion-time measurements.”

**Draft-holds/source-fails direction:** If the repairs were completed and the quoted numbers described assignment rather than their completion times, the draft sentence could hold even if the organisation also possessed separate completion-time measurements. The source's “There are no ... completion-time measurements” would then fail. The supplied source excludes that reverse case by that exact statement, so it is set aside as incompatible with the material. This does not exclude the first case or establish completed repairs.

**Smallest supported repair:** Replace the sentence with “Siffrorna beskriver alltså tiden från anmälan till tilldelning.” This keeps the supported clarification and removes the unsupported completion event. The preceding sentence already retains the absence of completion-time measurements. Alternatively, deleting only “, inte när reparationerna blev klara” removes the unsupported event without changing the remaining claim.
