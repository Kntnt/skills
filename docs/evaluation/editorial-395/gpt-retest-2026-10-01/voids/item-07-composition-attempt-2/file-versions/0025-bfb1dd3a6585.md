## Findings

### T1 — Translation finding: an added first-person plural actor changes the condition in Lind's quotation

**Draft:**

> Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan vi börjar i nästa byggnad, säger Lind.

**Source, complete usable quotation:**

> “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

The first source sentence's `We` applies to the earlier experience of agreeing on categories. In the second sentence, `I would` remains singular and conditional, and the deadline is the start of the next building. The source does not say that the preceding `We` group will itself begin in the next building. The draft inserts `vi` as a first-person-plural actor and ties the deadline to that group's beginning there. This is a meaning change in translated direct speech, with an unsupported subject and a potentially changed deadline. It is not a objection to the choice of a synonym or to Swedish style.

**Source true / draft false — compatible case:** Lind would reserve category-agreement time before the next building starts using the log, but that building's trial would be started by another maintenance group or an implementation team; the group included in her preceding `We` would not itself begin there. Her source statement can hold without the draft's first-person-plural starting action. None of “The maintenance team designed its categories”, “The trial has not yet expanded”, or “the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs” says who would start the next building or excludes delegation to another group.

**Draft true / source false — compatible case:** Another group would start the next building using the log before Lind's group begins its own work there. Lind would set the category-agreement time aside after that building's start but before her group's later beginning. The draft's deadline is met; the source's deadline is missed. The supplied statements “The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs” leave the future rollout arrangement unspecified and do not exclude different starting times for the building and Lind's group.

These are hypothetical arrangements compatible with the still-unexpanded trial; they are not assertions that either arrangement occurred. No supplied statement rules them out.

**Smallest supported repair:**

> Jag skulle avsätta den tiden före starten i nästa byggnad, säger Lind.

This keeps the singular speaker, conditional recommendation, demonstrative time reference, next building and before-start deadline. It removes only the added plural actor. The log/trial context makes `starten i nästa byggnad` intelligible without deciding who starts it.
