## Finding F1 — Email content narrowed to reports

**Type:** Source support; not a quotation-translation issue.

**Draft passage:** `Tidigare lagrades anmälningar per telefon och mejl var för sig.`

**Exact source passage:** “Previously telephone reports and emails were stored separately.”

**Changed support:** The source states that the separately stored items were telephone reports and emails. The draft presents the email items as `anmälningar` as well as presenting telephone reports that way. In Swedish, `anmälningar per telefon och mejl` ordinarily says that reports were made through both telephone and email. Email can contain other kinds of repair information, so the source category and the draft category are not coextensive.

**Source true, draft false — concrete compatible case:** Residents' initial repair reports were made only by telephone. The separately stored emails were maintenance staff's status updates about those repairs, rather than initial repair reports. A shared log was then tried to make the same information visible across shifts. This fits the supplied source, including the report-to-assignment measurements and continued telephone reporting, but the draft's assertion that the previously stored reports were made by email as well would be false. No supplied sentence excludes that case. In particular, the source calls these items “emails”, not email reports.

**Draft true, source false — reverse test:** If actual telephone and email repair reports were stored separately, those email reports would also be emails. The draft's storage assertion therefore entails the corresponding source assertion under the case's ordinary meaning. No compatible reverse failure case is available. The one-way narrowing is nevertheless a finding because the first case stands.

**Smallest supported repair:** Replace the sentence with `Tidigare lagrades telefonanmälningar och mejl var för sig.` This preserves the prior arrangement and separate storage while retaining the source's distinction between telephone reports and emails.
