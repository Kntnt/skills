- Source quotation 3 “I would” twice and “us”: first-person singular Lind and first-person plural customer group. Draft “Jag skulle” twice and “oss” preserves these distinctions. Neither language's first-person pronouns specifies natural gender.
- Source editorial framing “Her final quotation” conveys third-person singular feminine natural-gender information about Lind. ## Findings and concrete counterexamples

### S1 — Unbounded present tense in the main title

**Draft:** “Elm Quay samlar reparationsanmälningar i en logg”.

**Source:** “In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings.” “The trial lasted eight weeks.” “Elm Quay's internal trial note, 4 December 2025, says that 31 repair reports were entered.” “The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs.”

**Difference:** On an ordinary current-activity reading, “samlar” states collection now. The source establishes collection during the completed trial and a pending expansion decision. It does not establish ongoing collection after the trial. The issue concerns time and the state of knowledge, not the log, report type, customer agency or benefit.

**Source true, current-title claim false:** The customer entered the 31 reports during its eight-week trial, then paused new log entry while examining how categories handle larger repairs. It has not expanded and has not decided yet. This scenario is compatible with every quoted source statement. No supplied statement excludes the pause. Calling the title a conventional headline present does not supply evidence that new collection continues.

**Title true, historical-trial proposition false:** A customer could currently gather reports in a log without having decided on the specified September 2025 eight-week trial in two buildings—for example, by starting ordinary use in October. That would satisfy the title's bare present collection proposition but not the source's trial proposition. This reverse scenario is excluded for this particular customer by the supplied statement “In September 2025 its own maintenance team decided to trial a shared repair log in two buildings” together with “The trial lasted eight weeks.” It is therefore set aside as a scenario for this source package. The first direction remains compatible and sufficient to expose the extra current-activity claim.

**Smallest supported repair:** “Elm Quay samlade reparationsanmälningar i en logg”. This retains the subject, action and title's editorial emphasis, while locating the established activity in the past. “Elm Quay prövade en gemensam reparationslogg” is also supported, but changes more wording than necessary.

**Classification:** Source-support finding; not a translated-quotation defect. If the publisher intends a continuing-use assertion, new evidence that the customer continued entering reports after the trial would close the gap. That evidence is absent from this supplied package.
