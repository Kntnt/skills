## Findings

### F1 — Source-support: measurement exclusion broadened across languages

Draft: “The report measures neither age nor functional ability nor **familiarity with digital technology**.”

Source: “Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller **digital vana**.”

The third term changes what is excluded from measurement. “Digital vana” concerns being accustomed to using digital technology. “Familiarity with digital technology” can also mean knowing about it without practical accustomedness. The source therefore does not establish every measurement absence the English sentence can assert.

**Source-true/draft-false case:** the pilot report could include a test of users’ knowledge of digital tools learned from printed manuals, while measuring neither their practical accustomedness to using those tools, age, nor functional ability. It could still count bookings rather than unique people. Such a report would satisfy the supplied exclusions but would measure a form of familiarity. No supplied statement excludes that case; the source does not describe every other possible measure in the report.

**Reverse-direction test:** a report measuring users’ habitual digital use while omitting a separate knowledge assessment could distinguish practical accustomedness from knowledge-based familiarity. As a case for this actual report, however, it is excluded by the explicit statement “Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana.” The surviving first-direction case is sufficient to establish the draft’s broader absence claim.

**Smallest supported repair:** “The report measures neither age nor functional ability nor **how accustomed users are to using digital technology**.” This retains the measured attributes, certainty and report attribution while narrowing the third term to the supplied one.

No other source-support defect was found. The administration’s explanation remains attributed in its complete sentence; the report’s counts and exclusions remain qualified; the organisation’s absence of a claim is not turned into knowledge of non-occurrence; and the author’s advocacy is not treated as an empirical finding.
