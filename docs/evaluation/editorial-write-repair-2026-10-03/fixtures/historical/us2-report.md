## Findings

### F1 — Source support: “digital experience” changes the unmeasured variable

Draft passage: “The report measures neither age, functional ability, nor digital experience.”

Source passage: “Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana.” The antecedent of “Den” is “Rapporten” in “Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer.”

Difference: What is measured. “Digital experience” can mean the quantity or history of a person's exposure to digital systems; “digital vana” describes being accustomed to, or familiar with, their use. A negative measurement claim about one does not establish the same absence for the other. No supplied sentence makes the two variables interchangeable or separately rules out measurement of digital experience.

Test, source true / draft false: The pilot could have asked users how many years they had used digital systems, while asking nothing about their current familiarity or accustomedness. Counting years of experience would measure digital experience, yet would not measure digital vana: a person could retain a long history of use while being unfamiliar with present interfaces after a long interruption. Such a question is compatible with the supplied source, which excludes measurement of digital vana, age, and functional ability but does not exclude years of digital use. Thus the source's negative statement can hold while the draft's expanded negative statement fails.

Test, draft true / source false: Conversely, a hypothetical report could record current comfort/familiarity when booking online while recording no amount or history of digital experience. That would measure vana without measuring experience understood as accumulated exposure. This converse scenario is excluded as an account of the actual supplied report by “Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana.” Setting that direction aside does not remove the first, compatible case.

Smallest supported repair: Replace “digital experience” with “digital familiarity”: “The report measures neither age, functional ability, nor digital familiarity.” Preserve the report attribution, negation, and other two variables.

Classification: Source-support finding from meaning changed across languages outside quotation. It is not a translated-quotation finding or a general preference about style.
