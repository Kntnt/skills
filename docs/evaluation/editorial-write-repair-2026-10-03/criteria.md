## Judgment and causal rules

Article, report, parent disposition and account have separate results. Supported, false and disputed findings remain distinct; report completion confers no semantic validity. Both independent readings are retained, split semantic points remain unmet/undetermined. Missing plaintext dispatch/contract return/transient bytes remain missing, not established by later output. Native one-seat baseline/candidate and controlled component replay results remain distinct. No same-seat/causal claim is made from historical measurements.

The initial red loop must reach the real source-comparison/parent/composition seam and capture exact symptom. Bytes/counts/contract-presence alone are not semantic tests. After a red loop exists, minimize full context one variable at a time and publish3–5 falsifiable hypotheses before probes; hypotheses are intentionally not asserted during this preparation. At most one revise, no outcome-seeking repeat, and remaining established causes stay in the authorized repair pipeline rather than being declared solved by opening a ticket.

Final independent acceptance evaluates C-context through C-effects together on eventual integrated Write runtime, across opinion/article/case-study, English-source and Swedish-source into en_US/en_GB/sv, supported true errors and valid paraphrase/voice/quotation controls. Source-blind Redline pairs are separate measured controls, with explicit no-change/public-private distinction. Every applicable corpus criterion is recorded; these targeted criteria do not replace them or claim universal quality. Complete original problem, controls, catalog and all four CONTRIBUTING gates must be assessed before closure.
