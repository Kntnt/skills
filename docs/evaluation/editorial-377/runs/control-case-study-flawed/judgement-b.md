## 1. Differences

Numbered in document order. "Class" is one of: mechanical correction (M), repair of a visible defect (R), change of taste (T), change to what a claim says (C) with the dimension named.

**D-01 — Title.**
Before: `# Vår fantastiska lösning räddade Elm Quay`
After: `# Två arbetsdagar till tilldelning i Elm Quays försök`
Class: **R** + **C (causality, certainty)**. The old title asserts that the supplier's solution rescued the customer — a causal claim the body's own limiting sentence excludes, plus the evaluation "fantastiska". The new title states a measure the text carries ("Tiden till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar") and asserts no cause.

**D-02 — Ingress.**
Before: `**Elm Quay registrerade 31 ärenden. Elm Quay registrerade 31 ärenden.**`
After: `**Under åtta veckor registrerade Elm Quay 31 ärenden, akuta ärenden undantagna. Elm Quays försöksanteckning redovisar också tiden till tilldelning, och arbetsledaren Maya Lind ger sitt omdöme om försöket.**`
Class: **R** (the sentence was printed twice — the duplicate ingress) + **C (scope)**. The second instance is gone and the surviving one gains the eight-week window and the exclusion of urgent cases. Both additions are taken from the body's own sentence ("registrerades 31 ärenden under åtta veckor, med akuta ärenden undantagna"), so nothing is invented; the scope of the ingress widens to what the text already holds. One sub-nuance: in the body the measure is attributed ("Enligt … försöksanteckning"), in the new ingress the first sentence states it flatly and the attribution follows in the next sentence. Source present in context, attribution not lost.

**D-11 — Second subheading.**

## 4. R1

**Pass.**

The passage that decides it is the second remaining observation, because it is where the run could most cheaply have cheated and did not:

> **"programvaran" har inte längre någon syftning.** … Detta är en iakttagelse som rundans egen reparation skapade: den följde av att meningen "Vår programvara orsakade därför hela förbättringen." togs bort, och den meningen var textens enda koppling mellan Svale och försöket. Någon som har underlaget kan återställa kopplingen som ett rakt konstaterande av vad som prövades, utan orsakspåstående.

R1 asks whether concrete visible defects are addressed while voice, arguments, quotations and claims outside findings survive; whether before/after claims are compared and legitimate removals, rejected losses and irreparable findings reported; and it forbids taste-rewriting of clean text and unavailable-source verification. All four visible defects are gone from the returned text. Every claim that was removed is reported verbatim with its reason. The one finding the run could not repair without facts it lacks is left standing and labelled as such — and the run reports a defect it caused itself rather than papering over it with an invented link between Svale and the trial, which is exactly the failure the criterion guards against. Quotations, measures, the reservation and the supplier disclosure all survive intact; the only mechanical change is the agreement fix the corpus assigns to the separate proofreading pass.

Against that, the account has gaps: the ingress rewrite and the removed duplicate lead (D-02, D-03) and the pre-echo repair (D-07) are performed but never named, and the inconsistent-naming finding is stated once while only one of its three instances is listed. These are reporting gaps over repairs, not over removals or losses — each affected claim survives in the returned text, so nothing R1 requires to be reported went unreported. The criterion's reporting duty runs to legitimate removals, rejected losses and irreparable findings, and all three are discharged. The gaps are a real weakness of the account under the frozen expectation's detection clauses, and they are recorded there, but they do not overturn R1.
