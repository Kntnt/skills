## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is the third paragraph. The input reads:

> Det som retar mig är något annat — att tid i kalendern så lätt behandlas som ett resultat i sig. Timmen är bokad, alltså har något hänt. Det är min reflektion, inte något jag har mätt hos andra.

The returned text reads:

> Det som retar mig är något annat – att vi så lätt behandlar tid i kalendern som ett resultat i sig. Timmen är bokad, alltså har något hänt.

Three of R1's requirements fail on this passage at once.

**Claims outside findings were not preserved.** The reply names no defect inside either deleted sentence, and I can verify none from the input. Both sentences were removed for belonging to a category the run disliked. D4 is hardening in the third form — a sentence whose only work is to limit, deleted outright — and the reply's stated ground for it, that the limit survives in `Det som retar mig`, does not hold: that clause carries no statement about measurement. D1 is the same form, with the same unsupported survival claim.

**A clean text was rewritten to satisfy taste.** D3 changes nothing that was wrong. `att tid i kalendern så lätt behandlas som ett resultat i sig` is correct Swedish, and its agentless passive is load-bearing in a column whose target is an institutional habit rather than a group of colleagues. Naming `vi` as the actor is a stylistic preference applied to working prose, and the reply concedes the cost in its own words — `räckvidden snävades in` — while filing it as a change that cost nothing.

**The two together harden the paragraph.** The input asserts that an unspecified somebody lets calendar time stand as a result, and then says outright that this is the writer's reflection and not a measurement of other people. The returned text asserts that `vi` do it, with the disclaimer gone. What was an openly unmeasured personal impression is now an unqualified statement about a named group. That is a gain in certainty, attribution and scope, and it is exactly the loss R1 is written to catch.

**What the run got right, for the record.** D2 is a correct locale repair — the spaced en dash is the Swedish form — and the two deletions were disclosed rather than hidden, under a heading that exists for the purpose. That candour is real and it is why the deletions are diagnosable at all. It does not offset the losses: the one legitimate correction went unreported, and the two reported ones rest on survival claims the returned text does not support.

**Verdict: R1 fails.**
