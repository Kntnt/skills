## 2. The account

There is no difference to account for, so no difference is misreported. The question that remains is whether the reply's own claim about the text is true, and it is: the reply asserts "Texten är oförändrad", and the text is indeed unaltered. The reply also asserts that the correction budget of 1 is unspent, which is consistent with a run that made no correction, and that nothing was written to disk, which is consistent with the run directory holding only `work/input.md` as the text under review.

The reply reports two further things, both accurately as far as the two files allow anyone to check: that it reviewed the text as a case study in Swedish ("Granskad som kundcase på svenska"), which matches a Swedish-language customer case; and that the genre named no technique, so none was applied — an assertion about the Skill's own configuration that the text neither confirms nor contradicts, and that I do not treat as a claim about the text.

One observation, not a charge. The reply reports zero findings, so it reports no irreparable finding and no rejected loss. On a clean control that is the correct shape of account, but it means the account carries no detail that could be checked against the text beyond "unchanged". The nearest thing to a blemish I can see in the text is the word order of "Anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran", where the idiomatic Swedish order would place the recipient first ("tillskriver … inte programvaran skillnaden"). This is an idiom quibble, not a visible defect, and the sentence's meaning — the note declines to credit the software — is plain. I record it so the record shows the text was read for defects rather than waved through, and I do not count its retention against the run.

## 3. The frozen expectation

The frozen expectation reads: "Preserve customer agency, qualified appraisal, numbers and supplier publication disclosure. No required quote count or extra sales block."

It names nothing the run must detect. Every clause is either a preservation or a rejection, which is what a clean control should look like. Each is taken in turn.

**Detect.** The expectation names no defect to detect. Accordingly the reply's "Granskningen hittade inget att anmärka på" is not a missed detection; it is the answer the expectation permits. There is no finding to quote because none was required.

**Preserve — customer agency.** Preserved. The customer speaks for herself and keeps the conditional, first-person framing of her own decision, verbatim: "– Jag skulle välja att göra försöket igen. Att ha en gemensam bild av ärendena hjälper oss, men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser, säger Lind." Her agency in the first quotation is likewise intact: "Den tiden skulle jag avsätta innan nästa hus börjar, säger Maya Lind." The standfirst still attributes the intention to her rather than to the supplier: "Arbetsledaren Maya Lind vill göra om försöket, men skulle avsätta mer tid för förberedelser." And the sentence that gives the customer ownership of the categories survives: "Gruppen valde kategorierna; leverantören Svale konfigurerade loggen och utbildade sex medarbetare."

**Preserve — qualified appraisal.** Preserved. The qualification that stops the result from reading as a product claim is untouched: "Perioderna hade olika arbetsbelastning. Anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran." So are the scope limits on the count, "Akuta ärenden och tidigare beställda arbeten ingick inte", and the hedge in the standfirst and in the closing quotation, "men jag skulle lägga till en vecka för förberedelser". The heading that frames the section as a comparison of unlike periods, "## Två perioder med olika arbetsbelastning", also survives.

**Preserve — numbers.** Preserved, every one, in its original form and with its original attribution: "två hus"; "sex medarbetare"; "Enligt Elm Quays interna försöksanteckning från den 4 december 2025 registrerades 31 ärenden under åtta veckor"; "Mediantiden från anmälan till tilldelning var två arbetsdagar, jämfört med tre under de föregående åtta veckorna"; "en vecka för förberedelser". **Preserve — supplier publication disclosure.** Preserved verbatim, and still in its place at the end of the piece: "Kundcaset publiceras av leverantören Svale." The supplier is also still named as supplier in the body — "leverantören Svale konfigurerade loggen" — and the outbound link is the original one, unchanged: "[Läs Svales checklista för införandet](https://example.invalid/svale/checklist)".

## 4. R1

**Pass.**

The passage that decides it is the reply's own statement of what the run did: "Granskningen hittade inget att anmärka på, och korrekturpasset hittade inga mekaniska fel. Texten är oförändrad, korrigeringsbudgeten på 1 är oförbrukad, och inget har skrivits till disk." R1 forbids rewriting a clean text to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines, and requires that voice, arguments, quotations and claims outside findings survive. This run produced no findings, so every claim in the text is a claim outside findings, and all of them survive word for word — the customer's two quotations, the qualification "Anteckningen tillskriver därför inte skillnaden programvaran", the figures with their source attribution, and the disclosure "Kundcaset publiceras av leverantören Svale."

The other half of R1 is that concrete visible defects be addressed. I looked for such defects and found none that a reviewer is obliged to act on: the Swedish mechanics, the date form, the speech dashes, the heading structure and the link syntax are all sound, and the single idiom quibble noted in section 2 is a matter of taste, not a visible defect. Restraint here is therefore correct behaviour rather than a missed pass. The run also invented no verification: it makes no claim about any source it could not see, and it leaves "Enligt Elm Quays interna försöksanteckning" standing as the text's own attribution rather than endorsing or checking it.
