## 4. R1

**Pass.**

The passage that decides it is the disclaimer in "Six months, both routes and something to measure". That sentence is where this text is most exposed: it is a refusal to assert, sitting in the paragraph that proposes the trial, immediately after a section attacking the council for not costing anything, and it carries a pronoun the Skill had itself identified as defective. It was the one place where a Skill that mistakes tidying for editing would have cut the sentence as redundant, or turned "We make no claim to have funded or costed that trial" into "We have not costed that trial" and called it plain English. It did neither. It changed the subject and left extent and strength alone — "it still disclaims both the funding and the costing of that trial, and it was neither shortened nor softened", which is true of the delivered text and not merely asserted in the reply.

The rest supports the same verdict. Three findings, all on concrete visible defects locatable in the text without any source: one body called *the officers* in the lead and *the administration* twice thereafter; a first person plural with no antecedent; and system vocabulary ("in two flows") in a general-interest British newspaper piece. The pilot-figures paragraph — the passage carrying the numbers, the report title, the Swedish italics and four consecutive limiting sentences — came back untouched, as did the closing argument and the whole final section. The voice survives: the first-person stance sentences, the concessions ("That is a fair thing to want", "I take it at face value"), the byline. Nothing was rewritten to a word count or a readability target, and nothing was checked against a source the Skill did not have.

Two things push against the pass without reaching it. D1 rewords a claim about a document nobody in this run can see, and does so under a jargon finding, losing "two flows" and gaining "the same"; a repair that kept the claim intact ("enter the details into two separate systems") was available. D4 unifies a name across a claim about a future action where the input gives no warrant for the identity. Both fall inside stated findings, both are reported, neither raises what the text asserts, and D1's scope move is disclosed by the Skill itself rather than buried. R1 asks that claims *outside* findings be preserved; they were, without exception.
