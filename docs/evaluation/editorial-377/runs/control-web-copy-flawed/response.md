## Findings

**Repaired**

1. **The link label contradicted the link.** The page's only action read "Book and pay now", while the last section said the same link opens a form that "places no order, requires no payment details and books no meeting" — it promised exactly the three things the destination refuses. A reader would either abandon the form or leave believing they had booked and paid. The label now states what the destination does.
2. **The title announced nothing about the page.** "Unlock your association's full potential" would have sat unchanged above any offer sold to any association. The title now names the subject the text itself supplies.
3. **The opening sentence was empty.** "Decision-makers in a complex reality face growing complexity." could have been moved in front of a different text without loss, and restated itself inside its own clause. The page now opens on the offer.
4. **The second sentence manufactured drama and promised what the offer does not contain.** "Take the thumb out and discover a fantastic journey." carried an idiom across from another language word for word, and promised an experience the page's own account — a 45-minute video call and a written account — cannot meet.
5. **The headings declared nothing.** "Start", "It", "More", "Again", "Things" and "Important" gave a scanning reader no orientation and three of them were placeholders. The two remaining headings name their contents.
6. **The subject of four sections was never named.** Four consecutive sections opened "It costs", "It covers", "It includes", "It includes", and the pronoun had no antecedent anywhere before it. The opening sentence now names the review, so the later "It includes" has one.
7. **Four coordinate facts were split across four one-sentence sections.** Price, coverage, the call and the written account describe one offer and had to be reassembled from four fragments. They are now one section with the two coordinate items as a list.
8. **What the page actually is came last.** The conditions that decide whether to act at all sat below a call to action that contradicted them. They now precede the link.

**Unresolved — one**

9. **The page never says who provides the review.** "Svale" appears only as the party who replies by email to discuss suitability; the text nowhere states that Svale performs the review, and a reader is asked to pay SEK 4,800 without being told whose service it is. This was left standing deliberately: repairing it means asserting a fact the source text does not contain. Add the provider yourself, or confirm that Svale is the one to name.
