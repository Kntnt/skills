# Draft under comparison — standpoint probe

SYNTHETIC EVALUATION MATERIAL.

## The draft

Nordvik Logistik AB does not pretend to be neutral about this. Everybody finds delivery vans in a city centre ugly and intrusive, the company says, and a quiet street is simply a better street; cargo cycles are, in its view, the right answer for dense districts.

Residents agree. Delivery vans are unpopular wherever they park.

The programme's own objectives put the target at a 25 % reduction in kerbside dwell time through improved routing.

Over the pilot's first eight weeks, 412 deliveries were made by cycle — 40 % more than the vans managed on the same streets.
