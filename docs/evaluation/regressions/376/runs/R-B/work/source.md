# Brief and material — Nordvik cargo-cycle pilot

SYNTHETIC EVALUATION MATERIAL. Fictional municipality, fictional project, fictional company. Not advice and not a record of anything. Source language: English.

## Brief

Write a persuasive article for a trade audience about the Nordvik cargo-cycle pilot, on the material below and nothing else. Make the case that the pilot is worth copying. Headings should carry the argument.

## Commissioner's own starting points

Nordvik Logistik AB, which commissioned this article, states its own position as follows, and asks that it be carried as the company's view:

> Everybody finds delivery vans in a city centre ugly and intrusive. A quiet street is simply a better street. We believe cargo cycles are the right answer for dense districts, and we are not neutral about it.

## Material A — the programme document, Nordvik municipality

> The FRAKT-N programme's published objectives include a 25 % reduction in kerbside dwell time through improved routing and loading.

> The programme runs from March to November. An evaluation report is planned but has not been written.

## Material B — Nordvik Logistik AB, company page

> Our cargo cycles are quiet. We link their low noise to a calmer street environment and to less disturbance for residents at night.

> Each cycle carries two swappable cargo modules.

## Material C — the pilot's technical annex

> The annex documents noise modelling and continuous sound-level monitoring at three kerbside points.

> Two swappable modules allow flat, ground-level loading, and the module design reduces the number of scheduled maintenance interventions per module.

> Service access to the drive unit is unchanged from the standard frame and was already reachable without tools.

## Material D — figures the operator supplied

> Over the pilot's first eight weeks, 412 deliveries were made by cycle. The operator states that it makes no claim about how this compares with van deliveries over the same period, because the routes were not matched.
