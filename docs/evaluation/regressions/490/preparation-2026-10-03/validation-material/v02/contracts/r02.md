A claim removed because the finding named that claim itself remains part of the claim account, and so does every claim a correction left standing with its scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning moved, and every claim a correction added — one the corrected text asserts and the text before it has no counterpart for, an inference drawn from what that text already said among them where it asserts something that text did not say. A claim that has a counterpart in the text before is changed rather than added. A headline, a standfirst and a subheading carry claims like any other passage, and a changed claim is accounted for by what it now asserts rather than how far its wording moved; a correction that took a limit, a connective or a bounding clause out of the text is accounted for by what the reader no longer has, however little of the text went with it. The account is of the text delivered rather than of the text received, and nothing a reviewing Skill says about the claims as a whole contradicts a finding the same reply states.
