# Why meetings should be replaced immediately

In today's fast-paced world, meetings are everywhere, and their impact has never been more important. Teams has spent countless hours in rooms discussing work instead of doing it. Experts agree that this is a substantial opportunity to unlock productivity.

The editor's quoted label is "Focus first", but the draft repeats the same claim without evidence. One thing is clear: every organisation should eliminate recurring meetings immediately. This isn't about calendars — it's about fundamentally reimagining how work gets done.

At the end of the day, the teams that thrive will be those that embrace asynchronous communication. The question isn't whether to change — it's how quickly you can begin.
