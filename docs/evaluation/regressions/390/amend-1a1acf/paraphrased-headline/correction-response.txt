# Library opens from ten until two on Saturdays

The Saturday service will run as a limited trial.

By Alex Reed

For three months, the trial adds Saturday access to the library's existing weekday service. Weekday hours stay unchanged throughout the three months.

## Riverton will review the trial in January

Riverton staff will record Saturday visits during October, November and December. The council will use those counts at its January review to decide whether Saturday opening continues.

## Saturday visitors can use the usual entrance

Saturday opening runs from ten until two. Visitors enter through the same door as on weekdays, and can borrow and return books at the usual desk.
