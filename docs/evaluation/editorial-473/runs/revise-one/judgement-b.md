# Judgement

## 1. Differences

1. Headline. Before: "Tallmora Energi inviger den 3 juni en ny solpark med 12 000 solpaneler vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora". After: "Solpark med 12 000 solpaneler invigs i Tallmora den 3 juni". Class: repair of a visible defect (headline length). It also changes what the headline claims: **attribution** (the sender no longer appears as the one who inaugurates the park, and the verb is now passive) and **scope** (drops "ny" and "vid Kvarnbacken"). The reply reports this change under "Påståenden".
2. Summary, second sentence. Before: "... och beräknas ge 7,5 gigawattimmar el om året, vilket enligt bolagets egen beräkning motsvarar hushållselen i omkring 1 500 villor i kommunen." After: "... och beräknas ge 7,5 gigawattimmar el om året." Class: repair of a visible defect (summary length). The clause goes to the body, as the next item shows.
3. Body, first paragraph. Before: nothing between "... hur soligt året blir." and "Elen levereras ...". After: "Enligt bolagets egen beräkning motsvarar 7,5 gigawattimmar om året hushållselen i omkring 1 500 villor i kommunen." Class: the clause from item 2, moved into the body. It is recast as its own sentence rather than moved word for word ("vilket" becomes "7,5 gigawattimmar om året"). Attribution ("enligt bolagets egen beräkning"), certainty ("omkring") and scope ("i kommunen") are unchanged, and the reply reports the move.
4. Notes block label. Before: "Noter till redaktionen: Parken har 40 växelriktare. ..." After: "Parken har 40 växelriktare. ..." (label removed). Class: repair of a visible defect (notes to the editor put back as background).
5. Picture link. Before: the last sentence of the notes paragraph, "Bilder från solparken finns på https://example.se/press/kvarnbacken-bilder." After: the same sentence as its own paragraph before the contact. Class: a part moved whole.

Nothing else differs. The quotation, the other body paragraph, the background, the contact and the organisation description are word for word the same as in the input.
