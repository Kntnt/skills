# Judgement

## 1. Differences

1. Headline. Before: "Tallmora Energi inviger den 3 juni en ny solpark med 12 000 solpaneler vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora". After: "Tallmora Energi inviger solpark med 12 000 solpaneler den 3 juni". Class: repair of a visible defect (headline length). It drops "ny" and the place from the headline. Both still stand in the summary, and the reply reports the loss.
2. Summary, first sentence. Before: "Tallmora Energi AB inviger den 3 juni 2027 en ny solpark vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora, på den mark där kommunens grustäkt låg fram till 2019." After: "Den 3 juni 2027 inviger Tallmora Energi AB en ny solpark på den mark vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora där kommunens grustäkt låg fram till 2019." Class: change of taste. The reply calls it a repair of repeated headline wording. The claim is unchanged.
3. Summary, second sentence. Before: "..., vilket enligt bolagets egen beräkning motsvarar hushållselen i omkring 1 500 villor i kommunen." After: the sentence ends at "... 7,5 gigawattimmar el om året." Class: repair of a visible defect (summary length). The clause is removed here and moved in item 4.
4. Body, first paragraph. Before: the paragraph opens "Siffran för årsproduktionen är en uppskattning, ...". After: it opens with a new sentence, "Den beräknade årsproduktionen på 7,5 gigawattimmar motsvarar enligt bolagets egen beräkning hushållselen i omkring 1 500 villor i kommunen." Class: repair of a visible defect. This is the clause from item 3, recast as a sentence of its own. It keeps its attribution ("enligt bolagets egen beräkning") and its certainty ("beräknade"). It is not moved whole.
5. History paragraph. Before: a paragraph of its own after "Området är inhägnat ...", reading "Grustäkten vid Kvarnbacken avvecklades 2019. Tallmora Energi fick tillstånd att bygga solparken 2025, och bygget började i augusti 2026." After: the same two sentences, word for word, placed right after the label "Noter till redaktionen:" and joined into one paragraph with the notes. Class: part moved whole (into the notes).

There are no other differences. The quotation, "Området är inhägnat ...", the rest of the notes, the contact and the description are identical.

## 3. D1, the planted defects

- **Headline over 70 characters: fired.** Finding 1 names it: "**Rubriken var för lång.** Den var 97 tecken när texten kom in, och gränsen är 70." The headline is rewritten.
- **Summary over 60 words: fired.** Finding 2 names it: "**Summeringen var för lång.** Den var 62 ord när texten kom in, och gränsen är 60. Jämförelsen med villorna har flyttats till brödtexten."
- **Detail kept below the release as notes to the editor where the background is its place: not fired.** No finding names the notes as the wrong form or place for this detail. The reply treats "Noter till redaktionen:" as the background ("står nu i bakgrunden", finding 4 and the claims section). It even moves more background detail into the notes, so the defect is deeper in the delivered text than in the input.

## 4. P1, the order

**Fail.** Headline, summary, first quotation and body stand in the right order, and so do contact and description. Between the body and the contact, though, the delivered text has notes to the editor, a part the standard does not place. The notes hold the background detail and the image link. The standard wants background to stand as its own part, followed by links and attachments as their own part. The delivered text has no background part and no separate links part, only a notes block in their place.
