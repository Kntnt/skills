## 1. Differences

1. Headline. Before: "Tallmora Energi inviger den 3 juni en ny solpark med 12 000 solpaneler vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora". After: "Tallmora Energi inviger den 3 juni en ny solpark vid Kvarnbacken". Class: repair of a visible defect (headline length). The panel count and "i Tallmora" leave the headline but stand unchanged in the summary. The reply reports this under "Ändrade påståenden".
2. Summary, first sentence, main clause. Before: "Tallmora Energi AB inviger den 3 juni 2027 en ny solpark vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora". After: "Den 3 juni 2027 invigs Tallmora Energi AB:s nya solpark vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora." Class: change to what a claim says, **attribution**. The active "Tallmora Energi AB inviger" becomes a passive with no agent, so the sentence no longer says who inaugurates. The reply reports the change (finding 3 and "Ändrade påståenden"), and the headline still names the company as the one that inaugurates.
3. Summary, first sentence, trailing clause. Before: ", på den mark där kommunens grustäkt låg fram till 2019" (in the summary). After: "Solparken ligger på den mark där kommunens grustäkt låg fram till 2019." at the start of the background paragraph. Class: part moved whole, as the repair of a visible defect (summary length). The only rewording is the subject and verb needed to make the clause a sentence. The reply reports the move (finding 2).
4. Notes. Before: "Noter till redaktionen: Parken har 40 växelriktare. Panelerna står … i närheten." After: "Parken har 40 växelriktare. Panelerna står … i närheten." as its own paragraph directly after the background paragraph. Class: repair of a visible defect (background detail kept as notes to the editor). The label is struck and the sentences are unchanged.
5. Image link. Before: "Bilder från solparken finns på https://example.se/press/kvarnbacken-bilder." inside the notes paragraph. After: the same sentence as its own paragraph before the contact. Class: part moved whole.

No other differences. The two body paragraphs, the contact and the organisation description are unchanged.
