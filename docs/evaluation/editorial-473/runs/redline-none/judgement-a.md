Class: the repair of a visible defect (headline length). The headline no longer gives the panel count or "i Tallmora". Both still stand in the summary, and the reply reports the removal.
- Summary, first sentence: "Tallmora Energi AB inviger den 3 juni 2027 en ny solpark vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora, på den mark där kommunens grustäkt låg fram till 2019." → "Den 3 juni 2027 invigs Tallmora Energi AB:s nya solpark vid Kvarnbacken i Tallmora." The reply reports both points under "Ändrade påståenden". The reply gives the rewording as its own finding (finding 3, repetition of the headline), which is a change of taste.

Class: the repair of a visible defect (summary length). The clause moved, and its content is unchanged. It now stands next to "Grustäkten vid Kvarnbacken avvecklades 2019.", which says nearly the same thing.
