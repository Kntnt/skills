# Notes from the Halldin interview

Eleven vans took a full service in the first year; tyre changes and similar short jobs are not counted in that figure. Two mechanics took the three-day high-voltage course in Gothenburg and volunteered for it, which Halldin says surprised her. A third, Jonas, declined until the work was actually there.

One charger for the whole workshop turned a four-hour job into a two-day one and forced week-ahead planning that the workshop had never done. The charger was bought after the first vans arrived; Halldin's advice is to buy it first.

Her warning to other workshops is not to promise a completion date in the first year, because the customer remembers the date and not the word probably.
