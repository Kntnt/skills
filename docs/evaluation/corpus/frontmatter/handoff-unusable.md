# The stop that came back

Removing the Kvarnvägen stop saved forty seconds a journey and cost the transport committee eight months of correspondence. Thirty-eight of the ninety-six complaints logged during the trial named that stop, and twenty-one of the survey's free-text answers named it as well, which is a level of agreement the committee had not previously seen about anything.

The operator's own recommendation was to restore it, with a Sunday-only omission. That is the recommendation of a body that measured the forty seconds and then read the letters, and it is worth noting that nothing about the forty seconds turned out to be wrong.
