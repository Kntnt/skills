# What the archive costs to keep

The library's digital archive holds 214,000 items and grows by about 9,000 a year. For most of its life the cost of keeping it was invisible: storage got cheaper faster than the archive got bigger, so the line in the budget never moved and nobody asked what was on it.

That stopped being true in 2023. Two things changed at once. The archive began accepting video, which is roughly forty times the size per item of what it held before, and the storage price per terabyte flattened for the first time since the archive was built. The budget line moved by 60 per cent in a year, and it moved in a year when nothing about the service the public sees had changed at all.

So the question in front of the board is not whether the archive is worth keeping, which nobody disputes. It is whether an archive that accepts everything offered to it is the same service as the one the board approved in 2011, and if it is not, who decides what it declines.
