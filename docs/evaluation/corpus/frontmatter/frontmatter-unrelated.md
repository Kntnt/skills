# Three notes on estimating

An estimate that nobody will be held to is a guess, and everyone treats it as one. An estimate that somebody will be held to is a negotiation, and everyone treats it as one of those instead. The interesting estimates are the ones in between, and there are fewer of them than the literature suggests.

The most useful question I have found is not "how long will this take" but "what would have to be true for this to take two days". The answers arrive quickly, they are concrete, and about a third of the time somebody says one of them out loud and immediately knows it is false.

The second note is that a range is only honest if both ends are possible. A range of three to five days, quoted by someone who has already decided it is four, is a single number wearing a costume.
