# Note on the quarter's licensing review

The review was organised in the week beginning 3/4, and the committee's brief was to judge whether our current analysis tools are worth what we pay for them. Two of the four are, in the committee's judgement; the other two were licensed for a project that finished in autumn and have been renewed twice since by default.

The larger of the two costs £14,500 a year. Its defenders argue that cancelling it would mean re-learning a workflow that four people have specialised in, and that the licence includes support we have called on twice. The committee's view was that "support we have called on twice" is an argument for a support contract rather than for a licence, and that the re-learning cost is real but is paid once.

The minute records the committee's summary of both renewals as "renewed twice by default". The recommendation is to cancel both at the next renewal date, to keep the data export from each, and to revisit in a year if anyone can show the work has got harder.
