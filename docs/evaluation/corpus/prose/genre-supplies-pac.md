# The tool library in its fourth year

The tool library occupies two rooms behind the laundry of a housing association of 380 households, and has been open since March 2021. It lends hand tools, power tools, garden equipment, and a small number of larger items to any household in the association, and it charges nothing for a loan.

Stock at the last count, taken on 12 January against the catalogue, was 412 items in six categories: 168 hand tools, 94 power tools, 71 items of garden equipment, 43 ladders and other access equipment, 24 measuring and testing instruments, and 12 items the catalogue lists as miscellaneous. The count is taken once a year, and the January figure is the fourth of them.

Membership stands at 296 households, of which 190 borrowed at least one item during the twelve months to 31 December. The booking system recorded 1,412 loans over that period. Monthly loans ran from 61 in December to 187 in May. The mean loan lasted 2.4 days, and 38 loans were returned more than a week after their due date.

The rooms open on Tuesday evenings from six until eight and on Saturday mornings from nine until one, which is 312 opening hours in the year. Two members are paid for four hours a week each to run the openings, and eleven volunteers logged 640 hours between them over the same twelve months.

Repairs are made in the rooms where the tools are kept. Thirty-one items were repaired during the year, nine were replaced after a repair was attempted, and four were written off as beyond repair and not replaced. The association's budget for the library was 24,000 kronor, of which 21,300 was spent: 14,800 on replacements and new stock, 4,200 on consumables and spare parts, and 2,300 on insurance.

The stock, repair, and expenditure figures come from the catalogue and the association's ledger. The loan, membership, and overdue figures come from the booking system. The volunteer hours come from the sign-in sheet kept inside the door.
