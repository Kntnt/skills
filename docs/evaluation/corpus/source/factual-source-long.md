Fagervik kommun — utvärdering av linje 4, försöksperiod 2024. Invented material, written for evaluation use; nothing in it describes a real municipality, service, or person.

## Background

Line 4 connects the Fagervik ferry terminal with the hospital, the upper-secondary school, and the northern housing area at Rönnhöjden. Before the trial it ran twice an hour between 06:00 and 19:00 on weekdays, and once an hour on Saturdays. It did not run on Sundays. The route had 41 stops and took 38 minutes end to end in the timetable, and 44 minutes in practice according to the operator's own vehicle logs for the whole of 2023.

The municipal transport committee decided on 12 October 2023 to run a twelve-month trial on line 4, beginning 1 January 2024. The trial had three parts, adopted together and never separated: a Sunday service at one departure an hour, six stops removed from the route, and a fare cut from 32 to 20 kronor for a single journey. The committee's own minutes record that the fare cut was added late in the discussion, at the request of two members, and that no separate budget line was created for it.

## What was measured

The operator counted boardings automatically at every door. The counts are complete for the whole trial except for the period 3 to 19 April, when the counting equipment on two of the six vehicles was out of service; the operator's report treats those seventeen days as missing rather than as zero, and the figures below exclude them.

Total boardings in 2024 were 412,000, against 331,000 in 2023. Sunday boardings, which had no 2023 equivalent, were 18,400. Weekday boardings rose from 268,000 to 312,000. Saturday boardings were almost unchanged, at 63,000 against 62,600.

End-to-end journey time fell from 44 minutes to 39 minutes, measured the same way from the same vehicle logs. The six removed stops had accounted for 2.1 per cent of boardings in 2023.

Fare revenue fell from 10.6 million kronor to 8.2 million. The municipality's subsidy rose by 3.1 million kronor over the year, of which the operator's report attributes 2.4 million to the fare cut and 0.7 million to the Sunday service. The report states plainly that this split is an estimate, because the three changes took effect on the same day.

## The satisfaction survey

A postal and online survey was sent to 4,000 households in the route's catchment in November 2024. The response rate was 18 per cent. Of those who responded, 71 per cent said the service had improved, 6 per cent said it had worsened, and the remainder said it was unchanged or gave no answer. The survey report itself states that respondents to a transport survey are more likely than non-respondents to use the service, and that the satisfaction figure should therefore not be read as a figure for the catchment as a whole.

The free-text answers most often mentioned the Sunday service. The removed stops appear in 34 answers, of which 29 are negative; 21 of those 29 name the same stop, Kvarnvägen, which served a care home.

## Complaints and incidents

The municipality logged 96 complaints about line 4 during the trial, against 71 in 2023. The largest single category was the removal of the Kvarnvägen stop, with 38 complaints. Punctuality complaints fell from 29 to 12.

There were no reportable safety incidents on the route during the trial. In 2023 there had been one, a passenger falling while boarding at the ferry terminal.

## What the operator recommends

The operator's final report of 14 January 2025 recommends keeping the Sunday service and the shortened route, and restoring the Kvarnvägen stop with a Sunday-only omission. It does not make a recommendation about the fare, on the ground that the fare is a political decision about who should pay rather than an operational one, and it notes that it has no way to separate the fare effect from the Sunday effect in the boarding figures.

The transport committee has not yet decided. Its chair, Ingrid Salomonsson, is quoted in the report's covering letter saying that the committee will take the fare question to the full council because of the size of the subsidy increase.

## Known limits of this material

The three changes were introduced simultaneously, so no causal claim about any one of them can be supported by the boarding figures alone. Seventeen days of counting are missing. The satisfaction figure rests on an 18 per cent response rate. No comparison with any other route in the municipality was made, and no attempt was made to control for the opening of the new ferry service in March 2024, which the operator's report mentions once and does not quantify.
