Write an article about why our support team stopped using a shared inbox.

Audience: readers of a trade publication for customer-service managers, who know the tools and do not need them explained. Length: about 700 words.

What happened. Four agents worked one shared inbox for two years. Two of them told us in a retrospective on 14 January that they routinely opened a message, saw no assignee, and left it, assuming a colleague had it; the retrospective notes record eleven messages that went unanswered for more than three working days in December. We moved to per-agent assignment on 3 February, with an unassigned queue that anybody may claim and a rule that a claimed message is answered or handed back the same day.

Since February the longest unanswered time has been one working day, measured over nine weeks. The team is the same size. One agent, Miriam Adler, said in the March retrospective that the change mostly removed the question of whether something was hers.

We do not know whether the improvement would survive a larger team, and we have not compared this with any other approach.
