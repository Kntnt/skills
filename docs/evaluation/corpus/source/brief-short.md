Write a short piece for our engineering blog about replacing our weekly status meeting with a written update.

Audience: other small software teams. Length: about 400 words.

What happened. The meeting ran forty-five minutes for six people, and most of that time each person was listening to work they were not involved in. We replaced it with a written update posted every Monday before 10:00, and kept an optional fifteen-minute call on Thursdays for anything that needed a conversation. After three months, two of the six said they missed the informal chat at the start of the old meeting. Nobody asked to go back.

We have not measured whether anything shipped faster.
