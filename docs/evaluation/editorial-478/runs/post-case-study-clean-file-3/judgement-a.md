## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is finding 3, which rewrote a working opening sentence:

> `Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.`

It became `Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna i sin gemensamma reparationslogg.`

The original is grammatical, idiomatic Swedish. It is a deliberate fronted construction that opens the case on the goal, and the reply concedes that its emphasis is now lost. Rewriting it is a change of taste in a text the corpus calls conforming, and that is what R1 forbids.

Finding 1 is the most defensible of the three, since the headline credits the organisation with an appraisal that one employee made with a reservation. But a headline attributing an employee's quote to the organisation is common news practice, not a concrete defect. The fix also cost the trial framing ("loggförsöket" became "loggen") and broke the 60-character headline limit the text met.

Finding 2 repairs a marginal referent issue and pushes the standfirst past its 40-word figure.

The reply is honest about every change it made, apart from the standfirst count. That honesty does not cure the rewriting of a clean text.
