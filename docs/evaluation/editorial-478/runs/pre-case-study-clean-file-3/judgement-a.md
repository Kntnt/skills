## 1. Differences

     - The goal moves from the group seeing the information ("Att se … var vad underhållsgruppen ville få ut av") to the log showing it ("ville att den skulle visa").
     - The possessive "sin gemensamma reparationslogg" becomes "en gemensam reparationslogg", so the body no longer says the log was the group's own.
     - A new assertion enters the body: "gjorde ett försök med". The headline ("loggförsöket") and the standfirst ("provade … under åtta veckor") already say this, so the fact is not new to the text. It is new to this sentence, and the actor has changed from Elm Quay Housing to the maintenance group.
   - This is not a mechanical correction: the original sentence has no spelling, punctuation or grammar error.

**Fail.** The decision rests on R1's clause "Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste."

The frozen expectation treats this control as a clean text: it conforms to the anatomy, and every other clause is about preservation or rejection. The run still rewrote the opening sentence on the strength of two findings that do not name a concrete visible defect:

- **Finding 1** ("byggd som en engelsk konstruktion … lästes brödtextens första mening som översatt") is a preference about word order. "X var vad Y ville få ut av Z" is grammatical Swedish, and the sentence works.
- **Finding 2** ("'Försöket' saknade en referent i brödtexten") rests on reading the body as if the headline ("loggförsöket") and the standfirst ("provade en gemensam reparationslogg i två hus under åtta veckor") did not stand directly above it. In the text as published, "Försöket" has an obvious referent.

- "ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg" (the group wanted to see the information, in its own log) became "ville att den skulle visa" (the log was to show it)
- "sin" was lost.

The run does a lot well. It reports every change accurately, it preserves every item the expectation names, and it makes none of the named rejected findings. Those strengths do not cure an unneeded change of meaning to a clean text.
