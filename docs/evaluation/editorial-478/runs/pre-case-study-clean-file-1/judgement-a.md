# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

`diff work/input.md work/output.md` shows exactly two changed lines. There is no frontmatter in either file. The byline, the subheadings, the body, the quotes, the formatting and the link are byte-identical.

1. Headline. Before: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quay om loggförsöket` (59 characters). After: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger arbetsledare om loggförsöket` (63 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, **attribution**. The organisation Elm Quay was the speaker. Now an unnamed supervisor (arbetsledare) is the speaker. The change was offered as the repair of a visible defect: in the body the appraisal comes from one person, Maya Lind, not from the organisation. That case can be made, but it is a borderline one. In Swedish headlines, "säger <organisation>" is an accepted shorthand when a named employee speaks for her team, and Lind's quote says "hjälper oss". The side effects: the customer is no longer named in the headline, and the headline now breaks the 60-character norm that the received text met.
2. Standfirst, third sentence. Before: `Här är vad gruppen gjorde, …`. After: `Här är vad underhållsgruppen gjorde, …`. Class: the repair of a visible defect, a small one. Inside the standfirst, "gruppen" has no explicit antecedent. The change narrows the reference to the group that the body names. The standfirst stays at 40 words. No claim changes, because the body already says the maintenance group ran the trial.

There are no mechanical corrections.
