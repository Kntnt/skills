## 1. Differences

There are two differences between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. A line diff confirms that the frontmatter (there is none), the byline, the body, the subheadings, the quotations, the numbers, the disclosure, the link and the formatting are all byte-identical.

1. **Headline (line 1).** Before: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quay om loggförsöket` (59 characters). After: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger arbetsledare om loggförsöket` (63 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, specifically **attribution**. The speaker of the headline's appraisal moves from the customer organisation, Elm Quay, to an unnamed "arbetsledare". The customer drops out of the headline altogether. As a side effect, a headline that met the 60-character limit now breaks it.
2. **Standfirst (line 3).** Before: `Här är vad gruppen gjorde, …`. After: `Här är vad underhållsgruppen gjorde, …` (40 words before and after). Class: a change of taste, at most a marginal repair. The standfirst's actors are Elm Quay Housing and Maya Lind, and "gruppen" reads naturally as their team. The change only makes the reference more specific. What the sentence claims is unchanged.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The deciding passage is finding 1 and its result. The run changed the headline from "säger Elm Quay" to "säger arbetsledare", and the reply records that "Kunden Elm Quay nämns inte längre i rubriken" and that the headline is now "63 tecken mot normens högst 60".

The text was clean, and a headline that attributes a statement to the organisation its spokesperson works for is a working journalistic convention, not a visible defect. The rewrite changes the attribution of a claim and removes the customer agency that the expectation names for preservation. It also breaks a limit the original met.

R1 says clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste. This is a rewrite of that kind, and one that also loses something the expectation requires to be kept. The standfirst change is harmless taste, and every other preserved element survives intact. That does not outweigh the headline loss.
