**2. Claim changes the account misses**

- **Lead, last sentence: attribution of what the trial required.**
  - **Before:** "Vad det krävde, och vad det gav, går att följa i gruppens egna anteckningar."
  - **After:** "Vad det krävde berättar arbetsledaren Maya Lind, och vad som registrerades går att följa i en intern försöksanteckning."
  - **What the difference did:** the delivered text now says Lind tells everything about what the trial required. That includes content the text as it arrived gave to the group's notes. It also includes content the body gives in the narrator's own voice: that the categories were the group's own, that Svale configured the log after them, and that Svale trained six staff. Lind tells none of this in the delivered text. The attribution has therefore moved both from the notes and from the narrator to Lind.

It names only the notes as the voice the content was taken from and leaves out the narrator. It also adds "Utöver det som räknas upp ovan har inget påstående ändrats." It reports the change short.
