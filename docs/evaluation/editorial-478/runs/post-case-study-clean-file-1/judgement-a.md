# Judgement a

## 1. Differences

There is no frontmatter in either file. The standfirst, byline, subheadings, formatting and every other paragraph are byte-identical. There are two differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quay om loggförsöket` (59 characters). After: `# Gemensam ärendebild hjälper gruppen, säger Elm Quays arbetsledare` (65 characters). Class: this changes what a claim says. It changes **attribution**, moving the speaker from the organisation Elm Quay to "Elm Quays arbetsledare". It also changes **scope** twice: "gruppen" adds a beneficiary that the headline's reading of Lind's "oss" supplies, and dropping "om loggförsöket" removes what the appraisal is about. The original headline was not a visible defect. The appraisal is spoken on Elm Quay's behalf by its arbetsledare, the standfirst names her in the next line, and attributing a quote to the customer organisation is ordinary practice in customer-case headlines. That makes this a change of taste that also alters a claim. Side effect: the headline goes from 59 characters, which meets the 60-character norm, to 65, which breaks it.
2. **First sentence of the opening paragraph.** Before: `Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg.` After: `Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville med sin gemensamma reparationslogg kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna.` Class: change of taste, with a slight change of **meaning**. The fronted infinitive with "var vad" is idiomatic and readable Swedish, not a visible defect. The rewrite turns "this was what the group wanted to get out of the log" (the log's purpose) into "the group wanted, with the log, to be able to see" (a wish). The result is no clearer than the original.

It also says openly that the new headline is 65 characters, over the 60 norm. One justification is inaccurate: the reply says "om loggförsöket" was dropped "så att rubriken håller sig inom 70 tecken". The original headline was 59 characters and already well inside that limit. The length problem was created by the rewrite itself.
2. **First sentence.** Reported as finding 2, and accurately. The reply quotes both versions and, under "Påståenden", correctly says the sentence no longer presents the information as the log's purpose.

The reply's statement that nothing else changed and that the proofreading pass made no changes matches the diff.
