## 1. Differences

There are two differences between `work/input.md` and `work/output.md`. The frontmatter (none), the standfirst, the byline, the subheadings, the body after the first sentence of the opening paragraph, the quotations, the numbers, the disclosure and the link are byte-identical.

1. **Headline.** Before: "Gemensam ärendebild hjälper, säger Elm Quay om loggförsöket" (59 characters). After: "Gemensam ärendebild hjälper gruppen, säger Elm Quays arbetsledare" (65 characters). Class: a change to what a claim says, in **attribution** (from the organisation to its arbetsledare) and **scope** ("om loggförsöket" is gone, so the headline no longer says the verdict is about the trial; "gruppen" narrows who it helps). This is not the repair of a visible defect. A customer-case headline that credits a statement by the customer's manager to the customer organisation is ordinary headline metonymy, and the body names the speaker straight away. The change also takes a headline that met the 60-character norm and makes it break that norm.
2. **First sentence of the opening paragraph.** Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." After: "Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville med sin gemensamma reparationslogg kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna." Class: a change of taste, with a slight change to **meaning** (the original states the log's purpose: "var vad … ville få ut av"; the new sentence states only a wish). The pseudo-cleft with the fused relative "vad" (= "det som") is standard, idiomatic Swedish. It is not a grammatical or visible defect.

There are no mechanical corrections.
