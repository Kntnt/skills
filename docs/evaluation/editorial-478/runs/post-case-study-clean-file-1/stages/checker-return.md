**1. False statements**

1. Statement: "Inledningsstyckets första mening (fynd 2) påstår samma sak som förut: att underhållsgruppen ville kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna med sin gemensamma reparationslogg." The account repeats this when it says "Utöver dessa har inga påståenden tagits bort, ändrats eller lagts till."
   - Text: the delivered text, compared with the text as it arrived.
   - Contradicting passages: the text as it arrived said "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg." The delivered text says "Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville med sin gemensamma reparationslogg kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna."
   - What is true: the old sentence was a pseudo-cleft (the reply calls it one in finding 2). That construction says shared visibility was *what* the group wanted to get out of the log, so it was the aim of the log. The new sentence only says the group wanted, among other things, to be able to see that information with the log. The claim that this was the aim is gone, so the sentence does not say "samma sak som förut", and the sentence after it ("inga påståenden … ändrats") is false for this passage.

**2. Claim changes the account misses**

1. Lead, first sentence.
   - Before: "Att se samma information över skiftgränserna var vad underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville få ut av sin gemensamma reparationslogg."
   - After: "Underhållsgruppen på Elm Quay Housing ville med sin gemensamma reparationslogg kunna se samma information över skiftgränserna."
   - What the change did: the earlier text named shared visibility across shifts as the thing the group wanted from the log, meaning its aim. The delivered text states it as one wish the group had for the log, with no claim that it was the aim. The meaning of the claim has moved: the exhaustive framing ("var vad … ville få ut av") is lost.
   - What the account says: it reports the sentence as unchanged in claim: "påstår samma sak som förut", and "Utöver dessa har inga påståenden tagits bort, ändrats eller lagts till."
