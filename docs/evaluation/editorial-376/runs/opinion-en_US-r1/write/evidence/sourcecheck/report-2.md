## 4. Term-crossing checks (Swedish → English, outside quoted speech)

Each term was tested for whether something in this context could fall under one term and not the other.

- **"föreningslokaler" / "lokaler" → "community rooms" (L5, and "rooms" at L9, L13, L19).** "Lokal" covers premises of any size — a hall, a gym, a clubroom — and "förenings-" marks them as booked by associations. "Community rooms" reads to an American reader as rooms open to the community, and "room" understates a hall. A hall bookable only by registered associations falls under "föreningslokal" but not comfortably under "community room". The narrowing carries no argumentative weight here: the piece's claims concern how the seven are booked, not what they are, and every count attached to them ("seven", "two") is exact. **Note, not a finding** — recorded because a later editorial pass may prefer "community premises" or "the seven premises local clubs book".
- **"funktionsförmåga" → "ability" (L9).** "Funktionsförmåga" is functional ability (the disability dimension); bare "ability" is broader. In the run of "neither age nor ability nor digital habits" an ordinary reader takes the disability sense, and the sentence's work — that the report holds no user attribute at all — is unchanged. **Note, not a finding.** "functional ability" would be tighter.
- **"digital vana" → "digital habits" (L9).** "Vana" is accustomedness or practised familiarity; "habits" leans to frequency of use. Both name a user attribute the report does not hold, and the claim is a negative over that whole area. **Note, not a finding.**
- **"tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" → "how long each route takes per booking" (L19).** A genuine change of what is measured. **F3.**
- **"handlingarna" → "the documents" (L15).** Exact in scope: the case papers before the board. **OK.**
- **"tjänsteutlåtandet" → "the staff recommendation" (L13).** Names the same document and keeps it as a recommendation rather than a decision. **OK.**
- **"kommunstyrelsen" → "the municipal executive board" (L5) / "the board".** Standard rendering; no power or status is added. **OK.**
- **"dubbel administration" → "double administration" (L13).** Carried in the source's own terms. **OK.**
- **"Öppna beslut"** left untranslated as a proper name. **OK.**
- **"från september" → "in September" / "the September removal" (L5, L13, L25).** "From September" is the start of a state; "in September" is when it happens. Both place the removal at September and neither attaches a date within the month. **OK.**

## 6. Checked and deliberately not reported

Recorded so the judgement is visible rather than silent.

- **(a) Tense/modality of the proposed trial (L19): "the administration records … and asks …" for "ska förvaltningen registrera … och be …".** The indicative present sits inside a sentence frame that has already marked the whole thing as a proposal ("Öppna beslut proposes a six-month trial … During it, …"), and the referent of "it" is a trial that does not exist. No reader takes it as a report of current practice, and the section heading ("Six months that would produce …") holds the conditional. Defensible as written. If an editor wants the modality visible at clause level, "would record … and would ask" is the minimal change, but it is not a defect.
- **(b) "before anyone has weighed" (L21) for "kommunen först måste kunna väga".** The source gives her position as: the municipality must first *be able to* weigh work cost against user value. The draft states it as: settling before the weighing has been *done*. These could come apart where the figures exist but no one has performed the comparison — except that the material forecloses that case in this context by stating no time measurement or savings calculation exists in the papers at all, so being able to weigh and having weighed are not separable here. It is also carried as her standpoint, which the brief gives as hers ("Hon menar att …"). Defensible as written.
- **(c) "the board can remove the phone channel" (L25) narrowing "en kanal".** A narrowing of a stated permission, not a contradiction of it; the phone channel is the one under decision. Defensible as written.
- **(d) Dropping "Kommunens" before "pilotrapport" (L9).** An omitted descriptor, not an altered claim; no rival owner is implied, and the surrounding sentences place the report inside the municipal process. Defensible as written.
- **(e) Length.** The brief sets "ungefär 400 ord"; the draft runs a little over 420. The stated length has not been used to license any addition: every factual claim traces to the material, and the piece adds argument, not material. Length itself is an editorial matter outside this comparison.

## 7. Findings

Three. Each is a defect to repair before delivery; all three are **source-support** findings. There are **no translation findings** (see §5).

### F1 — The 18 June meeting is stated as the decision occasion (L5, and the heading at L23). Low severity.

**Draft:** "On 18 June the municipal executive board decides whether phone booking of Lervik's seven community rooms ends in September." Heading: "What the board can decide on 18 June."

**Source:** "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september."

**What differs — modality and event.** The material establishes that a staff recommendation stands *before* the board's meeting of 18 June and proposes removal from September. It does not state that the board decides the matter at that meeting. The draft converts "a recommendation is before the meeting" into "the board decides at the meeting".

**Case that stands:** the item is taken up on 18 June — presented, debated, referred back for the missing time figures or remitted for consultation — and the decision falls at a later meeting, with September removal still the proposal on the table. Nothing supplied excludes this; I can quote no statement that does. The brief's framing (that the board "bör skjuta upp" the switch, and that the ending name "kommunstyrelsens möjliga beslut") makes 18 June the occasion the piece addresses, which is why this reads naturally — but the occasion being addressed is not the same as the decision being taken there. Noted as low severity for that reason.

**Smallest supported repair:** L5, "decides whether" → "takes up whether" (or "considers whether"). The heading at L23 rests on the same premise; "What the board can decide on 18 June" → "What the board can do on 18 June" removes it. Nothing else in the piece depends on the distinction: the ask ("Postpone the September removal and order the trial") works either way.

### F2 — "for the first time" extends an absence beyond the documents (L25). Low severity.

**Draft:** "… and say for the first time what that choice costs and what it buys."

**Source:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna."

**What differs — scope of the negative.** The material states the absence within the case documents. "For the first time" asserts that no one has ever stated the cost, in any context.

**Case that stands:** a cost estimate for the two booking flows was produced earlier — in the procurement of the web system, or a budget memo — and simply does not appear among the papers before the board. The material is compatible with this: it locates the absence in "handlingarna" and nowhere wider. No supplied statement excludes it.

**Smallest supported repair:** delete "for the first time" — "and say what that choice costs and what it buys" carries the same rhetorical close. If the emphasis is wanted, tie it to the documents: "and say, as the June papers never did, what that choice costs and what it buys."

### F3 — The trial's measurement is specified more finely than the proposal does (L19). Low severity.

**Draft:** "During it, the administration records how long each route takes per booking …"

**Source:** "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg …"

**What differs — the thing measured.** The source specifies time consumption broken down *per booking route*. The draft specifies a per-booking figure for each route. Per-booking timing is one way to satisfy the source, not what it says.

**Case that stands:** the administration logs total staff hours spent on the phone route and on the web route across the six months, with no per-booking timing kept. That records "tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" and does not record "how long each route takes per booking". Nothing supplied excludes it — and the distinction is live in the piece, because this sentence is the specification of the trial the board is being asked to order.

**Smallest supported repair:** "records how long each route takes per booking" → "records the time each route takes" (or "records time spent per booking route"). The argument is unaffected: either way the trial produces the time figure §2 says the papers lack.
