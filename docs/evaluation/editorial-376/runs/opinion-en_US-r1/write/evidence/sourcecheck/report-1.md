## 2. Pronouns: person, number, natural gender

Accounted separately from referent identification.

- **Draft "I" (5×), "we"/"our" (1×: "We have not costed").** First person singular is Sanna Ek; first person plural is Öppna beslut. The material supplies both standpoints (”Sanna Eks ståndpunkt”, ”Föreningen gör inget anspråk …”), and the byline makes her the writer, so the person shift from the material's third person is warranted. The plural "we" for a single named association asserts no additional membership claim beyond ”föreningen”.
- **Natural gender.** The material asserts feminine natural gender for Sanna Ek (”Hon motsätter sig inte …”, ”Hon menar att …”). The draft carries her statements in the first person and uses no gendered pronoun for her; it therefore neither asserts nor contradicts that feature. No repair is owed: nothing in the draft misgenders her, and the brief attaches no requirement that her gender be conveyed.
- **Grammatical vs natural gender.** ”Därefter kan **den** avgöra …” is Swedish common-gender agreement with *kommunstyrelsen*, a body. It carries no natural-gender information, and the draft's "the board" correctly asserts none.
- **”de” in ”varför **de** väljer telefon eller webb”** → draft "why **they** chose". Third person plural, users, no natural gender in either language. Number preserved.
- **Draft "it" (the report; the board; the channel), "its" (the report's reason; the channel's users).** Third person singular for institutions and objects; no natural gender asserted, none available in the material.
- **Draft "you" in the title.** Second person addressed to the deciding body; imports no claim about any person.
- **Draft "someone", "nobody", "anyone".** Indefinite, number-neutral, no gender. ”någon” in the source is likewise indefinite and ungendered; the draft's "someone cannot use the web" preserves that.
- **”personalen”/"staff", ”invånarna”/"residents", ”användarna”/"users".** Number and definiteness carried in each case; "what share of residents" preserves ”hur stor andel av invånarna”.

## 3. Translation check (meaning and voice of rendered speech)

The one passage of the commissioning party's speech is rendered as the author's own prose:

> ”Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben. Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används.”
> → "A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone cannot use the web. It is a reason to find out why the phone is still being used."

Meaning, stance, certainty and the distinctive two-beat structure (denial of proof, then the redirection to a question) are all expressed in idiomatic American English rather than Swedish syntax. ”går via telefon” becomes "comes in by phone", which is how the arrival of a booking is said in English; ”ta reda på” becomes "find out"; ”fortfarande” is kept. Nothing is added, narrowed or sharpened, no implicit referent has been filled in beyond what the material settles, and no self-correction or hedge has been dropped. **No translation finding.**

Other cross-language observations, none of them obstructions to the source's reading, and none reported as defects:

- *funktionsförmåga* → "ability". Broader in isolation; disambiguated by the surrounding series. A closer rendering would be "physical or cognitive ability", but the present wording is defensible, and this is a preference, not an obstruction.
- *föreningslokaler* → "community rooms". The Swedish names premises let to associations; "community rooms" is wider in the abstract, but the referent here is fixed to the seven rooms at issue and the report title is retained in Swedish, so no case stands in which one term applies and the other does not.
- *tjänsteutlåtande* → "staff recommendation", *kommunstyrelsen* → "municipal executive board", *förvaltningen* → "the administration", *handlingarna* → "the documents". Each names the same thing in this context.

## 4. Findings

Ordered by weight. Each is a defect to repair before delivery; all are source-support findings, not translation findings.

### F1 — "24 calls" counts something the material does not count (line 9)

**Draft:** "Those 24 calls therefore cannot tell the board what share of residents is unable to book digitally."
**Material:** ”Under försöket gjordes 96 bokningar via webb och 24 via telefon.” / ”Telefonbokningarna kan därför inte användas för att säga …” / ”Rapporten räknar bokningar, inte unika personer.”
**Missing support:** The figure 24 is a count of *bookings* made by phone. The material nowhere gives a number of calls, and expressly warns that the report counts bookings rather than people.
**Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** one resident telephones once and books three different rooms for a season. The 24 phone bookings then arose from fewer than 24 calls. Nothing in the material excludes this — indeed the material's own caution that bookings are not unique people points the same way — and the draft's sentence is in the very paragraph arguing that the count must not be read as a count of something else.
**Smallest repair:** "Those 24 bookings therefore cannot tell the board …"

### F2 — a claim about the administration's objection becomes a claim about the documents' contents (line 13)

**Draft:** "That objection is double administration, and I will not pretend it is anything meaner: nothing in the documents calls the people who phone lazy or expensive."
**Material:** ”Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra.”
**Changed support:** The material establishes what the administration's real objection *is* and *is not*. The draft's clause after the colon asserts instead what the documents do or do not *say* — a claim about their text that the material does not make. The material's one statement about what the documents lack is specific and different: ”Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna.”
**Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the tjänsteutlåtande contains a passing line about telephone handling being the costlier route for staff, while the administration's actual objection to keeping both channels remains double administration. The source sentence still holds; the draft's "nothing in the documents calls the people who phone … expensive" does not. Nothing in the material excludes such a line.
**Smallest repair:** replace the clause after the colon with the material's own contrast — "the objection is not that the people who phone are lazy or expensive."

### F3 — "two of the rooms" asserts a subset the material does not state (line 9)

**Draft:** "covers an eight-week trial in two of the rooms"
**Material:** ”gäller ett åtta veckor långt försök i två lokaler” (against ”alla sju lokaler” in the separate passage about the staff proposal).
**Missing support:** The material gives two premises for the pilot and seven for the proposed removal, but never says the two are among the seven.
**Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the pilot ran in two premises that are not among the seven now at issue — a larger hall and a study room, say. ”i två lokaler” still holds; "two of the rooms" does not. No supplied statement excludes it.
**Smallest repair:** "an eight-week trial in two rooms".

### F4 — a disclaimer is stated as a fact (line 19)

**Draft:** "We have not costed that trial and make no claim to fund it"
**Material:** ”Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket.”
**Changed support:** The material says the association makes no claim to have costed or financed the trial. The draft asserts as fact that no costing was done, and converts financing from something not claimed to have happened (”ha finansierat”) into a prospective offer declined ("to fund it"). The material supports the disclaimer, not the underlying fact, and the brief's boundaries make the cost question the one place uncertainty must be preserved (”med kostnadsosäkerheten bevarad”).
**Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the association has a rough internal estimate it does not stand behind publicly. It then still ”gör inget anspråk på att ha … kostnadsberäknat försöket”, while "We have not costed that trial" is false.
**Smallest repair:** "We make no claim to have costed or funded that trial".

### F5 — "the staff time" narrows the material's "arbetskostnaden" (line 21)

**Draft:** "before anyone has weighed the staff time against what the channel is worth to its users"
**Material:** ”Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna.”
**Changed support:** ”Arbetskostnaden” is the cost of the work; "the staff time" is one input to it. The narrowing matters because this sentence states the author's own test for the decision.
**Case in which the draft fails and the source holds:** the cost of keeping the phone route includes a licence or handset line and a half-day of training alongside the minutes per booking. Weighing only the staff time then leaves ”arbetskostnaden” unweighed, so her stated condition is unmet while the draft's is met. Nothing supplied excludes such items, and the material contains no cost breakdown at all.
**Smallest repair:** "the cost of the work against what the channel is worth to its users".

## 5. Editorial questions (not findings, no repair proposed)

- **"On 18 June the municipal executive board decides whether …" (line 5), and the heading "What the board can decide on 18 June" (line 23).** The material supplies a board meeting on that date and a staff proposal written for it; it does not state that the question is settled at that meeting. But the brief's own thesis (”kommunstyrelsen bör skjuta upp …”) and its judging boundary (”Slutet ska namnge kommunstyrelsens möjliga beslut”) both take the board as the body that decides this. The line between the brief's presupposition and an added fact is genuinely unsettled here, so this is reported as **the brief's own unevidenced premise**, adopted by the draft, rather than as a defect. If the commissioning party wants it narrowed, "the proposal before the board on 18 June" would do it without touching anything else.
- **"A channel closed on an unmeasured inconvenience" (heading, line 11).** Read alone, the heading can be taken to say the channel is already closed; read as an ordinary reader would in context, the section's first sentence ("proposes removing") settles it. Flagged for the editor's eye only.
