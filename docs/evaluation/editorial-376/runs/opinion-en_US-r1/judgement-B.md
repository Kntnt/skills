## 3. F1 — source discipline, on the text the user received

**Fail** — narrowly, on the three disclosed defects. Nothing invented, no fabricated study, protest, legal requirement, discrimination case, party motive or saving appears anywhere; the failures are three phrase-level over-reaches, each of which a reader would take as fact.

Unsupported as written:

1. **Added event and chronology.** "On 18 June the municipal executive board decides whether phone booking of Lervik's seven community rooms ends in September", and the heading "What the board can decide on 18 June." The material gives only "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september." A staff recommendation standing before a meeting is not a decision taken at it; the item could be presented, debated and referred back, with the decision falling later. The claim is made twice, once in the lead sentence a reader trusts most.
2. **Widened scope of a negative.** "and say for the first time what that choice costs and what it buys." The material places the absence inside the case papers ("finns inte i **handlingarna**"). "For the first time" asserts that the cost has never been stated anywhere — an earlier procurement or budget estimate outside the papers is not excluded by anything supplied. This crosses the unknown/absent line the criterion names.
3. **Changed thing measured.** "the administration records how long each route takes per booking" for "registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg". Reverse test: total staff hours logged for the phone route and for the web route over six months falls under the source's term and not the draft's. The distinction is live because this sentence is the specification of the trial the board is asked to order.

Checked and found supported, including the places where this brief's criterion bites:

- **The three unmeasured variables.** "it measures neither age nor ability nor digital habits" for "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana." The third item is **habits**, not skill or ability — the criterion's requirement is met. On the second item, "ability" is broader in isolation than *funktionsförmåga* (functional ability), so in the abstract something — language competence, say — could fall under the draft's term and not the source's; in the run "age … ability … digital habits" the disability reading is the one an ordinary reader takes, and the sentence's work (the report holds no user attribute) is unchanged. Recorded, not counted as a failure.
- **Unknown kept apart from absent.** "Those 24 bookings therefore cannot tell the board what share of residents is unable to book digitally. They cannot tell it that nobody is, either." The second sentence is the criterion's own point, and the draft makes it unprompted.
- **Count-size judgement.** None. 96 and 24 are never called high, low or modest, and the counts are attributed to the report as the brief requires.
- **Author perspective.** Ek's cleared standpoint is carried in first person under her byline, at its original strength: "I am not against digital booking" for "Hon motsätter sig inte digital bokning" — not converted into support for digital booking.
- **The administration's motive.** "That objection is double administration, and I will not pretend it is anything meaner: it is not that the people who phone are lazy or expensive" tracks "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning … är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra." No false motive is imputed, and the criticism is not neutralised.
- **Cost uncertainty.** "We make no claim to have costed or funded that trial; what it would cost is for the board to weigh" carries both disclaimers as disclaimers, and the close leaves the cost with the board, as the boundaries demand.
- **Two residual softenings, both defensible.** "before anyone has weighed" for "kommunen först måste kunna väga" broadens the actor and shifts *being able to weigh* to *having weighed*; since the material says no time or savings figure exists in the papers at all, the two do not come apart here. "Six months that would produce the missing figures" is hedged and heads a section whose named missing item is the time measurement.

## 5. L1 — professional English, no Swedish carried across

**Pass.**

Idiom and syntax are native throughout, and several sentences are better than merely correct: "They cannot tell it that nobody is, either"; "Neither side of that scale has a number on it, which is a thin basis for a permanent decision"; "I will not pretend it is anything meaner"; "A booking that comes in by phone is not proof that someone cannot use the web" — English word order and English collocation, not "Att en bokning går via telefon" carried over. Collective nouns take singular agreement ("the board is asked", "Öppna beslut proposes"), and "what share of residents **is** unable" is correct.

Two places worth naming, neither enough to fail:

- **"enter the same details in two flows"** is the one phrase that shows its Swedish ("två flöden"); an American reader would more naturally read "in two systems" or "in two workflows".
- **"costed"** ("We make no claim to have costed or funded that trial") is chiefly British; US usage would be "priced" or "put a cost on". It entered as the round-1 checker's own proposed repair.

Institutional renderings — "municipal executive board", "the administration", "staff recommendation", "community rooms" — are the conventional English for a Swedish municipal process and read as deliberate naming of a foreign body, not as transfer.

**Kept out of this verdict, as the criterion requires: locale mechanics.** The dates are written in British order — "8 April 2026" and "18 June" — where en_US convention is "April 8, 2026" and "June 18". That is a mechanics question, not an idiom one, and no checker raised it; neither report examined locale mechanics at all.
