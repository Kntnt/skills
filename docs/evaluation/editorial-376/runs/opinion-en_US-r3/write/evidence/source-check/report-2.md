## 4. Findings

### F1 — "two of the seven premises" asserts a set membership the material does not state (source support)

**Draft (line 9):** "The pilot report of April 8 covers eight weeks in **two of the seven** premises".
**Material:** *"gäller ett åtta veckor långt försök i **två lokaler**"*. The seven appear only in the separate sentence *"telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler"*.

**What differs:** scope. The source states a count of pilot premises; the draft states that those premises are members of the seven the proposal covers.

**Both directions.** A case where the draft holds and the source's wording would still be satisfied is the ordinary one and needs no naming. A case where the source holds and the draft fails, compatible with the supplied material: Lervik has eight association premises; the pilot ran in two of them, one of which is the eighth — a hall taken out of service since — and the June proposal accordingly covers the remaining seven. Nothing in the material excludes this. The material never states that seven is the total number of association premises, never says where the pilot ran, and never links the pilot premises to the proposal's scope. I can quote no supplied statement that sets the case aside, so the case stands.

**Why it matters:** the relation carries argumentative weight in context — it tells the reader the evidence base covers a named fraction of what the decision would change.

**Smallest supported repair:** delete the set relation. "The pilot report of April 8 covers eight weeks in two premises: 96 bookings through the web, 24 by phone." The sentence keeps the contrast with "all seven" from the opening paragraph without asserting the overlap.

### F2 — "a labor cost with a number attached" names cost where the trial records time (source support)

**Draft (line 23):** "That gives the board what it lacks now: **a labor cost with a number attached**, and a reason for the phone from the people still using it."
**Material:** the proposed trial records *"tidsåtgång per bokningsväg"* — time taken per booking route. The material contains no step converting that into money, and lists the missing items as *"Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning"* — two distinct things, of which the proposal supplies the first.

**What differs:** the thing measured. Time per route is not a labor cost; a labor cost is time priced.

**Both directions.** A case compatible with the supplied material in which the source holds and the draft fails: the trial runs its six months, the administration duly logs minutes per booking on each route, and no one prices those minutes — so the board is handed measured time and still has no cost figure to set against user value. The material requires nothing more of the administration than *registrera tidsåtgång*, and the association explicitly *"gör inget anspråk på att ha ... kostnadsberäknat försöket"*. Nothing excludes the case. It also sits badly with 1.15 in the draft's own text: the association disclaims costing, then the trial is said to deliver a cost.

**Smallest supported repair:** name the measured quantity. "That gives the board what it lacks now: the time each route actually takes, in numbers, and a reason for the phone from the people still using it." If the cost framing is wanted for the link to the closing section, the supported form is the association's own case rather than a promised output — e.g. "the time each route actually takes, so that a labor cost can rest on something".
