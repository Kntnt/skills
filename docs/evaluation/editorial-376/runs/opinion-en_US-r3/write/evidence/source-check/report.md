The repair boundary for spoken quotation is therefore not engaged; there is no transcript, no fragmentary answer, and nothing welded together. No quote-approval question arises, and the draft makes no claim about approval.

Translation check on that passage, as target-language speech:

- "Att en bokning går via telefon" → "A booking that comes in by phone": same event, idiomatic US English; no added agency ("comes in" is as passive about who booked as the Swedish).
- "är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben" → "is not proof that someone cannot use the web": the double negative, the modality ("kan inte" = incapacity, not disinclination) and the indefinite "någon" → "someone" all carry. Certainty unchanged: a denial that one thing proves another, not a claim that the person can use the web.
- "Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används" → "It is a reason to find out why the phone is still used": "ett skäl" (a reason, not the reason) and "fortfarande" (still) both carry; the impersonal passive is kept rather than assigning the finding-out to a named party.

No obstruction to an ordinary US reader; no source-language syntax survives. **No translation finding on this passage.**

## 4. Findings

Each is a defect to repair before delivery, with the support that is missing or changed and the smallest supported repair. F1 is both a source-support and a translation finding and is marked as such; F2–F7 are source-support findings.

### F1 — "community halls" / "halls" names a wider and differently shaped thing than "föreningslokaler" *(source support; arises in translation)*

- Draft: "all seven community halls" (lead); "two halls" (§1); "in all seven halls" (lead); "all seven halls" (§3).
- Source: "digital bokning av föreningslokaler" (brief); "Bokning av föreningslokaler"; "i två lokaler"; "för alla sju lokaler"; "i alla sju lokaler".
- What differs: the thing named. "föreningslokal" is a premises made available to associations; nothing in the word says it is a hall, and nothing says it is for the community at large. "Community hall" adds both.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: two of the seven are small meeting rooms let to local associations — a rehearsal room and a committee room above the library. They are "föreningslokaler" and "lokaler"; calling them community halls misdescribes them, and "all seven community halls" then names a set the municipality does not have. Nothing supplied excludes this: the material never states the size, type or public availability of any premises.
- Case where the draft holds and the source fails: a large hall the municipality rents out to private parties and not to associations would be a community hall but not a "föreningslokal"; the proposal's scope is the latter.
- Smallest repair: use the association-premises sense once and a short form after — lead: "remove phone booking from all seven association premises"; then "two of them", "all seven", "in all seven". If a single noun is wanted throughout, "venues" or "premises" carries "lokaler" without adding "hall".

### F2 — "We have not funded this trial or costed it" asserts what the material only declines to claim

- Draft: "We have not funded this trial or costed it."
- Source: "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."
- What differs: whose assertion, and its modality. The material records the absence of a claim; the draft makes the opposite claim. What is unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened — the claims contract states this in terms.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: the association's treasurer worked up a rough internal cost for the six months but the association does not put it forward, having decided the figure is the municipality's to establish. "Gör inget anspråk på att ha kostnadsberäknat" holds; "we have not costed it" is false.
- Nothing supplied excludes it: the material states only what the association claims, never what it has or has not done.
- Smallest repair: "We make no claim to have funded this trial or costed it." The paragraph's argument — that the weighing is the board's job — is unchanged.

### F3 — "enter the same booking twice" specifies a duplication the source does not describe

- Draft: "staff should not have to enter the same booking twice."
- Source: "Motivet i utlåtandet är att personalen ska slippa föra in uppgifter i två flöden."; "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning ... är dubbel administration."
- What differs: the thing described. "föra in uppgifter i två flöden" is entering information into two workflows; "the same booking twice" asserts that one and the same booking record is keyed in two times over.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: a phone booking is written into a call log with the caller's name and callback number, and the reservation is then entered into the booking system. Information goes into two flows and the administration is double, but no booking is entered twice — the two entries hold different fields. "Föra in uppgifter i två flöden" holds; "the same booking twice" does not.
- Nothing supplied excludes it: the material never says the two flows take the same data.
- Smallest repair: "staff should not have to enter booking details into two separate workflows." This keeps the objection recognisable and the section's demand for a number intact.

### F4 — "Nothing in these documents says ..." is a wider negative than the material supports

- Draft: "Nothing in these documents says that people who call are lazy or expensive, and I will not pretend otherwise."
- Source: "Förvaltningens verkliga invändning mot att behålla båda kanalerna är dubbel administration, inte att telefonanvändare är lata eller dyra."; "Inga ... besparingar är belagda."
- What differs: scope and whose assertion. The material establishes what the administration's real objection is and is not; the draft makes a claim about the contents of the whole document set. The source's own wording ("verkliga") distinguishes the real objection from how the case may be presented, which is the opposite of a guarantee about what the papers contain.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: the staff report includes a background line that handling calls ties up reception time in a way that is hard to defend. No saving is calculated, so "inga besparingar är belagda" still holds, and double administration is still the real objection — but a document now does say something close to "expensive". The draft's negative is then false.
- Nothing supplied excludes it: the material never enumerates the documents' full contents, only their motive and what they lack.
- Smallest repair: "The administration's objection is not that people who call are lazy or expensive, and I will not pretend otherwise." This keeps the refusal to invent a motive — which the boundaries require — and drops only the claim about document contents.

### F5 — "worth to residents" changes whose value is weighed

- Draft: "weighing it against what those routes are worth to residents is the board's job, not ours."
- Source: "Hon menar att kommunen först måste kunna väga arbetskostnaden mot värdet för användarna."
- What differs: the population. "användarna" are the people who book; "residents" are the population of Lervik. The draft elsewhere uses "people in Lervik" correctly for "invånarna" (#12), so the two are being used as distinct terms within the draft itself.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: several of the seven premises are booked mainly by association secretaries, some of whom live in the neighbouring municipality, and by a regional choir that rents a hall weekly. The value for those users is real and is what the source says must be weighed; they are not residents.
- Case in the other direction: a resident who never books has no value at stake in the routes, yet falls under "residents".
- Nothing supplied excludes either: the material never says the users are residents, nor that residents are users.
- Smallest repair: "what those routes are worth to the people who use them."

### F6 — "does not record" claims more than "mäter inte"

- Draft: "It does not record age, functional ability, or how used to digital services anyone is."
- Source: "Den mäter inte ålder, funktionsförmåga eller digital vana."
- What differs: the act denied. Not measuring is compatible with having the data on hand; not recording is not.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: the booking form asks for a birth year, so the figure sits in the underlying data, but the report never measures or reports against it. "Mäter inte ålder" holds; "does not record age" does not.
- Nothing supplied excludes it: the material states only what the report measures.
- Smallest repair: "It does not measure age, functional ability, or how used to digital services anyone is."

### F7 — "how long a booking takes" narrows "tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" to a per-booking figure

- Draft: "The administration records how long a booking takes on each route."
- Source: "Under försöket ska förvaltningen registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg."
- What differs: what is measured. "Tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" is the time each route takes — which may be recorded as staff time per route over a period. The draft commits the proposal to timing individual bookings.
- Case where the draft fails and the source holds: the administration logs the hours reception spends on phone bookings each week against the hours spent on web bookings, and divides nothing. The association's proposal is met; "how long a booking takes" is not what was recorded, and the draft has committed the association to a method it did not propose.
- Nothing supplied excludes it: the material specifies the unit as the route, not the booking.
- Smallest repair: "The administration records the time each route takes." The next paragraph's "a labor cost with a number attached" follows from this as well as from the narrower version.

## 5. Editorial questions (not findings)

- **Q1 — "with the figure in front of it" (#26).** Read in context, this is advocacy: the board should not settle the matter until a cost figure exists. On a stricter reading it could be heard as implying a figure already exists, which would sit oddly two sentences after "We have not funded this trial or costed it". The passage is defensible as written and no repair is proposed; if the author wants the advocacy reading fixed, "and should say so with a cost in front of it" settles it. This is an editorial choice, not a defect.
- **Q2 — length.** The brief asks for "ungefär 400 ord"; the draft is 469 words. Length compliance is general editorial review and outside this comparison. It is noted only because a stated length constrains the use of material: nothing in the excess is invented — every additional passage traces to the material or is the author's argument on it — so there is no claims finding here.
