### 1.3 The June 18 meeting and what is before it

**Source:** "Tjänsteutlåtandet inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni föreslår att telefonbokning tas bort för alla sju lokaler från september."

- *Thing named:* "tjänsteutlåtande" → "staff memo". A tjänsteutlåtande is the administration's written report and recommendation to the board; "staff memo" names the same document in its role as the item before the board, and the draft consistently treats it as a proposal the board may reject, which is what the material says it is.
- *Scope:* "alla sju lokaler" → "all seven association facilities". - *Modality:* "inför kommunstyrelsens möte den 18 juni" (prepared for the June 18 meeting) → "On June 18 the municipal executive board will consider". A document written for a meeting is a document that meeting takes up; the material supplies no competing item and no postponement. Counter-case tested: a meeting rescheduled so the memo is taken up later — the material says the memo is *inför* that meeting, i.e. the board's June 18 agenda item, and the brief's boundary requires the ending to "namnge kommunstyrelsens möjliga beslut" at that meeting, so the material treats the June 18 consideration as given.

## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — The heading "Nobody has measured what the change saves" widens an absence in the documents into a universal negative

**Draft passage (heading, line 13):** "Nobody has measured what the change saves"

**Source passages:** "Någon tidsmätning eller ekonomisk besparingsberäkning finns inte i handlingarna." and "Inga lagkrav, protester, diskrimineringsfall, partipolitiska motiv eller besparingar är belagda."

**What is missing or changed:** The material supports an absence in a defined place — the decision documents — and an absence of established savings. The heading asserts that no measurement exists anywhere and that nobody performed one. What is unclaimed in the handlingar is not thereby known not to have happened. A concrete case compatible with the material: the administration's own IT unit timed both booking routes during the pilot and never appended that note to the June 18 papers; then the supplied sentence holds and the heading fails. No supplied statement excludes it — the material speaks only about what the handlingar contain and what is belagt. The draft's body makes the correctly scoped claim one line later ("the documents contain no time measurement and no savings calculation"), so the heading is the only place the claim is overstated, and a heading carries its claim to a reader who reads nothing else.

**Smallest supported repair:** "The documents don't measure what the change saves"

(Alternatives of the same size, if the run's headings are kept nominal: "No measurement of what the change saves" or "The savings are nowhere in the documents". The repair changes four words of one heading and nothing in the body.)

## 5. Editorial questions (not defects; no repair proposed)

### 5.1 "knowing this time what each route costs and why people use it" (line 27)

The cost half is supported by the proposal's "registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg". The reasons half rests on answers the material makes voluntary ("be användarna frivilligt ange"), so a low response rate would leave the board knowing less than the clause promises. The draft does carry "voluntarily" where the mechanism is described, and the clause is the author advocating her own proposal in the conditional mood of the paragraph. Reported here rather than as a finding because the line between advocacy and a factual promise is genuinely unsettled at this wording. If the run wants it closed, "and what users say about why they use it" is the smallest change, but the present wording is defensible.

### 5.2 "we have not calculated" (line 27)

The material says "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket" — makes no claim to have costed it, which is not quite the same as stating it was not costed. The draft's second clause ("we do not pretend otherwise") is the material's own shape, and the boundary "med kostnadsosäkerheten bevarad" together with "Kostnaden behöver kommunstyrelsen ta ställning till" presupposes an uncosted trial. This is the brief's own unevidenced statement about the commissioning party, made against that party's interest; weakening it would put at risk the disclaimer the boundary requires. No repair proposed.
