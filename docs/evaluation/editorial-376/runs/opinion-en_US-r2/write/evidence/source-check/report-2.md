## 2. Findings

### Finding 1 — an unclaimed fact turned into an asserted negative

**Draft:** "What the six months themselves cost, we have not calculated, and we do not pretend otherwise."
**Source:** "Föreningen gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket."

**What differs:** whose assertion, and the state of knowledge. The material says the association *makes no claim* to have costed the trial. The draft says the association *has not* costed it. A non-claim is turned into a first-person assertion of a negative fact about the association.

**Both directions.** Case compatible with the supplied material in which the source holds and the draft fails: Öppna beslut has an internal rough costing of the six months that it has chosen not to put forward in this debate, and therefore makes no claim to have costed the trial in these documents. Nothing supplied excludes that case — the material states only what the association claims, never what it has or has not done, and no passage says the association lacks a figure. In the reverse direction the draft's stronger statement entails the source's weaker one, so no case separates them that way. The case stands, so this is a finding however natural the wording reads. It is precisely the contract's "what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened".

**Support missing:** any supplied statement that the association has not made such a calculation.

**Smallest supported repair:** replace "we have not calculated" with a rendering of the non-claim, leaving the rest of the sentence untouched:

> What the six months themselves cost, we make no claim to have calculated, and we do not pretend otherwise.

This keeps the sentence's position, voice, length and the cost uncertainty the brief's boundary requires ("med kostnadsosäkerheten bevarad"), and it keeps the following sentence's "That figure is for the board to weigh" working.

## 3. Translation check (speech)

One passage of supplied speech is rendered in the draft.

**Source:** ”Att en bokning går via telefon är inte ett bevis för att någon inte kan använda webben. Det är ett skäl att ta reda på varför telefonen fortfarande används.”
**Draft:** "A booking that comes in by phone is no proof that someone can't use the web. It's a reason to find out why the phone is still being used."

- *Meaning.* "är inte ett bevis för" → "is no proof that": same denial, same object. "någon inte kan använda webben" → "someone can't use the web": same indefinite subject, same inability, not widened to "no one" or narrowed to a named group. "ett skäl att ta reda på" → "a reason to find out": same, and not strengthened to "a duty" or "the reason".
- *Stance.* The speaker declines to draw a conclusion and asks for an inquiry instead; both clauses keep that, and neither is turned into a complaint about the administration.
- *Certainty.* No hedge is removed and none added. "is no proof" keeps the epistemic point rather than converting it into "shows that people can use the web".
- *Distinctive wording.* The image the passage turns on — a booking as evidence, or not — survives intact. Nothing is smoothed into the register of the surrounding argument; the second sentence keeps the source's short, flat turn.
- *Idiom.* "comes in by phone", "find out why the phone is still being used" and the contractions "can't" and "It's" are idiomatic American English rather than Swedish syntax carried over. The passive "is still being used" matches "fortfarande används" and is natural here. No Swedish word order, no calque.
- *Referents.* "webben" → "the web" and "telefonen" → "the phone" are the two booking routes the material has already established; no implicit referent needed making explicit beyond what the context settles, and none was invented.
- *Presentation.* The draft does not put quotation marks around it. That is not a fidelity defect: the speaker is the named author, the material permits reference as well as quotation, and the draft does not attribute the words to anyone other than the person who said them. Quote approval is outside this check, and the draft neither claims nor needs it.

**No translation findings.**

Meaning crossing languages outside quoted speech was checked in section 1 with the rest of the source support; the only term-level crossings worth naming — "kommunstyrelsen"/"municipal executive board", "tjänsteutlåtande"/"staff memo", "föreningslokaler"/"association facilities", "funktionsförmåga"/"functional ability", "dubbel administration"/"double administration" — each name the same thing in this context, with the single residual noted in Observation C.

## 4. Observations and editorial questions (not findings; no repair required)

These are passages where a difference exists but the draft is defensible as written. They are recorded so the decision is visible, not as defects.

**A. "the report was not built to tell the difference."** The source gives what the report counts ("räknar bokningar, inte unika personer"), not what it was designed to do. A report could in principle capture caller identity and still tally bookings. But the supplied description *is* a description of its construction, and an ordinary reader takes the clause as "the report does not distinguish them", which is supplied. If a tighter wording is wanted, "the report does not tell us the difference" costs nothing.

**B. "enter the same information in two flows."** The source is "föra in uppgifter i två flöden", without "samma". The duplication is nonetheless supplied by the administration's stated objection, "dubbel administration". Strictly, two flows could capture partly different fields; practically, the material calls the problem double administration, which is what "the same information" names. Defensible.

**C. "Double administration is a real objection."** "verklig invändning" in the source means the *actual* objection, as against the imputed one; English "a real objection" can also be heard as "a valid objection", which would be the author conceding merit. In context — the clause is followed at once by the denial of the imputed motive — the actual-objection reading governs, and a concession of merit would in any case be the sender's own evaluation, which she is entitled to make. Left as an editorial question, not a defect: if the ambiguity is unwelcome, "the administration's real objection is double administration" removes it.

**D. "records how much time a booking takes by each route."** The source is "registrera tidsåtgång per bokningsväg" — time expenditure per booking route, which an aggregate monthly figure per route would satisfy without ever timing a single booking. The draft's wording commits the trial to per-booking timing. Against that: with 96 web and 24 phone bookings, a per-route total is uninformative for the comparison the trial exists to make, so the per-booking reading is the natural one, and the payoff sentence ("a labor cost that can be set against a value for users") holds either way. Defensible. "records how much time each booking route takes" would track the source word for word if preferred.

**E. "the board can remove the phone route, change it, or keep it."** The source says "om en kanal ska tas bort, ändras eller behållas" — a channel, unspecified. The draft names the phone route, which is the channel the whole decision concerns. The sentence lists what the board can do rather than claiming these are the only options, so it excludes nothing the source leaves open. Defensible.

**F. "will consider a staff memo" (June 18).** The source says the memo is "inför" the meeting. Carried as considered at it, which both the thesis and the brief's ending boundary presuppose. Defensible; recorded in section 1.

**G. "without being told what keeping it costs."** Rests on the documents' stated absence; an oral figure at the meeting is not excluded by the material. Read in its paragraph as the author's argument from the absence just stated. Defensible; recorded in section 1.
