## Method and scope

The accounting below separates independently checkable propositions, including propositions embedded in recommendations. Each entry gives the draft passage, its complete proposition, the precise negation of that proposition, and the supplied passage that rules the negation out. Source identifiers refer to the verbatim passages in the source register below; identifying an identifier and its relevant words identifies that supplied statement, not an outside source.

For advocacy and evaluative language, the checkable proposition concerns what Ek or her association advocates or argues. The negation therefore concerns that position, rather than pretending the source can prove that a policy judgment is objectively correct. The report separately checks the factual premises of those judgments. In an opinion piece signed by Ek, the first-person stance is hers; “our” refers to Öppna beslut.

Ordinary contextual meaning governs the comparison. For example, a heading contrasting bookings with barriers describes what the booking counts establish; it is not a claim that no obstacle of any kind existed. A proposed study providing a basis for assessment is not a promise of complete knowledge about every resident. Nevertheless, inviting voluntary explanations does not ensure that any explanations will be supplied; that narrower issue is tested below. Qualifications shared across clauses are retained below. Negative claims about what an organization claims are not converted into claims about whether the underlying event happened.

## Source-support findings and smallest repairs

### F1 — An unclaimed act of financing became an unclaimed act of securing funding

**Location:** “Öppna beslut has not claimed to have costed the trial or secured its funding.” Accounting entries 47–48.

**What is supported:** The association makes no claim to have costed the trial and no claim to have financed the trial. The board still needs to address cost.

**What changed:** “Secured its funding” concerns obtaining or arranging funding, which can include a commitment for future payment. “Ha finansierat” concerns having financed the trial. The source does not provide the broader or different statement about what funding arrangements the association claims to have secured. Because the sentence reports what the association claims, evidence that the source does not mention a secured-funding claim is insufficient to establish that the association makes no such claim.

**Exact failed test:** The draft's funding proposition is “Öppna beslut does not claim to have secured funding for the proposed trial.” Its negation is “Öppna beslut claims to have secured funding for the proposed trial.” S10 does not rule that negation out. An assertion of a conditional donor commitment, without an assertion that the association has financed or costed the trial, is the concrete compatible counterexample given above.

**Smallest supported repair:** Replace “secured its funding” with “financed it”:

> Öppna beslut has not claimed to have costed the trial or financed it.

This preserves the negative's scope over both claims, the warranted certainty about the association's position, and the sentence's voice. It does not change the sentence into “The trial has not been costed or funded,” which the source would not establish. Supporting the current wording instead would require a supplied statement specifically saying that the association makes no claim to have secured the trial's funding.

### F2 — No time measurement became no staff-time quantification

**Location:** “The case for removing phone booking therefore rests on an administrative concern that those documents do not quantify in staff time or projected savings.” Accounting entry 37; the projected-savings component is separately accounted for in entry 38.

**What is supported:** The documents contain no time measurement and no financial savings calculation. Double administration is the stated and actual objection.

**What changed:** A quantity can be estimated without being measured. The source's statement about missing “tidsmätning” therefore does not establish the draft's broader absence of any staff-time quantification. This is a claim about the documents' contents, not merely a judgment that their evidence is inadequate.

**Exact failed test:** The draft asserts that these documents do not quantify the administrative concern in staff time. The negation is that these documents do quantify that concern in staff time. A document giving an explicitly unmeasured estimate of ten extra minutes per phone booking, without a financial savings calculation, would satisfy the negation while remaining compatible with the supplied source. No supplied statement excludes that counterexample.

**Smallest supported repair:** Replace the relative clause with the narrower documented absence:

> The case for removing phone booking therefore rests on an administrative concern for which those documents provide neither a time measurement nor a calculation of projected savings.

The argument, attribution, and following “too thin a basis” judgment can stand. The repair preserves certainty about what is absent rather than weakening it to “may lack.” Supporting the original wording would require confirmation that the documents contain no numerical estimate of staff time either.

### F3 — A voluntary inquiry is presented as yielding evidence of reasons

**Locations:** “This would give the board a basis for assessing both the workload and users’ reasons” and the closing “with evidence about the work involved and why users choose it.” Accounting entries 45 and 56.

**What is supported:** Under the proposed trial staff would record time and invite users to give reasons voluntarily. The proposal is designed to investigate channel choice. Reasons actually supplied would be evidence of users' reported reasons, without establishing prevalence or independently verifying motives.

**What changed:** The first sentence presents a basis for assessing reasons as an outcome of carrying out the proposal. The ending likewise assumes that evidence about reasons will be available. Carrying out an invitation to volunteer information is not the same event as obtaining that information. Reconstructing either sentence as conditional on reasons actually being supplied would add a condition the draft does not state.

**Exact failed test:** The relevant negations are that carrying out the proposed trial would not give the board a basis for assessing users' reasons, and that the board would not have evidence about those reasons afterward. A trial in which all users decline the voluntary invitation is compatible with the source and leaves those negations possible. This does not challenge the suitability of asking users, demand proof of every motive, or require complete participation.

**Smallest supported repairs:** Keep the measured-workload result and condition the reasons component on information being supplied:

> This would give the board a basis for assessing the workload and any reasons users volunteer.

Replace the closing evidence phrase with:

> —using the recorded time and any reasons users volunteer.

These changes preserve the proposal and its expected practical value while retaining the source's voluntary-response condition. No additional material is necessary for the repaired statements. Keeping an unconditional claim that explanations will be obtained would require support beyond the supplied invitation plan.
