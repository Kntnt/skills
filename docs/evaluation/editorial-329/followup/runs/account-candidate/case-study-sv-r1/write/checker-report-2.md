## Translated-quotation findings

Q1 preserves the desired evening/morning information relationship, customer ownership of the categories, and Svale's assisting role. Its first-person collective voice and plain matter-of-fact tone remain. No concrete meaning or voice obstruction found.

Q2 preserves greater time spent reaching agreement than entering the first reports, the first-person conditional recommendation and its before-next-building timing. One local target-language obstruction remains:

**T1 — “innan nästa byggnad kommer i gång”.** In Swedish, “byggnaden kommer i gång” leaves the activity unstated and treats the building itself as something that starts operating. English “before the next building starts” is shorthand for the rollout/activity at the next building. Here the supplied trial and not-yet-expanded context settles that activity. The reader can recover the intended meaning, but the literal subject–predicate pairing is not idiomatic clear speech for a housing building. Smallest supported repair: **“innan försöket startar i nästa byggnad”**. This makes the settled activity explicit while retaining the same future timing and conditional recommendation; it does not assert that expansion has happened or been decided. This is a translation finding, not a missing-source claim.

Q3 preserves the conditional choice to repeat, the unhedged first-person assessment of usefulness, the contrast “men”, and one extra preparation week. “En gemensam vy” is a natural context-specific rendering of “one view”, not an added performance claim. The practical and qualified voice remains. No other concrete translation obstruction found.
