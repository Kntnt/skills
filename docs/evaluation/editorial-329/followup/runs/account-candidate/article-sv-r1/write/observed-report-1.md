## Findings and smallest repairs

### F1 — Source support / sampling and threshold wording

Passage: “Ett kort mätförsök kan visa när lufttemperaturen passerar en vald gräns.”

The verb “passerar” can make the proposition about identifying threshold crossings rather than identifying samples below a threshold. The supplied record establishes only five-minute sampling and passes with at least one below-threshold reading. It does not establish a crossing time or even that crossings occurred during the recorded window. Temperatures could already have been below the threshold at the first recorded sample, which is compatible with every source statement.

Smallest supported repair: “Ett kort mätförsök kan visa när lufttemperaturen ligger under en vald gräns.” This keeps the explanation and confidence but removes the unsupported event of crossing. If explicit sampling precision is desired, “Ett kort mätförsök kan visa vid vilka mättillfällen lufttemperaturen ligger under en vald gräns.” The shorter repair is sufficient in light of the following five-minute explanation.

No other source-support change is required. In particular, preserve the count without evaluation, the stated uncertainty about cause and duration, and the clear distinctions between planning, recommendations and accomplished measurements.
