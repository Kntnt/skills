## Findings and minimal repair

### F1 — Source support: current consideration is not established

Draft: “The eight-week trial remains unexpanded while the team considers how its categories work for larger repairs.”

The source establishes that expansion has not happened and that a future decision will follow checking the categories for larger repairs. It does not establish that the team is currently considering/checking this issue. The present-tense “while ... considers” introduces that current activity. A team that has scheduled this work but not begun it is a concrete source-compatible counterexample.

Smallest supported repair preserving the actual decision sequence:

“The eight-week trial remains unexpanded; the team will decide after checking how its categories work for larger repairs.”

All other listed propositions are supported. No change to warranted certainty, the customer's agency, the quantitative comparison, Lind's qualified support, supplier attribution, or the optional document link is needed.
