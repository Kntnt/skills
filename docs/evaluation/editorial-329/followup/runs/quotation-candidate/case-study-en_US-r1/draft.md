# Elm Quay brings repair reports into one shared view

Elm Quay Housing tested a shared repair log in two buildings to give maintenance staff on different shifts access to the same information. Its supervisor says the shared view helps, but would allow more preparation time for another trial. The trial note records a shorter assignment time but does not attribute the difference to the software.

Elm Quay manages 640 apartments. In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log. Previously, telephone reports and emails had been stored separately. The team chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show each repair’s status.

In an email interview, maintenance supervisor Maya Lind described the aim and the division of work:

“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

The team kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale configured the log and trained six staff in two sessions.

“We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts,” Lind said.

## What the trial figures show

Elm Quay’s internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, records 31 repair reports entered during the eight weeks. The figures exclude emergencies and work ordered before the trial.

Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, compared with three in the preceding eight-week period. The workloads differed, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. These are assignment times; the source contains no measurements of completion time, costs, or resident satisfaction.

## A qualified case for trying again

Lind’s own assessment includes a reservation:

“I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

The trial has not expanded. Before deciding, the team will check how its categories work for larger repairs. Lind’s assessment concerns Elm Quay’s experience; it is not a recommendation of Svale for every housing company.
