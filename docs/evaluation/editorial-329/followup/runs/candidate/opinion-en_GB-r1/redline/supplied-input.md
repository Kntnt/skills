# Lervik needs evidence before ending telephone bookings

By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut

Lervik’s municipal executive board should postpone a permanent move to online-only booking for community venues. Our association, Öppna beslut, proposes a six-month trial across all seven venues, keeping telephone and web bookings available while the council gathers the evidence needed to decide their future.

## The pilot counts bookings, not people

The council’s pilot report, *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, dated 8 April 2026, records 96 web bookings and 24 telephone bookings during an eight-week trial at two venues. Those figures describe transactions. They do not tell us how many different people used either route.

Nor did the report measure age, disability or digital skills. Its telephone bookings cannot establish what proportion of Lervik’s residents are unable to book online. A telephone booking is a reason to ask why someone chose that route, not proof that they could not use the website.

That distinction matters to the decision. The council needs to understand what users value about each booking route before it can weigh that value against staff costs.

## Double administration deserves a measured answer

The officers’ report prepared for the board’s 18 June meeting proposes removing telephone bookings from all seven venues from September. Its stated reason is to spare staff the work of entering information into two separate workflows.

That is a practical objection which our proposal must address. Keeping both routes requires the board to consider the administrative burden. Yet the papers contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings. The case for removing a booking route permanently is being made without quantifying the work it would save.

I do not oppose digital booking. I oppose making this permanent change before the board can weigh the cost of the work against the value to users.

## Test both routes, then decide their future

During our proposed six-month trial, staff would record the time spent on each booking route and invite users to explain, voluntarily, why they chose telephone or web. Extending the trial to all seven venues would put the proposed service under examination across the whole network.

Öppna beslut makes no claim to have costed or funded this proposal. The board must assess its cost; keeping both routes cannot simply be assumed to be cost-free.

The municipal executive board should postpone the permanent switch, address the cost of a six-month trial and approve testing both routes at all seven venues. It can then decide whether a booking route should be removed, changed or retained.
