# Briar County volunteers share records of new donations

Briar County Theater Archive cataloged 47 incoming donations during an eight-week pilot of Index Lantern. Volunteers could consult the same records, while uncertain dates remained visible. A decision on moving older records is still pending.

The archive holds donated programs, photographs, and posters from local productions. Volunteers had described new donations in separate spreadsheets. In February 2026, the collection team chose to test a shared catalog for incoming donations, leaving existing collections outside the pilot.

The team agreed on fields for production title, year, donor, and location, retaining a free-text note for uncertain dates. Collection coordinator Lena Ortiz explained the work in an email headed “Catalog pilot: notes for publication”:

“Agreeing on the fields took two meetings. The import was the short part.”

Index Lantern imported the team’s spreadsheet template and provided one remote training session for five volunteers. The archive chose the descriptions and checked the imported records.

The archive’s May 6 pilot note records the 47 donations and reports that volunteers could consult the same records. Eleven records retained uncertain dates. The note contains no before-and-after time measurement, cost calculation, or assessment of public access.

Ortiz’s assessment was qualified:

“I would use the shared catalog again for new donations. I would test the descriptions on more posters before we bring in the older collection.”

The archive will review the field definitions before deciding whether to move older records into the catalog.

This case is based on the email and pilot note supplied by the archive for publication by Index Lantern.
