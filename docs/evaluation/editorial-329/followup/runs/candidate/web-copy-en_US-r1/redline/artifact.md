# Review your shared room’s booking routine

Svale helps boards of smaller housing cooperatives take stock of how residents book a shared room. The review gives your board a shared understanding of the current routine and two possible ways to simplify it.

## What the review includes

The service covers the booking routine for one shared room in one housing cooperative. It includes:

- A 45-minute video meeting with two representatives from your board.
- A written summary of the current steps, points that are unclear, and two possible simplifications.

Your board needs to be able to describe its existing booking rules and show the current instructions for residents.

The review does not include legal advice, booking system installation, or interviews with residents. Svale does not guarantee a particular amount of time saved or fewer conflicts.

## Price and scope

The fixed price is **SEK 4,800, including VAT**, for one room in one housing cooperative. The price covers the video meeting and written summary described above.

Payment terms and the delivery time for the written summary are not specified.

## Express interest and hear from Svale

Use the interest form to provide your name, housing cooperative, and email address. Submitting the form is an expression of interest, not an order. It does not book a meeting, and no payment details are required.

Svale will reply by email within three business days to check whether the service is a fit and suggest a meeting time.
