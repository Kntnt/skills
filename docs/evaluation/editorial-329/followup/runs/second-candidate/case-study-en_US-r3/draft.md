# Elm Quay brings repair reports into one shared view

Elm Quay Housing’s maintenance team tried a shared repair log in two buildings to help staff on different shifts see the same information. Supervisor Maya Lind says the shared view helps, but she would allow more preparation time before another trial. The team has yet to expand it.

*Published by Svale Systems.*

Elm Quay Housing manages 640 apartments. In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to trial the log. Previously, telephone reports and emails were stored separately. The team chose Svale Systems after testing whether its log could show the status of each repair.

In an email interview, maintenance supervisor Maya Lind explained what the team wanted to change:

“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

The maintenance team designed the categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale configured the log and trained six staff members during two sessions. For Lind, the setup offered a lesson for future implementation:

“We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

The trial lasted eight weeks. Elm Quay’s internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, recorded 31 repair reports, excluding emergencies and work ordered before the trial. Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, compared with three in the preceding eight-week period.

The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. It contains no measurements of costs, resident satisfaction, or repair completion time. The assignment figures therefore offer no measurement of how long repairs took to finish.

Lind’s assessment includes both a reason to repeat the trial and a reservation about preparation:

“I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

The team will decide whether to expand the trial after checking how the categories work for larger repairs. That question remains open; Lind’s assessment is not a recommendation of Svale for every housing company.
