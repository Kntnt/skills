# Elm Quay brings repair reports into one shared view

Elm Quay Housing’s maintenance team tested a shared repair log in two buildings to help staff on different shifts see the same information. Supervisor Maya Lind would repeat the trial, but would allow more preparation time. The team has yet to decide whether to expand it.

*Published by Svale Systems, the supplier.*

Elm Quay manages 640 apartments. Before the trial, telephone reports and emails were stored separately. In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to try a shared log. It chose Svale Systems after testing whether the log could show each repair’s status.

“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log,” Lind said in an email interview.

The team designed the categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale configured the log and trained six staff members over two sessions.

## Making time to agree on categories

For Lind, the setup work offered a lesson for any next building.

“We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

The trial ran for eight weeks. Elm Quay’s internal trial note, dated December 4, 2025, records 31 repair reports entered in the log. That count excludes emergencies and work ordered before the trial.

Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, compared with three in the preceding eight-week period. The workloads differed between the periods, and the note explicitly does not attribute the difference to the software. No cost, resident satisfaction, or repair completion time measurements are available.

## A qualified case for trying it again

Lind’s assessment includes both a benefit and a reservation:

“I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

That is her judgment of Elm Quay’s experience, not a recommendation of Svale for every housing company. The trial has not expanded. Before deciding whether to extend it, the team will check how the categories work for larger repairs.
