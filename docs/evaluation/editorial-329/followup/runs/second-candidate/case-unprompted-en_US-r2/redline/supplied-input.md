# Briar County archive shares records for new donations

Briar County Theater Archive cataloged 47 incoming donations during an eight-week pilot of Index Lantern, with volunteers able to consult the same records. The trial excluded existing collections, and the archive has yet to decide whether to move older records into the catalog.

The archive holds donated programs, photographs, and posters from local productions. Volunteers had described new donations in separate spreadsheets before the collection team chose to test a shared catalog in February 2026.

The team agreed on fields for production title, year, donor, and location, retaining a free-text note for uncertain dates. Collection coordinator Lena Ortiz explained the choice in an email supplied for this Index Lantern–published case:

“We kept the uncertain dates visible. An empty field and an estimated year tell the next volunteer different things.”

Index Lantern imported the team’s spreadsheet template and provided one remote training session for five volunteers. The archive chose the descriptions and checked the imported records.

“Agreeing on the fields took two meetings. The import was the short part,” Ortiz wrote.

The archive’s May 6 pilot note records the 47 donations and reports that eleven records retained uncertain dates. It contains no before-and-after time measurement, cost calculation, or assessment of public access.

Ortiz’s appraisal was qualified:

“I would use the shared catalog again for new donations. I would test the descriptions on more posters before we bring in the older collection.”

The archive will review the field definitions before deciding whether to move older records into the catalog.
