# Briar County archive shares records for new donations

Briar County Theater Archive cataloged 47 incoming donations during an eight-week pilot with Index Lantern. Volunteers could consult the same records, according to the archive’s pilot note. The archive will review its field definitions before deciding whether to move older records into the shared catalog.

The archive holds programs, photographs, and posters from local productions. Volunteers had described new donations in separate spreadsheets. In February 2026, the collection team chose to test Index Lantern for incoming donations; existing collections were outside the pilot.

The team agreed on fields for production title, year, donor, and location, retaining a free-text note for uncertain dates. Collection coordinator Lena Ortiz explained the choice in an email supplied for this case:

“We kept the uncertain dates visible. An empty field and an estimated year tell the next volunteer different things.”

Index Lantern imported the team’s spreadsheet template and provided one remote training session for five volunteers. The archive chose the descriptions and checked the imported records.

“Agreeing on the fields took two meetings. The import was the short part,” Ortiz wrote.

The archive’s May 6 pilot note records that 11 records retained uncertain dates. It contains no before-and-after time measurement, cost calculation, or assessment of public access.

Ortiz’s assessment remained specific to the pilot:

“I would use the shared catalog again for new donations. I would test the descriptions on more posters before we bring in the older collection.”

This case, published by supplier Index Lantern, draws on the email and pilot note supplied by the archive.
