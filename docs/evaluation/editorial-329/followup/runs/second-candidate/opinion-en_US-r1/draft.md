# Lervik should test both booking options before cutting one

*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

Lervik’s municipal executive board should postpone a permanent switch to online-only booking for community facilities. Öppna beslut proposes a six-month trial at all seven facilities, keeping both phone and online booking. Before removing an option, the municipality needs evidence that lets it weigh staff time against the value to users.

## The pilot counts bookings, not people

The municipality’s April 8, 2026, pilot report, *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, records 96 online bookings and 24 phone bookings during eight weeks at two facilities. Those figures describe transactions. They do not tell us how many different people booked, or anything about their age, abilities, or digital skills.

We therefore cannot use the phone bookings to estimate the share of residents unable to book online. A phone booking does not prove that someone cannot use the website. It gives us a reason to find out why people still use the phone.

That distinction matters. The case for keeping both options during a further trial should rest on what we need to learn, without claiming the pilot has already answered it.

## Duplicate administration deserves a fair test

The staff memorandum for the board’s June 18 meeting proposes ending phone booking at all seven facilities in September. Its reason is duplicate administration: staff must enter information through two workflows. That is a concrete objection, and any proposal to retain both channels must address it.

But the documents contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings. The board is being asked to remove an option across all seven facilities without that basis for weighing the benefit. Identifying duplicate work does not establish how much time a change would save.

## Decide the trial’s cost, then gather the evidence

During the six-month trial we propose, staff would record the time spent on each booking channel and invite users to explain voluntarily why they chose phone or online booking. I do not oppose online booking. The question is whether removing phone booking is justified.

Öppna beslut makes no claim to have costed or funded the trial. The board must assess its cost. It should postpone the permanent change and approve a six-month trial with both options, addressing that cost in its decision. Afterward, it can decide whether to remove, modify, or retain a booking channel, with evidence about both the work involved and its value to users.
