## Findings and smallest supported repairs

### Finding 1 — Source support: unclaimed costing becomes an asserted absence of costing

**Draft passage:** “Öppna beslut has neither costed the trial nor claimed to have secured its funding.”

**What changed:** The first coordinated verb is “costed,” so the sentence categorically denies that costing has occurred. The second coordinated verb is “claimed,” so only the financing clause retains the source’s knowledge/claim limitation. S9 places both financing and costing inside the association’s nonclaim: “gör inget anspråk på att ha finansierat eller kostnadsberäknat försöket.” The source does not establish that the trial has not been costed.

**Smallest supported repair:** “Öppna beslut has not claimed to have costed the trial or secured its funding.”

This moves both activities within the scope of “claimed,” preserves the existing financing wording, and preserves the subsequent board responsibility and uncertainty about cost. No other passage requires revision for this finding.
