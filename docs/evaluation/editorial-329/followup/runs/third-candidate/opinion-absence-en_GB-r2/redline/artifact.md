# Investigate rehearsal use before letting Bellwick Hall’s wing

*By Amina Reed, chair of Room to Rehearse*

Bellwick Hall’s trustees should investigate evening rehearsal use before committing its former storage wing to a three-year commercial letting. As chair of Room to Rehearse, I want that option tested before a choice is made.

The trustees’ paper of 3 July 2026 makes a serious case for predictable income to help meet maintenance costs. Commercial letting may prove the better option. But the paper names neither a prospective tenant nor a rental figure, so it cannot yet tell residents what that income would be.

Our survey of 14 local music groups gives a reason to investigate an alternative. Nine said they would consider using a rehearsal room on weekday evenings. Those responses are expressions of interest, not booking commitments. We did not ask what groups would pay or how often they would attend. The survey is a reason to investigate a rehearsal room, not a promise that it will pay its way.

Our treasurer’s signed note confirms that we have not prepared a cost estimate for converting the wing. I accept that gap. Our submission also makes no claim that we have secured the building owner’s consent for rehearsal use; that statement does not establish whether consent exists.

A feasibility study should examine sound insulation, access, likely running costs and potential income. That would give the trustees a basis for comparing rehearsal use with commercial letting.

I am asking the trustees to defer the three-year letting decision for eight weeks, commission the study and publish a comparison of the two uses before choosing.
