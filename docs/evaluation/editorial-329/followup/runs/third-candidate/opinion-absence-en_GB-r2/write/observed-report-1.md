## Source-support findings

### F1 — Unsupported change in whose knowledge is described

Draft: “though we have no evidence that the study can be completed within that period”.

Source: “There is no evidence that a study can be completed within eight weeks; this is her requested timetable.” The source package is complete as supplied material, not an exhaustive inventory of the association’s private knowledge. The draft turns a limitation of the supplied evidence into a first-person factual assertion about the association’s possession of evidence. The precise negation is not excluded, as the counterexample in entry 33 demonstrates.

Smallest supported repair: delete “, though we have no evidence that the study can be completed within that period”, leaving “I am asking for an eight-week deferral.” This keeps the supported duration explicitly framed as a request and does not promise that the study can be finished within that duration. If additional explanation is wanted, “Eight weeks is my requested timetable” is supported by S16, but is not necessary for the minimal repair.

All other reviewed propositions are supported factual claims, supported attributed positions, or advocacy within the brief. In particular, retain the definite statement that no conversion-cost estimate has been prepared, and retain the distinction between an absent consent representation and an unknown actual consent status.
