# Briar County volunteers share records for new donations

Briar County Theater Archive cataloged 47 incoming donations during an eight-week pilot with Index Lantern. Volunteers could consult the same records, according to the archive’s pilot note. The collection coordinator would use the catalog again for new donations, with further testing before bringing in older collections.

The archive holds donated programs, photographs, and posters from local productions. Volunteers had described new donations in separate spreadsheets. In February 2026, the collection team chose to test a shared catalog for incoming donations; existing collections stayed outside the pilot.

The team agreed on fields for production title, year, donor, and location, retaining a free-text note for uncertain dates. Index Lantern imported the team’s spreadsheet template and provided one remote training session for five volunteers. The archive chose the descriptions and checked the imported records.

“Agreeing on the fields took two meetings. The import was the short part,” collection coordinator Lena Ortiz wrote in an email supplied for this Index Lantern–published case.

## Keeping uncertainty visible

The archive’s May 6 pilot note records that 11 records retained uncertain dates. Ortiz explained the distinction in her email: “We kept the uncertain dates visible. An empty field and an estimated year tell the next volunteer different things.”

The note contains no before-and-after time measurement, cost calculation, or assessment of public access. The archive will review the field definitions before deciding whether to move older records into the catalog.

“I would use the shared catalog again for new donations. I would test the descriptions on more posters before we bring in the older collection,” Ortiz wrote.
