## Findings requiring repair

1. **Source support: absence of a comparison becomes absence of supporting material.** Draft: “Det finns inget underlag för en jämförelse med andra leverantörer.” Source: “No comparison with another supplier is available.” These are different knowledge claims: an unavailable comparison does not establish that no basis for one exists. Counterexample: the source package could contain no completed supplier comparison, while Elm Quay has test notes permitting one. Smallest supported repair: “Någon jämförelse med en annan leverantör finns inte tillgänglig.” Keeping “i underlaget” as a scope marker is also possible: “Underlaget innehåller ingen jämförelse med en annan leverantör.” This finding does not claim that a comparison was actually made.

2. **Source support: expansion decision becomes continuation decision.** Draft: “Innan teamet beslutar om en fortsättning ska det kontrollera hur kategorierna fungerar för större reparationer.” Source: “The trial has not yet expanded; the team will decide after checking how the categories work for larger repairs.” In context the pending decision concerns expansion. “En fortsättning” can instead assert a pending decision on whether to continue the existing trial at all. Counterexample: the team has already decided to keep using the log in the original buildings but has yet to decide whether to expand it. That is compatible with the source but contradicts the draft’s broader pending continuation decision. Smallest repair: replace “en fortsättning” with “en utökning”. The supported larger-repair check and ordering should stay.
