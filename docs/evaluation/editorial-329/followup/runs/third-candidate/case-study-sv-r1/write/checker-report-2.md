## Translated-quotation comparison

### First quotation

Source: “We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log.”

Draft: “Vi ville att kvällsskiftet skulle kunna se vad morgonskiftet redan hade gjort. Kategorierna tog vi fram själva; Svale hjälpte oss att lägga in dem i loggen”.

Assessment: meaning, stance, and certainty preserved in idiomatic Swedish. “Skulle kunna se” correctly expresses intended access, not achieved universal use. “Tog vi fram själva” renders the team’s authorship, explicitly settled by S7, while the adjacent assistance clause keeps the division of work. The direct, practical collective voice remains. No translation finding.

### Second quotation

Source: “We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

Draft: “Vi lade mer tid på att komma överens om kategorierna än på att registrera de första anmälningarna. Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan nästa byggnad kommer i gång.”

Assessment: the first sentence preserves the comparison, initial-report scope, and practical voice. The second preserves the conditional first-person recommendation and refers back to category-agreement time.

Translation finding T1: “innan nästa byggnad kommer i gång” transfers the English building-as-activity metonym into Swedish with an unclear activity. An existing building does not itself “komma i gång”; without naming the trial, the phrase can instead evoke a construction project beginning. The supplied context settles that the next building would begin participation in the log trial, and does not supply a construction project. This is a concrete referent obstruction, not a proposed synonym or a change in Lind’s advice.

Smallest supported repair: “Jag skulle avsätta den tiden innan försöket kommer i gång i nästa byggnad.” This names the implicit trial while retaining “skulle”, “den tiden”, and Lind’s straightforward spoken register. It does not assert that expansion is approved: the recommendation remains hypothetical. No underlying source-support defect is assigned to the intended reading of the current sentence.

### Third quotation

Source: “I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

Draft: “Jag skulle välja att göra om försöket. Att ha en gemensam vy över anmälningarna hjälper oss, men jag skulle avsätta en extra vecka för förberedelser.”

Assessment: the translation keeps the hypothetical choice, asserted experienced benefit, collective beneficiary, contrasting reservation, and exact extra week. “Göra om försöket” in the preceding “skulle välja” construction reads as choosing the trial again, not an assertion that it failed and must be repaired. “Gemensam vy” is ordinary interface-related language in this shared-log context and preserves the single-view meaning. No translation finding.
