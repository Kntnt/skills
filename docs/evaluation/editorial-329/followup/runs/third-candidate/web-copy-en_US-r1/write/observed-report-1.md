- “Review your shared room’s booking routine” is an imperative invitation. Its presupposition that this is an available service is accounted for in claims 1–2. The possessive addresses the intended board; it does not assert property ownership or a particular reader's circumstances.
- “What the review includes” labels the supported inclusions in claims 5–17; it introduces no extra deliverable.
- “Price and what your board needs to prepare” labels claims 20–27. The requested preparation is describing rules and showing current instructions, without an invented document-production requirement.
- “Express interest and check whether it fits” is process advocacy, supported by claims 28 and 32–38. It does not assert that every association qualifies.
- “You get” governs both listed deliverables; each is accounted for above. The two possible simplifications are proposed possibilities, not changes already implemented or guaranteed improvements.
- “Use” and “Open” invite action; the claims about the action's destination and consequences are accounted for above. The draft does not imply direct booking, payment on submission, free service, payment terms, or a summary-delivery deadline.
