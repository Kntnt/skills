# Review your shared room’s booking routine

Svale helps boards of smaller Swedish tenant-owner associations take stock of how a shared room is booked. The review gives your board a shared picture of the current routine and two possible ways to simplify it.

## What the review includes

The review covers the booking routine for one room in one association. You get:

- A 45-minute video meeting with two representatives from your board.
- A written summary of the current steps, points that are unclear, and two possible simplifications.

The focus is on understanding the routine you already have. The review does not include legal advice, installation of a booking system, or interviews with residents. Svale does not guarantee any particular time savings or fewer conflicts.

## Price and what your board needs to prepare

The fixed price is **SEK 4,800, including VAT**, for one room in one association.

Your board needs to be able to describe its existing booking rules and show the instructions currently given to residents. Two board representatives take part in the video meeting.

## Express interest and check whether it fits

Use the interest form to start a conversation with Svale about whether this review suits your association. The form asks for your name, association, and email address.

**Expressing interest is not an order.** No payment details are required, and submitting the form does not book a meeting.

Svale will reply by email within three business days to check whether the review is a fit and suggest a meeting time.
