# Review your communal room’s booking routine

Svale helps boards of smaller Swedish tenant-owner associations understand how bookings for a communal room currently work. The review gives your board a shared picture of the routine and two possible ways to simplify it.

## What the review includes

The service covers one communal room in one association. It includes:

- A 45-minute video meeting with two board representatives.
- A written summary of the current booking steps, points that are unclear and two possible simplifications.

The summary gives your board something concrete to discuss when considering changes to the booking routine.

## What your board needs to prepare

Your board needs to be able to describe its existing booking rules and show the instructions currently provided to residents. Two representatives take part in the video meeting.

The review is relevant if your board wants to take stock of those arrangements and consider possible simplifications.

## Price and scope

The fixed price is **SEK 4,800 including VAT**, covering one room in one association.

The review does not include legal advice, installation of a booking system or interviews with residents. Svale does not guarantee a particular time saving or fewer disputes.
