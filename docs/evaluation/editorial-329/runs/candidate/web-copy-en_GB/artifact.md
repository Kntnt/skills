# A clearer picture of your room booking routine

Svale helps boards of smaller Swedish tenant-owner associations review how a communal room is booked. The review gives your board a shared picture of the current routine and two possible ways to simplify it.

If your board wants to understand the steps involved and where instructions are unclear, this review offers a defined scope: one communal room in one association.

## What the review includes

The service consists of:

- A 45-minute video meeting with two representatives of your board.
- A written summary of the current booking steps, points of uncertainty and two possible simplifications.

The summary gives your board something concrete to consider when discussing its booking routine. Svale does not guarantee a particular time saving or fewer conflicts.

## Price, preparation and scope

The fixed price is **SEK 4,800 including VAT**, covering one room in one association.

Your board needs to be able to describe its existing booking rules and show the instructions currently provided to residents. Two board representatives take part in the video meeting.

The review does not include legal advice, installation of a booking system or interviews with residents. Its focus is the existing booking routine and possible simplifications.

## Express your interest and find out what happens next

To express interest, provide your name, association and email address using the form below. Submitting the form is not an order, and no payment details are required.

Svale will reply by email within three working days to check whether the service is a suitable fit and suggest a meeting time. The form itself does not book a meeting.
