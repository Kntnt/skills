# Lervik should test both booking routes before cutting one

*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

Lervik’s municipal executive board should postpone a permanent switch to online-only booking for community venues. Our association, Öppna beslut, proposes a six-month trial with both telephone and web booking available at all seven venues. Before removing a service, the board needs evidence that allows it to weigh staff time against its value to users.

## The pilot counts bookings, not people

The council’s pilot report, *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, dated 8 April 2026, records 96 web bookings and 24 telephone bookings during an eight-week trial at two venues. Those figures establish that both routes were used. They do not establish how many people depended on either.

The report counts bookings, not unique individuals, and measures neither age, disability nor digital confidence. It cannot tell us what proportion of residents cannot book online. A telephone booking is a reason to ask why someone chose that route; it is no proof that they could not use the web.

These limits matter to the decision. A trial covering two venues leaves questions that should be answered before one booking route is withdrawn across all seven.

## Double administration deserves a measured answer

The officers’ report prepared for the board’s 18 June meeting proposes ending telephone booking at every venue from September. Its stated reason is to spare staff from entering information through two separate processes.

That is a legitimate operational concern. Staff time belongs in this discussion. Yet the papers contain neither measurements of the time involved nor a calculation of financial savings. The board is being asked to remove a booking route without knowing the scale of the administrative benefit.

I support digital booking. But its availability does not settle whether telephone booking should disappear. That judgement requires a comparison the current papers cannot provide.

## Six months to establish what each route offers

During our proposed trial, staff would record time spent on each booking route and invite users to explain, voluntarily, why they chose telephone or web booking. This would give the board evidence about both administrative work and users’ reasons.

Öppna beslut has neither costed nor secured funding for the trial. Keeping both routes open has a cost that the board must consider; our proposal does not make that uncertainty disappear.

The board should defer the permanent withdrawal, address the trial’s cost and approve six months with both routes available at all seven venues. It can then decide whether a route should be removed, changed or retained.
