# Keep the Saturday repair session under review

The tool library should retain its Saturday repair session for the next quarter and record how many repairs can be completed during it. The 2025 annual figures show that repairs use volunteer time, but they do not establish whether a different opening pattern would improve completion.

## Repairs accounted for 40 volunteer hours

The library's 2025 annual log records 120 volunteer hours in total, of which 40 were spent on repairs. Twenty tools were repaired and six remained awaiting parts at year end. The log does not record when parts were ordered or how long each repair took.

Repairs therefore accounted for one third of recorded volunteer time. That is a description of how the hours were used, not a measure of efficiency: the log cannot show whether the six outstanding tools were delayed by the session's length or by parts availability.

## Record the constraint before changing the hours

The tool library's log for the next quarter should distinguish tools awaiting parts from work awaiting volunteer time. Until that distinction is available, the figures in ‘Repairs accounted for 40 volunteer hours’ support monitoring the Saturday session rather than claiming that longer opening would clear the outstanding repairs.
