# Lervik needs evidence before ending phone bookings

*Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

Lervik’s municipal executive committee should postpone a permanent switch to online-only booking for community venues. Our association, Öppna beslut, proposes a six-month trial across all seven venues, keeping both telephone and web bookings. Before removing a service, the committee needs evidence of its value to users and the staff time it requires.

## Booking figures leave key questions unanswered

The municipality’s pilot report, *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, dated 8 April 2026, records 96 web bookings and 24 telephone bookings during an eight-week trial at two venues. Those figures establish that both routes were used.

They count bookings, however, rather than individual people. The report does not measure age, disability or digital confidence. It cannot tell us what proportion of Lervik’s residents are unable to book online.

A telephone booking is no proof that someone cannot use the web. It is a reason to find out why they still use the telephone. Removing that option before investigating its use leaves a question unanswered at precisely the point when the answer matters.

## The workload objection deserves a measurement

The officers’ report prepared for the committee’s meeting on 18 June proposes ending telephone bookings at all seven venues from September. Its stated reason is to spare staff from entering information through two workflows.

That is a concrete administrative concern. Keeping both routes requires the committee to weigh staff costs against their value to users. Yet the papers contain neither measurements of staff time nor a calculation of financial savings. The case for removing telephone booking therefore rests on an unmeasured benefit.

I do not oppose digital booking. I oppose making a permanent decision on evidence that leaves this balance unresolved.

## Use six months to inform the decision

During our proposed trial, staff would record the time spent on each booking route. Users would be invited to explain voluntarily why they chose the telephone or the web. Extending the trial to all seven venues would broaden the evidence beyond the two already studied.

Öppna beslut has neither costed nor funded this proposal. The committee must consider its cost; retaining both routes cannot simply be assumed to be cost-free.

The municipal executive committee should postpone the permanent switch, address the cost of the proposed six-month trial and authorise it with both routes retained. It can then decide whether a booking route should be removed, changed or kept, weighing measured staff time against what users say they need.
