# Lervik needs evidence before ending phone bookings

*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

Lervik’s municipal executive board should postpone a permanent switch to online-only booking for community venues. Our association, Öppna beslut, proposes a six-month trial with both phone and web booking at all seven venues. Before removing an option, the board needs evidence about the staff time it requires and its value to users.

## The pilot counted bookings, not barriers

The municipality’s April 8, 2026, pilot report records 96 web bookings and 24 phone bookings during eight weeks at two venues. Those figures establish that both channels were used. They do not establish how many residents need help booking online.

The report counts bookings, not unique people. It measures neither age nor disability nor digital skills. Using the phone bookings to claim that a particular share of residents cannot book online would stretch the evidence beyond what it says.

But those limits also matter when deciding to withdraw phone booking. A phone booking does not prove that someone cannot use the web. It gives the municipality a reason to ask why that person chose the phone.

## Administrative work deserves a measurement

The staff recommendation prepared for the board’s June 18 meeting proposes ending phone bookings at all seven venues from September. Its rationale is that staff should no longer have to enter information through two workflows.

That is a concrete objection to keeping both channels, and it deserves a concrete answer. Yet the documents contain no measurement of staff time and no calculation of financial savings. The board is being asked to make a permanent change without the evidence needed to weigh the workload against the value users receive.

I do not oppose online booking. I oppose treating this decision as ready when the central tradeoff remains unmeasured.

## Test both channels, then decide

During the proposed six-month trial, staff would record the time spent on each booking channel. Users would be invited to explain voluntarily why they chose phone or web booking. Extending the trial to all seven venues would also move the inquiry beyond the original two.

Öppna beslut has neither costed nor funded this proposal. The board must assess its cost; keeping both channels cannot simply be assumed to be free.

The board should postpone the permanent switch and approve a six-month trial after addressing that cost. With evidence about workload and users’ reasons, it can then decide whether to remove, modify, or retain a booking channel.
