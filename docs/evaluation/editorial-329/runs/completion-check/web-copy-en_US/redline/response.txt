# A clearer picture of your shared room’s booking routine

Svale helps boards of smaller Swedish tenant-owner associations review how residents book a shared room. The review gives your board a shared understanding of the current routine and two possible ways to simplify it.

## What the review includes

The service covers one shared room in one association. It includes:

- A 45-minute video meeting with two board representatives.
- A written summary of the current booking steps, points that are unclear, and two possible simplifications.

The focus is on understanding the routine you already have. The written summary gives your board a basis for considering the two possible changes.

## What your board needs to prepare

Your board needs to be able to describe its existing booking rules and show the instructions currently provided to residents. Two board representatives take part in the video meeting.

## Price and scope

The fixed price is **SEK 4,800, including VAT**, for one room in one association.

The review does not include legal advice, installation of a booking system, or interviews with residents. Svale does not guarantee a specific time saving or fewer conflicts.
