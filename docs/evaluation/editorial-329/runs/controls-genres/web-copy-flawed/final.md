# Booking review for your association's shared room

Svale reviews the booking steps for one shared room in one housing association.

## Price and included work

The Svale review costs SEK 4,800 including VAT. It includes:

- A 45-minute video call with two board representatives.
- A written account of the booking steps, unclear points and two possible simplifications.

## Express interest in a Svale review

[Open the expression-of-interest form](https://example.invalid/svale/intresse).

The link opens an expression-of-interest form asking for a name, association and email address. It places no order, requires no payment details and books no meeting. Svale replies by email within three working days to discuss whether the review is suitable and suggest a meeting time.
