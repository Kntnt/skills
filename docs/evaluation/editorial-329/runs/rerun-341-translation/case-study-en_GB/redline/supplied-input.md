# Elm Quay brings repair reports into one shared view

Elm Quay Housing’s maintenance team trialled a shared repair log in two buildings to help staff on different shifts see the same information. Supervisor Maya Lind would repeat the trial, with more preparation time. The eight-week exercise recorded a shorter median time to assignment, but its findings do not establish a software effect.

*Published by Svale Systems. This case uses fictional source material.*

Elm Quay manages 640 flats. Before the trial, telephone reports and emails were stored separately. In September 2025, its maintenance team decided to test a shared log, choosing Svale Systems after checking whether it could show the status of each repair.

“We wanted the evening shift to see what the morning shift had already done. The categories were ours; Svale helped us put them into the log,” Lind said in an email interview.

The maintenance team designed the categories and kept telephone reporting open for residents. Svale configured the log and trained six staff during two sessions. For Lind, the preparation brought a lesson for any further rollout.

“We spent more time agreeing on the categories than entering the first reports. I would set that time aside before the next building starts.”

Elm Quay’s internal trial note, dated 4 December 2025, recorded 31 repair reports during the eight weeks. It excluded emergencies and work ordered before the trial.

Median time from report to assignment was two working days during the trial, compared with three in the preceding eight-week period. The periods had different workloads, and the note explicitly did not attribute the difference to the software.

The figures concern assignment, rather than completion of repairs. There were no measurements of cost, resident satisfaction or completion time, so the trial provides no measured answers on those outcomes.

Lind’s assessment of the experience retained a practical reservation.

“I would choose to do the trial again. Having one view of the reports helps us, but I would leave an extra week for preparation.”

The trial has not yet expanded. Before deciding whether to extend it, the team will check how the categories work for larger repairs. Lind’s willingness to repeat the exercise is a judgement about Elm Quay’s experience, rather than a recommendation for every housing company.
