# Lervik should test both booking routes before closing one

*By Sanna Ek, spokesperson for Öppna beslut*

Lervik’s municipal executive committee should postpone a permanent switch to online-only booking for community premises. Öppna beslut proposes a six-month trial with both telephone and web booking across all seven venues. Before removing a service, the municipality needs evidence about its workload and its value to users.

## The pilot counted bookings, not barriers

The municipality’s pilot report, *Bokning av föreningslokaler*, dated 8 April 2026, records 96 web bookings and 24 telephone bookings during eight weeks at two venues. Those figures establish that both routes were used. They do not explain why.

The report counts bookings, not individual people, and measures neither age, disability nor digital confidence. We cannot use its telephone figures to calculate how many residents are unable to book online. A telephone booking is a reason to investigate why someone chose that route.

That distinction matters. An argument for keeping telephone booking must respect the limits of the evidence. So must a proposal to abolish it.

## The administrative objection needs measuring

The officer report prepared for the committee’s 18 June meeting proposes removing telephone booking at all seven venues from September. Its stated reason is to spare staff from entering information through two separate workflows.

Duplicate administration is a relevant objection. Staff time has a cost, and the committee needs to weigh that cost against the value users get from having a choice. Yet the papers contain neither measurements of the time involved nor a calculation of financial savings.

That leaves a gap at the centre of the proposal. The committee is being asked to make a permanent change without the information needed to assess its stated benefit. I do not oppose digital booking. I oppose making this decision on evidence that cannot answer the central question.

## Use six months to inform the decision

During our proposed trial, the administration should record the time spent on each booking route and invite users to explain voluntarily why they choose telephone or web booking. Extending the trial to all seven venues would allow the decision to draw on experience across the service.

Öppna beslut has neither funded nor costed this proposal. The committee must assess that cost.

It should postpone the permanent switch and approve a six-month trial with both routes, making provision for its cost. Afterwards, it can decide whether to remove, change or retain a booking route, weighing administrative cost against value to users.
