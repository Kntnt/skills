## Findings

**Fixed**

1. **Headline:** "Unlock your association's full potential" promised an unspecified benefit, used corporate filler ("unlock") and didn't say what the page offers.
2. **Opening paragraph:** it said nothing specific. It repeated itself ("complex reality … growing complexity") and talked about "decision-makers" in the abstract. "Take the thumb out" is a word-for-word translation of the Swedish idiom *ta tummen ur* and makes no sense in English. "A fantastic journey" promised something the offer doesn't include.
3. **"It" with nothing to refer to:** four sections opened with "It" before the page had said what "it" was. The review and Svale weren't named until the last paragraph.
4. **Vague headings:** "Start", "It", "More", "Again", "Things" and "Important" didn't say what their sections contained.
5. **Split-up offer:** the price, the scope and the two deliverables of one offer sat in four one-sentence sections, all built the same way.
6. **Misleading link:** "Book and pay now" contradicted the page's own statement that the link opens an expression-of-interest form that places no order, takes no payment and books no meeting. The link also came before the offer was described, far from the explanation of what it does.
7. **British wording:** "working days" in copy for an American reader.

**Unresolved**

8. **"two board representatives":** the text doesn't say whose board they come from, the housing association's or Svale's, so a reader can't tell who takes part in the call. Only someone who knows the offer can settle this, so the wording is unchanged.
