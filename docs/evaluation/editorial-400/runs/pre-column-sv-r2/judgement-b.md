# Judgement b

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md`, which runs from line 4 to line 25. There are two differences. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline, the paragraph breaks and every body sentence except one are byte-identical.

## 1. Differences

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Mötesmallen saknar en ruta för syftet`.
   Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning**, and also a change of taste.
   - Meaning: the body names one missing box, the box "för det beslut vi ska kunna fatta när vi går därifrån". The new headline names a different object, a box "för syftet", and so tells the reader what the missing box is for. The body never says that. It says the template does not ask what the time should lead to, and the author's own proposal is a question ("vad behöver vi förstå tillsammans?"), not a box for the purpose.
   - Taste: the old headline is a deliberately oblique column headline, and it is part of the piece's voice. Its ambivalence ("the box that isn't there") matches how the column ends, where the author doubts whether one more box helps. Nothing about it is misspelt, ungrammatical or contradictory. Replacing it with an explanatory label is a preference, justified in the reply by headline limits ("37 tecken och 6 ord, inom rubrikens gränser").
   - Scope, certainty, attribution, chronology and causality: no change. "Mötesmallen" still points to the one template the column looks at.
   - Consequent on nothing. Nothing else follows from it.
2. **Paragraph 3, dash.** Before: `något annat — att tid` (spaced em dash). After: `något annat – att tid` (spaced en dash).
   Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. The spaced en dash is the usual Swedish form in running text. The spacing was already there. Nothing else in the sentence changed.

Frontmatter and formatting: unchanged. The heading level, the byline line and the paragraphing are identical.

## 2. The account

1. **Headline: reported, and mostly accurate.** It is reported as finding 1, "Rubriken gick inte att förstå på egen hand … Rubriken är nu ”Mötesmallen saknar en ruta för syftet”". Under Påståenden it is also reported as a changed claim: "**Ändrade:** ett, i rubriken … Förut sa rubriken bara att en ospecificerad ruta inte finns. Nu säger den att mötesmallen saknar en ruta för syftet."
   - The account is honest about the meaning shift. It says: "Observera att brödtexten konkret talar om en ruta för ”det beslut vi ska kunna fatta”. Rubriken säger ”syftet”…"
   - It overstates two things. It presents the oblique headline as a defect, not as a choice of voice. And it presents "syftet" as grounded in the text, when "syftet" is the reviewer's own synthesis. The body's "också när syftet är att undersöka något tillsammans" uses the word for a different purpose, and the reply does not cite it.
2. **Dash: reported, and accurate.** The reply says: "det engelska långa tankstrecket har ersatts av det svenska korta tankstrecket med mellanslag." That is correct. The em dash already had spaces, and "med mellanslag" describes the result. The reply lists this outside the findings, and outside the budget of one correction, as a mechanical fix. That is a fair classification.

The reply's statements "Borttagna: inga" and "Tillagda: inga" are accurate for the body. "Kartan … är oförändrad" is accurate for the frontmatter.

## 4. R1

**Fail.**

This passage in the reply decides it:

> "**Rubriken gick inte att förstå på egen hand.** Den gamla rubriken, ”Rutan som inte finns”, sa inte vilken ruta det gällde … Rubriken är nu ”Mötesmallen saknar en ruta för syftet”: 37 tecken och 6 ord, inom rubrikens gränser."

The input is a clean column. Its only visible defect is a locale dash, and the Skill fixed that correctly. The Skill then spent its correction budget on rewriting the headline, which is working voice, to satisfy a headline guideline. That is a taste change, the kind R1 forbids on clean text. The rewrite also changes the meaning of the headline's claim: the missing box becomes a box "för syftet" instead of the body's box for a decision. The reply admits the tension but does not resolve it.
