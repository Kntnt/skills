## 1. Differences

A line-by-line diff of `work/input.md` against the returned text (lines 4–28 of `response.md`) shows exactly one difference. The title, standfirst, byline, body paragraphs, quotations, numbers, closing disclosure and link are byte-identical. There is no frontmatter in either version, and no formatting changed.

1. Before: `## Kunden vill ge förberedelserna mer tid` (38 characters). After: `## Maya Lind ser tillbaka på reparationsloggen` (43 characters).
   **Class: a change of taste that also changes what a claim says.** Two things changed:
   - **attribution**: the wish moves from "Kunden" to "Maya Lind" by name.
   - **meaning**: the heading no longer states that the customer wants more time for preparation. It now says only that Lind "looks back on" the log.

   This is not the repair of a visible defect. The original heading accurately describes its section, which is what the anatomy asks a subheading to do. The same reservation already appears in the standfirst ("skulle avsätta mer tid för förberedelser") before the heading does. So the stated harm, that the heading reveals the quote before Lind says it, is not a defect the text actually has. The replacement is also vaguer: it no longer says what the section is about. It is also longer than the 36–39-character range the frozen expectation says the text's subheadings meet.
