# Judgement a

The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`, lines 4–27. I compared it line by line with `work/input.md`. The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and all other formatting are identical. There are two differences.

## 1. Differences

1. **Heading (input line 8).**
   Before: `# Mallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`
   After: `# Vår mötesmall har rutor för allt utom poängen`
   Class: **a change to what a claim says**, in **scope** and **attribution**. "Mallen" (an unnamed template) becomes "Vår mötesmall": the template is now named as a meeting template (scope) and is now said to be "ours", the writer's and her colleagues' (attribution of ownership). The body already says both things ("Jag granskar bibliotekets mötesmall", "våra möten", "Av Nora Vik, bibliotekarie"), so the heading claims nothing that the input does not already state. The change from "en ruta för allt" to "rutor för allt" is part of the same rewrite. It is a **change of taste** and does not change the meaning: both forms say that the template provides a box for every item except the point. The Skill presents the whole heading change as the repair of a visible defect (finding 4: the heading cannot be understood on its own). I accept that the defect is visible, because "Mallen" in the heading has no antecedent until the first sentence of the body. The defect is mild, though. A column heading that works as a teaser is a normal form, so the repair is close to a genre-guideline change.

2. **Dash in the paragraph that starts "Där börjar tvivlet" (input line 22).**
   Before: `... genom att utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien som svarar ...`
   After: `... genom att utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien som svarar ...`
   Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. The spaced em dash becomes the spaced en dash that Swedish typography uses as tankstreck. It does not depend on the other difference.

Nothing else differs. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`) is unchanged.

It wrote none of them and left them to the writer, so it did not rewrite a mostly clean column to fit anatomy guidelines. Because the repair is small, faithful to the body and fully reported, I judge that it does not reach the level of the rewrite for taste that R1 forbids.
