## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md` (lines 4–27). The frontmatter, the byline, the paragraph breaks and the trailing newline are identical. There are two differences.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Mallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`. After: `# Mötesmallen har en ruta för allt utom poängen`. Class: **change to what a claim says, by scope**. The headline's subject narrows from an unspecified "the template" to "the meeting template". This also changes the meaning of the headline, because it now names the referent itself instead of leaving the reader to find it in the first sentence. The narrowing matches the body ("bibliotekets mötesmall", paragraph 1), so no surviving claim now says more than the body does. This is not hardening, because the headline asserts nothing new about the template. The defect the Skill gives as the reason is the definite form "Mallen" with no referent before the body. That defect can be seen in the input, but it is mild: a column headline whose referent the first sentence resolves is ordinary practice. The edit sits close to a change of taste.
2. **Dash in paragraph 6.** Before: `utöka formuläret — bibliotekarien`. After: `utöka formuläret – bibliotekarien`. Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. The em dash becomes the Swedish en dash. The spacing was already there in the input and did not change. This difference does not follow from difference 1.

No other character differs.
