## 1. Differences

I compared `work/input.md` line by line with the fenced text in `response.md` (lines 4–24). There is exactly one difference. The text has no frontmatter, and the formatting is unchanged: same heading levels, the bold standfirst, the italic report title, the ”…” quotation marks and the paragraph breaks.

Class: **a change of taste** that also changes what the headline claims (**scope**).
