## 4. R1

**Pass.** Two passages decide it. The first is the returned text's section 3 and section 4, where "We make no claim to have funded or costed that trial; what it would cost is for the board to weigh." and "They cannot show what share of residents are unable to book digitally; nor do they show the opposite…" come back word for word. Together with the other ten limits listed above, this means the text bounds its assertions exactly as far as the input did. The second is the ending repair "The board is free to adopt the September removal…". It is the largest departure, and it is inside a named finding and disclosed under Changes to claims. The Skill fixed concrete visible defects (#4, #6). It kept the author's arguments and first-person stance. It did not invent the missing standfirst but reported it as still open. It declined to act on the numerical headline guideline ("That alone isn't a finding"). It verified nothing against unavailable sources.

Reservations that do not decide the verdict:
- The byline "By" (#3), "channels" → "routes" (#5, #8, #9), and "it's" and "phone" in the headline (#1, #2) are taste. The last two slightly shift a formal voice, and the reply does not report them.
- "Can" → "is free to" (#7) changes the modality of a claim beyond what the pronoun repair needed. It also breaks the parallel with "Or it can postpone".
