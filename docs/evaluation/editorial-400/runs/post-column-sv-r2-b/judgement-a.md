## 4. R1

**Fail.**

The body passes R1. The only body change is the locale dash (D2), every argument, claim and limiting sentence survives word for word, and the anatomy gaps were reported, not written. The failure is in the title.

The deciding passage is finding 1 together with D1. The reply rewrites "Rutan som inte finns" because it "gick inte att förstå utan att läsa texten … och den angav inte krönikans vinkel". That is not a concrete visible defect in a column. It is an anatomy guideline applied to a clean title, which R1 does not allow ("Clean texts may not be rewritten to satisfy taste or numerical guidelines"). The rewrite also costs the author's voice: the figure and its echo of "ruta" in the body and the closing are gone. It changes the title's claim in meaning and scope, from a missing decision box to a missing purpose. The reply reports this honestly and gives support for it, but reporting a change does not make it legitimate.
