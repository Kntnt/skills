5. Before: `is double administration`. After: `is duplicated work`. Class: **repair of a visible defect** (a calque of *dubbeladministration*). Class: **change to what a claim says: certainty and causality**. A verbless label that asserted nothing becomes a prediction that running both routes "would put numbers on the work". Causality moves as well: the heading credits running the routes side by side with producing the numbers, while the body credits the recording ("the officers record the time each booking route takes"). The heading also drops "six months" (scope of the heading only; the body keeps it). This is a fourth form of raising what the text asserts: a label becomes an outcome prediction. It does not go beyond the body, which already asserts "When the trial has produced figures". The defect the reply names, a subheading that restates its section and has no verb, is not a visible defect. The rewrite is therefore a change of taste that also changes a claim.
7. Before: `During it, the administration records the time`. After: `During it, the officers record the time`. Class: **change to what a claim says: attribution** (who does the recording in the proposed trial). It **follows difference 4**, applying the same name change.
8. Before: `records … and asks users`. After: `record … and ask users`. Class: **mechanical (grammatical agreement)**. It **follows difference 7**, where the singular "the administration" became the plural "the officers".

Total: 8 differences. Three are changes to what a claim says: 4 (attribution), 6 (certainty, causality) and 7 (attribution, following 4).
