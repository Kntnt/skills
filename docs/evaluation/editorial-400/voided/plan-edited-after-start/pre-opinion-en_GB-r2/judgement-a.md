## 4. R1

**Pass.**

The deciding evidence is that every claim-bearing sentence in the input comes back with its claim intact. The Skill's statement "No claim was removed, changed or added" is true when checked line by line. All thirteen limiting sentences and headings survive with their limits whole. Nothing was hardened. The only visible defect that needed repair, the section-opening "It" whose antecedent sat in the subheading, was fixed by naming the actor the text already meant ("The board can adopt…"). The missing standfirst was reported and not invented. The Skill made no claim to have checked unavailable sources.

Reservation, not enough to fail: D1 (the "By" byline) and D2/D4/D5 (the "channel"/"route" term change) are changes of taste made to a text that had no defect there. R1 bars rewriting a text to satisfy taste. Here that amounts to four words on three lines, it leaves the author's voice and arguments untouched, and the reply reports all of it accurately. I do not count it as rewriting, but a stricter reading could.
