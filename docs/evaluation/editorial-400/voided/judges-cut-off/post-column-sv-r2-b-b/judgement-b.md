Files read: `work/input.md` and `response.md`. The returned text is the fenced block in `response.md`, lines 4–25. I compared it line by line with the input.

## 1. Differences

I found two differences. The frontmatter (`genre: column`, `technique: none`, `language: sv`), the byline, the paragraph breaks and all other formatting are identical.

1. **Headline.** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall frågar efter tid men inte syfte`.
   Class: a **change of taste**, and it is also a **change to what a claim says**, by **scope** and **meaning**.
   - Why taste: the old headline had no visible defect. It is an allusive column headline that the first paragraph explains at once ("Någon ruta för det beslut … finns inte"), and it carries the author's voice. The reply rewrote it because of a headline guideline: it should stand alone, state the angle, and fit a length (54 characters).
   - Scope: the old headline named one missing box. The new one characterises what the whole template asks for and does not ask for.
   - Meaning: the body says the missing box is the one for a *beslut*. The headline says *syfte*. The reply admits this: "Ordet ”syfte” är rubrikens eget". The body partly supports the wider word ("Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om"; "varför vi behöver just varandras tid"), but the headline is still a new formulation of the claim and not one the author wrote.
   - Hardening: none of the three forms applies, because no limiting sentence or disclaimer is touched. I found no fourth form. The new headline is not more certain than the body's own flat "finns inte".
2. **Paragraph 3, dash.** Before: `något annat — att tid` (a spaced em dash). After: `något annat – att tid` (a spaced en dash).
   Class: a **mechanical correction**, in the Swedish locale form: the Swedish tankstreck is the en dash. It does not follow from difference 1.
