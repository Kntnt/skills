Compared: `work/input.md` against the fenced text in `response.md` (lines 4–25). A line-by-line comparison finds exactly two differing lines. Frontmatter, byline, paragraph breaks, the final newline and every other body sentence are byte-identical.

## 1. Differences

1. **Headline (line 8).** Before: `# Rutan som inte finns`. After: `# Bibliotekets mötesmall frågar efter tid men inte syfte`. Class: **a change to what a claim says, by meaning and scope**, made as **a change of taste**, not as the repair of a visible defect. The old headline names the text's one concrete observation: a missing box. The body says the box is for "det beslut vi ska kunna fatta" (a decision). The new headline says something different and broader: the template as a whole asks about *time* and not about *purpose*. That changes the meaning, from a missing decision box to a missing purpose. It also changes the scope, from one box to what the whole template is oriented towards. The body does come near the new claim ("Vad tiden ska leda till frågar den inte om"; "också när syftet är att undersöka"), but the headline also leaves out that the template asks for participants and an agenda. The old headline had no visible defect. It was clear, grammatical and tied to the column's central image, and the first paragraph echoes it on purpose. The Skill's reasons for the rewrite (the headline is not self-explanatory, it shows no angle, it "repeats" the first paragraph, and "54 tecken, 8 ord") are genre and anatomy preferences, not defects.
2. **Dash (line 16).** Before: `något annat — att` (em dash U+2014 with spaces). After: `något annat – att` (en dash U+2013 with spaces). Class: **mechanical correction (locale form)**. Swedish writes a spaced en dash as its tankstreck. Nothing else depends on it.

No difference is consequent on another.
