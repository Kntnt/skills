# Validation b

## 1. The headline alone

A reader who sees only "Björkskolans temperaturgivare ger halva svaret" would take the article to be about temperature sensors at a school called Björkskolan. They would expect the readings to answer only part of some question, most likely about the indoor climate there.

Can that reader tell what the article is about: **yes**. "Björkskolans temperaturgivare" names the subject: the temperature sensors at one named school. "ger halva svaret" says what the article reports: the sensors answer the question only partly. The headline does not say what the question is. Still, temperature sensors in a school point clearly enough to the indoor temperature, so the reader knows the article is about how much the sensor readings showed.

## 2. The headline against the article

Does the headline say anything the article does not support: **no**.

- "temperaturgivare" and "Björkskolans": the article is about "fyra veckors mätningar i Björkskolans sex klassrum" taken by the sensors ("registrerade givarna i Björkskolan").
- "halva svaret" is a figure of speech, not a measured share. The article supports it at that strength. The readings answer *when* the air was cold but not *why*. The article says: "Fyra veckors mätningar pekar ut tillfällen att undersöka närmare, men förklarar inte varför luften var kall." The technician's quote says the same: "Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare. Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då." The article also lists what the sensors did not measure ("varken luftdrag eller elevernas upplevelse"), so it supports a partial answer and nothing stronger.
- The headline makes no claim about how many readings were low or how serious the problem was, and it does not say the problem was solved. So it does not go beyond the article's "14 av 120 lektionspass" or its stated limits.

## 3. Class

**working**: the reader can tell that the article reports what Björkskolan's temperature sensors showed, and "halva svaret" matches the article's own split between knowing when it was cold and not knowing why.
