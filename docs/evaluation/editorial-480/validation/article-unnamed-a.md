# Validation a

## 1. The headline alone

Headline: "Siffrorna visar när, inte varför" ("The figures show when, not why").

Seen alone in a list, it reads as an article about some data or statistics that reveal the timing of something but not its cause. The reader cannot tell which figures, what event or what field. It could be about crime, sales, illness, accidents or anything else that is measured.

Can the reader tell what the article is about: **no**. "Siffrorna" has no referent, and "när" and "varför" have no object. Nothing in the headline names the school, the classrooms, the temperature, the sensors or the measurement trial.

## 2. The headline against the article

Does the headline say anything the article does not support: **no**.

- "visar när": supported by "Ett lågt värde säger alltså när luften var kall" and "Fyra veckors mätningar pekar ut tillfällen att undersöka närmare".
- "inte varför": supported by "men förklarar inte varför luften var kall" and Rask's quote "Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då."

The headline does not claim more certainty or scope than the article gives. It makes no claim about cause, figures or scale beyond what the article itself states.

## 3. Class

**unclear**. The headline matches the article's claim, but a reader who sees only the headline cannot tell that the article is about temperature measurements in a school's classrooms.
