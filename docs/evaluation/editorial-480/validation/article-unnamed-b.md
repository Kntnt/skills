# Validation b

Headline: "Siffrorna visar när, inte varför"

## 1. The headline alone

A reader who sees only the headline learns that some figures show when something happened but not why it happened. The reader does not learn what the figures are, what happened, or where. It could be crime statistics, sales data, sick leave or anything else that is measured.

Can the reader tell what the article is about: **no**. The headline has no subject. "Siffrorna" (the figures) does not say what figures they are, and "när" and "varför" do not say what event they refer to. Nothing in the headline points to temperature, classrooms, a school or a measurement trial.

## 2. The headline against the article

The headline claims that the figures show *when* but not *why*. The article supports this at the same strength:

- "Fyra veckors mätningar pekar ut tillfällen att undersöka närmare, men förklarar inte varför luften var kall."
- "Ett lågt värde säger alltså när luften var kall, men inte hur rummet kändes."
- Elin Rask: "Vi vet när vi behöver titta närmare. Vi vet ännu inte varför det blev kallt just då."

The article does say the report gives no duration ("Hur länge temperaturen låg under den anger rapporten inte"). The data still records which lesson periods had a low reading, so "visar när" holds. The headline says nothing about figures, scope or certainty that the article does not back.

Does the headline say anything the article does not support, or support at that strength: **no**.

## 3. Class

**unclear** — the headline is accurate to the article, but a reader who sees it alone cannot tell that the figures are temperature readings from classrooms in a school, or what the article is about at all.
