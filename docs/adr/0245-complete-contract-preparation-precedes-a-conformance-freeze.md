# Complete-contract preparation precedes a conformance freeze

Recorded on 2026-10-03 for #493 and #499. A valid target distinction does not
establish that the rest of a fixture conforms.

## What the two earlier readings established

#486's four contrast validations correctly distinguished a supported denominator
inference from an explicit unfulfilled sensor-position/window-distance promise.
Their brief asked readers to examine the lead, support and follow-through; it
provided no anatomy contract and did not ask for global conformance. The frozen
expectations nevertheless declared the inference article conforming and all
other parts of the promise article conforming.

The inference lead referred to “De 14 lektionspassen” before introducing the
condition selecting those periods without its standfirst. The promise contrast
removed the lead's earlier introduction of the office, leaving “Kontoret” in the
body dependent on the standfirst. Both file runs supplied those supported
references, and both judges of each failed them as taste changes because the
standfirst supplied the antecedent. The actual anatomy requires the body to
work with its standfirst covered, and its review extension calls for naming
such referents. Thus the judging expectation and restricted resource packet
introduced a conflict; those judgements alone establish no new product defect.

The two response pairs retain separate headline findings, and their original
failures remain. None of the eight old judgements or four validations is
regraded. [The additive diagnosis](../evaluation/preparation-493.md) preserves
the exact source map and both kinds of reading.

## The choice

Separate preparation from the final evaluation freeze. Commit a finite
preparation plan and complete actual resource manifest, then have two fresh
readers account for every applicable rule of each complete fixture. Counted
anatomy and semantic anatomy are distinct. Target propositions and unrelated
defects are distinct. Only after both complete reports support a proposed
conformance expectation are the final inputs, expectations, judge briefs and
evaluation plan committed.

This spends additional preparation work and may reject an otherwise useful
target contrast. It prevents the alternative's stronger cost: a frozen expected
pass acquired from a narrow validation silently becoming authority over an
unexamined contract. Another alternative was a standfirst-dependence exception;
that would weaken a product requirement to preserve a fixture and is refused.

Already frozen failed proposals remain evidence. A bounded new preparation may
author an alternative, but it creates a new prospective campaign rather than
editing a freeze to fit the result. The separation matters permanently because
historical classifications remain true in their stated scope while no longer
being mistaken for complete validation. The current procedure and neutral
validator brief live in [the preparation guide](../evaluation/harness/README.md).
