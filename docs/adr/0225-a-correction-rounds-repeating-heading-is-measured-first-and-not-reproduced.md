# Redline's correction agent is not told to restore a heading it cannot word apart — measured first, and not reproduced

This record decides **not** to change what Redline's correction agent does with a headline or subheading it writes. The change would have made the agent read every heading it wrote or changed against what it stands over, and restore or leave unwritten any heading it could not word apart from that passage. The defect it answered was measured first, against the product as it stands, and did not reproduce, so no wording was committed and nothing ships. Issue #433, filed from #402's evaluation.

## The defect, and the fix it was given

**#402 lost `opinion-flawed`'s ending with a rejected round.** The control ends `Nu är det dags att agera.` inside `## Diskussion`, the section that carries its argument, and #402 made such a closing a present ending that is repaired by giving it a section of its own. In #402's runs, the correction round built that section from the body's own actor and act, and the re-review then rejected the whole round, as step 7 of Redline's `SKILL.md` requires, because a headline or subheading the correction agent had just written repeated what it stands over. The delivered text came back as received, with its ending still inside the argument.

**Thomas settled where the fix goes.** Step 7's whole-round rejection stays exactly as #389 decided it. The loss starts in the correction agent, so the fix goes there: a correction round never returns a heading that repeats what it stands over. The ticket's readiness addenda set that out in four files: the rule in Redline's correction brief, a sentence in `article-anatomy.review.md` holding a new ending's subheading to what every subheading is (the one `df6bb4a1` added and `0d015bdc` reverted), its assertion in `tests/test_kntnt.py`, and the matching sentence in Redline's `help.md`. The addenda also made the change conditional on a measurement: where a pre-change arm, staged on the tree #429 leaves, delivers the ending in more than half of its `opinion-flawed` runs, the result is recorded as not reproduced and nothing ships. Thomas's ruling concerns step 7 and does not override that gate.

## Why nothing is shipped

[`docs/evaluation/editorial-433/plan.md`](../evaluation/editorial-433/plan.md) was frozen at `fc45e604`, before the first run and before any wording. It ran the pre-change arm first, staged from `2afeb95e`, which holds #429's change to how the review reads headings. The arm ran `opinion-flawed` three times, and `article-flawed`, `case-study-flawed` and `column-flawed` once each. Every run was a fresh top-level Claude Code session on `claude-opus-5-5` at high deliberation, and every run had two blind judges.

**Whether a run delivers the ending was read from the returned text, not from the judges.** Blind judges had recorded the ending clause met on texts with no ending section, so the plan fixed a check of its own: the last level-2 section is one the input did not have, it holds the closing call to action, and the call names kommunstyrelsen and keeping telephone booking.

**Two of three runs deliver the ending.** One run returned `## Nästa steg är ett halvårs försök` over *Kommunstyrelsen bör besluta att behålla telefonbokningen under ett halvårs försök i alla sju lokaler.*, and another returned `## Både kommunstyrelsen och förvaltningen behöver agera` over *Kommunstyrelsen bör behålla telefonbokningen …*. In both, the argument stays in the section before, under a rewritten heading, and both judges of each run agree with the check. The third run's round was rejected, and the text came back as received. The rejection was not for a heading repeating what it stands over. It was for an attribution shift: the new headline and the new ending both put *Föreningen*'s demand to measure time use in the writer's own voice. [`results.md`](../evaluation/editorial-433/results.md) quotes each run's last section and reads the check condition by condition.

**So the shape #433 was filed for did not occur, and there is no miss to measure a change against.** No round of the arm was rejected for a heading the correction agent wrote. The rule the ticket would have added reads every heading of every correction round against its passage, which adds work to a brief every Redline round on an article, case study, column or opinion piece loads. The only evidence that could justify that work is a miss the shipped product makes, and on this input, on this tree, it did not make one. As the plan settles, no candidate was committed, the candidate arm was not run, and nothing was filed. The five files the change would have touched, and step 7, are byte-identical to `2afeb95e`. This is the method of [ADR-0212](0212-a-quotation-is-read-in-the-language-it-is-written-in.md), [ADR-0214](0214-a-bridge-prepares-a-quotation-rather-than-pre-says-it.md), [ADR-0221](0221-unslop-keeps-its-whole-passage-permission-measured-and-left-alone.md) and [ADR-0223](0223-a-part-that-asserts-past-a-kept-limit-is-measured-first-and-not-written-into-the-limit-rule.md).

**The tree moved between #402's measurement and this one, and this record rests on that on purpose.** #402 measured before #429 changed how the review reads headings, and #433 was blocked on #429 so that its baseline would be taken after that change. This record does not say #402's runs were misread. It says that on the tree #429 leaves, the ending #402 lost is delivered in most runs.

## What this record does not decide

**The correction brief's existing duty stands.** It already tells the agent to read every `heading_pairs` entry against its candidate, to repair an echo its own correction introduced, and to report one it cannot repair. This record adds nothing to that and takes nothing from it. The heading echo is still a defect, and step 7 still rejects the round that creates one.

**A three-run arm is small, and the rejections vary.** The one lost ending was lost to an attribution shift that another run's re-review let through, reported as an added claim. That variance is recorded, not filed: under this ticket's gate, a not-reproduced arm files nothing, and no open ticket owns a round rejected for that shift.

**What reopens this record** is a run of the current product in which a correction round is rejected for a heading the agent wrote or changed repeating what it stands over, and the round's other repairs are lost with it. `opinion-flawed` staged as `docs/evaluation/editorial-433/plan.md` stages it, and read by the check that plan states, is the frozen method to copy. The plan's *What is under test* sets out the candidate this record declined to write.

**Unslop is untouched.** Its correction brief carries no `heading_pairs` and loads neither `headlines.md` nor `article-anatomy.review.md`, so it has no heading of its own to repeat.

**Nothing here reaches the GPT family.** The protocol forbids a Claude session from starting a Codex Harness, so Redline was not measured there.
