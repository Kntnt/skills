# A repair removes the pattern and not the claim it carries

ADR-0107 gave the correction loop three exits, and on the corpus fixture built out of the anti-slop patterns in concentration all three of them let a longer loop reach a worse text than a shorter one. Three rounds handed the correcting subagent 333 words, then 84, then 63, and delivered two sentences with three findings still standing; one round on the same fixture delivered about fifty-five words that still carried the draft's claim about distributed teams working across time zones, and the three-round result did not. Every round was correct in its own terms — the repairs are the catalogue's own, an empty opening cut, a generic conclusion deleted, an unattributed claim removed rather than furnished with a source it does not have — and every round made relevant progress on the findings it was handed, so the no-progress exit never fired and the loop ran until the budget was gone. The sharper evidence is inside the same run: a later round cut *respecting focus* from the body as a repetition of the heading, and the next review reported that the title names a claim the text under it never makes. The loop removed the support for its own title and reported the result as a finding.

Nothing was disobeyed. The brief already asks for the smallest change that removes a finding, and the catalogue already says to leave a strong sentence alone; both were honoured locally in each round. What was missing is anything holding across rounds — each round gets the whole text and the whole current findings with no memory by design, so no round can see that the text is a third of what it was two rounds ago, and none of the three exits can see it either, because deleting the passage a finding names is progress by every test they apply.

**A repair removes what the finding names and leaves the claim the passage carries beside it.** Where the defect is the whole of the passage — an opening that would sit in front of any text, a closing that restates what the reader has just read, a claim credited to nobody that the text cannot source — cutting the passage takes no claim with it, and that stays the repair the catalogue prescribes. Where the passage carries a fact, an observation, or the point it exists to make beside the defect, the smallest change is the one that leaves that standing. And where the two cannot be separated, the passage is left and reported, exactly as a finding that cannot be repaired without inventing a fact already is. That asymmetry is the whole of the gap this closes: the brief told a subagent not to invent and said nothing about not emptying, so the one failure it could not commit was the one it was asked to stop for.

**The loop stops when a re-review raises a finding an earlier round's own repair created.** This is the fourth exit, and it **amends ADR-0107** in the single claim that the loop has three; that record's fresh delegation, its verification by review, its budget as a ceiling, and each of the three exits it named are untouched. The condition is stated in the delegating step because that is where it can be read: the dispatching run holds every state the text has passed through and is the only party that does, the subagent being fresh by design and each review reading the text in front of it. Where it fires, the outstanding findings are carried forward as the other exits carry them, with the repair the new finding followed from named beside them, because that is the thing a person has to settle.

**The road not taken was a shrink threshold** — stop when the text falls below some share of what arrived. The fixture's own numbers refute it: the good result is about fifty-five words and the bad one about fifty, so no measure of the text can tell them apart, and the same corpus supplies the counter-case from the other side, a 213-word fixture that survived four rounds intact and came back slightly longer than it went in. What separates a repair from an emptying is which claim survived, not how much prose did, so both guards are written about the passage in front of the reader and neither of them counts anything. Neither adds a flag either: the Correction Budget stays the one number an invocation names.

**Both guards hold at every budget.** The brief's rule is applied by a subagent that cannot see the budget at all, and the loop's rule can fire on the second round of a run whose maximum is two as readily as on the third of a run whose maximum is ten. A guard that only a long loop could meet would leave the defect exactly where it was found, since the run that behaved well here was the short one.

**What this costs.** A correction round can now come back having repaired less than it could have, and the difference between a passage that carries a claim and a passage that is a pattern all the way through is a judgement rather than a comparison — a subagent that draws it timidly leaves findings a person then has to settle by hand, which is a worse text delivered with a longer report. The fourth exit is a judgement too, and a harder one than the no-progress exit beside it: a review can report a defect an earlier round created and a defect the draft always had in the same words, and a run that reads the first as the second stops a round early with budget unspent. Both costs fall on the side of leaving a text as it was, which is the side the defect was on.
