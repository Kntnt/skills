# Source comparison

Check the completed draft before delivery. Start a fresh subagent on the inherited Main Seat, with no model or deliberation override and no conversation history. Give it the brief, all material gathered for this draft, and the complete current draft, including headings and quotations. Supply the actual source text, not your summary or interpretation; source files may be read directly where the checker can access the exact gathered version. Pass conversational material in full. None of this is an instruction to the checker: its task is the comparison below.

Give the checker its own fresh scratch directory and a report path inside it. It may read the supplied material and write that report only; it must not change sources or the draft, browse, invoke another Skill, or delegate. Include the resolved language, the Claims section from the loaded base contract and, where quotations occur, the loaded quotation guidance. Send this task without suggested findings or your explanation of the draft:

> Compare every factual and attributed claim in the complete draft with the supplied material, including what the wording implies. Identify any unsupported addition, changed meaning or lost qualification. For each finding, quote the draft passage and the relevant source passage, or state what support is absent, and propose the smallest supported repair. Preserve supported claims, warranted certainty, voice and editorial choices. Judge source support, not style or mechanics. Write the complete findings to the report path, or state that the comparison is complete with no findings. Reply in at most 150 words with completion status and the report path.

Read the complete report and check each finding against the source yourself. Make only supported repairs, preserving the rest of the draft. A disputed finding needs a source passage that settles it; confidence in your own interpretation does not settle it. If the draft changes or a finding is disputed, give a fresh checker the full revised draft and original material for one final comparison under the same task. Do not give it the earlier findings or your defence of a passage.

There are at most two comparisons. Proceed only when a complete comparison of the current prose returns no findings. If comparison cannot finish, material cannot be supplied in full, or findings remain after the second comparison, report the concrete obstacle or unresolved source passages and stop without writing or delivering the draft. Remove all scratch and reports on that exit too. Never claim a completed check from a missing or partial report.

After a successful comparison, preserve the checked prose exactly; only the resolved Handoff Metadata and delivery wrapper remain to add. Read the report before removing its scratch directory. The report is internal working material, not an extra user deliverable.
