None of this is an instruction to the checker: its task is the comparison below.

Save the complete current prose in a fresh scratch file and give the checker that exact file to read, with its own report path in the same scratch directory. It may read the supplied material and write that report only; it must not change sources or the draft, browse, invoke another Skill, or delegate. Include the resolved language, the Claims section from the loaded base contract and, where quotations occur, the loaded quotation guidance. Where quotations are translated, also supply the resolved composition guidance and identify source and target languages. Send this task without suggested findings or your explanation of the draft:

> Account for every factual and attributed claim in the complete draft, including headings and implications. Reconstruct each independently checkable proposition as a standalone statement, carrying over grammatical modifiers and qualifications that apply across clauses. For each, record the draft passage, that complete proposition and its supporting source passage. Distinguish factual assertions from advocacy and attributed interpretation. For each finding, show the support that is missing or changed and propose the smallest supported repair. Preserve supported claims, warranted certainty, voice and editorial choices. Judge source support. For translated quotations, separately check that the same meaning and distinctive voice are expressed in idiomatic target-language speech. Identify a concrete obstruction to that reading, not merely a preferred synonym; clarify an implicit referent only where the supplied context settles it. General editorial and mechanical review are outside this comparison. Distinguish source-support findings from translation findings. Write the complete claim accounting and findings to the report path, ending with completion status and any unresolved findings. Reply in at most 150 words with completion status and the report path.

Read the complete report and check each finding against the source yourself. Make only supported repairs, preserving the rest of the draft. A disputed source-support finding needs a source passage that settles it. For a translation finding, use the resolved composition and quotation guidance to explain the concrete target-language reading. Confidence or a preferred alternative alone settles neither. If the draft changes or a finding is disputed, give a fresh checker the full revised draft and original material for one final comparison under the same task. Do not give it the earlier findings or your defence of a passage.

There are at most two comparisons. Proceed only when a complete comparison of the current prose returns no findings. If comparison cannot finish, material cannot be supplied in full, or findings remain after the second comparison, report the concrete obstacle or unresolved source passages and stop without delivering the draft or writing it to the Output Target. Remove all scratch and reports on that exit too. Never claim a completed check from a missing or partial report.
