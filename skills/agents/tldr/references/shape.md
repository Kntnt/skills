# The shape of a summary

Read this when rendering a summary, whether it was asked for by name or produced under TL;DR mode.

## Language

Answer in the language the user was using before the reply being summarised — theirs, never the one you happened to write in. A language named in the invocation wins over that, however it is written: `sv`, `en_GB`, `engelska`, `AmE`, `brittiskengelska`, `svara på engelska`. A user who mixes languages is read on the balance of what they typed, not on its last word.

## Altitude

Write for a reader who does not hold the details. File names, flags, identifiers, and line numbers say nothing to someone who was not watching, and a paragraph packed with them is one they skip — which costs more than the detail was worth. Say what was done and why it matters. The numbering is what lets them ask for a detail they actually want.

## Structure

Three sections in this order, each introduced by a heading and preceded by a horizontal rule so the sections are visible at a glance:

- **What happened** — the substance.
- **What I decided** — choices made on the user's behalf that they should know about and need not act on.
- **What needs you** — anything that requires the user to do or decide something.

All three always appear. Their empty forms are the point of the whole thing: *nothing was decided that you had not already sanctioned*, and *nothing needs you*. A section that shows up only sometimes puts the reader back to scanning for it.

Never put a decision you made and a decision the user must make in the same section. Sorting those two piles is the service being rendered.

## Paragraphs

Each item is a short numbered paragraph of two or three sentences, with a blank line between them. Number them continuously across all three sections, so the reader can ask about item 7 without having to say which section it was in.

Not bullets. At this density a bullet list reads as one block, which is the thing being fixed.

## Honesty

Say what you could not see. Where a compaction has taken part of the range, name that in one line: *nothing needs you* is worth reading only if you saw everything you claim to have summarised.
