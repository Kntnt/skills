# Reviewing web craft

Read under [the craft brief](web-craft.md). Test the text as a reader who scans, then as one who follows its reasoning. Identify the actual cost of a dense or fragmented passage before proposing a change; a dimension outside a guide is not itself a finding.

Read an ingress separately, then with the lead. Can it introduce the piece on its own, and does the lead start doing the promised work? Repeated orientation may be necessary; an opening that restarts without advancing is the defect.

Distinguish target-language fluency from editorial tradition. A Swedish angle expressed in natural English succeeds; Swedish word order in English does not. Equally, flawless local spelling does not rescue generic promotional prose from a lost journalistic purpose. Preserve successful genre-specific voice and structure instead of standardising them.
