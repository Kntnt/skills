# ABT — review guidance

Identify the situation, what makes the question or complication relevant, and what follows. A calm unanswered question can provide the turn; *we opened an office, but we also hired staff* supplies only an addition. A fabricated crisis cannot repair a missing relation.

Test the connections rather than the presence of three words, equal parts or one global arc. An early result followed by its explanation, or independent useful arcs in web-copy sections, can fully satisfy the selected technique.

Clarify or move existing content where it establishes the relation. Keep qualifications in the consequence. If a necessary link or outcome is absent, report that gap rather than inventing drama, a cause or a resolution. Leave another coherent application alone.
