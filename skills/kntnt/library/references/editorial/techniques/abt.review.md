# ABT — review guidance

Identify the situation and its stakes, the complication or question, and what follows. Read the visible text alone, never asking whether the material had stakes or causes the text omits. Test the connections rather than three words, equal parts or one global arc.

**Parker's test.** A transition between parts or sections that only adds, *and then*, where a *but* or *therefore* belongs shows the relation missing there: *we opened an office, but we also hired staff* supplies only an addition. It complements the check that the relations hold without the words.

**General to specific.** Specifics a reader cannot place, because their general point comes later or never, are a finding; an opening scene, or a genre setting its own order, is none.

**Stakes.** A complication arriving before a reader can tell why the situation matters is a finding, unless the situation already shows what depends on it, an early answer in the standfirst or lead precedes the arc, or an opening scene carries the stakes.

**The situation.** Situation the complication does not need, delaying it, is a finding; context the text's reader needs for it to bite is none.

**One complication.** Two problems of equal rank competing for one consequence are a finding; independent arcs, such as sections with their own reader questions, a teaser's arc or a nested or revisited arc, are none.

**The consequence.** An action or conclusion answering a problem the text never raised is a finding; a But whose causes the text does not give, answered by a consequence taking up its question, is none.

**In medias res.** Situation laid out before the scene leaves an anecdote fronting an ordinary opening. Situation left out crowds facts in after the scene, spending its tempo.

**Correction.** Move or clarify existing content: bring stated stakes ahead of the complication and a delayed complication forward, order a cause under its complication, and keep qualifications in the consequence. A missing stake, cause, link or outcome is reported and never written in; a fabricated crisis cannot repair a missing relation. Leave another coherent application alone.
