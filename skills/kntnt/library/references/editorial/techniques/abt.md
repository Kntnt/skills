# ABT

*And, But, Therefore*: a structure connecting the necessary situation, a genuine complication or question, and the response or consequence the material supports. It shapes the reasoning or story without prescribing three paragraphs or visible labels.

## What this technique asks for

Give enough situation for the reader to understand what follows. Let a relevant obstacle, contrast or unanswered question make the next step worth following. Show the answer, decision or consequence that follows from it; an open or conditional result is a valid outcome.

The relations must hold even without the words *and*, *but* and *therefore*. A question need not be a crisis, and a response need not be a triumph. For example: a school records room temperatures; the readings locate cold periods but do not explain them; the next investigation links readings to room use. Nothing in that arc requires an emergency or a claim that sensors solved the problem.

Apply the structure at the level the genre's task needs. An article's ingress or an opinion's thesis can give the answer early while the body develops the arc. Web-copy sections may each answer an independent reader question; they need no invented global conflict. The selected relations still have to do real work at that level.

Nest or revisit an arc when that helps a complex explanation. In medias res is an available opening into an event the material actually supplies, not a required scene or reveal. Let content and reader need decide the scale, within the genre's form.
