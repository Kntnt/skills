# PAC — review guidance

Locate the factual starting point or question, the interpretive work and the warranted conclusion. A descriptive premise is sufficient; do not demand a falsifiable thesis or manufacture a counterargument. For a hypothesis, examine whether the text actually tests it rather than merely restating it.

Check the inferential step. *Repairs took a third of volunteer time, so doubling opening hours will clear every repair* claims an effect the figures do not establish. Clarify the existing distinction between an observation and its implications; do not add evidence to rescue the conclusion.

An answer at the top is not a failure when the subsequent reasoning supports it. Diagnose missing analysis, an unsupported conclusion or a connection the reader cannot follow, rather than moving a successful summary or forcing a preferred section pattern. Use existing support; report what the text cannot settle.
