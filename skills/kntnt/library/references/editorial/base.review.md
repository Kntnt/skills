# Reviewing the base contract

Diagnose failures of [the base contract](base.md) in the text in front of you. A finding identifies the passage, the requirement and what the reader loses. Another successful choice, a house-style preference and a mechanical error are not editorial findings; mechanics belongs to the separate final pass. Repetition of whole sentences or passages is an editorial matter, even when the duplication is verbatim; Proofread's duplicate-word correction does not own it.

Read for the intended reader's missing step: an unexplained term, a transition asserting a relation the surrounding sentences do not establish, a section whose place obscures the argument. Cover the paratext to find unresolved references in the body. Judge rhythm by its effect, not sentence counts; purposeful repetition, a personal rough edge and a useful digression may be doing essential work.

Compare the strength of a claim with the support visible in this text. A number contradicting its stated denominator, a causal conclusion following only a sequence, or a certainty contradicted by a nearby qualification is diagnosable. A plausible quotation or experience is not disproved by the absence of its original source. Review no unseen material, request none, and add no source-verification caveat.

Use [the anti-slop catalogue](anti-slop.md) to recognise empty performance, not to prohibit a working figure, contrast or ending. Test suspected filler by what its removal costs; preserve any fact, implication or voice it carries beside the defect. Strong supported criticism needs no generic softening.

A sentence can do its work by bounding what the text asserts: that something is not measured, not claimed, not witnessed or not general, or that what follows is an observation of a document rather than a scene from an occasion. Such a sentence is where *Preserve the state of knowledge: what is unknown or unclaimed is not thereby known not to have happened*, *Keep uncertainty where it exists and certainty where it is warranted* and *Preserve chronology, scope and qualifications wherever a claim appears* are carried, and the text alone shows which sentence it is: read it out, and the claims around it assert more than they did. That cost is what the removal test above answers, so recognise one by what it bounds and by which claim loses that bound, never by its form. A finding lies inside such a sentence or nowhere: name a limit another sentence already states in full, or a limit contradicting another passage of this text, or leave the sentence exactly as it stands. Naming the whole of it as the defect names nothing inside it.

Make the smallest correction that removes a finding. Move or clarify existing material where it supplies the missing connection. Introduce no new evidence, experience, attribution or outcome. If repair would change a claim or a quotation's meaning, or requires information absent from the text, leave the passage and report the unresolved finding. A claim removed because the finding named that claim itself remains part of the claim account, and so does every claim a correction left standing with its scope, certainty, attribution, chronology, causality or meaning moved, and every claim a correction added — one the corrected text asserts and the text before it has no counterpart for, an inference drawn from what that text already said among them where it asserts something that text did not say. A claim that has a counterpart in the text before is changed rather than added. The account is of the text delivered rather than of the text received, and nothing a reviewing Skill says about the claims as a whole contradicts a finding the same reply states.

Compare each correction with its input, including claims beside the defect, then reread the surrounding passage for broken references or transitions. Compare it difference by difference and not by its claims alone, and trace each one to a finding or to what repairing that finding required: a difference answering neither is not a correction, whatever it improves, and the passage it touched belongs back as it was. A correction is a candidate until that comparison and the review after it have run: where the review establishes a defect the correction introduced and its input did not have, the correction is not accepted and its input stands. Preserve functioning wording, voice and formatting outside the finding; concision is not permission to summarise the text away.
