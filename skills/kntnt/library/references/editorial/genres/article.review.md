# Article — review guidance

Read the opening as a sequence: does the lead begin explaining or reporting after the ingress has introduced the piece, or does the text start twice? Restore a missing function from existing content without treating necessary orientation as waste.

Follow the promised angle through the sections and ending. Topic accumulation, a concept explained after the reasoning needs it, or a sales close unrelated to the explanation gives the reader a specific problem; a different successful opening does not. Keep relevant technical substance when improving navigation.

A missing byline is not a finding when no author is supplied. Apply the full form to a complete article, not an explicitly requested excerpt. Report a required part that cannot be supplied from the text rather than inventing its content.
