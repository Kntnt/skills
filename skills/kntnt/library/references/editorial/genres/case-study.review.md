# Case study — review guidance

Locate whose experience and judgement carry the account. Supplier *we* in narrative, ungrounded superiority and a rescue story that makes the customer helpless are diagnostic; a speaker using *we* in their own quotation is not.

Read bridges beside quotations and the ingress beside the body's opening. Remove a redundant pre-echo while keeping the attribution and any distinct fact. A calm account with a qualified customer appraisal can be complete without more drama, quotations or selling.

Check whether stated results and reservations support the narrative's conclusions. Preserve the customer's reservations and agency. A visibly absent appraisal is a gap to report, not satisfaction to infer; a plausible quotation is not a gap merely because its interview is unavailable. Resolve only what the text itself supplies.
