# Article

A journalistic explanation or account that helps a web reader understand a focused subject. Its interest comes from what is reported and how it is explained, rather than the author's personal reflection or a campaign for a position. A blog post is an article published on a blog.

Apply the shared [web craft brief](../web-craft.md).

Follow the [article anatomy](../article-anatomy.md).

## What this genre asks for

Make the angle clear early and let it govern the material. Explain concepts and connections in the order this reader needs them; a technical explanation can be direct and technical. People and quotations contribute where the material gives them a useful role.

A complete article follows the anatomy: the standfirst introduces the offer, and the lead begins the explanation or reporting. An explicitly requested excerpt takes only the parts it needs.

End by fulfilling the opening's promise: a clearer understanding or a supported conclusion. The call to action is the useful next step the explanation supports, and it is commercial only where the assignment and material warrant it.

## The technique this genre is ordinarily written with

None.
