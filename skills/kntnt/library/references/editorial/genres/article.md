# Article

A piece written to be published and read voluntarily — in a newspaper, a magazine, a trade publication, or a site that publishes such things. Its reader chose it, owes it nothing, and may stop at any sentence, and every requirement below follows from that.

## What this genre asks for

**One angle, and the reader has it early.** An article is not about a subject; it is about one thing said about a subject. *Why a support team gave up its shared inbox* is an angle, *our support team* is a subject, and a draft that never narrows from the second to the first is a survey with a headline on it. The angle is settled before the first sentence is written, is unmistakable within the opening paragraphs, and decides what else belongs: material that is true of the subject but says nothing about the angle is cut, however much of it there is.

**The reader is held rather than assumed.** Nothing obliges anybody to reach the fourth paragraph, so each one gives a reason to want the next: a fact not yet explained, a tension not yet resolved, a consequence not yet drawn. Where a passage exists because the writer knew it rather than because the reader needs it here, it goes, moves, or shrinks to the clause it deserves.

**It reports rather than asserts.** A reader with no stake in the subject reads to find out what happened, not to be told what to conclude, so the material is at the surface of the text: the incident, the count, the date, the thing that was tried and what it did. Conclusions the article draws stand on material the reader has already been shown, and a paragraph of judgement whose evidence stayed in the notes is asking to be taken on trust the reader has no reason to extend.

**People are in it where the material has them.** An article about a change is an article about somebody who changed something or had something changed, and where the material names those people, says what they did, or quotes them, they belong in the text with the role that makes them relevant. Where it names nobody, none is invented and no composite stands in for one.

**The title carries the angle.** Where the artifact has a title, it says the one thing the article says rather than naming the subject, and it promises exactly what the article delivers. A question the article never answers, and a promise it cannot keep, are both spent in the first line a reader reads.

**A stated length is a constraint.** Where the request or the publication gives a length, the article is written to it within a small margin, and cutting to it is part of writing rather than an operation afterwards. Where none is given, the article runs as long as its angle takes and stops there.

## What this genre does not do

It does not make every text a news story. An article may be an argument, a profile, an explanation, or an account of what happened, and the angle and the audience decide which; what none of them becomes is a document written to be filed. Where the material is a question a named group has to decide, that is a report, and where it is an announcement addressed to editors, that is a press release.

It adds no requirement the base contract already holds. Register, structure, sentences, words, claims, openings, endings, formatting, and consistency are stated there once, and a rule repeated here would be one rule made into two things to keep true.
