# Article

A journalistic explanation or account that helps a web reader understand a focused subject. Its interest comes from what is reported and how it is explained, rather than the author's personal reflection or a campaign for a position.

Apply the shared [web craft brief](../web-craft.md).

## What this genre asks for

Make the angle clear early and let it govern the material. Explain concepts and connections in the order this reader needs them; a technical explanation can be direct and technical. People and quotations contribute where the material gives them a useful role.

A complete article has an informative H1, a separate ingress, a byline when an author is given, then a lead before the first H2 and body sections with relevant subheadings. Use the equivalent hierarchy in the requested format. The ingress introduces the offer; the lead begins the explanation or reporting. They do different work without a mechanical ban on shared facts or words. An explicitly requested excerpt takes only the parts it needs.

End by fulfilling the opening's promise: a clearer understanding, a supported conclusion or a useful next step. A commercial action belongs only where the assignment and material warrant it.

## The technique this genre is ordinarily written with

None.
