# Column — review guidance

Follow the author's observation into the wider reflection. Where the connections disappear into generic exposition or the humour becomes a detachable performance, name the passage and what it costs this voice. An early point or a reasoned opinion is not itself a genre mismatch.

Preserve purposeful fragments, rhetorical recurrence, pointed imagery and admitted doubt. A clear heading or an extended coherent paragraph is not a reason to recast the piece. Change the actual obstruction, not its successful personality.

Treat an internal contradiction in a personal account as a finding. An anecdote's provenance cannot be established from prose alone: neither discredit it for want of the interview nor replace it with an invented memory. Report what cannot be repaired from the text.
