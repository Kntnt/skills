### Added
- `/mirror` crawls: from the start page it fetches, breadth first and one request at a time, every page and file under the root that links reach — `a` and `area` with `href`, and `link` with `rel` `next`, `prev`, `canonical` or `alternate` — with each page's resources, and rewrites the links between saved pages to relative paths while links out of scope stay absolute. A link out of scope is recorded as `out-of-scope` and one that redirects out of scope as `redirect-out`, neither fetched; an HTML page in scope is read for links in turn and anything else, a PDF for instance, is saved as a `file`. The `www.` form of the start page's host folds onto it during normalisation, so the tree has one directory for the site. `robots.txt` is read once per host with the run's identity and headers and obeyed for pages and files, the start page included, as RFC 9309 matches groups for the product token `kntnt-mirror` or the first word of `--user-agent`; resources are exempt, a `4xx` allows everything, a `5xx` or no answer allows everything and is reported, a longer `Crawl-delay` is honoured, and `--ignore-robots` turns the rules off. `--max-pages` caps pages and files at admission, 5000 by default and `0` for none, recording what comes after as `over-cap`; `--delay` spaces requests; `--no-links` fetches the start page and its resources only. On a case-folding file system the later of two paths differing only in case takes `~2` before its extension. A page with scripts and under 200 characters of text is counted as a suspected JavaScript shell, and the report now counts pages, files and resources fetched, out of scope, redirected out, stopped by robots, over the cap and suspected shells (issue #317).
