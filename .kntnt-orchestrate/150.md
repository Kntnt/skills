`CHANGELOG.md` — under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Added`:

- The fixture corpus now stages code. `prose/code-carrying.md` and `prose/code-carrying-sv.md` are an English article and its Swedish counterpart whose prose earns findings on both editorial layers — the anti-slop catalogue's patterns, and three mechanical errors of its own in the English one and four in the Swedish — wrapped around code the findings do not concern: a fenced block, an indented block, and two inline code spans in each. The code is baited the way a careless pass would be caught: its comments, its docstring and its thrown message carry the same patterns as the article around them, identifiers are named after the very patterns a pass is hunting, and a misspelling sits inside a string literal and another inside an indented comment, the Swedish fixture adding a compound written apart. Every editorial Skill is under a preservation obligation that names code and, since ADR-0125, under a contract saying a code sample is quoted material whose contents produce no findings and are never altered — and neither claim was answerable from a corpus that carried none, which is why the Unslop evaluation had to stage run-local probe material that the GPT-family run over the same corpus commit has nothing corresponding to answer. Both fixtures carry an index entry in the corpus README's five-field format, stating that no finding lies inside the code and that every byte of it comes back as it arrived, and `code` is now a required tag in the coverage vocabulary: the suite fails when no fixture claims it, and holds each fixture that does to staging all three forms a sample takes and to an entry that says which they are, that the code carries a mechanical error of its own, and what a run may not do with either (issue #150).
