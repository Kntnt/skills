CHANGELOG.md

### Changed

- Model Selector's `capture` now produces a Usage Record rather than a `RunObservation`: what one finished session cost and how long it took on the exact Seat it ran on, carrying no outcome, no checker, and no Cohort, appended immediately — one record per Seat a session ran on — to its own `usage-records.jsonl` beside the evidence ledger, idempotent by session identity and Seat. The bounded pending-review store, the `review` verb and its `save`/`failed`/`ignore` actions, the debounced notification path, and the retention bounds and abandoned-draft reconciliation they depended on are gone: a Harness's own lifecycle payload never carried a checker or a failure signal to answer the outcome branch they existed to reach, so ordinary work is now measured and never judged, and a `session.error` signal ends a session exactly as a normal end does. A Usage Record enters no derived frontier, contributes to no success rate, and produces no evidence record, and the evidence ledger continues to hold only judged routed work (ADR-0156, narrowing ADR-0090, ADR-0133, and ADR-0145).
