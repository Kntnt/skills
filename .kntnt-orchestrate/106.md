`CHANGELOG.md` — under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Added`:

- `/redline` now repairs what its review found, which completes the Skill: `--max` is the Correction Budget, it accepts any non-negative integer, and it defaults to `1`, so an ordinary review includes one correction and one opportunity to verify it. `0` keeps the reviewing behaviour — the findings come back for a human to act on and the closing mechanical pass is still performed — and a larger number bounds a longer loop explicitly; a negative, non-integral, or otherwise malformed value is refused before anything is reviewed or written. While findings remain and budget remains, each correction is delegated to a subagent started fresh for that round, carrying no earlier findings and no earlier attempt, so the framing of one round cannot bias the next. It receives the complete current Text Artifact, the complete findings of the most recent review, the resolved genre, technique, and language, and the requirement to preserve everything those findings do not concern — each whole rather than summarised, a summary being the dispatching session's reading of the text and that reading being exactly what a fresh subagent exists to be without. The budget falls by one per correction, and what comes back is reviewed again from the top rather than accepted on the correcting subagent's report, an agent reporting on its own work being the one reader who cannot check it. The loop stops at the first of three conditions: no findings left, which leaves the rest of the budget unspent so a clean text is not rewritten to use up a number; a correction that made no relevant progress, which reports what remains instead of repeating a round already shown to achieve nothing; and a spent budget, which delivers the text with the findings that are left, marked unresolved, through the routing the review contract already specifies for a response target and for a file target. The closing `proofread` pass still happens exactly once, after the loop and whichever condition stopped it, and nothing substantive follows it. Subagents remain a hard Capability whatever budget an invocation names. ADR-0107 is the record (issue #106).
