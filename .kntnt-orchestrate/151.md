`CHANGELOG.md` — under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- An `/orchestrate` subagent's wait no longer outlives the command it waits on. The waiting rule every brief carries said to start a wait and never said what became of it, so a subagent that waited by a mechanism blind to its command's completion discharged the rule by leaving one behind — and on a Harness that wakes a session when a child stops, each leftover woke the run, which answered by starting another: nineteen were alive at once after a finished run. The rule now says in its own one wording that the wait ends with the command it waits on and survives neither it nor the turn, stated as properties the mechanism must have rather than as any Harness's tool, while still forbidding a turn to end with the command running. Beside it every brief now carries a rule reaching processes as the confinement rule reaches paths: a subagent stops what it started before it reports, and names in its report anything it deliberately leaves standing. The step that dispatches and triages subagents accounts for those leftovers rather than trusting each subagent to have none, and sweeps a waking that is only one instead of triaging it. ADR-0127 is the record; it also settles why the rule needed a second half, why the process rule stands beside the confinement rule rather than inside the waiting rule, and why no brief may name the facility it waits with (issue #151).
