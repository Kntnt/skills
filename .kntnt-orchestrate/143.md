`CHANGELOG.md` — under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- The correction briefs `/redline` and `/unslop` dispatch from now name one way for the filled-in brief to reach its subagent: it is handed over directly, as the whole of that subagent's instruction, and never written to a file with a path sent in its place. The briefs used to say that writing the brief somewhere and telling the subagent to read it was fine wherever it cost less than repeating it, and every run took that differently — across a handful of runs of one Skill in one Harness, some passed a path under a freshly made temporary directory, one put the process id in the name, and four independently chose the same fixed path under `/tmp`. Two of those four overlapped in time, and one run's brief was deleted underneath it and read back by its subagent as the other run's brief, holding differently worded findings against an unrelated text; that run recovered only because its subagent noticed the text was not the one it had been sent to repair. The permissive sentence is replaced rather than supplemented, so there is one rule in one place, and the delegating step now reaches the brief for how the handover is made as well as for what it carries. A brief holding the user's whole text is no longer left at a name anything else on the machine can guess, read, or replace, which mattered for a single invocation as much as for two. ADR-0118 records the decision (issue #143).
