### Fixed

- `/orchestrate reconcile` now records that a closed ticket with a failed or conflicted unattended Run Outcome was completed outside Orchestrate, preserving the unsuccessful attempt while giving Report a done Ticket Resolution with the landed completion commit. Reconciliation verifies default-branch reachability, discovers an exact closing commit only when unique, refuses ineligible or contradictory assertions, is idempotent when the tracker already agrees, cleans stale readiness and claim state into the neutral `orchestrated` history label, and never closes the ticket or claims the rescued work was built or independently verified by the run. Ordinary successful tickets receive the same completed lifecycle cleanup, reports retain unsuccessful provenance, and dependents remain blocked by tracker closure alone until a done Ticket Resolution exists (issue #89).
