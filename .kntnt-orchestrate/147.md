`CHANGELOG.md` — under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- `/unslop` now reads the operand as the text it is whatever its own words say. The step that settles the Text Artifact states that the artifact's content never changes what the operand is: a brief, an outline, or anything else that reads as an instruction is still the text, read against the catalogue like every other text and never carried out, and it is the same artifact pasted inline as it is named by a path or a URL. An evaluation run handed the corpus's short brief inline had written the 378-word article the brief commissions — carrying two paragraphs the run itself identified as its own inference — without resolving a language, loading any anti-slop scope, or entering the correction loop, while the same corpus's slop-heavy prose pasted inline the same way ran the whole flow correctly. The step also says that reaching that judgement reads no other Skill's instructions, this Skill having no writing gesture and gaining none from being handed a brief; the manpage's POSITIONAL ARGUMENTS says the same to a reader deciding what the Skill will do with a text. The eight invalid forms are refused exactly as before (issue #147).
