# Entries this ticket owes the run

## `CHANGELOG.md`

Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Added`:

- Where a rule and a decision go is written down, and `docs/adr/` is given an ingress that says what it is. Two functions had been living in one format: the archive answers why a rule became what it is, and it was consumed as what applies now, so an agent derived today's law by reading a hundred and fifty immutable records forward and applying supersessions by hand — and every record outrun by a later one carried a pointer somebody had to write and maintain. `docs/rules/docs.md` now states the model: the rules modules are the authority on the present and are edited rather than grown, the archive is historical, read on demand and never loaded by default, the subject decides the module and a definition belongs in `CONTEXT.md`, a rule governing one Skill's own behaviour lives in that Skill's shipped files and nowhere central, audience decides where any document goes, a decision earns a record only when all three of `/domain-modeling`'s criteria hold at once — hard to reverse, surprising without its context, the result of a real trade-off — and `AGENTS.md` is a pointer list holding no rule of its own, every entry written to the `read when` convention. It also states that every Skill body is authored under `/writing-for-agents`, which governs how the prose is written while `skills.md` governs what the body carries; `skills.md`'s Body section points there for it. `docs/adr/README.md` is the archive's ingress: the directory is historical, the rules modules are the authority, a record is read as of its own date, and a number is an address rather than a position. ADR-0075's outrun-pointer duty is retired with that reader — a later record no longer annotates the earlier one, the absence of a pointer now says nothing, and the reasoning is in the reform's own record, *How this repository records decisions and writes tickets*. `AGENTS.md` gains a `read when` line for the module and stops offering the archive as the place to decide architecture, sending a reader there to trace why a rule became what it is instead; `general.md`'s sweep no longer asks a change to write the pointer it can no longer write. No record is deleted or rewritten (ADR-0075).
