Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Changed` (the subsection has to be created if the wave has not already added it, above the `### Fixed` one that is there):

- A model this machine has measured doing a kind of work is now chosen ahead of one it has only estimated, whether or not either clears the confidence bar. Where no measured point clears the bar, the answer is the cheapest — or quickest — of the measured points the evidence cannot tell apart from its best one, rather than an untested estimate that cleared the bar on a seeded capability or whichever measured point happened to be cheapest; the answer says when that band decided it and names the point it was drawn around. The plain answer, the step up after a failure, the alternatives and the judge the grader asks for all follow the one rule, and the bar itself is unchanged (ADR-0204). This ships together with the Trial of issue #374, which is how a model with no rows for a kind is still reached; neither is released without the other (#372).
