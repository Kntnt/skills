- `/redline` no longer reads a step an opinion sets before a pending decision as the author's forecast of its outcome. Reviewing the clean debate article in the evaluation corpus, it read the standfirst's *pröva båda bokningsvägarna ett halvår till innan den ena stängs* as the author conceding that a booking route closes, although the headline and the lead make that closure the decision the author asks to postpone, and it deleted the clause or hedged it into *innan det avgörs om den ena ska stängas*. The opinion genre's review half now says that such a passage names the decision as the text presents it, pending, proposed or possible, and is no finding where the headline, the standfirst and the lead make the outcome the question the author wants weighed. A text that states the outcome as settled while another passage leaves it open is still a finding. No word and no part of the text is exempted. Measured on `claude-opus-5-5` at high deliberation, three of six runs changed that clause before the change and none of six after it. A variant stating the closure as settled was caught in three of three runs both before and after, and the controls held. A lead sentence removed from the clean article in one run whose instructions the change does not touch is filed as #486 (#479).
