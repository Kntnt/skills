- A true finding at Write's last source comparison no longer costs either the draft or the gate. The contract allowed at most two comparisons, forbade any prose change after the last one, and stopped the delivery wherever an accepted defect remained — so a productive final comparison left only two ways out, and the Claude-family evaluation of #362 took both: two runs spent about a thousand seconds each and delivered nothing over a defect one clause would have repaired, and a third applied the checker's own proposed wording after the last comparison and delivered prose no checker had read. Write now delivers the prose exactly as the final comparison read it and reports each remaining finding beside the draft — the passage, the problem, what the material carries instead, and the smallest repair the checker proposed — with the account saying the draft comes with known defects before it says anything else about it. No prose changes after the final comparison, the checker's own wording included, and the final comparison is the last one the run made, whether that is the second or the first. Stopping is now only for prose no comparison has read, and a stop never destroys the draft: the current prose is preserved where the user can still reach it, with its comparison status and what was and was not compared, after a real article run removed the scratch that held the only copy of a finished draft and its check reports. Findings stay beside the text unless the user asks for them marked in the document, where the prose stays exactly as the comparison read it and each marking is separable from it. The comparison task also keeps whose a statement is: a valuation or a position the brief gives as the commissioning party's own needs no external evidence to be expressed as theirs, while a figure, an event or a claim about an actual population stays a factual claim whoever supplied it (#376, #381).
