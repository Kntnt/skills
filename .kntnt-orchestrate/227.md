### Added

- Model Selector's `config reset` now takes `--evidence [--yes]`, the mirror of its bare form: bare removes configuration and keeps measurement, `--evidence` removes measurement and keeps configuration. It shows the exact paths and row or byte counts of everything this machine measured — `run-observations.jsonl`, `derived-frontiers.json` and `quota-observations.jsonl`, `standing-policy.json` and `standing-policy-history.jsonl`, and the whole `capture/` subdirectory and `usage-records.jsonl` — names any of them absent as absent, requests confirmation, and removes exactly those seven, leaving every other file `references/evidence-ledger.md`'s `## Store` table names untouched along with `config.json` and its history. `--yes` is new to this Skill and answers the confirmation unattended; it is valid only combined with `--evidence` and is refused, not ignored, on every other form. Each owning engine gains its own `purge` verb — `scripts/standing_policy.py`, `scripts/routed_observations.py` reached through Model Selector's `scripts/observations.py`, and the Skill's own `scripts/capture.py` — previewing without `--yes` as a success rather than the refusal `policy reset` gives an unconfirmed call, and removing with it. A Standing Policy override is measurement rather than configuration, so this discards it and its history outright instead of restoring the shipped default the way `config policy reset` does; that command is unchanged and stays the narrower act. Removing `capture/` turns capture off along with its consent record while the Harness hooks stay installed, and `capture --on` turns it back on (ADR-0159, narrowing ADR-0149 and ADR-0150).
