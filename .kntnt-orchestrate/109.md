### Added

- `docs/evaluation/records/` now holds the Redline Skill's Claude-family record: the whole shared fixture corpus run against `/redline` as a user of it runs it, with every outcome written in the protocol's format so the matching GPT-family run can be compared by reading rather than by re-running. Forty-three invocations, each in its own turn and its own throwaway copy of the corpus, with every side effect read from a checksum of the working copy taken before and after rather than from what the run said about itself, and with each run's own tool trace read as well — the three things this Skill's contract deliberately keeps out of its output are what a correction subagent was actually given, whether a returned correction was reviewed again, and whether the mechanical pass ran once. Per-field precedence held at every level, including the form the corpus cannot stage: two invocations, each carrying a text whose `kntnt` map holds a single key, settled all three parameters at once from three different sources and rotated which source settled which — a flag, a map value and a Contextual Instruction deciding language, genre and technique in one run, and the same three deciding technique, language and genre in the other. Against the corpus itself, two explicit flags settled genre and language while the technique still fell through to a map that said otherwise, an explicit flag sat beside a technique named in a Contextual Instruction and a language inferred from the text, the map's own values reached a run that named none of them, and `en_UK` stopped a run as unusable metadata unless a flag had already settled that parameter. The review found all seven of the anti-slop patterns by name on the fixture that carries them in concentration, and all seven again in Swedish on Swedish instances rather than on the catalogue's English examples, and left competent prose alone, never compared any artifact with source material and never remarked that none was supplied, and reached real contradictions inside the artifacts themselves — the long municipal evaluation's 2024 boardings, whose breakdown misses its own stated total by 18,600 while the 2023 pair reconciles exactly. Every correction was delegated to a subagent started fresh with the complete current text and the complete current findings, verbatim, and every returned correction was reviewed again rather than accepted: one re-review found two defects the first review had missed and said so. The loop stopped at each of the three conditions the contract names: clean text with the budget unspent, at a budget of one and again at a budget of five; the budget spent with the findings carried forward; and, twice and independently, a correction that made no relevant progress — a round that returned the text it was given word for word, stopping the loop with three of five rounds unspent in one run and one of four in the other, on an essay held to a genre whose remaining findings a corrector may not repair without inventing facts. and the closing mechanical pass ran exactly once in every run that reached it, with `normalize` and `catalog` surviving under `en_US`, the British forms surviving under `en_GB`, `hen` surviving in Swedish, and an ambiguous `3/4` left ambiguous under both English locales. Output was exercised through the real Skill seam across the response default, a new file, an existing file, an existing directory, an ascending collision suffix that stepped past two occupants to `interview-notes-3.md`, a filename derived from a URL, In-place Editing, and every documented refusal, none of which left a partial effect behind. Five real defects were found and filed rather than absorbed: a run inferring a genre reads a genre it does not load, as Write does (issue #140); two invocations were refused that the contract does not refuse (issue #141); the correction brief reaches its subagent through a path each run invents, and two runs collided over the same one (issue #143); and a larger Correction Budget emptied a text instead of converging on one, a repair creating the finding the next review reported (issue #144). A sixth defect is that nothing says which language a findings report is written in, so the same Swedish text was reported on in Swedish by one run and in English by another (issue #145). Two situations could not be staged from the corpus at all — it carries no Swedish fixture with concentrated slop and no partial `kntnt` map — and are judged on run-local probe material quoted in full in the record, leaving the corpus, its index and its floors untouched and the gap in the corpus itself open (issue #142). One fixture is recorded as skipped with its reason, and four runs as discarded with theirs (issue #109).
