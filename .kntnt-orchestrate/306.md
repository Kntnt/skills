### Changed

- Model Selector orders the points that clear the floor on the price of a finished job rather than the price of one attempt. A point that fails is paid for again: one that succeeds 0.82 of the time needs about 1.22 attempts per finished job, and one at 0.98 about 1.02. So a dearer point that is right the first time can cost less in total than a cheaper one run again. Among the candidates whose chance of success clears the floor, the order is now one attempt's price divided by that chance, or one attempt's elapsed time divided by it under `--objective=time`. With `claude-opus-5` at `low` finishing about 0.82 of the time and `medium` about 0.98 for about 1.14 times the money and 1.11 times the time, the answer is now `medium` on both objectives where it was `low`. The same figure orders the step up after a failure, including the step taken where nothing measured clears the floor, and the entries of `alternatives` that clear it. The judge, which asks the same ranking, is picked the same way. Nothing else moves: no point below the floor is taken for being cheap, points measured for the kind still come first, the candidates below the floor keep their order on chance, and exploration still judges what counts as cheaper than the answer, and the order it tries candidates in, per attempt, so a point that once scored badly can still be retried. No constant enters the division, and the expected-cost arithmetic removed in 0.25.x stays removed. `expected` now carries `per_success_cost_usd` and `per_success_seconds` beside the per-attempt figures, and so does each entry in `alternatives`; each is null where its per-attempt figure is null, and both are null on an inheriting answer. `docs/rules/routing.md`, `help.md`, `SKILL.md`, `references/measurement.md`, Orchestrate's `help.md` and `SKILL.md`, and the README say so, and ADR-0188 records the rule, amending ADR-0184 (issue #306).
