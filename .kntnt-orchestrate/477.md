- `/redline` now delivers its reply as the reply checker read it, corrected only where a checker item says. After the check the run used to write the reply again, and a sentence the checker had passed could come back false: a case study's note that declines to credit the software with a result came back as a note that rules the software out, or that rejects the result outright. `reply-check.md` now has the run correct what an item names, keep the rest of that statement as drafted, deliver every other sentence in the draft's words and add nothing about either text, and the Help page says the rest of the reply goes out as the checker read it. Measured on `claude-opus-5-5` over `case-study-clean` and two contrast cases that tell a withheld attribution from an explicit denial and a real unsupported causal claim, a reply carried a statement made false after the check in 7 of 12 runs before the change and in 2 of 12 after it, and none after it misreported what a text says of a cause. The remaining misses are filed as #489, #490 and #491 (#477).

## The reply goes out as the checker read it — #477

The Claude-family evaluation of [#477](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/477), which was conditional on the measurement: Redline corrects only what a reply-checker item names and delivers the rest of the reply in the draft's words. Read it with [`../editorial-477/plan.md`](../editorial-477/plan.md), [`../editorial-477/results.md`](../editorial-477/results.md) and the diagnosis made before the plan, [`../editorial-477/diagnosis/README.md`](../editorial-477/diagnosis/README.md). Every run is a top-level session made with the #388 staged runner, and every run's drafted reply, checker return and delivered reply are kept apart in its packet.

- [`redline-claude-2026-10-01-477.md`](redline-claude-2026-10-01-477.md) — twenty-four counted `Redline` runs in Claude Code 2.1.286 on `claude-opus-5-5` at high deliberation, with two blind reply judges and two blind correction judges on every run. `case-study-clean` ran as three response-target and file-target pairs, and two contrast cases — its lead given an unsupported causal claim, with the note withholding the attribution in one and denying the cause in the other — three times each, in a pre-change arm run first and against the candidate. Five candidate runs cut off by a usage limit are kept apart as void and were made again. Before the change 7 of 12 replies carried a false statement made or reworded after the check, one of them a withheld attribution delivered as a rejection; after it 2 of 12 did, and none misreported a cause. Every contrast run still found the causal claim and the check left every text as it was, so the change ships. The remaining misses are filed as #489, #490 and #491.
