## CHANGELOG.md

Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- `/write` now leaves the quotation standing under a subheading the point it is there to make, rather than saying it first in the subheading. `headlines.md` told a writer to word a subheading from its whole section in words its first sentence does not use, and a subheading that pre-spent a quotation two sentences down met that rule: the reader then met the customer's own judgement as the heading said again. `headlines.md` now has a section on a subheading standing over a quotation — it leaves the quotation its judgement, figure or concession, and names the subject, the occasion or the speaker where the quotation is the section's point — and defines the quotation bridge, the sentence or clause immediately before a quotation, with the subheading outside it. Write's heading check reads each subheading against the quotation under it, and its heading repair, Redline's correction brief and `headlines.review.md` are bounded the same way. Measured on `case-study` in Claude Code on `claude-opus-5-5`, the subheadings counted as a pre-echo fell from 14 across seven drafts to none across seven, with no quotation bridge classed as restating its quotation in either arm; `article`, `column` and `opinion` and the Redline side were not measured (#396).
