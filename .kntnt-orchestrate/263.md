### Changed

- The coding standard lives at `docs/rules/`. Its three modules — `general.md`, `python.md`, and `skills.md` — are unchanged in content and in what they require, but the directory they sit in is about to hold rules that are not about code: the Manager's behavioural law, how a ticket is written, how a decision is recorded. A directory named for the coding standard would have had to be either renamed later or filled with documents its name denies, so it is renamed now, while the only thing to repoint is a handful of links. `AGENTS.md`'s References lines, the standard's step in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the research note behind the manpage profile, and every path the suite resolves point at the new directory under the same phrasing they used before. Nothing under `skills/` refers to it, so no shipped file and no Catalog entry changed. A record's own link to the old path is historical and stays as it was written.
