- A token category a measurement recorded as nought is forecast as nought rather than falling back to the kind's shipped prior. Only a category no row recorded at all is unmeasured, and only that one takes the prior; a row carrying a category as null is still no evidence about it and weighs against nothing beside it. Codex reports no cache writes on every attempt it exposes usage for, so every `high` attempt forecast for a Codex model was priced 239 200 cache-write tokens the vendor never bills — around three dollars an attempt on the one figure the ranking orders by, making the model look two to four times dearer than it is. Where every recording row measured a positive amount the forecast is exactly what it was, a nought among them lowers it in proportion, and a forecast of nought is a real price rather than a null one. The elapsed time is unchanged: no harness reports an attempt that took no time, so a nought there remains a clock that failed to record (#371).
