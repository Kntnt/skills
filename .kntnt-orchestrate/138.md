`CHANGELOG.md` — one bullet under `### Fixed` of `## [Unreleased]`:

- A draft from `/write` is no longer padded up to a length its material cannot reach. The Collection's editorial contract now settles that conflict where the requirement it protects already lives: a length that was asked for constrains how much of the material to use and never licenses material the source does not carry, a text the material cannot fill is delivered at the length the material supports with what further material would close the gap named beside it, and circumstantial detail about the case a text reports — a duration, a manner, a motive, an absence, a state of affairs offered as background — is a claim like any other and is supported or absent. `/write`'s own account of a run is answerable to the same contract: it says where the material stopped when the draft is short of a stated length, and asserts no fidelity it has not established. The Skill still stops at the first draft, both rules are language-independent, and the two fixtures that exposed the failure were re-run against the changed Skill and recorded (issue #138).
