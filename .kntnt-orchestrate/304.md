### Changed

- Model Selector chooses among the points it has measured doing the kind of work asked about before it considers any point it only has an estimate for. It took the cheapest point whose estimated chance of finishing cleared the floor, whatever that estimate rested on, so a prior with no row behind it, or a verdict carried over from another kind, won whenever it looked cheaper. On 2026-09-11 `--kind=mechanical` answered `gpt-5.6-luna`, whose only rows are seven `implement` builds with two passed, over `claude-opus-5` at `high`, with 17 measured `mechanical` rows averaging 0.94. Before that day's purge of short own-session rows, `--kind=analyze` answered an untried `gpt-6-astra` at both stakes over 53 measured rows. Where a point measured for the kind — enough rows in that exact kind, model and deliberation — clears the floor, the answer is now the cheapest of those, and only where none does is the whole pool ranked as before. Nothing is filtered out: an unmeasured point still appears among the alternatives, can still be named with `--after`, and is still tried on the one reversible call in ten that explores. The step up after a failure prefers a measured point on the same terms, and the judge, which asks the same ranking, is chosen among measured reviewers wherever one clears the bar (ADR-0187).
