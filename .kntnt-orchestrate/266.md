### Changed

- How a ticket is written is stated in one place, `docs/rules/tickets.md`. It was settled across three records and a practice none of them held: ADR-0067's four assertions about what a ticket may claim while it waits, ADR-0099's `Builds alone` declaration, ADR-0065's rule that the requirement is the whole thread, the shape every ticket here takes, and the readiness addenda that arrive as comments after the body is filed. An author met them in four places or, more often, in none. The module states the four assertions with each rule's reason in a phrase and the record cited beside it, the Solo Ticket declaration and what a scheduler does with it, the four parts a ticket carries — what to build, acceptance criteria a fresh agent can check, `Blocked by`, and the vantage line naming the commit the ticket was written against — and that the requirement is the whole thread rather than the body: a comment outranks the body it amends, a readiness addendum voids what it corrects, and a ticket is read to the end of its thread before it is built. `AGENTS.md` replaces its two References lines pointing at records with one line pointing at the module, under the same `read when` phrasing; every record stays where it is and is reached through the module, which cites each of them beside the rule it settles (ADR-0065, ADR-0067, ADR-0099).
