### Changed

- How a ticket is written is stated in one place, `docs/rules/tickets.md`. It was settled across two records and a practice neither of them held: ADR-0067's four assertions about what a ticket may claim while it waits, ADR-0099's `Builds alone` declaration, the shape every ticket here takes, and the readiness addenda that arrive as comments after the body is filed. An author met them in three places or, more often, in none. The module states the four assertions with each rule's reason in a phrase and the record cited beside it, the Solo Ticket declaration and what a scheduler does with it, the four parts a ticket carries — what to build, acceptance criteria a fresh agent can check, `Blocked by`, and the vantage line naming the commit the ticket was written against — and that the requirement is the whole thread rather than the body: a comment outranks the body it amends, a readiness addendum voids what it corrects, and a ticket is read to the end of its thread before it is built. `AGENTS.md` replaces its two References lines pointing at the records with one line pointing at the module, under the same `read when` phrasing; both records stay where they are and are reached through it (ADR-0067, ADR-0099).
