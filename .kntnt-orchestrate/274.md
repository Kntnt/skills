`CHANGELOG.md` — one entry under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Changed`:

- The Manager reads its own invocation through the engine. `skills/kntnt/SKILL.md` opens by running `kntnt.py invoke --here="$HERE"` with the payload on stdin, continues from the JSON on exit 0, and prints stdout verbatim and stops on any other exit. The help routing its body and `steps/help.md` carried, the verb list of `## Arguments`, and the prose that said what the script does with a flag a verb has no use for are gone — the engine reads all of it off the `argument-hint` and the shipped pages, which are unchanged. `## Arguments` keeps what the engine cannot know: what `--project`, `--yes` and `--dry-run` mean to the verbs, and that only `--yes` in the current Formal Invocation authorizes an unattended Global Update. The Steps read the verb from `path` and pass the flags on as they stand; the help step runs the script on the engine's operand and nothing else. The engine's dependency gate reads the Manager's own directory as declaring nothing, the Manager being no Catalog entry and carrying no marker for the sweep to read. Every help form prints the same page as before, held by the suite against what the script printed for it; every refusal uses the engine's common diagnostic: the fault in one line, the addressed SYNOPSIS, and that page's own `--help` route, on stdout. `docs/rules/skills.md` says what the `## Invocation` section of a body that calls the engine carries (ADR-0181).
