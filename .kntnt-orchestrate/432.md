Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- `/redline` no longer reports a web page's own form as missing, or copies a fact from a subheading into the section under it. The web-copy review guidance said a reference to a form needs an included or explicitly specified form, and read literally that made every page naming its own form incomplete. It now says a reference to a form, button or link is a finding only where the copy places or describes it differently from what the artifact shows, such as *the form below* over what is only a link, a link labelled as something it does not do, or an action the page contradicts. The shared review guidance now says a heading may carry a fact that its section does not repeat, and that the finding is a body that needs the heading to be understood. No base half changed, so Write is unaffected. Measured on `web-copy-clean`: all six runs before the change did both, and none of the six after it did; `web-copy-flawed`'s misleading *Book and pay now* link is still repaired (#432).
