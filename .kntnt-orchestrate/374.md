# Entries this ticket owes files the run writes

## CHANGELOG.md

Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Added`:

- A model this machine holds too few rows of its own for on a kind of work is now given a Trial: three reversible jobs of that kind, at whatever they cost, after which it is judged on its own record like everything else. Measured-first ranking shuts such a model out of every plain answer and the bounded exploration beside it only ever tries something cheaper than the answer, so a dearer untested model was reached by no route at all and a cheaper one by about four calls in a thousand — some 750 builds for the three rows that make a point judgeable. A Trial is owed only to a model that could plausibly win, its point having to be at least as likely to finish as the bound drawn around the best point the call has measured, so a weak model is left to the cheap exploration as before; the point tried is that model's at the answer's own level or the nearest it supports, one model is tried at a time, and the scope, either lock, the Quota Guard and the deliberation ceiling narrow a Trial exactly as they narrow the ranking. While one is owed every reversible call of that kind is a Trial, so the three rows arrive together; nothing is remembered between calls, the count being the store's own rows. `explored` now carries `trial` beside the two dimension names, and the note says the call was a Trial, names the point tried and says what the evidence would have chosen (ADR-0207). This ships together with the ranking change of issue #372, which is what it is the door out of; neither is released without the other (#374).
