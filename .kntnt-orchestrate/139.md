CHANGELOG.md — one bullet under `### Fixed` of `## [Unreleased]`:

- `/write` and `/redline` now say how a genre is inferred where the invocation, a recognized `kntnt` map, the current instruction, and the conversation all leave it unsettled, and what a run may read to infer one: the genre directory's listing, and the opening of each installed genre resource — its `# <Name>` heading and the paragraph under it — and nothing further of any resource the run does not resolve. The shared precedence had always put inference above the `general` default, but the only reading either resolution step authorised was the listing, and a listing carries filenames; a run inferring a genre therefore had a question the installed names could not answer, and answered it by opening a candidate to find out what that genre asks for. Seven of twenty-three Write runs and four Redline runs against the fixture corpus read the body of a genre they had not resolved, every one of them on an invocation that named no genre and never otherwise. Both loading steps are unchanged and still admit only what they always admitted — the base contract, the genre that was settled on and no other, the language scopes the resolver returned, an optional technique, and the review halves Redline loads beside them — and the editorial resource format page now records that the opening every genre and technique carries is what inference reads (issue #139).
