`CHANGELOG.md` — one entry under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Added`:

- The invocation grammar and the form of a Skill have one record each. Twenty-four decision records answered two questions between them — *how is an invocation read* and *what does a Skill ship* — and a reader tracing why a payload splits where it does, or why a frontmatter field is written twice, read a chain of records forward and reconciled them by hand. Their rules are now stated in `docs/rules/skills.md`, and for the two that are the collection's own promises in `docs/rules/collection.md`, which leaves the records holding one thing worth keeping: the reasoning. `docs/adr/0176-how-an-invocation-is-read.md` carries the grammar's — why a payload has two halves and what the reserved separator costs the collection in permutation, what a Contextual Instruction may settle and the one word of the refusal rule that was too wide, the two tolerances that made strictness visible and the flag-presence rule that follows from judging validity per invocation, why a value attaches with `=` and why the valued-flag registry is derived rather than written by hand, the free-text operand that forces one invocation order and the grammar with nothing to shadow that makes the separator optional, where help lives and how a subcommand page is addressed, the two Library engines that read their own command line, and the mode whose scope is the conversation it is typed in. It records as withdrawn the guarantee that a directly installed Skill kept the Envelope contract on its own manpage, so a dead promise is not read as live reasoning. `docs/adr/0177-what-a-skill-ships.md` carries the form's — the portability stance behind shipping Skills rather than harness command files, the parser everybody else uses and the offline cold start it costs, the flat prefixed `metadata` namespace and the foreign reader that coerces rather than refuses, the dependency lists a Skill owns and the checker the collection shares, the body paid on every invocation, the directories that say what a file is for, why shared implementation has no honest owner among its consumers, when a model may start a Skill and what its description then has to bound, and what a retired name is free for. Both state no rule the modules state and legislate nothing; where either needs a rule it cites the module. Why-chains that continue in records still standing are pointed at rather than retold (ADR-0029, ADR-0080, ADR-0113, ADR-0132), and one that runs back through a record the sweep deletes is written out in full instead. Every record they fold stays exactly where it is until that sweep, and none of them is edited. The suite's consolidation check now reads every bin the reform has written a record for in one pass, so the next such record adds a line to a map rather than a check of its own.
