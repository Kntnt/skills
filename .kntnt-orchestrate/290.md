### Fixed

- A measured token count is read at the level it was measured at, so the deliberation ladder still costs something once a point has rows behind it. The shipped prior was scaled by a level's `tokens` and `reasoning` factors and a measurement was not, so the first row filed against a model and a kind forecast one appetite for all five of that model's levels — and the level with the largest capability bonus then won every comparison that reads chance and price together, having become free. On the maintainer's own store `implement` ranked Opus 5 at `max` with no rows above Opus 5 at `high` with twenty-three at 0.957, both forecast the same 11.2 million cache reads, and did the same for three other models. Every row is now divided by the factors of the level it ran at before it is pooled, and the pooled figure scaled to the level being asked about, so a row taken at one rung prices the whole ladder rather than flattening it; a row from a model with no effort control carries factor one. The elapsed-time forecast is normalised the same way. Cost is pooled over a model's rows for the kind rather than over the exact `(kind, model, level)` cell first, those rows being one sample at one baseline once they are normalised — `p_success` keeps all three tiers, where the level a row ran at is the condition being estimated rather than a scale factor.
