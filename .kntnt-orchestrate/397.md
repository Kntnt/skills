## CHANGELOG.md

Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- `/redline` now reports a part a finished text lacks instead of writing it. Where an `article`, `case-study`, `column` or `opinion` arrived without a standfirst, subheadings or a call to action, the review wrote them in — a standfirst about a column's author in the third person above her own byline, a closing exhortation in her voice after her deliberate ending — and a working headline was reworded to reach a word band. The review extension of the article anatomy now says that a required part the text does not have is a finding left for a person to fill, that a part the text has is repaired from its own content, that a misplaced part such as a closing sign-off is moved into place, that ending content is a present ending, that a failure caused only by an absent part is reported as that part, and that a headline, subheading or paragraph meeting its requirements is not rewritten, split or merged only to come inside a *should* band. The anatomy says in one sentence that a review reports what a finished text lacks, and Redline's correction brief, its step 7 and the article genre's review half no longer have anybody write a missing part. Measured on the four #362 drafts in Claude Code on `claude-opus-5-5`, `R1` went from none of four runs to six of eight and no run wrote a missing part; the two misses, and three clean controls that came back with a rewritten heading, are filed as #429–#432 (#397).
