Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Fixed`:

- Every routed build was counted twice. `/orchestrate` files a `checker` row at the verdict that established an attempt's outcome, carrying the verdict's grade and the role's kind and no tokens at all — nothing on Claude Code exposes what an attempt spent to the session that dispatched it, and all 98 checker rows in the maintainer's store carry null tokens. The capture pass then read that same builder's transcript at the orchestrating session's end and filed a second row under an identity of its own, this one with the tokens, with whatever a judge or a free signal made of two excerpts of it, and with whatever kind the judge chose. Three such pairs sit in the store from 2026-09-05, matched on elapsed seconds within ten of each other; in one of them a verifier's 1.0 stands beside a judge's 0.18 under `analyze`. So each routed attempt appeared as one graded attempt with no cost beside one costed attempt with a weaker grade, and the estimator read both. Every routed builder brief now opens with the `attempt_id` the routing decision already held, capture takes that name as the unit's identity in place of the hash it computes, and a filing under an identity the store already holds is folded into the row it holds rather than added beside it: each null filled by whichever side measured it, the grade and the reading of the work that came with it from the higher authority, and the seat from whichever side read the transcript. One build is one row, in either filing order, and a filing repeated after a crash changes nothing at all.
