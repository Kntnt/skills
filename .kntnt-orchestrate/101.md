### Added

- `docs/evaluation/` now holds one shared fixture corpus for the editorial Skills and the protocol a run against it is judged and recorded under. The corpus is built from the specification rather than from any implementation, and each fixture documents what the material is, how it reaches a Skill, and what a correct run must never do with it — briefs, an interview transcript with spoken-language quotations, a long factual source, clean and mechanically flawed prose in two languages, concentrated AI slop, material for several genres and techniques, Text Artifacts with present, conflicting, unusable, unrelated, and absent frontmatter, inline, file, and immutable-URL sources, and the seven situations the output contract distinguishes. The protocol states blinded semantic judging with the five things a criterion may never let pass, defines the record format two provider families are compared by reading, and states the provider-isolation rule in both directions: a Codex session runs only GPT-family evaluations, a Claude session only Claude-family ones, and no cross-provider orchestration exists anywhere around the corpus. Nothing asserts exact generated prose or claims a model writes perfectly, and ADR-0093 records the reasoning (issue #101).
