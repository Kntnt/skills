`CHANGELOG.md` — one entry under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Changed`:

- Every Skill body opens with the engine call, and the prose that asked the model to do the engine's work is gone. Each of the fifteen `SKILL.md` files besides the Manager's now begins the same way: where the Manager is — `$HERE/../kntnt/` if it exists, else `kntnt/` under a Global harness skills directory, with `npx skills add Kntnt/skills` as the fix where neither is found — then `kntnt.py invoke --here="$HERE"` with the payload on stdin, continued from the JSON on exit 0 with the Capabilities answered first, and stdout printed verbatim on any other exit; `$HERE` and `$LIBRARY` keep their one-line definitions. The dependency preamble goes, the engine making that check; the `## Invocation` section goes, the engine splitting the Envelope and routing help off the shipped pages, and one sentence of it stays — the Contextual Instruction the JSON carries is applied under `library/references/invocation-envelope.md`, the semantic half no engine can judge; the parse step goes, and a step that read a flag reads it from the JSON. Each `## Arguments` keeps only what the engine cannot know — what an operand means, a value vocabulary the `SYNOPSIS` does not spell out, an exclusion between two flags, what a flag means to the Steps — and states no refusal: the grammar line, the order sentence and the list of invalid forms were the engine's to read off the `argument-hint` and the pages, and it refuses before a step is read. The one refusal the engine cannot make — a value whose form the page admits and whose meaning the Skill rejects, such as a `--max` that is not a non-negative integer or a genre nothing installs — is made by the step that settles that value, in the collection's shape. `brief` and `tldr`, which declared no binary, now declare `uv` in `kntnt.binaries` and in `compatibility`, the engine running on it, and their manpages' `DEPENDENCIES` and the README's dependency paragraph say so. Every page's `DIAGNOSTICS` is read against what the engine returns for a dash-prefixed token, an undeclared flag and an incomplete form on that Skill, and two corrections follow: `ready-for-agent-check`'s page promised to refuse every option, while a grammar that declares no flag and no command path takes a dash-prefixed token as operand (ADR-0176), so its `DIAGNOSTICS` now says the token is read as a ticket reference, one nothing resolves, that step 1 refuses in the collection's shape — and the rule says such a page states what the token is read as; and the engine, which let a declared flag written after an operand through on the root form of a Skill that also has command paths — `/orchestrate #12 --yes` — now refuses it there as every page's `DIAGNOSTICS` states, and the suite pins both readings against the shipped pages. Every other page is unchanged, its `## INVOCATION ENVELOPE` pointer and `DIAGNOSTICS` true of what the engine does. `docs/rules/skills.md` states the engine-first form in place of the preamble bullet and the Invocation bullet, and its refusal bullet says the engine refuses and the body adds only what the Skill leaves undone when it stops. Every Skill's own steps do what they did; user-facing behaviour is identical, every help form and every documented refusal producing what it produced, as the engine's tests hold against the shipped pages. The suite's assertions that pinned the old body form are rewritten to hold the new one or retired where the engine's tests hold what they held, and the Catalog is regenerated (ADR-0181).
