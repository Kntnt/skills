### Added

- Model Selector holds a subscription channel out of a machine-chosen answer while its weekly window is running ahead of the week — more than ten points past the share of the window elapsed, or at ninety per cent whatever the pace. Quota is a guard rather than a currency: the ranking stays on list price and the window only narrows the pool, never emptying it, never overruling a `--model` lock and never refusing. The Codex figure is read from Codex's own session logs and the Claude figure from `quota.json`, which the `statusline` Feature now writes out of the payload it already reads; `status` reports every window and says plainly where there is none (#373).
