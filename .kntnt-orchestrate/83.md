- Absent `--model`, `/orchestrate` now judges each builder's model per ticket instead of inheriting the orchestrating session's, because the absence of a choice is inheritance: three interviewed overnight runs ran every subagent — builder, verifier, repairer, checker — on the orchestrator's own model, `model` null in every plan, and their unanimous finding was that capability was never the limit, so the runs were paying the strongest model's price for mechanical builds the collection's own delegation rule says to send down the ladder. The orchestrator now chooses, from the ticket's own text, the cheapest model it may give the builder that it judges able — a mechanical rename is not a redesign — never above its own; repairing a collision and amending a failed build are building and delegate the same way, with the amend's own signal weighed, a ticket back for its amend having just demonstrated it was harder than it looked. Every verdict — ticket verification, repair verification, the wave check — runs on the orchestrator's own model, stated as the rule that the saving is never taken at the last thing that would catch a mistake, the interviewed runs' verifiers on that arrangement having been the strongest part of every run. `--model` keeps exactly its meaning as the override that pins every builder and switches the judgment off, and the manpage now advises running `/orchestrate` from the most capable model available — the Skill cannot choose the orchestrator's own seat, the session's model being the developer's move, and the run's judgment calls are only as good as the model asked to make them. ADR-0074 is the record this implements (issue #83).
