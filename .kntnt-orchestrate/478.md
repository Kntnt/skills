## The attribution a sentence moves when it credits a person with the narrator's content — #478

The Claude-family evaluation of [#478](https://github.com/Kntnt/skills/issues/478). The question was whether Redline's claim account still leaves out an attribution a run moved, as #475's run did when its lead credited Maya Lind with requirements the narrator stated. If it did, a widened second list in the reply checker was to repair it. Read it with [`../editorial-478/plan.md`](../editorial-478/plan.md) and [`../editorial-478/results.md`](../editorial-478/results.md). It copies #475's and #468's method, keeps each run's received text, final Text Artifact, drafted reply, checker return and delivered reply apart, and replays the checker on three frozen contrasts as diagnosis.

- [`redline-claude-2026-10-01-478.md`](redline-claude-2026-10-01-478.md) — sixteen counted `Redline` runs in Claude Code 2.1.286 on `claude-opus-5-5` at high deliberation, with two blind judges on every run: `case-study-clean` as three response-target and file-target pairs and `case-study-flawed` twice, in a pre-change arm staged from `fb169087` and against a candidate. Three runs cut off by the account's usage limit were voided and made again. No run in either arm carries a class 1 or class 2 miss. The narrator-to-Lind move occurred once, in the candidate arm, where the widened checker caught it. In replays of #475's saved input the pre-change checker caught it once in three, and the candidate in three of three. The defect is recorded as not reproduced: the candidate is reverted, nothing ships, nothing is filed, and ADR-0233 records why.
