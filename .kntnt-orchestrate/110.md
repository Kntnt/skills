- `/unslop` applies the shared anti-slop pass to one supplied text and does nothing else. It is the gesture for a text that is otherwise finished: the seven patterns the Collection's catalogue carries — false contrasts, empty openings, importance inflation, vague attribution, synonym cycling, robotic rhythm, and generic conclusions — applied by what they do in the resolved language rather than matched as the English strings the catalogue writes them with, and nothing outside that lens corrected. It selects no genre, no technique, and no editorial contract, and it loads none of them: the shared catalogue and the anti-slop scope of the resolved Language Resource are the whole of what it reads, so the wider composition, review, and mechanics guidance stays with the Skills contracted to act on it, and `/redline` remains the one workflow for the whole editorial contract — this Skill can neither replace it nor grow into it. It runs no mechanical pass and declares no Dependency on one, its page naming `/proofread` as the separate gesture for spelling, grammar, and punctuation. The language is the one parameter it resolves, by the Collection's own precedence — the Formal Invocation, a recognised `kntnt` map in the text's own leading frontmatter, the current Contextual Instruction, applicable Conversation Context, inference from the text, then the language of the supplied text — with a materially mixed language asked about rather than guessed; a recognised map may supply that one value, no map is created or synchronised, unrelated document frontmatter is never read as configuration, and an unusable recognised value no flag supersedes is reported as such rather than read as the value it resembles. Correction reuses the Collection's existing loop contract rather than restating it: `--max` is any non-negative integer and defaults to `1`, `0` reports without correcting, each correction goes to a subagent started fresh with the complete current text, the complete current findings, and the resolved language, the budget falls once per correction, the returned text is read again rather than accepted on its own report, and the loop stops on clean text with the rest unspent, on a correction that made no relevant progress, and on the spent budget, the last two delivering whatever is still unresolved beside the text. Source material stays outside the contract, one invocation processes one text, delivery follows the Collection's shared output contract including In-place Editing and its refusals, and subagents are a hard Capability whatever budget an invocation names. It is user-invoked: a model never starts it, because *this text reads like AI* is a judgement the author is entitled to make. ADR-0112 records why one lens applied alone ships narrower than the contract it belongs to (issue #110).
