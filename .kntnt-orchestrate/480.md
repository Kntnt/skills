### Fixed

- `/redline` no longer reports a working allusive headline as a finding because its words read as references the text has not yet filled in. A review asked such a headline for the text's particulars, meaning what was measured and what was found, and read its contrast as an ellipsis that only the standfirst completes. It then rewrote the headline, or left it for the author as unresolved. `headlines.review.md` now says that a reader of the heading alone is owed its subject and not its particulars, and that what the heading leaves for the text to fill in is the allusion at work, not a reference left unresolved. A heading that names no subject at all is still a finding. Measured on `claude-opus-5-5` over `article-clean`, a finding against its headline came in one run of six before the change and in none of six after it. Three headlines frozen and validated blind before the change were still read correctly under it: a second working allusion was left alone, and a headline naming no subject and an overclaiming headline were each detected in every run. The remaining misses are filed as #487 and #488 (#480).
