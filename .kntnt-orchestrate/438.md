CHANGELOG.md, under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Changed`:
- `/write` now tells the writer what a quotation bridge may carry, in the case-study genre file it loads, and no longer only in the review half it does not. The genre said that *a bridge should prepare a quotation rather than pre-say it*, a coined verb with no test, and the wording that said what to do about it was in `casestudy.review.md`, which Write never reads. The sentence is replaced by a requirement and a reader test: a quotation bridge should carry the speaker, the occasion or the question the quotation answers where the material gives one, and any fact the quotation does not itself give, and should not carry the quotation's judgement, figure or concession; a reader who has just read the quotation bridge meets the quotation as new material, never as the quotation bridge said again. The review half and `headlines.md` are unchanged. A control over 14 Write runs on `claude-opus-5-5` at high deliberation, with two blind judges on each draft, found no quotation bridge classed as restating its quotation and no subheading over a quotation classed as a pre-echo in either arm, so the change ships as the ticket's ship rule required. The GPT-family retest stays #395. (#438)
