Undoing any of them needed nothing the run lacked, since it holds every state the text has passed through. A returned correction is now a candidate rather than the current text until the independent review of it has run, and what that review establishes is what settles whether the candidate is accepted at all: a finding is repair-created where the passage it names differs from the state before the round that wrote it and the requirement it names was not violated there, established against the retained states rather than from how the finding words itself, so that a preference and a defect the text arrived with and nobody had noticed yet are not attributed to a repair. A repair-created defect — an unsupported or strengthened claim, a limit a repair dropped, a heading repeating what it stands over, material a round removed, anything else the loaded contract holds — now rejects the whole round that introduced it, whatever else that round repaired, and the round's complete pre-round Text Artifact comes back verbatim as the text the run delivers; where the introducing round was not the last, the state immediately before it is restored and every state built on it is discarded. Nothing of a rejected round is kept, and earlier accepted work survives only so far as the restored state holds it. The attempt keeps the budget it spent: restoration refunds nothing, spends nothing, and authorizes no wording of the run's own, no second attempt at the finding the rejected round answered and no correction to make up for what was discarded. The findings carried forward are the restored text's own — the findings that commissioned the rejected round, every one of them unresolved — and the delivery says the round was attempted and rejected and what its repair had introduced, keeping that apart from what the delivered text still carries, so a defect the run discarded is never reported as present and a finding carried forward is never reported as repaired. Both correction briefs now tell a fresh subagent what a defect of its own costs it, so a finding it cannot repair without creating another is a finding it leaves and says so. The rule names where it applies, because the first staged run of it without that accepted a round whose own two new subheadings its re-review had just failed: a part the round wrote is where a repair-created defect is established most often, a finding having commissioned the part does not put what it says beyond the check, the text having been worse without the part is no reason to keep a defective one, and the third stop condition is tested before the fourth so that a spent budget never answers for a defect the run created. #383's restoration of differences that trace to no finding still runs before the candidate is reviewed, the claim-loss and limiting-sentence protections are unchanged, and exactly one closing mechanical pass still follows the loop (#389).
