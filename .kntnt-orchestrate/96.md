- Routed execution can now hand back evidence: `/model-selector observe` turns completed routed attempts into a sanitized importable artifact in caller-owned scratch, `/orchestrate` records what each routed attempt's independent verdict established and names that artifact in its existing report, and delegation does the same only where an objective checker, frozen rubric, declared failure signal, or explicit user confirmation judged the work. An artifact carries the exact routed and actually served point, outcome, provenance, and whatever usage, cost, quota, and latency facts the environment exposed — unavailable ones stay `null` — and never a prompt, response, reasoning, source body, diff, terminal output, secret, transcript, or unnecessary absolute path. Mechanical hinders, open decisions, discovered dependencies, tracker failures, and merge collisions stay distinct from model failure and never lower a configuration's measured quality, self-reported success establishes nothing, and an unjudged decision remains audit data. Emission and import are idempotent, conflicting identities overwrite nothing, and `/model-selector record` remains the only thing that writes the evidence ledger (issue #96).
