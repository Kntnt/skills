Under `## [Unreleased]`, in `### Changed`:

- `/ready-for-agent-check` now reports a requirement that contradicts a rule. The rule may be one the repository states now, in its rules modules, its agent guide or a Skill's shipped files. It may also be one that another ticket this ticket names will write, meaning a blocker or a ticket cited as the source of a rule it relies on. The reviewer reads that named ticket's whole thread from the tracker, as a builder with tracker access would. Such a finding is always a Stop, and it quotes the ticket's sentence, the rule's sentence, and which of the two the ticket would have to change. A ticket that sets out to change the rule is not reported. Both stops of the unattended run of 2026-09-23 to 2026-09-28 were of this shape and reached `/orchestrate`'s wave check instead: #397 against a rule the repository already stated, and #416 against the rule its blocker #394 was set to write. Tickets that name nothing in common are still not compared, and the wave check stays the safety net for them. The review brief now counts eight things that stop a builder, and `help.md` lists the new criterion under REVIEW CRITERIA (#440).
